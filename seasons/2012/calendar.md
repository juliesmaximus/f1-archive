## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Stepped Noses:** Mandated maximum bulkhead height of 550 mm created the iconic 'platypus' stepped nosecone aesthetic.
- **Exhaust Regulations:** Exhaust exits strictly defined to prevent blown-diffuser downforce exploitation.
- **Calendar Expansion:** 20 Grands Prix, introducing Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas.
