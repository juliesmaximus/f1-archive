## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Seven Different Winners in Seven Races:** Unbroken all-time F1 record (Button, Alonso, Rosberg, Vettel, Maldonado, Webber, Hamilton).
- **Sebastian Vettel Youngest Triple World Champion:** At 25 years, 145 days, Sebastian Vettel won his third consecutive World Championship after a miraculous recovery drive from a lap-1 crash in Brazil.
- **Circuit of the Americas Debut:** Austin, Texas inaugurated modern Formula 1 in the United States.
- **Michael Schumacher's Final F1 Race:** The 7-time World Champion retired permanently from Formula 1 after his 306th start.
