## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Seven Winners in Opening Seven Races:** An unprecedented start to a Formula 1 season saw seven different drivers from five different teams win the first seven Grands Prix: Jenson Button (Australia), Fernando Alonso (Malaysia), Nico Rosberg (China, maiden win), Sebastian Vettel (Bahrain), Pastor Maldonado (Spain, maiden win for Williams since 2004), Mark Webber (Monaco), and Lewis Hamilton (Canada).
- **Sebastian Vettel Youngest Triple World Champion:** At 25 years, 145 days, Sebastian Vettel secured his third consecutive World Drivers' Championship, surviving a first-lap spin at Interlagos in wet conditions that damaged his exhaust and floor, carving through the field from 22nd to finish 6th and defeat Fernando Alonso by 3 points (281 to 278).
- **Red Bull Racing Third Consecutive Constructors' Championship:** Red Bull Racing claimed their third consecutive Constructors' Championship with 460 points, 7 victories, and 8 pole positions.
- **Michael Schumacher Final Formula 1 Season:** Schumacher achieved his 155th and final podium with 3rd place at Valencia, retiring definitively from Formula 1 at the end of the season.
- **COTA Debut:** The Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas made its debut as the new host of the United States Grand Prix, won by Lewis Hamilton.
