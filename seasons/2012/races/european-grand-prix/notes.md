## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of Fernando Alonso's absolute greatest masterpieces. Starting 11th on the grid, Alonso delivered an aggressive, virtuoso drive through the field. Sebastian Vettel dominated until his alternator failed on lap 33, while Romain Grosjean also retired from 2nd with alternator failure. Alonso swept to an emotional home victory, weeping on the podium. Michael Schumacher finished 3rd, scoring his 155th and final Formula 1 podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Pastor Maldonado collided with Lewis Hamilton on the penultimate lap at Turn 12, putting Hamilton into the wall.
