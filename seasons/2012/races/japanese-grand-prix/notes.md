## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Disaster for Fernando Alonso. At Turn 1 on lap 1, Kimi Räikkönen's front wing clipped Alonso's left-rear tyre, puncturing it and spinning Alonso into retirement. Sebastian Vettel executed a flawless Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap) to slash Alonso's championship lead to 4 points. Kamui Kobayashi drove a heroic race to finish 3rd, scoring an emotional podium on home soil as 140,000 fans chanted 'Kamui! Kamui!'

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Kobayashi became only the third Japanese driver in F1 history to score a podium.
