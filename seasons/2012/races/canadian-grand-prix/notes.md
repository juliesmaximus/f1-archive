## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Seven races, seven different winners! Lewis Hamilton executed a brilliant two-stop strategy. Alonso and Vettel attempted to finish on a one-stop strategy, but their tyres hit the 'cliff' in the final 8 laps. Hamilton carved past both like they were standing still to claim an emphatic victory. Romain Grosjean and Sergio Pérez charged to 2nd and 3rd.
