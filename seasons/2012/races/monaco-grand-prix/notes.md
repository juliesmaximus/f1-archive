## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Michael Schumacher set the fastest time in Saturday qualifying with a sublime lap, but a 5-place penalty carried over from Spain denied him pole. Mark Webber inherited pole and controlled the race at the front of a breathless 6-car train (Webber, Rosberg, Alonso, Vettel, Hamilton, Massa) separated by just 6 seconds at the finish line! Six different winners in the first six races.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Webber became the first Australian two-time winner of the Monaco Grand Prix.
