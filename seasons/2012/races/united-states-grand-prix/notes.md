## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic inaugural Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas. Sebastian Vettel led from pole, but Lewis Hamilton shadowed him relentlessly. On lap 42, as Vettel was delayed lapping Narain Karthikeyan through the esses, Hamilton deployed DRS down the back straight to sweep past and take victory. Red Bull Racing clinched their third consecutive World Constructors' Championship.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari deliberately broke the gearbox seal on Felipe Massa's car to drop him 5 places, promoting Alonso to the cleaner odd-numbered side of the grid.
