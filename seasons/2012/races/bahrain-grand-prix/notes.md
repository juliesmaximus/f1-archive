## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel became the fourth different winner in the first four races of 2012. Vettel led from pole position, withstanding intense pressure from Kimi Räikkönen, who attacked relentlessly in the Lotus. Romain Grosjean finished 3rd, scoring his maiden Formula 1 podium.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Race took place amidst political protests in Bahrain; high security surrounded the paddock.
