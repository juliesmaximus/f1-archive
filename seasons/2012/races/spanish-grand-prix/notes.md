## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the greatest upsets in modern Formula 1 history. Lewis Hamilton set fastest time in qualifying but was disqualified for stopping on track with insufficient fuel sample, handing pole to Pastor Maldonado. In the race, Maldonado drove an astonishingly composed race, beating local hero Fernando Alonso by 3.1 seconds to claim Williams' first victory since Brazil 2004! Ninety minutes after the race, a catastrophic fire erupted in the Williams garage; Maldonado heroically carried his 12-year-old cousin to safety.
