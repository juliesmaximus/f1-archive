## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton led from pole until his gearbox failed on lap 23. Sebastian Vettel inherited the lead to take victory, igniting an astonishing late-season championship comeback. Michael Schumacher rear-ended Jean-Éric Vergne's Toro Rosso under safety car, receiving a 10-place penalty.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's heartbreaking retirement proved to be the catalyst for his decision to leave McLaren and sign with Mercedes for 2013.
