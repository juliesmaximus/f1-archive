## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel made it four consecutive victories, leading every single lap from pole position to beat Fernando Alonso by 9.4 seconds. Alonso drove with furious aggression, passing Webber on the back straight with KERS and DRS to limit the damage to 13 points.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Webber suffered a KERS failure mid-race, leaving him vulnerable to Alonso.
