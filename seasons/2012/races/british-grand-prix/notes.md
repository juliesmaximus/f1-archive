## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso led from pole position on hard tyres, but Mark Webber stalked him relentlessly on soft tyres. With 4 laps remaining, Webber swept around the outside of Alonso into Brooklands to snatch victory by 3.0 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Massive Friday monsoon rains flooded spectator car parks, forcing Silverstone to turn away 30,000 ticket holders on Saturday.
