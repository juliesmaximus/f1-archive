## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Jenson Button won the season opener for the third time in four years. Button surged past pole-sitter Hamilton into Turn 1 and controlled the race flawlessly. Sebastian Vettel passed Hamilton under Safety Car conditions to claim 2nd, while Kimi Räikkönen drove from 17th to 7th on his F1 comeback.
