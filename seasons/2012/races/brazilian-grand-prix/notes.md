## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The greatest title decider in modern Formula 1 history. Vettel led Alonso by 13 points. In tricky drizzle, Vettel was tagged by Bruno Senna at Turn 4 on lap 1, spinning around and suffering a gaping hole in his left sidepod! Facing backward in 24th (last) place with a damaged car, Vettel mounted an epic recovery. In changing wet-dry conditions, Hamilton led until colliding with Nico Hülkenberg on lap 55. Jenson Button inherited victory. Alonso crossed the line in 2nd. But Vettel fought his way to 6th place—which was enough by 3 points (281 to 278) to become the youngest triple World Champion in Formula 1 history at 25 years, 145 days! Michael Schumacher finished 7th in his final career Grand Prix.
