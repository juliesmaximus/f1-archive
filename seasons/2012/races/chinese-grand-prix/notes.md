## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic breakthrough for Nico Rosberg and Mercedes. Starting from his maiden career pole position, Rosberg drove an untouchable race to score his first Formula 1 victory in his 111th Grand Prix start. This was the first Grand Prix victory for the Mercedes factory works team since Juan Manuel Fangio at Monza in 1955. Button and Hamilton completed the podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Michael Schumacher retired from 2nd when his right-front wheel nut was cross-threaded in a botched pitstop.
