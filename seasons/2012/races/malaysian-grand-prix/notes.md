## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An immortal wet-weather masterclass by Fernando Alonso in an uncompetitive Ferrari F2012. After a 51-minute red flag for monsoon rain, Alonso took the lead with lightning-quick tyre switches. 22-year-old Sergio Pérez launched an electrifying charge in the Sauber, hunting Alonso down to within 0.5 seconds before running wide at Turn 14 with six laps to go. Alonso held on to take an emotional victory.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Pérez's maiden podium in 2nd for Sauber ignited speculation of a future Ferrari drive.
