## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The 'Leave me alone, I know what I'm doing' race. Lewis Hamilton led comfortably until a fuel pressure failure halted his McLaren. Kimi Räikkönen took the lead in the Lotus and delivered his immortal radio message when his race engineer warned him about tyre temperatures. Sebastian Vettel started from the pitlane after a qualifying fuel infringement, broke his front wing against a DRS board under safety car, and charged through the field with blistering overtakes to finish 3rd on the podium behind Räikkönen and Alonso!

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Vettel's pitlane-to-podium charge preserved his championship lead at 10 points.
