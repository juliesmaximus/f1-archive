## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso drove a flawless race from pole position to become the first driver to win three races in 2012, holding off Jenson Button and Sebastian Vettel. Vettel overtook Button off the track at the hairpin on the penultimate lap and was handed a 20-second post-race penalty, dropping him from 2nd to 5th.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
