## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Horror at La Source. At the start, Romain Grosjean squeezed Lewis Hamilton against the pit wall, launching Grosjean's Lotus airborne directly over the cockpit of Fernando Alonso's Ferrari—missing Alonso's head by mere inches! The crash eliminated Alonso, Hamilton, Grosjean, and Pérez. The FIA handed Grosjean a historic one-race ban. Jenson Button dominated the race from pole position to win by 13.6 seconds ahead of Sebastian Vettel.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Grosjean became the first driver banned from a Grand Prix since Michael Schumacher in 1994.
