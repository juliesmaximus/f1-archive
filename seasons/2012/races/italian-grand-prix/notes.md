## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton controlled the race from pole position. Sergio Pérez drove an astonishing second stint on fresh soft tyres for Sauber, slicing past Räikkönen, Massa, and Alonso to finish 2nd. Both Red Bulls retired with alternator failures.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Sebastian Vettel received a drive-through penalty for forcing Alonso off track through the Curva Grande.
