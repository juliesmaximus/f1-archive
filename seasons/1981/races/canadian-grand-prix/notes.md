## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
VILLENEUVE'S BLIND WING DRIVE & LAFFITE VICTORY: In sodden, freezing conditions, Nelson Piquet took pole. Jacques Laffite in the Ligier JS17 drove an inspired wet-weather race to seize the lead and take victory by 6.73 seconds over John Watson. The race entered motorsport legend due to Gilles Villeneuve: after colliding with Elio de Angelis, the front wing of Villeneuve's Ferrari broke and folded back vertically directly in front of his cockpit visor, blinding his forward vision in pouring rain! Villeneuve refused to pit, peering around the obstruction and sliding through corners by feel. When the wing eventually tore off, Villeneuve drove the wingless Ferrari across the line to take an impossible 3rd place!
