## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
FIRST CARBON-FIBRE VICTORY (MCLAREN MP4/1): Renault locked out the front row with turbo power (Arnoux pole, Prost 2nd). Both Renaults suffered mechanical trouble, allowing British crowd favorite John Watson in John Barnard's revolutionary carbon-fibre McLaren MP4/1 to charge from 5th on the grid. Watson passed Carlos Reutemann and hunted down the leaders to take an emotional home victory by 40.65 seconds over Carlos Reutemann and Jacques Laffite! This marked the **first World Championship victory for a carbon-fibre composite monocoque chassis**, transforming race car construction worldwide forever.
