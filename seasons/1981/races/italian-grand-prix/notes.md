## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WATSON SURVIVES MONZA ESCAPE & PROST WINS: Italian GP returned to Monza after the 1980 Imola hiatus. René Arnoux took pole for Renault. On lap 19, John Watson suffered a horrific 150 mph crash entering the Lesmo curve; the carbon-fibre monocoque of his McLaren MP4/1 sheared away from the engine but remained entirely intact, saving Watson's life and proving the structural superiority of carbon fibre. Alain Prost dominated to win by 22.17 seconds over Alan Jones and Carlos Reutemann. Nelson Piquet suffered engine failure on the final lap, finishing 6th.
