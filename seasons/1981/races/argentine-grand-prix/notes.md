## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
BRABHAM HYDROPNEUMATIC SUSPENSION CONTROVERSY: To circumvent FISA's mandatory 6 cm ride height rule, Brabham designer Gordon Murray fitted the BT49C with a hydropneumatic suspension system. The car sat at legal 6 cm height when stationary in scrutineering, but at speed aerodynamic downforce compressed the hydraulic fluid, lowering the car flush to the ground to recreate full ground effect! Nelson Piquet claimed pole and crushed the opposition, winning by 26.96 seconds over home hero Carlos Reutemann. Competitors protested, but the FIA declared the system legal.
