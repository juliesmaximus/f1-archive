## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
VILLENEUVE'S MONACO TURBO MASTERPIECE: Nelson Piquet qualified on pole in the Brabham. In the race, Piquet led comfortably until lap 53, when he crashed into the barrier at Tabac after being distracted by backmarker traffic. Alan Jones inherited the lead in his Williams, but with 4 laps remaining, fuel vaporization caused Jones's engine to misfire. Gilles Villeneuve in the heavy, uncooperative Ferrari 126CK turbo pulled off an astonishing pass along the pit straight with three laps to go, wrestling the turbo car through the twisting streets to claim Ferrari's **historic first victory with a turbocharged car**!
