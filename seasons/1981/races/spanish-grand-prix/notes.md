## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE LEGENDARY JARAMA TRAIN: Gilles Villeneuve qualified 7th in the Ferrari 126CK turbo. At the start, Villeneuve made a ferocious start along the outside to jump to 3rd, then passed Carlos Reutemann for 2nd into Turn 1. When pole-sitter Alan Jones spun off on lap 14, Villeneuve took the lead. The Ferrari was massively fast on Jarama's long straight (producing over 600 bhp) but handled like a truck through the slow, twisty corners. For the final 66 laps, Jacques Laffite (Ligier), John Watson (McLaren), Carlos Reutemann (Williams), and Elio de Angelis (Lotus) swarmed all over Villeneuve's gearbox. Villeneuve positioned his Ferrari with surgical perfection on every corner, holding off all four cars in a breathtaking high-speed tactical masterclass. Villeneuve crossed the finish line to win by 0.22 seconds, with all five cars covered by just 1.24 SECONDS—one of the closest and most celebrated finishes in Grand Prix history!
