## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALAIN PROST'S MAIDEN GRAND PRIX VICTORY: Nelson Piquet dominated the first part of the race from pole position in the Brabham. On lap 58, a violent rainstorm struck Dijon, red-flagging the race. At the restart on a drying track for the remaining 22 laps, young Frenchman Alain Prost in the Renault RE30 turbo made a blistering start to take the lead. Prost stormed away on aggregate time to claim his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory** by 2.29 seconds ahead of John Watson (McLaren MP4/1) and Nelson Piquet, initiating the legendary career of 'The Professor'.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The maiden Formula One victory of Alain Prost, who would go on to win 51 Grands Prix and 4 World Championships.
