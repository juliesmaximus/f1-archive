## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S HOCKENHEIM TRIUMPH: Alain Prost took pole in the Renault RE30 turbo and led for 20 laps until Alan Jones (Williams) overtook him. Jones led until lap 38, when an intermittent misfire slowed him, forcing an unscheduled pit stop. Nelson Piquet in the Brabham BT49C seized the lead and managed his Goodyear tires through late rain to win by 11.52 seconds over Alain Prost and Jacques Laffite. Carlos Reutemann retired with engine failure, allowing Piquet to slash Reutemann's championship lead to 8 points.
