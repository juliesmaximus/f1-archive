## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S TITLE AGONY IN LAS VEGAS CAR PARK: Set up in the asphalt parking lot of the Caesars Palace hotel, the flat, repetitive counter-clockwise layout caused immense physical neck strain on drivers in 37°C heat. Carlos Reutemann took pole in the Williams FW07C. At the start, Reutemann suffered severe gearbox trouble and slipped down the field, appearing listless and emotionally drained. Alan Jones dominated to win the final race of his career by 20.048 seconds over Alain Prost. Nelson Piquet suffered violent vomiting and neck collapse from exhaustion in his cockpit, barely able to hold his head upright over the final 20 laps. Piquet dragged his Brabham BT49C across the line in 5th place to score 2 points, clinching the **1981 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship** by a single point over Reutemann (50 to 49)!
