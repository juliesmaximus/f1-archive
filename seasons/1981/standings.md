# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1981 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Nelson Piquet won his first FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Brabham BT49C Ford Cosworth DFV, scoring 50 net points with 3 victories. Carlos Reutemann finished as runner-up by a single point with 49 points.
