# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1981 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the revised 1981 regulations, only the best **11** results across the 15 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Nelson Piquet won his first FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Brabham BT49C Ford Cosworth DFV, scoring 50 net points (50 gross) with 3 victories. Carlos Reutemann finished as runner-up by a single point with 49 points.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for all top six finishers of each constructor at each Grand Prix. All results counted towards the Constructors' Championship (no dropped scores), and both cars of each constructor could score points.

Williams Grand Prix Engineering captured their second consecutive World Constructors' Championship with 95 points, winning 4 Grands Prix with Alan Jones and Carlos Reutemann.

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Drivers' Dropped Scores Rule:** For 1981, the split-halves system was dropped; the best 11 results from all 15 rounds counted towards the Drivers' Championship.
- **Constructors' Scoring Rule:** All scores counted towards the Constructors' Championship (no dropped rounds), and all cars of each constructor were eligible to score points.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Nelson Piquet won his first World Championship by 1 point at Caesars Palace, Las Vegas.
  - John Barnard's McLaren MP4/1 scored the first-ever Grand Prix win for a carbon-fibre monocoque (John Watson at Silverstone).
  - Alain Prost scored his maiden Formula One victory at the French Grand Prix at Dijon in the Renault RE30.
  - Gilles Villeneuve scored two historic victories in the Ferrari 126CK: at Monaco and at Jarama (Spanish GP), where he headed a famous 5-car train covered by 1.24 seconds at the flag.
  - Carlos Reutemann and Alan Jones's bitter Williams rivalry ignited when Reutemann defied team orders to win in Brazil.
  - ABBA session drummer Slim Borgudd scored his sole career F1 championship point for ATS in Britain.
  - Eliseo Salazar scored Chile's first-ever Formula One World Championship point for Ensign in the Netherlands.
