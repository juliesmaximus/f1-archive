# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1981 - Calendar

The 1981 Formula One season was the 32nd season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 15 championship rounds between March 15 and October 17, 1981.

The 1981 season was an epic, fiercely fought championship characterized by monumental technical landmarks, bitter team rivalries, and the closest championship finish in years:
- **Nelson Piquet's First World Championship:** Brazilian Nelson Piquet captured his **first World Drivers' Championship** in the Gordon Murray-designed Brabham BT49C Ford Cosworth DFV, edging Williams driver Carlos Reutemann by a single point (50 to 49) in a grueling finale under the scorching desert sun in the Caesars Palace car park in Las Vegas.
- **The Carbon-Fibre Revolution:** John Barnard and Ron Dennis (who merged Project Four with McLaren) introduced the revolutionary **McLaren MP4/1**, the **first carbon-fibre composite monocoque in Formula One history**. At Silverstone, John Watson drove the MP4/1 to an emotional home victory, proving the chassis was vastly stiffer, lighter, and safer than aluminum predecessors.
- **Alain Prost's Maiden Victory:** Future four-time World Champion Alain Prost scored his **maiden Formula One victory** at the French Grand Prix at Dijon-Prenois in the Renault RE30 turbo, initiating an era of French supremacy.
- **Gilles Villeneuve's Miracle Drives:** Gilles Villeneuve tamed the ill-handling turbocharged Ferrari 126CK to score two of the greatest victories in F1 history: around the tight barriers of Monaco and holding off a frantic five-car train covered by just 1.24 seconds at Jarama (Spanish GP).
- **Williams Intra-Team Civil War:** Carlos Reutemann defied team pit-board orders to let team leader Alan Jones pass in torrential rain in Brazil, causing an unbridgeable feud that fractured Williams and ultimately handed the Drivers' title to Piquet, though Williams comfortably retained the **World Constructors' Championship**.
