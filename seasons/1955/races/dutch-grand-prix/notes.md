## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Post-Le Mans Racing Landscape**: Held just eight days after the catastrophic Le Mans disaster (in which 84 spectators were killed when Pierre Levegh's Mercedes 300 SLR crashed into the crowd), European governments began banning motorsport events. The French, German, Swiss, and Spanish Grands Prix were cancelled, leaving Zandvoort, Aintree, and Monza as the only remaining European rounds.
- **Mercedes Formation 1-2**: Fangio and Moss gave another exhibition of Silver Arrows supremacy, leading every lap and crossing the line separated by just 0.3 seconds.
- **Mieres Fastest Lap**: Argentine driver Roberto Mieres set the fastest race lap (1:40.900) in his factory Maserati 250F, claiming his sole World Championship bonus point.
