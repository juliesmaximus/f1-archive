## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Post-Le Mans Tensions: Running eight days after the Le Mans tragedy, heavy safety discussions permeated the paddock. Mercedes management decided to race under strict sporting decorum.
- Mercedes Formation 1-2: Fangio and Moss drove in perfect sync, crossing the finish line just 0.3 seconds apart for another commanding Mercedes 1-2.
- Mieres Maiden Fastest Lap: Argentine driver Roberto Mieres set the fastest race lap (1:48.3) in his factory Maserati 250F, finishing 4th.
- Musso Scores First Maserati Podium: Luigi Musso finished 3rd in the Maserati 250F, one lap behind the Silver Arrows.
