## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XIII Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Historic Name Notes**: 13th Monaco Grand Prix; designated the honorary 'Grand Prix d'Europe'; first Monaco GP included on the championship calendar since 1950.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit de Monaco
- **Location**: Monte Carlo and La Condamine, Principality of Monaco
- **Host Country**: Monaco

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Alberto Ascari Harbour Plunge**: On lap 80, having inherited the lead after Stirling Moss suffered engine failure, Alberto Ascari misjudged the chicane in his Lancia D50, crashed through straw bales and barriers, and somersaulted into the harbour. Ascari swam to safety with a broken nose, rescued by local boatmen.
- **Tragic Ascari Fatality 4 Days Later**: Just four days after escaping the harbour plunge, on 26 May 1955, Ascari visited Monza to watch Eugenio Castellotti test a Ferrari 750 Monza sports car. Borrowing Castellotti's helmet, Ascari suffered a fatal crash at Curva del Vialone on lap 3, dying at age 36.
- **Maiden Win for Trintignant & First French Winner**: Maurice Trintignant claimed his maiden World Championship victory in a Ferrari 625, becoming the FIRST FRENCH DRIVER TO WIN A FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX.
- **Mercedes Double Mechanical DNF**: Both Silver Arrows retired while leading: Fangio on lap 49 (transmission failure) and Moss on lap 80 (engine failure).
