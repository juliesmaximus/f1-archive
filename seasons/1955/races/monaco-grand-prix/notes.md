## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari's Harbour Plunge: On lap 80, having just inherited the lead when Stirling Moss's Mercedes engine blew, Alberto Ascari's Lancia D50 clipped the chicane barrier, crashed through straw bales and light hoardings, and somersaulted into the Mediterranean harbour. Ascari swam to the surface with a broken nose and bruises, rescued by boatmen.
- Ascari Fatal Monza Crash (4 Days Later): Ascari miraculously survived the harbour crash, but just four days later on 26 May 1955, he visited Monza to watch Eugenio Castellotti test a Ferrari 750 Monza sports car. Borrowing Castellotti's helmet, Ascari crashed at Curva del Vialone on lap 3 and was killed instantly at age 36.
- Maurice Trintignant Maiden Win: French driver Maurice Trintignant scored his FIRST Formula One World Championship victory in a Ferrari 625, becoming the FIRST FRENCH DRIVER TO WIN A WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX.
- Mercedes Double DNF: Both Fangio (transmission, lap 49) and Moss (blown engine, lap 80) retired from the lead.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First French driver to win a World Championship Grand Prix: Maurice Trintignant.
- First car to plunge into Monaco harbour during a Grand Prix (Alberto Ascari).
- Final Grand Prix appearance for double World Champion Alberto Ascari.
