## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari's Harbour Plunge: On lap 80, having inherited the lead after Mercedes drivers Fangio and Moss both retired with mechanical failures, Alberto Ascari misjudged the chicane at the exit of the tunnel, crashed through straw bales, and plunged into the Mediterranean harbour! Ascari surfaced and was rescued by frogmen with only a broken nose.
- Mercedes Collapse: Both Fangio (transmission) and Moss (blown engine while leading by over a minute) retired, clearing the way for an unexpected result.
- Maiden Win for Trintignant: Maurice 'Le Pétoulet' Trintignant scored his maiden Formula One victory and Scuderia Ferrari's only victory of the 1955 season.
- Ascari's Final Grand Prix: Ascari tragically lost his life 4 days later in an accident testing a Ferrari sports car at Monza on 26 May.
