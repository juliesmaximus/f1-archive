# 1955 Round 1: III Gran Premio de la República Argentina - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: III Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Historic Name Notes**: 3rd Argentine Grand Prix; renowned as one of the most physically punishing endurance races in Formula One history.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre
- **Location**: Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Host Country**: Argentina
- **Track Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 10 m (33 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 96 laps / 375.552 km (233.357 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1955-01-16

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Extreme heatwave; ambient temperature reaching 40°C (104°F) with tarmac temperature over 55°C (131°F).

- **Pole Position**: José Froilán González (Lancia D50) – 1:43.100
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) – 1:48.300 (Lap 45) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) – 3:00:38.600 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) – 3:00:38.600 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : José Froilán González, Nino Farina & Maurice Trintignant (Ferrari 625 #12) – +1:29.600 (+89.600s) [2 pts each, shared drive]
- **3rd** : Nino Farina, Maurice Trintignant & Umberto Maglioli (Ferrari 625 #10) – +2 laps [1.33 pts each, shared drive]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Fangio Superhuman Solo Victory**: While overwhelming heat forced almost every other driver to seek relief drivers, Juan Manuel Fangio was one of only two drivers (along with Roberto Mieres) to complete the entire grueling 3-hour race solo. Fangio suffered second-degree friction and heat burns on his right leg from the exhaust chassis tube, leaving a permanent scar.
- **Unique Shared Drive Points Matrix**: Extreme heat led to continuous driver swaps across the Ferrari entries. Nino Farina and Maurice Trintignant remarkably shared BOTH the 2nd and 3rd placed cars:
  - Car #12 (2nd place): González, Farina, Trintignant split 6 points (2 points each).
  - Car #10 (3rd place): Farina, Trintignant, Maglioli split 4 points (1.33 points each).
  - Farina and Trintignant each scored 3.33 points from two separate classified finishing positions in the same Grand Prix.
