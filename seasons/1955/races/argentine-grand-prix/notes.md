## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: III Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Historic Name Notes**: 3rd Argentine Grand Prix; celebrated as one of the most physically punishing races in Grand Prix history.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre
- **Location**: Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Host Country**: Argentina
- **Track Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 10 m (33 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 96 laps / 375.552 km (233.357 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1955-01-16

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Brutal heatwave; ambient temperature 40°C (104°F), track surface temperature exceeding 55°C (131°F).
- **Spectator Attendance**: Over 200,000 spectators.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Spectators fanned themselves under scorching sun; track hospital treated dozens of heat exhaustion cases.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Fangio's Superhuman Solo Drive: Amidst crushing heat that caused drivers to collapse and vomit, Juan Manuel Fangio was one of only two drivers (along with Roberto Mieres) to complete the 3-hour race without relief. Fangio suffered second-degree burns on his right leg from the hot chassis tubes, leaving a permanent scar.
- Bizarre Shared Drive Points: Drivers swapped cars repeatedly. Nino Farina and Maurice Trintignant finished BOTH 2nd and 3rd! For 2nd place, González, Farina, and Trintignant split 6 points (2 points each). For 3rd place, Farina, Trintignant, and Umberto Maglioli split 4 points (1.33 points each). Thus Farina and Trintignant each scored 3.33 points from two different finishing positions in the same race!
- Ferrari 2-3-4 Formation: Despite Fangio's victory for Mercedes, Ferrari occupied positions 2, 3, and 4 through their shared driver relays.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Only instance in World Championship history where two drivers (Farina and Trintignant) scored championship points for finishing both 2nd and 3rd in the same Grand Prix.
- Fangio won by 1 minute 29.6 seconds in 3 hours 00 minutes 38.6 seconds of racing.
