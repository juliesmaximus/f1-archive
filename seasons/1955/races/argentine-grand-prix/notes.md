# 1955 Round 1: Argentine Grand Prix Verified Race Record

- **Official Race Title**: III Gran Premio de la Republica Argentina
- **Circuit**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre (Buenos Aires, Argentina)
- **Track Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 mi) | **Race Distance**: 96 laps (375.552 km (233.357 mi))
- **Date**: 1955-01-16
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Scorching heat (ambient air 40°C / 104°F, track surface >55°C / 130°F)

## Verified Key Results
- **Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) - 3:00:38.6 (124.738 km/h)
- **Pole Position**: José Froilán González (Ferrari 625) - 1:43.1
- **Official Fastest Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) - 1:48.3 (Lap 45) [1 point]

## Podium Finishers & Points
- 1st: Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) - 96 laps, 3:00:38.6 (9 pts)
- 2nd: José Froilán González, Nino Farina & Maurice Trintignant (Ferrari 625) - 96 laps, +1:29.6 (2 pts each)
- 3rd: Nino Farina, Maurice Trintignant & Umberto Maglioli (Ferrari 625) - 94 laps, +2 Laps (1.33 pts each)

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The Race of the Furnaces: Brutal heat caused physical collapse among drivers. Fangio and Roberto Mieres were the ONLY two drivers to complete the entire 3-hour race without relief drivers.
- Widespread Driver Swapping: Ferrari swapped drivers across cars frantically to stay in the race. Farina and Trintignant shared cars so heavily that both finished on the podium TWICE (2nd and 3rd), receiving shared fractions of points for both positions!
- Fangio's Iron Constitution: Fangio drove his Mercedes W196 solo for the entire 3 hours, suffering severe leg burns from the exhaust pipes touching the chassis cockpit, but took victory.
