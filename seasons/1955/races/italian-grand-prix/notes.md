## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Debut of the 10 km Banked Monza: First World Championship race run on the newly constructed 10.000 km combined track, featuring the concrete high-speed banked oval (*sopraelevata*). Average speeds shattered all European records at >206 km/h.
- Lancia D50 Transfers to Ferrari: Following Alberto Ascari's death and financial difficulties, Gianni Lancia formally gifted all Lancia D50 cars, assets, and design rights to Scuderia Ferrari in July. Castellotti, Villoresi, and Farina drove Lancia-Ferraris at Monza.
- Mercedes Farewell: In their final Formula One race as a factory team, Mercedes finished 1-2 with Fangio and Taruffi.
- Fangio Clinches 3rd World Championship: Juan Manuel Fangio celebrated his 3rd World Drivers' Championship with his 4th victory of the season.
