## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Stirling Moss Maiden Victory**: 25-year-old Stirling Moss took his FIRST EVER World Championship Grand Prix win, becoming the FIRST BRITISH DRIVER TO WIN HIS HOME GRAND PRIX on British soil.
- **Moss Grand Slam**: Moss achieved a flawless performance: pole position (2:00.400), fastest lap (2:00.400), led the majority of laps, and won the race.
- **Historic Mercedes-Benz 1-2-3-4 Clean Sweep**: Mercedes executed an unprecedented lockout of the top 4 positions: 1st Moss, 2nd Fangio (+0.2s), 3rd Kling (+1:11.8s), and 4th Piero Taruffi (+1:16.0s).
- **The 'Did Fangio Let Him Win?' Debate**: On the final corner, Fangio closed right behind Moss, crossing the line just 0.200s adrift. When Moss later asked Fangio if he had let him win on home soil, Fangio famously replied: *"No, today you were just better than me"*.
