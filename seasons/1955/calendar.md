### Cancelled Grands Prix (Post-Le Mans Disaster)
- **French Grand Prix** (Reims-Gueux, scheduled 3 July 1955) - Cancelled
- **German Grand Prix** (Nürburgring Nordschleife, scheduled 31 July 1955) - Cancelled
- **Swiss Grand Prix** (Circuit Bremgarten, scheduled 21 August 1955) - Cancelled and motor racing permanently banned in Switzerland
- **Spanish Grand Prix** (Pedralbes Circuit, scheduled 23 October 1955) - Cancelled
