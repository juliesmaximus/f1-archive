## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Scoring System Revolution:** Top 8 score: 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 (previously top 6: 10-6-4-3-2-1).

- **HANS Device:** Mandatory driver safety equipment for basal skull fracture protection.
- **Tyre War Escalation:** Fierce battle between Michelin and Bridgestone culminating in post-Hungary tread measurement clarification.
