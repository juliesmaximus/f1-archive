## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sensational opening round under the new 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 points system and single-lap qualifying. In changing wet-dry conditions, Juan Pablo Montoya led late in the Williams-BMW but spun with 11 laps remaining at Turn 1, handing victory to David Coulthard. Kimi Räikkönen finished 3rd after an intense duel with Michael Schumacher, whose bargeboard was damaged over curbs.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First race under single-lap qualifying (one run, race fuel). Tyre choices on the damp grid reshuffled order completely.
