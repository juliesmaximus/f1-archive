## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Juan Pablo Montoya scored a supreme Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap), winning by an astonishing 1 minute and 5 seconds. At Turn 1 on lap 1, Ralf Schumacher, Kimi Räikkönen, and Rubens Barrichello triggered a massive three-way collision, eliminating all three title contenders immediately. Michael Schumacher suffered a left-rear tyre puncture with 5 laps remaining, dropping from 2nd to 7th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ralf Schumacher was initially handed a 10-place grid penalty for causing the first-corner collision, later overturned to a fine on appeal.
