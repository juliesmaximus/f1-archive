## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
History was made as 22-year-old Fernando Alonso became the youngest Grand Prix winner in Formula 1 World Championship history (22 years, 26 days), breaking Bruce McLaren's 1959 record. Alonso dominated the race from pole, remarkably lapping reigning World Champion Michael Schumacher on lap 44. Kimi Räikkönen finished 2nd, tightening the championship battle to within 2 points.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Michelin's wide front tyres drew a formal protest from Ferrari and Bridgestone after the race, alleging tread contact patches exceeded the 270 mm legal width when heated and worn.
