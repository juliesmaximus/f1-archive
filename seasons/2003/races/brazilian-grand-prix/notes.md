## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the most chaotic, bizarre races in modern F1 history (F1's 700th World Championship GP). Torrential rain produced a river flowing across Curva do Sol, claiming Michael Schumacher, Juan Pablo Montoya, Jenson Button, and Antonio Pizzonia in identical aquaplaning spins. Barrichello retired from the lead with fuel pressure failure. On lap 53, Fisichella passed Räikkönen for the lead. On lap 54, Mark Webber crashed heavily at the Curva do Café, scattering debris. Fernando Alonso struck a detached wheel at 240 km/h, obliterating his Renault against tyre barriers. The race was red-flagged on lap 55.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Post-race timing initially declared Kimi Räikkönen the winner under the countback rule (lap 53). Five days later at the FIA court in Paris, official telemetry proved Fisichella had started lap 56 before the red flag, meaning countback went to lap 54 when Fisichella led! Fisichella was officially awarded his maiden victory in a special podium exchange at Imola.
