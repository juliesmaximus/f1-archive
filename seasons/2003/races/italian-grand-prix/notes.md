## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The fastest Grand Prix in Formula 1 history. Michael Schumacher and Juan Pablo Montoya engaged in a breathtaking wheel-to-wheel battle through the Variante del Rettifilo and Roggia chicane on lap 1. Schumacher prevailed to win at an all-time record average race speed of 247.585 km/h (153.842 mph) in 1 hour, 14 minutes, and 19.838 seconds. The championship standing: Schumacher 82, Montoya 79, Räikkönen 75.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
