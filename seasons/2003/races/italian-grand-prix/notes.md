## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A chaotic, championship-defining race. When rain fell in bursts, Bridgestone's intermediate tyres proved vastly superior to Michelin's. Michael Schumacher charged from 7th to victory. Montoya received a controversial drive-through penalty for colliding with Barrichello, finishing 6th and ending his title hopes. Kimi Räikkönen finished 2nd to remain the sole challenger.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Stewards' penalty on Juan Pablo Montoya drew heavy criticism from Williams and fans as a normal racing incident.
