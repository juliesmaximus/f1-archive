## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An all-time classic thriller. On lap 11, defrocked Irish priest Neil Horan ran onto Hangar Straight wearing a kilt and carrying banners, running directly towards Formula 1 cars traveling at 175 mph! The Safety Car was scrambled, scrambling strategies. Rubens Barrichello drove arguably the race of his life, executing sensational wheel-to-wheel overtakes on Räikkönen, Montoya, and Trulli to claim an emphatic victory.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The track invader breach triggered massive security reviews by the FIA and Silverstone management; Horan was tackled by heroic marshal Stephen Green and subsequently sentenced to prison.
