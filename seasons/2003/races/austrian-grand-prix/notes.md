## 4. Race Narrative & Historical Milestones
- **Schumacher's Pitstop Fire:** On lap 23 during his first scheduled pit stop, Michael Schumacher's Ferrari F2003-GA caught fire when fuel leaked from the rig onto the hot engine bodywork. Schumacher remained completely calm in the cockpit as Ferrari mechanics swiftly extinguished the flames. He rejoined without losing the lead battle, losing roughly 12 seconds.
- **Battle with Räikkönen:** After resolving the pit stop drama, Schumacher chased down McLaren's Kimi Räikkönen and overtook him on lap 32 into the Remus curve to secure his 3rd consecutive victory of the 2003 season.
- **Final Grand Prix at A1-Ring:** This marked the final Formula 1 Grand Prix held at the classic A1-Ring before the circuit was decommissioned and extensively rebuilt as the Red Bull Ring, returning to the Formula 1 calendar in 2014.
