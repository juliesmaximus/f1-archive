## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Williams-BMW broke a 20-year Monaco drought with Juan Pablo Montoya claiming victory in a breathless 4-way battle, crossing the line just 0.6 seconds ahead of Kimi Räikkönen and 1.7s ahead of Michael Schumacher. Remarkably, there was not a single on-track overtaking maneuver in the entire race—all positions were decided by pit stop strategy and qualifying.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Jenson Button suffered an enormous 185 mph crash into the chicane barrier during Saturday morning practice and was hospitalized, withdrawing from the race.
