## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Debut of Ferrari's new F2003-GA (named in tribute to Gianni Agnelli). Michael Schumacher held off Fernando Alonso, whose brilliant charge in the Renault ignited 'Alonsomania' across Spain, finishing only 5.7 seconds behind the 5-time champion. Rubens Barrichello completed the podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Kimi Räikkönen started from the back after crashing in qualifying and collided with Antônio Pizzonia's stalled Jaguar on the grid.
