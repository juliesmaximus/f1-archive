## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Kimi Räikkönen (McLaren-Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Fernando Alonso (Renault) - 1:37.044
- **Fastest Lap:** Michael Schumacher (Ferrari) - 1:36.412 (Lap 45)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso scored his maiden career pole position at 21 years old, leading early before Kimi Räikkönen produced an impeccably measured drive to take his maiden Formula 1 victory by 39 seconds over Rubens Barrichello. Alonso finished 3rd on the podium battling high fever and dehydration.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Michael Schumacher collided with Jarno Trulli at Turn 2 on the opening lap and received a drive-through penalty, recovering to 6th.
