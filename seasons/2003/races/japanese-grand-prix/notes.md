## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Championship decider. Michael Schumacher needed just 1 point to secure the title, but qualified 14th after rain hit his flying lap. Schumacher had a tumultuous race, breaking his front wing against Takuma Sato's BAR-Honda and surviving a near-collision with brother Ralf. Rubens Barrichello brilliantly controlled the race to win and take the win from Räikkönen. Schumacher crossed the line 8th to score the single point needed to claim his historic 6th World Drivers' Championship, surpassing Juan Manuel Fangio's 46-year-old record.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Dramatic final laps as Schumacher survived intense attacks from Da Matta and Ralf Schumacher to secure the decisive 8th place.
