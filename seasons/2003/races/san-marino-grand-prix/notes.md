## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Deeply emotional weekend for the Schumacher brothers: their mother, Elisabeth Schumacher, passed away in Cologne early Sunday morning. Michael and Ralf decided to race, locking out the front row and dueling into Tamburello. Michael executed a dominant three-stop strategy to take his first win of 2003. Wearing black armbands, Michael wept openly on the podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Both Schumacher brothers were excused by the FIA from the post-race press conference due to compassionate leave.
