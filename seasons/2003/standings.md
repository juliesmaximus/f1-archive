## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Michael Schumacher's Record 6th Title:** Michael Schumacher surpassed Juan Manuel Fangio's 46-year-old record of 5 World Drivers' Championships, winning his 6th crown by just 2 points (93 to 91) over Kimi Räikkönen in a tense finale at Suzuka.
- **Fernando Alonso's Historic Maiden Win:** At the Hungarian Grand Prix, 22-year-old Fernando Alonso became the then-youngest Grand Prix winner in Formula 1 history, lapping Michael Schumacher on the way to victory.
- **New 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 Points System:** The FIA introduced a revised points structure rewarding the top 8 finishers, designed to keep championship battles tighter by narrowing the gap between 1st (10) and 2nd (8).
- **HANS Device Mandated:** The Head and Neck Support device became mandatory for all Formula 1 drivers, vastly improving driver safety.
- **Giancarlo Fisichella's Delayed Maiden Win:** Fisichella scored Jordan's final F1 win in a chaotic, red-flagged Brazilian GP at Interlagos. Initially awarded to Räikkönen on the podium, the FIA Court of Appeal corrected the countback timing error five days later and officially declared Fisichella the winner.
- **Tyre War Climax:** A high-intensity battle between Michelin and Bridgestone prompted an FIA technical clarification on front tyre width after Hungary, forcing Michelin to redesign its tyre tread for Monza and Indianapolis.
