## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Michael Schumacher's Record 6th Title:** Michael Schumacher surpassed Juan Manuel Fangio's legendary 1957 record of 5 World Drivers' Championships, winning his 6th crown by just 2 points over Kimi Räikkönen.
- **Fernando Alonso's Historic Maiden Win:** At the Hungarian Grand Prix, 22-year-old Fernando Alonso became the youngest Grand Prix winner in Formula 1 history.
- **New 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 Points System:** Designed to keep championship fights closer, rewarding podium consistency and narrowing the gap between 1st (10) and 2nd (8).
- **HANS Device Mandated:** Head and Neck Support device became mandatory for all Formula 1 drivers starting at the Australian GP.
