## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The Brawn GP fairytale began! Brawn GP locked out the front row in their first-ever qualifying session and scored an astounding 1-2 finish on debut, Jenson Button leading Rubens Barrichello home. On lap 55, Sebastian Vettel and Robert Kubica collided while battling for 2nd, crashing into the wall and finishing behind the Safety Car.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The 'Liegate' scandal: Lewis Hamilton and McLaren sporting director Dave Ryan deliberately misled stewards regarding overtaking Jarno Trulli under the safety car, leading to Hamilton's disqualification and Ryan's dismissal.
