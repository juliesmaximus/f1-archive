## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Rubens Barrichello scored an emotional, long-awaited victory—his first win in five years (since 2004 Chinese GP). McLaren led with Hamilton until a botched pitstop (tyres not ready) delayed Hamilton by 5 seconds, allowing Barrichello to emerge ahead and take victory, dedicating the win to injured countryman Felipe Massa.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Luca Badoer substituted for Massa at Ferrari after 10 years away from racing, struggling at the back of the grid.
