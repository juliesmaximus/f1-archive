## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A weekend overshadowed by tragedy. In Q2, an 800-gram steel spring detached from Rubens Barrichello's Brawn GP car and bounced down the track, striking Felipe Massa's helmet at 160 mph. Massa was knocked unconscious and crashed heavily into the tyre wall at Turn 4, suffering a skull fracture; he was rushed to hospital for emergency life-saving surgery. In the race, Lewis Hamilton delivered McLaren's first victory of 2009, marking the first-ever win for a KERS-hybrid powered car in Formula 1 history.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Massa's life-threatening incident prompted the FIA to mandate stronger Zylon helmet visors.
