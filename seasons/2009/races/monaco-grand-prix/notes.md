## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Jenson Button controlled the race from pole position to lead a Brawn GP 1-2 ahead of Barrichello. After taking the chequered flag, Button accidentally parked his car in the pitlane instead of the pit straight; he memorably jogged the entire length of the pit straight in full racing overalls, helmet in hand, waving to ecstatic fans to reach the royal podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
