## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Mark Webber scored an emotional maiden Formula 1 victory in his 130th Grand Prix start. Starting from his first career pole position, Webber made contact with Rubens Barrichello off the line and received a drive-through penalty. Unfazed, Webber unleashed devastating speed, recovering from the penalty to lead a Red Bull 1-2 ahead of Sebastian Vettel, shouting 'Yes! Yes! Yes! You beauty!' on team radio.
