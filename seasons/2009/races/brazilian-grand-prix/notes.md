## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Jenson Button became World Champion! In a wild Saturday qualifying halted by rain for 2 hours and 41 minutes, Rubens Barrichello took pole while Button qualified a lowly 14th. In the race, Button produced the drive of a true champion, executing ruthless overtakes on Grosjean, Nakajima, and Kobayashi. Mark Webber dominated to win the race, while Barrichello suffered a puncture late on. Button finished 5th, clinching the 2009 FIA Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship! Brawn GP sealed the World Constructors' Championship, completing the most improbable fairytale in motorsport history.
