## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Astonishing weekend. Giancarlo Fisichella claimed a sensational, historic pole position for Force India. At the safety car restart on lap 5, Kimi Räikkönen used Ferrari's KERS boost button down the Kemmel Straight to blast past Fisichella into Les Combes. Fisichella shadowed Räikkönen for the entire race, finishing just 0.9 seconds behind to score Force India's first-ever podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First-lap pileup at Les Combes eliminated championship contenders Button and Hamilton alongside Grosjean and Alguersuari.
