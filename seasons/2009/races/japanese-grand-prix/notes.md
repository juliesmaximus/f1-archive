## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Formula 1 returned to Suzuka for the first time in three years. Sebastian Vettel delivered a dominant lights-to-flag victory from pole position, beating Jarno Trulli and Lewis Hamilton. The win kept Vettel's championship hopes alive heading into the final two rounds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Timo Glock suffered a severe crash at the final corner in qualifying, sustaining a cracked vertebra and missing the rest of the season.
