## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Brawn GP recorded another 1-2 finish. Rubens Barrichello led early on a three-stop strategy, but Button was switched to a two-stop strategy mid-race, jumping Barrichello to win. Barrichello expressed public frustration over team strategy.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Huge multi-car collision at Turn 2 on lap 1 eliminated Trulli, Sutil, and both Toro Rossos.
