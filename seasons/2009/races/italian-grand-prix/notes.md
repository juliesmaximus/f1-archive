## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Brawn GP pulled off a masterly tactical 1-2 finish. While pole-sitter Hamilton ran a two-stop strategy, Barrichello and Button executed an immaculate one-stop strategy. Barrichello took victory, and on the final lap, Hamilton crashed violently at the second Lesmo corner while hunting Button, handing 2nd place to Button.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
