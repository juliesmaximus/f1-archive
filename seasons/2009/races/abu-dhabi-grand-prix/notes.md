## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Inaugural Grand Prix at the spectacular Yas Marina Circuit, the first twilight-to-night race in Formula 1 history. Lewis Hamilton led from pole until a brake failure forced his retirement on lap 20. Sebastian Vettel dominated the race to lead a Red Bull 1-2 ahead of Mark Webber, securing runner-up spot in the World Drivers' Championship. Newly crowned champion Jenson Button finished 3rd.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
