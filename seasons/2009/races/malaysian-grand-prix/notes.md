## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A torrential monsoon struck on lap 31, drowning the circuit in standing water and forcing a red flag. As darkness fell over Sepang, the race was declared official after 31 of 56 laps. Jenson Button was awarded the victory with half-points awarded (only the 5th half-points race in F1 history). Kimi Räikkönen famously ate an ice cream in the Ferrari hospitality while the race was halted.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First half-points race in Formula 1 since the 1991 Australian Grand Prix.
