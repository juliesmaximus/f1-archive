## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Lewis Hamilton (McLaren-Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Lewis Hamilton (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:47.891
- **Fastest Lap:** Fernando Alonso (Renault) - 1:48.240 (lap 53)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton controlled the night race from pole position to score his second victory of 2009. Sebastian Vettel pressured Hamilton until he received a drive-through penalty for speeding in the pitlane, dropping to 4th. Fernando Alonso finished 3rd on the podium for Renault just days after the Crashgate scandal erupted into the public domain.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Flavio Briatore and Pat Symonds were banned from Formula 1 by the FIA World Motor Sport Council in the days preceding the Grand Prix.
