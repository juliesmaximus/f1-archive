## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel took pole position but ran wide at Turn 9 on the opening lap, handing the lead to Jenson Button. Button checked out, winning by 6.7 seconds over Mark Webber to secure his 6th victory from the first 7 races of the season.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Button became the first driver to win 6 of the opening 7 rounds since Michael Schumacher in 2004.
