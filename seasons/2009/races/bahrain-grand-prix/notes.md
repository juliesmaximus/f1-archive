## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Toyota locked out the front row with Trulli and Glock, but Jenson Button produced an inspired first lap, pulling off a daring pass on Lewis Hamilton into Turn 4. Button jumped the Toyotas during the first pitstop window to take his third win in four races.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Toyota's decision to run harder prime tyres in the middle stint cost them a potential maiden victory.
