## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Red Bull Racing's maiden Formula 1 victory. In torrential rain, 21-year-old Sebastian Vettel delivered a wet-weather clinic, leading teammate Mark Webber in an astonishing 1-2 finish for Red Bull. Brawn GP's Button and Barrichello finished 3rd and 4th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Just days earlier, the FIA Court of Appeal declared the 'Double Diffuser' legal, vindicating Brawn, Toyota, and Williams.
