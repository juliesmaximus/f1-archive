## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Aerodynamic Overhaul:** Wide front wing (1,800 mm), narrow high rear wing (750 mm / 950 mm), winglets and flick-ups banned.
- **Slick Tyres Return:** Bridgestone slick tyres reintroduced.
- **KERS Permitted:** 60 kW (80 bhp) kinetic energy recovery systems introduced.
- **Final Refuelling Season:** Last season allowing in-race refuelling.
