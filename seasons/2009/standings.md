## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Brawn GP Fairytale Double Championship:** Rising from the ashes of Honda's withdrawal, Brawn GP won both the World Drivers' (Jenson Button) and Constructors' Championships in their only season.
- **The Double Diffuser Revolution:** Brawn GP, Toyota, and Williams pioneered the double diffuser aerodynamic concept, legally upheld by the FIA.
- **Return of Slick Tyres:** Slick racing slicks returned to Formula 1 after an 11-year absence (1998–2008 grooved era).
- **Emergence of Red Bull Racing:** Adrian Newey's RB5 and Sebastian Vettel scored Red Bull's maiden pole and victory in China, finishing runners-up.
- **Inaugural Abu Dhabi Twilight Race:** Yas Marina Circuit debuted with an innovative day-into-night format.
