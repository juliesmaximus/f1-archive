## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Brawn GP Fairytale Double Championship:** Rising from the ashes of Honda's sudden withdrawal in December 2008, Ross Brawn led the newly formed Brawn GP team to win both the World Drivers' Championship (Jenson Button) and Constructors' Championship in their sole season of existence.
- **The Double Diffuser Revolution:** Brawn GP, Toyota, and Williams exploited a loophole in the new aerodynamic regulations with a double-deck diffuser, which was upheld as fully legal by the FIA International Court of Appeal on April 15, 2009.
- **Return of Slick Tyres:** Smooth slick tyres returned to Formula 1 after an 11-year absence, significantly increasing mechanical grip.
- **Emergence of Red Bull Racing:** Designed by Adrian Newey, the Red Bull RB5 claimed the team's first-ever pole and victory at the Chinese Grand Prix (Sebastian Vettel), establishing Red Bull as a dominant championship contender.
- **Inaugural Abu Dhabi Twilight Grand Prix:** Formula 1 staged its first-ever day-into-night twilight Grand Prix at the state-of-the-art Yas Marina Circuit.
