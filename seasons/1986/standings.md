# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1986 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

The 1986 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship was decided by the best 11 results out of 16 rounds. Points were awarded to the top six finishers on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis.

Alain Prost captured his second consecutive World Drivers' Championship driving the McLaren MP4/2C TAG-Porsche, scoring 72 net points (74 gross) with 4 victories and 11 podiums, becoming the first back-to-back champion since Jack Brabham in 1960. Nigel Mansell (Williams-Honda) finished runner-up with 70 net points (72 gross, 5 victories) after his heart-breaking tire blowout in Adelaide. Nelson Piquet (Williams-Honda) finished 3rd with 69 net points (4 victories).
