# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1986 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Alain Prost captured his second consecutive FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the McLaren MP4/2C TAG-Porsche, scoring 72 net points (74 gross) with 4 victories, becoming the first back-to-back champion since Jack Brabham in 1960. Nigel Mansell (Williams-Honda) finished runner-up with 70 net points (72 gross).

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Williams Grand Prix Engineering captured their third World Constructors' Championship with a dominant 141 points, winning 9 Grands Prix with Nigel Mansell and Nelson Piquet in the Williams FW11 Honda Turbo. McLaren International finished runner-up with 96 points.
