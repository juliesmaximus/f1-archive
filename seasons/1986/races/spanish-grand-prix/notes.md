## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE 0.014-SECOND JEREZ FINISH: Ayrton Senna claimed pole for Lotus. In the closing stages, Senna led on badly worn tires. Nigel Mansell in the Williams-Honda pitted for fresh tires on lap 63 and tore after Senna, closing a 20-second deficit at nearly 4 seconds per lap! On the 72nd and final lap, Mansell lunged inside at the final corner. They drag-raced side-by-side down the pit straight, separated by inches: Senna took victory by **0.014 seconds**—the third-closest finish in Formula One history!
