## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE 0.014-SECOND JEREZ FINISH: Ayrton Senna led from pole on severely degraded Goodyear tires. Nigel Mansell in the Williams-Honda pitted for fresh tires on lap 63 and tore after Senna, erasing a 20-second deficit at nearly 4 seconds per lap. On the 72nd and final lap, Mansell made a desperate dive inside at the final hairpin. Both cars drag-raced side-by-side down the main straight, with Senna crossing the line just 0.014 seconds ahead—the 3rd closest finish in Formula One history!
