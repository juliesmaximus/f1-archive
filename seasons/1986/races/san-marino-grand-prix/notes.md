## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST'S FUEL WEAVE ON THE BRINK: Ayrton Senna claimed pole for Lotus. In the race, the 195-litre fuel limit pushed turbo engines to starvation. Nelson Piquet led until lap 28 when Alain Prost in the McLaren MP4/2C took over. On the final lap, Prost's fuel readout read ZERO approaching the final chicane (Variante Bassa); the engine cut out! Prost violently swerved the McLaren left and right to slosh the final thimbleful of fuel into the fuel collector, bump-starting the engine and coasting across the finish line to take victory by 7.64 seconds over Nelson Piquet!
