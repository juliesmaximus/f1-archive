## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
DRAMATIC FUEL LOTTERY: Keke Rosberg ran out of fuel while holding 2nd on lap 58. Alain Prost took the lead, but his McLaren began spluttering on the final approach to the finish line on lap 60. Prost frantically wiggled the steering wheel back and forth to slosh the final droplets of fuel into the fuel pickups, coasting across the line to take victory before the car stopped completely! Nelson Piquet finished 2nd, while Gerhard Berger took 3rd for Benetton's and his own maiden podium. Michele Alboreto (Ferrari) completed 56 laps before retiring with turbo failure; Formula1.com classified him as NC (DNF), whereas Jolpica listed him in 10th position.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Prost's fuel-sloshing victory showcased supreme tactical energy conservation during the peak turbo fuel-restriction era.
