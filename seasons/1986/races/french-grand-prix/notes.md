- **Specific Regulations:** First race using the shortened 3.813 km Paul Ricard circuit layout, bypassing the ultra-high-speed Courbe de Signes section following Elio de Angelis' fatal crash.

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
Ayrton Senna claimed pole, but spun into the gravel on lap 4 after encountering oil from Andrea de Cesaris' blown engine. Nigel Mansell charged to the front, executing a 2-stop tire strategy to defeat Alain Prost by 17.1 seconds on French home turf. Nelson Piquet completed the podium in 3rd.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mansell's commanding victory propelled him to the lead of the World Championship standings.
