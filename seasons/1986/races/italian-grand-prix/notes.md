## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST DISQUALIFIED & PIQUET WINS: Alain Prost's race car suffered ignition failure before the green flag; he sprinted to the pit lane and took the start in the spare car. As switching cars after the formation lap began was illegal under sporting regulations, Prost was shown the black flag and disqualified on lap 27. On track, Nelson Piquet took victory ahead of teammate Nigel Mansell to seal a Williams-Honda 1-2 finish, with Stefan Johansson taking 3rd for Ferrari. Alex Caffi completed 45 laps (+6 laps) and was classified 11th on Formula1.com (Jolpica recorded him as NC/R under the 90% threshold).
