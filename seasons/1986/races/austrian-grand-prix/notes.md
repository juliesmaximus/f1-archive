## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
Teo Fabi scored Benetton's first-ever pole position with an astonishing qualifying lap powered by the 1,400 hp BMW turbo engine. In the race, both Benettons suffered mechanical setbacks. Alain Prost took control and led comfortably to score his 3rd win of the season, finishing a full lap ahead of Ferrari teammates Michele Alboreto and Stefan Johansson. Christian Danner scored his first career World Championship point in 6th for Arrows.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Prost revived his title defense with a dominant victory while both Williams cars suffered mechanical retirements.
