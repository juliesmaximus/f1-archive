## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST MANUALLY PUSHING HIS CAR: Keke Rosberg claimed pole and led until his McLaren ran dry on the final lap. Alain Prost was running 3rd on the final lap when his McLaren also ran out of fuel exiting the Motodrom. In an unforgettable display of determination, Prost jumped out of his car and desperately pushed it uphill toward the finish line! He collapsed from exhaustion just short of the line as cars rushed past, but was classified 6th. Nelson Piquet claimed victory ahead of Ayrton Senna and Nigel Mansell.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The image of Alain Prost physically pushing his stalled McLaren toward the line became one of Formula One's enduring icons of driver grit.
