## Technical & Sporting Regulations
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 classified finishers.
- **Championship Count Rule:** Best 11 results counted towards the Drivers' Championship; all results counted for the Constructors' Championship.
- **Specific Regulations:** Starting grid strictly restricted to 20 starters due to Monaco circuit constraints.

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
Alain Prost executed a flawless weekend, securing pole position, leading 71 of 78 laps, setting fastest lap, and taking his 3rd career Monaco victory. Teammate Keke Rosberg finished 2nd, delivering a dominant McLaren-TAG 1-2 finish. Ayrton Senna rounded out the podium in 3rd. Tragically, four days after the race on May 15, Elio de Angelis suffered a fatal crash during private testing at Circuit Paul Ricard when the rear wing of his Brabham BT55 detached at high speed.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Prost became only the second driver after Graham Hill to win three consecutive Monaco Grands Prix (1984, 1985, 1986).
