## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
Nigel Mansell produced a peerless drive, overtaking pole-sitter Ayrton Senna on lap 31 and dominating the remainder of the race to win by 18.7 seconds. Senna ran out of fuel on the final lap while holding 2nd place, dropping to 4th at the line. Alain Prost capitalized to finish 2nd and Nelson Piquet 3rd. Mansell's 5th win secured the Constructors' Championship for Williams-Honda.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
