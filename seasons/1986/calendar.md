# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1986 - Calendar

The 1986 Formula One season was the 37th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 championship rounds between March 23 and October 26, 1986.

The 1986 season stands as one of the most legendary, dramatic, and horsepower-crazed championships in Formula One history:
- **The Peak of Unrestricted Turbo Horsepower:** Qualifying turbo engines reached their zenith: 1.5-litre turbocharged engines from BMW, Renault, Honda, and TAG-Porsche generated upwards of **1,400+ brake horsepower** at 5.5 bar boost. At Monza, Gerhard Berger's Benetton-BMW clocked an all-time turbo speed trap record of **351.22 km/h (218.2 mph)**.
- **The Adelaide Three-Way Showdown & Alain Prost's Miracle:** Nigel Mansell entered the Adelaide season finale needing only 3rd place to become World Champion. On lap 64 at 180 mph down Brabham Straight, Mansell's rear tire violently exploded in a shower of sparks! Williams pitted teammate Nelson Piquet for safety, allowing "The Professor" Alain Prost to nurse his fuel in the McLaren MP4/2C TAG-Porsche to win the race and claim his **second consecutive World Drivers' Championship** (the first back-to-back champion since Jack Brabham in 1960). Williams-Honda captured the **World Constructors' Championship**.
- **The 0.014-Second Jerez Drag Race:** At the inaugural Spanish Grand Prix at Jerez, Ayrton Senna (Lotus-Renault) held off a storming Nigel Mansell (Williams-Honda) by **0.014 seconds** at the line in the third-closest finish in F1 history.
- **First Race Behind the Iron Curtain:** Formula One made history at the inaugural Hungarian Grand Prix at the Hungaroring before 200,000 spectators, immortalized by Nelson Piquet's full-lock drifting pass around Ayrton Senna.
- **Benetton & Gerhard Berger's Maiden Win:** Gerhard Berger won the Mexican Grand Prix at high altitude without stopping for tires on Pirelli rubber, delivering Benetton's and Berger's historic maiden Grand Prix victories.
