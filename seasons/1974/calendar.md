# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1974 - Calendar

The 1974 Formula One season was the 25th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 15 championship rounds between January 13 and October 6, 1974.

The 1974 season was one of the most closely contested championship battles in Grand Prix history, coming down to a dramatic finale at Watkins Glen with Brazilian **Emerson Fittipaldi** and Ferrari's **Clay Regazzoni** tied on 52 points. Driving the Gordon Coppuck-designed **McLaren M23** Ford Cosworth DFV, Fittipaldi finished 4th in the finale to clinch his second World Drivers' Championship, delivering **McLaren's first-ever International Cup for F1 Manufacturers**.

The season also marked the emergence of future triple World Champion **Niki Lauda**, who scored his maiden Grand Prix victories for Ferrari in Spain and the Netherlands, and South African **Jody Scheckter**, who won his maiden races for Tyrrell in Sweden and Great Britain.
