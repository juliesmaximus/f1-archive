### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
REUTEMANN WINS FOR BRABHAM: Niki Lauda took pole position to the cheers of 95,000 Austrian fans and led until an engine valve failure on lap 17 forced his retirement. Carlos Reutemann in the Gordon Murray Brabham BT44 seized the lead and pulled away to win by 42.92 seconds over Denny Hulme (McLaren) and James Hunt (Hesketh 308). Regazzoni finished 5th, expanding his championship lead to 5 points over Scheckter and 9 over Fittipaldi.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Carlos Reutemann's second career Grand Prix victory. Gordon Murray's Brabham design established itself as a front-runner.
