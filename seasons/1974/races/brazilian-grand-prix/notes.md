## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren) — 1:24:37.060  
- **Pole Position:** Emerson Fittipaldi  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Humid, sunny start, followed by an apocalyptic tropical rainstorm on lap 31  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 110,000 ecstatic Brazilian fans  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Curva do Sol grandstands, Arquibancadas da Reta Oposta, and hillside slopes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI'S RAIN TRIUMPH AT HOME: Emerson Fittipaldi qualified on pole in the McLaren M23. Local hero Carlos Pace led early in the Surtees before tyre trouble. Fittipaldi took the lead on lap 16. On lap 31, a ferocious tropical thunderstorm flooded the track, with zero visibility and cars aquaplaning uncontrollably. The race was red-flagged and declared after 32 laps. Fittipaldi won by 13.5 seconds over Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari) and Jacky Ickx (Lotus). Full points awarded.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi scored back-to-back Brazilian Grand Prix home victories (1973, 1974), taking the championship lead.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Race stopped after 32 of 40 scheduled laps (254.72 km) due to torrential rain.
