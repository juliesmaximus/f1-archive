## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Carlos Reutemann (Brabham) — 1:42:40.960  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda (1:16.580)
- **Fastest Lap:** Carlos Reutemann (Brabham-Ford) — 1:18.160 (Lap 58)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
CARLOS REUTEMANN'S MAIDEN WIN: The race was held under deep grief following the fatal accident of American driver Peter Revson in pre-race testing on March 22. Niki Lauda took pole for Ferrari. Carlos Reutemann drove a brilliant race in Gordon Murray's Brabham BT44, taking the lead on lap 10 and pulling away to score his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory by 0.39s over Jean-Pierre Beltoise (BRM) and Mike Hailwood (McLaren).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First Formula One World Championship victory for Carlos Reutemann and the first win for Gordon Murray's Brabham BT44 design.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Shadow team withdrew from the race in tribute to Peter Revson.
