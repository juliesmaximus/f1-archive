### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
REGAZZONI WINS & HAILWOOD'S CAREER ENDS: Clay Regazzoni took pole in the Ferrari 312B3-74 and led from green flag to chequered flag in a dominant display, winning by 50.7 seconds ahead of Jody Scheckter (Tyrrell) and Carlos Reutemann (Brabham). Niki Lauda crashed out on lap 1 while tangling with Scheckter. Tragic accident on lap 13: motorcycle legend Mike Hailwood crashed his McLaren M23 heavily at Pflanzgarten, suffering severe compound fractures to both legs that ended his illustrious international driving career.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Clay Regazzoni took sole possession of the World Drivers' Championship lead with 44 points.
