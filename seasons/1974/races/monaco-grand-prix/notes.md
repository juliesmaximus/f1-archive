## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Ronnie Peterson (Team Lotus) — 1:58:03.700  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 45,000 spectators lining harbour balconies and terraces  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Swimming Pool grandstands, Quai Albert 1er, and Casino square terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAP 1 CHAOS & PETERSON WINS: Niki Lauda took pole for Ferrari. On lap 1, an eight-car collision at Mirabeau and the chicane eliminated Hulme, Pace, Beltoise, Merzario, Brambilla, and Carlos Reutemann! Lauda led until an engine failure stopped him on lap 32. Ronnie Peterson drove a ferocious, sideways masterclass in the Lotus 72E to win by 28.8s ahead of Jody Scheckter (Tyrrell) and Jean-Pierre Jarier (Shadow). Only 9 of 25 starters finished.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Ronnie Peterson scored his maiden Monaco Grand Prix victory.
