## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Ronnie Peterson (Team Lotus) — 1:21:55.020  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Hot, sunny, dry; 29°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 75,000 spectators packing the hillside natural bowl  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Start-finish grandstands and hillside natural terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL GP AT DIJON-PRENOIS: The French GP moved to the fast, undulating 3.28 km Dijon-Prenois circuit. Niki Lauda took pole position and led early before handling vibrations set in. Ronnie Peterson in the Lotus 72E launched an inspired attack, passing Lauda on lap 17 and pulling away to take victory by 20.3s over Lauda and Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari 2-3). Jody Scheckter was 4th for Tyrrell.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Ronnie Peterson scored Lotus's 2nd win of 1974, keeping four drivers locked in the championship fight.
- **Regulatory Framework:** First Formula One Grand Prix held at Dijon-Prenois.
