### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
SCHECKTER WINS AMIDST PIT LANE DRAMA: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312B3-74 and led comfortably for 69 laps. On lap 70, a rear tyre puncture forced Lauda to pit. Jody Scheckter swept past in the Tyrrell 007 to take victory by 0.3s over Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren). Post-race chaos: Lauda exited the pits just as the chequered flag fell but was blocked by track marshals who had closed the pit exit prematurely. Following intense Ferrari protests, the RAC stewards awarded Lauda 5th place on appeal.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jody Scheckter moved to 35 points, tied with Fittipaldi and just three points behind Regazzoni and Lauda (38 pts each) in a four-way title fight.
