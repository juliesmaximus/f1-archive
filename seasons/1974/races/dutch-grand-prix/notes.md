### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FERRARI 1-2 DOMINATION: Niki Lauda took pole position in the Ferrari 312B3-74 and led every single one of the 75 laps, pulling away to win by 8.25s over teammate Clay Regazzoni for an emphatic Ferrari 1-2 finish! Emerson Fittipaldi was 3rd for McLaren, and Mike Hailwood was 4th. Lauda's 2nd victory elevated him to the top of the World Championship standings tied with Regazzoni on 38 points.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Scuderia Ferrari assumed control of both the World Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
