- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Crisp, sunny, cool autumn day; 13°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Boot, and Seneca spectator knolls  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI & McLAREN CLINCH DUAL TITLES AMIDST TRAGEDY: Carlos Reutemann took pole in the Brabham BT44. Title contender Clay Regazzoni suffered severe damper failure, making multiple pitstops and dropping out of contention to finish 11th. Emerson Fittipaldi drove a calculated, sensible race to finish 4th, CLINCHING HIS SECOND WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP (55 net points) and delivering McLAREN'S FIRST-EVER CONSTRUCTORS' CHAMPIONSHIP! Carlos Reutemann won the race by 10.73s over teammate Carlos Pace in a Brabham 1-2 sweep, with James Hunt 3rd. Tragically, Austrian rookie Helmuth Koinigg was killed on lap 10 when a rear suspension failure sent his Surtees into the outer barrier, decapitating him on impact.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi became a double World Champion at age 27. McLaren celebrated their maiden International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Record $300,000 total purse. Denny Hulme retired from Formula One after this race.
