**Date:** 1974-09-08

**Track Length:** 5.78 km (3.591 miles)

## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Ronnie Peterson (Team Lotus) — 1:22:56.600  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda (1:33.160)
- **Fastest Lap:** Carlos Pace (Brabham-Ford) — 1:34.200 (Lap 46)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry late summer weather; 27°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Tribuna Centrale, Vedano, Ascari, and Parabolica spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FERRARI ENGINE HEARTBREAK & PETERSON WINS: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312B3-74 and led comfortably until his Flat-12 engine suffered a broken water pipe on lap 32. Teammate Clay Regazzoni took over the lead until his engine blew on lap 40 to the despair of 130,000 tifosi! Ronnie Peterson in the Lotus 72E swept through to win by 0.8 seconds over a charging Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren). Jody Scheckter finished 3rd in the Tyrrell.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Fittipaldi's 2nd place moved him within three points of championship leader Regazzoni (46 pts vs. 43 pts), with Scheckter close behind on 41 pts.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 52 laps (300.56 km).
