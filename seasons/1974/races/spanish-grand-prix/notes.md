## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 2:00:29.560  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold rain at the start, drying track in the second half; 16°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 60,000 spectators across the Jarama hillsides  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstands, Pegaso curve terraces, and Farina hillsides  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
NIKI LAUDA'S MAIDEN GRAND PRIX VICTORY: Niki Lauda took pole position in the Ferrari 312B3-74. In damp conditions, Ronnie Peterson led in the Lotus 76 before an engine failure forced him out. As the track dried, Lauda and teammate Clay Regazzoni took complete command on slicks, delivering a crushing 1-2 finish for Scuderia Ferrari! Lauda won by 35.6 seconds over Regazzoni to score his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory! Emerson Fittipaldi finished 3rd in the McLaren.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first career Grand Prix victory for Austrian legend Niki Lauda, launching Ferrari's championship challenge.
