# XXXII Grand Prix de Belgique - Race Dossier & Verification Notes

**Round:** 5 of 15  
**Date:** 1974-05-12  
**Circuit:** Complexe Européen de Nivelles-Baulers  
**Location:** Nivelles, Walloon Brabant, Belgium (30°37'11"N, 4°19'38"E)  
**Track Length:** 3.724 km (2.314 miles)  
**Altitude:** 135 m  
**Scheduled Distance:** 85 laps (316.540 km)  
**Official Race Title:** XXXII Grand Prix de Belgique  

---

## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren) — 1:44:20.570  
- **Pole Position:** Clay Regazzoni (1:09.820)
- **Fastest Lap:** Denny Hulme (McLaren-Ford) — 1:11.310 (Lap 37)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 24°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent start-finish grandstand and wide grass spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI EDGES LAUDA BY 0.35s: Clay Regazzoni took pole for Ferrari. Emerson Fittipaldi launched from 4th on the grid in the McLaren M23 to grab the lead on lap 39. Niki Lauda hunted him down in a thrilling pursuit, closing to the gearbox of Fittipaldi's McLaren. Fittipaldi held on by just 0.35 seconds at the chequered flag! Jody Scheckter finished 3rd in the Tyrrell 007, and Clay Regazzoni was 4th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Fittipaldi retook the lead of the World Drivers' Championship with 22 points.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Final Formula One Grand Prix held at the Nivelles-Baulers circuit.
