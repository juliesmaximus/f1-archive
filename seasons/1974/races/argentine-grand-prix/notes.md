## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Denny Hulme (McLaren) — 1:41:02.010  
- **Pole Position:** Ronnie Peterson (1:50.780)
- **Fastest Lap:** Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari) — 1:52.100 (Lap 38)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Scorching summer heat; 32°C ambient, track temperature 48°C  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent concrete stadium grandstands along the Curva del Ciervo and pit straight  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
REUTEMANN RUNS DRY & HULME WINS: Ronnie Peterson took pole for Lotus. Local hero Carlos Reutemann in the Gordon Murray-designed Brabham BT44 led from lap 3 to lap 52 with a 30-second lead, on the verge of an ecstatic home victory. Incredibly, on the penultimate lap, his Brabham ran dry of fuel and sputtered to a halt! Denny Hulme swept past in the McLaren M23 to take his 8th and final career Grand Prix victory by 9.2s over Niki Lauda (scoring 2nd on his Ferrari debut) and Clay Regazzoni.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme's final Formula One victory. Niki Lauda announced his arrival as a front-runner for Scuderia Ferrari.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 7 of first 8 races and best 6 of last 7 races counted.
