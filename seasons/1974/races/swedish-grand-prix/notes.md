### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR JODY SCHECKTER & TYRRELL 1-2: Jody Scheckter qualified on pole position in the Derek Gardner-designed Tyrrell 007. In the race, Scheckter and French teammate Patrick Depailler dominated from the start, delivering a flawless 1-2 sweep for the Tyrrell Racing Organisation! Scheckter won by 0.38 seconds over Depailler to score his MAIDEN Formula One victory! James Hunt finished 3rd in the Hesketh 308.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jody Scheckter became the first South African driver to win a World Championship Formula One Grand Prix, entering championship contention.
