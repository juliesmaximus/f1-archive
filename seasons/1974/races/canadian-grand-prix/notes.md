### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI TIES REGAZZONI FOR CHAMPIONSHIP LEAD: Emerson Fittipaldi took pole in the McLaren M23. Niki Lauda seized the lead on lap 1 and led for 67 laps until running over debris at Turn 3, crashing into the barrier. Fittipaldi took over the lead, holding off Clay Regazzoni's Ferrari by 13.03 seconds to take a crucial victory! Ronnie Peterson took 3rd. With this victory, Emerson Fittipaldi and Clay Regazzoni entered the final race tied on exactly 52 POINTS EACH!
