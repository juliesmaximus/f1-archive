# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1974 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and the best **6** results from the final 7 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Brazilian Emerson Fittipaldi won his second World Drivers' Championship with 55 net points (55 gross), defeating Ferrari's Clay Regazzoni (52 net points / 52 gross) in the Watkins Glen season finale.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and best **6** results from the final 7 races counted towards the championship.

McLaren-Ford won their maiden International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 73 net points (75 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 7 results from rounds 1–8 and best 6 results from rounds 9–15 counted towards both championships.
  - McLaren-Ford scored 75 gross points, dropping 2 points (5th place finish at Monaco / 6th in Belgium) to total 73 net points.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Emerson Fittipaldi won his second World Drivers' Championship, and McLaren won their first-ever International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
  - Maiden Grand Prix victories for Niki Lauda (Jarama, Zandvoort), Carlos Reutemann (Kyalami, Österreichring, Watkins Glen), and Jody Scheckter (Anderstorp, Brands Hatch).
  - Denny Hulme scored his final career victory in Argentina and retired from Formula One at the end of the season.
  - Tragic losses of Peter Revson during Kyalami testing prior to Round 3, and Helmuth Koinigg in the Watkins Glen season finale.
