- **Round**: 4 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Friday, 30 May 1958
- **Location**: Indianapolis Motor Speedway, Speedway, Indiana, USA
- **Circuit Length**: 4.023 km (2.500 miles)
- **Distance**: 200 laps (804.672 km / 500.000 miles)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Tragic Lap 1 Multi-Car Pile-Up**: Entering Turn 3 on the opening lap, front-row starters Dick Rathmann and Ed Elisian touched wheels and spun into the outside wall. A massive 15-car pile-up ensued. 1957 pole-sitter Pat O'Connor was launched over another car and flipped upside down; his fuel tank ruptured, and O'Connor died from fatal head and burn injuries. The race was yellow-flagged for 30 minutes.
- **Jimmy Bryan's Triumph**: Arizona driver Jimmy Bryan drove a determined, patient race with an oil-streaked car to take victory in George Salih's laydown Epperly roadster, following up Sam Hanks' 1957 win in the same chassis.
- **Exclusion from Manufacturers' Cup**: Indianapolis 500 points counted towards the World Championship of Drivers, but was explicitly excluded from the newly inaugurated International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, overcast morning clearing to sunny conditions; completely dry track.
