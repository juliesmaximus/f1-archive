## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Catastrophic 15-Car Opening Lap Crash: On lap 1 entering Turn 3, front-row starters Dick Rathmann and Ed Elisian touched wheels, triggering a massive 15-car pileup. Popular pole contender Pat O'Connor was launched into the air, landed upside down, and was killed instantly when his fuel tank ruptured into flames.
- Yellow Flag Confusion: The race was slowed under yellow flag conditions for 20 laps while wreckage was cleared.
- Jimmy Bryan Victory: Veteran Jimmy Bryan in the Belond AP Special Epperly-Offenhauser took victory by 27.6 seconds over George Amick.

## Records Set or Equalled
