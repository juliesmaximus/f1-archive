## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Peter Collins Fatal Crash: On lap 11, pursuing Tony Brooks' Vanwall for the lead at the uphill Pflanzgarten section, Peter Collins ran slightly wide onto the grassy verge. His Ferrari caught a drainage ditch, flipped into the air, and somersaulted into the birch trees. Collins was thrown from the cockpit and suffered critical head injuries, dying that evening in a Bonn hospital aged 26.
- Devastation for Mike Hawthorn: Following in 3rd place, Mike Hawthorn witnessed his best friend's horrific crash. Hawthorn retired his car two laps later with clutch failure and was completely inconsolable.
- Tony Brooks Masterclass Victory: Tony Brooks drove a brilliant, tenacious race to win for Vanwall by over three minutes ahead of the Cooper-Climax rear-engined cars of Roy Salvadori and Maurice Trintignant.

## Records Set or Equalled
