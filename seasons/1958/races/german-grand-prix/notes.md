# 1958 German Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XX Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Round**: 8 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 3 August 1958
- **Location**: Nürburgring Nordschleife, Nürburg, Germany
- **Circuit Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 miles)
- **Distance**: 15 laps (342.150 km / 212.602 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 9:14.000
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 9:09.200 (Lap 9) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Tony Brooks (Vanwall) - 2:21:15.000 (145.338 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Tony Brooks (Vanwall) - 15 laps
  2. Roy Salvadori (Cooper-Climax) - 15 laps (+3:29.700)
  3. Maurice Trintignant (Cooper-Climax) - 15 laps (+5:11.200)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Tragic Death of Peter Collins**: On lap 11, while pursuing Tony Brooks for the lead, 26-year-old Peter Collins lost control of his Ferrari at the tricky downhill Pflanzgarten section. The car flew over the trackside embankment and struck a tree; Collins suffered massive head trauma and died in hospital later that evening. The tragedy devastated the Ferrari team and his close friend Mike Hawthorn, who retired from the race with a clutch failure shortly afterwards.
- **Tony Brooks' Victory**: Tony Brooks drove a brilliant, steady race to take his second victory of the season in the Vanwall.
- **Cooper Double Podium**: Privateers Roy Salvadori and Maurice Trintignant brought their Cooper-Climax cars home in 2nd and 3rd.
- **F2 Field Combined**: To boost grid size, Formula 2 cars were entered alongside the F1 field; Bruce McLaren finished 5th overall (1st in F2 class), driving a Cooper T45.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Overcast, cool, damp morning drying to dry track in the afternoon.
