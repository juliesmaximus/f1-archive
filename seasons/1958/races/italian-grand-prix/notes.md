# 1958 Italian Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXIX Gran Premio d'Italia
- **Round**: 10 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 7 September 1958
- **Location**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (5.750 km Road Course), Monza, Italy
- **Circuit Length**: 5.750 km (3.573 miles)
- **Distance**: 70 laps (402.500 km / 250.102 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 1:40.500
- **Fastest Lap**: Phil Hill (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 1:42.900 (Lap 65) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Tony Brooks (Vanwall) - 2:03:47.800 (195.078 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Tony Brooks (Vanwall) - 70 laps
  2. Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari) - 70 laps (+24.200s)
  3. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 70 laps (+28.300s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Tony Brooks Spoils Ferrari's Party**: On Ferrari's home soil, Tony Brooks delivered a masterclass victory for Vanwall, hunting down Mike Hawthorn in the closing laps and taking the win by 24.2 seconds.
- **Phil Hill's Formula 1 Breakthrough**: Making his Formula 1 works debut for Ferrari, American sports car star Phil Hill led multiple laps, set the fastest lap of the race (1:42.900), and finished 3rd, scoring his maiden World Championship podium.
- **Moss's Gearbox Heartbreak**: Stirling Moss led early from pole before his Vanwall's gearbox failed on lap 17, severely damaging his championship chances.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, sunny Italian late-summer afternoon; fast and dry track throughout.
