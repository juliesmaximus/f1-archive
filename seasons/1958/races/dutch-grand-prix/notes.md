# 1958 Dutch Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: VII Grote Prijs van Nederland
- **Round**: 3 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Monday, 26 May 1958
- **Location**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort, Netherlands
- **Circuit Length**: 4.193 km (2.605 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (314.475 km / 195.405 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stuart Lewis-Evans (Vanwall) - 1:37.100
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 1:37.600 (Lap 64) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 2:04:49.200 (151.166 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 75 laps
  2. Harry Schell (BRM) - 75 laps (+47.900s)
  3. Jean Behra (BRM) - 75 laps (+1:42.300)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Vanwall & Moss Mastery**: Stirling Moss led from the second lap to the finish, scoring Vanwall's first win of the season and setting the fastest lap of the race (1:37.600).
- **BRM Double Podium**: British Racing Motors (BRM) enjoyed one of their best early World Championship performances, with Harry Schell finishing 2nd and Jean Behra 3rd.
- **All-British Car Top Four**: For the first time in World Championship history, British-built cars swept the top four places (Vanwall 1st, BRM 2nd and 3rd, Cooper-Climax 4th with Roy Salvadori).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Pleasant, sunny coastal weather with dry sand-swept asphalt.
