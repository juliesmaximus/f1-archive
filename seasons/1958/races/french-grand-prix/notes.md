# 1958 French Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: 44e Grand Prix de l'ACF
- **Round**: 6 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 6 July 1958
- **Location**: Reims-Gueux, Reims, France
- **Circuit Length**: 8.302 km (5.159 miles)
- **Distance**: 50 laps (415.100 km / 257.931 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 2:21.700
- **Fastest Lap**: Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 2:24.900 (Lap 45) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 2:03:21.300 (201.905 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari) - 50 laps
  2. Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 50 laps (+24.600s)
  3. Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari) - 50 laps (+59.700s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Hawthorn's Dominant Grand Slam**: Mike Hawthorn claimed pole, fastest lap, and led every lap to secure his third career Grand Prix victory (and his only win of the 1958 championship season).
- **Tragic Death of Luigi Musso**: On lap 10, while pushing hard in 2nd place chasing teammate Hawthorn, Italian driver Luigi Musso ran wide at the fast, curving Muizon corner, somersaulted into a ditch, and was thrown from his Ferrari. He suffered fatal head injuries and died in hospital later that afternoon.
- **Fangio's Farewell Grand Prix**: Five-time World Champion Juan Manuel Fangio drove his final World Championship race in a private Maserati 250F, finishing 4th (+2:30.600). When Hawthorn approached to lap Fangio on the final lap, Hawthorn respectfully braked and refused to lap the maestro, allowing Fangio to cross the line on the lead lap. Fangio announced his retirement from motor racing immediately afterwards.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Oppressively hot summer afternoon with temperatures exceeding 35°C; completely dry track.
