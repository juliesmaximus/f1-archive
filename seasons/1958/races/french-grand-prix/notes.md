## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Luigi Musso Fatal Crash: On lap 10, pursuing team leader Mike Hawthorn for the lead at over 150 mph through the dangerous sweeping Gueux curve, Italian star Luigi Musso lost control of his Ferrari Dino 246, vaulted the ditch, and struck the bank. Musso was thrown from the car and suffered fatal head injuries, dying hours later in hospital aged 33.
- Hawthorn Dominant Win: Shaken by the loss of his teammate, Hawthorn drove to his sole victory of the 1958 season, leading all 50 laps.
- Fangio's Farewell Grand Prix: 5-time World Champion Juan Manuel Fangio drove his 51st and final World Championship Grand Prix in a Maserati 250F. Running 4th on the final lap, Hawthorn refused to lap Fangio out of respect, slowing behind the Argentine maestro as they crossed the line.
- Fangio Announces Retirement: In the paddock afterwards, Fangio calmly stated: 'It is finished.' He retired from Grand Prix racing with 5 World Championships, 24 wins from 51 starts (47.06% win rate), 29 poles, and 23 fastest laps.
