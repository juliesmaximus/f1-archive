## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Mike Hawthorn Britain's First World Champion: Stirling Moss did everything possible to win the championship: he took the lead on lap 1, set the fastest lap (2:22.5), and won the race by 1 minute 24 seconds. However, Mike Hawthorn's Ferrari teammate Phil Hill obeyed team orders and waved Hawthorn past into 2nd place on lap 40. Second place gave Hawthorn 6 points, bringing his net total to 42 points — beating Stirling Moss (41 points) by exactly ONE POINT to become the FIRST BRITISH DRIVER TO WIN THE FORMULA ONE WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP.
- Vanwall Wins Inaugural Constructors' Championship: With Moss's victory, Vanwall scored 48 net points to defeat Scuderia Ferrari (40 net points) and become the INAUGURAL WINNERS OF THE INTERNATIONAL CUP FOR F1 MANUFACTURERS.
- Stuart Lewis-Evans Fatal Crash: On lap 41, Vanwall driver Stuart Lewis-Evans suffered a catastrophic engine seizure at high speed. The car spun off the road, slammed into barriers, and exploded in flames. Severely burned, Lewis-Evans was airlifted to England where he succumbed to his injuries six days later on 25 October 1958 aged 28.
- Hawthorn Retires on the Spot: Deeply shaken by the loss of his friends Musso and Collins, Hawthorn announced his retirement from motor racing immediately after securing the title.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Mike Hawthorn crowned 1958 Formula One World Champion of Drivers (First British World Champion).
- Vanwall crowned inaugural Formula One World Constructors' Champion (International Cup for F1 Manufacturers).
- First World Championship Grand Prix held on the African continent.
- Only World Championship race held at Ain-Diab Circuit in Casablanca, Morocco.
