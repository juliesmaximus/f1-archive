# 1958 Moroccan Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: VII Grand Prix du Maroc
- **Round**: 11 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 19 October 1958
- **Location**: Ain-Diab Circuit, Casablanca, Morocco
- **Circuit Length**: 7.618 km (4.734 miles)
- **Distance**: 53 laps (403.754 km / 250.881 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 2:23.100
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 2:22.500 (Lap 21) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 2:09:15.100 (187.427 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 53 laps
  2. Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari) - 53 laps (+1:24.700)
  3. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 53 laps (+1:25.500)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **First British World Drivers' Champion**: Entering the season finale, Mike Hawthorn led Stirling Moss by 8 points (under best-6 counting). Moss needed to win and set fastest lap while hoping Hawthorn finished 3rd or lower. Moss executed his part to perfection, winning the race and setting the fastest lap (2:22.500). However, Ferrari teammate Phil Hill magnanimously obeyed team orders and slowed down, waving Hawthorn through into 2nd place with 2 laps to go. Hawthorn clinched the 1958 World Championship by just a single point (42 to 41), becoming the first British World Drivers' Champion in history.
- **Vanwall Wins Inaugural Manufacturers' Cup**: Vanwall secured 48 net points (from 6 victories), defeating Ferrari (40 points) to become the first winner of the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
- **Tragic Crash of Stuart Lewis-Evans**: On lap 42, Stuart Lewis-Evans suffered a catastrophic engine seizure in his Vanwall. The car spun off the high-speed road, ruptured its fuel tank, and burst into intense flames. Lewis-Evans escaped from the wreckage with severe burns over most of his body and was airlifted back to England, where he tragically died six days later in hospital. The heartbreak of Lewis-Evans' death led Vanwall owner Tony Vandervell to withdraw the team from full-time racing at the end of the year.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, dry desert-maritime weather along the Atlantic coast.
