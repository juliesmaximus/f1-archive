# 1958 British Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XI RAC British Grand Prix
- **Round**: 7 of 11 in the 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Saturday, 19 July 1958
- **Location**: Silverstone Circuit, Silverstone, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom
- **Circuit Length**: 4.711 km (2.927 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (353.325 km / 219.546 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 1:39.400
- **Fastest Lap**: Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 1:40.800 (Lap 50) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Peter Collins (Ferrari 246 Dino) - 2:09:04.200 (164.240 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Peter Collins (Ferrari) - 75 laps
  2. Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari) - 75 laps (+24.200s)
  3. Roy Salvadori (Cooper-Climax) - 75 laps (+50.600s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Peter Collins' Final Victory**: British driver Peter Collins made a sensational start from the second row to lead into the first corner and led all 75 laps in a masterclass drive, claiming a commanding Ferrari 1-2 finish with Mike Hawthorn. Tragically, this would be Collins' final Grand Prix victory and finish before his fatal accident at the Nürburgring two weeks later.
- **Vanwall Troubles**: Stirling Moss suffered an engine failure on lap 25, while Tony Brooks retired with gearbox trouble on lap 40.
- **Salvadori's Cooper Podium**: Roy Salvadori drove the factory Cooper-Climax to 3rd place, finishing ahead of Harry Schell's BRM.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Cool, overcast British summer day with dry track throughout.
