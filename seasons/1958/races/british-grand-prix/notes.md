## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Peter Collins Silverstone Triumph: In front of his ecstatic home crowd, Peter Collins took the lead at Copse on lap 1 and dominated the race, winning by 24.2 seconds over Ferrari teammate Mike Hawthorn.
- Ferrari 1-2 Sweep: Ferrari celebrated a 1-2 finish on British soil, with Hawthorn extending his World Championship lead.
- Collins' Final Career Victory: This proved to be the 3rd and final Grand Prix victory of Peter Collins' life, occurring exactly two weeks before his fatal crash at the Nürburgring.
