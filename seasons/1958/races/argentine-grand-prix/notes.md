## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Rear-Engine Revolution Begins: Stirling Moss drove Rob Walker's tiny 1.96-litre mid-engined Cooper T43-Climax, competing against the powerful 2.5-litre front-engined factory Ferraris and Maseratis.
- The Great Tyre Deception: Rob Walker and Moss faked preparation for a mid-race tyre stop, fooling the Ferrari team into waiting for Moss to pit. Instead, Moss nursed his Continental tyres without stopping for the entire 80 laps, crossing the line with tyre canvas exposed down to the cords to win by 2.7 seconds over Luigi Musso's Ferrari.
- First World Championship Win for Rear-Engine Car: This was the FIRST FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP VICTORY FOR A REAR/MID-ENGINE CAR, completely transforming the future of Formula One engineering.
- First Win for a Privateer Entrant: Rob Walker Racing Team became the first independent privateer team to win a World Championship Grand Prix.
- Fangio's Final Home GP: Fangio finished 4th in his final home Grand Prix before his mid-season retirement.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship Grand Prix victory for a rear/mid-engine chassis (Cooper).
- First World Championship victory for Coventry Climax engines.
- First World Championship victory for Rob Walker Racing Team (privateer entrant).
