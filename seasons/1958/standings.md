# 1958 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1958 World Championship of Drivers and the inaugural International Cup for F1 Manufacturers. Only each driver's best 6 results out of the 11 rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship tally. Only each constructor's best 6 results out of the 10 eligible Grands Prix counted towards the Manufacturers' Cup (Indianapolis 500 did not count toward the Manufacturers' Cup).

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Drivers' Championship Points**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers. 1 bonus point awarded for fastest lap.
- **Manufacturers' Cup Points**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers for each make (only highest-placing car of each constructor scored). No bonus point for fastest lap.
- **Best 6 rule**: Only best 6 results counted for both Drivers and Constructors.
- **Shared drives**: Shared drives were abolished for scoring points in the World Drivers' Championship starting in 1958.
