# 1950 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the inaugural 1950 World Championship of Drivers. The World Championship of Constructors was not introduced until 1958.

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Points system**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers. 1 bonus point awarded for the fastest lap (split equally if shared).
- **Best 4 rule**: Only each driver's best 4 results out of the 7 championship rounds counted towards their final championship tally.
- **Shared drives**: Points scored by shared cars were divided equally between drivers.
