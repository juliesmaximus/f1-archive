# 1950 Round 2: XI Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XI Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco
- **Historic Name Notes**: First Monaco Grand Prix of the World Championship era; 11th Monaco GP run since the event's inception in 1929.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit de Monaco
- **Location**: Monte Carlo and La Condamine, Principality of Monaco
- **Host Country**: Monaco
- **Track Length**: 3.180 km (1.976 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 15 m (49 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 100 laps / 318.000 km (197.596 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1950-05-21

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Warm, sunny, calm sea breeze with unusual coastal swells near Tabac.
- **Spectator Attendance**: Estimated 30,000 spectators across Monaco hillsides and balconies.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Temporary wooden grandstands installed at Quai Albert 1er along the harbour front.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Tabac Wave Multi-Car Pileup (Lap 1): On the opening lap, an unusually high Mediterranean tidal wave washed over the seawall at the Tabac corner, drenching the track. Nino Farina, running 2nd behind Fangio, spun on the wet asphalt. Luigi Fagioli, Toulo de Graffenried, Robert Manzon, Louis Rosier, Prince Bira, and 4 others slammed into the stranded cars, eliminating 10 of the 21 starters on lap 1 alone.
- Fangio's Miracle Escape: Juan Manuel Fangio led the race and navigated through the pileup on lap 2 after noticing spectators in the grandstands were looking away from him down the road rather than watching the leader — alerting him to slow down before arriving at the blind Tabac corner blind wreckage.
- Scuderia Ferrari World Championship Debut: Ferrari made their official F1 World Championship debut with the 125 F1 supercharged 1.5L V12 (Alberto Ascari 2nd, Raymond Sommer 4th, Luigi Villoresi DNF).
- Louis Chiron Podiums at Age 50: Local Monégasque hero Louis Chiron (50 years, 291 days) finished 3rd in a Maserati 4CLT, becoming the oldest driver to achieve an official podium in Formula One history (a record that stands unbroken).
- Only 7 cars were running at the finish, with Fangio lapping the entire field by at least one full lap.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Oldest driver to score a World Championship podium: Louis Chiron (50 years, 291 days) — all-time record.
- Maiden World Championship win and pole position for Juan Manuel Fangio.
- First World Championship podium for Scuderia Ferrari (Alberto Ascari, 2nd).
