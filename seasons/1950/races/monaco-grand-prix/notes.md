# 1950 Round 2: XI Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XI Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco
- **Historic Name Notes**: First Monaco Grand Prix of the World Championship era; 11th running of the Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco since 1929.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit de Monaco
- **Location**: Monte Carlo and La Condamine, Principality of Monaco
- **Host Country**: Monaco
- **Track Length**: 3.180 km (1.976 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 15 m (49 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 100 laps / 318.000 km (197.596 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1950-05-21

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Sunny and dry, but high sea spray / water flooded the harbour section at Tabac on lap 1.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 158) – 1:50.200
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 158) – 1:51.000 [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo) – 3:13:18.700 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo) – 3:13:18.700 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : Alberto Ascari (Ferrari) – +1 lap [6 pts]
- **3rd** : Louis Chiron (Maserati) – +2 laps [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Lap 1 Tabac Pileup**: On the opening lap, water flooding the track at Tabac caused a multi-car pileup eliminating 10 of the 21 starters (including Nino Farina, Luigi Fagioli, Toulo de Graffenried, Robert Manzon, and Louis Rosier).
- **Scuderia Ferrari World Championship Debut**: Ferrari made their World Championship debut with Alberto Ascari finishing 2nd (+1 lap) and Raymond Sommer finishing 4th (+3 laps).
- **Louis Chiron Record Podium**: Local Monégasque driver Louis Chiron finished 3rd at the age of 50 years, 291 days, establishing the all-time record as the oldest driver to achieve a Formula One World Championship podium.
- **Fangio Grand Slam**: Fangio qualified on pole (1:50.200), set the fastest race lap (1:51.000), led all 100 laps, and won by over a full lap, scoring his maiden World Championship victory.
- **Attrition**: Only 7 cars took the chequered flag out of 21 starters.
