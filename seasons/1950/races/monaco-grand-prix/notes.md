## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Scuderia Ferrari World Championship Debut: Ferrari made its official World Championship debut entering the 125 supercharged (Ascari 2nd, Villoresi retired).
- Tabac Multi-Car Pileup: On Lap 1, a sudden rogue wave from the harbor splashed over the seawall at Tabac, leaving a slick of sea water. Nino Farina spun, triggering a catastrophic pileup that eliminated 10 of the 19 starters on the opening lap, including Fagioli, Schell, de Graffenried, Trintignant, and Manzon.
- Fangio Escapes: Fangio had already exited the corner before the wave hit. Approaching on Lap 2, he noticed spectators looking away from the track rather than at him, sensed danger, braked early, and navigated through the wreckage.
- Fangio led all 100 laps to win by a full lap over Ascari, scoring his maiden World Championship victory.
