## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- AAA Regulations Nuance: Run under AAA Contest Board technical specifications (4.5L atmospheric Offenhauser engines) rather than FIA Formula One regulations. Counted towards the F1 Drivers' World Championship from 1950 to 1960.
- Zero European Participation: No regular European Formula One drivers or constructors entered due to logistics, cost, and the Swiss GP being scheduled just 5 days later.
- Rain Stoppage: Rain halted the race on Lap 138 of 200; official classification was declared permanent under AAA rules.
- Shared Points: Joie Chitwood and Tony Bettenhausen shared the #26 Kurtis Kraft, finishing 5th and sharing 2 points (1 point each).
