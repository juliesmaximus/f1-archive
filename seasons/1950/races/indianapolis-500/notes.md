## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Rain-Shortened Race: Scheduled for 200 laps (500 miles), the race was red-flagged on lap 138 due to torrential downpours. Officials declared the race complete at 138 laps (345 miles). Full championship points were awarded.
- Johnnie Parsons Win: Driving the Kurtis Kraft-Offenhauser #1 Wynn's Friction Proofing Special, Parsons took victory and earned 9 World Championship points (8 for win, 1 for fastest lap: 1:09.77 / 129.00 mph).
- Shared Drives: Joie Chitwood and Tony Bettenhausen shared the #8 Kurtis Kraft, finishing 5th and splitting the 2 points (1 point each).
- European Absence: No regular European Grand Prix drivers entered this event due to logistical distance and incompatible technical regulations (AAA front-engine roadsters running 4.5L atmospheric Offenhauser engines).
