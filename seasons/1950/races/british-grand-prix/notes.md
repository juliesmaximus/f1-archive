# 1950 Round 1: The Royal Automobile Club Grand Prix d'Europe / III RAC British Grand Prix - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: The Royal Automobile Club Grand Prix d'Europe / III RAC British Grand Prix
- **Historic Name Notes**: First World Championship race designated with the honorary title 'Grand Prix d'Europe'. Also officially designated as the III RAC British Grand Prix.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Silverstone Circuit
- **Location**: Silverstone, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom
- **Host Country**: United Kingdom
- **Track Length**: 4.649 km (2.889 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 150 m (492 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 70 laps / 325.430 km (202.213 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1950-05-13

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Dry, mild, sunny.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Alfa Romeo 158) – 1:50.800
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Alfa Romeo 158) – 1:50.600 (Lap 2) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Nino Farina (Alfa Romeo) – 2:13:23.600 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Nino Farina (Alfa Romeo) – 2:13:23.600 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : Luigi Fagioli (Alfa Romeo) – +2.600s [6 pts]
- **3rd** : Reg Parnell (Alfa Romeo) – +52.000s [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Inaugural Championship Grand Prix**: First round in the history of the FIA Formula One World Championship of Drivers.
- **Alfa Romeo Front-Row Lockout**: Alfa Romeo swept the front row of the 4-3-4 grid (Farina, Fagioli, Fangio, Parnell).
- **First Grand Slam**: Nino Farina achieved the first Grand Slam (pole position, fastest lap, race win, and led all 70 laps from start to finish).
- **First 1-2-3 Podium Sweep**: Alfa Romeo scored the first constructor 1-2-3 finish in World Championship history.
- **Juan Manuel Fangio DNF**: Retired from 2nd position on lap 62 with an oil pipe issue / engine failure.
