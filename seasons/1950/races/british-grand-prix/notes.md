# 1950 Round 1: The Royal Automobile Club Grand Prix d'Europe / III RAC British Grand Prix - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: The Royal Automobile Club Grand Prix d'Europe / III RAC British Grand Prix
- **Historic Name Notes**: First World Championship race designated with the honorary title 'Grand Prix d'Europe'. Also officially designated as the III RAC British Grand Prix.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Silverstone Circuit
- **Location**: Silverstone, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom
- **Host Country**: United Kingdom
- **Track Length**: 4.649 km (2.889 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 150 m (492 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 70 laps / 325.430 km (202.213 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1950-05-13

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Dry, sunny, and mild (approx 18-20°C).
- **Spectator Attendance**: Estimated 100,000 to 120,000 spectators including British Royal Family.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Woodcote and Abbey grandstands were fully packed with royal enclosures erected for King George VI, Queen Elizabeth, and Princess Margaret.

- **Pole Position**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Alfa Romeo 158) - 1:50.8

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Nino Farina (Alfa Romeo) — 2:13:23.600 [9 pts]

- **3rd** : Reg Parnell (Alfa Romeo) — +52.000 [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Inaugural race of the FIA Formula One World Championship of Drivers.
- Royal Attendance: King George VI and Queen Elizabeth attended the race with Princess Margaret and Lord & Lady Mountbatten — the first and only time a reigning British monarch attended an official British Grand Prix.
- Alfa Romeo Front Row Lockout: The four factory Alfa Romeo 158 'Alfetta' supercharged 1.5L cars monopolized the entire four-abreast front row (Farina, Fagioli, Fangio, and British guest driver Reg Parnell).
- Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina achieved the first 'Grand Slam' / 'Chelem' in F1 history: pole position (1:50.8), fastest race lap (1:50.6 on lap 2), victory, and led all 70 laps from start to finish.
- Reg Parnell struck a hare on lap 13, causing minor denting to the Alfa Romeo's nose cowling, but continued to finish on the podium (3rd).
- Juan Manuel Fangio was running second on lap 62 when an oil pipe failed, causing engine seizure and his first World Championship retirement.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship pole position, fastest lap, race win, and Grand Slam: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina.
- First constructor 1-2-3 podium sweep in World Championship history: Alfa Romeo (Farina, Fagioli, Parnell).
