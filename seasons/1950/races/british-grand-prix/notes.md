## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Inaugural World Championship Grand Prix in Formula One history.
- Royal Attendance: King George VI and Queen Elizabeth attended with Princess Margaret and Lord & Lady Mountbatten — the only time a reigning British monarch has attended an official British Grand Prix.
- Alfa Romeo Front-Row Lockout: The 4 factory Alfa Romeo 158 'Alfettas' qualified 1-2-3-4 (Farina, Fagioli, Fangio, Parnell).
- Juan Manuel Fangio retired on lap 62 with an oil pipe failure while running second.
- Giuseppe Farina achieved Formula One's first-ever 'Grand Slam' (pole position, victory, fastest lap, and led every lap from start to finish).
