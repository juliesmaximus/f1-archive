## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Title Decider: Three-way title fight between Farina (22 pts), Fangio (26 pts), and Fagioli (24 pts).
- Fangio's Double Retirement: Fangio started on pole and set fastest lap, but his gearbox seized on lap 23. He took over teammate Piero Taruffi's Alfa Romeo on lap 25, only for that car to suffer engine valve failure on lap 34, eliminating Fangio from championship contention.
- Ferrari Shared Drive Drama: Alberto Ascari retired his Ferrari 375 on lap 21, then took over teammate Dorino Serafini's car on lap 47, storming back to finish 2nd. Ascari and Serafini shared the 6 points for 2nd place (3 points each).
- Inaugural World Champion: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina led 74 laps to win the race and clinch the inaugural FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship with 30 net points.
