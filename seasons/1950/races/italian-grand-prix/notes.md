## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Three-Way Championship Title Fight: Entering the finale, three Alfa Romeo drivers could win the inaugural World Championship: Juan Manuel Fangio (26 pts), Luigi Fagioli (24 net pts), and Nino Farina (22 pts).
- Fangio Double DNF: Fangio started from pole position, but his gearbox seized on lap 23. He took over teammate Piero Taruffi's Alfa Romeo 158 on lap 25, but that car also suffered engine failure (valve failure) on lap 34, leaving Fangio with 0 points.
- Farina Crowned Inaugural Champion: Nino Farina led 59 laps and drove to victory, scoring 8 points to leap from 3rd to 1st in the standings with 30 points, becoming the FIRST EVER FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPION.
- Shared Podiums for Ascari and Serafini: Alberto Ascari retired his factory Ferrari 375 on lap 21 (overheating), but took over teammate Dorino Serafini's Ferrari 375 on lap 47. Ascari drove the shared car to 2nd place, earning 3 points each for Ascari and Serafini (split 6 pts).
- Fagioli Finished 3rd: Luigi Fagioli finished 3rd, scoring 4 gross points, but dropped those points under the best 4 of 7 rule, finishing with 24 net points in 3rd place overall.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina crowned the inaugural FIA Formula One World Champion of Drivers.
- First three-way title showdown at a season finale in Formula One history.
- First World Championship podium achieved in a shared car: Alberto Ascari and Dorino Serafini (Ferrari 375, 2nd).
