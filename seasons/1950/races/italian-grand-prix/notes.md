## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Three-Way Title Fight**: Entering the race, Juan Manuel Fangio (26 pts), Luigi Fagioli (24 net pts), and Nino Farina (22 pts) were all eligible to win the World Championship.
- **Fangio Double DNF**: Fangio started from pole, but retired with a gearbox failure on lap 23. He took over teammate Piero Taruffi's #60 Alfa Romeo on lap 25, but retired again with engine/valve failure on lap 34. However, Fangio retained 1 point for setting the fastest race lap (2:00.000 on lap 7) prior to his retirement.
- **First World Champion Crowned**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina led 59 laps to win the race and score 8 points, raising his net total to 30 points and becoming the FIRST EVER FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPION.
- **Shared Ferrari 2nd Place**: Alberto Ascari retired his Ferrari #48 on lap 21 due to engine overheating, then took over Dorino Serafini's Ferrari #48 on lap 47, driving it to 2nd place. Ascari and Serafini split the 6 points for 2nd place (3 points each).
- **Fagioli 3rd Place Drops Score**: Luigi Fagioli finished 3rd (+95.600s) scoring 4 gross points, but under the best 4 of 7 regulation, dropped these points to finish on 24 net points in 3rd overall.
