# 1950 Round 6: 37e Grand Prix de l'ACF (Automobile Club de France) - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: 37e Grand Prix de l'ACF (Automobile Club de France)
- **Historic Name Notes**: 37th Grand Prix organized by the Automobile Club de France; held at the triangular high-speed Reims-Gueux road circuit.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Reims-Gueux
- **Location**: Gueux, Marne, Champagne, France
- **Host Country**: France
- **Track Length**: 7.816 km (4.857 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 88 m (289 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 64 laps / 500.224 km (310.825 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1950-07-02

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Intense summer heatwave, ambient temperatures reaching 38°C (100°F).

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 158) – 2:30.600
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 158) – 2:35.600 (Lap 52) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo) – 2:57:52.800 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo) – 2:57:52.800 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : Luigi Fagioli (Alfa Romeo) – +25.700s [6 pts]
- **3rd** : Peter Whitehead (Ferrari) – +3 laps [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Heatwave Attrition**: Severe cockpit heat and engine thermal strain decimated the field; only 7 cars out of 20 starters were classified at the finish.
- **Fangio Grand Slam**: Fangio qualified on pole (2:30.600), set the fastest lap (2:35.600), won the race, and led all 64 laps, scoring his second Grand Slam of the season.
- **Shared 5th Place Points**: Philippe Étancelin and Eugène Chaboud shared car #16 (Talbot-Lago T26C-DA) to finish 5th (+5 laps), receiving 1 point each (split 2 points). Chaboud became the first driver to score a championship point without taking the start.
- **Maiden Ferrari Podium for Whitehead**: Privateer Peter Whitehead finished 3rd in a private Ferrari 125, scoring his first World Championship podium.
- **Championship Lead Shift**: Fangio took the lead of the World Championship standings with 26 gross points.
