# 1950 Round 5: XII Grote Prijs van België / XII Grand Prix de Belgique - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XII Grote Prijs van België / XII Grand Prix de Belgique
- **Historic Name Notes**: 12th running of the Belgian Grand Prix; held on the ultra-fast 14.120 km public road course in the Ardennes.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps
- **Location**: Francorchamps, Stavelot, Malmedy, Belgium
- **Host Country**: Belgium
- **Track Length**: 14.120 km (8.774 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 410 m (1345 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 35 laps / 494.200 km (307.082 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1950-06-18

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Warm and sunny across the Ardennes valley.
- **Spectator Attendance**: Estimated 50,000 spectators.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Grandstands situated at the bottom of the Eau Rouge climb and opposite the pit lane.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Alfa Romeo 158) - 4:37.0
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Alfa Romeo 158) - 4:34.1 [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Fangio (Alfa Romeo) - 2:47 (8 pts)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Juan Fangio (Alfa Romeo) — 2:47 [8 pts]
- **2nd** : Luigi Fagioli (Alfa Romeo) — +14.000 [6 pts]
- **3rd** : Louis Rosier (Talbot-Lago) — +2:19.000 [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Speed Record: The 14.120 km Spa course tested extreme high speeds. Fangio won at an average speed of 177.097 km/h (110.043 mph).
- Farina Pit Stop Drama: Nino Farina started on pole and led early, but suffered transmission overheating and fuel pressure issues in the closing laps, dropping from 1st to 4th place behind Fangio, Fagioli, and Louis Rosier.
- Rosier Back-to-Back Podiums: Louis Rosier once again utilized the no-stop strategy in his private Talbot-Lago T26C to finish on the podium (3rd), 2 minutes 19 seconds behind Fangio.
- Ascari Ferrari 275 F1 Debut: Ferrari introduced their new unblown 3.3-litre V12 engine in the 275 F1 driven by Alberto Ascari, finishing 5th to score 2 points.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Fastest average race speed in World Championship history to date: 177.097 km/h (Fangio).
- Fangio equalled Farina with 2 World Championship race wins.
