## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Max Verstappen 4th World Championship:** Max Verstappen clinched his fourth consecutive World Drivers' Championship at the Las Vegas Grand Prix (Round 22), becoming only the sixth driver in Formula 1 history to win four or more World Titles (joining Fangio, Prost, Schumacher, Vettel, and Hamilton). The championship was highlighted by a masterclass victory in torrential rain at São Paulo, charging from 17th on the grid to win by 19.4 seconds and setting 17 consecutive fastest laps.
- **McLaren First Constructors' Title in 26 Years:** McLaren won their ninth Constructors' World Championship and first since 1998, scoring 666 points with Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri in an intense season-long fight with Ferrari and Red Bull.
- **Maiden Grand Prix Winners:**
  - **Lando Norris:** Scored his long-awaited maiden Grand Prix victory at Miami (Round 6) in his 110th start, adding subsequent wins at Zandvoort, Singapore, and Abu Dhabi to finish runner-up in the World Championship.
  - **Oscar Piastri:** Achieved his maiden Grand Prix victory at Hungary (Round 13) in his second season, followed by an aggressive victory at Azerbaijan.
- **Charles Leclerc Emotional Triumphs:** Leclerc broke the "Monaco curse" to score a historic, highly emotional maiden victory at his home Monaco Grand Prix, and executed a daring 1-stop strategy to win at Monza in front of the passionate Tifosi.
- **Lewis Hamilton Record 9th Silverstone Victory:** Hamilton ended a 945-day winless streak with a fairytale victory at the British Grand Prix, becoming the first driver in F1 history to win the same Grand Prix 9 times, before claiming his 105th career victory at the Belgian Grand Prix following George Russell's post-race disqualification for an underweight car.
- **Alpine Interlagos Miracle Double Podium:** In severe wet weather at São Paulo, Esteban Ocon (P2) and Pierre Gasly (P3) achieved an astounding double podium for Alpine, vaulting the Enstone team from 9th to 6th in the Constructors' Championship in a single afternoon.
