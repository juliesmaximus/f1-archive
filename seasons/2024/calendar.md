## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Record 24-Round Schedule:** Expanded global championship featuring the return of the Chinese Grand Prix in Shanghai for the first time since 2019.
- **Sprint Weekend Realignment:** Reordered weekend format to improve flow: Friday Free Practice 1 followed by Sprint Shootout; Saturday morning Sprint race, followed by Saturday afternoon Grand Prix Qualifying; Sunday Grand Prix.
- **Parc Fermé Rules Adjustment:** Parc fermé reopened between the Sprint race and Grand Prix Qualifying, permitting teams to adjust setups for the Grand Prix.
- **Cost Cap Governance:** Operational spending ceiling capped at $135 million adjusted for inflation and calendar expansion.
- **Final Year of Fastest Lap Bonus Point:** 2024 marked the final championship season awarding a bonus championship point for fastest lap (regulation discontinued by the FIA for 2025 onwards).
- **Historic Competitive Balance:** Seven different Grand Prix winners across four different constructors (McLaren, Ferrari, Red Bull, Mercedes), producing the closest multi-team championship fight in over a decade.
