## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Ferrari Ends 21-Year Drivers' Championship Drought:** Michael Schumacher won 9 races to capture his 3rd World Championship and Ferrari's first Drivers' title since Jody Scheckter in 1979.
- **Häkkinen-Schumacher Spa Duel:** Mika Häkkinen executed what is widely voted as the greatest overtake in Formula 1 history at Spa-Francorchamps, passing both backmarker Ricardo Zonta and leader Michael Schumacher simultaneously at 200 mph into Les Combes.
- **Rubens Barrichello's Maiden Victory:** Rubens Barrichello scored his emotional first Formula 1 victory at Hockenheim, starting 18th on the grid in changeable rain conditions and bravely staying out on dry slick tyres while the stadium section was drenched.
- **Return of the United States Grand Prix:** Formula 1 returned to the United States for the first time since 1991, racing on a newly constructed 4.192 km road course inside the historic Indianapolis Motor Speedway.
- **McLaren Austrian GP Penalty:** McLaren was deducted 10 Constructors' Championship points when an FIA fuel/engine management seal was found missing from Häkkinen's car after his victory at the A1-Ring.
