## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Ferrari Ends 21-Year Drivers' Championship Drought:** Michael Schumacher won 9 races to capture his 3rd World Championship and Ferrari's first Drivers' title since Jody Scheckter in 1979.
- **Häkkinen-Schumacher Spa Duel:** Mika Häkkinen executed what is widely voted as the greatest overtake in Formula 1 history at Spa-Francorchamps, passing both Ricardo Zonta and Michael Schumacher simultaneously at 200 mph into Les Combes.
- **Rubens Barrichello's 18th-to-1st Maiden Win:** Barrichello took his first Grand Prix win at Hockenheim in the wet on dry slick tyres from 18th on the grid.
- **Return of the US Grand Prix at Indianapolis:** F1 returned to the United States on the IMS road course before 220,000 spectators.
