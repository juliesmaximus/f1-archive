## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Engine Rules:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated engines (maximum 10 cylinders). V10 engines became universal across all teams.
- **Safety Testing:** Frontal impact test speed raised from 13 m/s to 14 m/s. Roll hoop strength tests increased by 50%.
