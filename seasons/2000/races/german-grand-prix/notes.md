## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most dramatic, emotional races in F1 history: Rubens Barrichello scored his maiden Grand Prix victory from 18th on the grid. Schumacher crashed on lap 1 after colliding with Fisichella. On lap 25, a disgruntled former Mercedes employee walked alongside the track, triggering a Safety Car. When torrential rain hit the stadium section while the long forest straights remained bone dry, Barrichello made the heroic gamble to stay out on slick tyres. Barrichello drove faultlessly in the wet to win, weeping uncontrollably on the podium as Brazil's national anthem played for the first time since Ayrton Senna in 1993.
