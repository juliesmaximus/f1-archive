## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Tragedy shadowed the race when fire marshal Paolo Gislimberti was fatally struck by a detached wheel in a violent 5-car pileup at the Variante della Roggia on lap 1. Schumacher won from pole to equal Ayrton Senna's tally of 41 career victories; in the post-race press conference, Schumacher broke down in uncontrollable tears when asked about the achievement. Häkkinen finished 2nd and Ralf Schumacher 3rd.
