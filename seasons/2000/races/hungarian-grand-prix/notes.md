## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mika Häkkinen made a lightning start from 3rd on the grid to seize the lead into Turn 1, leading all 77 laps to take victory ahead of Schumacher and Coulthard. With this win, Häkkinen took the lead of the World Championship for the first time in 2000.
