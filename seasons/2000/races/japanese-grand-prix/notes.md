## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The historic championship coronation. Mika Häkkinen made a lightning start from 2nd to lead into Turn 1. Schumacher shadowed him relentlessly. As light drizzle fell on lap 37, Schumacher stayed out three laps longer before his second pit stop, setting blistering lap times. Schumacher emerged from the pit lane ahead of Häkkinen and defended his lead to win by 1.8 seconds. Michael Schumacher clinched his 3rd World Championship and ended Scuderia Ferrari's 21-year Drivers' Championship drought (their first since Jody Scheckter in 1979).
