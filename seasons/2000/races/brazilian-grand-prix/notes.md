## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher won his second consecutive race, passing pole-sitter Häkkinen early before Häkkinen retired with engine failure. David Coulthard finished 2nd on the road but was disqualified due to an illegal front wing endplate height (7 mm too low), promoting Giancarlo Fisichella (Benetton) to 2nd and Heinz-Harald Frentzen (Jordan) to 3rd.
