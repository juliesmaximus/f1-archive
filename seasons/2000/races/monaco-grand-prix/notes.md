## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Schumacher led comfortably from pole by 37 seconds until lap 55, when his exhaust broke and cracked his rear left pushrod suspension, forcing his retirement. David Coulthard inherited the victory to win ahead of Rubens Barrichello and Giancarlo Fisichella.
