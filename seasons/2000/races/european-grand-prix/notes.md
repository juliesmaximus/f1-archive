## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A classic Schumacher wet-weather masterclass. Häkkinen launched like a rocket from 3rd on the grid to lead into Turn 1. When rain fell on lap 11, Schumacher showed his wet mastery, passing Häkkinen and pulling away to win by 13.8 seconds. Coulthard finished 3rd.
