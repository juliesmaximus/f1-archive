## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Fierce duel between Coulthard and Schumacher. Coulthard attempted an inside pass at the Adelaide hairpin; Schumacher squeezed him, prompting Coulthard to make a famous hand gesture. On lap 40, Coulthard completed a clean overtake to take the lead and win. Häkkinen took 2nd and Barrichello 3rd as Schumacher retired with engine failure.
