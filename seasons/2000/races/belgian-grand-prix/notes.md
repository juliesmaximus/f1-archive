## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Widely acclaimed as the greatest overtake in Formula 1 history. Schumacher led from lap 13, aggressively defending his position against Häkkinen down the Kemmel straight. On lap 41, approaching Les Combes at 200 mph, both drivers caught Ricardo Zonta's BAR to lap him. While Schumacher dived to the left of Zonta, Häkkinen made an audacious, split-second dart down the inside right, squeezing through to pass both cars simultaneously in a single breathtaking manoeuvre. Häkkinen won ahead of Schumacher and Ralf Schumacher.
