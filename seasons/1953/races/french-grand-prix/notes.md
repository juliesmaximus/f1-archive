## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The Race of the Century: A relentless four-car slipstreaming battle between Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 500), Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati A6GCM), José Froilán González (Maserati), and Alberto Ascari (Ferrari). The lead changed hands dozens of times over 60 laps.
- Hawthorn Maiden Victory: 24-year-old Mike Hawthorn passed Fangio at the final Thillois hairpin on the final lap to win by just 1.0 second, becoming the FIRST BRITISH DRIVER TO WIN A FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX.
- End of Ascari Streak: Ascari finished 4th (+4.6s), ending his historic winning streak at 9 consecutive European Grand Prix starts.
- Top 4 Separated by 4.6 Seconds: After 429 km of full-throttle racing, the top 4 finishers crossed the line separated by under five seconds.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First British driver to win a World Championship Grand Prix: Mike Hawthorn.
- End of Alberto Ascari's record streak of consecutive Grand Prix wins.
