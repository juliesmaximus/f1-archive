## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **'The Race of the Century'**: Widely regarded as one of the greatest races in Grand Prix history. For 60 laps, four drivers — Mike Hawthorn, Juan Manuel Fangio, José Froilán González, and Alberto Ascari — swapped the lead multiple times on every lap in a ferocious slipstreaming duel around the fast Reims triangular circuit.
- **Maiden Win for Hawthorn & First British Winner**: 24-year-old Mike Hawthorn took his maiden World Championship victory, defeating Fangio by just 1.0 second. Hawthorn became the FIRST BRITISH DRIVER to win a Formula One World Championship Grand Prix.
- **Ascari Winning Streak Snapped**: Alberto Ascari finished 4th (+4.600s), ending his historic streak of 9 consecutive World Championship Grand Prix victories.
- **Shared Fastest Lap Points**: Fangio and Ascari set identical fastest laps of 2:41.100, splitting the bonus point to receive 0.5 points each.
