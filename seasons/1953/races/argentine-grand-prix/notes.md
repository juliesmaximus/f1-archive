# 1953 Round 1: I Gran Premio de la República Argentina - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: I Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Historic Name Notes**: Inaugural Argentine Grand Prix in World Championship history; first World Championship round held in South America.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre (later Autódromo Oscar y Juan Gálvez)
- **Location**: Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Host Country**: Argentina
- **Track Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 10 m (33 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 97 laps / 379.464 km (235.788 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1953-01-18

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Intense tropical summer heat, ambient temperatures exceeding 38°C and tarmac over 45°C.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Alberto Ascari (Ferrari 500) – 1:55.400
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Alberto Ascari (Ferrari 500) – 1:48.400 (Lap 73) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Alberto Ascari (Ferrari 500) – 3:01:04.600 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Alberto Ascari (Ferrari 500) – 3:01:04.600 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : Luigi Villoresi (Ferrari 500) – +1 lap [6 pts]
- **3rd** : José Froilán González (Maserati A6GCM) – +1 lap [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Ascari 7th Consecutive Grand Prix Victory**: Ascari won by one full lap, extending his winning streak to 7 consecutive World Championship Grand Prix starts.
- **Track Incursion Incident**: An overwhelming crowd broke through perimeter fences. On lap 31, a child ran onto the track in front of Nino Farina's Ferrari; Farina swerved violently and crashed into the perimeter spectator embankment. The race continued under dangerous conditions without interruption.
- **Fangio Comeback DNF**: Juan Manuel Fangio made his return to Grand Prix racing following his 1952 injury, running 2nd before his Maserati A6GCM suffered transmission failure on lap 36.
- **Oscar Alfredo Gálvez Points on Debut**: Local hero Oscar Alfredo Gálvez drove a Maserati A6GCM to 5th place (+1 lap), scoring 2 championship points in his sole World Championship appearance.
