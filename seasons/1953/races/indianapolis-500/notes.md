## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **The Hottest 500**: Intense heat caused severe heat exhaustion among drivers; half of the 33 starters required relief drivers. Tragically, driver Carl Scarborough died of heat exhaustion after retiring from the race.
- **Bill Vukovich Iron-Man Drive**: Bill Vukovich refused relief, driving the entire 500-mile race solo with ice poured into his cockpit during pit stops, leading 195 of the 200 laps to win by over 3.5 minutes.
- **Shared Relief Points**:
  - Sam Hanks and Duane Carter shared car #3 to finish 3rd, receiving 2 points each.
  - Fred Agabashian and Paul Russo shared car #59 to finish 4th (+4:39.290), receiving 1.5 points each.
