## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari Sets All-Time Winning Streak Record: Ascari scored his 7th consecutive Grand Prix victory (or 9th consecutive excluding Indy 500), an official record that stood for 60 years until Sebastian Vettel in 2013.
- Maserati Challenge Fades: González led early until his engine failed on lap 11. Fangio took the lead until his engine failed on lap 13.
- Fangio Final-Lap Crash: Fangio took over Johnny Claes's Maserati, but crashed heavily on the final lap at Stavelot, escaping uninjured.
