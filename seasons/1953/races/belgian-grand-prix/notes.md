## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Ascari 9th Consecutive European GP Victory**: Alberto Ascari won his 9th consecutive European championship Grand Prix start, leading home teammate Luigi Villoresi for another Ferrari 1-2.
- **Maserati Early Charge & Collapse**: Juan Manuel Fangio (pole) and José Froilán González led the opening laps before González's throttle broke on lap 11 and Fangio suffered engine failure on lap 13.
- **Onofre Marimón Maiden Podium**: Young Argentine protégé Onofre Marimón drove steadily in his Maserati A6GCM to finish 3rd (+1 lap), securing his first World Championship podium.
- **Fangio Final Lap Crash**: Fangio took over Johnny Claes's Maserati on lap 13, charged into 3rd position, but crashed violently on the final lap at Stavelot when a steering rod broke; he escaped uninjured.
