## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Farina Final Career Victory**: 46-year-old defending 1950 champion Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina took advantage of Ascari's troubles to score his 5th and final career Formula One World Championship victory.
- **Ascari Wheel Loss Drama**: Ascari started on pole and pulled out an early lead, but on lap 5 his right-front wheel detached at high speed. He skilfully controlled the three-wheeled car into the pits.
- **Ascari Shared Car Charge & DNF**: Ascari took over teammate Luigi Villoresi's Ferrari 500 on lap 10, set the fastest lap of the race (9:56.000), but retired with engine failure on lap 15.
- **Mike Hawthorn Podium**: Hawthorn took 3rd place, securing another double podium for Ferrari alongside Farina.
