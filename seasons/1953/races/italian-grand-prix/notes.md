## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Final-Corner Final-Lap Mayhem: Ascari, Farina, and Fangio entered Curva di Vedano (Parabolica) on the final lap running nose-to-tail lapping backmarker Onofre Marimón. Ascari spun on oil; Marimón could not avoid him and collided heavily into Ascari's stationary Ferrari; Farina took to the grass to avoid them; Fangio threaded through the gap on the inside to win!
- Maserati's Maiden F1 Victory: Fangio scored Officine Alfieri Maserati's first-ever Formula One World Championship victory, ending Ferrari's complete domination of the 1952–1953 Formula Two era.
- Ascari escaped unhurt from the collision, ending his Ferrari era on an astonishing record of 11 wins from 15 World Championship starts.
