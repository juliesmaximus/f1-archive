# 1953 Round 9: XXIV Gran Premio d'Italia - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XXIV Gran Premio d'Italia
- **Historic Name Notes**: 24th Italian Grand Prix; season finale and final championship race of the 1952–1953 2.0-litre Formula Two era.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza
- **Location**: Monza, Lombardy, Italy
- **Host Country**: Italy
- **Track Length**: 6.300 km (3.915 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 162 m (531 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 80 laps / 504.000 km (313.171 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1953-09-13

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Sunny, warm, and dry autumn weather in the royal park of Monza.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Dramatic Final Lap Crash at Vedano**: On the 80th and final lap, Ascari led Fangio, Farina, and Onofre Marimón nose-to-tail. Entering the Curva di Vedano, Ascari spun violently across the track after hitting fluid or lapped traffic. Marimón had nowhere to go and slammed into Ascari's Ferrari. Farina swerved onto the grass, while Fangio darted past on the inside to take a thrilling victory by 1.4 seconds.
- **Maserati Maiden Championship Win**: Juan Manuel Fangio scored Officine Alfieri Maserati's FIRST EVER World Championship Grand Prix victory, ending Ferrari's monopoly of the 1953 championship.
- **End of the Formula Two Era**: This race was the final World Championship Grand Prix run under the 2.0-litre Formula Two regulations, before the 2.5-litre Formula One regulations took effect in 1954.
