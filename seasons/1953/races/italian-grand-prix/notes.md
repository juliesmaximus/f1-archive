## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Dramatic Final Lap Pileup: Entering the final Curva di Vedano on the 80th and final lap, Ascari led Fangio, Farina, and Onofre Marimón nose-to-tail. Ascari spun violently across the track after hitting oil. Marimón had nowhere to go and launched over Ascari's car. Farina took to the grass to avoid them. Fangio threaded the needle on the inside line to cross the line for a sensational victory!
- Fangio Maiden Maserati Win: Juan Manuel Fangio took his first World Championship victory for Officine Alfieri Maserati, ending Ferrari's complete domination of the 1953 season.
- Ascari and Marimón Unhurt: Both Ascari and Marimón walked away without serious injury despite the violent crash.
- End of the 2.0L Formula Two Era: This race marked the final World Championship Grand Prix run to Formula Two regulations before the introduction of the 2.5-litre Formula One regulations in 1954.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship victory for Maserati (Juan Manuel Fangio).
- End of the 1952-1953 Formula Two engine era in Formula One history.
