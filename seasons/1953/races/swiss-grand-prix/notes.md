## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari Clinches 2nd World Championship: Alberto Ascari won the race by 1 minute 12 seconds over Farina, mathematically clinching his SECOND CONSECUTIVE WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP.
- Ferrari Intra-Team Defiance: Team manager Nello Ugolini ordered Ascari and Farina to hold position to preserve their cars. Ascari pitted for fresh spark plugs on lap 54, emerging behind Farina. Despite pit signals to hold position, Ascari chased down and overtook Farina on lap 59 to take the win, causing immense anger from Farina.
- Fangio Engine Failure: Fangio started from pole position but retired on lap 29 with engine failure. He took over Felice Bonetto's Maserati, but that car also retired on lap 12.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Alberto Ascari became the FIRST TWO-TIME WORLD CHAMPION IN FORMULA ONE HISTORY.
