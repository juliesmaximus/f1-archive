## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Championship Clinch for Ascari: Alberto Ascari won his second consecutive World Drivers' Championship, becoming Formula One's first double World Champion.
- Ferrari Team Orders Rebellion: Ascari made an unscheduled pit stop for fresh spark plugs on lap 40, emerging in 3rd. Team manager Nello Ugolini ordered Farina and Hawthorn to hold position, but Ascari charged fiercely, overtook Hawthorn, and caught Farina. Farina resisted fiercely until Ascari squeezed through on lap 54 to win.
- Fangio Broken Gearbox: Fangio led early from pole until his gearbox failed on lap 12. He took over Felice Bonetto's Maserati, but that car also failed on lap 29.
