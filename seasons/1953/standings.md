# 1953 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1953 World Championship of Drivers. Run to 2.0-litre Formula Two regulations. Only each driver's best 4 results out of the 9 rounds counted towards the final championship tally.

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Points system**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers. 1 bonus point awarded for the fastest lap (split equally if shared).
- **Best 4 rule**: Only each driver's best 4 results counted.
- **Shared drives**: Points scored by shared cars were divided equally between drivers.
