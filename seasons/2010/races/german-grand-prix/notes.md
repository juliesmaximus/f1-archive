## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The 'Fernando is faster than you' scandal. Felipe Massa led from the start on the one-year anniversary of his horrific Hungary crash. Teammate Fernando Alonso stalked him closely. On lap 47, Ferrari race engineer Rob Smedley delivered the immortal coded radio message to Massa: 'Fernando is faster than you. Can you confirm you understood that message?' Massa visibly slowed on the exit of the hairpin on lap 49, allowing Alonso past to win. Ferrari was fined $100,000 by the FIA for illegal team orders.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Massive public and media outcry over Ferrari's blatant team orders violation; the FIA subsequently abolished the team orders ban for 2011.
