## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Mark Webber took the World Championship lead with a sensational strategic victory. Staying out on super-soft tyres during a Safety Car, Webber pounded in 35 consecutive qualifying laps to build a 23-second pit stop window. Vettel received a drive-through penalty for dropping more than 10 car lengths behind the Safety Car. Michael Schumacher nearly pushed Rubens Barrichello into the pit wall at 180 mph during a terrifying overtake, receiving a 10-place grid penalty for the next race.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Schumacher's ruthless squeeze on Barrichello was condemned across the paddock as one of the most dangerous maneuvers of his career.
