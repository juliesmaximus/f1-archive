## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel dominated from pole. On lap 9, Mark Webber suffered a terrifying 190 mph airborne somersault after clipping the rear of Heikki Kovalainen's Lotus, flying upside down and smashing into an advertising hoarding before landing on his wheels—Webber walked away completely unhurt. Lewis Hamilton passed the Safety Car as it exited the pitlane and received a drive-through penalty, but served it without losing 2nd place, infuriating 8th-placed Fernando Alonso.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Alonso launched a furious tirade over the radio, calling the race 'manipulated' after Hamilton maintained 2nd despite the penalty.
