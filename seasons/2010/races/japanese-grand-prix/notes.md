## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Saturday monsoon forced qualifying to Sunday morning. Red Bull locked out the front row and cruised to a dominant 1-2 finish, Vettel leading Webber home by 0.9 seconds. Fernando Alonso finished 3rd. Local hero Kamui Kobayashi electrified Japanese fans with five daring overtakes down the inside into the hairpin.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Opening lap carnage: Massa and Liuzzi crashed at Turn 1, while Petrov wiped out Hulkenberg.
