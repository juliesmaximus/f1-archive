## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ferrari triumph in front of delirious home tifosi. Fernando Alonso took pole position and engaged in a thrilling duel with Jenson Button. Ferrari pulled off a lightning-fast pit stop on lap 37 to jump Button into the Rettifilo chicane. Lewis Hamilton suffered a disastrous first-lap retirement after clipping Felipe Massa's rear wheel at the Roggia chicane, breaking his front suspension.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's DNF cost him the championship lead, handing it back to Mark Webber.
