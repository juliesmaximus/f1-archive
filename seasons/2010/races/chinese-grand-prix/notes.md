## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Another wet-weather tactical triumph by Jenson Button. As rain fell early, most frontrunners pitted for intermediate tyres. Button, Rosberg, and Kubica stayed out on dry slicks as the rain eased. When heavy rain finally arrived, Button managed his tyres to perfection to lead a McLaren 1-2 ahead of Lewis Hamilton, taking the outright lead of the World Championship.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Fernando Alonso jumped the start and was handed a drive-through penalty, fighting back to 4th.
