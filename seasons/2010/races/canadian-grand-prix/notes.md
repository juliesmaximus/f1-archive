## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A modern classic that changed Formula 1 tyre design forever. Bridgestone's super-soft tyres experienced massive graining and degradation, turning the race into a furious multi-stop tactical sprint. Lewis Hamilton hunted down Fernando Alonso and Mark Webber to take a thrilling victory, leading teammate Jenson Button in another McLaren 1-2.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The extreme excitement produced by heavy tyre degradation convinced Pirelli to design high-degradation tyres when they took over as sole supplier in 2011.
