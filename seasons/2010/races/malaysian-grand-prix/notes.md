## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Red Bull Racing crushed the field with a dominant 1-2 finish. Sebastian Vettel jumped teammate and pole-sitter Mark Webber into Turn 1 and never looked back, winning by 4.8 seconds. Nico Rosberg completed the podium in 3rd, scoring Mercedes GP's first podium since 1955.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Qualifying rain storm caught out Ferrari and McLaren, stranding Alonso, Massa, and Hamilton near the back of the grid.
