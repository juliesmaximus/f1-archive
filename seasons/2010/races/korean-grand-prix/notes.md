## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Catastrophe for Red Bull and triumph for Ferrari. Inaugural race at Yeongam began behind the Safety Car in treacherous monsoon rain. On lap 19, championship leader Mark Webber spun on curbs, hit the wall, and took out Nico Rosberg. Sebastian Vettel was cruising to victory until his Renault engine blew up in a catastrophic cloud of smoke on lap 45! Fernando Alonso swept past to win in fading twilight, seizing an astonishing 11-point championship lead with two races remaining.
