## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Civil war at Red Bull Racing! On lap 40, while running 1-2, Sebastian Vettel slipstreamed teammate Mark Webber down the back straight. As Vettel pulled alongside, he moved slightly right; the two Red Bulls collided at 190 mph! Vettel spun violently into retirement, gesticulating angrily that Webber was crazy, while Webber pitted for a new nose to finish 3rd. Lewis Hamilton and Jenson Button engaged in an epic wheel-to-wheel duel for the lead before Hamilton prevailed to head a McLaren 1-2.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The Red Bull collision fractured team relations between Webber, Vettel, and management for years.
