## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Fernando Alonso (Ferrari)
- **Pole Position:** Sebastian Vettel (Red Bull-Renault) - 1:54.101
- **Fastest Lap:** Fernando Alonso (Ferrari) - 1:58.287 (lap 45)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic debut of the 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 points system and total ban on in-race refuelling. Fernando Alonso became the sixth driver in Ferrari history to win on his debut for the Scuderia, leading Felipe Massa in an emotional 1-2. Sebastian Vettel dominated from pole until a spark plug failure crippled his engine on lap 34, dropping him to 4th. 7-time World Champion Michael Schumacher returned from three years of retirement, finishing 6th for Mercedes GP.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The bumpy 6.299 km 'Endurance layout' was heavily criticized for boring racing and was permanently abandoned for the original layout in subsequent years.
