## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Mark Webber entered the history books with a supreme Monaco Grand Slam (pole, win, led every lap), surviving four separate Safety Car periods. At the finish, Webber jumped into the Red Bull Energy Station swimming pool in full overalls. On the final corner of the final lap as the Safety Car pulled into the pitlane, Michael Schumacher overtook Fernando Alonso at Rascasse for 6th; stewards controversially ruled the race finished under Safety Car conditions and handed Schumacher a 20-second penalty.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Schumacher's 20-second penalty for passing Alonso under green flags on the final corner prompted a clarification of safety car restart rules.
