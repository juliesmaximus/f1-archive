## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The Miracle of Abu Dhabi. Four drivers entered with a mathematical shot at the title: Alonso (246), Webber (238), Vettel (231), and Hamilton (222). Vettel dominated from pole. On lap 11, Webber pitted after brushing the barrier; Ferrari reacted by pitting Alonso on lap 15 to cover Webber. It was a catastrophic strategic blunder: Alonso rejoined in traffic behind Vitaly Petrov's Renault. For 40 agonizing laps, Alonso attacked Petrov furiously, but the Renault's superior straight-line speed held Alonso at bay in 7th. Sebastian Vettel won the race and was told over team radio: 'Du bist Weltmeister!' At 23 years, 134 days, Sebastian Vettel became the youngest World Drivers' Champion in Formula 1 history, having not led the championship at any single point prior to the checkered flag!

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari's strategy blunder cost Alonso the world title, resulting in head of race engineering Chris Dyer losing his post.
