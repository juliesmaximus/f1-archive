## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton produced a masterclass in treacherous conditions, surviving a sudden rain shower on lap 35 where he slid off into the Rivage gravel trap, gently brushing the tyre wall before rejoining. Sebastian Vettel lost control entering the Bus Stop chicane and torpedoed directly into Jenson Button's sidepod, destroying Button's radiator and ending his race. Hamilton's win moved him back to the top of the standings.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Vettel received a drive-through penalty and was branded a 'crash kid' by McLaren's Martin Whitmarsh.
