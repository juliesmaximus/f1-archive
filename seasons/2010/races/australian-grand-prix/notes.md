## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Masterclass in tyre strategy. Defending champion Jenson Button made a bold gamble to pit for slick tyres on lap 6 on a damp track. Despite sliding off at Turn 3 on his out-lap, the gamble paid off spectacularly as track conditions dried, giving Button the lead when Vettel suffered a front wheel failure. Button controlled the race to score his maiden win for McLaren ahead of Robert Kubica and Felipe Massa.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton and Webber collided in the closing laps battling for 5th, triggering an angry post-race radio from Hamilton questioning McLaren's pit strategy.
