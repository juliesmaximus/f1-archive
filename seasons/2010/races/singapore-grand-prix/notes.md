## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso scored a legendary Grand Slam: pole, win, fastest lap, and led every single lap under relentless pressure from Sebastian Vettel for 61 laps, crossing the line just 0.293 seconds ahead! Lewis Hamilton retired following a high-speed collision with Mark Webber at Turn 7 after a Safety Car restart. Heikki Kovalainen's Lotus burst into flames on the pit straight; Kovalainen grabbed a fire extinguisher from marshals and put out the fire himself.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Stewards cleared Webber of wrongdoing in the collision with Hamilton.
