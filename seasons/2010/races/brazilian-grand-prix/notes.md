## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Rookie Nico Hülkenberg stunned the paddock by taking a miraculous pole position for Williams on a drying track. In the race, Sebastian Vettel and Mark Webber blew past Hülkenberg on lap 1 to score a commanding 1-2 finish, clinching Red Bull Racing's maiden FIA Formula 1 World Constructors' Championship! Alonso finished 3rd, taking an 8-point lead over Webber and 15 over Vettel into the Abu Dhabi showdown.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Red Bull refused to impose team orders to swap Vettel and Webber, setting up a four-way title finale.
