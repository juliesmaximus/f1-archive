## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Debut of the new 5.891 km Silverstone 'Arena' layout. Deep tension erupted at Red Bull when Christian Horner removed the only remaining new-spec front wing from Webber's car and gave it to Vettel for qualifying. On Sunday, Webber swept past pole-sitter Vettel into Copse at the start, as Vettel suffered a puncture after running wide, and dominated the race to win. On the cool-down lap, Webber delivered his immortal radio line: 'Not bad for a number two driver, cheers!'

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Webber's 'number two driver' comments exposed the bitter internal favoritism debate inside Red Bull.
