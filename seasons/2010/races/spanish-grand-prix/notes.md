## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Mark Webber delivered an untouchable lights-to-flag victory, winning by 24 seconds. On the penultimate lap, Lewis Hamilton suffered a sudden front-left wheel rim failure at 150 mph into Turn 3, crashing heavily out of 2nd place and promoting Fernando Alonso to 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Red Bull's blown diffuser and extreme qualifying downforce stunned rivals, Webber beating Hamilton's McLaren by nearly a full second in qualifying.
