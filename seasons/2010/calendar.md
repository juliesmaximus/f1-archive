## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Refuelling Banned:** Cars raced without mid-race refuelling for the first time since 1993.
- **25-Point System:** 25 points for 1st down to 1 point for 10th.
- **F-Duct Innovations:** Cockpit-controlled aerodynamic stall system pioneered by McLaren.
- **Blown Diffuser:** Red Bull pioneered routing exhaust gases through the diffuser floor for continuous downforce.
