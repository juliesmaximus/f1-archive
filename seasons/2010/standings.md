## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Sebastian Vettel Youngest World Champion:** At 23 years, 134 days, Sebastian Vettel won his maiden World Championship, clinching it in a dramatic 4-way finale in Abu Dhabi. Vettel led the championship for the first time only after the checkered flag at the final round.
- **Red Bull Racing Maiden Constructors' Title:** Adrian Newey's RB6 delivered Red Bull their first World Constructors' Championship, clinching the title at the penultimate round in Brazil with a 1-2 finish.
- **Permanent Ban on Refuelling:** Fuel stops were banned for the first time since 1993; cars started races on full fuel tanks (~160 kg) with mandatory tire compound changes during pit stops.
- **New 25-Point System:** The 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 points scale was introduced for the top 10 finishers to accommodate larger grids and reward race victories.
- **Michael Schumacher Comeback:** Seven-time World Champion Michael Schumacher returned to Formula 1 with the newly formed Mercedes GP factory team alongside Nico Rosberg.
- **Historic Four-Way Title Decider:** The championship came down to the final round in Abu Dhabi with four mathematical contenders: Fernando Alonso (246 pts), Mark Webber (238 pts), Sebastian Vettel (231 pts), and Lewis Hamilton (222 pts). Vettel won the race from pole while Alonso and Webber were trapped behind Vitaly Petrov's Renault, securing the title for Vettel by 4 points (256 to 252).
