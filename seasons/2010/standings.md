## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Sebastian Vettel Youngest World Champion:** At 23 years, 134 days, Sebastian Vettel won his maiden World Championship, clinching it in a dramatic 4-way finale in Abu Dhabi.
- **Red Bull Racing Maiden Constructors' Title:** Adrian Newey's RB6 delivered Red Bull their first World Constructors' Championship.
- **Permanent Ban on Refuelling:** Fuel stops banned forever; cars started on 160 kg fuel loads.
- **New 25-Point System:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 system inaugurated for top 10 finishers.
- **Michael Schumacher Comeback:** 7-time World Champion returned with Mercedes GP.
