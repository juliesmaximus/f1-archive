## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Vukovich Back-to-Back Indy 500 Wins: Bill Vukovich in the Fuel Injection Special Kurtis Kraft-Offenhauser won for the second consecutive year, leading 90 laps and winning by over a minute.
- First 140+ mph Qualifying: Jack McGrath became the first driver to qualify at an average speed over 140 mph (141.033 mph), taking pole position and setting fastest lap.
- Shared Drives: Mike Nazaruk, Art Cross, Johnnie Parsons, and Sam Hanks all completed shared drives among top-10 finishers.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Bill Vukovich became only the third driver to win back-to-back Indianapolis 500 races (after Wilbur Shaw and Mauri Rose).
