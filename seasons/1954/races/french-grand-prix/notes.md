- **Mercedes-Benz W196 Historic Debut**: 40 years to the day after Christian Lautenschlager won the 1914 French GP for Mercedes, the factory Silver Arrows returned to Grand Prix racing with the revolutionary W196 'Stromlinienwagen' (featuring a desmodromic valve straight-8 engine, mechanical direct fuel injection, and enclosed aerodynamic bodywork).
- **Sensational 1-2 Finish**: Fangio and teammate Karl Kling ran together in formation from start to finish, staging a photo-finish where Fangio edged Kling by a mere tenth of a second (0.100s).
- **Hans Herrmann Fastest Lap**: Third factory driver Hans Herrmann set the fastest lap of the race (2:32.900) before retiring with engine trouble, giving Mercedes a clean sweep of pole, fastest lap, and 1-2 victory on their debut.
