## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Sensational Debut of the Lancia D50: Vittorio Jano's radical Lancia D50 made its long-awaited debut featuring a 90° V8 engine integrated into the chassis and pannier fuel tanks between the wheels. Alberto Ascari stunned the field by taking pole position by 1.0 second.
- Ascari led the opening 9 laps before clutch failure forced him out.
- Hawthorn Victory in 'Squalo': Mike Hawthorn drove Ferrari's new 553 'Squalo' to a magnificent victory, withstanding fierce pressure.
- Mercedes Debris Overheating: Mercedes radiators were clogged by dry autumn leaves blowing across the Pedralbes streets; Fangio suffered severe overheating and barely coaxed his car home in 3rd.
