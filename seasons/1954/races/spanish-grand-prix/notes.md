## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Lancia D50 Sensational Debut: Vittorio Jano's masterpiece, the Lancia D50 (featuring revolutionary outboard pannier fuel tanks between the wheels and a stressed engine chassis), made its long-awaited debut. Alberto Ascari stunned the paddock by taking pole position (2:18.1) and leading the opening 9 laps before clutch failure forced his retirement.
- Debris Clogs Mercedes Radiators: Political propaganda paper leaflets were dropped from an aeroplane over Barcelona, littering the track. The paper was sucked into the air intakes and radiators of the Mercedes W196s, causing severe overheating. Fangio dropped to 3rd, and Kling retired.
- Hawthorn Ferrari Victory: Mike Hawthorn drove a flawless race in his Ferrari 625 to win by 1 minute 13 seconds over Luigi Musso's Maserati.
- Final Pedralbes Grand Prix: Pedralbes was permanently retired as a racing circuit following this event due to safety concerns and post-1955 street circuit prohibitions.
