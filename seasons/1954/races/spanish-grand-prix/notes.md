## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Lancia D50 Debut Stunner**: Vittorio Jano's masterpiece, the Lancia D50 (with pannier fuel tanks and stressed V8 engine), made its long-awaited debut. Alberto Ascari captured pole position (2:18.100), set fastest race lap (2:20.400), and led the first 9 laps before clutch failure forced his retirement.
- **Debris Clogs Mercedes Radiators**: Political leaflets dropped by plane over Barcelona littered the street track; the papers were sucked into the cooling intakes and radiators of the Mercedes W196s, causing severe overheating. Kling retired, and Fangio nursed his car to 3rd (+1 lap).
- **Hawthorn Win & Musso Maiden Podium**: Mike Hawthorn drove steadily to win by 1 minute 13 seconds, while Luigi Musso scored his first career World Championship podium in 2nd place.
- **Final Pedralbes Grand Prix**: Following safety revisions and the 1955 Le Mans disaster, the Pedralbes street circuit was permanently retired.
