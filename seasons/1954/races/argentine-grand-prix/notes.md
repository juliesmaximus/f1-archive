# 1954 Round 1: II Gran Premio de la República Argentina - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: II Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Historic Name Notes**: 2nd Argentine Grand Prix; inaugural championship race of the 2.5-litre Formula One engine regulations and debut of the Maserati 250F.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre
- **Location**: Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Host Country**: Argentina
- **Track Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 10 m (33 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 87 laps / 340.344 km (211.480 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1954-01-17

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Intense summer heat, sudden heavy rain squall on lap 30, followed by rapidly drying track conditions.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Ferrari 625) – 1:44.800
- **Fastest Race Lap**: José Froilán González (Ferrari 625) – 1:48.200 (Lap 46) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) – 3:00:55.800 (8 pts)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) – 3:00:55.800 [8 pts]

- **3rd** : José Froilán González (Ferrari 625) – +2:01.000 (+121.000s) [5 pts: 4 for 3rd + 1 FL]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **2.5-Litre Formula One Engine Era Inaugurated**: Replaced the 2.0-litre Formula Two rules with a maximum engine displacement of 2,500cc unblown or 750cc supercharged.
- **Maserati 250F Debut Victory**: Juan Manuel Fangio delivered a debut victory for the iconic Maserati 250F, skilfully managing tyres across changing dry-wet-dry conditions.
- **Ferrari Protest Dismissed**: When rain fell on lap 30, Fangio pitted for treaded tyres. Ferrari protested that five Maserati mechanics had worked on Fangio's car rather than the permitted three. Race stewards reviewed the pit stops, noted equivalent irregularities in other pit stalls, and confirmed Fangio's victory.
- **Farina and González Double Podium**: Scuderia Ferrari took 2nd (Farina) and 3rd (González).
