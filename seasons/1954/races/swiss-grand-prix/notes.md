# 1954 Round 7: XIV Großer Preis der Schweiz / XIV Grand Prix de Suisse - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XIV Großer Preis der Schweiz / XIV Grand Prix de Suisse
- **Historic Name Notes**: 14th Swiss Grand Prix; title decider for the 1954 championship; the final Swiss Grand Prix in Formula One World Championship history.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit Bremgarten
- **Location**: Bremgarten bei Bern, Canton of Bern, Switzerland
- **Host Country**: Switzerland
- **Track Length**: 7.280 km (4.524 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 550 m (1804 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 66 laps / 480.480 km (298.556 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1954-08-22

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Rain at start, wet and greasy tree-lined track, drying late in the race.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Fangio Clinches 2nd World Championship**: Juan Manuel Fangio drove a commanding race in wet and variable conditions, winning by 57.8 seconds over José Froilán González to mathematically clinch his SECOND WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP with two rounds remaining.
- **Title Won with Two Different Constructors**: Fangio achieved the historic feat of winning the World Championship after scoring points with two different constructors in the same season (Maserati for rounds 1 & 3; Mercedes-Benz for rounds 4–7).
- **Final Swiss Grand Prix**: This proved to be the final Formula One World Championship Grand Prix held on Swiss soil. Following the 1955 Le Mans disaster, the Swiss Federal government enacted a ban on all circuit motor racing in Switzerland.
- **Hans Herrmann Podium**: Mercedes driver Hans Herrmann finished 3rd (+1 lap), securing his first career World Championship podium.
