# 1954 Round 6: XVII Großer Preis von Deutschland / Grand Prix d'Europe - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XVII Großer Preis von Deutschland / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Historic Name Notes**: 17th German Grand Prix; designated the honorary 'Grand Prix d'Europe' for 1954; held around the 22.810 km Nürburgring Nordschleife.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Nürburgring Nordschleife
- **Location**: Nürburg, Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany
- **Host Country**: Germany
- **Track Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 620 m (2034 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 22 laps / 501.820 km (311.816 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1954-08-01

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Overcast, cool, and dry with cloudy skies over the Eifel mountains.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Tragic Loss of Onofre Marimón**: During Saturday practice on 31 July 1954, 30-year-old Argentine driver Onofre Marimón suffered brake failure going down the hill toward Wehrseifen, crashing through the hedge and down the slope. Marimón was tragically killed, becoming the FIRST DRIVER FATALITY DURING AN OFFICIAL FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP WEEKEND. Grief-stricken compatriots Fangio and González raced in his honor.
- **Mercedes Open-Wheel Debut**: Mercedes debuted the open-wheel ('Monoposto') variant of the W196, allowing Fangio to see the wheels on the twisty Nordschleife.
- **Fangio Marathon Victory**: Fangio drove the entire grueling 3-hour 45-minute race solo, winning by over 1.5 minutes over the shared Ferrari of González and Hawthorn.
