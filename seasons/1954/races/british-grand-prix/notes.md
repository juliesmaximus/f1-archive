# 1954 Round 5: VII RAC British Grand Prix - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: VII RAC British Grand Prix
- **Historic Name Notes**: 7th running of the RAC British Grand Prix at Silverstone; famous for the unique seven-way tie for fastest lap.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Silverstone Circuit
- **Location**: Silverstone, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom
- **Host Country**: United Kingdom
- **Track Length**: 4.711 km (2.927 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 150 m (492 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 90 laps / 423.990 km (263.455 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1954-07-17

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Rain showers before and during the race; greasy, tricky track conditions on the exposed airfield course.

- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Mercedes-Benz W196) – 1:45.000
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Alberto Ascari, Jean Behra, Juan Manuel Fangio, José Froilán González, Mike Hawthorn, Onofre Marimón, and Stirling Moss – 1:50.000 [shared: 1/7 pt = 0.14 pts each]
- **Race Winner**: José Froilán González (Ferrari 625) – 2:56:14.000 (8.14 pts: 8 win + 0.14 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : José Froilán González (Ferrari 625) – 2:56:14.000 [8.14 pts]
- **2nd** : Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari 625) – +1:10.000 (+70.000s) [6.14 pts]
- **3rd** : Onofre Marimón (Maserati 250F) – +1 lap [4.14 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Historic Seven-Way Tie for Fastest Lap**: Because hand-held stopwatches were only accurate to the nearest second, seven different drivers were officially timed at 1:50.000 for the fastest race lap. Under FIA regulations, the 1 bonus point was split equally, awarding 1/7 of a point (0.14 points) each to Ascari, Behra, Fangio, González, Hawthorn, Marimón, and Moss.
- **Ferrari 1-2 Masterclass**: José Froilán González repeated his 1951 triumph at Silverstone, leading from lap 1 to take his 2nd career Grand Prix victory by over a minute over teammate Mike Hawthorn.
- **Mercedes Streamliner Struggles on Cornering**: The enclosed streamlined Mercedes W196 struggled on the open curves of Silverstone. Fangio repeatedly clipped the oil drums marking the corner apexes, denting the front bodywork and finishing 4th (+1 lap).
