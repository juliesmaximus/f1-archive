## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Historic 7-Way Fastest Lap Point Split: Seven different drivers recorded an identical fastest race lap time of 1 minute 50.0 seconds due to hand-operated stopwatches timing only to whole seconds. The single championship bonus point was split equally seven ways: each driver was awarded 1/7 of a point (0.14 points)!
- Mercedes Streamliner Struggled: The bulky streamlined bodywork of the W196 proved difficult to place precisely on the windy, unbanked Silverstone airfield corners. Fangio repeatedly brushed the marker oil drums, damaging the front bodywork and finishing 4th.
- González Second Silverstone Win: José Froilán González dominated the race in his Ferrari 625, scoring his second career British Grand Prix victory by 1 minute 10 seconds over Hawthorn.

## Records Set or Equalled
- All-time record: 7 drivers sharing the fastest lap bonus point in a single Grand Prix (0.14 pts each).
- Second career Grand Prix victory for José Froilán González.
