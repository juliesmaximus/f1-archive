## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The Historic 7-Way Shared Fastest Lap Point: In an unprecedented quirk of 1950s hand-held manual stopwatch timing (measured only to the nearest integer second), seven drivers set an identical fastest lap of 1:50.0: Alberto Ascari, José Froilán González, Mike Hawthorn, Juan Manuel Fangio, Stirling Moss, Jean Behra, and Onofre Marimón. The FIA divided the single fastest lap point equally: each driver received 1/7 of a point (0.14 pts).
- Streamliner Struggles: Mercedes ran the streamlined full-bodied W196 cars; on the airfield circuit with oil-drum markers, Fangio could not see his front wheels, clobbered several barrels, dented the aluminium bodywork, and finished 4th.
- Ferrari 1-2: González scored his second Silverstone victory, leading Hawthorn home.
