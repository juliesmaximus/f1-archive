# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1985 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Alain Prost became the first French driver in Formula One history to win the FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship, scoring 73 net points (76 gross, 5 victories, 11 podiums) in the McLaren MP4/2B TAG-Porsche. Michele Alboreto finished as runner-up with 53 points for Scuderia Ferrari.

Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers in each Grand Prix. The best 11 results across the 16 rounds counted toward the Drivers' Championship.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

McLaren International captured their third World Constructors' Championship with 90 points, winning 6 Grands Prix with Alain Prost and Niki Lauda in the McLaren MP4/2B TAG-Porsche. Scuderia Ferrari finished runner-up with 82 points.

Under FIA regulations, chassis-engine combinations were scored as separate makes in the International Cup for F1 Constructors. All rounds and all finishes counted toward the Constructors' Championship.
