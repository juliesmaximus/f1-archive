## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST OPENS TITLE CRUSADE & ARNOUX FERRARI FAREWELL: Michele Alboreto scored pole position for Ferrari, but Alain Prost took control in the updated McLaren MP4/2B TAG-Porsche, winning by 3.2 seconds over Alboreto and Elio de Angelis (Lotus-Renault). Following the race, Ferrari abruptly and mysteriously terminated René Arnoux's contract (who finished 4th), replacing him with Swedish driver Stefan Johansson for the remainder of the season. Ayrton Senna retired from 3rd on lap 48 with an electrical failure in his Team Lotus debut.
