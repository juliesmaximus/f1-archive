## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ROSBERG'S STREET WAR ON HONDA POWER: In scorching heat on the concrete-lined streets of Detroit, polesitter Ayrton Senna battled severe tire degradation, hitting the wall on lap 51 while trying to chase down leader Keke Rosberg. Rosberg drove a ferocious, fearless race in the recalcitrant Williams-Honda FW10, controlling brake and tire temperatures to score Honda's first victory of the 1985 season by 28.0 seconds over Stefan Johansson (Ferrari) and Michele Alboreto.
