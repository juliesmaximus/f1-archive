## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S IMMORTAL ESTORIL MASTERPIECE: In blinding, monsoon torrential rain on flooded asphalt, 25-year-old Ayrton Senna delivered one of the greatest driving exhibitions in the history of Formula One. Starting from his maiden pole position in the Lotus 97T Renault, Senna dominated in a league of his own, sliding car control on the ragged edge through standing water while champions like Prost spun off on the main straight. Senna led every single lap and set fastest lap, lapping the entire field up to 2nd-place Michele Alboreto, whom he beat by a staggering 1 minute and 2.9 seconds. It was Senna's maiden World Championship Grand Prix victory.
