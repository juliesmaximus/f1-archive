## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S BIBLICAL MAIDEN VICTORY: In conditions so severe that drivers pleaded for the race to be stopped, 25-year-old Ayrton Senna started from his maiden pole position in the Lotus 97T Renault. Putting on a spellbinding display of supernatural car control, Senna was in a class of his own, sliding through rivers of standing water to claim his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory**. Senna won by **1 minute 2.97 seconds**, lapping the entire field up to 2nd place (Michele Alboreto) and achieving a Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led all 67 laps). Alain Prost spun out on the straight, and Niki Lauda retired with engine failure.
