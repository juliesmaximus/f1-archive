## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE FUEL-OUT MAYHEM & PROST DISQUALIFICATION: Ayrton Senna claimed pole and led for 47 laps until his Lotus ran completely out of fuel. Stefan Johansson in only his second race for Ferrari thrilled the crowd by charging from 15th to take the lead, only to run out of fuel half a lap later! Alain Prost crossed the line 1st, but ran out of fuel on the slowing-down lap. In post-race scrutineering, Prost's McLaren MP4/2B weighed in at 538 kg—2 kg below the mandatory 540 kg minimum limit. Prost was disqualified, handing victory to Elio de Angelis in the Lotus-Renault ahead of Thierry Boutsen (Arrows, who pushed his out-of-fuel car across the line for 2nd!).
