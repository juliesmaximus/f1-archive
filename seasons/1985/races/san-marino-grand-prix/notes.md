## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE IMOLA FUEL DRAMA & PROST UNDERWEIGHT DISQUALIFICATION: Regarded as the wildest fuel lottery race of the turbo era. Ayrton Senna led from pole until running completely out of fuel on lap 57. Stefan Johansson, in only his second race for Ferrari, thrilled the Italian crowd by charging from 15th into the lead, only to sputter to a halt out of fuel one lap later! Alain Prost crossed the line 1st in the McLaren MP4/2B, but his engine died on the slow-down lap. At post-race scrutineering, Prost's car weighed 538 kg—2 kg below the 540 kg minimum weight limit because all fuel and coolant had run dry. Prost was disqualified, and Elio de Angelis in the Lotus 97T was awarded the victory ahead of Thierry Boutsen (who pushed his fuel-starved Arrows across the line for 2nd) and Patrick Tambay.
