## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL'S MAIDEN WIN & PROST'S FIRST WORLD TITLE: A historic day for Formula One. In his 72nd Grand Prix start, British lion Nigel Mansell drove an inspired, magnificent race in the Williams FW10 Honda, taking his long-awaited maiden Grand Prix victory by 21.3 seconds over Ayrton Senna and Keke Rosberg. Meanwhile, Michele Alboreto's Ferrari burst into flames on lap 13 (Alboreto famously driving the flaming car into the pit lane to show team bosses the engine fragility). Alain Prost drove a calm, mature race to finish 4th, securing the 3 points needed to officially become France's first Formula One World Drivers' Champion.
