## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALBORETO WINS AT THE NÜRBURGRING: Teo Fabi claimed a sensational maiden pole position for Toleman-Hart. In the race, Michele Alboreto delivered a champion's drive in the Ferrari 156/85, overcoming a first-corner clash with teammate Johansson to hunt down and pass Alain Prost for the victory by 11.6 seconds. This victory gave Alboreto a five-point lead in the World Championship; tragically, it would prove to be Alboreto's final Formula One career win and Ferrari's last win until 1987.
