## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ROSBERG'S 160 MPH QUALIFYING LAP & PROST VICTORY: During Saturday qualifying, Keke Rosberg wrestled his 1,100 bhp Williams-Honda through the high-speed Silverstone curves on a damp track with a deflating rear tire, clocking an astonishing lap of 1:05.591 at an average speed of 160.9 mph (258.9 km/h)—the fastest lap in Formula One history for 16 years. In the race, Senna led early until his Renault engine stuttered out of fuel on lap 58.
