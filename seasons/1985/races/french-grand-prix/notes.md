## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET & PIRELLI'S MISTRAL MASTERPIECE: Down the 1.1-mile Mistral straight, the 1,100+ bhp turbo engines hit unprecedented top speeds exceeding 210 mph (338 km/h). Nelson Piquet and Brabham-BMW capitalized on special heat-resistant Pirelli qualifying and race tires. Piquet passed Keke Rosberg on lap 11 and pulled away to score Brabham's sole victory of 1985 by 6.6 seconds over Rosberg and Alain Prost.
