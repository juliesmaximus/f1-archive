## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
BRABHAM-BMW MONSTER SPEED & PIQUET WIN: Keke Rosberg took pole in the Williams-Honda at 155 mph. During qualifying, Marc Surer's Brabham-BMW turbo was clocked at an astronomical **338 km/h (210 mph)** down the Mistral Straight. In the race, Nelson Piquet in the Brabham BT54 BMW turbo took the lead on lap 11 and pulled away on durable Pirelli radial tires to score Brabham's and Pirelli's only victory of the season by 6.66 seconds over Keke Rosberg and Alain Prost.
