## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
FERRARI 1-2 IN MONTREAL: Elio de Angelis led from pole in his Lotus-Renault, but Michele Alboreto paced his Ferrari 156/85 brilliantly, taking the lead on lap 16. Teammate Stefan Johansson charged through from 4th into 2nd, and Ferrari held formation to score an emotional 1-2 finish—Ferrari's first 1-2 since Zandvoort 1983. The victory vaulted Alboreto into the lead of the World Championship.
