## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
INAUGURAL ADELAIDE CLASSIC & ROSBERG FAREWELL WILLIAMS WIN: Formula One made its debut in Adelaide on a superb parkland street circuit, creating an instant classic watched by over 110,000 passionate spectators. Ayrton Senna took pole and fought an epic duel with Keke Rosberg, colliding with Rosberg's Williams on lap 62 and destroying his front wing before retiring with engine failure. In his final race for Williams before moving to McLaren, Keke Rosberg drove a blistering race to claim victory by 43.1 seconds over the Ligier-Renaults of Jacques Laffite and Philippe Streiff (who famously collided on the final lap at the hairpin but limped home 2nd and 3rd). McLaren sealed the World Constructors' Championship.
