## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
INバUGURAL ADELAIDE GP & ROSBERG'S FINAL WIN: Formula One visited Australia for the first time in World Championship history on the magnificent Adelaide parkland street circuit. Ayrton Senna took pole for Lotus. In brutal 35°C heat, Keke Rosberg in his final race for Williams drove an aggressive, swashbuckling race, surviving a collision with Senna to take victory by 43.13 seconds over Jacques Laffite and Philippe Streiff in the Ligiers! Niki Lauda led in his final career Grand Prix before a brake failure spun him into the wall on lap 57. This marked **Keke Rosberg's 5th and final career Formula One victory**.
