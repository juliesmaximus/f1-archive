## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
NIKI LAUDA'S 25TH & FINAL VICTORY: Nelson Piquet took pole for Brabham. Niki Lauda started 10th in the McLaren MP4/2B and pitted early for fresh tires on lap 19. When Alain Prost pitted later, Lauda assumed the lead. Over the final 12 laps, Prost closed onto Lauda's rear gearbox; despite a severe handling imbalance and worn tires, Lauda positioned his car with surgical defense to take his **25th and final career Formula One victory by just 0.232 seconds** over Prost, with Ayrton Senna 3rd! Michele Alboreto finished 4th. This was the **final Dutch Grand Prix until 2021**.
