## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LAUDA'S 25TH & FINAL VICTORY IN HISTORIC DUEL: The final Dutch Grand Prix until 2021 produced a legendary masterclass from Niki Lauda. Starting 10th on the grid, Lauda took the lead on lap 34 after teammate Alain Prost suffered a slow pit stop. In the closing 12 laps, Prost chased down Lauda on fresh tires, filling Lauda's mirrors and trying repeatedly to pass around the outside at Tarzan. Lauda placed his car with inch-perfect precision, holding off Prost by just 0.232 seconds to claim his 25th and final Grand Prix victory. Ayrton Senna finished 3rd.
