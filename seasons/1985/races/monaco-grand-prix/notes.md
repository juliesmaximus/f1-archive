## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST MASTERS MONACO AS SENNA BLOWS TURBO: Ayrton Senna took pole and led early until his Renault turbo exploded in smoke on lap 13. Michele Alboreto took the lead for Ferrari but slipped on oil spilled from Nelson Piquet's blown engine at Sainte Dévote, dropping behind Alain Prost. Alboreto fought back brilliantly to re-pass Prost, but suffered a punctured rear tire that forced him into the pits. Prost drove an immaculate, mistake-free race to win by 7.5 seconds over Alboreto and Elio de Angelis.
