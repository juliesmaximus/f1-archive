## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST'S MONACO REVENGE: Ayrton Senna claimed pole for Lotus and dominated the opening 13 laps until his Renault turbo engine exploded into Sainte Dévote. Michele Alboreto took the lead for Ferrari, but slipped on oil at Sainte Dévote, allowing Alain Prost in the McLaren MP4/2B to sweep past. Alboreto suffered a puncture and charged back, but Prost drove with calculated restraint to win by 7.54 seconds over Alboreto and Elio de Angelis.
