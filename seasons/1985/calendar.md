# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1985 - Calendar

The 1985 Formula One season was the 36th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 championship rounds between April 7 and November 3, 1985.

The 1985 season was an era-defining championship marked by national triumphs, the rise of a new global icon, and the ultimate peak of the 1,000+ bhp turbo monsters:
- **Alain Prost: France's First World Champion:** After three agonizing runner-up finishes, "The Professor" Alain Prost captured his **maiden FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship** driving the McLaren MP4/2B TAG-Porsche, winning 5 Grands Prix and scoring 73 net points (76 gross) to become France's first-ever Formula One World Champion. McLaren International captured back-to-back **World Constructors' Championships** (90 points).
- **Ayrton Senna's Arrival as a Global Superpower:** Ayrton Senna moved to Team Lotus, scoring his **maiden Grand Prix victory** in torrential rain at Estoril (Portuguese GP) by over a minute, winning at Spa-Francorchamps, and securing an astonishing **7 pole positions** in the Lotus 97T Renault.
- **Michele Alboreto & Ferrari's Title Challenge:** Michele Alboreto led the World Championship midway through the season for Scuderia Ferrari in the 156/85, winning in Canada and Germany before late-season turbo unreliability cost him the title.
- **The 160 MPH Lap at Silverstone:** Keke Rosberg drove the 1,100 bhp Williams-Honda FW10 to set the fastest qualifying lap in Formula One history at **160.9 mph (258.9 km/h)** at Silverstone (1:05.591).
- **Niki Lauda's 25th & Final Victory:** Triple World Champion Niki Lauda scored his 25th and final career Formula One victory at Zandvoort (Dutch GP), resisting furious pressure from teammate Alain Prost by just 0.232 seconds.
- **Inaugural Australian Grand Prix in Adelaide:** Formula One traveled to Adelaide for the first Australian Grand Prix in World Championship history, creating an instant classic street race won by Keke Rosberg.
