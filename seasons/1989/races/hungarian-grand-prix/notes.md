## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Nigel Mansell's greatest career drives. Qualifying only 12th on the notoriously tight circuit, Mansell carved his way through the field. On lap 58, as Senna was boxed in behind Stefan Johansson's Onyx coming over the crest into Turn 3, Mansell made a daring lunge past both cars into the lead in a single breath-taking manoeuvre. Mansell won from 12th on the grid, with Senna 2nd and Thierry Boutsen 3rd.
