## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL'S MASTERCLASS FROM 12TH ON THE GRID: Nigel Mansell qualified a lowly 12th on the tight, twisty Hungaroring, where passing was considered impossible. Mansell drove an astonishing, ferocious race, slicing through the pack on hard tires. By lap 53, he was right on Ayrton Senna's tail. On lap 58 coming out of Turn 3, as Senna hesitated behind Stefan Johansson's Onyx to lap him, Mansell instantly feinted right, darted into the narrowing gap, and swept around Senna into the lead! Mansell pulled away to take one of his greatest victories by 25.9 seconds over Senna and Thierry Boutsen.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mansell's 12th-to-1st charge and ambush of Senna is universally celebrated as one of the greatest race drives in F1 history.
