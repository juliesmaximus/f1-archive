## Technical & Sporting Regulations
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 classified finishers.
- **Championship Count Rule:** Best 11 results counted towards the Drivers' Championship; all results counted for the Constructors' Championship.
- **Specific Regulations:** Nigel Mansell and Alessandro Nannini were shown the black flag and disqualified on lap 3 for exiting the pit lane before the green flag.

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
BOUTSEN'S MAIDEN WIN & WILLIAMS-RENAULT 1-2: In atrocious wet conditions, Alain Prost retired on lap 2 with suspension failure. Ayrton Senna took the lead and was cruising to victory with a 30-second lead when his Honda V10 engine suddenly blew on lap 66 with only 3 laps to go! Thierry Boutsen swept into the lead to claim his maiden Formula One victory and Williams' first victory with Renault V10 power, leading teammate Riccardo Patrese in a Williams 1-2. Andrea de Cesaris scored a shock 3rd place for Dallara-Ford.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The historic first Formula One victory for the legendary Williams-Renault V10 partnership.
