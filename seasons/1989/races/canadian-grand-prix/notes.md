## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Chaotic wet race. Prost retired on lap 2 due to suspension failure. Senna dominated in the wet and led until lap 66 of 69, when his Honda V10 engine blew up. Thierry Boutsen swept past to take his maiden Formula 1 victory, leading teammate Riccardo Patrese home for an emphatic Williams-Renault 1-2. Andrea de Cesaris took 3rd for Dallara.
