## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL'S FAIRYTALE FERRARI PADDLE-SHIFT DEBUT: On the debut of John Barnard's revolutionary Ferrari 640 featuring steering wheel-mounted semi-automatic paddle shifters, Nigel Mansell scored a shock victory. At the start, pole-sitter Ayrton Senna was sandwiched between Gerhard Berger and Riccardo Patrese into Turn 1, breaking his front wing and sending Berger into retirement. Mansell led, pitted for tires and a new steering wheel when the quick-release loosened, and drove to victory by 7.8 seconds ahead of Alain Prost. Maurício Gugelmin finished 3rd for March-Judd on home soil.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The historic first-ever Formula One victory for a semi-automatic paddle-shift gearbox, revolutionizing motorsport transmissions.
