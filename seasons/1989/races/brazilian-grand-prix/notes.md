## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The first race of the mandatory 3.5-litre naturally aspirated formula. Ayrton Senna took pole but collided with Gerhard Berger and Riccardo Patrese into Turn 1, breaking his front wing. Nigel Mansell scored a fairy-tale debut victory in the Ferrari 640—the first Formula 1 car equipped with a semi-automatic sequential paddle-shift gearbox, an engineering revolution designed by John Barnard. Alain Prost finished 2nd in the McLaren-Honda, and Maurício Gugelmin scored a sensational podium for March-Judd.
