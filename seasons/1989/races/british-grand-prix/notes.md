## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Senna took pole and led until lap 11, when his gearbox jammed in second gear, causing him to spin off into the gravel trap at Becketts. Prost took over the lead and cruised to victory ahead of Nigel Mansell (Ferrari) and Alessandro Nannini (Benetton). With this win, Prost opened up a 20-point championship lead.
