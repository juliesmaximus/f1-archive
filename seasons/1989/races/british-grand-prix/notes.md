## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA SPINS OUT & PROST WINS BACK-TO-BACK: Ayrton Senna took pole and led early, but on lap 12 entering Becketts, his gearbox jammed and Senna spun into the gravel trap! Alain Prost inherited the lead and controlled the race to take victory by 19.3 seconds ahead of home hero Nigel Mansell in the Ferrari. Alessandro Nannini finished 3rd for Benetton.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
