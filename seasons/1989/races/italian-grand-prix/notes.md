## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA BLOWS UP & PROST DROPS TROPHY TO TIFOSI: Ayrton Senna took pole and led with authority until his Honda V10 suffered a massive engine blowout entering the Ascari chicane on lap 44, trailing thick smoke. Alain Prost swept through to win the race ahead of Gerhard Berger and Thierry Boutsen. On the podium, having announced his move to Ferrari for 1990, Prost dropped the coveted Italian GP winner's trophy down into the sea of ecstatic Tifosi below, infuriating McLaren team principal Ron Dennis!
