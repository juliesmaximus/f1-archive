## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Prost announced prior to the race that he would leave McLaren to join Ferrari for 1990. Senna took pole and led comfortably until lap 44, when his Honda V10 suffered an unexpected oil failure and expired in a plume of smoke at the Curva Grande. Prost swept through to win to the adoration of the Ferrari tifosi. On the podium, Prost dropped the winner's trophy to the crowd, enraging team boss Ron Dennis.
