## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA THREE-IN-A-ROW: Ayrton Senna claimed pole and controlled the race with supreme authority, choosing the hard Goodyear tire compound to run non-stop. Alain Prost gambled on soft tires, blistered them within 15 laps, and had to make two pit stops, dropping to 5th. Senna won by 15.5 seconds ahead of Riccardo Patrese (Williams-Renault) and Michele Alboreto in the Tyrrell-Ford.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Senna achieved his 3rd consecutive victory of 1989 and built a commanding championship lead.
