## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Senna took his third straight win of the season, leading from pole to flag. Prost made a poor tyre choice (soft compound) and was forced to pit twice, finishing 5th. Riccardo Patrese finished 2nd for Williams-Renault, with Michele Alboreto taking 3rd in the Tyrrell-Ford (the team's first podium since 1983).
