## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Controversial collision between Senna and Mansell. Mansell overshot the Ferrari pit box, reversed down the pit lane (strictly illegal), and rejoined. Stewards waved the black flag at Mansell, but he failed to heed it. On lap 48, Mansell attempted to pass Senna into Turn 1 and the two collided, eliminating Senna. Gerhard Berger won for Ferrari ahead of Prost and Stefan Johansson's Onyx. Senna was left furious as Prost extended his points advantage.
