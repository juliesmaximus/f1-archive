## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL BLACK FLAG SAGA & ONYX SHOCK PODIUM: Nigel Mansell overshot his pit box, selected reverse gear, and reversed in the pit lane—a strict violation of FIA rules. Officials displayed the black flag to Mansell, but he ignored it for three laps. On lap 48, Mansell attempted an aggressive pass on Ayrton Senna into Turn 1; the two collided, eliminating Senna on the spot! Gerhard Berger dominated to take Ferrari's first win since 1988 ahead of Alain Prost, while Stefan Johansson scored an astonishing 3rd place for minnow team Onyx-Ford. Pierluigi Martini in the Minardi led lap 40, becoming the first Minardi in F1 history to lead a Grand Prix lap.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mansell was suspended for the Spanish GP, while Senna's DNF handed Prost a golden opportunity to seal the title.
