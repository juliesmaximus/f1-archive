## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ADELAIDE DELUGE & BOUTSEN MASTERCLASS: In treacherous monsoon conditions, World Champion Alain Prost withdrew after completing the opening lap in protest over lethal track safety. Ayrton Senna led from pole, building a 30-second lead until blinding spray caused him to crash violently into the back of Martin Brundle's Brabham on lap 14! Thierry Boutsen mastered the aquaplaning track to win his second rain race of the year, finishing 28.6 seconds ahead of Alessandro Nannini and Riccardo Patrese. Satoru Nakajima set the fastest lap of the race in his Lotus-Judd and finished 4th.
