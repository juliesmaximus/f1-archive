## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Extreme monsoon conditions. Several drivers protested the start due to dangerous aquaplaning. The race went ahead; Prost withdrew after completing a single lap on safety grounds. Senna led from pole and pulled away at three seconds per lap until lap 13, when he blinded by spray slammed into the rear of Martin Brundle's Brabham, ripping off his front suspension. Thierry Boutsen drove a masterly wet race to win for Williams-Renault ahead of Alessandro Nannini and Riccardo Patrese.
