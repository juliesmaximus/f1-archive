## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Inaugural Phoenix street race. Senna took pole and led early until his Honda V10 suffered electrical misfires, forcing him out on lap 33. Alain Prost inherited the lead and drove a disciplined race in extreme heat to win ahead of Riccardo Patrese (Williams) and Eddie Cheever (Arrows).
