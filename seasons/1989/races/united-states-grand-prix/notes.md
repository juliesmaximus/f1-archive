## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST SURVIVES PHOENIX FURNACE: Ayrton Senna took pole and led by over a second per lap until electrical misfires crippled his Honda V10 on lap 34. Alain Prost inherited the lead and nursed his McLaren through the punishing 100°F heat to take victory ahead of Riccardo Patrese and American Eddie Cheever in the Arrows-Ford. Christian Danner finished 4th for Rial-Ford, scoring the team's best-ever result.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Prost capitalized on Senna's failure to claim his first win of the season and slash the championship deficit.
