## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
GUGELMIN BARREL ROLL & PROST WINS AS ALESI SHINES: At the start, Maurício Gugelmin suffered brake failure, plowed into the back of Mansell and Boutsen, and launched into a spectacular inverted barrel roll over Thierry Boutsen's rear wing! Miraculously unhurt, Gugelmin took the restart in the spare car. Senna retired on lap 1 of the restart with differential failure. Alain Prost controlled the race from pole to win his home Grand Prix ahead of Nigel Mansell and Riccardo Patrese. 25-year-old Jean Alesi made a stunning Formula One debut for Tyrrell, finishing 4th!

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Alain Prost reclaimed the World Championship lead while Jean Alesi announced his arrival on the world stage.
