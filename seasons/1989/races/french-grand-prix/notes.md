## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Dramatic multi-car crash on lap 1 when Maurício Gugelmin locked up and flew upside down over the pack into Turn 1, triggering a red flag. Gugelmin restarted in the spare car. Prost dominated the restart and won comfortably, while Senna suffered a differential failure on the restart lap and retired immediately. Nigel Mansell finished 2nd for Ferrari and Riccardo Patrese 3rd.
