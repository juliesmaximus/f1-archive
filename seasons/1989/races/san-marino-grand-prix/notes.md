## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The race that irreparably fractured the Senna-Prost partnership. On lap 4, Gerhard Berger suffered a catastrophic front wing failure at Tamburello at 180 mph, slamming the wall and erupting into a violent fireball. Marshals heroically extinguished the blaze in 16 seconds, saving Berger with moderate burns and broken ribs. Before the restart, Senna and Prost had a pre-race agreement that whoever led into Tosa would not be challenged on the first lap. Prost led into the corner, but Senna passed him under braking. Senna won comfortably, with Prost furious over what he viewed as betrayal.
