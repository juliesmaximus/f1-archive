## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
BERGER'S TAMBURELLO FIRE & THE BROKEN PACT: On lap 4, Gerhard Berger suffered front wing failure at Tamburello at 180 mph, smashing into the wall and becoming engulfed in a terrifying ball of fire! Marshals extinguished the flames in 16 seconds, saving Berger's life with only second-degree burns. Before the restart, Senna and Prost agreed that whoever led into Turn 1 would not be contested. Prost led the restart, but Senna slipstreamed past him into Tosa on the opening lap! Senna went on to win by 40.2 seconds over Prost, with Alessandro Nannini finishing 3rd. Prost was incensed by the betrayal, permanently destroying their relationship.
