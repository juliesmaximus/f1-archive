## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S REDEMPTION IN MONACO: Determined to banish the ghosts of his 1988 Portier crash, Ayrton Senna drove an imperious, laser-focused race from pole position, leading all 77 laps to defeat teammate Alain Prost by 52.5 seconds. Stefano Modena took an unexpected 3rd place for Brabham-Judd, the team's first podium since 1987.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Senna's dominant victory gave him back-to-back Monaco wins and established his total control of the championship lead.
