## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A dominant masterclass from Senna from pole position to redemption after his 1988 crash. Senna led all 77 laps, winning by 52.5 seconds over Prost, who was hindered by getting trapped behind backmarkers. Thierry Boutsen finished 3rd in the Williams-Renault.
