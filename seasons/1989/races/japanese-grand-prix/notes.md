## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The defining controversy of modern Formula 1. Prost led from the start from 2nd on the grid. Senna stalked him closely. On lap 46, Senna dived down the inside approaching the Casino Triangle chicane. Prost turned in early, and the two McLarens interlocked wheels and slid to a halt in the escape road. Prost unbuckled and retired, believing both championships were decided. Senna waved to marshals to bump-start his car down the escape road, entered the pits to replace a crumpled nosecone, rejoined, and passed Alessandro Nannini on lap 51 to cross the finish line first. Following the race, FISA President Jean-Marie Balestre and the stewards disqualified Senna for cutting the chicane. Alessandro Nannini was awarded his sole Formula 1 victory, and Alain Prost was officially crowned the 1989 World Drivers' Champion.
