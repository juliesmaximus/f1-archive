## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE CASINO CHICANE CLASH & PROST WDC: Ayrton Senna took pole, but Alain Prost made a superior start and led until lap 47. Entering the Casino Chicane, Senna launched an aggressive dive down the inside; Prost turned in early, and the two teammates collided, interlocked wheels, and slid into the chicane escape road! Prost abandoned his car. Senna waved to marshals for a push-start down the escape road, bump-started his car, pitted for a new nosecone, passed Alessandro Nannini on lap 51, and crossed the line first. However, FISA stewards disqualified Senna for missing the chicane, handing Alessandro Nannini his maiden Grand Prix victory and mathematically clinching the **1989 World Drivers' Championship for Alain Prost**!
