## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The Best 11 Rule Impact:** Alain Prost scored 105 total points across the season compared to Ayrton Senna's 94 total points. However, because only each driver's best 11 finishes counted toward the championship, Prost had to drop 18 points (three 2nd-place finishes) while Senna dropped only 4 points (one 6th-place finish). Consequently, Senna won his first World Championship by 90 points to 87.
- **The End of the Turbo Era:** 1988 was the final year of the 1.5-litre turbocharged engines that had dominated Formula 1 since 1983. Maximum turbo boost was restricted from 4.0 bar (1987) down to 2.5 bar, and fuel tank capacity was slashed from 195 litres to 150 litres. Despite these restrictions, the McLaren MP4/4 Honda twin-turbo won 15 out of 16 races.
- **McLaren's Near-Clean Sweep:** McLaren achieved an unprecedented 15 wins from 16 races (a 93.75% win rate that stood as an all-time record until Red Bull's 2023 campaign). The only race McLaren did not win was the Italian Grand Prix at Monza, won by Ferrari's Gerhard Berger.
- **Enzo Ferrari Tribute:** Ferrari's historic 1-2 at Monza came just 27 days after the passing of Il Commendatore, Enzo Ferrari, at age 90.
