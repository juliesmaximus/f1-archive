# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1988 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

The 1988 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship was decided by the best 11 results out of 16 rounds. Points were awarded to the top six finishers on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis.

Ayrton Senna won his maiden World Drivers' Championship driving the McLaren MP4/4 Honda Turbo, scoring 90 net points (94 gross) with 8 victories, 11 podiums, and 13 pole positions. Alain Prost scored more total points (105 gross, 7 victories, 14 podiums), but because only the best 11 results counted toward the championship, Prost dropped 18 points (three 2nd-place finishes) while Senna dropped only 4 points (one 6th-place finish). Consequently, Senna secured the championship by 3 points following his miraculous 14th-to-1st comeback victory at Suzuka.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

All results counted towards the World Constructors' Championship. McLaren International utterly dominated the season, winning 15 of 16 Grands Prix and scoring an all-time record 199 points, finishing 134 points clear of runner-up Scuderia Ferrari.
