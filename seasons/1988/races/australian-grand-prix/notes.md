## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE TURBO FINALE: Alain Prost passed pole-sitter Ayrton Senna into Turn 1 and dominated the Adelaide finale, taking his 7th victory of the year by 36.7 seconds over Senna to seal McLaren's 10th 1-2 finish of the season. Nelson Piquet finished 3rd for Lotus-Honda. Prost finished the season with 105 gross points to Senna's 94, but Senna took the crown with 90 net points to 87.
