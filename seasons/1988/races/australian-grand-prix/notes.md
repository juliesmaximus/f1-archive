## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The season finale in Adelaide and the final race of the 1.5-litre turbo era. Senna started from pole, but Prost took the lead at Turn 1 and dominated the race to take his 7th victory of the season. Senna finished 2nd, cementing McLaren's 10th 1-2 finish of the year. Prost finished the season with 105 total gross points compared to Senna's 94, but under the best 11 scores rule, Senna won the World Championship with 90 counted points to Prost's 87. Nelson Piquet finished 3rd in the Lotus-Honda.
