## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most famous qualifying sessions and races in F1 history. In qualifying, Senna delivered what he later described as an out-of-body spiritual experience, taking pole by 1.427 seconds over teammate Prost. In the race, Senna built an astonishing 55-second lead over Prost. On lap 67, after team boss Ron Dennis urged him over the radio to slow down to protect the car, Senna lost concentration approaching Portier and clipped the inside barrier, crashing out into the armco. Stunned and heartbroken, Senna walked straight back to his Monaco apartment without returning to the pits. Prost inherited the victory ahead of Gerhard Berger and Michele Alboreto.
