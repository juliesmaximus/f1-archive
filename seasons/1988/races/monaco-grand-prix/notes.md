## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S TRANSCENDENTAL POLE & PORTIER CRASH: In Saturday qualifying, Senna delivered what he later called an out-of-body spiritual experience, qualifying 1.427 seconds faster than Alain Prost in identical equipment. In the race, Senna built a crushing 55-second lead over Prost. On lap 67, after team boss Ron Dennis urged him to back off to ensure the 1-2, Senna suffered a momentary lapse in concentration entering Portier and clipped the inside armco, spearing into the barrier! Stunned and heartbroken, Senna walked straight to his nearby apartment. Prost inherited victory ahead of Gerhard Berger and Michele Alboreto.
