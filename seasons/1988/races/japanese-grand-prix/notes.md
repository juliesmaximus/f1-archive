## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Championship decider and one of Ayrton Senna's defining career drives. Senna took pole but stalled on the sloping grid when the lights turned green. Aided by the downhill gradient, he bump-started his McLaren, dropping from 1st to 14th into Turn 1. Senna launched a blistering charge through the field, overtaking six cars on lap 1 alone. By lap 4 he was 4th, and by lap 10 he was 2nd behind Prost. As light rain fell on lap 28, Senna caught Prost out of the chicane and swept past into the lead down the pit straight. Senna took the chequered flag to clinch his first Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship. Because only the best 11 results counted, Senna's 8 wins gave him 90 counted points, securing the crown regardless of the final round.
