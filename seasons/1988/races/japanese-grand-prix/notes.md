## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S IMMORTAL SUZUKA RECOVERY & MAIDEN TITLE: Ayrton Senna started on pole position, but stalled his engine as the green light flashed! Aided by Suzuka's downward-sloping grid, Senna bump-started the car, but fell to 14th place by the first corner. Slicing through the field in masterly damp-track fashion, Senna gained 6 spots on lap 1, reached 4th by lap 4, and was 2nd behind Alain Prost by lap 11! On lap 28, with rain beginning to fall, Senna drafted Prost down the main straight, pulled alongside entering Turn 1, and boldly claimed the lead! Senna pulled away to win by 13.3 seconds and clinch his **first World Drivers' Championship** in tears of spiritual ecstasy. Thierry Boutsen finished 3rd.
