## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MCLAREN LAPS THE ENTIRE FIELD: Ayrton Senna scored his first victory for McLaren, leading teammate Alain Prost across the line in a dominant 1-2 finish. The superiority of the McLaren MP4/4 was so terrifying that Senna and Prost lapped the entire field up to 3rd-place finisher Nelson Piquet in the Lotus-Honda! Thierry Boutsen finished 4th for Benetton. Andrea de Cesaris retired on lap 1 with broken suspension (omitted from Formula1.com result table).
