## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
McLaren obliterated the competition, qualifying 3.3 seconds ahead of the third-placed Ferrari of Nelson Piquet (Lotus-Honda) and Berger. Senna started from pole and led every single lap to take his first victory for McLaren. Prost suffered a sluggish start dropping to 6th, but carved his way back to finish 2nd, completing a dominant McLaren 1-2. Third-placed Nelson Piquet in the Lotus-Honda was lapped by both McLarens, underscoring the MP4/4's massive aerodynamic and packaging superiority.
