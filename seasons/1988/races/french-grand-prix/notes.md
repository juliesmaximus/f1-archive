## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Prost's home Grand Prix at Le Castellet. Prost took his first pole position of the season, edging Senna by 0.478s. Prost led from the start, but during the mid-race tyre pitstops, Senna jumped ahead due to a slicker stop. Prost stalked Senna relentlessly and, on lap 61, lunged down the inside into the Beausset curve through backmarker traffic to retake the lead. Prost won to the delight of the French crowd, with Senna 2nd and Michele Alboreto 3rd in the Ferrari.
