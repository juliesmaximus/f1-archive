## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST STRIKES BACK ON HOME SOIL: Alain Prost claimed pole position ahead of Senna and led from the start. During the mid-race tire pit stops, Prost was delayed by a recalcitrant right-front wheel, allowing Senna to slip into the lead. Prost relentlessly hounded Senna for 25 laps. On lap 61, as Senna was held up by traffic through the S-bends, Prost dived down the inside entering Le Beausset to retake the lead, going on to take a rapturous home victory ahead of Senna and Michele Alboreto.
