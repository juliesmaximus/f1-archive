## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S WET-WEATHER MASTERCLASS & MANSELL HEROICS: In torrential rain, Gerhard Berger led early from pole. Ayrton Senna carved through the spray, overtook Berger around the outside at Bridge corner on lap 14, and disappeared into the distance to take victory by 23.3 seconds. Nigel Mansell drove an extraordinary race in the underpowered naturally aspirated Williams-Judd, passing both Ferraris to finish 2nd, while Alessandro Nannini claimed his maiden podium in 3rd. Alain Prost withdrew due to severe handling issues in the wet.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Senna established his unassailable superiority in wet-weather conditions and closed the title deficit to Prost.
