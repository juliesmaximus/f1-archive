## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A wet masterclass by Ayrton Senna. In dry qualifying, Ferrari had locked out the front row (Berger pole, Alboreto 2nd) with McLaren on row two. On race day, heavy rain soaked Silverstone. Senna seized 2nd at the start, chased down Berger, and executed a sensational pass through bridge corner on lap 14. Prost struggled severely with car handling in the spray and pulled into the pits to retire voluntarily on lap 24. Nigel Mansell drove an inspired race in the active-suspension Williams-Judd, finishing 2nd and setting fastest lap, while Alessandro Nannini claimed his maiden podium in 3rd for Benetton.
