## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA OVERTAKES PROST AT THE HAIRPIN: Alain Prost beat pole-sitter Ayrton Senna into the first chicane and led the early laps. On lap 19, Senna closed rapidly down the Casino Straight, tucked into Prost's slipstream, and executed a clinical out-braking maneuver down the inside into the hairpin! Senna pulled away to win by 5.9 seconds over Prost, with Nelson Piquet taking 3rd in the Lotus-Honda.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Senna achieved his first on-track overtake of Alain Prost in equal machinery, igniting their head-to-head title duel.
