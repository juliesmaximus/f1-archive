## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Return of the Canadian Grand Prix after missing 1987 due to local sponsorship disputes. Senna claimed pole and engaged in a direct head-to-head battle with Prost. On lap 19, Senna slipped past Prost at the hairpin after Prost was slightly held up by backmarkers. Senna controlled the pace and fuel consumption precisely to take the win by 5.9 seconds over Prost, with Thierry Boutsen taking 3rd in the Benetton-Ford.
