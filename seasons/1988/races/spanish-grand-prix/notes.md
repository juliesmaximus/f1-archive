## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
Alain Prost made a brilliant start from 2nd to lead pole-sitter Ayrton Senna into the first corner. Prost drove a peerless race, pulling away steadily to win by 26.2 seconds ahead of Nigel Mansell in the Williams-Judd. Alessandro Nannini finished 3rd for Benetton. Senna struggled with an erroneous fuel computer reading and dropped to 4th place.
