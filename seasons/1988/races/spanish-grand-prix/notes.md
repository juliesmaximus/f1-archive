## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Prost delivered an imperious performance from 2nd on the grid, passing polesitter Senna at the start and leading all 72 laps to keep his championship hopes alive. Nigel Mansell finished 2nd for Williams, while Alessandro Nannini completed the podium in 3rd. Senna conserved fuel and settled for 4th behind Gugelmin's March.
