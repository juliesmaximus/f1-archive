## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Another wet race dominated by Ayrton Senna. Senna took pole and led every single lap around the high-speed forest straights of Hockenheim, managing visibility and hydroplaning expertly. Prost finished 13.6 seconds back in 2nd, while Gerhard Berger took 3rd for Ferrari. This victory marked Senna's third consecutive win in 1988, drawing him level with Prost in championship contention.
