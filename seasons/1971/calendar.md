# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1971 - Calendar

The 1971 Formula One season was the 22nd season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 11 championship rounds between March 6 and October 3, 1971.

The 1971 season was defined by the commanding supremacy of **Jackie Stewart** and Ken Tyrrell's **Tyrrell Racing Organisation**. Driving the Derek Gardner-designed **Tyrrell 003** powered by the Ford Cosworth DFV V8, Stewart won six Grands Prix (Spain, Monaco, France, Britain, Germany, Canada) to secure his second World Drivers' Championship with 62 net points, clinching the title at the Österreichring with three races remaining. Tyrrell captured their first and only International Cup for F1 Manufacturers as a chassis constructor.

The season also featured the **closest finish in Formula One World Championship history** at Monza, where Peter Gethin won by 0.01 seconds in a five-car blanket separated by just 0.61 seconds at an average speed of 150.75 mph (242.615 km/h).
