# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1971 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **4** results from the final 5 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Jackie Stewart won his second World Drivers' Championship with 62 net points (62 gross), winning six Grands Prix in the Tyrrell 003.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **4** results from the final 5 races counted towards the championship.

Tyrrell-Ford won the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 73 net points (73 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split into two mini-seasons: best 5 of first 6 rounds, best 4 of final 5 rounds.
  - March-Ford scored 34 gross points, dropping 1 point to finish with 33 net points.
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated or 1.5-litre supercharged/turbocharged. Minimum car weight 530 kg.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jackie Stewart dominated to win his second World Championship, claiming six victories.
  - Ken Tyrrell's team won their first and only Constructors' Championship as an independent chassis manufacturer.
  - Peter Gethin won the 1971 Italian Grand Prix at Monza by 0.01 seconds over Ronnie Peterson in the closest finish in Formula One history, with the top five cars separated by 0.61 seconds at a record average race speed of 242.615 km/h.
  - François Cevert took his maiden (and only) Grand Prix victory at Watkins Glen (United States GP).
  - Tragic losses: Pedro Rodríguez was killed in an Interserie sports car race at the Norisring on July 11; Jo Siffert was killed in the non-championship World Championship Victory Race at Brands Hatch on October 24.
