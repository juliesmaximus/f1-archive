### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL GP AT PAUL RICARD: The French Grand Prix moved to the revolutionary new Circuit Paul Ricard, acclaimed as the world's most modern and safest motorsport facility with innovative wide runoff areas. Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 003 and dominated the race, leading every lap to win by 28.1 seconds. Young French teammate François Cevert finished 2nd, delivering a jubilant 1-2 finish for the Tyrrell Racing Organisation before an ecstatic French crowd! Emerson Fittipaldi completed the podium in 3rd for Lotus.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First 1-2 finish for the Tyrrell constructor. Circuit Paul Ricard set a new global benchmark for circuit safety and facilities.
