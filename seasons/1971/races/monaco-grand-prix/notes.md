## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell) — 1:52:21.300  
- **Pole Position:** Jackie Stewart  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Heavy rain, standing puddles, persistent drizzle; 18°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 45,000 soaked spectators lining harbour balconies and cliffs  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Quai Albert 1er grandstands and Casino square terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
STEWART'S WET-WEATHER DOMINATION: Jackie Stewart stunned the paddock in qualifying by taking pole position by an enormous 1.2 seconds in the Tyrrell 003. In the rain-swept race, Stewart led from start to finish, demonstrating sublime car control on Goodyear wet tyres to win by 18.3 seconds. Young Swedish driver Ronnie Peterson drove brilliantly in the March 711 (featuring the distinctive raised oval front wing dubbed the 'tea tray') to finish 2nd, scoring his maiden Formula One podium! Jacky Ickx finished 3rd for Ferrari.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart's second Monaco Grand Prix victory. Ronnie Peterson firmly established his status as one of F1's most exciting young talents.
