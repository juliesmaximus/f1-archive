## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Mario Andretti (Ferrari) — 1:47:35.500  
- **Pole Position:** Jackie Stewart  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 26°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 85,000 spectators across the Kyalami amphitheatre  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne spectator grass banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR MARIO ANDRETTI WITH FERRARI: Denny Hulme qualified on pole in the McLaren M19A and led comfortably for 75 of the 79 laps. With four laps remaining, Hulme's front suspension broke, forcing him into the pits. Mario Andretti, driving in his very first race for Scuderia Ferrari in the 312B Flat-12, swept into the lead to take his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory! Jackie Stewart finished 2nd in the Tyrrell 001, and Clay Regazzoni took 3rd for Ferrari.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Mario Andretti became the first driver since Giancarlo Baghetti in 1961 to win on his debut race for Scuderia Ferrari.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 4 of last 5 races counted.
