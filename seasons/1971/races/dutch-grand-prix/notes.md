- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Relentless torrential rain, thick spray, standing water, and gale-force coastal winds  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main pit straight grandstand and Tarzan hairpin dunes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE RAIN MASTERCLASS OF ICKX & RODRÍGUEZ: Run in treacherous torrential rain. Jacky Ickx (Ferrari 312B2 on Firestone rain tyres) and Pedro Rodríguez (BRM P160) engaged in a mesmerising, heroic wet-weather duel, pulling away from the entire field by nearly two minutes. On lap 30, Ickx seized the lead and held on to win by 7.99 seconds over Rodríguez. Clay Regazzoni was a distant 3rd, a full lap down. World Champion Jackie Stewart spun on lap 3, recovering to finish 11th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Tragically, this proved to be the final Grand Prix for Mexican hero Pedro Rodríguez, who was killed three weeks later in an Interserie sports car race at the Norisring on July 11, 1971.
