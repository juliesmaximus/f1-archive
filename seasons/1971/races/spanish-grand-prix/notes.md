- **Fastest Lap:** Jacky Ickx (Ferrari) — 1:25.100 (Lap 69)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 24°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Estadi Olímpic terraces, Miramar grandstands, and Teatre Grec spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR TYRRELL 003: Jacky Ickx took pole for Ferrari, but Jackie Stewart grabbed the lead on lap 6 in the brand-new, bespoke Tyrrell 003. Stewart drove a masterclass in tactical tyre management on Goodyear tyres, holding off Ickx's charging Ferrari 312B2 by 3.4 seconds at the chequered flag! Chris Amon took 3rd for Matra. Only 9 of 22 starters finished the race.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First Formula One World Championship victory for the Tyrrell 003 chassis, which became one of the most successful individual chassis in Grand Prix history.
