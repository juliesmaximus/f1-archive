### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
STEWART CLINCHES 2ND WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Swiss driver Jo Siffert drove a flawless race in the BRM P160, taking pole position, setting fastest lap, leading all 54 laps, and holding off a charging Emerson Fittipaldi (Lotus) to win by 4.12 seconds, with Tim Schenken 3rd in the Brabham. Jackie Stewart suffered a broken halfshaft and crashed on lap 35. However, when his sole mathematical title rival Jacky Ickx suffered an engine failure on lap 31, JACKIE STEWART MATHEMATICALLY CLINCHED HIS SECOND WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP and the Tyrrell Racing Organisation clinched their first International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with three rounds remaining!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart became a double World Champion, cementing the Tyrrell team's status as the dominant force in Grand Prix racing.
