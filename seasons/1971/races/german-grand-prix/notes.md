## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell) — 1:29:16.300  
- **Pole Position:** Jackie Stewart  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient, excellent visibility  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 250,000 spectators camping across the Eifel forests  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Start-und-Ziel grandstand, South curve, Karussell, and Brünnchen banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
RETURN TO THE REVAMPED NORDSCHLEIFE: Formula One returned to the Nürburgring following a massive renovation that added continuous steel Armco barriers, flattened severe crests, and widened runoff areas. Jackie Stewart took pole position with a lap of 7:19.0. Stewart and teammate François Cevert drove an immaculate race to score another crushing 1-2 finish for Tyrrell, Stewart winning by 30.1 seconds over Cevert! Clay Regazzoni finished 3rd for Ferrari. With 5 wins in 7 races, Stewart moved to 51 points, needing just one more point to clinch the championship.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Stewart and Tyrrell stood on the absolute verge of the double World Championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Continuous Armco barriers installed around the entire 14-mile circuit.
