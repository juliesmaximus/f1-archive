- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Paddock grandstand, Corner 2 spectator banks, and Moss Corner infield  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
RAIN AND FOG SHORTEN RACE: Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 003. In treacherous conditions of torrential rain and dense fog rolling off Lake Ontario, Stewart dominated from start to finish. The race was red-flagged and declared after 64 of the scheduled 80 laps due to zero visibility and standing water. Stewart won by 38.3 seconds over Ronnie Peterson's March, with Mark Donohue scoring a podium on his Formula One debut in 3rd for Penske Racing (McLaren M19A).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart's 6th victory of the season and Mark Donohue's debut podium for Roger Penske.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Race shortened to 64 laps (253.25 km) due to torrential rain and fog. Full points awarded as over 75% distance completed.
