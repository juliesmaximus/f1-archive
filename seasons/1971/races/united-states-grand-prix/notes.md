### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR FRANÇOIS CEVERT ON EXPANDED CIRCUIT: Watkins Glen debuted its newly extended 3.377-mile layout, incorporating the new 'Boot' section. Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 003. Stewart led early before tyre blistering dropped him to 5th. His glamorous 27-year-old French teammate François Cevert seized the lead on lap 14 and drove an immaculate race to score his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory by 40.05 seconds ahead of Jo Siffert (BRM) and Ronnie Peterson (March)! Cevert pocketed the richest winner's purse in motorsport history ($50,000). Tragically, this would be Jo Siffert's final Formula One podium; the Swiss star was killed three weeks later at Brands Hatch.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
François Cevert became only the second Frenchman after Maurice Trintignant to win a World Championship Grand Prix, establishing himself as Jackie Stewart's heir apparent.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Circuit lengthened from 3.701 km to 5.435 km with the addition of the 'Boot' section.
