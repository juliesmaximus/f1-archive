## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The First Father-Son World Champions:** Damon Hill won the 1996 World Championship with 8 victories, joining his father Graham Hill (1962, 1968) as the first father-and-son champions in Formula 1 history.
- **Jacques Villeneuve's Sensational Rookie Season:** 1995 IndyCar champion and Indy 500 winner Jacques Villeneuve took pole position on his F1 debut in Melbourne, scored 4 wins and 11 podiums, and fought for the title until a loose rear wheel retired him in the Suzuka finale.
- **Michael Schumacher's Maiden Ferrari Campaign:** Schumacher joined Scuderia Ferrari alongside Eddie Irvine, switching from V12 to V10 power (Ferrari 046). He took three victories, including an iconic rain-soaked masterclass at Catalunya (winning by 45 seconds) and an emotional win at Monza in front of the Tifosi.
- **Olivier Panis Monaco Miracle:** In a wet-dry Monaco Grand Prix that saw 19 cars retire, Olivier Panis drove from 14th on the grid to score Ligier's first Grand Prix win since 1981 in a race where only three cars crossed the finish line.
- **107% Qualifying Rule Implementation:** The rule effectively barred under-funded cars from starting races if they failed to lap within 107% of the pole position time in qualifying, leading to the eventual withdrawal of Forti Corse mid-season.
