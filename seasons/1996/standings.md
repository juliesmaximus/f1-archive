## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The First Father-Son Champions:** Damon Hill clinched the 1996 World Championship, joining his late father Graham Hill (1962, 1968) to form the first father-and-son World Champions in Formula 1 history.
- **Introduction of the 107% Rule:** To ensure minimum competitive speeds and safety, the FIA introduced the 107% rule during qualifying.
- **Enhanced Head Protection Cockpits:** Cockpit side-protection surrounds were raised from 400 mm to 550 mm with energy-absorbing Confor foam to safeguard driver helmets against lateral impacts.
- **Jacques Villeneuve's Sensation Debut:** Jacques Villeneuve won four races and took three pole positions in his rookie season, taking the championship battle down to the final round.
- **Olivier Panis Monaco Upset:** Olivier Panis drove from 14th on the grid to win the wet Monaco Grand Prix in a race where only three cars crossed the finish line.
