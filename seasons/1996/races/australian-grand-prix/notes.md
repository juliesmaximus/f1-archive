## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 debut at Melbourne's Albert Park, replacing Adelaide. Canadian rookie Jacques Villeneuve stunned the world by taking pole position on his Formula 1 debut for Williams-Renault. Martin Brundle suffered a terrifying barrel-roll crash at Turn 3 on lap 1, destroying his Jordan but walking away unhurt. Villeneuve led 50 laps until an oil leak forced him to slow with 5 laps remaining, allowing teammate Damon Hill to win.
