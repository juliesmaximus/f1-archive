## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A legendary victory sending the tifosi into absolute delirium: Michael Schumacher won the Italian Grand Prix for Ferrari, their first win at Monza since Gerhard Berger in 1988. Damon Hill led from pole until clipping the tyre barrier at the Roggia chicane on lap 6. Schumacher won ahead of Jean Alesi and Mika Häkkinen.
