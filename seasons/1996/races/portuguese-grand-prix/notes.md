## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Featuring one of the most audacious overtakes in F1 history: Jacques Villeneuve swept around the outside of Michael Schumacher's Ferrari through the ultra-fast 150 mph Curva Parabólica on lap 16 to take 2nd. Villeneuve went on to win ahead of Damon Hill and Michael Schumacher, keeping the title battle alive into the final round.
