## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A historic day: Jacques Villeneuve scored his maiden Formula 1 Grand Prix victory in only his fourth career start. Villeneuve led from the start and held off a ferocious late charge from Michael Schumacher's Ferrari, winning by just 0.762 seconds. David Coulthard scored McLaren-Mercedes' first podium of the season in 3rd.
