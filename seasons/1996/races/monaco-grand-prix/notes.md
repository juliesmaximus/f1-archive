## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most extraordinary, chaotic races in Formula 1 history. Starting on a soaking track, Michael Schumacher crashed into the wall at Lower Mirabeau on lap 1. Damon Hill led comfortably until his Renault engine blew up in the tunnel on lap 40. Jean Alesi took over the lead until suspension failure eliminated him. Olivier Panis, starting 14th in the Ligier-Mugen-Honda, made brilliant overtakes (including muscling past Irvine at the hairpin) and timed his tyre changes to perfection. Only three cars crossed the finish line (Panis, Coulthard, and Herbert); Panis took a miraculous maiden victory—the only win of his career and Ligier's final F1 win.
