## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher drove a tactical masterpiece for Ferrari. When a heavy crash for Jos Verstappen brought out the Safety Car on lap 10, Schumacher pitted immediately, while polesitter Villeneuve stayed out an extra lap. Schumacher jumped into the lead and defended masterfully against Villeneuve to claim his second win for Ferrari, with Mika Häkkinen 3rd.
