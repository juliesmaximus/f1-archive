## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Widely regarded as Michael Schumacher's greatest individual drive: the 'Regenmeister' (Rainmaster) masterpiece. In apocalyptic rain, Schumacher made a terrible start dropping to 6th. Finding grip lines where none existed, Schumacher drove up to four seconds per lap faster than the entire field, passing Alesi and Villeneuve with ease. Schumacher took his first victory for Scuderia Ferrari by 45 seconds over Jean Alesi, with Jacques Villeneuve 3rd. Damon Hill spun off three times and retired.
