## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The championship decider. Villeneuve took pole but made a poor start, dropping to 6th, while Damon Hill rocketed into the lead. On lap 37, Villeneuve's right-rear wheel detached at Turn 1, eliminating him from the race. With Villeneuve's retirement, Damon Hill officially became the 1996 Formula 1 World Drivers' Champion. Hill won the race ahead of Michael Schumacher and Mika Häkkinen, becoming the first son of a World Champion (Graham Hill) to win the World Championship.
