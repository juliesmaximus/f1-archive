## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Traction Control Ban:** Complete elimination of traction control, launch control, and engine braking assistance.
- **Standardized SECU:** Standard Electronic Control Unit manufactured by McLaren Electronic Systems (MES) mandated for all teams.
- **Gearbox Longevity:** Each gearbox had to last four consecutive race weekends (5-place penalty for premature replacement).
- **Inaugural Night Race:** Singapore Grand Prix under 1,600 bespoke projectors.
