## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Felipe Massa (Ferrari)
- **Pole Position:** Robert Kubica (BMW Sauber) - 1:33.096
- **Fastest Lap:** Heikki Kovalainen (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:33.193 (lap 49)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Robert Kubica scored BMW Sauber's historic maiden pole position. At the start, Felipe Massa surged past Kubica into Turn 1 and led a dominant Ferrari 1-2 ahead of Kimi Räikkönen. Lewis Hamilton suffered a nightmare race: stalled his anti-stall at the start, rear-ended Fernando Alonso's Renault on lap 2, broke his front wing, and finished 13th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Kimi Räikkönen took over the World Championship lead with 19 points.
