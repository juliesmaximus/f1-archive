## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Inaugural Grand Prix around the Valencia harbour and swing bridge. Felipe Massa scored a flawless Grand Slam: pole, win, fastest lap, and led every lap. Kimi Räikkönen suffered an unsafe pit release, running over his refueller Pietro Timpini before retiring with engine failure.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari was fined €10,000 for Massa's unsafe release into the path of Adrian Sutil in the pitlane.
