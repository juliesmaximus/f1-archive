## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Miracle at Monza. 21-year-old Sebastian Vettel took an astonishing pole position in pouring rain for Minardi's successor, Scuderia Toro Rosso. In the race, Vettel drove with astonishing maturity, leading almost every lap in treacherous wet conditions to claim his and Toro Rosso's maiden Formula 1 victory by 12.5 seconds over Heikki Kovalainen. At 21 years, 73 days, Vettel became the youngest race winner and pole-sitter in Formula 1 history.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First Italian Grand Prix victory for an Italian team other than Ferrari since Maserati in 1957.
