## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton scored a supreme Grand Slam under massive pressure: pole, win, fastest lap, and led every lap. Hamilton finished 14.9 seconds ahead of Felipe Massa (after Räikkönen let Massa past for championship points), entering the Brazil finale with a 7-point lead.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari openly swapped positions between Räikkönen and Massa on lap 49.
