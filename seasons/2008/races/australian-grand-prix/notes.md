## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Lewis Hamilton (McLaren-Mercedes)

- **Fastest Lap:** Heikki Kovalainen (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:27.418 (lap 43)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Total attrition and carnage in 38°C heat without traction control. Lewis Hamilton controlled the race flawlessly from pole position. Only 7 of the 22 starters were running at the finish (later reduced to 6 classified finishers after Rubens Barrichello was disqualified for exiting the pitlane under red lights).

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First race under the complete ban on traction control and standardized SECU electronics; numerous drivers spun violently off curbs.
