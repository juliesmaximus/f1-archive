## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen achieved a Grand Slam: pole, win, fastest lap, and led almost every lap. Ferrari finished 1-2 with Massa. On lap 22, Heikki Kovalainen suffered a terrifying 140 mph crash when his left-front wheel rim failed, burying his McLaren deep into the tyre barrier; he miraculously escaped without serious injury.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Safety barriers took 10 minutes to extract Kovalainen's car, triggering lengthy safety car period.
