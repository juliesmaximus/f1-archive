## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the greatest wet-weather masterclasses in Formula 1 history. In treacherous torrential rain where almost every driver spun repeatedly (Felipe Massa spun five separate times!), Lewis Hamilton drove in a completely different category. Overtaking teammate Kovalainen on lap 5, Hamilton stormed into the distance, lapping everyone up to 3rd place and winning by a staggering 1 minute and 8.5 seconds over Nick Heidfeld.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's 68.5-second winning margin was the largest victory margin in Formula 1 since Damon Hill at Adelaide in 1995.
