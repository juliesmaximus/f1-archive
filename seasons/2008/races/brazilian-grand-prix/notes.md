## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The most dramatic, heart-stopping championship conclusion in Formula 1 history. Hamilton needed to finish 5th or higher if Massa won. Massa dominated from pole to win the race before his home crowd. With 5 laps to go, torrential rain unleashed over Interlagos. Frontrunners pitted for intermediate tyres, but Toyota gambled on leaving Timo Glock out on dry slicks, moving Glock to 4th. On lap 69, Sebastian Vettel overtook Hamilton, demoting Hamilton to 6th! In the Ferrari pit box, Massa's family celebrated as Massa crossed the line as World Champion. But on the final lap, as Glock struggled for grip in pouring rain, Hamilton caught the sliding Toyota approaching Junção corner with just 400 metres remaining, sweeping past to take 5th place! At 23 years, 301 days, Lewis Hamilton became the youngest World Drivers' Champion in Formula 1 history, clinching the title by 1 point (98 to 97). Ferrari won their 16th World Constructors' Championship.
