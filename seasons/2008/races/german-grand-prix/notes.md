## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Timo Glock suffered a violent suspension failure on the pit straight on lap 36, triggering a safety car. While all rivals pitted, McLaren mistakenly kept Hamilton out on track. Rejoining 5th after his subsequent stop, Hamilton launched an electrifying charge: overtaking Kovalainen, Massa (with a stunning dive down the inside at the hairpin), and Piquet Jr. to score an unforgettable comeback win.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
