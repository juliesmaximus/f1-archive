## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Cruel heartbreak for Felipe Massa. Starting 3rd, Massa pulled off a sensational move around the outside of Hamilton into Turn 1 to take the lead. Massa dominated completely until just three laps from the finish, when his Ferrari engine suffered a catastrophic blowout on the main straight! Heikki Kovalainen swept past to score his first and only Formula 1 victory, becoming F1's 100th Grand Prix winner.
