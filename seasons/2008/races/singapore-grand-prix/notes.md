## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic inaugural Formula 1 night race, and the scene of 'Crashgate'. Fernando Alonso started 15th and pitted unexpectedly early on lap 12. On lap 14, teammate Nelson Piquet Jr. deliberately spun his Renault into the concrete barrier at Turn 17 on team orders, deploying the Safety Car. With the pitlane closed under rules, Alonso surged to the front. In Ferrari's pitstop under safety car, the electronic pit light turned green prematurely: Felipe Massa tore away tearing off the fuel hose, spraying fuel across the pitlane! Massa dropped to the back. Alonso won the race.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
In 2009, Piquet Jr. confessed that team principal Flavio Briatore and Pat Symonds ordered him to crash deliberately to trigger the safety car that won Alonso the race.
