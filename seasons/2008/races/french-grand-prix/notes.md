## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen scored Ferrari's 200th pole position and dominated until a cracked exhaust pipe detached and began melting his engine cover on lap 35. Felipe Massa swept past to take victory, with Räikkönen nursing the crippled Ferrari home in 2nd. Massa took the World Championship lead for the first time in his career (first Brazilian to lead the championship since Ayrton Senna in 1993).
