- **Winner:** Robert Kubica (BMW Sauber)
- **Pole Position:** Lewis Hamilton (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:17.886
- **Fastest Lap:** Kimi Räikkönen (Ferrari) - 1:17.387 (lap 14)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic day for Poland and BMW Sauber. Hamilton led from pole until a Safety Car on lap 19 triggered pit stops. At the pit exit, the red exit light was illuminated. Räikkönen and Kubica stopped side-by-side. Hamilton failed to see the red light in time and rammed directly into the rear of Räikkönen's Ferrari, eliminating both from the race! Robert Kubica capitalized to score his and BMW Sauber's maiden Formula 1 victory, leading teammate Nick Heidfeld in a historic 1-2—exactly one year after Kubica's near-fatal crash at the same venue.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton received a 10-place grid penalty for the subsequent race in France for causing the pitlane collision.
