## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the most controversial steward rulings in F1 history. Rain hit the circuit with three laps remaining. Hamilton hunted down leader Kimi Räikkönen. At the Bus Stop chicane, Hamilton cut the chicane, yielded the position back to Räikkönen down the straight, and immediately lunged down the inside into La Source to take the lead. Räikkönen spun into the wall two laps later. Hamilton took the chequered flag, but stewards handed him a 25-second post-race penalty for 'gaining an advantage', handing victory to Felipe Massa.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The 25-second penalty outraged fans and McLaren, who appealed unsuccessfully.
