## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of Lewis Hamilton's most magnificent victories. On lap 6 of a wet race, Hamilton clipped the barrier at Tabac, puncturing his right-rear tyre. McLaren pitted him for a new tyre and heavy fuel load. The strategy proved a stroke of pure genius: as the track dried, rivals were forced to stop for dry tyres while Hamilton had already fuelled to the finish, pulling out a 40-second lead to win. On lap 68, Kimi Räikkönen lost control out of the tunnel and smashed violently into the rear of Adrian Sutil's Force India, robbing Sutil of a dream 4th place.
