## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Kimi Räikkönen (Ferrari)
- **Pole Position:** Felipe Massa (Ferrari) - 1:35.748
- **Fastest Lap:** Nick Heidfeld (BMW Sauber) - 1:35.366 (lap 55)

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen delivered a commanding performance to take victory by 19.5 seconds over Robert Kubica's BMW Sauber. Pole-sitter Felipe Massa spun off into the gravel at Turn 8 on lap 30 while running 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Both McLarens received 5-place grid penalties for impeding Heidfeld and Alonso in qualifying.
