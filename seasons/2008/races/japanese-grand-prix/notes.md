## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Complete chaos at Turn 1. Hamilton locked up heavily, forcing Räikkönen wide. On lap 2, Massa and Hamilton collided at the chicane, spinning Hamilton around. Both title contenders received drive-through penalties. Fernando Alonso took full advantage of the chaos, overtaking Robert Kubica during the pitstops to take back-to-back victories for Renault.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Both Hamilton and Massa received penalties in a bitterly contested stewards ruling.
