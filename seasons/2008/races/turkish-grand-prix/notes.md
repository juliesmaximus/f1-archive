## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Felipe Massa achieved a legendary hat-trick, winning the Turkish Grand Prix for the third consecutive year from pole position. Lewis Hamilton ran an aggressive three-stop strategy to preserve his delicate Bridgestone tyres, pulling off a breathtaking overtake on Massa for the lead at Turn 12 before pitting to finish 2nd.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
