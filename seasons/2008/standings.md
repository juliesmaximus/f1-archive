## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton Youngest World Champion:** At 23 years, 301 days, Lewis Hamilton clinched his maiden World Drivers' Championship on the final corner of the final lap at Interlagos.
- **Ferrari's 16th Constructors' Title:** Scuderia Ferrari won the World Constructors' Championship with 172 points.
- **Inaugural Night Race in Singapore:** Formula 1 staged its first floodlit night race at the Marina Bay Street Circuit.
- **Sebastian Vettel & Toro Rosso Miracle at Monza:** At 21 years, 73 days, Sebastian Vettel became the youngest race winner in F1 history in pouring rain.
- **The Crashgate Controversy:** Nelson Piquet Jr.'s deliberate crash at Singapore orchestrated Fernando Alonso's victory.
