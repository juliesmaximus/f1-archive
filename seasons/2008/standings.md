## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton Youngest World Champion:** At 23 years, 301 days, Lewis Hamilton clinched his maiden World Drivers' Championship on the final corner (Turn 12, Junção) of the final lap at Interlagos, passing Timo Glock for 5th position to edge Felipe Massa by a single point (98 to 97).
- **Ferrari's 16th Constructors' Championship:** Scuderia Ferrari won their 16th (and most recent) World Constructors' Championship with 172 points, 8 victories, and 19 podiums.
- **Inaugural Night Race in Singapore:** Formula 1 staged its first floodlit night race at the Marina Bay Street Circuit.
- **Sebastian Vettel & Toro Rosso Miracle at Monza:** In extreme wet conditions at the Italian Grand Prix, 21-year-old Sebastian Vettel became the youngest pole-sitter and race winner in Formula 1 history, taking a stunning lights-to-flag maiden victory for Scuderia Toro Rosso (formerly Minardi).
- **Robert Kubica & BMW Sauber Breakthrough:** Robert Kubica scored his maiden victory and BMW Sauber's sole Grand Prix triumph with a historic 1-2 finish at the Canadian Grand Prix, exactly one year after his horrific accident at the same circuit.
- **Crashgate Scandal:** At the Singapore Grand Prix, Renault ordered Nelson Piquet Jr. to crash deliberately on lap 14 to bring out the Safety Car, triggering pit-stop timing that handed victory to teammate Fernando Alonso.
