### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE DISQUALIFICATION CONTROVERSY: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312T2. At the start, Clay Regazzoni collided with Lauda at Paddock Hill Bend, triggering a multi-car pileup that wrecked James Hunt's suspension. The race was red-flagged. Hunt drove his damaged McLaren through the pit lane bypass. Amidst furious crowd chants of 'We want Hunt!', stewards allowed Hunt to start the restarted race in his repaired primary car. Hunt won on track by 52 seconds ahead of Lauda to ecstatic British roars. Ferrari immediately protested that Hunt had not completed the full opening lap under his own power. In September, the FIA Tribunal upheld Ferrari's appeal, DISQUALIFYING HUNT and awarding victory to Niki Lauda, with Jody Scheckter 2nd and John Watson 3rd!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
A bitterly contentious legal battle that cost Hunt 9 championship points and awarded Lauda an additional 3 points.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Red flag restart rules strictly enforced on FIA appeal.
