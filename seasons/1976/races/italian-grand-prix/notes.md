### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE MIRACLE OF MONZA — LAUDA'S HEROIC RETURN: Just 42 days after receiving the Last Rites, Niki Lauda returned to race, his face raw, unhealed, and bleeding through his fireproof balaclava, unable to blink his right eyelid. Overcoming terrifying flashbacks in Friday practice, Lauda qualified an astounding 5th in the Ferrari 312T2! In the race, Ronnie Peterson took a magnificent upset victory in the March 761 ahead of Clay Regazzoni and Jacques Laffite. Niki Lauda drove an astonishing race of grit and determination to FINISH 4th, earning three precious championship points to wild tears and cheers from the tifosi! James Hunt was penalized to the back of the grid for a fuel octanage technicality and spun off on lap 11.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lauda's Monza comeback is universally recognized as the greatest, most heroic return in sporting history.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Variante della Roggia chicane added to slow speeds before Lesmo corners.
