### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE SIX-WHEELED TYRRELL P34 HISTORIC 1-2 VICTORY: Jody Scheckter qualified on pole position in the revolutionary six-wheeled Tyrrell P34 (featuring four 10-inch front wheels). In the race, Scheckter and French teammate Patrick Depailler dominated in their six-wheelers, scoring an iconic 1-2 finish! Scheckter won by 19.76 seconds over Depailler, with Niki Lauda 3rd for Ferrari. This remains the FIRST AND ONLY VICTORY FOR A SIX-WHEELED CAR in Formula One World Championship history! Mario Andretti led early in the new Lotus 77 before an engine failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the most famous and photographically iconic moments in motorsport history. Derek Gardner's design achieved immortality.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Six-wheeled cars officially validated and approved under technical regulations.
