## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 28°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 100,000 Brazilian spectators  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Curva do Sol grandstands, Arquibancadas da Reta Oposta, and hillside slopes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA COMMENCES DEFENSE WITH WIN: James Hunt took pole position on his McLaren M23 debut. In the race, Clay Regazzoni led early before tangling with Jarier. World Champion Niki Lauda in the Ferrari 312T swept into the lead on lap 9 and pulled away to win by 21.47 seconds ahead of Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell) and Tom Pryce (Shadow). Hunt crashed out on lap 26 when a sticking throttle caused him to spin.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Niki Lauda opened his title defense with victory. Emerson Fittipaldi debuted for the Brazilian Copersucar-Fittipaldi team, finishing 11th.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring: best 7 of first 8 races and best 7 of last 8 races counted.
