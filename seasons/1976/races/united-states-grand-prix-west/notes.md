### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL LONG BEACH GP & FERRARI 1-2: Formula One debuted on the thrilling, scenic street circuit of Long Beach. Clay Regazzoni produced a flawless Grand Slam in the Ferrari 312T: pole position, fastest lap, led all 80 laps, and won by 42.4 seconds ahead of teammate Niki Lauda for a crushing Ferrari 1-2 sweep! Patrick Depailler finished 3rd in the Tyrrell 007. James Hunt crashed into the wall on lap 3 while tangling with Depailler.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Created by Chris Pook, the Long Beach Grand Prix quickly became the 'Monaco of the United States'. Ferrari held a commanding 1-2 in the championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** First official World Championship United States Grand Prix West.
