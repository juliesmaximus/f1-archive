### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HUNT CELEBRATES 29TH BIRTHDAY WITH WIN: Ronnie Peterson took pole for March. On his 29th birthday, James Hunt took the lead in the McLaren M23 on lap 12 and fended off ferocious challenges from Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari) and Mario Andretti (Lotus) to win by 0.92 seconds over Regazzoni! Hunt moved within 14 points of the hospitalized Lauda (58 pts vs. 44 pts), with news breaking that Lauda was planning an impossible return at Monza.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
