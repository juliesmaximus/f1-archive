**Round:** 16 of 16  
**Date:** 1976-10-24  
**Circuit:** Fuji Speedway  
**Location:** Oyama, Suntō District, Shizuoka Prefecture, Japan (35°22'18"N, 138°55'36"W)  
**Track Length:** 4.359 km (2.709 miles)  
**Altitude:** 580 m (foothills of Mount Fuji)  
**Scheduled Distance:** 73 laps (318.207 km)  
**Official Race Title:** 1st F1 World Championship in Japan

- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Apocalyptic torrential rain, dense mountain fog, standing rivers of water across the main straight; 13°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Massive permanent pit straight grandstand facing Mount Fuji and hairpin spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE FUJI DELUGE & HUNT CLINCHES BY 1 POINT: Mario Andretti took pole for Lotus. Run in an apocalyptic torrential downpour with zero visibility and standing rivers of water. On lap 2, championship leader Niki Lauda pulled into the Ferrari pits and withdrew on safety grounds, calmly explaining: 'My life is worth more than a title.' Emerson Fittipaldi and Carlos Pace also withdrew. James Hunt led from the start in the McLaren M23. As the track dried, Hunt's wet tyres blistered severely, forcing an emergency pitstop on lap 68 that dropped him to 5th. In a frantic, flat-out charge through the spray over the final 4 laps, Hunt passed Alan Jones and Clay Regazzoni to cross the line in 3rd place! Unaware of his position, Hunt exited the car furious until Teddy Mayer and Ken Tyrrell shouted: 'James, you finished 3rd! You're the World Champion!' Mario Andretti won the race for Lotus (his first win since 1971), Patrick Depailler 2nd in the Tyrrell P34. HUNT WON THE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP BY A SINGLE POINT (69 to 68 points)! Scuderia Ferrari clinched the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The immortal climax to the greatest season in Formula One history, later immortalized in Ron Howard's 2013 feature film 'Rush'. James Hunt became Britain's 6th World Champion.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Inaugural Formula One World Championship Japanese Grand Prix.
