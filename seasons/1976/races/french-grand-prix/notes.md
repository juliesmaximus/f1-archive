- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstands, Virage du Pont, and wide paved runoffs  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FERRARI ENGINE FAILURES & HUNT CAPITALIZES: James Hunt took pole position in the McLaren M23. Niki Lauda seized the lead at the start and led comfortably until his Ferrari Flat-12 engine suffered a catastrophic valve failure on lap 8. Teammate Clay Regazzoni took over the lead until his engine also blew on lap 17! James Hunt swept into the lead, pulling away to take victory by 12.7s over Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell P34) and John Watson (Penske PC4).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Hunt took his 2nd win of 1976, initiating his dramatic championship comeback.
