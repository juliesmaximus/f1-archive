## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** James Hunt (McLaren) — 1:42:40.742  
- **Pole Position:** James Hunt  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, crisp, sunny autumn day; 12°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 120,000 spectators packing the Watkins Glen hills  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Boot, and Seneca spectator knolls  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HUNT'S MASTERCLASS CLOSES GAP TO 3 POINTS: James Hunt took pole position in the McLaren M23. In the race, Jody Scheckter seized the lead in the six-wheeled Tyrrell P34. Hunt fought back relentlessly, overtaking Scheckter on lap 46 to win by 2.03 seconds! Niki Lauda drove a heroic race to finish 3rd on the podium for Ferrari. Hunt's 6th victory of the season moved him to 65 points against Lauda's 68 points, setting up the most sensational championship showdown in Formula One history in Japan!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Set the stage for the immortal Fuji title decider with just 3 points separating Lauda and Hunt.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Record $350,000 total purse. Scheckter confirmed moving to Wolf for 1977.
