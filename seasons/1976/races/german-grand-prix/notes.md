### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
NIKI LAUDA'S HORRIFIC CRASH & THE END OF THE NORDSCHLEIFE: Prior to the race, championship leader Niki Lauda urged fellow drivers to boycott the race due to inadequate track safety, marshaling, and fire cover across the 14-mile course; the vote failed by one vote. In the race, on lap 2 between Breidscheid and Bergwerk, Lauda's Ferrari suffered rear suspension failure at 140 mph, slammed into the rock embankment, rebounded onto the track in flames, and was struck by Brett Lunger. Fellow drivers Arturo Merzario, Guy Edwards, Brett Lunger, and Harald Ertl plunged into the inferno, Merzario desperately unbuckling Lauda from the burning cockpit. Lauda suffered severe facial burns and lung inhalation damage. The race was restarted over 14 laps. James Hunt dominated to win by 27.7s ahead of Jody Scheckter (Tyrrell P34) and Jochen Mass (McLaren). THIS WAS THE FINAL FORMULA ONE GRAND PRIX EVER HELD ON THE NORDSCHLEIFE.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The pivotal moment of the 1976 season. Lauda received the Last Rites in Mannheim hospital, while Hunt launched his championship charge.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Continuous 14-mile circuit permanently stripped of Formula One Grand Prix status on safety grounds.
