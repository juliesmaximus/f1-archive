### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
JOHN WATSON & TEAM PENSKE MAIDEN VICTORY: Scuderia Ferrari boycotted the race while Niki Lauda fought for his life in hospital. James Hunt took pole for McLaren. In a thrilling race, Northern Irishman John Watson in Roger Penske's Penske PC4 Ford Cosworth DFV drove brilliantly, passing Jody Scheckter on lap 12 and pulling away to win by 10.79s over Jacques Laffite (Ligier) and Gunnar Nilsson (Lotus)! James Hunt finished 4th. Watson fulfilled a famous bet by shaving off his trademark beard after taking Penske's SOLE Formula One World Championship victory!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first and only Formula One victory for American racing titan Roger Penske as a chassis constructor.
