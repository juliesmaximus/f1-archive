### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA COMMANDS MONACO: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312T2 and drove a masterclass in precision driving to win by 11.13 seconds ahead of the two radical six-wheeled Tyrrell P34s of Jody Scheckter and Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell 2-3)! Hunt retired on lap 24 with an engine fire. Clay Regazzoni crashed at the chicane on oil.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lauda's 4th win in 6 races gave him 51 points out of a maximum possible 54, establishing a colossal 36-point lead in the championship.
