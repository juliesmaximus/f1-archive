## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** James Hunt (McLaren) — 1:42:20.430  
- **Pole Position:** James Hunt (1:18.520)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jochen Mass (McLaren-Ford) — 1:20.930 (Lap 34)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstands, Pegaso curve terraces, and Farina hillsides  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
TALL AIRBOXES BANNED & HUNT REINSTATED: New FIA regulations took effect banning tall over-the-head induction airboxes and restricting rear wing overhang. James Hunt took pole position in the modified McLaren M23. Hunt drove a brilliant race to win on track by 30.9s over Niki Lauda (Ferrari 312T2). Post-race scrutineers disqualified Hunt because the McLaren's rear track was 1.5 cm too wide, awarding victory to Lauda. McLaren appealed, proving that tyre heat expansion caused the discrepancy. Two months later, the FIA Court of Appeal REINSTATED JAMES HUNT as the rightful winner!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Hunt's first victory for McLaren, sparking the bitter political and sporting feud with Ferrari.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Induction airbox height restricted to car roll-bar height.
