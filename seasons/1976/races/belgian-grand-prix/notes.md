## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 1:42:53.230  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda (1:26.550)
- **Fastest Lap:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 1:25.980 (Lap 61)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 26°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered start-finish grandstand and grass mounds around Terlamen  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FERRARI 1-2 SWEEP: Niki Lauda qualified on pole in the new Ferrari 312T2 and dominated the race, pulling away to take victory by 3.46 seconds ahead of teammate Clay Regazzoni, sealing another Ferrari 1-2 sweep! Jacques Laffite finished 3rd in the screaming Matra-powered Ligier JS5 (scoring Ligier's maiden Formula One podium). Hunt spun off on lap 35 with transmission failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lauda's 3rd win of the season moved him to 39 points, leading Regazzoni by 20 points and Hunt by 33.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 70 laps (295.4 km).
