# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1976 - Calendar

The 1976 Formula One season was the 27th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 championship rounds between January 25 and October 24, 1976.

Widely regarded as the most dramatic, legendary, and emotionally charged season in Formula One history, 1976 centered upon the epic duel between Ferrari's **Niki Lauda** and McLaren's **James Hunt**:
- **Lauda's Dominance & Crash:** Lauda dominated the first half of the season with five victories. At the German Grand Prix on August 1—the final F1 race on the classic Nürburgring Nordschleife—Lauda suffered a horrific fiery crash at Bergwerk, receiving the Last Rites in hospital with severe burns and damaged lungs.
- **The Miracle of Monza:** Just 42 days later, his face heavily bandaged and bleeding through his helmet, Lauda returned to race at Monza, finishing an astonishing 4th.
- **Hunt's Charge & The Fuji Deluge:** Hunt mounted a furious second-half fightback, winning six races. In the torrential downpour of the Mount Fuji season finale in Japan, Lauda withdrew on safety grounds, and Hunt charged through the spray to finish 3rd and **clinch the World Drivers' Championship by a single point (69 to 68)**.
- **Innovations:** The season also witnessed the only victory in history for a six-wheeled car (Jody Scheckter in the Tyrrell P34 in Sweden) and the inaugural United States Grand Prix West at Long Beach.
