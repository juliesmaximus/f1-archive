# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1976 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and the best **7** results from the final 8 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

British driver James Hunt won his first and only World Drivers' Championship with 69 net points (69 gross), defeating Ferrari's Niki Lauda (68 net points / 68 gross) by a single point in the Mount Fuji finale.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and best **7** results from the final 8 races counted towards the championship.

Scuderia Ferrari won their fourth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 83 net points (83 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 7 results from rounds 1–8 and best 7 results from rounds 9–16 counted towards both championships.
  - McLaren-Ford scored 75 gross points, dropping 1 point (6th place at Monaco) to finish with 74 net points.
  - Tyrrell-Ford scored 88 gross points, dropping 17 points to finish with 71 net points.
- **Technical Regulations:**
  - Tall over-the-head induction airboxes banned from round 4 (Spanish GP); maximum airbox height restricted to 85 cm from the ground.
  - Six-wheeled cars officially permitted (Derek Gardner's Tyrrell P34).
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - James Hunt won the World Drivers' Championship by a single point (69 to 68) over Niki Lauda in the legendary Mount Fuji deluge.
  - Niki Lauda's near-fatal crash at the Nürburgring Nordschleife (the final F1 Grand Prix on the classic circuit) and miraculous return 42 days later at Monza.
  - Jody Scheckter won the Swedish Grand Prix at Anderstorp in the six-wheeled Tyrrell P34 (only 6-wheeled victory in F1 history).
  - John Watson scored the sole Formula One victory for Team Penske at the Österreichring.
  - Mario Andretti won the season finale at Fuji Speedway, scoring Team Lotus's first win in over two years.
  - Inaugural Formula One World Championship Grands Prix at Long Beach (USA West) and Mount Fuji (Japan).
