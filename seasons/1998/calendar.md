# 1998 FIA Formula 1 World Championship - Season Calendar

The 1998 FIA Formula 1 World Championship was the 49th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 rounds across 5 continents, running from March 8 to November 1, 1998. Mika Häkkinen won his maiden World Drivers' Championship with McLaren-Mercedes after a season-long battle with Michael Schumacher, while McLaren captured their first World Constructors' Championship since 1991.

## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Grooved Tyres Mandate:** To reduce cornering speeds, slick tyres were banned. Cars had to run grooved dry tyres with circular longitudinal grooves (3 grooves on front tyres, 4 grooves on rear tyres, minimum 2.5 mm depth).
- **Narrow-Track Chassis Dimensions:** Overall car width was reduced from 2,000 mm to 1,800 mm to further diminish aerodynamic grip and mechanical cornering stability.
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated engines (maximum 10 cylinders).
- **McLaren Brake Steer Ban:** The controversial second brake pedal ('fiddle brake') allowing independent rear brake bias was banned after Round 1 in Australia.
