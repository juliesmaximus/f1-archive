## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The Grooved Tyre Revolution:** Slicks were abolished in favour of grooved dry tyres (3 grooves front, 4 grooves rear) and overall chassis width was shrunk from 2.0 m to 1.8 m, completely transforming chassis dynamics and driving styles.
- **Mika Häkkinen's World Title:** Mika Häkkinen achieved his dream of becoming World Champion, taking 8 victories and 9 pole positions in Adrian Newey's McLaren MP4/13, clinching the title at the Suzuka finale.
- **The Deluge of Spa-Francorchamps:** The 1998 Belgian Grand Prix featured the largest pile-up in F1 history on lap 1 (13 cars destroyed), followed by Schumacher crashing into the back of Coulthard's McLaren in torrential spray, an infamous pit-lane confrontation, and Damon Hill leading Ralf Schumacher home for Jordan's historic maiden 1-2 victory.
- **Silverstone Pit-Lane Finish:** Michael Schumacher won the British Grand Prix by entering the pit lane on the final lap to serve a 10-second stop-go penalty, crossing the start/finish line before reaching the Ferrari garage.
