## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Introduction of Grooved Tyres & Narrow Track:** To drastically curtail cornering speeds, slick tyres were completely banned. All cars were required to run grooved dry tyres (3 grooves in front, 4 grooves in rear). Overall car width was simultaneously reduced from 2,000 mm to 1,800 mm.
- **Mika Häkkinen's First World Championship:** Overcoming his near-fatal 1995 crash, Mika Häkkinen won 8 races to become Finland's second World Champion (following Keke Rosberg in 1982).
- **McLaren's Constructors' Crown:** McLaren won their 8th World Constructors' Championship and their first with engine partner Mercedes-Benz.
- **The Spa-Francorchamps Deluge:** The 1998 Belgian Grand Prix featured the biggest start pile-up in F1 history (13 cars destroyed), followed by Schumacher's collision with Coulthard in blinding spray and Jordan's historic maiden 1-2 victory.
- **Silverstone Pit-Lane Finish:** Schumacher served a 10-second stop-go penalty on the final lap by crossing the finish line inside the pit lane to win the British Grand Prix.
