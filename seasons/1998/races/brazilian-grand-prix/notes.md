## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Following a formal protest by Ferrari, McLaren's innovative 'fiddle brake' (second brake pedal for rear-wheel yaw steering) was banned. Despite the ban, McLaren dominated again, with Häkkinen winning from pole position ahead of teammate Coulthard and Michael Schumacher in 3rd.
