## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A legendary strategic masterpiece from Ross Brawn and Michael Schumacher. Realizing Schumacher could not pass the McLarens on track, Brawn switched Schumacher to an aggressive three-stop strategy, instructing him over the radio: 'Michael, you have 19 laps to pull out 25 seconds.' Schumacher drove 19 qualifying-speed laps, building the necessary buffer to emerge from his final pit stop in the lead, winning ahead of Coulthard and Villeneuve. Häkkinen suffered shock absorber failure and dropped to 6th.
