## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A delirious 1-2 victory for Ferrari at Monza. Michael Schumacher won ahead of teammate Eddie Irvine, drawing level with Mika Häkkinen on 80 points in the World Championship standings with two races remaining. Häkkinen led early until brake problems caused him to spin off, recovering to finish 4th.
