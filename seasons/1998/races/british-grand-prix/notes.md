## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most bizarre and controversial race finishes in history. In heavy rain, Häkkinen led until spinning into the gravel, damaging his front wing. Schumacher took the lead but was issued a 10-second stop-go penalty on lap 57 for overtaking Wurz under yellow flags. Ferrari boss Ross Brawn noticed the penalty was issued too late and instructed Schumacher to serve the penalty on the final lap. Schumacher entered the pit lane, crossed the start/finish line located in the pit lane to win the race, and then stopped at the Ferrari pit box! Häkkinen finished 2nd and Irvine 3rd.
