## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Debut of narrow-track cars and grooved tyres. McLaren-Mercedes obliterated the field with Adrian Newey's MP4/13, qualifying 0.7s ahead of Schumacher's Ferrari and lapping the entire field during the race! Controversy struck when Häkkinen pitted mistakenly due to a misheard radio call; Coulthard honored a pre-race agreement and allowed Häkkinen past with two laps remaining to take the win, sealing a dominant 1-2. Heinz-Harald Frentzen finished 3rd, a lap behind.
