## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher struck back for Ferrari with a brilliant aggressive drive. Schumacher made contact with Coulthard while overtaking him for 2nd, spinning Coulthard around, then chased down and passed Häkkinen during pit stops to take his first win of the season. Häkkinen finished 2nd and Eddie Irvine 3rd.
