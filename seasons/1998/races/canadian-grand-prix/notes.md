## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A chaotic race featuring three start attempts and five Safety Car periods. Both McLarens retired with mechanical issues (Häkkinen gearbox, Coulthard throttle). Michael Schumacher took victory despite serving a 10-second stop-go penalty for forcing Frentzen off track while exiting the pit lane. Fisichella finished 2nd and Irvine 3rd.
