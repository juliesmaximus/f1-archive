## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The championship decider. Michael Schumacher took pole position but stalled on the grid before the start, demoting him to the very back of the 22-car grid. Häkkinen grabbed the lead into Turn 1. Schumacher drove an astonishing recovery through the field, reaching 3rd place by lap 28. On lap 31 down the main straight, Schumacher's right-rear Goodyear tyre exploded after running over carbon debris from a previous crash, ending his title hopes. Mika Häkkinen cruised to victory to win his first Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship, with Eddie Irvine 2nd and David Coulthard 3rd. McLaren-Mercedes clinched the World Constructors' Championship.
