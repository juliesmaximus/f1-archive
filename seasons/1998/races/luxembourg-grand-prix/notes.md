## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A crucial psychological breakthrough for Mika Häkkinen. Ferrari locked out the front row (Schumacher pole, Irvine 2nd). Häkkinen passed Irvine with a daring lunge into Turn 1, then jumped Schumacher during the second round of pit stops, holding him off to win by 2.2 seconds. The win gave Häkkinen a 4-point lead heading into the Suzuka decider.
