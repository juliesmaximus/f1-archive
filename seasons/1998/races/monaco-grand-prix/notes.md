## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Häkkinen claimed his maiden Monaco Grand Prix victory from pole position, leading every lap. Schumacher made an aggressive move on Wurz at the hairpin, bending his rear suspension and forcing an unscheduled pit stop; he recovered to finish 10th. Giancarlo Fisichella finished 2nd for Benetton and Eddie Irvine 3rd.
