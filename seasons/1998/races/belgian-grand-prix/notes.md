## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most famous, chaotic races in motorsport history. On lap 1, Coulthard lost control on the damp metal drain cover exiting La Source, triggering a catastrophic 13-car pileup that blocked the track and necessitated a red flag. On the restart, Häkkinen spun into Herbert, eliminating both. Schumacher took the lead and pulled out a 40-second advantage. On lap 25 in blinding spray down the Kemmel straight, Schumacher approached Coulthard to lap him; Coulthard lifted on the racing line, and Schumacher slammed into the back of the McLaren at 140 mph, ripping off his right-front wheel. Both cars limped to the pits, where a furious Schumacher stormed down to the McLaren garage to physically confront Coulthard ('Are you trying to f***ing kill me?!'). Damon Hill led teammate Ralf Schumacher home to score Jordan Grand Prix's first ever Formula 1 victory in an unforgettable 1-2 finish, with Jean Alesi 3rd for Sauber.
