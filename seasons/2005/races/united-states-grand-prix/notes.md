## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The darkest, most controversial farce in modern Formula 1 history. Multiple catastrophic Michelin tyre failures during Friday practice (Ralf Schumacher hospitalized after crashing at 190 mph into Turn 13 banking) revealed Michelin tyres could not survive the banking. Michelin informed the FIA that without an emergency chicane at Turn 13, their cars could not race safely. Ferrari, Jordan, and Minardi (on Bridgestone tyres) refused to alter the track. All 14 Michelin cars completed the formation lap and pulled into the pitlane, abandoning the race. Only 6 Bridgestone cars started. Michael Schumacher won ahead of Barrichello, while Tiago Monteiro celebrated Jordan's first podium since 2003 to the sound of boos and flying beer cans from furious fans.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Catastrophic public relations disaster that destroyed Formula 1's reputation in the United States for years; Michelin offered ticket refunds and bought 20,000 tickets for the 2006 race.
