## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Fernando Alonso (Renault)
- **Pole Position:** Fernando Alonso (Renault) - 3:01.902 (aggregate)
- **Fastest Lap:** Pedro de la Rosa (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:31.447 (lap 43)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Alonso drove another flawless race to score back-to-back victories. Pedro de la Rosa, deputizing for the injured Juan Pablo Montoya at McLaren, drove an inspired race with numerous daring overtakes to finish 5th and set the fastest lap. Jarno Trulli finished 2nd, confirming Toyota as genuine front-runners.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Michael Schumacher debuted Ferrari's new F2005 chassis, qualifying 2nd but retiring on lap 12 with hydraulic failure—his first mechanical retirement since Hockenheim 2001.
