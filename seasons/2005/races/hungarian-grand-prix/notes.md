## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Michael Schumacher took pole position and led early, but Kimi Räikkönen used McLaren's devastating race pace to jump Schumacher during the second pitstops, pulling away to win by 35.5 seconds. Fernando Alonso damaged his front wing against Ralf Schumacher at Turn 1 and finished out of the points in 11th.
