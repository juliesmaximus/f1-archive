## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Renault delivered a masterclass in the season finale, Fernando Alonso dominating from pole position to win by 4.0 seconds over Kimi Räikkönen. Alonso's victory sealed the World Constructors' Championship for Renault (191 points to McLaren's 182), completing a dream championship double.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Bizarre drain cover incident: on lap 30, a metal drain cover became dislodged and was struck by Juan Pablo Montoya's McLaren, triggering a safety car period.
