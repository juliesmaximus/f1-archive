## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Fernando Alonso (Renault)
- **Pole Position:** Fernando Alonso (Renault) - 3:07.672 (aggregate)
- **Fastest Lap:** Kimi Räikkönen (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:35.483 (lap 23)

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso dominated from pole position to claim his second career Grand Prix victory, leading Jarno Trulli's Toyota by 24 seconds to take the World Championship lead for the first time in his career. Toyota scored their first-ever Formula 1 podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Extreme tyre degradation in 54°C track temperatures under the no-tyre-change rule caused severe blistering across the field.
