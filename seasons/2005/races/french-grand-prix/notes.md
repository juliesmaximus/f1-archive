## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso dominated Renault's home Grand Prix from pole position, leading Kimi Räikkönen home by 11.8 seconds. Räikkönen drove an exceptional recovery race from 13th on the grid following a 10-place penalty for an engine failure in practice. Michael Schumacher completed the podium in 3rd.
