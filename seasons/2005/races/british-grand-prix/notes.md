## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Juan Pablo Montoya scored his first victory for McLaren-Mercedes with a breathtaking start from 3rd on the grid, aggressively muscling past pole-sitter Fernando Alonso into Becketts on lap 1. Montoya controlled the pace flawlessly to win by 2.7 seconds over Alonso. Räikkönen again charged from 12th on the grid (engine penalty) to finish 3rd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Held just three days after the London 7/7 bombings; the grid held a somber minute of silence in solidarity.
