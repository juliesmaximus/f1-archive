## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen drove an imperious race to win in Monte Carlo. When Christijan Albers spun at Mirabeau on lap 24 blocking the track, McLaren chose not to pit Räikkönen while Renault pitted both cars. Räikkönen built a massive 34-second lead before his pitstop, while Alonso's rear tyres wore down to canvas, dropping him behind Williams teammates Nick Heidfeld and Mark Webber.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Mark Webber scored his first career podium in 3rd after pulling off a daring dive down the inside of Alonso into the chicane.
