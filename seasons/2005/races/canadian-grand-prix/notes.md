## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
McLaren triumphed after both Renaults collapsed from the lead. Fisichella suffered hydraulics failure, and Alonso struck the 'Wall of Champions' on lap 38. Juan Pablo Montoya led until he was disqualified for exiting the pitlane under a red light during a Safety Car period, handing victory to teammate Kimi Räikkönen ahead of Michael Schumacher.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Montoya's pit exit black flag infuriated McLaren management.
