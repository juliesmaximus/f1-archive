## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
More agony for Kimi Räikkönen. Leading comfortably from pole with a 12-second margin, Räikkönen's McLaren suffered hydraulic failure on lap 35, handing an easy victory to Fernando Alonso. Juan Pablo Montoya charged from 20th on the grid (after crashing in qualifying) to take 2nd, while Jenson Button scored BAR-Honda's first podium of 2005.
