## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Inaugural Grand Prix at Hermann Tilke's masterpiece, Istanbul Park, featuring the legendary multi-apex, high-g Turn 8. Kimi Räikkönen controlled the race from pole position to win. Juan Pablo Montoya looked set to complete a McLaren 1-2 until he was rear-ended by Tiago Monteiro with two laps to go, damaging his diffuser and causing him to run wide at Turn 8 on the final lap, allowing Alonso to steal 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Turn 8 subjected drivers to continuous 5G lateral forces for nearly 8 seconds, causing severe neck strain across the grid.
