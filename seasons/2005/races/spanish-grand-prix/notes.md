## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen and McLaren-Mercedes produced an untouchable performance, leading every single lap from pole to win by 27.6 seconds over championship leader Fernando Alonso. The win ended Renault's unbeaten start to the 2005 season.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Final Grand Prix to use the unpopular two-session aggregate qualifying system; FIA abandoned aggregate qualifying immediately after this race.
