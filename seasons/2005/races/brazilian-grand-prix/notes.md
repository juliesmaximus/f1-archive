## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso made history! Needing only a podium to guarantee the title, Alonso drove a measured, disciplined race from pole to finish 3rd behind the McLaren 1-2 of Montoya and Räikkönen. At 24 years, 58 days, Fernando Alonso became the youngest World Drivers' Champion in Formula 1 history, ending Michael Schumacher's five-year reign and breaking Emerson Fittipaldi's 33-year record.
