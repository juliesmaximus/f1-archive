## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Renault delivered a masterclass under the new one-set-of-tyres-per-race rule. Giancarlo Fisichella controlled the race from pole position to take an emphatic victory. Teammate Fernando Alonso charged brilliantly from 13th on the grid to 3rd behind Rubens Barrichello.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First race under aggregated two-session qualifying (Saturday low fuel + Sunday morning race fuel) and complete ban on tyre changes.
