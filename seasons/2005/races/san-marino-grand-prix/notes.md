## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An immortal classic wheel-to-wheel thriller. Michael Schumacher started 13th and unleashed an unbelievable charge in the Ferrari F2005, lapping up to 2 seconds faster than the field. Schumacher caught race leader Fernando Alonso with 12 laps remaining. In a masterclass of defensive precision, Alonso positioned his Renault perfectly around every corner, holding off Schumacher by 0.215 seconds at the line.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
BAR-Honda was disqualified after Jenson Button's 3rd-place car was found to possess a concealed secondary fuel tank operating below the 600 kg minimum weight. The FIA International Court of Appeal banned BAR from the next two races (Spain and Monaco).
