## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Montoya drove a brave race on blistering rear tyres to hold off Fernando Alonso by 2.4 seconds, with Giancarlo Fisichella 3rd. Kimi Räikkönen suffered yet another 10-place engine penalty after taking fastest time in qualifying; he executed a 1-stop strategy and was on track to win before his left-rear tyre delaminated, forcing an unscheduled stop to finish 4th. Remarkably, every single one of the 20 starters finished the race—the first zero-retirement GP since 1961 Zandvoort.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Zero retirements in a 20-car field at Monza set a modern reliability benchmark.
