## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Heartbreak on the final lap. Kimi Räikkönen led comfortably but flat-spotted his right-front Michelin tyre while lapping Jacques Villeneuve mid-race. Under the no-tyre-change rule, the vibrations grew violently worse with every lap, rattling his suspension. As Räikkönen started the final lap, his right-front suspension snapped violently at 190 mph into Turn 1, hurtling the McLaren into the gravel. Fernando Alonso swept past to steal an extraordinary victory.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The crash sparked furious safety debates over the single-set tyre rule, as teams were reluctant to pit for safety due to the severe time penalty.
