## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Arguably the greatest Grand Prix victory of modern Formula 1 history. Rain during one-lap qualifying left championship frontrunners Räikkönen (17th), Alonso (16th), and Schumacher (14th) at the back of the grid. What followed was racing divinity: Alonso executed an audacious 205 mph pass on Michael Schumacher around the outside of the terrifying 130R corner. Meanwhile, Kimi Räikkönen drove like a demon, carving through the field with blistering pace. On the final lap, Räikkönen caught leader Giancarlo Fisichella, swept to the outside into Turn 1 at 200 mph, and pulled off an astonishing pass to win the race by 1.6 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Alonso was controversially ordered by race control to yield position back to Christian Klien after cutting the chicane, costing him 8 seconds.
