## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen claimed his second consecutive Spa victory. In wet-dry conditions, McLaren dominated with Montoya leading until Antonio Pizzonia (Williams) collided with him while unlapping himself with three laps to go! Alonso inherited 2nd, keeping one hand firmly on the world title.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Montoya was furious with Pizzonia, confronting him in the paddock after the race.
