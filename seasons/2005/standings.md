## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Fernando Alonso & Renault Double Triumph:** Fernando Alonso became the youngest World Drivers' Champion in Formula 1 history at 24 years, 58 days, winning 7 Grands Prix. Renault captured their first Constructors' World Championship as a full works constructor.
- **The Ban on Tyre Changes:** For 2005 only, tyre changes were banned during the race; a single set had to last qualifying and the entire 300 km race distance.
- **The Indianapolis Farce:** The infamous 6-car United States Grand Prix occurred after Michelin withdrew all 14 of its cars due to sidewall failure risks on the banked final turn.
- **Suzuka 2005 Miracle:** Kimi Räikkönen drove from 17th on the grid to win the Japanese Grand Prix with an iconic final-lap outside overtake on Giancarlo Fisichella.
