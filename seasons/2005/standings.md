## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Fernando Alonso & Renault Double Triumph:** Fernando Alonso became the youngest World Drivers' Champion in Formula 1 history at the time (24 years, 58 days), securing his title at the Brazilian Grand Prix with 7 victories and 15 podiums. Renault captured their first World Constructors' Championship as a full constructor with 191 points.
- **The Ban on Tyre Changes:** Under unique 2005 regulations, tyre changes during the race were prohibited except for safety punctures or changing weather conditions; a single set of tyres had to last both qualifying and the entire Grand Prix distance.
- **The Indianapolis Farce:** At the United States Grand Prix, catastrophic Michelin tyre failures in practice prompted Michelin to declare their tyres unsafe for Turn 13 banking. When compromise chicane proposals were rejected by the FIA, all 14 Michelin runners pulled into the pit lane after the formation lap, leaving just 6 Bridgestone-shod cars (Ferrari, Jordan, Minardi) to contest the race.
- **Suzuka 2005 Miracle:** Starting from 17th on the grid in changeable conditions, Kimi Räikkönen drove what is widely regarded as one of the greatest races in F1 history, sweeping around the outside of Giancarlo Fisichella into Turn 1 on the 53rd and final lap to take victory.
