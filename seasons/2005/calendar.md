## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **No Tyre Changes:** One set of tyres for qualifying and the entire race distance.
- **Two-Race Engine Rule:** Each 3.0L V10 engine had to last two full race weekends.
- **Downforce Reductions:** Raised front wing, moved rear wing forward, reduced diffuser dimensions.
- **Record Calendar:** 19 Grands Prix, introducing Istanbul Park in Turkey.
