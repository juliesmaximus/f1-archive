# 1951 Round 1: XI Großer Preis der Schweiz / XI Grand Prix de Suisse - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XI Großer Preis der Schweiz / XI Grand Prix de Suisse
- **Historic Name Notes**: 11th running of the Swiss Grand Prix at Circuit Bremgarten; season opener for the 1951 World Championship.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit Bremgarten
- **Location**: Bremgarten bei Bern, Canton of Bern, Switzerland
- **Host Country**: Switzerland
- **Track Length**: 7.280 km (4.524 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 550 m (1804 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 42 laps / 305.760 km (190.008 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1951-05-27

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Torrential rain throughout the afternoon; wet and slippery asphalt course through Bremgarten forest.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 159) – 2:35.900
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 159) – 2:51.100 (Lap 33) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 159) – 2:07:53.640 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Juan Manuel Fangio (Alfa Romeo 159) – 2:07:53.640 [9 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Alfa Romeo 159 Debut**: Alfa Romeo debuted the upgraded 159 'Alfetta', equipped with a De Dion rear axle and an uprated 1.5L supercharged straight-8 developing roughly 420 bhp.
- **Fangio Wet-Weather Dominance**: Starting from pole position (2:35.900), Fangio managed treacherous rain conditions, set the fastest race lap (2:51.100), and won by over 55 seconds.
- **Piero Taruffi Strong Ferrari Runner**: Driving the naturally aspirated 4.5L Ferrari 375, Piero Taruffi finished 2nd, the sole non-Alfa Romeo driver on the podium.
- **Fangio Ties Farina Career Wins**: Fangio earned his 4th career World Championship Grand Prix victory, tying Nino Farina's record.
