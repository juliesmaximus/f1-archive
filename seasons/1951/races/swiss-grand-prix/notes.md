## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Wet Weather Mastery: Rain fell heavily throughout the afternoon. Juan Manuel Fangio demonstrated supreme car control in the new Alfa Romeo 159, leading from pole to flag.
- Alfa Romeo 159 Evolution: Alfa Romeo introduced the upgraded 159 'Alfetta', featuring a De Dion rear axle and an uprated 1.5L supercharged straight-8 producing approximately 420 bhp.
- Piero Taruffi impressed in wet conditions, bringing his Scuderia Ferrari 375 into 2nd place (+55.2s), finishing as the highest non-Alfa Romeo.
- Farina Fuel Trouble: Defending world champion Nino Farina ran 2nd early on but slipped to 3rd after requiring an extra pit stop in deteriorating conditions.
