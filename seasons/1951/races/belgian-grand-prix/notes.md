## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Farina Masterclass**: Nino Farina drove an error-free race in his Alfa Romeo 159, leading from lap 15 to take victory by nearly three minutes over Alberto Ascari.
- **Fangio Wheel Jam Drama**: Juan Manuel Fangio started on pole position and set the fastest lap (4:22.100), but his race was undone during a pit stop when the left-rear wheel seized on its splines. The Alfa Romeo crew spent over 14 minutes attempting to dislodge it; Fangio eventually rejoined but finished 9th (+4 laps), outside the points.
- **Ferrari Closes the Gap**: Alberto Ascari (2nd) and Luigi Villoresi (3rd) gave Scuderia Ferrari a double podium finish behind Farina.
- **Farina Sets All-Time Win Record**: Farina claimed his 4th career Grand Prix victory, regaining sole leadership of the all-time wins table.
