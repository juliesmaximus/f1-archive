## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Farina Endurance Victory: Nino Farina claimed victory in his Alfa Romeo 159 by nearly three minutes over Alberto Ascari's Ferrari 375.
- Fangio Pit Stop Calamity: Juan Manuel Fangio's race was ruined during his scheduled pit stop when a rear wheel became jammed on its splines. Mechanics spent over 14 minutes hammering at the wheel before releasing him; he finished 9th, outside the points.
- Ascari Chassis Tire Degradation: Ferrari's 4.5L unblown V12 gave notice of closing the performance gap to Alfa Romeo, with Ascari finishing 2nd and Luigi Villoresi 3rd.

## Records Set or Equalled
