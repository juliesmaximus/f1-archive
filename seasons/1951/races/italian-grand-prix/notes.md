## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari Back-to-Back Wins: Alberto Ascari dominated Monza in his Ferrari 375, scoring his second consecutive Grand Prix win.
- Alfa Romeo Reliability Disaster: Alfa Romeo suffered catastrophic engine failures on home soil. Fangio retired on lap 39 with engine failure, and Farina retired on lap 6 with engine failure.
- Farina Shared Car Charge: After retiring his own car, Farina took over Felice Bonetto's Alfa 159 on lap 30 and charged through the field to finish 3rd, sharing 4 points with Bonetto (2 points each) plus 1 point for fastest lap.
- Championship Parity: Ascari's win moved him to within 2 points of championship leader Fangio (27 pts vs 25 pts gross), setting up a winner-take-all season finale.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Ascari became the first driver to win back-to-back Grand Prix races for Ferrari.
- Ferrari scored their third consecutive 1951 Grand Prix victory.
