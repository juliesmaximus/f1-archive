# 1951 Round 7: XXII Gran Premio d'Italia - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XXII Gran Premio d'Italia
- **Historic Name Notes**: 22nd running of the Italian Grand Prix; penultimate round of the 1951 World Championship.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza
- **Location**: Monza, Lombardy, Italy
- **Host Country**: Italy

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Ferrari 1-2 on Home Soil**: Alberto Ascari and José Froilán González scored Scuderia Ferrari's first ever 1-2 finish on home turf at Monza.
- **Alfa Romeo Engine Attrition**: Defending constructor champions Alfa Romeo suffered severe engine attrition on Monza's high-speed straights. Juan Manuel Fangio retired on lap 39 with engine failure, and Nino Farina retired his original car #34 on lap 6 with engine failure.
- **Farina Shared Car Charge**: After his own retirement, Farina took over teammate Felice Bonetto's car #40 on lap 30, charged back to finish 3rd (+1 lap), and set the fastest lap (1:56.500), earning 3 points (split 2 pts for 3rd + 1 FL).
- **Ascari Moves Within Two Points of Fangio**: With his second consecutive victory, Ascari moved to 25 net points, putting him just two points behind championship leader Fangio (27 counted points) entering the season finale.
