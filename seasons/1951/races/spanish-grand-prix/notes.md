## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Inaugural Spanish Grand Prix and Championship Decider: Fangio entered with 27 points (28 gross), Ascari with 25 points (28 gross).
- Ferrari Tyre Blunder: Ferrari fitted smaller 16-inch rear wheels instead of their usual 18-inch wheels, believing it would improve acceleration. The tyres overheated rapidly and threw their treads, forcing Ascari and González into multiple unscheduled pit stops.
- Ascari dropped to 4th place (+2 laps), losing the championship.
- Fangio Maiden World Championship: Fangio ran flawlessly on Pirelli tyres to take victory and clinch his first World Drivers' Championship with 31 net points (37 gross).
- End of an Era: This was Alfa Romeo's final World Championship race as a factory constructor until 1979; the Italian government refused to fund a new car for 1952.
