## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Ferrari Tyre Strategic Catastrophe**: Ferrari chose to fit smaller 16-inch wheels instead of 18-inch wheels, seeking rapid acceleration out of the street corners. On the abrasive surface of Avenida del Generalísimo Franco, the tyres severely overheated, shedding treads after only 8 to 10 laps. Ascari, González, and Villoresi were forced into multiple unscheduled pit stops to replace delaminating rubber.
- **Fangio Clinches Maiden World Championship**: Driving on durable 18-inch Pirelli tyres, Juan Manuel Fangio drove smoothly, took the lead on lap 4, and won by 54.280 seconds to clinch his FIRST FORMULA ONE WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP with 31 net points.
- **Ascari Relegated to 4th**: Alberto Ascari started from pole position (2:10.590) but suffered three tyre stops, finishing 4th (+2 laps) and securing 2nd in the World Championship with 25 net points.
- **Alfa Romeo Factory Farewell**: Following this race, Alfa Romeo withdrew from Grand Prix racing as a factory team due to escalating development costs and a lack of government subsidies, closing their historic inaugural era with back-to-back world drivers' titles.
