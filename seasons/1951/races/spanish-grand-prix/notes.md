- **Circuit Full Name**: Pedralbes Circuit

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ferrari Tyre Blunder Decides Championship: Ferrari elected to use smaller 16-inch wheels/tyres instead of 18-inch wheels, hoping for quicker acceleration. On the abrasive Pedralbes street surface, the smaller tyres severely overheated and threw treads within 10 laps. Ascari, González, and Villoresi were forced to make multiple unscheduled tyre stops.
- Fangio First World Title: Fangio drove flawlessly on durable 18-inch Pirelli tyres, won the Grand Prix by 54.28 seconds over José Froilán González, and clinched his FIRST FORMULA ONE WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP.
- Alfa Romeo Factory Retirement: With Alfa Romeo's public funding depleted and the 159 having reached the maximum limit of its 1.5L supercharged design, Alfa Romeo officially announced their withdrawal from Formula One racing following this race.
- Ascari relegated to 4th place (+2 laps), missing out on the championship despite leading early from pole position.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Juan Manuel Fangio crowned 1951 Formula One World Champion of Drivers (first of his 5 world titles).
- Final World Championship victory and appearance for the factory Alfa Romeo team until 1979.
