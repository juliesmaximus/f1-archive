## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Scuderia Ferrari Maiden Victory: José Froilán González ('El Cabezón' / 'The Pampas Bull') scored Scuderia Ferrari's FIRST EVER Formula One World Championship Grand Prix win, ending Alfa Romeo's unbeaten European streak.
- Ferrari Maiden Pole Position: González took Ferrari's maiden World Championship pole position on Friday (1:43.4), breaking Alfa Romeo's clean sweep of pole positions.
- Fuel Economy Decisive: The unblown 4.5L Lampredi V12 in the Ferrari 375 required only one pit stop for fuel, while the supercharged Alfa Romeo 159 consumed fuel at ~1.5 mpg and required two stops. This efficiency proved decisive as González beat Fangio by 51.0 seconds.
- Enzo Ferrari's Famous Words: Enzo Ferrari famously commented after the race: 'I have killed my mother', mourning the defeat of Alfa Romeo, the company where his racing career had started 30 years prior.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship victory and pole position for Scuderia Ferrari.
- First World Championship win for José Froilán González.
- First defeat of Alfa Romeo in a World Championship Grand Prix.
