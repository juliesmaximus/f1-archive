## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Scuderia Ferrari Maiden Victory**: Argentine driver José Froilán González ('El Cabezón') scored Scuderia Ferrari's FIRST EVER Formula One World Championship Grand Prix victory, ending Alfa Romeo's unbroken European winning streak.
- **Scuderia Ferrari Maiden Pole Position**: González captured Ferrari's first ever World Championship pole position during Friday qualifying with a time of 1:43.400.
- **Fuel Economy Supremacy**: Ferrari's naturally aspirated 4.5L Lampredi V12 required only a single pit stop for refuelling, whereas the supercharged 1.5L Alfa Romeo 159 required two stops due to heavy fuel consumption (~1.5 mpg). This tactical advantage allowed González to defeat Fangio by 51.000 seconds.
- **Enzo Ferrari Famous Quote**: Following the victory, Enzo Ferrari famously remarked: *"I wept with joy, but my tears of enthusiasm were also mingled with tears of sorrow, because I thought that today I have killed my mother"* (referring to Alfa Romeo, where his racing career had started).
