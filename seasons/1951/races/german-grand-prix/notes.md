# 1951 Round 6: XIV Großer Preis von Deutschland - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XIV Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Historic Name Notes**: 14th running of the German Grand Prix; first time the event was included on the official Formula One World Championship calendar.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Nürburgring Nordschleife
- **Location**: Nürburg, Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany
- **Host Country**: Germany
- **Track Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 620 m (2034 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 20 laps / 456.200 km (283.470 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1951-07-29

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Alberto Ascari Maiden Grand Prix Victory**: Alberto Ascari scored his FIRST career Formula One World Championship race victory, and the second consecutive victory for Scuderia Ferrari.
- **Ascari Pole Position**: Ascari earned pole position with a lap time of 9:55.800 around the demanding 22.810 km Nordschleife circuit.
- **Identical Qualifying & Fastest Lap Times**: In a remarkable coincidence, Juan Manuel Fangio's fastest race lap on lap 12 was 9:55.800 — the exact same time as Ascari's pole position time.
- **Fangio Gearbox Trouble**: Fangio ran into gearbox troubles (loss of 1st and 2nd gears) during the second half of the race, preventing him from chasing down Ascari and finishing 30.5 seconds behind.
