# 1951 Round 6: XIV Großer Preis von Deutschland - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XIV Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Historic Name Notes**: 14th German Grand Prix; first time the German Grand Prix was included in the Formula One World Championship calendar.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Nürburgring Nordschleife
- **Location**: Nürburg, Eifel Mountains, Rhineland-Palatinate, West Germany
- **Host Country**: West Germany
- **Track Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 620 m (2034 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 20 laps / 456.200 km (283.470 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1951-07-29

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Warm, dry, sunny across the Eifel mountain circuit.
- **Spectator Attendance**: Over 120,000 spectators across the 22.8 km perimeter.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Historic start-and-finish grandstand (Start-und-Ziel) opposite pit row.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Alberto Ascari Maiden Victory: Alberto Ascari achieved his FIRST Formula One World Championship Grand Prix victory, leading home a Ferrari 1-2 with José Froilán González.
- Nürburgring World Championship Debut: The formidable 22.810 km Nordschleife made its official World Championship debut. Ascari became the first driver to break the 10-minute barrier in official qualifying (9:55.8).
- Fangio Pit Tyre Woes: Juan Manuel Fangio led the opening laps, but two tyre changes and gearbox issues on his Alfa Romeo 159 relegated him to 2nd place, 30.5 seconds behind Ascari.
- Championship Race Opens Up: Ascari's victory threw the 1951 championship wide open, reducing Fangio's lead to 10 points with two rounds remaining.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Maiden World Championship win and pole position for Alberto Ascari.
- First World Championship 1-2 finish for Scuderia Ferrari (Ascari, González).
