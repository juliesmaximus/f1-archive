## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- All-Time Oldest Winner Record: Luigi Fagioli won at the age of 53 years, 22 days, setting a record as the oldest Grand Prix winner in Formula 1 history that still stands.
- Shared Victory Drama: Fangio's car developed magneto failure; team orders instructed Fagioli to pit on lap 20 and surrender his healthy car to Fangio. Fagioli took over Fangio's hobbled car and finished 11th. Fagioli was so infuriated by the team orders that he quit Grand Prix racing on the spot.
- Dual Shared Podium: 1st and 2nd places were both shared drives (Fagioli/Fangio 1st, Ascari/González 2nd).
- Longest Grand Prix: At 601.755 km, this remains the longest total race distance in Formula One World Championship history.
