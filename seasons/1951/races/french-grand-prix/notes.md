## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Shared Victory (Fagioli & Fangio): Juan Manuel Fangio's own car suffered magneto and fuel pipe failure on lap 15. At lap 20, Alfa Romeo team orders directed Luigi Fagioli to pit and hand his healthy 159 over to Fangio. Fangio mounted a fierce charge to win the race, while Fagioli took over Fangio's repaired car and finished 11th.
- Oldest Race Winner in F1 History: By virtue of sharing the winning car, Luigi Fagioli (53 years, 22 days) became the OLDEST GRAND PRIX WINNER IN FORMULA ONE HISTORY — a record that stands unbroken to this day.
- Fagioli Furious Resignation: Disgusted at being forced to give up his winning car to Fangio, Fagioli quit the factory Alfa Romeo team on the spot and never competed in another Formula One World Championship race.
- Second Shared Podium for Ascari & González: Alberto Ascari retired on lap 10 with gearbox failure, but took over teammate José Froilán González's Ferrari 375 on lap 35, driving it to 2nd place. Ascari and González shared the 6 points (3 points each).

## Records Set or Equalled
- Oldest Grand Prix winner in Formula One history: Luigi Fagioli (53 years, 22 days) — all-time record.
- Longest Grand Prix in Formula One history by distance: 601.832 km (373.961 mi).
