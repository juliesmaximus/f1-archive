## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Lee Wallard Breakthrough: Wallard dominated the race in the #99 Belanger Motors Kurtis Kraft-Offenhauser, leading 159 laps to win by nearly two minutes.
- Extreme Physical Toll: Wallard drove the final 40 laps with broken shock absorbers and damaged exhaust piping that severely burned his legs, requiring medical treatment immediately after victory.
- Sub-4-Hour Record: Wallard became the first driver to complete the Indianapolis 500 in under four hours (3 hours, 57 minutes, 38.05 seconds), averaging a record 126.244 mph (203.170 km/h).
- Tragic Aftermath: Just one week after his victory, Wallard suffered severe burns in a sprint car crash in Pennsylvania that effectively ended his professional racing career.

## Records Set or Equalled
