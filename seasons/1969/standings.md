# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1969 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **4** results from the final 5 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Jackie Stewart won his maiden World Drivers' Championship with 63 net points (63 gross), winning 6 of the 11 rounds for Matra International.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and best **4** results from the final 5 races counted towards the championship.

Matra-Ford won their maiden International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 66 net points (70 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 5 results from rounds 1–6 and best 4 results from rounds 7–11 counted towards both championships.
- **Aerodynamic Regulations:** Permanent ban on high aerofoils mounted to suspension uprights following terrifying crashes at Montjuïc; wings restricted to maximum 80 cm height from ground and rigidly affixed to sprung chassis.
- **Four-Wheel Drive (4WD):** Four constructors entered 4WD cars (Lotus 63, Matra MS84, McLaren M9A, Cosworth). Johnny Servoz-Gavin scored the only 4WD World Championship point in history (6th in Canada).
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jackie Stewart and Ken Tyrrell won their maiden World Championships.
  - Matra became the first French constructor to win the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
  - Graham Hill won a record 5th Monaco Grand Prix (a record unbroken until 1993).
  - Monza hosted the closest top-four finish in F1 history (separated by 0.19 seconds).
  - Jochen Rindt took his maiden Grand Prix victory at Watkins Glen.
