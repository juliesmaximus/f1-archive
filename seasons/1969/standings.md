# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1969 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **4** results from the final 5 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Jackie Stewart won his maiden World Drivers' Championship with 63 net points (63 gross), winning 6 of the 11 rounds for Matra International.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **4** results from the final 5 races counted towards the championship.

Matra-Ford won their maiden International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 66 net points (77 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split into two mini-seasons: best 5 of first 6 rounds, best 4 of final 5 rounds.
  - Matra-Ford scored 77 gross points, dropping 11 points to finish with 66 net points.
  - Brabham-Ford scored 51 gross points, dropping 2 points to finish with 49 net points.
  - McLaren-Ford scored 40 gross points, dropping 2 points to finish with 38 net points.
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated or 1.5-litre supercharged/turbocharged. Minimum car weight 500 kg.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jackie Stewart clinched his first World Drivers' Championship, winning 6 of the 11 Grands Prix for Ken Tyrrell's Matra International.
  - Matra became the first and only French constructor to win the World Championship with a chassis powered by the customer Ford Cosworth DFV engine.
  - Massive high wings mounted on suspension uprights were banned mid-season following violent rear wing collapse accidents for Graham Hill and Jochen Rindt at the Spanish GP at Montjuïc.
  - Jochen Rindt scored his maiden World Championship victory at Watkins Glen.
  - Graham Hill suffered severe double leg fractures in a violent crash at the United States GP at Watkins Glen after unbuckling his belts following a puncture.
