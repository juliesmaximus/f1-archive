- **Fastest Lap:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 3:02.700 (Lap 27)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Hot, sunny, dry; 30°C ambient, loose volcanic stones on circuit edges  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent pits grandstand and volcanic hillside natural terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jackie Stewart delivered a total Grand Slam for Matra on French home soil: pole position, fastest lap, led every single one of the 38 laps, and won by 57.1s over teammate Jean-Pierre Beltoise to secure an ecstatic Matra 1-2 finish! Jacky Ickx finished 3rd in the Brabham BT26A. Loose volcanic gravel thrown up by tyres caused multiple punctures and struck drivers' visors.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First French Grand Prix 1-2 finish for a French constructor since Delahaye in 1938. Stewart's 4th win in 5 races.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 38 laps (306.09 km).
