### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE CLOSEST 1-2-3-4 FINISH IN F1 HISTORY: Jochen Rindt took pole for Lotus. The race became an epic 68-lap slipstreaming battle between Stewart, Rindt, Beltoise, and McLaren. Exiting the Curva Parabolica on the final lap, all four cars accelerated side-by-side towards the line. Jackie Stewart crossed the line to WIN BY JUST 0.08 SECONDS over Jochen Rindt, with Jean-Pierre Beltoise 0.09s behind in 3rd, and Bruce McLaren 0.10s behind in 4th! The top 4 cars were separated by just 0.19 SECONDS! With this 6th victory of the season, Jackie Stewart MATHEMATICALLY CLINCHED HIS MAIDEN WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP and Matra won the Constructors' Championship with 3 rounds remaining!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart became Scotland's second World Champion. Matra became the first French constructor to win the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
