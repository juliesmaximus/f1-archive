### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
EMERGENCY WING BAN: Following practice accidents, the CSI met on Friday morning and banned all high suspension-mounted wings with immediate effect. Cars ran stripped of aerofoils for the remainder of the weekend. Jackie Stewart led from pole in the Matra MS80 until driveshaft failure sidelined him on lap 22. Graham Hill took command in the wingless Lotus 49B to take his FIFTH career Monaco Grand Prix victory by 17.3s over Piers Courage (Frank Williams Racing Brabham) and Jo Siffert (Rob Walker Lotus).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Graham Hill set a monumental record of 5 Monaco Grand Prix victories (1963, 1964, 1965, 1968, 1969), cementing his title as 'King of Monaco'. His record stood for 24 years until Ayrton Senna scored his 6th Monaco win in 1993.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Wings banned mid-weekend; maximum 16 starters permitted by organizers.
