**Track Length:** 4.104 km (2.55 miles)

## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 1:50:39.100  
- **Pole Position:** Jack Brabham  (1:20.000)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 1:21.600 (Lap 50)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 26°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jack Brabham took pole position in the Brabham BT26A Ford Cosworth DFV. Jackie Stewart in the Ken Tyrrell Matra MS10 took the lead into the first corner and dominated the race, winning by 18.8s over World Champion Graham Hill (Lotus 49B). Denny Hulme finished 3rd for McLaren. Stewart laid down a clear marker for the 1969 World Championship.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Brabham abandoned the unreliable Repco V8 engine in favour of customer Ford Cosworth DFVs.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 4 of last 5 races counted.
