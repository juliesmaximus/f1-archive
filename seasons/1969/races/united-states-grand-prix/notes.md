## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jochen Rindt (Lotus-Ford) — 1:57:56.840  
- **Pole Position:** Jochen Rindt  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, bright autumn sunshine; 14°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 100,000 spectators packing the Watkins Glen knolls  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Bog, and Seneca hillside mounds  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR JOCHEN RINDT & TRAGEDY FOR GRAHAM HILL: Jochen Rindt dominated the race in the Lotus 49B, leading 99 of 108 laps to claim his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory, taking home the record $50,000 winner's purse! Piers Courage finished 2nd for Frank Williams Brabham, and John Surtees took 3rd for BRM. On lap 88, double World Champion Graham Hill suffered a punctured tyre, spun off the track, unfastened his seatbelts to push-start the car, and on rejoining suffered a catastrophic rollover crash, being thrown from the Lotus and suffering compound fractures to both legs.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jochen Rindt broke his duck to become a Grand Prix winner. Graham Hill faced months of agonizing rehabilitation but courageously returned to racing in 1970.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Record $200,000 total prize fund ($50,000 to the winner).
