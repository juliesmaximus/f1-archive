## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 2:06:42.080  
- **Pole Position:** Jochen Rindt  (1:20.850)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 1:22.940 (Lap 5)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Breezy and overcast, dry track; 20°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstand and Tarzan hairpin dunes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
NEW WING REGULATIONS: The CSI codified new rules: aerofoils had to be rigidly mounted to the sprung chassis structure, maximum height restricted to 80 cm from the ground, and maximum width restricted to the car bodywork. Jochen Rindt took pole in the newly winged Lotus 49B. Jackie Stewart charged past Rindt on lap 16 to take his 3rd victory in 4 races by 24.5s over Jo Siffert (Lotus) and Chris Amon (Ferrari). Lotus, Matra, and McLaren tested experimental four-wheel-drive (4WD) cars in practice.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart opened up a massive 15-point championship lead (27 pts vs. Hill's 12).
- **Regulatory Framework:** New permanent wing dimension rules enforced.
