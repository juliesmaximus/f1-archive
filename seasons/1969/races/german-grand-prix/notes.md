## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jacky Ickx (Brabham-Ford) — 1:49:55.400  
- **Pole Position:** Jacky Ickx  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, clear, dry summer weather; 26°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 220,000 spectators camping across the Eifel forest  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Start-und-Ziel tribüne, Karussell, and Brünnchen spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Tragedy in practice: German driver Gerhard Mitter was killed when the rear suspension on his BMW 269 F2 car collapsed at Flugplatz; BMW withdrew all their cars. In the race, Jacky Ickx took pole and set a blistering new lap record of 7:43.8 in the Brabham BT26A Ford Cosworth DFV. Ickx battled Jackie Stewart until Stewart suffered gearbox problems, Ickx pulling away to win by 57.7 seconds over Stewart. Bruce McLaren finished 3rd. Johnny Servoz-Gavin scored 6th in the four-wheel-drive Matra MS84, marking the ONLY World Championship point ever scored by a 4WD car in Formula One history!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jacky Ickx cemented his reputation as a Nürburgring master, scoring Brabham's first win of the season.
- **Regulatory Framework:** F2 cars ran concurrently but were ineligible for World Championship points.
