### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
ONE OF THE GREATEST SLIPSTREAMING DUELS IN GRAND PRIX HISTORY: Jochen Rindt took pole in the Lotus 49B. In the race, Rindt and Jackie Stewart (Matra MS80) ran nose-to-tail in an unforgettable wheel-to-wheel dogfight, officially exchanging the lead over 30 times at speeds exceeding 150 mph. On lap 62, a loose rear wing endplate on Rindt's Lotus began rubbing against his left rear tyre, forcing an emergency pitstop. Stewart cruised home to win by a full lap over Jacky Ickx (Brabham) and Bruce McLaren. Rindt recovered to finish 4th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Stewart scored his 5th victory of the season, taking 45 of a possible 54 points to establish an unassailable 28-point lead over Bruce McLaren.
