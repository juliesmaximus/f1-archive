## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Denny Hulme (McLaren-Ford) — 1:54:08.800  
- **Pole Position:** Jack Brabham  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry, thin air; 23°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 90,000 Mexican fans packing the Peraltada stadium  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Colossal Estadio grandstands encircling Peraltada hairpin and pit straight terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jack Brabham took pole position in the Brabham BT26A Ford Cosworth DFV. In the race, Denny Hulme in the McLaren M7A took the lead on lap 10 and drove a commanding race to win by 2.56 seconds ahead of Jacky Ickx, with Jack Brabham finishing 3rd. World Champion Jackie Stewart finished 4th. Jacky Ickx's 2nd place secured him runner-up in the World Drivers' Championship behind Stewart (37 points).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The final Grand Prix of the 1960s decade. Denny Hulme's victory gave McLaren their 2nd win of the season and secured 4th in the Drivers' Championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 4 of last 5 races counted.
