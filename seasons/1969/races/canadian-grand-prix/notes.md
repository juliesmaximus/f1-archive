### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jacky Ickx took pole in the Brabham BT26A Ford Cosworth DFV. On lap 33, Ickx and Jackie Stewart tangled while battling for the lead; Stewart was forced into retirement with a broken steering arm, while Ickx was pushed back onto the track by marshals without penalty. Ickx charged through to win by 46.2 seconds over team boss Jack Brabham, securing a dominant 1-2 sweep for Brabham-Ford! Jochen Rindt completed the podium in 3rd for Lotus.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jacky Ickx's second win of the season elevated him into 2nd place in the World Drivers' Championship.
