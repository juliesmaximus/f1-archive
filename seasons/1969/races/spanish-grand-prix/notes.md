### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE HIGH-WING DISASTER: Both Lotus 49Bs sported extreme, towering 6-foot aerofoils mounted directly on the rear suspension uprights. On lap 9, Graham Hill's wing struts sheared under high-speed downforce at the blind crest of the pit straight; the car barrel-rolled into the Armco. Ten laps later on lap 19, race leader Jochen Rindt suffered an identical wing failure at the exact same crest; Rindt's car slammed into the barriers and collided with Hill's stationary wreckage, somersaulting into the trees and leaving Rindt with a broken nose, skull fracture, and concussion. Jackie Stewart drove an immaculate race to win by two full laps in the new Matra MS80 ahead of Bruce McLaren and Jean-Pierre Beltoise. Only 5 cars finished.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The Montjuïc double crash sparked universal driver outrage and forced the CSI to take emergency regulatory action to ban dangerous high wings.
