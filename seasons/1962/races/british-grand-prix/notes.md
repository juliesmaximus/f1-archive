- Jim Clark Maiden Grand Slam: Jim Clark delivered a flawless demonstration of speed, taking pole position (1:53.6), setting fastest lap (1:55.0), winning the race, and leading all 75 laps to score the FIRST GRAND SLAM OF HIS CAREER.

- Final Aintree Grand Prix: This was the final Formula One World Championship Grand Prix held at the Aintree circuit, with the British GP alternating between Silverstone and Brands Hatch thereafter.
