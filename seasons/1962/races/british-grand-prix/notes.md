# 1962 British Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XV RAC British Grand Prix
- **Round**: 5 of 9 in the 1962 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Saturday, 21 July 1962
- **Location**: Aintree Motor Racing Circuit, Liverpool, United Kingdom
- **Circuit Length**: 4.828 km (3.000 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (362.100 km / 225.000 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:53.600
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:55.000 (Lap 36)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:26:20.800 (148.667 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 75 laps
  2. John Surtees (Lola-Climax) - 75 laps (+49.200s)
  3. Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax) - 75 laps (+1:44.800)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Clark's Dominant Grand Slam**: Jim Clark recorded his first career Grand Slam (pole position, led every lap, fastest lap, and race victory), lapping the entire field up to 4th place.
- **Farewell to Aintree**: This was the fifth and final time the British Grand Prix was hosted at the historic Aintree circuit before the event moved to Silverstone and Brands Hatch.
- **Surtees' Second Podium**: John Surtees drove a strong, disciplined race to finish 2nd in the Lola Mk4, 49.2 seconds behind Clark.
- **Championship Tightens**: Clark's win brought him within 1 point of championship leader Graham Hill (who finished 4th).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Overcast, cool British summer day with dry asphalt.
