## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Debut of the Lotus 25 Monocoque: Colin Chapman unveiled the Lotus 25, the FIRST MONOCOQUE CHASSIS IN FORMULA ONE HISTORY, constructed from folded Duralumin sheets rather than steel spaceframe tubes. Jim Clark led early on before clutch failure forced retirement.
- Graham Hill Maiden Victory: Graham Hill took his FIRST CAREER WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX VICTORY in the BRM P57, winning by 27.2 seconds over Trevor Taylor's Lotus.
- Lola Maiden Pole: John Surtees claimed Lola's first-ever Formula One pole position on the team's World Championship debut.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship race for a monocoque chassis: Lotus 25.
- Maiden World Championship Grand Prix victory for Graham Hill.
- Maiden pole position for Lola Cars (John Surtees).
