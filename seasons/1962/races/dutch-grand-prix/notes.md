# 1962 Dutch Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: X Grote Prijs van Nederland / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Round**: 1 of 9 in the 1962 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 20 May 1962
- **Location**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort, Netherlands
- **Circuit Length**: 4.193 km (2.605 miles)
- **Distance**: 80 laps (335.440 km / 208.433 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: John Surtees (Lola-Climax Mk4) - 1:32.500
- **Fastest Lap**: Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax T60) - 1:34.400 (Lap 5)
- **Race Winner**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 2:11:02.100 (153.307 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Graham Hill (BRM) - 80 laps
  2. Trevor Taylor (Lotus-Climax) - 80 laps (+27.200s)
  3. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 80 laps (+1:21.100)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Graham Hill's Maiden Grand Prix Victory**: In his 34th World Championship start, 33-year-old British driver Graham Hill drove an authoritative race in the new BRM P57 V8 to claim his maiden Formula One World Championship victory.
- **Revolutionary Debut of the Lotus 25**: Colin Chapman unveiled the Lotus 25, the first fully stressed aluminum monocoque chassis in Formula One history. Driven by Jim Clark, it revolutionized race car design by placing the driver in a reclining bathtub cockpit, dramatically reducing frontal cross-section and increasing torsional stiffness. Clark qualified 3rd and led early before clutch failure forced him to pit.
- **John Surtees Lola Pole**: In Lola's first-ever Formula One race, John Surtees took pole position with a time of 1:32.500.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Cool, windy North Sea coastal weather; dry track throughout.
