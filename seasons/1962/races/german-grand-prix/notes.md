# 1962 German Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXIII Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Round**: 6 of 9 in the 1962 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 5 August 1962
- **Location**: Nürburgring Nordschleife, Nürburg, Germany
- **Circuit Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 miles)
- **Distance**: 15 laps (342.150 km / 212.602 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Dan Gurney (Porsche 804) - 8:47.200
- **Fastest Lap**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 10:12.200 (Lap 3)
- **Race Winner**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 2:38:45.300 (129.324 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Graham Hill (BRM) - 15 laps
  2. John Surtees (Lola-Climax) - 15 laps (+2.500s)
  3. Dan Gurney (Porsche) - 15 laps (+4.400s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Torrential Deluge & 15-Lap Three-Way Dogfight**: Torrential mountain rain flooded parts of the Nordschleife, delaying the start by over an hour. Once underway, Graham Hill, John Surtees, and Dan Gurney engaged in a heart-stopping three-way battle in blinding spray across all 15 laps, crossing the finish line separated by just 4.4 seconds.
- **Clark's Forgotten Switch**: Jim Clark stalled on the grid because he forgot to switch on his electric fuel pump. He joined dead last, mounted an astonishing wet-weather recovery drive, and finished 4th.
- **Hill's Crucial Championship Leap**: Hill's victory extended his lead in the World Championship to 7 points over Jim Clark.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Violent thunderstorms and torrential rain pre-race; extremely wet, standing water drying slowly in the final laps.
