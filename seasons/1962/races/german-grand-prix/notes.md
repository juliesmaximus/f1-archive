- **Historic Name Notes**: 23rd German Grand Prix at the Nürburgring Nordschleife.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Nürburgring Nordschleife
- **Location**: Nürburg, Eifel Mountains, Rhineland-Palatinate, West Germany

- **Spectator Attendance**: Over 300,000 spectators.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Start-und-Ziel grandstand soaked in mist and rain.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Graham Hill's Wet Weather Mastery: In atrocious conditions with torrential rain and flooding across the 22.8 km circuit, Graham Hill drove a masterclass in his BRM P57, holding off John Surtees (Lola) and Dan Gurney (Porsche) to win by 2.5 seconds.
- Dan Gurney Pole: Dan Gurney scored Porsche's sole World Championship pole position (8:47.2).
- Clark Stalls on Grid: Jim Clark forgot to switch on his fuel pump at the start, dropped to 26th, and mounted a sensational wet recovery to finish 4th.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Only Formula One World Championship pole position for Porsche (Dan Gurney).
- Graham Hill took the lead of the World Drivers' Championship.
