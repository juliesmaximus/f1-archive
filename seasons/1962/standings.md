# 1962 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1962 World Championship of Drivers and the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers. Only each driver's best 5 results out of the 9 rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship tally. Only each constructor's best 5 results out of the 9 rounds counted towards the Manufacturers' Cup.

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Points System**: 9-6-4-3-2-1 awarded to the top 6 classified finishers in both the Drivers' Championship and Manufacturers' Cup (Constructors win point increased to 9).
- **Best 5 rule**: Only best 5 results counted for both Drivers and Constructors.
- **Constructors' Eligibility**: Only the highest-placing car of each make scored points.
