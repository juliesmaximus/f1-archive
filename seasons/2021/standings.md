## 1. World Drivers' Championship Final Classification
Scoring system: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 awarded to top 10 finishers, plus 1 bonus point for fastest lap if finishing in top 10. Sprint Qualifying (rounds 10, 14, 19) awarded 3-2-1 points to top 3 finishers. Round 12 (Belgian GP) awarded half points (12.5-9-7.5-6-5-4-3-2-1-0.5) due to completing fewer than 75% distance. ## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Max Verstappen Maiden World Championship:** At 24 years, 73 days, Max Verstappen clinched his maiden title on the final lap of the final round in Abu Dhabi, becoming the first Dutch World Champion in F1 history and ending Mercedes' 7-year drivers' championship streak.
- **Historic Title Battle:** Verstappen and Hamilton entered the Abu Dhabi finale tied on 369.5 points each after 21 fiercely contested rounds featuring collisions at Silverstone, Monza, and Jeddah.
- **Single-Season Podiums Record:** Max Verstappen set an all-time Formula 1 record of 18 podium finishes in a single season (10 wins, 8 second places).
- **Mercedes Record 8th Consecutive Constructors' Crown:** Mercedes won their 8th consecutive WCC (2014–2021) with 613.5 points.
- **Fairytale Maiden Wins:** Esteban Ocon scored his maiden victory for Alpine at the Hungarian GP; Daniel Ricciardo delivered McLaren's first victory since 2012 leading a 1-2 finish at Monza.
- **Lewis Hamilton 100th Grand Prix Win:** Hamilton became the first driver in Formula 1 history to reach 100 career Grand Prix victories at the Russian GP in Sochi.
