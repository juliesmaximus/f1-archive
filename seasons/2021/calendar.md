## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Sprint Qualifying Format:** Debuted across three rounds (Silverstone, Monza, Interlagos) featuring 100 km Saturday sprint races awarding 3-2-1 championship points and setting Sunday's grid.
- **Downforce Reductions:** Floor triangular cut-outs, shortened rear diffuser strakes, and clipped brake duct winglets introduced to reduce downforce by ~10% for tyre safety.
- **Budget Cap Inauguration:** Historic $145 million operational cost cap introduced across all 10 constructors.
- **Half Points at Spa-Francorchamps:** Severe rain caused the shortest Grand Prix in history (3 official laps behind Safety Car, no green flag racing), awarding half points for the first time since Malaysia 2009.
