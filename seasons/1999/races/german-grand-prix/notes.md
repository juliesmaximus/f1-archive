## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mika Salo substituted for the injured Schumacher and produced a fairytale drive, leading the race comfortably. Salo selflessly yielded the lead to teammate Eddie Irvine on team orders to bolster Irvine's title hopes, sealing a Ferrari 1-2. Häkkinen suffered a high-speed blowout at 190 mph.
