## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mika Häkkinen led comfortably until lap 30, when he selected first gear instead of second into the Prima Variante chicane, spinning off into retirement. Overwhelmed with emotion, Häkkinen walked into the Monza park trees and was captured by television cameras weeping in anguish. Heinz-Harald Frentzen scored his second win of the season for Jordan, ahead of Ralf Schumacher and Mika Salo.
