## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Championship-altering disaster for Michael Schumacher. On lap 1 entering Stowe corner at 190 mph, a rear brake bleed nipple failure left Schumacher without brakes. His Ferrari skipped through the gravel trap and impacted the tyre barrier head-on at 67 mph. Schumacher suffered broken tibia and fibula bones in his right leg, sidelining him for six races. David Coulthard won the race ahead of Eddie Irvine and Ralf Schumacher.
