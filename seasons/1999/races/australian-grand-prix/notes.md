## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Eddie Irvine scored his maiden Formula 1 Grand Prix victory for Ferrari in an extraordinary race of high attrition. McLaren locked out the front row by a second, but both Häkkinen and Coulthard suffered mechanical failures. Heinz-Harald Frentzen finished 2nd for Jordan and Ralf Schumacher 3rd on his Williams debut.
