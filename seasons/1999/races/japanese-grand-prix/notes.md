## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The championship decider. Eddie Irvine entered the race with a 4-point lead over Mika Häkkinen. Schumacher took pole, but Häkkinen produced one of the greatest race starts of his career, jumping Schumacher into Turn 1. Häkkinen drove an imperious, faultless race to win by 5 seconds over Schumacher, clinching his 2nd consecutive Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship. Eddie Irvine finished 3rd, securing Ferrari's first World Constructors' Championship in 16 years (since 1983).
