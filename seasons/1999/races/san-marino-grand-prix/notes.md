## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mika Häkkinen dominated from pole until lap 18, when he made an unforced error exiting the final chicane and spun heavily into the wall. Michael Schumacher inherited the lead, jumped Coulthard during pit stops, and won to the ecstatic cheers of the Ferrari tifosi. Coulthard finished 2nd and Barrichello 3rd.
