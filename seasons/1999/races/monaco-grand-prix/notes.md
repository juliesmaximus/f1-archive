## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher scored an imperious victory, jumping polesitter Häkkinen into Sainte-Dévote at the start and leading all 78 laps. Eddie Irvine finished 2nd to seal Ferrari's first 1-2 finish in Monaco in 44 years (since 1955). Häkkinen took 3rd.
