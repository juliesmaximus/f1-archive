## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A legendary wet thriller. In torrential rain, polesitter Rubens Barrichello led for 44 laps in the Stewart-Ford. Heinz-Harald Frentzen and Jordan-Mugen-Honda ran a brilliant non-stop fuel strategy, while rivals pitted twice. Frentzen took a sensational victory ahead of Häkkinen and Barrichello.
