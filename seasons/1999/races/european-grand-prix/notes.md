## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most chaotic wet-dry races in F1 history. Leaders Frentzen, Coulthard, and Fisichella all retired from the lead. Ferrari botched Eddie Irvine's pit stop, losing 28 seconds when mechanics only had three tyres ready. Johnny Herbert drove a brilliant race on intermediate tyres to score Stewart Grand Prix's first and only Formula 1 victory, with Jarno Trulli 2nd for Prost and Rubens Barrichello 3rd.
