## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The birth of the 'Wall of Champions'. Three Formula 1 World Champions (Damon Hill, Michael Schumacher, and Jacques Villeneuve) all crashed into the outside concrete wall exiting the final chicane during the race. Mika Häkkinen took victory behind the Safety Car, ahead of Giancarlo Fisichella and Eddie Irvine.
