## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Inaugural Malaysian Grand Prix at Hermann Tilke's state-of-the-art Sepang circuit. Michael Schumacher made an extraordinary comeback from his broken leg, claiming pole position by 0.947s. In the race, Schumacher played the ultimate team player: leading easily, yielding the lead to Irvine, deliberately holding up Häkkinen, and allowing Irvine to win. Post-race scrutineering disqualified both Ferraris for illegal bargeboard dimensions (10 mm outside tolerance), which would have crowned Häkkinen champion, but the FIA International Court of Appeal reinstated Ferrari a week later.
