## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Tyre Regulations:** Four mandatory continuous longitudinal grooves on both front and rear dry-weather tyres.
- **Accident Extraction Seat:** Mandatory detachable driver survival seat introduced to allow injured drivers to be extracted safely without removing them from their seating posture.
