## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Mika Häkkinen's Back-to-Back Championships:** Mika Häkkinen clinched his second consecutive Drivers' Championship at the Suzuka finale, winning 5 races and scoring 76 points.
- **Ferrari's Constructors' Crown Ended 16-Year Drought:** Ferrari claimed their first World Constructors' Championship since 1983 (128 points), led by Eddie Irvine and Michael Schumacher.
- **Four Grooves on Front Tyres:** To further slow cars, front tyres were mandated with 4 longitudinal grooves, matching the rear tyres.
- **Schumacher's Silverstone Crash & Return:** Michael Schumacher suffered a broken leg at Silverstone on lap 1, sidelining him for six rounds before his sensational return at Sepang.
- **Inaugural Malaysian Grand Prix at Sepang:** Formula 1 made its debut at Hermann Tilke's Sepang International Circuit, introducing modern wide-run-off racetrack architecture.
