## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Mika Häkkinen's Back-to-Back Championships:** Mika Häkkinen clinched his second consecutive Drivers' Championship at the Suzuka finale, winning 5 races, scoring 11 pole positions, and totaling 76 points.
- **Ferrari's Constructors' Crown Ended 16-Year Drought:** Ferrari claimed their first World Constructors' Championship since 1983 (128 points), led by Eddie Irvine (4 wins, 74 pts) and Michael Schumacher (2 wins, 44 pts), with critical podium support from substitute Mika Salo (2 podiums, 10 pts).
- **Four Grooves on Front Tyres:** To reduce cornering speeds, front tyres were mandated with 4 longitudinal grooves, matching the rear tyres.
- **Schumacher's Silverstone Crash & Return:** Michael Schumacher suffered a broken leg at Silverstone on lap 1, sidelining him for six rounds before his sensational return at Sepang.
- **Inaugural Malaysian Grand Prix at Sepang:** Formula 1 made its debut at Hermann Tilke's Sepang International Circuit, introducing modern wide-run-off racetrack architecture.
- **Stewart Grand Prix's Maiden Win:** Johnny Herbert took a sensational victory at the chaotic European Grand Prix at the Nürburgring, Stewart Grand Prix's only Formula 1 victory before being acquired by Ford to become Jaguar Racing in 2000.
