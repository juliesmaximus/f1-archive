# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1982 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Keke Rosberg won the 1982 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Williams FW08 Ford Cosworth DFV, scoring 44 net points with a single race victory (the Swiss Grand Prix at Dijon). Didier Pironi finished as runner-up with 39 points despite missing the final five rounds after his severe accident at Hockenheim.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Scuderia Ferrari captured their seventh World Constructors' Championship with 74 points and 3 victories, triumphing despite the tragic death of Gilles Villeneuve and the career-ending accident of Didier Pironi. McLaren finished runner-up with 69 points.
