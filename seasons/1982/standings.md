# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1982 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Keke Rosberg won the 1982 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Williams FW08 Ford Cosworth DFV, scoring 44 net points with a single race victory (the Swiss Grand Prix at Dijon). Didier Pironi finished as runner-up with 39 points (tiebreak over John Watson by virtue of two second places and two third places vs Watson's two second places and one third place) despite missing the final five rounds after his severe accident at Hockenheim.

Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers in each Grand Prix. The best 11 results across the 16 rounds counted toward the Drivers' Championship.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Scuderia Ferrari captured their seventh World Constructors' Championship with 74 points and 3 victories, triumphing despite the tragic death of Gilles Villeneuve and the career-ending accident of Didier Pironi. McLaren-Ford finished runner-up with 69 points.

Under FIA regulations, chassis-engine combinations were scored as separate makes in the International Cup for F1 Constructors (hence Brabham-BMW and Brabham-Ford were classified separately). All rounds and all finishes counted toward the Constructors' Championship.
