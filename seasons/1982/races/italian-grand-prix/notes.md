## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ANDRETTI'S FAIRY-TALE MONZA POLE FOR FERRARI: Following the injuries to Pironi and Tambay, Enzo Ferrari summoned 42-year-old 1978 World Champion Mario Andretti for a heroic return. In front of an ecstatic sea of Tifosi, Andretti stunned the paddock by capturing pole position with a lap of 1:28.473. In the race, René Arnoux delivered a commanding drive to win for Renault, but Ferrari celebrated wildly as Patrick Tambay finished 2nd and Mario Andretti came home 3rd, putting Ferrari on the brink of the Constructors' Championship. Championship leader Keke Rosberg suffered a broken rear wing and finished 8th, keeping the championship fight alive until the final round.
