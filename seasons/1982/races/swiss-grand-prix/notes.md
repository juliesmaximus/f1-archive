## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
KEKE ROSBERG'S SOLE WIN ON THE ROAD TO THE TITLE: Held across the border at Dijon-Prenois due to Switzerland's longstanding ban on motor racing. Patrick Tambay withdrew before the start due to severe pinched nerve shoulder pain, leaving Ferrari with no entries. Alain Prost took pole and led 78 of the 80 laps in the Renault RE30B, but began suffering handling troubles as his skirt seals deteriorated. Keke Rosberg chased down the Renault with relentless tenacity in the Williams FW08, overtaking Prost with two laps remaining to score his first and only victory of the 1982 season. The win vaulted Rosberg into the championship lead with 42 points, three ahead of the sidelined Pironi.
