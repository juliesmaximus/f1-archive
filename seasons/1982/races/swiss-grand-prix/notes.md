## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
KEKE ROSBERG'S CHAMPIONSHIP WIN: Staged in France because motor racing was banned in Switzerland following the 1955 Le Mans disaster. Alain Prost took pole in the Renault turbo. Prost led until lap 78 when handling and fuel vapor lock slowed him. Keke Rosberg in the Williams FW08 Ford DFV swept past to claim his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory** by 4.15 seconds over Alain Prost! With this victory, Rosberg took the lead of the World Championship with 42 points.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Keke Rosberg's only victory of the 1982 season, which proved decisive in clinching the World Championship.
