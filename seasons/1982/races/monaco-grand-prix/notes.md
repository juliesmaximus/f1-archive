## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE RACE NOBODY WANTED TO WIN: Alain Prost led from pole in the Renault until lap 74, when rain began falling. Prost crashed into the barrier coming out of the chicane at 100 mph. Riccardo Patrese took the lead in his Brabham, but spun on oil and wet asphalt at the Loews Hairpin on lap 75, stalling his car. Didier Pironi (Ferrari) inherited the lead, but his battery died inside the tunnel on the final lap! Andrea de Cesaris (Alfa Romeo) would have inherited the lead, but ran out of fuel before reaching the line. Derek Daly (Williams) had broken wings and a seized gearbox. Meanwhile, Patrese, whose Brabham was stranded on a downhill slope at Loews, was pushed by marshals for safety, bump-started his engine, and crossed the line to take his **maiden Formula One victory** in absolute disbelief!

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The most chaotic, surreal final laps in Formula One history, immortalized by James Hunt's commentary: 'We've got this ridiculous situation where we're sitting by the start-finish line waiting for a winner to come by, and we don't seem to have one!'
