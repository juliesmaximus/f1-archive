## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE RACE NOBODY WANTED TO WIN: Regarded as the most chaotic final laps in Grand Prix history. Alain Prost led comfortably until lap 74, when rain began falling on the harbor section. Prost slid into the barriers at the chicane, smashing his Renault. Riccardo Patrese inherited the lead, but spun on oil and wet asphalt at the Loews hairpin on lap 75, stalling his Brabham. Didier Pironi led starting the final lap, but his Ferrari sputtered to a halt in the tunnel with a dead battery and out of fuel. Andrea de Cesaris ran out of fuel before he could take the lead. Derek Daly had damaged his Williams gearbox in a crash and parked. Meanwhile, marshals had pushed Patrese out of a dangerous position on the downhill slope at Loews, allowing him to bump-start his car. Patrese navigated past the abandoned cars on the track to cross the finish line, completely unaware that he had just won his maiden Grand Prix!
