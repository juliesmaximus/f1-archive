## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LAUDA MASTERCLASS AT BRANDS HATCH: Keke Rosberg claimed pole position for Williams, but suffered a fuel pressure failure on the parade lap and was forced to start from the back of the grid. Niki Lauda seized the opportunity in the McLaren MP4/1B, battling past Derek Warwick's surging Toleman and passing Riccardo Patrese to lead 67 of the 76 laps, taking his second win of the season. Didier Pironi finished 2nd for Ferrari to take the lead of the World Drivers' Championship. Brian Henton set the fastest lap of the race on lap 63 in his Tyrrell 011 Ford Cosworth—the sole fastest lap of Henton's Formula 1 career.
