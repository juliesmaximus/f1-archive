## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
KEKE ROSBERG'S CROWN & ALBORETO'S MAIDEN WIN: In brutal 100°F desert heat on the tortuous, anti-clockwise parking lot circuit of Caesars Palace, Keke Rosberg needed only a top-5 finish to secure the World Championship. Driving with supreme maturity and poise in the Williams FW08, Rosberg finished 5th to clinch the 1982 World Drivers' Championship with 44 points—becoming only the second driver in F1 history (after Mike Hawthorn in 1958) to win the World Championship with just a single race victory. Meanwhile, 25-year-old Michele Alboreto drove an inspired race in the nimble Tyrrell 011 Ford Cosworth, taking his maiden Grand Prix victory and becoming the record 11th different winner in 16 races. Scuderia Ferrari secured the World Constructors' Championship with 74 points.
