## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALBORETO'S MAIDEN WIN & KEKE ROSBERG WORLD CHAMPION: Alain Prost took pole in the Renault. In the twisting, flat car park layout, Michele Alboreto in the nimble Tyrrell 011 Ford DFV drove a brilliant tactical race, overtaking Prost on lap 52 to score his **maiden Formula One victory** and Tyrrell's first win in four years! Keke Rosberg drove with supreme tactical composure to finish 5th, scoring 2 points to mathematically clinch the **1982 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship** with 44 points! Scuderia Ferrari captured the **World Constructors' Championship** with 74 points.
