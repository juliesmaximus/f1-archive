## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WATER-COOLED BRAKES CONTROVERSY & DISQUALIFICATION: In suffocating tropical heat, Nelson Piquet (Brabham BT49D) and Keke Rosberg (Williams FW07D) finished 1st and 2nd on the road. Piquet was so physically exhausted by the extreme lateral g-forces and heat that he fainted on the podium. However, both Brabham and Williams had exploited a loophole by fitting massive water tanks ostensibly for "water-cooled brakes", which emptied during the opening laps, allowing the cars to race significantly underweight (under the 580 kg minimum limit) and then topping up with water at the post-race inspection. Following an official protest by Ferrari and Renault, the FIA International Court of Appeal disqualified both Piquet and Rosberg on April 20, 1982. Alain Prost was declared the official winner, with John Watson 2nd and Nigel Mansell 3rd.
