## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WATER-COOLED BRAKES DISQUALIFICATION: Nelson Piquet (Brabham BT49D) and Keke Rosberg (Williams FW07C) crossed the line 1st and 2nd after a punishing physical battle in 36°C heat (Piquet fainted on the podium). However, both Brabham and Williams had fitted large water tanks supposedly for 'brake cooling', which were drained during the race (running underweight) and topped up after the race before weighing. Ferrari and Renault protested. The FIA Court of Appeal disqualified both Piquet and Rosberg for running underweight, handing victory to Alain Prost (Renault) with John Watson 2nd and Michele Alboreto 3rd.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The disqualification ignited full-scale war between FISA and FOCA constructors, prompting the FOCA boycott of Imola.
