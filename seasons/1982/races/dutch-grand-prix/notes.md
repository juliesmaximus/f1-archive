## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIRONI'S DOMINANCE & TOLEMAN'S FIRST FASTEST LAP: Scuderia Ferrari returned to two cars for the first time since Zolder, signing French veteran Patrick Tambay into car #27 alongside championship contender Didier Pironi. Pironi delivered an absolute masterclass: qualifying 4th, he stormed past Niki Lauda and Alain Prost into the lead on lap 5, pulling away with devastating pace to win by 21.6 seconds over Nelson Piquet (Brabham-BMW). The victory vaulted Pironi within one point of championship leader John Watson. On lap 13, Derek Warwick set the fastest lap in the Toleman TG181C Hart turbo—the first fastest lap in the history of the Toleman team.
