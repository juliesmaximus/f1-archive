## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
FRENCH 1-2-3-4 & ARNOUX TEAM ORDER DEFIANCE: Renault turbo power dominated (Arnoux pole, Prost 2nd). Renault management instructed Arnoux to let Prost pass to boost Prost's championship hopes. Arnoux ignored all pit signals, storming away to win by 7.77 seconds over Prost, with Didier Pironi 3rd and Patrick Tambay 4th! This created the **historic first and only 1-2-3-4 finish by French drivers in Formula One history** (all driving French cars with French engines). Pironi extended his championship lead to 9 points.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
The historic all-French 1-2-3-4 finish; René Arnoux's open defiance led to his departure from Renault at year-end.
