## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALL-FRENCH 1-2-3-4 & RENAULT CIVIL WAR: French motorsport celebrated an extraordinary historic milestone as four French drivers in French cars swept the top 4 positions on home soil: René Arnoux and Alain Prost (Renault 1-2) ahead of Didier Pironi and Patrick Tambay (Ferrari 3-4). However, the race caused a bitter civil war inside Renault: prior to the race, team orders dictated that Arnoux was to yield to Prost if both cars ran 1-2, as Prost was challenging for the championship. Arnoux ignored pit signals and cruised to victory by 17.3 seconds. Prost was furious, and the fallout prompted Arnoux to sign with Ferrari for 1983. On lap 11, Jochen Mass survived a terrifying crash at the 170 mph Signes curve, vaulting catch fencing into spectator barriers, which prompted Mass to announce his immediate retirement from F1.
