## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE DEATH OF GILLES VILLENEUVE & WATSON VICTORY: On Saturday, May 8, during final qualifying, Gilles Villeneuve was desperate to beat Didier Pironi's lap time. On an in-lap, Villeneuve came upon Jochen Mass's slow March approaching the Terlamenbocht. Mass moved right to yield; Villeneuve simultaneously committed to the right. The Ferrari struck Mass's rear wheel at 140 mph, was launched airborne, cartwheeled violently, and disintegrated; Villeneuve was thrown from the cockpit into the catch-fence, sustaining fatal fractures. Ferrari withdrew from the race in mourning. In Sunday's race, Keke Rosberg led until tire wear dropped him behind John Watson, who drove a brilliant tactical race in the McLaren MP4/1B to win by 7.27 seconds.
