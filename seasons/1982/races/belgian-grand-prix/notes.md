## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE TRAGIC LOSS OF GILLES VILLENEUVE: During final qualifying on Saturday afternoon, Gilles Villeneuve was desperate to beat teammate Didier Pironi's time following the bitter betrayal at Imola two weeks earlier. With 8 minutes left in the session, Villeneuve came over the blind crest before the Terlamen chicane and encountered Jochen Mass's slow-moving March. As Mass moved right to let him through, Villeneuve also swerved right; the Ferrari struck the back of the March at roughly 140 mph and was catapulted into the air, somersaulting violently through the catch fencing. Villeneuve was thrown from the cockpit across the track and suffered fatal neck fractures, succumbing that evening at St. Raphael Hospital in Leuven. Ferrari withdrew Pironi and withdrew entirely from the race. On Sunday, John Watson drove a brilliant race from 10th on the grid, passing Keke Rosberg with two laps remaining to take an emotional victory for McLaren.
