## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PALETTI TRAGEDY & BMW TURBO MAIDEN WIN: The circuit was officially renamed Circuit Gilles Villeneuve. Didier Pironi took pole in the Ferrari. At the start, Pironi stalled. The pack swerved around the stationary Ferrari, but 23-year-old Italian rookie Riccardo Paletti (Osella), unsighted from 23rd on the grid, slammed into the back of Pironi's Ferrari at 110 mph. The Osella burst into flames; Paletti suffered fatal chest injuries. At the restart, Nelson Piquet in the Brabham BT50 powered by the BMW M12/13 turbo engine drove a masterclass, claiming the **historic first Formula One victory for BMW turbo power**, winning by 13.79 seconds over Riccardo Patrese.
