## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
CIRCUIT RENAMED FOR GILLES & TRAGIC DEATH OF RICCARDO PALETTI: Prior to the race, the Île Notre-Dame circuit was officially dedicated as Circuit Gilles Villeneuve. At the standing start, polesitter Didier Pironi stalled his Ferrari. While most of the grid swerved past, 23-year-old Italian rookie Riccardo Paletti (Osella), starting from the back and accelerating at over 100 mph, unsighted behind other cars, slammed directly into the rear of Pironi's stationary Ferrari. The Osella was crushed and burst into flames as Pironi and medical delegate Prof. Sid Watkins rushed to help. Paletti suffered fatal injuries and died shortly after arriving at Royal Victoria Hospital. Following a long delay, the race restarted; Nelson Piquet drove a flawless race in the Brabham BT50 to score the historic first Formula 1 victory for BMW's M12/13 turbocharged engine.
