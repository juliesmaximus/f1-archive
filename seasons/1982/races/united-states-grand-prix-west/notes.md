## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LAUDA'S COMEBACK WIN: Andrea de Cesaris took a sensational maiden pole position for Alfa Romeo. Niki Lauda, in only his third race back from a two-year retirement, started 2nd in John Barnard's carbon-fibre McLaren MP4/1B. Lauda pressured de Cesaris into a mistake on lap 15, took the lead, and drove to a flawless victory by 14.66 seconds over Keke Rosberg! Gilles Villeneuve finished 3rd in the Ferrari fitted with a staggered twin rear wing, but was disqualified after Ken Tyrrell successfully protested the wing as an illegal aerodynamic device.
