## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LAUDA'S COMEBACK WIN & FERRARI'S DOUBLE-WING DISQUALIFICATION: 22-year-old Andrea de Cesaris captured his maiden pole position for Alfa Romeo, becoming the youngest pole-sitter in Formula One history at the time. In the race, Niki Lauda delivered a masterclass in the carbon-fibre McLaren MP4/1B, passing de Cesaris on lap 15 and cruising to victory in only his third Grand Prix since returning from a two-year retirement. Gilles Villeneuve finished 3rd on the road in the Ferrari 126C2, which was fitted with a radical dual-element rear wing consisting of two aerofoils staggered horizontally to circumvent maximum width regulations (each wing complied with width rules individually, but together they spanned the full car width). Ken Tyrrell protested the wing; the stewards declared it illegal and disqualified Villeneuve, promoting Riccardo Patrese to 3rd.
