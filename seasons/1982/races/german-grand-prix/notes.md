## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIRONI'S CAREER-ENDING CRASH & TAMBAY'S EMOTIONAL TRIUMPH: On Saturday morning in torrential rain, championship leader Didier Pironi was testing tires. In blinding spray on the straight between the pits and the first chicane, Pironi struck the rear of Alain Prost's slow Renault, catapulting the Ferrari into a sickening high-speed airborne somersault that shattered Pironi's legs, instantly ending his Formula 1 career. Pironi's dry Friday time remained on pole, but the slot was left empty on the grid. In the race, Patrick Tambay, carrying Ferrari alone, drove an emotional, faultless race to score his maiden Grand Prix victory. On lap 19, race leader Nelson Piquet was taken out by Eliseo Salazar at the Ostkurve chicane; Piquet climbed from his Brabham and physically assaulted Salazar with punches and kicks on live global television.
