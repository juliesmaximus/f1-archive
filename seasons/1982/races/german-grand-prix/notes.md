## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE PIRONI DISASTER & TAMBAY'S EMOTIONAL WIN: On Saturday morning practice in blinding rain, championship leader Didier Pironi was on pole. Sighting past Derek Daly, Pironi slammed into the rear of Alain Prost's slow-moving Renault at 170 mph. The Ferrari cartwheeled through the air and disintegrated, crushing both of Pironi's legs and ending his racing career. In Sunday's race, Nelson Piquet led comfortably in the Brabham-BMW until lap 18, when backmarker Eliseo Salazar turned in on him at the Ostkurve chicane; Piquet famously punched and kicked Salazar on television! Patrick Tambay, driving Gilles Villeneuve's vacated No. 27 Ferrari, drove an emotional race to take his **maiden Formula One victory** by 16.37 seconds over René Arnoux and Keke Rosberg.
