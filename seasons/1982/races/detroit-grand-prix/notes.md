## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WATSON WINS FROM 17TH ON THE GRID: Alain Prost qualified on pole for Renault. On the tight, bumpy 90-degree street circuit around the Renaissance Center, Michelin tires on the McLarens came alive. John Watson started a lowly 17th on the grid. In a mesmerizing exhibition of overtaking, Watson carved through the field, passing three cars in a single lap, and swept past teammate Niki Lauda into the lead on lap 37 to win by 15.73 seconds over Eddie Cheever (Ligier) and Didier Pironi (Ferrari)! This stood as one of the lowest starting positions ever to win a Grand Prix.
