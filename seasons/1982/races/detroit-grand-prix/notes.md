## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WATSON'S RECORD 17TH-TO-1ST STREET FIGHT: The inaugural Detroit GP on a punishing, narrow street circuit through downtown Detroit produced one of the greatest comeback drives in Formula 1 history. Starting 17th on the grid, John Watson capitalized on McLaren's exceptional Michelin tire management, overtaking car after car on the tight street corners. By lap 37, Watson and teammate Niki Lauda had surged into 2nd and 3rd behind leader Keke Rosberg. When Rosberg encountered gearbox trouble, Watson blew past into the lead, pulling away to win by 15.7 seconds over Lauda for a stunning McLaren 1-2 finish. Riccardo Paletti (Osella) had qualified 23rd but destroyed his car in a heavy warm-up crash and did not start.
