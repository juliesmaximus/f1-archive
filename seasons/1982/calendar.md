# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1982 - Calendar

The 1982 Formula One season was the 33rd season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 championship rounds between January 23 and September 25, 1982.

The 1982 season is widely remembered as the most tumultuous, tragic, and open championship in Formula One history:
- **11 Different Winners (All-Time Record):** A record 11 different drivers won races across the 16 rounds (Prost, Lauda, Pironi, Watson, Patrese, Piquet, Arnoux, Tambay, de Angelis, K. Rosberg, Alboreto). No driver won more than two races.
- **Keke Rosberg's Improbable Championship:** Flying Finn Keke Rosberg won only a single race (the Swiss Grand Prix at Dijon) in the naturally aspirated Williams FW08 Ford Cosworth DFV, using ferocious consistency and maximum commitment to capture the **World Drivers' Championship** with 44 points.
- **The Kyalami Drivers' Strike:** At the season opener in South Africa, Grand Prix drivers led by Niki Lauda and Didier Pironi staged a historic strike, locking themselves overnight in the Sunnyside Park Hotel banquet room in Johannesburg to force FISA to abandon restrictive superlicence clauses.
- **Ferrari's Tragedy & Triumph:** Scuderia Ferrari suffered unbearable grief: Gilles Villeneuve was killed at Zolder during qualifying for the Belgian Grand Prix, and Didier Pironi suffered career-ending leg injuries at Hockenheim while leading the World Championship. Despite these catastrophes, Patrick Tambay and Mario Andretti rallied the team to capture the **World Constructors' Championship** (74 points).
- **BMW Turbo's Maiden Victory:** Nelson Piquet took the first victory for BMW's fearsome M12/13 turbocharged engine at Montreal.
- **Colin Chapman's Final Wave:** At the Österreichring, Elio de Angelis scored his maiden victory in the Lotus 91 by just 0.050 seconds over Keke Rosberg, marking Colin Chapman's final victory celebration before his untimely death in December 1982.
