## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton became a four-time World Champion! At the start, Vettel, Verstappen, and Hamilton went three-wide into Turn 1. Verstappen took the lead; Vettel clipped Hamilton's right-rear tyre with his front wing, puncturing Hamilton's tyre and damaging Vettel's wing. Both limped to the pits, rejoining last. Max Verstappen dominated the race to take a crushing victory by 19.6 seconds over Bottas. Vettel fought back to 4th, but it was not enough: Hamilton crossed the line 9th to officially clinch the 2017 FIA Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship! Hamilton joined Juan Manuel Fangio, Alain Prost, Michael Schumacher, and Sebastian Vettel as a four-time champion.
