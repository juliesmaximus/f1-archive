## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The final nail in Ferrari's 2017 title coffin. On the grid, Ferrari mechanics worked frantically around Vettel's engine cover; a £5 spark plug had failed. Starting 2nd, Vettel lacked power and retired on lap 4. Lewis Hamilton controlled the race from pole position, withstanding late pressure from Max Verstappen in traffic to win by 1.2 seconds, opening up a commanding 59-point championship lead.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Vettel's third reliability/incident disaster in four races virtually handed the title to Hamilton.
