## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Debut of the ultra-fast, wide-track wider-tyre aerodynamic era. Pole-sitter Lewis Hamilton pitted early on lap 17 to cover tyre degradation, rejoining behind Max Verstappen's Red Bull. Unable to pass on track, Hamilton was held up as Sebastian Vettel pitted six laps later, emerging in the lead to take an emphatic victory by 9.9 seconds. Ferrari scored their first season-opening victory since 2010. Valtteri Bottas scored 3rd on his Mercedes debut.
