## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen scored his first pole position in nine years (since France 2008) and led early. Ferrari pitted Räikkönen on lap 34; Vettel stayed out, pumped in blistering qualifying laps on clear track, and jumped Räikkönen during his pitstop to take victory, leading a Ferrari 1-2. This was Ferrari's first Monaco win since Michael Schumacher in 2001. Jenson Button made a one-off comeback deputizing for Fernando Alonso (who was racing in the Indy 500).

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Räikkönen looked visibly devastated on the podium after losing the win to Vettel via pit strategy.
