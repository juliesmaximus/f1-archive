## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel jumped Hamilton into Turn 1 at the start, but Hamilton deployed DRS on lap 6 to sweep past and take victory. Mercedes AMG Petronas officially clinched their fourth consecutive FIA Formula 1 World Constructors' Championship! On the final lap, Max Verstappen made a breathtaking dive down the inside of Kimi Räikkönen at Turn 17 to steal 3rd on the podium; however, stewards controversially ruled Verstappen had placed all four wheels off track, handing him a 5-second penalty in the green room and demoting him to 4th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Verstappen being ejected from the podium cooldown room caused massive fan and Red Bull outrage.
