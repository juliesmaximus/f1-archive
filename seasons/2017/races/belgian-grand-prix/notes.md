## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
In his 200th Grand Prix start, Lewis Hamilton matched Michael Schumacher's all-time record of 68 pole positions on Saturday. In the race, Hamilton resisted relentless, ferocious pressure from Sebastian Vettel for 44 laps, particularly on a late Safety Car restart where Vettel attacked down the Kemmel Straight on softer tyres, winning by 2.3 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Force India teammates Pérez and Ocon collided twice on the run to Eau Rouge, destroying tyres and prompting team orders.
