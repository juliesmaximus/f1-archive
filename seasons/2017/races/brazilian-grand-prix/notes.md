## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel pounced on pole-sitter Valtteri Bottas into Turn 1 on the opening lap and controlled the race to take his first win since Hungary. Lewis Hamilton crashed in Q1 and started from the pitlane, producing an astonishing comeback drive through the field to finish 4th, just 5.4 seconds behind the winner! Felipe Massa finished 7th in his final home Grand Prix, greeted on the podium by his son Felipinho to rapturous applause from the Brazilian crowd.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's charge from pitlane to 4th was hailed as one of the best drives of 2017.
