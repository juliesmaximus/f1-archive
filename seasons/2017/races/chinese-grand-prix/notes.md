## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton produced a peerless Grand Slam: pole position, victory, fastest lap, and led every lap. In mixed conditions, Antonio Giovinazzi crashed heavily on the pit straight in his Sauber, deploying the Safety Car. Max Verstappen drove an astonishing opening lap, slicing from 16th on the grid to 7th, eventually charging to 3rd on the podium behind Vettel.
