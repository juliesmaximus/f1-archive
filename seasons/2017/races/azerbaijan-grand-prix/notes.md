## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The 'Baku Road Rage' scandal. In one of the wildest, most chaotic races in modern history, Hamilton led behind the Safety Car on lap 19. Hamilton slowed on corner exit; Vettel rear-ended the Mercedes, then pulled alongside furiously, gesticulating and deliberately swerving his Ferrari into Hamilton's sidepod! Following a red flag for debris, Hamilton's cockpit headrest came loose, forcing an unscheduled stop. Stewards handed Vettel a 10-second stop-go penalty for dangerous driving. Daniel Ricciardo, having dropped to 17th after an early pitstop, pulled off an astonishing triple overtake into Turn 1 at the restart to snatch victory! 18-year-old rookie Lance Stroll scored a historic maiden podium in 3rd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Vettel's deliberate swerve into Hamilton provoked international fury, nearly resulting in an FIA court disqualification.
