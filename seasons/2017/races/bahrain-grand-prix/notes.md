## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Valtteri Bottas scored his maiden career pole position on Saturday. In the race, Sebastian Vettel jumped Hamilton off the line and undercut Bottas during an early Safety Car period (caused by Stroll and Sainz colliding). Hamilton was handed a 5-second penalty for driving unnecessarily slowly in the pitlane entry to delay Daniel Ricciardo. Vettel held off Hamilton by 6.6 seconds to retake the outright championship lead.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's pitlane delay penalty cost Mercedes a potential victory.
