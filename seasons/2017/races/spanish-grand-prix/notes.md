## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A heavyweight slugfest between Hamilton and Vettel. Vettel grabbed the lead into Turn 1 with an aggressive start. On lap 38, Vettel emerged from his final pitstop side-by-side with Hamilton into Turn 1; the two banged wheels, Hamilton forced wide onto the runoff! On lap 44, with fresher soft tyres, Hamilton slipstreamed Vettel down the main straight, pulled to the outside, and executed a clinical DRS overtake into Turn 1 to win by 3.4 seconds.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
