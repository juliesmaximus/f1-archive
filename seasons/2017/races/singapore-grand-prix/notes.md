## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The championship turned to dust in 5 seconds. Ferrari took pole on a circuit where they were vastly superior to Mercedes. At the start in pouring rain, Kimi Räikkönen made a rocket launch down the inside; Vettel squeezed left to defend against Verstappen; Verstappen was sandwiched between the two Ferraris! The three collided violently, Räikkönen's t-boned Ferrari wiping out Verstappen and Fernando Alonso into Turn 1! Vettel suffered sidepod damage, spun on his own coolant at Turn 3, and crashed out. Lewis Hamilton sliced from 5th to the lead within three corners, controlling the race in the wet to take an astonishing victory and extend his lead to 28 points.
