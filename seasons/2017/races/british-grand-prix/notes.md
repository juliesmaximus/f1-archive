## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton achieved an untouchable Grand Slam, leading a Mercedes 1-2 ahead of Bottas. Complete catastrophe struck Ferrari in the final three laps: Kimi Räikkönen suffered a left-front tyre delamination on lap 49, dropping to 3rd; on the very next lap, Sebastian Vettel suffered an identical puncture, limping to the pits and tumbling to 7th! Hamilton slashed Vettel's championship lead from 20 points to a single point.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
