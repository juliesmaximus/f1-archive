## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ferrari locked out the front row in qualifying for the first time since France 2008. But at the start, Valtteri Bottas used a monstrous slipstream down the long run to Turn 2 to sweep past both Ferraris into the lead! Bottas drove an impeccable race under intense pressure from Sebastian Vettel in the closing laps, withstanding a 0.6-second margin to score his maiden Formula 1 victory in his 81st start.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Bottas became the fifth Finnish driver in F1 history to win a Grand Prix.
