## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Valtteri Bottas produced an otherworldly reaction time off the line (0.201 seconds), timing the lights so perfectly that rivals protested he had jumped the start, but telemetry cleared him. Bottas controlled the race, withstanding intense pressure from Sebastian Vettel to win by just 0.6 seconds. Daniel Ricciardo defended 3rd brilliantly against Hamilton.
