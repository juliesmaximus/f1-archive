## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One day after celebrating his 20th birthday, Max Verstappen pulled off an audacious overtake on Lewis Hamilton into Turn 1 on lap 4 and pulled away to score the second victory of his career by 12.7 seconds! More heartbreak for Ferrari: Vettel started last after a turbo failure in qualifying, charging to 4th, while Räikkönen suffered engine failure on the grid and did not start. On the cool-down lap, Lance Stroll collided with Vettel, ripping the Ferrari's left-rear suspension off.
