## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
History at the Temple of Speed. In a wet, treacherous qualifying, Lewis Hamilton shattered Michael Schumacher's record by taking his 69th career pole position—the most in Formula 1 history. On Sunday, Mercedes crushed Ferrari on home soil, Hamilton leading a 1-2 ahead of Bottas by 36 seconds over Sebastian Vettel in 3rd. Hamilton took the outright lead of the World Championship for the first time in 2017.
