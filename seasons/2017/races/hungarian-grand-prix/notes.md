## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ferrari locked out the front row and controlled the race. Sebastian Vettel suffered a crooked steering wheel from lap 25, forced to steer constantly off-center. Teammate Kimi Räikkönen acted as a loyal rear guard, protecting Vettel from the charging Mercedes. Valtteri Bottas let Hamilton past on lap 45 to attack the Ferraris; when Hamilton could not pass, he honorably slowed on the final corner of the final lap to hand 3rd place back to Bottas!

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's sportsmanship in returning the podium position to Bottas cost him 3 crucial championship points.
