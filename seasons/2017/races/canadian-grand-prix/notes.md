## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton matched Ayrton Senna's 65 career pole positions on Saturday and was presented with a race-worn Senna helmet by the Senna family on live television, breaking into tears. In the race, Hamilton delivered a flawless Grand Slam to lead a Mercedes 1-2 ahead of Valtteri Bottas. Max Verstappen sliced his front wing across Vettel's nose into Turn 1, breaking Vettel's front wing and forcing an early pitstop.
