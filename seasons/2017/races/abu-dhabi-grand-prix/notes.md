## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Valtteri Bottas scored a flawless Grand Slam: pole position, victory, fastest lap, and led every lap to lead a Mercedes 1-2 ahead of Lewis Hamilton, with Sebastian Vettel completing the podium in 3rd. Immediately following the podium ceremony, Formula 1 unveiled its new modern corporate logo, marking the end of the 23-year-old 'flying F1' logo era.
