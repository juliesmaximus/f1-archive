## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton 4th World Championship:** Hamilton equalled Prost and Vettel with his 4th title, breaking Schumacher's all-time pole position record (taking his 69th pole at Monza).
- **The Aerodynamic Revolution:** Wider cars (2.0 m) and wider tyres (+25%) shattered circuit lap records worldwide.
- **The Baku Road Rage Incident:** Sebastian Vettel swerved into Lewis Hamilton behind the Safety Car.
- **The Singapore Start Disaster:** Double Ferrari retirement and Verstappen crash in wet twilight.
- **Liberty Media Era Begins:** Formula 1 acquired by Liberty Media, ushering in digital expansion.
