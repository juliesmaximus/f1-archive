## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **2017 Aero Regulations:** Total car width expanded to 2,000 mm; front tyres widened to 305 mm; rear tyres widened to 405 mm.
- **Low Swept Rear Wing:** Rear wing lowered to 800 mm with delta rake.
- **Engine Token System Abolished:** Power Unit development opened without token restrictions.
