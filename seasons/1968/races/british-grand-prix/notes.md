- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Brabham Straight grandstands, Druids grandstands, and South Bank spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HISTORIC FINAL PRIVATEER WIN: Lotus introduced high rear wings attached directly to the rear suspension uprights on the Lotus 49B. Graham Hill took pole and led until his rear driveshaft failed. Teammate Jackie Oliver took over the lead until his Cosworth DFV engine blew. Swiss privateer Jo Siffert, driving Rob Walker's private dark-blue Lotus 49B Cosworth DFV, engaged in a scintillating duel with Chris Amon's Ferrari, holding him off to win by 4.4 seconds! Siffert's win was the first Grand Prix victory for a Swiss driver and the LAST EVER victory for a privateer entrant (Rob Walker Racing Team) in Formula One history.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Rob Walker's private team had lost their entire workshop and cars in a devastating fire weeks earlier; Siffert's win with a newly delivered Lotus chassis remains one of F1's greatest fairy tales.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
