- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main straight covered grandstands, Pegaso curve spectator terraces, and Farina hillsides  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
First championship race following Jim Clark's tragic death. Team Lotus arrived sporting radical new red, white, and gold Gold Leaf livery—the first full commercial title sponsorship in Formula One history. Graham Hill wore a black armband in memory of Clark, driving with immense resolve to win by 15.9s over Denny Hulme (McLaren). Only 5 cars finished. Chris Amon took pole for Ferrari but retired with fuel pump failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The inaugural Spanish Grand Prix at Jarama. Graham Hill rallied Lotus through unimaginable grief to take the lead of the World Championship.
