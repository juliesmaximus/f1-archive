- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Sunny, warm, dry, thin air; 24°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Colossal Estadio grandstands encircling Peraltada hairpin and pit straight terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THREE-WAY TITLE DECIDER: Jo Siffert took pole for Rob Walker Lotus. In the race, championship contenders Stewart and Hulme both suffered catastrophic misfortune: Hulme suffered rear suspension failure and crashed on lap 10, while Stewart was hobbled by engine misfires and fuel pressure failure, dropping to 7th. Graham Hill drove a commanding, peerless race to victory by 1m 19s over Bruce McLaren and Jackie Oliver! With this triumph, Graham Hill CLINCHED his second World Drivers' Championship (48 net points), dedicating the crown to his fallen teammate Jim Clark. Team Lotus won the Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Manufacturers).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Graham Hill became a double World Champion, celebrated for holding Team Lotus together through its darkest hour. Lotus captured their 3rd Constructors' title.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 5 of last 6 races counted.
