### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
The Canadian Grand Prix moved to the beautiful, undulating Circuit Mont-Tremblant in the Laurentian mountains. Jochen Rindt took pole for Brabham. In the race, Denny Hulme and Bruce McLaren demonstrated crushing dominance, scoring a magnificent 1-2 finish for McLaren, with Hulme taking his second consecutive Grand Prix victory. Pedro Rodríguez completed the podium in 3rd for BRM. Graham Hill finished 4th. Stewart retired.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme moved into a tie with Graham Hill at the top of the World Drivers' Championship with 33 points each, setting up a thrilling 3-way title showdown with Jackie Stewart.
