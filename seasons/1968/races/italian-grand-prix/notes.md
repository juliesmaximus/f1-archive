## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Denny Hulme (McLaren-Ford) — 1:40:14.800  
- **Pole Position:** John Surtees  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry late summer weather; 25°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 100,000 tifosi packing the Monza grandstands and parkland  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Tribuna Centrale, Vedano, Ascari, and Parabolica spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Mario Andretti made his Formula One debut for Lotus and set pole position. However, Andretti and Bobby Unser flew back to Indiana on Friday night to compete in the Hoosier 100 dirt track race on Saturday; under Italian sporting rules barring drivers from racing within 24 hours of another event, both were banned from starting! In the race, Denny Hulme dominated in the McLaren M7A Ford Cosworth DFV to win by 1m 28s over Johnny Servoz-Gavin (Matra) and Jacky Ickx (Ferrari). Both Hill and Stewart retired.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme launched himself into championship contention, closing to within three points of leader Graham Hill.
