- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Quai Albert 1er grandstands and Casino square terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
A race of staggering attrition where only 5 of 16 starters finished the 100 laps! Graham Hill took pole and led almost the entire race in the Gold Leaf Lotus 49B (featuring early ducktail spoiler and front diveplanes) to take his 4th career Monaco victory by 2.2s ahead of Richard Attwood's BRM. Lucien Bianchi finished 3rd in the Cooper-BRM, and Ludovico Scarfiotti was 4th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Graham Hill's 4th Monaco win earned him the undisputed moniker of 'King of Monaco'. Final race of Ludovico Scarfiotti, who died a week later in a hillclimb crash at Rossfeld.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Straw bales banned; steel Armco barriers added to harbour chicane.
