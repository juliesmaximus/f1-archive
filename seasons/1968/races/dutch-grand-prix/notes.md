## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 2:46:11.200  
- **Pole Position:** Chris Amon  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Relentless torrential rain, standing puddles, thick spray, and howling North Sea winds  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 50,000 soaked spectators enduring the dunes  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main pit straight grandstand and Tarzan hairpin dunes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Driving with a broken wrist supported in a special plastic cast (from an F2 crash at Jarama), Jackie Stewart delivered a wet-weather tour de force in Ken Tyrrell's Matra MS10 Ford Cosworth DFV, running Dunlop wet tyres. Stewart took the lead from Graham Hill on lap 4 and disappeared into the torrential rain, winning by a crushing 1 minute 33.9 seconds ahead of Jean-Pierre Beltoise (Matra-MS11 V12). Graham Hill recovered from a spin to take 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First Formula One World Championship victory for French constructor Matra and for team owner Ken Tyrrell. Celebrated Dunlop's wet-weather tyre breakthrough.
