## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 2:19:03.200  
- **Pole Position:** Jacky Ickx  (9:04.000)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jackie Stewart (Matra-Ford) — 9:36.000 (Lap 8)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Drenching, catastrophic rainstorm, thick pea-soup fog, and standing water across the entire 14-mile circuit  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Start-und-Ziel grandstand and Karussell / Brünnchen natural amphitheatre terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
JACKIE STEWART'S GREATEST MASTERPIECE: In conditions widely described as borderline unraceable due to torrential rain and fog, Jackie Stewart started from 6th on the grid in the Ken Tyrrell Matra-Cosworth MS10. By the end of lap 1, Stewart had pulled out an astonishing 8-second lead. Driving with peerless car control on Dunlop wet tyres and with high aerodynamic wings, Stewart extended his lead with every lap. Stewart crossed the finish line to WIN BY 4 MINUTES 3.2 SECONDS ahead of Graham Hill! Jochen Rindt took 3rd for Brabham.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Considered universally by historians, drivers, and analysts as one of the greatest, most heroic wet-weather drives in all of motorsport history. Stewart coined the term 'The Green Hell' (Die Grüne Hölle) for the Nürburgring.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 14 laps (319.34 km).
