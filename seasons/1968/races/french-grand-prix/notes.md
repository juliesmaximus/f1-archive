- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, continuous torrential rain and dense fog on the downhill descent  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 65,000 spectators lining the Normandy hillside grandstands  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent Nouveau Monde hairpin grandstands, pit straight stands, and forested earthen mounds  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
A tragic, sombre French Grand Prix. On lap 3, French veteran Jo Schlesser, making his debut in Honda's experimental air-cooled magnesium-chassis Honda RA302 (which team leader John Surtees had refused to drive as a deathtrap), lost control at the downhill Six Frères curve. The car hit the earthen bank, overturned, and its full fuel load ignited the magnesium bodywork in an incinerating inferno, killing Schlesser instantly. Young Belgian Jacky Ickx mastered the terrifying conditions in the Ferrari 312 to take his MAIDEN Grand Prix victory by nearly two minutes ahead of John Surtees (Honda RA301). Jackie Stewart was 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Following Schlesser's horrific death, Honda immediately cancelled the RA302 project and withdrew completely as a works team from Formula One at the end of 1968. Formula One never returned to Rouen-les-Essarts.
