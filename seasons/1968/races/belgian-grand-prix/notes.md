## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Bruce McLaren (McLaren-Ford) — 1:40:02.100  
- **Pole Position:** Chris Amon  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cloudy, dry track with cool mountain breeze; 19°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 55,000 spectators across the Ardennes valleys  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstand, Eau Rouge hillside banks, and Blanchimont spectator crests  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HISTORIC FIRST VICTORY FOR McLAREN: Ferrari fitted towering rear aerofoils to Chris Amon and Jacky Ickx's 312s, kicking off Formula One's dramatic high-wing aerodynamic era. Amon took pole by 3.7 seconds! In the race, Denny Hulme and Jackie Stewart fought for the lead. On the penultimate lap, Stewart's Matra ran dry of fuel. Bruce McLaren in his papaya-orange McLaren M7A Ford Cosworth DFV swept past to take the chequered flag, unaware he had won until he pulled into the pits! Pedro Rodríguez finished 2nd for BRM, and Jacky Ickx was 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Monumental milestone: Bruce McLaren scored the FIRST Formula One World Championship victory for the McLaren constructor, joining Jack Brabham as the only men to win a Grand Prix in a car bearing their own name.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
