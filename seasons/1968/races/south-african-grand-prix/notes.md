## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Ford) — 1:53:56.600  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 27°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 85,000 spectators across the Kyalami amphitheatre  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HISTORIC FINAL WIN FOR JIM CLARK: Jim Clark took pole, set fastest lap, led 73 of 80 laps, and won by 25.3s over teammate Graham Hill to score a dominant Team Lotus 1-2. This was Clark's 25th career Grand Prix victory, breaking Juan Manuel Fangio's 11-year-old record of 24 wins. Tragically, it would be Clark's final World Championship race before his fatal accident at Hockenheim on April 7, 1968. Jochen Rindt took 3rd for Brabham.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The final Grand Prix appearance and final victory of the peerless Jim Clark.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 5 of last 6 races counted.
