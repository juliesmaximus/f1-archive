# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1968 - Calendar

The 1968 Formula One season was the 19th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 12 championship rounds between January 1 and November 3, 1968.

The 1968 season was a historic turning point marked by immense tragedy, revolutionary aerodynamic experimentation, and commercial transformation:
- **Tragedy of Jim Clark:** Double World Champion Jim Clark won the season opener in South Africa to break Fangio's all-time win record (25 wins). Three months later on April 7, Clark was killed in a Formula Two race at Hockenheim, plunging international motorsport into profound grief.
- **Commercial Sponsorship:** Lotus broke with traditional national racing colours (British Racing Green) to introduce full commercial sponsorship livery (Gold Leaf Team Lotus red, gold, and white).
- **Aerodynamics & Wings:** The sudden arrival of inverted aerofoils and towering high wings mounted directly to suspension uprights transformed car performance and cornering speeds.
- **Graham Hill's Leadership:** Graham Hill rallied Team Lotus following Clark's death, winning three Grands Prix to claim his second World Drivers' Championship, with Lotus taking the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
- **Bruce McLaren's Breakthrough:** Bruce McLaren delivered the maiden Grand Prix victory for the McLaren constructor at Spa-Francorchamps.
