# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1968 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Graham Hill won his second World Drivers' Championship with 48 net points (48 gross), guiding Team Lotus through the tragedy of Jim Clark's death to capture both titles.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the championship.

Team Lotus won the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 62 net points (75 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split into two mini-seasons: best 5 of first 6 rounds, best 5 of final 6 rounds.
  - Lotus-Ford scored 75 gross points, dropping 13 points to finish with 62 net points.
  - McLaren-Ford scored 51 gross points, dropping 2 points to finish with 49 net points.
  - Ferrari scored 37 gross points, dropping 5 points to finish with 32 net points.
  - Brabham-Repco scored 12 gross points, dropping 2 points to finish with 10 net points.
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated or 1.5-litre supercharged/turbocharged. Minimum car weight 500 kg.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jim Clark achieved his 25th and final Grand Prix victory at Kyalami on January 1, surpassing Juan Manuel Fangio's 11-year-old record of 24 wins.
  - Clark was tragically killed at Hockenheim on April 7 in an F2 event.
  - Graham Hill won three Grands Prix (Spain, Monaco, Mexico) to claim his second World Drivers' Championship.
  - Bruce McLaren took the McLaren team's first Grand Prix victory at Spa-Francorchamps.
  - Jo Siffert won the British Grand Prix at Brands Hatch in Rob Walker's privateer Lotus 49B, the last privateer entrant win in F1 history.
  - Mario Andretti took pole position on his World Championship debut at the United States Grand Prix (Watkins Glen) driving for Lotus.
