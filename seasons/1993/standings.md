## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Prost's 4th World Title & Retirement:** Alain Prost became a four-time World Champion (1985, 1986, 1989, 1993), retiring with an all-time record 51 Grand Prix victories and 798.5 career points.
- **Peak of Driver Aids & Electronic Systems:** 1993 marked the technological zenith of electronic driving aids in Formula 1. The Williams FW15C featured active suspension, traction control, anti-lock brakes (ABS), telemetry-controlled push-to-pass power steering, and fly-by-wire controls. At the end of 1993, the FIA announced a total ban on all active electronic aids for 1994.
- **Senna's Donington Masterpiece:** Ayrton Senna's opening lap at the European Grand Prix at Donington Park is universally cited as the finest lap in Formula 1 history ('The Lap of the Gods').
- **The Senna-Prost Farewell:** The Australian Grand Prix at Adelaide marked Ayrton Senna's 41st and final career victory, his final race for McLaren, and the emotional reconciliation on the podium with retiring rival Alain Prost.
