## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Prost's 4th World Title & Retirement:** Alain Prost secured his fourth Drivers' World Championship (1985, 1986, 1989, 1993) at the Portuguese Grand Prix, finishing second behind Michael Schumacher. Prost retired at the conclusion of the season holding all-time records of 51 Grand Prix victories and 798.5 career championship points.
- **Apex of Driver Aids & Active Technology:** 1993 marked the technological peak of electronic driving aids in Formula 1. The Williams FW15C featured active suspension, traction control, ABS braking, telemetry-controlled power steering, and fly-by-wire controls. Following safety concerns and rising costs, the FIA announced a complete ban on active suspension and electronic driver aids for 1994.
- **Senna's Donington Masterpiece:** Ayrton Senna's opening lap at the European Grand Prix at Donington Park is celebrated as 'The Lap of the Gods', moving from 5th to 1st in torrential rain in under one lap, ultimately lapping the entire field except 2nd-placed Damon Hill.
- **The Senna-Prost Reconciliation:** At Adelaide, Ayrton Senna claimed his 41st and final career Formula 1 victory in his final race for McLaren. On the podium, Senna pulled his long-time rival Alain Prost up to the top step in an emotional public embrace and reconciliation.
