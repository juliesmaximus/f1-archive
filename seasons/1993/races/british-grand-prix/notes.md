## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Alain Prost claimed his 50th career Grand Prix victory, becoming the first driver in Formula 1 history to reach the half-century milestone. Damon Hill led until lap 41 when his Renault engine suffered a catastrophic failure. Michael Schumacher finished 2nd and Ayrton Senna 3rd.
