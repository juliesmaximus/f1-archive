## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Damon Hill made it three wins in a row. Prost led comfortably from pole position until lap 49 of 53, when his Renault V10 engine blew up down the main straight. Hill swept through to win ahead of Jean Alesi (Ferrari) and Michael Andretti, who scored his first and only F1 podium in his final race before departing McLaren.
