## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Alain Prost returned from his 1992 sabbatical in devastating form with Williams-Renault. Prost took pole by 0.887s, fought off Ayrton Senna in the customer Ford-powered McLaren, and won by 1m19s in difficult changing conditions. Senna finished 2nd and Mark Blundell scored a maiden podium for Ligier.
