## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The emotional end of an era: Ayrton Senna's 41st and final Formula 1 victory, his final race for McLaren, and the final Grand Prix for 4-time World Champion Alain Prost. Senna took pole and led from start to finish, winning ahead of Prost and Damon Hill. On the podium, Senna embraced Prost and pulled him up onto the top step together, bringing a dignified and heartfelt conclusion to the greatest rivalry in motorsport history.
