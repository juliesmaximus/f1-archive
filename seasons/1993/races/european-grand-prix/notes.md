## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Widely acclaimed as the greatest single opening lap in Formula 1 history: 'The Lap of the Gods.' Senna started 4th on a soaking wet Donington track, dropped to 5th into Redgate, then systematically overtook Michael Schumacher, Karl Wendlinger (around the outside at the Craner Curves), Damon Hill at McLean's, and finally Alain Prost into the Melbourne Hairpin—all on lap 1. In a race of constantly changing weather requiring up to seven pit stops, Senna drove flawlessly to lap the entire field except 2nd-placed Damon Hill, winning by 1m23s.
