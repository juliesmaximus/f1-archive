## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A momentous day for Damon Hill, who scored his maiden Formula 1 World Championship victory. Hill became the first son of a World Champion (Graham Hill) to win a Formula 1 Grand Prix. Prost stalled on the warm-up lap, started from the back, and lost his rear wing, while Senna retired with throttle failure. Riccardo Patrese finished 2nd for Benetton and Gerhard Berger 3rd for Ferrari.
