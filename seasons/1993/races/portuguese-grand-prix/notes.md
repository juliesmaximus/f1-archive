## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The championship coronation. Prior to the weekend, Alain Prost announced he would retire from Formula 1 at the end of 1993. Michael Schumacher scored his second career victory for Benetton, holding off Alain Prost by 0.982s. By finishing 2nd, Alain Prost mathematically secured his 4th Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship, joining Juan Manuel Fangio as the only 4-time champions in history. Damon Hill finished 3rd.
