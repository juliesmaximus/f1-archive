## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A legendary victory for Ayrton Senna on home soil. Prost led until lap 29 when an apocalyptic deluge hit Interlagos. Multiple cars hydroplaned off, including Prost who collided with Christian Fittipaldi's Minardi down the pit straight, bringing out the Safety Car (the first official deployment of the modern Safety Car era in F1). Senna timed his tyre pit stops to perfection, passed Damon Hill on the drying track, and took a roaring victory to the ecstasy of the Brazilian crowd.
