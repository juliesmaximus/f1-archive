## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna made history by taking his record 6th Monaco Grand Prix victory, surpassing Graham Hill's legendary record of 5 wins. Alain Prost was penalized with a 10-second stop-go penalty for jumping the start, and later stalled his engine in the pits. Damon Hill finished 2nd and Jean Alesi 3rd for Ferrari.
