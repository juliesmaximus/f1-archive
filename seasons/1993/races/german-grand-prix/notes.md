## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Prost took his fourth consecutive victory of the season, but was aided by heartbreaking misfortune for teammate Damon Hill. Hill led comfortably until the penultimate lap (lap 44 of 45), when his left-rear tyre blew out at 190 mph. Michael Schumacher finished 2nd in his home Grand Prix, with Mark Blundell 3rd for Ligier.
