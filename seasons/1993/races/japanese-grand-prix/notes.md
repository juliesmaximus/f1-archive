## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna put on a wet-weather masterclass to claim victory ahead of Alain Prost and Mika Häkkinen (scoring his maiden podium in only his second race for McLaren). After the race, Senna was enraged by rookie Eddie Irvine (Jordan), who had unlapped himself aggressively in the wet; Senna confronted Irvine in the Jordan motorhome, resulting in a famous heated altercation.
