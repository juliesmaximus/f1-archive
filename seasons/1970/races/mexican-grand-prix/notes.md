- **Fastest Lap:** Jacky Ickx (Ferrari) — 1:43.110 (Lap 46)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Sunny, warm, dry; 24°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Colossal Estadio grandstands encircling Peraltada hairpin and grass verges  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
CHAOTIC CROWD SCENES & TRACK INVASION: An unruly crowd of over 200,000 spectators broke through wire fences and sat directly on the grass verges just feet from racing cars. Jackie Stewart and local hero Pedro Rodríguez walked the circuit with megaphones begging fans to move back to prevent a cancellation. In the race, Stewart's Tyrrell struck a stray dog crossing the track at 150 mph, breaking the front suspension. Jacky Ickx and Clay Regazzoni took a commanding 1-2 finish for Ferrari, with Denny Hulme 3rd. Ickx finished 2nd in the World Championship with 40 net points behind Rindt's 45.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The chaotic crowd scenes and safety failures caused Formula One to cancel the Mexican Grand Prix; the race did not return to the championship calendar for 16 years until 1986.
