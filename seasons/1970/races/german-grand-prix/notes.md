### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
NÜRBURGRING BOYCOTT: Drivers, led by Jackie Stewart and the Grand Prix Drivers' Association (GPDA), refused to race at the Nürburgring Nordschleife due to lack of Armco barriers and medical safety, forcing the German GP to move to the ultra-fast Hockenheimring for the first time. In the race, Jacky Ickx (Ferrari 312B Flat-12) and Jochen Rindt (Lotus 72 DFV) staged a breathtaking, high-speed slipstreaming duel, exchanging the lead officially 5 times. Rindt made a decisive move on lap 48 to win by just 0.7 seconds ahead of Ickx! Denny Hulme finished 3rd. It was Rindt's 4th consecutive victory and 5th of the season.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first World Championship Grand Prix at Hockenheim. Tragically, this would be Jochen Rindt's sixth and final career Grand Prix victory.
