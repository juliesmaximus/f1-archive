- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent pit straight grandstand and hillside banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Ken Tyrrell introduced the brand-new, in-house designed Tyrrell 001 Ford Cosworth DFV driven by Jackie Stewart. Stewart took pole position on the car's debut and led comfortably until an axle stub broke on lap 32. Jacky Ickx and Clay Regazzoni took command to deliver their second 1-2 sweep in three races for Ferrari, Ickx winning by 14.8 seconds. Chris Amon was 3rd for March. With this win, Ickx moved to 28 points, needing to win both remaining races to surpass Rindt's 45 points.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The maiden race for the Tyrrell Racing Organisation as a constructor. Set up the historic title scenario for Watkins Glen.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 90 laps (383.85 km).
