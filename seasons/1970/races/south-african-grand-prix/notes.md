## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jack Brabham (Brabham) — 1:49:35.400  
- **Pole Position:** Jackie Stewart  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 85,000 spectators surrounding the Highveld circuit  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HISTORIC FINAL WIN FOR JACK BRABHAM: 43-year-old triple World Champion Sir Jack Brabham took his 14th and final career Formula One World Championship victory in the new Brabham BT33 Ford Cosworth DFV, passing Jackie Stewart on lap 20 and pulling away to win by 8.1s over Denny Hulme (McLaren). Stewart finished 3rd in the new March 701.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jack Brabham became the second-oldest winner of modern post-war Grands Prix after Luigi Fagioli. First race for the newly founded March Engineering constructor.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 6 of first 7 races and best 5 of last 6 races counted.
