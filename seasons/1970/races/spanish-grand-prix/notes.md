## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (March) — 2:10:58.200  
- **Pole Position:** Jack Brabham  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Sunny, hot, dry; 27°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 50,000 spectators across the Jarama hillsides  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstands, Pegaso curve terraces, and Farina hillsides  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HORRIFIC LAP 1 FIRE: Jack Oliver's BRM suffered brake failure at the end of the straight, smashing into Jacky Ickx's Ferrari 312B. Both fuel tanks ruptured and exploded into a terrifying inferno, burning for over an hour. Both drivers miraculously escaped with non-life-threatening burns. Jackie Stewart dominated from 3rd on the grid in the March 701 Cosworth DFV to win by a full lap over Bruce McLaren and Mario Andretti (scoring March's first podium). Jochen Rindt debuted the radical new Lotus 72, retiring with engine failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Maiden World Championship victory for March Engineering in only their second Grand Prix. Bruce McLaren's 47th and final career podium before his fatal testing crash.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Only 16 cars permitted to start by organizers, causing immense controversy among non-qualifying entrants.
