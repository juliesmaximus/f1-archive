## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jochen Rindt (Team Lotus) — 1:50:43.400  
- **Pole Position:** Jochen Rindt  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, bright, dry, breezy; 22°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 65,000 spectators packing the seaside dunes  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstand and Tarzan hairpin dunes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR THE LOTUS 72 & TRAGEDY OF PIERS COURAGE: Jochen Rindt took pole position and dominated in the revised, sorted Lotus 72 (with side-mounted radiators and inboard front brakes), taking its maiden victory by 30.0s over Jackie Stewart (March) and Jacky Ickx (Ferrari). However, the podium was devoid of champagne due to the tragic death of 28-year-old British driver Piers Courage on lap 22; Courage's Frank Williams De Tomaso-Ford suffered steering failure at Tunnel Oost, climbed the embankment, overturned, and its magnesium chassis ignited in a violent fire.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first World Championship victory for the revolutionary Lotus 72—a design so advanced it would win 20 Grands Prix and 3 Constructors' titles through 1975.
