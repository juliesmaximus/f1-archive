## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari) — 1:39:07.100  
- **Pole Position:** Jacky Ickx  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry late summer weather; 26°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 120,000 tifosi packing the parkland  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Tribuna Centrale, Vedano, Ascari, and Parabolica spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FATAL ACCIDENT OF JOCHEN RINDT: In Saturday practice, championship leader Jochen Rindt ran his Lotus 72 with wings removed to match Ferrari's top speed on the long Monza straights. Approaching the Curva Parabolica at 180 mph, the right front inboard brake shaft sheared under braking; the car veered sharply left, striking the poorly installed Armco barrier which peeled open. Rindt was not wearing his crotch straps (a habit to enable quick exit in fire) and submarined under the dashboard, sustaining fatal throat injuries. The motorsport world was shattered. In the race, Ferrari's Clay Regazzoni drove an inspired race in only his 5th Grand Prix start to take his MAIDEN Formula One victory before delirious tifosi! Jackie Stewart was 2nd in the March, and Jean-Pierre Beltoise 3rd in the Matra.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Clay Regazzoni became a national hero in Italy. Jochen Rindt remained frozen at the top of the World Championship standings with 45 points, with three races remaining for Jacky Ickx (19 pts) to surpass him.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Team Lotus withdrew completely from the race following Rindt's fatal crash.
