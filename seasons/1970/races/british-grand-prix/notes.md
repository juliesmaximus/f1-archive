## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jochen Rindt (Team Lotus) — 1:57  
- **Pole Position:** Jochen Rindt  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 24°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 100,000 spectators packing the Kent bowl  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Brabham Straight grandstands, Druids grandstands, and South Bank natural terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
BRABHAM RUNS DRY ONCE AGAIN: Jochen Rindt took pole in the Lotus 72. Jack Brabham hounded Rindt throughout the race, seizing the lead on lap 69. Brabham built a comfortable 13-second lead and looked certain to win. Incredibly, on the final lap exiting Clearways, Brabham's BT33 sputtered and ran completely dry of fuel! Rindt flashed past to take his 3rd consecutive victory by 32.9 seconds. Brabham coasted across the line for 2nd. Denny Hulme was 3rd for McLaren. Post-race drama: Rindt was briefly disqualified for an illegal rear wing aerofoil before Colin Chapman successfully proved the wing complied with regulations.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Rindt's 3rd consecutive victory extended his championship lead to 36 points vs. Brabham's 25 points.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Rear aerofoil height and overhang rigorously checked by RAC scrutineers.
