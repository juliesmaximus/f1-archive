### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE MOST DRAMATIC MONACO FINISH IN HISTORY: Jackie Stewart led from pole until an engine misfire sidelined him on lap 27. Jack Brabham took the lead in his Brabham BT33 and appeared certain to win, holding a 9-second lead with two laps remaining. Behind him, Jochen Rindt in the older Lotus 49C drove like a man possessed, breaking the lap record lap after lap. On the final corner of the final lap (Gazomètre hairpin), Brabham came upon backmarker Ronnie Peterson, locked his front brakes on the dust off-line, and slid nose-first into the straw bales! Rindt swept past to take an astonishing, miraculous victory by 23.1s! Brabham restarted to limp home 2nd, Henri Pescarolo took 3rd for Matra.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the most famous final-lap moments in Formula One lore. Rindt's relentless pressure forced an uncharacteristic error from the veteran Brabham.
