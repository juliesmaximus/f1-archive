## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jochen Rindt (Team Lotus) — 1:55:57.000  
- **Pole Position:** Jacky Ickx  (2:58.220)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jack Brabham (Brabham-Ford) — 3:00.750 (Lap 29)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Hot, bright, sunny; 29°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent pits grandstand and volcanic hillside natural terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jacky Ickx took pole for Ferrari, but his engine blew on lap 14. Jean-Pierre Beltoise inherited the lead for Matra before suffering a puncture from the notorious volcanic stones. Jochen Rindt took command in the Lotus 72, nursing severe facial pain after being struck in the visor by a stone, to win by 7.61s ahead of Chris Amon (March) and Jack Brabham. Denny Hulme took 4th for McLaren.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Rindt took the lead of the World Drivers' Championship, establishing the Lotus 72 as the class of the field.
