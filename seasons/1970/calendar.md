# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1970 - Calendar

The 1970 Formula One season was the 21st season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 13 championship rounds between March 7 and October 25, 1970.

The 1970 season is etched in motorsport history by the triumph and tragic immortality of Austrian driver **Jochen Rindt**. Driving Colin Chapman's revolutionary wedge-shaped **Lotus 72** Ford Cosworth DFV, Rindt won five Grands Prix (Monaco, Netherlands, France, Great Britain, Germany). During Saturday practice for the Italian Grand Prix at Monza, Rindt was fatally injured when a brake shaft failed. When rookie teammate Emerson Fittipaldi won the United States Grand Prix at Watkins Glen, Ferrari's Jacky Ickx was mathematically prevented from surpassing Rindt's points total, making Jochen Rindt the **first and only posthumous World Champion in Formula One history**. Team Lotus secured their fourth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
