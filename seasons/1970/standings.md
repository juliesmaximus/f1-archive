# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1970 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **6** results from the first 7 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Austrian Jochen Rindt won the World Drivers' Championship posthumously with 45 net points (45 gross), having won five Grands Prix before his fatal accident at Monza.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **6** results from the first 7 races and best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the championship.

Team Lotus won their fourth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 59 net points (59 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 6 results from rounds 1–7 and best 5 results from rounds 8–13 counted towards both championships.
- **Safety Milestones:**
  - Mandatory Armco barriers installed at Spa and Charade.
  - Drivers successfully boycotted the Nürburgring Nordschleife for the German GP, forcing a move to Hockenheim.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jochen Rindt became the first and only posthumous World Champion in Formula One history.
  - The debut of the revolutionary Lotus 72, introducing side-mounted radiators, inboard front brakes, and wedge-shaped aerodynamics.
  - Jack Brabham scored his 14th and final career victory at Kyalami.
  - Clay Regazzoni and Emerson Fittipaldi scored their maiden Grand Prix victories.
  - Ken Tyrrell debuted his own chassis constructor, the Tyrrell 001.
