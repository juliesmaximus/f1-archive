## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Williams continued their mid-season domination on the forest straights of Hockenheim. Nigel Mansell scored his third consecutive victory, converting pole position into a commanding win ahead of teammate Riccardo Patrese for a Williams-Renault 1-2.

The race featured intense drama behind the Williams pair. Alain Prost attempted to pass Ayrton Senna into the first chicane on lap 38. Senna defended aggressively, forcing Prost off onto the grass escape road, where the Ferrari stalled. Prost angrily accused Senna of dangerous driving. Senna's luck ran out on the final lap when his McLaren ran completely out of fuel for the second consecutive race, coasting to a halt and dropping to 7th, outside the points. Jean Alesi took 3rd for Ferrari.
