## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna made it four victories from the opening four rounds of 1991 with a masterclass across the streets of Monte Carlo. Starting from pole position, Senna led every single lap to secure his 4th career Monaco Grand Prix win (and third consecutive).

Behind him, Stefano Modena qualified an outstanding 2nd for Tyrrell-Honda and ran comfortably in 2nd place until lap 43, when his Honda V10 blew up inside the tunnel, coating the asphalt in oil. Riccardo Patrese crashed on Modena's oil moments later. Nigel Mansell finished 2nd for Williams, while Jean Alesi took 3rd for Ferrari.
