## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Debut of Silverstone's redesigned, flowing layout including Bridge and Priory corners. Mansell took pole and dominated the race to win by 42 seconds. Senna ran out of fuel on the final lap while in 2nd place. On the slowdown lap, Mansell famously stopped his Williams FW14 and gave Senna a ride back to the pits on his sidepod—one of the most iconic images in F1 history. Gerhard Berger finished 2nd and Alain Prost 3rd.
