## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A legendary weekend at Silverstone on a redesigned layout that added sweeping infield complexes at Becketts, Priory, Brooklands, and Luffield. Nigel Mansell ignited "Mansell Mania" by claiming pole position with a stunning lap of 1:20.939, nearly seven-tenths faster than Senna.

In the race, Mansell led from start to finish, setting fastest lap and cruising to an overwhelming 42-second victory. Title rival Ayrton Senna had run 2nd all afternoon until his McLaren ran out of fuel on the final lap at Club corner.

On his slowing-down lap, Mansell stopped his Williams FW14 to pick up the stranded Senna, giving him a ride on the sidepod back to the pit lane amid rapturous scenes from thousands of track-invading fans. Gerhard Berger inherited 2nd, while Prost finished 3rd.
