## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Williams confirmed their FW14 chassis as the fastest package in Formula 1 by taking a dominant 1-2 finish. Riccardo Patrese took pole position, but dropped behind teammate Nigel Mansell and Jean Alesi at the start. Patrese methodically charged back, overtaking Mansell for the lead on lap 15 and holding him off to the flag to take his fourth career Grand Prix victory.

Ayrton Senna suffered a terrifying crash during Friday qualifying when his McLaren overturned in the high-speed Peraltada curve, leaving him upside down in the tyre barrier with neck and leg bruises. Despite the crash, Senna started the race and drove through the pain barrier to finish 3rd.
