## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 debut at Magny-Cours in central France, replacing Circuit Paul Ricard. Nigel Mansell and Alain Prost fought an intense duel. Mansell executed a thrilling dummy overtake on Prost into the Adelaide hairpin with 21 laps to go to take the victory. Senna finished 3rd.
