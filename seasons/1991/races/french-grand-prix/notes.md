## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 inaugurated a new venue as the French Grand Prix relocated from Circuit Paul Ricard to the Circuit de Nevers Magny-Cours in central France. Riccardo Patrese secured his third consecutive pole position, but stalled on the grid at the start.

Home favourite Alain Prost delighted the French fans by taking the lead in the revised Ferrari 643. Nigel Mansell pursued Prost aggressively in his Williams FW14, mounting a thrilling chase. On lap 54, Mansell made a daring move around the outside of Prost into the Adelaide hairpin to take the lead. Mansell pulled away to claim his first victory of the 1991 season, with Prost 2nd and Senna 3rd.
