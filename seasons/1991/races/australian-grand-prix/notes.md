## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The shortest race in Formula 1 World Championship history at that time. Torrential monsoonal rain inundated Adelaide, causing zero visibility and catastrophic aquaplaning. Starting from pole, Senna led while behind him cars spun off in every direction: Jean Alesi, Nicola Larini, and Pierluigi Martini crashed on the pit straight, Nigel Mansell crashed heavily into the wall on the Brabham Straight on lap 16, and Gerhard Berger spun twice.

Senna furiously waved from his cockpit as he drove down the pit straight on lap 16, demanding that the race be stopped. Officials threw the red flag on lap 16. By regulation, results were backdated to the end of lap 14. Lasting just 24 minutes and 34 seconds (52.92 km), it stood as the shortest race in F1 history for 30 years until Spa 2021.

Half points were awarded (Senna 5 pts, Mansell 3 pts, Berger 2 pts, Piquet 1.5 pts, Patrese 1 pt, Morbidelli 0.5 pt). McLaren clinched their 7th Constructors' Championship.

---

## Ferrari Turmoil & Prost Sacking
Following the Japanese Grand Prix, Alain Prost publicly criticized Ferrari, comparing the handling of the Ferrari 643 to an "unpleasant truck". Ferrari responded by terminating Prost's contract on the spot before Adelaide. Test driver Gianni Morbidelli was drafted into Prost's seat, finishing 6th and scoring 0.5 points in his sole Ferrari Grand Prix appearance.
