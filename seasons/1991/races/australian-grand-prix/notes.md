## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The shortest race in Formula 1 World Championship history (at the time). A catastrophic tropical deluge inundated Adelaide with standing rivers across the track. Multiple cars hydroplaned into the walls on laps 12 to 14, including Mansell and Berger. Senna frantically waved from his cockpit for the race to be stopped. The red flag was shown on lap 14; with results counted back to lap 14, Senna was declared the winner after just 52.92 km (24 minutes and 34 seconds of racing). Half points were awarded.
