## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The first race under the new 10-point winner system and without dropped scores. Senna secured pole in the new McLaren MP4/6 Honda V12 and led all 81 laps to victory. Alain Prost finished 2nd for Ferrari and Nelson Piquet 3rd for Benetton.
