## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The 1991 season commenced on a slightly revised 3.721 km layout in downtown Phoenix. It marked the final United States Grand Prix held in Phoenix and the introduction of the new 10-point scoring system for race victories (10-6-4-3-2-1), along with the complete abolition of dropped scores.

Ayrton Senna claimed pole position in the new McLaren MP4/6 powered by Honda's RA121E V12 engine. Senna dominated from the start, leading every single lap to claim the maximum 10 points. Alain Prost finished 2nd in his Ferrari 642, with Nelson Piquet rounding out the podium for Benetton. The new Tyrrell 020 equipped with Honda V10 engines demonstrated excellent reliability, finishing 4th with Stefano Modena and 5th with Satoru Nakajima.
