## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Championship decider. Mansell needed to win to keep his title hopes alive. Chasing Senna closely, Mansell suffered a brake/spin off on lap 10 at the first corner, sliding into the gravel trap. With Mansell eliminated, Ayrton Senna officially clinched his 3rd Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship. On the final lap, Senna slowed down at the final chicane to gift the race victory to his faithful teammate Gerhard Berger.
