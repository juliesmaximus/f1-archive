## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A momentous weekend in Formula 1 history: 22-year-old German rookie Michael Schumacher made his Grand Prix debut for Jordan-Ford, substituting for Bertrand Gachot. Schumacher stunned the paddock by qualifying 7th on his first appearance at Spa. His clutch failed on lap 1, but his pace caught the eye of Benetton boss Flavio Briatore. Ayrton Senna won the race ahead of Gerhard Berger (McLaren 1-2) after Mansell's Williams suffered electrical failure.
