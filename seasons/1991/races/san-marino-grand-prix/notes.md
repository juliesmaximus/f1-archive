## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A torrential downpour moments before the start transformed the track into a skating rink. On the formation lap, Alain Prost lost control of his Ferrari 642 at the Rivazza corner, spinning into the grass and stalling his engine, recording an embarrassing DNS on Ferrari's home turf. Gerhard Berger also spun off at Rivazza on the warmup lap but kept his Honda engine running.

At the green light, Nigel Mansell's Williams collided with Stefano Modena's Tyrrell on the opening lap at Rivazza, putting Mansell out. Senna mastered the wet conditions, leading from start to finish for a McLaren-Honda 1-2 with Berger.

The sensation of the race was Finnish driver JJ Lehto (Jyrki Järvilehto), who produced a magnificent, error-free drive in the BMS Scuderia Italia Dallara-Judd to claim 3rd place, securing his first career podium and the only podium in Dallara's Formula 1 constructor history.
