## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Wet pre-race drama when Alain Prost spun his Ferrari into the wet grass on the formation lap at Rivazza and stalled, failing even to start the race. Senna led from pole position to win ahead of teammate Gerhard Berger, sealing a McLaren 1-2, with JJ Lehto taking a stunning maiden podium in 3rd for Scuderia Italia Dallara.
