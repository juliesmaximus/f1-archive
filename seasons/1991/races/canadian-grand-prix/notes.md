## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most famous last-lap blunders in Formula 1 history. Nigel Mansell dominated the race in the Williams-Renault, opening a 56-second lead. On the final lap, approaching the hairpin, Mansell began waving prematurely to the cheering Canadian crowd. He allowed the engine revs to drop too low in the slow corner, stalling his Renault V10. His former teammate Nelson Piquet swept past to take an astonishing victory for Benetton. Stefano Modena finished 2nd for Tyrrell and Riccardo Patrese 3rd.
