## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most astonishing finishes in Grand Prix history. Williams locked out the front row and Nigel Mansell dominated the race, leading by over 50 seconds on the final lap. Approaching the Casino hairpin less than half a mile from the chequered flag, Mansell began waving prematurely to the cheering Canadian crowd. In doing so, he let the engine revs of his Williams FW14 drop too low while in 6th gear, stalling his Renault V10 engine.

Mansell ground to a halt at the edge of the circuit. Nelson Piquet, cruising in 2nd place in his Benetton B191, swept past the stranded Williams to claim an unexpected victory—the 23rd and final Formula 1 Grand Prix victory of Piquet's legendary career. Stefano Modena finished 2nd for Tyrrell, with Patrese taking 3rd. Both McLarens retired with mechanical failures, ending Senna's four-race winning streak.
