## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Ayrton Senna's most legendary, emotional victories: his maiden home Grand Prix win in Brazil. Senna led comfortably until his gearbox began failing in the closing laps, losing third, fourth, and fifth gears until only 6th gear remained. Rain began pouring down on lap 60. Senna wrestled the car around the slippery track exclusively in 6th gear, keeping the engine from stalling in low-speed hairpins. He crossed the finish line 2.9 seconds ahead of Riccardo Patrese, collapsed from extreme muscle spasms and exhaustion, and screamed in triumph on the team radio.
