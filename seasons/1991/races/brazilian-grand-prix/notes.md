## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most famous and emotional victories in Formula 1 history. Ayrton Senna had never won his home Grand Prix in seven previous attempts. Starting from pole, Senna built a lead over Nigel Mansell until Mansell spun on lap 60 with gearbox failure.

With seven laps remaining, Senna's McLaren gearbox began failing catastrophically: first losing 4th gear, then 3rd, and finally 5th gear. Senna was forced to drive the final four laps exclusively in 6th gear, keeping the car running through low-speed corners by feathering the throttle while rain began to fall. The extreme physical exertion caused violent muscle spasms in his neck and shoulders.

Senna held on by 2.991 seconds to cross the line, screaming in agony and euphoria over team radio. Unable to exit the cockpit unaided, he had to be lifted from the car and driven to the medical centre before appearing on the podium, where he could barely lift the trophy above his head before the ecstatic Brazilian fans.
