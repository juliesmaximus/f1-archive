## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna and McLaren responded decisively to Williams' recent winning streak. Qualifying on pole position by 1.2 seconds over Patrese and Mansell, Senna controlled the race from the front around the twisty Hungaroring where overtaking was virtually impossible.

Mansell spent the entire afternoon attempting to pressure Senna, but the Brazilian made no errors to claim victory by 4.599 seconds, ending Williams' three-race winning streak and extending his championship lead to 12 points.

Bertrand Gachot delivered a memorable performance in the Jordan 191, setting the fastest lap of the Grand Prix on lap 71 (1:21.547), the first and only fastest lap in Jordan Grand Prix's debut season.
