## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Williams pit stop disaster. Mansell led from Patrese until his tyre pit stop on lap 29, when his right-rear wheel was improperly secured and bounced off down the pit lane. Mechanics reattached the wheel in the pit lane transit road (a strict safety breach). Mansell was black-flagged and disqualified, handing victory to Riccardo Patrese ahead of Ayrton Senna and Jean Alesi.
