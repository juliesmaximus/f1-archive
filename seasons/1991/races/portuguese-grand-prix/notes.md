## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A disastrous afternoon for Nigel Mansell's championship ambitions. Williams locked out the front row and Mansell took the lead from pole-sitter Patrese on lap 18. Mansell pulled away comfortably until his scheduled tyre stop on lap 29.

As Mansell was waved away from his pit box, the right-rear wheel had not been properly secured. The wheel detached and bounced down the pit lane, leaving Mansell stranded on three wheels. Williams mechanics rushed down the pit lane and fitted a new wheel in the middle of the pit transit lane.

Mansell rejoined in 17th place and mounted a furious charge, but was shown the black flag and disqualified on lap 50 for receiving outside assistance in an unauthorized area of the pit lane. Riccardo Patrese inherited the victory, with Senna finishing 2nd to extend his championship lead to 24 points.
