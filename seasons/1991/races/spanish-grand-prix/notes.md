## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The inaugural Grand Prix at the newly built Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya in Montmeló produced one of the most iconic images in Formula 1 history.

Damp conditions at the start forced drivers onto wet tyres. On lap 5, with the track drying rapidly, Nigel Mansell closed down Ayrton Senna along the 1-kilometre pit straight. The two title rivals engaged in an unforgettable wheel-to-wheel drag race at 190 mph, separated by mere inches with sparks showering from their titanium skid blocks, neither driver lifting until Mansell out-braked Senna into Turn 1.

Mansell went on to dominate the race and win by 11.3 seconds over Alain Prost, with Patrese 3rd and Senna 5th after a spin at the final corner. The victory kept Mansell's championship hopes alive heading into Suzuka.
