## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 debut at the brand new Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya. Produced one of the most famous wheel-to-wheel duels in history: Mansell and Senna racing side-by-side down the 1-kilometre pit straight, sparks flying from their undertrays, wheels inches apart at 190 mph. Mansell held the inside line into Turn 1 to take the lead and won the race ahead of Alain Prost and Riccardo Patrese.
