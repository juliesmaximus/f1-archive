## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna took pole position, but Williams demonstrated superior race pace around Monza's long straights. Mansell bided his time in 2nd place until lap 34, when he executed a clinical overtake on Senna under braking for the Prima Variante chicane.

Senna pitted for fresh tyres and fought back to claim 2nd, but Mansell cruised to victory to trim Senna's championship lead to 18 points. Alain Prost finished 3rd for Ferrari in front of the home Tifosi.

Michael Schumacher made his Benetton debut following his controversial switch from Jordan, out-qualifying three-time World Champion teammate Nelson Piquet and finishing 5th to score his maiden World Championship points.
