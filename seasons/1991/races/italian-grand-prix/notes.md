## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher's first race for Benetton following a fierce contract dispute with Jordan. Schumacher scored his first career points finishing 5th. Nigel Mansell overtook Ayrton Senna on lap 34 to take a magnificent victory for Williams-Renault, with Senna 2nd and Alain Prost 3rd.
