## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.5-litre naturally aspirated engines (maximum 12 cylinders). McLaren used Honda RA121E V12; Williams used Renault RS3 V10; Ferrari used Tipo 037 V12; Benetton used Ford HBA5/HBA6 V8.
- **Circuit Debuts:** Circuit de Nevers Magny-Cours replaced Circuit Paul Ricard as the permanent host of the French Grand Prix; Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya made its debut hosting the Spanish Grand Prix.
- **Points Scoring System:** 10-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers (first season in history to award 10 points for a victory).
- **Abolition of Dropped Scores:** 1991 was the first season in Formula 1 World Championship history where all race results counted towards both the Drivers' and Constructors' Championships (best-results rule permanently abolished).
- **Shortest Race in History:** The Australian Grand Prix was red-flagged after 14 laps due to extreme monsoonal flooding. At 52.92 km and 24m34s, it stood as the shortest race in World Championship history until the 2021 Belgian Grand Prix. Half points were awarded.
