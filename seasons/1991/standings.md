## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Abolition of Dropped Scores:** 1991 was the first season in Formula 1 World Championship history where all race results counted towards both the Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
- **10 Points for Victory:** Winner's points were increased from 9 to 10 points (10-6-4-3-2-1 system).
- **Senna's Third Crown:** Ayrton Senna became a three-time World Champion (1988, 1990, 1991), joining Jack Brabham, Jackie Stewart, Niki Lauda, and Nelson Piquet as the youngest 3-time champion in F1 history at age 31.
- **Michael Schumacher Debut:** Future seven-time World Champion Michael Schumacher made his sensational debut at Spa for Jordan-Ford, qualifying 7th, before being signed by Benetton-Ford for the remainder of the season.
- **Adelaide Shortest Race Record:** The Australian Grand Prix was red-flagged after 14 laps due to extreme monsoonal flooding. At 52.92 km and 24m34s, it stood as the shortest race in World Championship history until the 2021 Belgian Grand Prix. Half points were awarded.
- **Ferrari Turmoil & Prost Sacking:** Ferrari failed to win a single race in 1991. Following the Japanese Grand Prix, Alain Prost publicly likened the Ferrari 643's heavy handling to a "truck", resulting in Ferrari terminating his contract before the season finale in Adelaide. Gianni Morbidelli was promoted to drive Prost's car in Adelaide, scoring 0.5 points for 6th place.
