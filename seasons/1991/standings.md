## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Abolition of Dropped Scores:** 1991 was the first season in Formula 1 World Championship history where all race results counted towards both the Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
- **10 Points for Victory:** The winner's points were increased from 9 to 10 points (10-6-4-3-2-1 system).
- **Senna's Third Crown:** Ayrton Senna became a three-time World Champion (1988, 1990, 1991), joining Brabham, Stewart, Lauda, and Piquet as the youngest 3-time champion in F1 history at age 31.
- **Michael Schumacher Debut:** Formula 1's future seven-time World Champion made his debut at Spa for Jordan-Ford, qualifying 7th, before signing with Benetton for the remainder of the season.
- **Adelaide Shortest Race Record:** The Australian Grand Prix was red-flagged after 14 laps due to extreme monsoonal flooding. At 52.92 km and 24m34s, it stood as the shortest race in World Championship history until Spa 2021.
