## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
McLaren-Mercedes heartbreak turned into championship triumph for Villeneuve. Mika Häkkinen took his maiden pole position and led comfortably, with Coulthard 2nd. Within one lap of each other (laps 42-43), both Mercedes engines suffered identical catastrophic failures. Jacques Villeneuve took over the lead to win ahead of Jean Alesi and Heinz-Harald Frentzen. Schumacher was eliminated on lap 1 in a collision with brother Ralf.
