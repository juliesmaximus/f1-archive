## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Villeneuve scored back-to-back victories from pole position, fending off a ferocious late-race attack from Eddie Irvine's Ferrari to win by just 0.979 seconds. Ralf Schumacher scored his maiden Formula 1 podium in 3rd for Jordan-Peugeot after surviving a collision with teammate Fisichella. Michael Schumacher was taken out on lap 1 after colliding with Barrichello.
