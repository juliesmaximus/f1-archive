## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
David Coulthard scored a breakthrough victory for McLaren-Mercedes—their first Grand Prix win since Ayrton Senna in Adelaide 1993, ending a 50-race drought and debuting the iconic silver-and-black West livery. Polesitter Jacques Villeneuve was eliminated at Turn 1 in a collision with Eddie Irvine. Michael Schumacher finished 2nd and Mika Häkkinen 3rd.
