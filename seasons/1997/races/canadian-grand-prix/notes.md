## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher took victory from pole position. Local hero Jacques Villeneuve crashed into the 'Wall of Champions' on lap 2. The race was red-flagged on lap 54 following a horrifying crash for Olivier Panis, who suffered double fractures to both legs when his Prost slammed the tyre barrier at high speed. Jean Alesi and Giancarlo Fisichella completed the podium.
