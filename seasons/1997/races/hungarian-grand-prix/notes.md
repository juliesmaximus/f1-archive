## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nearly the greatest underdog upset in Formula 1 history. Reigning World Champion Damon Hill in the uncompetitive Arrows-Yamaha on Bridgestone tyres qualified 3rd, passed Schumacher on lap 11, and led the race by an astonishing 35 seconds! On the penultimate lap, a hydraulic throttle-valve failure trapped Hill's car in 3rd gear. Jacques Villeneuve swept past on the grass on the final lap to take the win. Hill nursed the stricken Arrows home for an unforgettable 2nd place, with Johnny Herbert 3rd for Sauber.
