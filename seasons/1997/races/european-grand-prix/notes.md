## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most dramatic, historic finales in motorsport history. In qualifying, three drivers set the EXACT same pole lap time to the thousandth of a second: Jacques Villeneuve, Michael Schumacher, and Heinz-Harald Frentzen all set 1:21.072! Villeneuve took pole as he set it first. In the race, Schumacher led with Villeneuve tracking him closely. On lap 48, Villeneuve made a bold dive down the inside at the Dry Sac hairpin. Schumacher turned in aggressively and rammed into the side of the Williams. Schumacher's Ferrari bounced off and became beached in the gravel trap, out of the race. Villeneuve's damaged car limped on; on the final lap, he allowed McLaren teammates Mika Häkkinen and David Coulthard past, with Häkkinen taking his maiden Formula 1 victory. By finishing 3rd, Jacques Villeneuve clinched the 1997 World Drivers' Championship. Following a disciplinary hearing, the FIA disqualified Schumacher from the 1997 Championship for deliberate foul play.
