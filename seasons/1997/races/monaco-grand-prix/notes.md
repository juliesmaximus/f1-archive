---

## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A wet-weather masterclass from Michael Schumacher. While Williams made a disastrous gamble starting both cars on slick tyres on a soaking track, Schumacher started on intermediates and built an unbelievable 22-second lead on lap 1 alone! Schumacher cruised to victory by 53 seconds over Rubens Barrichello (scoring Stewart Grand Prix's maiden podium) and Eddie Irvine.
