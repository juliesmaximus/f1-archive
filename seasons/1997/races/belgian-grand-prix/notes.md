---

## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher produced another wet masterclass. After the first 3 laps ran behind the Safety Car due to torrential rain, Schumacher overtook Alesi and Villeneuve on lap 5, pulled away at five seconds per lap, and won by 26 seconds over Giancarlo Fisichella (Jordan) and Heinz-Harald Frentzen.
