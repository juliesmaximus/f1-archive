## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher took a vital victory, aided by team tactics from Eddie Irvine who led early and let Schumacher through while holding up Villeneuve. Villeneuve raced under appeal after being handed a suspended ban for ignoring yellow flags in Saturday practice; he finished 5th but was subsequently disqualified, leaving Schumacher with a 1-point championship lead heading into the finale.
