## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Jacques Villeneuve won from pole position, managing his Goodyear tyres through extreme blistering while Ferrari suffered severe degradation. Olivier Panis drove an extraordinary race on Bridgestone tyres for Prost Grand Prix, finishing 2nd, just 5.8s behind Villeneuve. Jean Alesi took 3rd for Benetton.
