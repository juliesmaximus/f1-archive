## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most emotional victories in modern F1 history. 37-year-old Gerhard Berger returned after missing three races due to illness and the sudden death of his father in an aeroplane accident. Berger secured pole position, set fastest lap, and drove an inspired race to take the 10th and final Grand Prix victory of his career—also the final victory for Benetton Formula. Schumacher finished 2nd and Mika Häkkinen 3rd.
