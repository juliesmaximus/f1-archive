## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 returned to Austria at the modernized A1-Ring (built on the site of the Österreichring). Jacques Villeneuve scored a vital victory, while Schumacher was penalized with a 10-second stop-go penalty for passing Frentzen under yellow flags, finishing 6th. David Coulthard finished 2nd and Heinz-Harald Frentzen 3rd.
