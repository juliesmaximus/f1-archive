## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Heinz-Harald Frentzen scored his maiden Formula 1 victory for Williams-Renault, withstanding intense pressure from Michael Schumacher's Ferrari over the closing laps to win by 1.237 seconds. Eddie Irvine took 3rd. Jacques Villeneuve retired with gearbox failure.
