## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Jacques Villeneuve took victory after polesitter Schumacher retired with a wheel bearing failure while leading. Mika Häkkinen led until his Mercedes engine expired with 7 laps to go. Jean Alesi and Alexander Wurz (scoring a maiden podium in his third race) completed the podium for Benetton.
