## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Schumacher Breaks All-Time Wins Record:** Michael Schumacher claimed his 52nd career Formula 1 victory at Spa-Francorchamps, surpassing Alain Prost's record of 51 Grand Prix wins. Schumacher clinched his 4th World Championship at Hungary, tying Prost's career championship total.
- **Re-Introduction of Traction Control:** From Round 5 in Spain, the FIA permitted electronic driver aids including traction control, launch control, and fully automatic downshifts.
- **Debuts of Future Champions:** Kimi Räikkönen (Sauber) and Fernando Alonso (Minardi) made their Formula 1 debuts at the Australian season opener. Räikkönen scored a point for 6th on his debut.
- **Williams-BMW Breakthroughs:** Williams-BMW scored four victories, with Ralf Schumacher taking his maiden win at Imola and Juan Pablo Montoya scoring his maiden win at Monza. The Schumacher brothers became the first siblings to finish 1-2 in Formula 1 history at Montreal.
- **Mika Häkkinen's Sabbatical:** Two-time champion Mika Häkkinen announced a sabbatical at the end of 2001, which ultimately became permanent retirement from Formula 1.
