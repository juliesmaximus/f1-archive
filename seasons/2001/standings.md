## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Schumacher Breaks All-Time Wins Record:** Michael Schumacher claimed his 52nd victory at Spa-Francorchamps, breaking Alain Prost's long-standing record of 51 wins. He equaled Prost's tally of 4 World Championships.
- **Re-Introduction of Traction Control:** From Round 5 in Spain, the FIA re-legalized traction control, launch control, and fully automatic downshifts.
- **Debuts of Future Champions:** Kimi Räikkönen (Sauber) and Fernando Alonso (Minardi) made their Formula 1 debuts in Australia.
- **Williams-BMW Resurgence:** Juan Pablo Montoya burst onto the scene, scoring his maiden victory at Monza; Ralf Schumacher scored his maiden victory at Imola, and the brothers scored the first 1-2 brother finish in F1 history at Montreal.
