## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Another battle of the brothers. Michael Schumacher defended aggressively against brother Ralf at the start, swerving across the track to protect the inside line. Michael took victory ahead of Juan Pablo Montoya and David Coulthard after Ralf served a 10-second stop-go penalty for crossing the white line at pit exit.
