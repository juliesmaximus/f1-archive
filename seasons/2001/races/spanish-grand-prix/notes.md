## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The re-legalization of electronic traction control, launch control, and automatic downshifts. Mika Häkkinen produced a dominant performance and held a commanding 42-second lead on the final lap. Approaching Turn 4 with less than half a lap remaining, Häkkinen's clutch shattered, leaving his McLaren coasting to a halt in heartbreak. Michael Schumacher swept past to take victory, with Juan Pablo Montoya scoring his maiden F1 podium in 2nd and Jacques Villeneuve 3rd for BAR-Honda.
