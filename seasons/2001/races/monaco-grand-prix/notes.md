## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Polesitter David Coulthard stalled on the grid before the formation lap due to a software glitch in McLaren's new launch control, starting from the back. Michael Schumacher dominated to take his 5th Monaco Grand Prix victory, leading Rubens Barrichello home for a Ferrari 1-2. Eddie Irvine scored Jaguar Racing's maiden podium in 3rd.
