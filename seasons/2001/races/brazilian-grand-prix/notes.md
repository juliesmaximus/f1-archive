## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Juan Pablo Montoya announced his arrival on the world stage in only his 3rd Grand Prix. Following an early Safety Car restart, rookie Montoya executed an audacious, fearless dive down the inside of Michael Schumacher into the Senna S, muscling the Ferrari aside to lead the race. Montoya led until lap 38, when Jos Verstappen's Arrows violently rear-ended him while being lapped, eliminating Montoya. David Coulthard overtook Schumacher in the wet to win, with Schumacher 2nd and Nick Heidfeld scoring a maiden podium for Sauber in 3rd.
