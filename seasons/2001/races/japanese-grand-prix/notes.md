## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Season finale at Suzuka and Mika Häkkinen's final Formula 1 Grand Prix before taking his sabbatical (which became permanent retirement). Michael Schumacher took pole and led comfortably to score his 9th win of the season, finishing ahead of Juan Pablo Montoya and David Coulthard.
