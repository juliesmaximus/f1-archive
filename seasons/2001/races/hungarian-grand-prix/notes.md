## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The coronation of Michael Schumacher. Schumacher took pole position, equaling Ayrton Senna's all-time record of 41 pole positions, and led every lap to win. With this victory, Michael Schumacher equaled Alain Prost's all-time record of 51 career Grand Prix wins and clinched his 4th World Drivers' Championship with four races to spare. Ferrari clinched the World Constructors' Championship.
