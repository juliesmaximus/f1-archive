## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A historic milestone: Ralf Schumacher and Michael Schumacher scored the first brother 1-2 finish in Formula 1 World Championship history. Ralf shadowed his brother before executing an overcut during pit stops, taking victory for Williams-BMW by 20 seconds. Mika Häkkinen finished 3rd.
