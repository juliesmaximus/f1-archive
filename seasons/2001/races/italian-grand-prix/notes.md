## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A deeply emotional weekend following the September 11 terrorist attacks in the USA and Alex Zanardi's horrific crash in Germany. Ferrari removed all sponsor decals and painted their nosecones matte black in mourning. Juan Pablo Montoya took pole and scored his maiden Formula 1 victory for Williams-BMW, with Rubens Barrichello 2nd and Ralf Schumacher 3rd.
