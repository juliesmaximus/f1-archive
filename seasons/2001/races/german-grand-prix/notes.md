## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The final Grand Prix held on Hockenheim's legendary long forest straights before circuit redesign. Williams-BMW locked out the front row with their unmatched top speed. Ralf Schumacher won comfortably ahead of Rubens Barrichello and Jacques Villeneuve. Michael Schumacher retired with fuel pressure failure.
