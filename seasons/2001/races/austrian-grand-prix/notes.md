## Historical Context & Narrative Summary

Controvery struck on the final lap when Ferrari ordered Rubens Barrichello to yield 2nd place to Michael Schumacher over the team radio ('Let Michael pass for the championship'); Barrichello reluctantly moved aside on the final straight.
