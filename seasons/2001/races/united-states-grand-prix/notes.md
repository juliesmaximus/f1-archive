## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Held under immense security just weeks after September 11, with overwhelming unity and support from the international paddock. Mika Häkkinen drove an inspired race to claim the 20th and final Grand Prix victory of his illustrious career. Michael Schumacher finished 2nd and David Coulthard 3rd.
