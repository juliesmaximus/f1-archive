## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A masterclass in rain driving and pit strategy from Ferrari. On lap 3, an apocalyptic tropical downpour flooded the circuit, causing both Ferraris to slide off into the gravel at Turn 6. Both recovered and pitted simultaneously for intermediate tyres while rivals chose full wets. The intermediate tyres were superior as the track dried rapidly; Schumacher and Barrichello sliced through the field to score an unbelievable Ferrari 1-2, with Coulthard 3rd.
