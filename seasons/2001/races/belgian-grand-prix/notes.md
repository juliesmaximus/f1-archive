## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher made history by scoring his 52nd career Grand Prix victory, officially surpassing Alain Prost to become the most successful driver in Formula 1 history. The race was red-flagged on lap 5 following a terrifying 170 mph crash for Luciano Burti at Blanchimont. David Coulthard finished 2nd and Giancarlo Fisichella scored a sensational podium for Benetton in 3rd.
