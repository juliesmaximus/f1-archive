## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ralf Schumacher scored his maiden Formula 1 Grand Prix victory for Williams-BMW, propelling the potent BMW P80 V10 engine to its first win. David Coulthard finished 2nd and Rubens Barrichello 3rd, while Michael Schumacher retired with suspension failure.
