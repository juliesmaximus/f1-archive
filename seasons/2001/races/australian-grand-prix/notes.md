## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher took victory from pole position for Ferrari, ahead of David Coulthard and Rubens Barrichello. Future World Champions Kimi Räikkönen (Sauber) and Fernando Alonso (Minardi) made their Grand Prix debuts, with Räikkönen scoring a point for 6th on debut. The race was shadowed by tragedy when track marshal Graham Beveridge was fatally struck by a flying wheel following a high-speed collision between Jacques Villeneuve and Ralf Schumacher.
