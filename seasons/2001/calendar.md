## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Engine Rules:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated V10 engines.
- **Driver Aids Return:** Traction control and launch control permitted from Round 5 (Spanish GP).
- **Crash Test Upgrades:** Three-stage side impact test introduced; higher roll hoop load specifications.
