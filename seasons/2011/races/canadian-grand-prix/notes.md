## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The longest race in Formula 1 history: 4 hours, 4 minutes, and 39 seconds. Torrential rain suspended the race on lap 25 for over two hours. Jenson Button endured a nightmare: collided with teammate Hamilton on the pit straight (retiring Hamilton), collided with Alonso at Turn 3 (spinning Alonso out), served a drive-through penalty for speeding behind the safety car, suffered a puncture, and sat in 21st (dead last) on lap 40! Over the final 30 laps on a drying track, Button unleashed the drive of a lifetime, slicing through the field. On the very final lap, Button pressured leader Sebastian Vettel into Turn 6; Vettel touched the damp line, slid wide, and Button swept past to claim an immortal, miraculous victory!

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Button made six visits to the pitlane (5 pitstops + 1 penalty) and still won the Grand Prix—an unbroken all-time record.
