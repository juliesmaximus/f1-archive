## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel scored a dominant Grand Slam: pole, win, fastest lap, and led every lap. In an extraordinary demonstration of modern engineering perfection, every single one of the 24 starters finished the race—setting the all-time Formula 1 record for the most classified finishers in a single Grand Prix (24 cars).
