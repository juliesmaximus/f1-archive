## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An all-time Monaco classic. Heading into the final 10 laps, a three-way dogfight for the lead materialized: Sebastian Vettel on 54-lap-old bald tyres, stalked nose-to-tail by Fernando Alonso on fresher tyres and Jenson Button on fresh super-softs! On lap 69, a multi-car crash at Swimming Pool injured Vitaly Petrov and brought out a red flag. Crucially, under red flag rules, teams were permitted to change tyres on the grid! Vettel restarted on fresh tyres and sprinted to victory.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The red flag tyre-change rule robbed fans of what promised to be the most dramatic final laps in Monaco history.
