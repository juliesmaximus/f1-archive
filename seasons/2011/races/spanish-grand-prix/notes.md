## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
At the start, Fernando Alonso launched into the lead from 4th on the grid with a sensational dive into Turn 1 to the ecstasy of Spanish fans. However, Ferrari's hard tyres proved disastrous, and Alonso was eventually lapped. Sebastian Vettel fought off ferocious, relentless attacks from Lewis Hamilton over the final 15 laps, crossing the finish line just 0.6 seconds ahead to claim his fourth win of the season.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Massive pace difference between Pirelli soft and hard compounds left all cars from 5th downwards lapped.
