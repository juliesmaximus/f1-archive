## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Red Bull overcame terrifying tyre blistering on their heavily cambered front tyres to score a 1-2 finish. Mark Webber pulled off one of the greatest overtakes in Formula 1 history, diving down the inside of Fernando Alonso into the 190 mph compression of Eau Rouge wheel-to-wheel! Michael Schumacher celebrated the 20th anniversary of his F1 debut with a sensational drive from 24th (last) on the grid to 5th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Webber's Eau Rouge pass on Alonso became an instant motorsport legend.
