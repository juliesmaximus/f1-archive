## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel shattered Nigel Mansell's 19-year-old record by scoring his 15th pole position of the season. In the race, Vettel developed a short-shifting gearbox problem mid-race, allowing teammate Mark Webber to overtake him on lap 30 and take his first victory of 2011. Red Bull finished 1-2, while Jenson Button passed Fernando Alonso late on to secure 3rd place and runner-up spot in the World Drivers' Championship.
