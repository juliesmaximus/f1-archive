## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel matched Nigel Mansell's 1992 record of 14 pole positions in a single season. However, at Turn 2 on the opening lap, Vettel suffered a sudden right-rear tyre puncture and suspension collapse—his first mechanical retirement in over a year. Lewis Hamilton dominated the race to take a flawless victory, holding off Fernando Alonso by 8.4 seconds, with Jenson Button completing the podium.
