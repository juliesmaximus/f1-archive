## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel held off Lewis Hamilton off the line and controlled the race to make it two wins from two. Jenson Button executed superior tyre management to finish 2nd, while Nick Heidfeld delivered a stunning start from 6th to take 3rd for Renault. Hamilton and Alonso engaged in a ferocious duel, Alonso damaging his front wing against Hamilton's rear tyre; both received 20-second post-race penalties.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton penalized for weaving defensively; Alonso penalized for causing a collision.
