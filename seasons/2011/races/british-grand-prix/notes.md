## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ferrari's sole victory of 2011 on the 60th anniversary of Ferrari's first-ever World Championship Grand Prix win at Silverstone (González in 1951). In damp conditions, a botched pitstop by Red Bull on lap 27 (stuck wheel nut) cost Vettel the lead. Fernando Alonso took full advantage, driving away to win by 16.5 seconds. On the final lap, Mark Webber was ordered by Red Bull over team radio to 'maintain the gap' behind Vettel, but ignored orders to attack Vettel furiously into Vale.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Held under a temporary, controversial clampdown on off-throttle blown diffusers, which was repealed immediately after the weekend.
