## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel controlled the night race from pole position to win by 1.7 seconds over Jenson Button, putting him on the brink of his second World Championship. Lewis Hamilton and Felipe Massa collided yet again, Hamilton clipping Massa's right-rear tyre and receiving a drive-through penalty. After the race, a furious Massa confronted Hamilton in the TV media pen, patting him sarcastically on the shoulder and saying: 'Good job, bro!'
