## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic inaugural Indian Grand Prix at the spectacular Buddh International Circuit designed by Hermann Tilke. Sebastian Vettel achieved a flawless Grand Slam: pole position, victory, fastest lap on the final lap, and led every single lap of the race. Jenson Button finished 2nd and Fernando Alonso 3rd. Lewis Hamilton and Felipe Massa collided yet again at Turn 5 on lap 24, with Massa receiving a drive-through penalty before retiring with suspension failure.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
