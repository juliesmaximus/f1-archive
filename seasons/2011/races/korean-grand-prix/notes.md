## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton ended Red Bull's streak of 16 consecutive pole positions on Saturday. On Sunday, Sebastian Vettel overtook Hamilton at Turn 4 on the opening lap and dominated the race to win. Red Bull Racing officially clinched their second consecutive FIA Formula 1 World Constructors' Championship with three races to spare.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton finished 2nd after defending relentlessly against Mark Webber for 25 laps.
