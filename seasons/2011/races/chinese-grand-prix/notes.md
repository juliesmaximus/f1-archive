## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A modern tactical thriller. Sebastian Vettel attempted a two-stop strategy, but in the closing laps his Pirelli tyres degraded rapidly. Lewis Hamilton, on a fresh three-stop strategy, carved through the field and hunted Vettel down, pulling off an exquisite late-braking overtake into Turn 7 with 5 laps remaining to win by 5.1 seconds. Mark Webber drove one of the greatest recovery drives in F1 history, slicing from 18th on the grid to finish 3rd on the podium!

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Webber's stunning 15-position charge on soft tyres showcased the overtaking power of Pirelli rubber and DRS.
