## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso ignited Monza by taking the lead into Turn 1 from 4th on the grid. On lap 5, Sebastian Vettel pulled off an astonishing, fearless overtake around the outside of Alonso through the 170 mph Curva Grande with two wheels on the grass! Vettel pulled away to win by 9.5 seconds. Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton fought an epic 30-lap duel for 3rd place.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Vitantonio Liuzzi lost control on the grass at the start, careering backward into Turn 1 and wiping out Petrov and Rosberg.
