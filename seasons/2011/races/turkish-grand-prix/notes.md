## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The race with the most overtakes in modern Formula 1 history: 79 overtakes during the Grand Prix! Sebastian Vettel led a Red Bull 1-2 ahead of Mark Webber, with Fernando Alonso scoring Ferrari's first podium of 2011 in 3rd. Most drivers executed aggressive four-stop pit strategies due to Turn 8 tyre destruction.
