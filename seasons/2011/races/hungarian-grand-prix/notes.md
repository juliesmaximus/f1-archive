## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Jenson Button celebrated his 200th Grand Prix start with a sublime wet-weather victory at the circuit where he scored his maiden win in 2006. Hamilton led until spinning at the chicane on lap 47; when drizzle returned, Hamilton gambled on intermediate tyres while Button stayed out on dry slicks. Hamilton was forced to pit again and served a drive-through penalty for an unsafe spin rejoin, while Button swept to victory.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton's spin rejoin forced Paul di Resta onto the grass, earning Hamilton a penalty.
