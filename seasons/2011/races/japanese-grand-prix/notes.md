## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel became the youngest double World Champion in Formula 1 history! Needing just a single point to clinch the title, Vettel finished 3rd behind a masterly Jenson Button (who took victory for McLaren) and Fernando Alonso. At 24 years, 98 days, Vettel broke Fernando Alonso's record to become back-to-back World Drivers' Champion.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
