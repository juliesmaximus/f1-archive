## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Debut of Pirelli tyres and the Drag Reduction System (DRS). Reigning champion Sebastian Vettel dominated from pole position without using KERS, winning by 22.2 seconds over Lewis Hamilton. Vitaly Petrov drove an inspired race to score Renault's first podium of the season in 3rd. Sergio Pérez finished 7th on debut for Sauber executing an audacious 1-stop strategy, but both Saubers were disqualified post-race for an illegal 3 mm rear wing radius.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Sauber rear wing disqualification promoted Adrian Sutil to 9th and Paul di Resta to 10th on his F1 debut.
