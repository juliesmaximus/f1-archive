## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of Lewis Hamilton's most masterly, aggressive performances. In cold 14°C weather, Hamilton, Webber, and Alonso engaged in a spellbinding three-way tactical battle with continuous lead changes. Hamilton executed an audacious outside overtake on Alonso into Turn 2 on lap 33 and held on to win. Vettel finished 4th after jumping Massa in the pits on the final lap.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First time in 2011 that Sebastian Vettel failed to qualify or finish on the podium.
