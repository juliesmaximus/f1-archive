## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Sebastian Vettel Youngest Double World Champion:** At 24 years, 98 days, Sebastian Vettel won his second World Championship with 11 victories and an all-time record 15 pole positions.
- **Introduction of DRS & Pirelli Tyres:** Drag Reduction System and high-degradation Pirelli rubber transformed overtaking in Formula 1.
- **Jenson Button's 4-Hour Canadian GP Miracle:** The longest race in F1 history won by Button from 21st (last) on the final lap.
- **Inaugural Indian Grand Prix:** Debut of the Buddh International Circuit in Greater Noida.
