## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **DRS Introduced:** Drag Reduction System rear-wing flap permitted within 1 second in DRS zones.
- **Pirelli Tyre Era:** Pirelli appointed sole tyre supplier, introducing high-degradation compound characteristics.
- **Double Diffuser & F-Duct Ban:** 2009/2010 aerodynamic loopholes permanently closed.
- **107% Qualifying Rule Return:** Cars failing to achieve 107% of the fastest Q1 time denied race start.
