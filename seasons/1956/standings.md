# 1956 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1956 World Championship of Drivers. Only each driver's best 5 results out of the 8 championship rounds counted towards the final championship tally.

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Points system**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers. 1 bonus point awarded for the fastest lap (split equally if shared).
- **Best 5 rule**: Only each driver's best 5 results counted.
- **Shared drives**: Points scored by shared cars were divided equally between drivers. Under official FIA/Formula 1 rules, if a driver drove multiple shared cars scoring points in the same race, they only received points for their highest-placed finish.
