## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Peter Collins' Legendary Sportsmanship: Fangio suffered a broken steering arm on lap 30 and pitted, seemingly out of the championship. Teammate Luigi Musso refused Ferrari's orders to surrender his car. On lap 35, Peter Collins pitted for fresh tyres. Although Collins had a mathematical chance to win the World Drivers' Championship himself if he won with fastest lap and Fangio did not score, Collins unhesitatingly offered his car to Fangio, saying Fangio deserved the title. Fangio drove the D50 to 2nd place, securing his 4th World Drivers' Championship. Collins and Fangio split the 6 points for 2nd place (3 points each), dropping Collins to 3rd in the championship.
- Moss Monza Triumph: Stirling Moss won the race in his Maserati 250F, nearly running out of fuel on the banking on the final laps until Maserati teammate Luigi Piotti pushed his car into the pits.
- Tyre Havoc on Banking: The extreme centrifugal forces on the 10.000 km banked oval shredded tyres across the grid, with multiple tyre blowouts for Castellotti, Musso, and de Portago.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Juan Manuel Fangio crowned 1956 Formula One World Champion of Drivers (4th title, 3rd consecutive).
- Peter Collins revered forever for the ultimate act of selflessness in motorsport history.
- First World Championship title won with Lancia-Ferrari.
