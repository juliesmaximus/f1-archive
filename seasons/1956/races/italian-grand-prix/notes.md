## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Peter Collins' Ultimate Sportsmanship: Fangio started on pole but retired on lap 19 with a broken steering arm. Luigi Musso refused to give up his car to Fangio. On lap 35, Peter Collins pitted for tyres while running in 2nd; seeing Fangio without a car, Collins unselfishly stepped out and gave his car to Fangio, sacrificing his own chance at the World Championship. Collins stated: 'I am only 25; Mr. Fangio deserves the title.'
- Fangio 4th World Championship: Fangio drove Collins' car to 2nd place, securing his 4th World Championship.
- Moss Wins on Fumes: Stirling Moss ran out of fuel on the banked oval with 5 laps remaining; teammate Luigi Piotti used his Maserati to bump Moss's car all the way back to the pit lane, where mechanics splashed fuel in time for Moss to re-join and win!
- Connaught Podium: Ron Flockhart scored a sensational 3rd place for British privateer Connaught.
