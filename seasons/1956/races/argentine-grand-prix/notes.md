# 1956 Argentine Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: IV Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Round**: 1 of 8 in the 1956 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 22 January 1956
- **Location**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre, Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Circuit Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 miles)
- **Distance**: 98 laps (383.376 km / 238.219 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 1:42.500
- **Fastest Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 1:45.300 (Lap 52) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Luigi Musso & Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 3:00:03.700 (127.748 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Luigi Musso & Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari) - 98 laps
  2. Jean Behra (Maserati) - 98 laps (+24.400s)
  3. Mike Hawthorn (Maserati) - 96 laps (+2 laps)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Shared Drive Victory**: Juan Manuel Fangio suffered a broken fuel pump on lap 22 in car #30. Team manager Sculptore ordered Luigi Musso (car #34) to pit on lap 30 and hand his car to Fangio. Fangio rejoined and mounted a relentless charge through the field, overtaking Stirling Moss and Jean Behra to take victory. The 8 winning points were split equally (4 points each), with Fangio earning an additional 1 point for the fastest lap (total 5 pts).
- **Maserati Threat**: Stirling Moss led until his engine blew on lap 81. Jean Behra inherited 2nd place and Mike Hawthorn took 3rd, giving Maserati two podium finishers behind the shared Ferrari.
- **Scoring Debut**: Brazilian driver Chico Landi and Italian driver Gerino Gerini shared 4th place in car #10 (Maserati), earning 1.5 championship points each—becoming the first Brazilian driver to score World Championship points.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, dry summer conditions throughout the race afternoon.
