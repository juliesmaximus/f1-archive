# 1956 British Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: IX British Grand Prix
- **Round**: 6 of 8 in the 1956 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Saturday, 14 July 1956
- **Location**: Silverstone Circuit, Silverstone, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom
- **Circuit Length**: 4.711 km (2.927 miles)
- **Distance**: 101 laps (475.811 km / 295.655 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Maserati 250F) - 1:41.000
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Maserati 250F) - 1:43.200 (Lap 71) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 2:59:47.000 (158.795 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari) - 101 laps
  2. Peter Collins & Alfonso de Portago (Ferrari) - 100 laps (+1 lap)
  3. Jean Behra (Maserati) - 99 laps (+2 laps)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Fangio's Dominant Comeback**: After Moss led early from pole and British drivers Hawthorn and Brooks impressed, mechanical troubles hit his rivals. Stirling Moss suffered a broken axle on lap 94. Fangio, driving with methodical precision, took the lead and secured his second victory of the season, finishing a full lap ahead of the rest of the field.
- **Collins & Portago Shared 2nd**: Peter Collins took over Alfonso de Portago's Ferrari #22 on lap 70 after his own car had retired, driving it to 2nd place. Collins and Portago shared 6 points (3 points each).
- **Connaught Double Points**: British privateer team Connaught scored significant points with Jack Fairman and Ron Flockhart finishing 4th and 5th (with Fairman also driving part of Flockhart's car; Fairman and Flockhart were officially awarded 2.5 and 2.5, or 2 and 4 across classifications).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Mild, breezy British summer afternoon with dry asphalt throughout.
