## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Fangio Silverstone Masterclass: Juan Manuel Fangio drove a patient, measured race to take victory in his Ferrari D50 by a full lap over the field.
- British Front-Running Heartbreak: British fans were thrilled as Stirling Moss (Maserati) and Mike Hawthorn (BRM P25) led early on. However, Hawthorn's transmission failed on lap 24, and Moss's engine blew on lap 94 while chasing Fangio.
- De Portago Shared Podium with Collins: Spanish nobleman Alfonso de Portago started the race in his Ferrari D50, then handed over to championship contender Peter Collins on lap 70. Collins brought the car home in 2nd place, earning 3 points each for de Portago and Collins.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Maiden World Championship podium for Alfonso de Portago (shared with Collins).
- First British Grand Prix victory for Juan Manuel Fangio.
