# 1956 French Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: 42e Grand Prix de l'ACF
- **Round**: 5 of 8 in the 1956 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 1 July 1956
- **Location**: Reims-Gueux, Reims, France
- **Circuit Length**: 8.302 km (5.159 miles)
- **Distance**: 61 laps (506.422 km / 314.676 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 2:23.300
- **Fastest Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 2:25.800 (Lap 61) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Peter Collins (Ferrari D50) - 2:34:23.400 (196.814 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Peter Collins (Ferrari) - 61 laps
  2. Eugenio Castellotti (Ferrari) - 61 laps (+0.300s)
  3. Jean Behra (Maserati) - 61 laps (+5.800s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Photo-Finish Thriller (0.3s Margin)**: Peter Collins and Eugenio Castellotti engaged in an intense slipstreaming duel across the high-speed Reims straights. Collins crossed the line just 0.3 seconds ahead of Castellotti to take his second consecutive Grand Prix victory.
- **Bugatti & Gordini Appearances**: The race saw the one-off appearance of the mid-engine Bugatti Type 251 driven by Maurice Trintignant (retired on lap 18 with throttle linkage failure).
- **Fangio's Fuel Line Delay**: Pole-sitter Juan Manuel Fangio was running among the leaders when an ignition/fuel line issue forced a pit stop. He recovered to finish 4th, setting the fastest lap of the race on the final lap (2:25.800) to collect 4 points (3 for 4th + 1 for FL).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Very hot, sunny midsummer afternoon; track temperatures elevated on the asphalt straights.
