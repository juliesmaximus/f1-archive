# 1956 Indianapolis 500 - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XLth Indianapolis International Motor Sweepstakes
- **Round**: 3 of 8 in the 1956 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Wednesday, 30 May 1956
- **Location**: Indianapolis Motor Speedway, Speedway, Indiana, USA
- **Circuit Length**: 4.023 km (2.500 miles)
- **Distance**: 200 laps (804.672 km / 500.000 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Pat Flaherty (Watson-Offenhauser) - 4:07.260 (4-lap qualification aggregate; 145.596 mph)
- **Fastest Lap**: Paul Russo (Kurtis Kraft-Novi) - 1:02.320 (Lap 19; 144.416 mph) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Pat Flaherty (Watson-Offenhauser) - 3:53:28.840 (206.785 km/h / 128.490 mph)
- **Podium**:
  1. Pat Flaherty (Watson-Offenhauser) - 200 laps
  2. Sam Hanks (Kurtis Kraft-Offenhauser) - 200 laps (+20.450s)
  3. Don Freeland (Phillips-Offenhauser) - 200 laps (+1:30.230)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **First Watson Roadset Victory**: Pat Flaherty dominated from pole position, leading 127 laps and claiming AJ Watson's first victory as an Indianapolis 500 constructor.
- **Asphalt Repaving**: For the first time in IMS history, almost the entire track was paved with asphalt, with only a 600-foot strip of original bricks left exposed on the main straightaway ("The Yard of Bricks"). Speeds broke all previous IMS records.
- **Novi Heartbreak**: Paul Russo took the early lead with his supercharged Novi V8, setting the fastest lap of the race (1:02.320) and leading 11 laps before crashing heavily in Turn 1 on lap 22 due to a blown tire.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, overcast morning clearing to sunny conditions; dry track throughout.
