## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- USAC Era Begins: Following the AAA's complete withdrawal from motorsport at the end of 1955, the new United States Auto Club (USAC) sanctioned the Indianapolis 500.
- Pat Flaherty Dominance: Irish-American Pat Flaherty in the John Zink Special Kurtis Kraft-Offenhauser captured pole position and led 127 laps to win by 20.45 seconds over Sam Hanks.
- Paul Russo Novi Speed: Paul Russo set the fastest race lap (1:02.32 / 144.416 mph) in the screaming supercharged Novi V8 before crashing into the outer wall on lap 22 due to a blown tyre.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Maiden and only Formula One World Championship race victory for Pat Flaherty.
- Only fastest lap in World Championship history for the Novi supercharged engine (Paul Russo).
