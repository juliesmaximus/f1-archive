## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Maiden Grand Prix Victory for Peter Collins: 24-year-old British driver Peter Collins won his first Formula One World Championship Grand Prix for Scuderia Ferrari.
- Moss Rear Wheel Detachment: Running 2nd on lap 10, Moss lost a rear wheel at Blanchimont, saved the car from crashing, ran back to the pits, and took over teammate Cesare Perdisa's car, charging back to 3rd place.
- Fangio Transmission Failure: Fangio dominated from pole until a transmission failure on lap 24 forced his retirement.
- Belgian hero and journalist Paul Frère finished 2nd for Ferrari in a guest drive.
