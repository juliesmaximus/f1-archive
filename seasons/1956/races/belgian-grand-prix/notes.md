# 1956 Belgian Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XVIII Grand Prix de Belgique
- **Round**: 4 of 8 in the 1956 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 3 June 1956
- **Location**: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps, Stavelot, Belgium
- **Circuit Length**: 14.120 km (8.774 miles)
- **Distance**: 36 laps (508.320 km / 315.855 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Ferrari D50) - 4:09.800
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Maserati 250F) - 4:14.700 (Lap 30) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Peter Collins (Ferrari D50) - 2:40:00.300 (190.616 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Peter Collins (Ferrari) - 36 laps
  2. Paul Frère (Ferrari) - 36 laps (+1:51.300)
  3. Cesare Perdisa & Stirling Moss (Maserati) - 36 laps (+3:16.600)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **First Career Win for Peter Collins**: 24-year-old British driver Peter Collins drove a commanding, error-free race to claim his maiden Formula 1 World Championship victory.
- **Fangio's DNF**: Fangio started from pole and led convincingly until lap 24 when his Ferrari's transmission failed, forcing him into retirement.
- **Moss's Wheel Failure & Shared Recovery**: Stirling Moss lost a rear wheel at the crest of the fast Raidillon on lap 10, miraculously keeping the spinning car under control. He walked back to the pits, took over teammate Cesare Perdisa's Maserati #34 on lap 13, set the fastest lap of the race (4:14.700), and fought back to claim 3rd place on the podium (splitting 4 points = 2 points each, plus 1 FL point for Moss = 3 pts).
- **Home Podium for Paul Frère**: Belgian journalist-racing driver Paul Frère drove a steady race in the factory Ferrari D50 to finish 2nd, earning 6 championship points in his final Grand Prix appearance.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Overcast with damp track drying out during the afternoon; cool temperatures.
