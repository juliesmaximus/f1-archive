## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Peter Collins Maiden Grand Prix Victory: 24-year-old English driver Peter Collins scored his FIRST career Formula One World Championship victory in his Ferrari D50, mastering changing damp conditions to win by nearly two minutes.
- Fangio Transmission Failure: Juan Manuel Fangio led comfortably from pole position until lap 24 when his transmission suffered catastrophic failure, forcing his retirement.
- Moss Wheel Shedding: Stirling Moss was running second when his rear-left wheel sheared off at Blanchimont at 120 mph. Moss kept the Maserati off the barriers, walked back to the pits, took over teammate Cesare Perdisa's car, and charged back to finish on the podium (3rd).
