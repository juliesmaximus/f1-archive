## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Aerodynamic Porpoising Mitigations:** Floor edges raised by 15 mm and diffuser throat height raised to eliminate aerodynamic oscillation and bouncing at high speeds.
- **Roll Hoop Structural Safety:** Strengthened roll hoop load tests introduced following Guanyu Zhou's inverted crash at Silverstone in 2022.
- **Sprint Shootout Inauguration:** Introduction of a standalone Saturday morning "Sprint Shootout" qualifying session, completely decoupling the Sprint race from the Sunday Grand Prix starting grid.
- **Budget Cap Enforcement:** Operational cost cap reduced to $135 million base level.
- **Inaugural Las Vegas Grand Prix:** Debut of the high-speed 6.2 km street circuit incorporating the world-famous Las Vegas Strip, held on a Saturday night in cold ambient conditions.
