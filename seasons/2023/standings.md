## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Max Verstappen Most Dominant Season in Formula 1 History:** Max Verstappen clinched his third consecutive World Drivers' Championship during the Saturday Sprint in Qatar. Verstappen obliterated numerous all-time Formula 1 records:
  - Most wins in a single season: 19 victories (86.36% win percentage).
  - Most consecutive Grand Prix wins: 10 straight victories (Miami through Monza), surpassing Sebastian Vettel's record of 9 from 2013.
  - Most championship points in a season: 575 points.
  - Most laps led in a single season: 1,003 laps (the first driver in F1 history to surpass 1,000 laps led in a calendar year).
  - Most podium finishes in a season: 21 podiums in 22 races.
- **Red Bull Racing RB19 All-Time Constructor Dominance:** Red Bull scored 21 victories from 22 Grands Prix (95.45% win rate), breaking McLaren's 1988 all-time season win percentage record (15 wins from 16 races, 93.75%).
- **Sole Non-Red Bull Victory:** Carlos Sainz took victory at the Singapore Grand Prix for Ferrari, breaking Red Bull's 15-race winning streak and denying them an unprecedented clean sweep of the entire championship.
- **Fernando Alonso Renaissance:** 42-year-old two-time World Champion Fernando Alonso scored 8 podium finishes for Aston Martin in his first season with the team.
- **Oscar Piastri Rookie Triumph:** Australian rookie Oscar Piastri claimed 2 podium finishes for McLaren and won the Qatar Sprint race.
