## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Schumacher's 100% Podium Record:** Michael Schumacher finished on the podium in every single race of the season (11 wins, 5 second places, 1 third place across 17 rounds)—an all-time Formula 1 record that has never been equaled.
- **Earliest Championship Clinch in F1 History:** Schumacher clinched his 5th World Championship on July 21 at the French Grand Prix (Round 11 of 17) with six rounds remaining.
- **Equaling Fangio's 5 Titles:** Schumacher matched Juan Manuel Fangio's 45-year-old record of 5 World Drivers' Championships.
- **Ferrari's Record Dominance:** Ferrari scored 221 points, matching the combined points tally of all ten rival constructors combined (221 pts).
- **Austria Team Orders Controversy:** Ferrari ordered Rubens Barrichello to yield the victory on the final lap, prompting intense public backlash, a $1 million fine from the FIA for podium protocol disruption, and a subsequent ban on team orders affecting race results.
- **Montoya's Historic Monza Lap:** Juan Pablo Montoya recorded the fastest lap in Formula 1 history in qualifying at Monza at an average speed of 259.827 km/h (161.449 mph).
- **Arrows Liquidation:** Arrows entered financial collapse mid-season, deliberately failing to qualify at the French GP and collapsing before the Hungarian GP, withdrawing from the championship.
