## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
David Coulthard produced a defensive masterclass for McLaren-Mercedes. Starting 2nd, Coulthard jumped polesitter Montoya into Sainte-Dévote. Coulthard held off relentless, race-long pressure from Michael Schumacher's Ferrari to take victory by 1.062 seconds, with Ralf Schumacher 3rd.
