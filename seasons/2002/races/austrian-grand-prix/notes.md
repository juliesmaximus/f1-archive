## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The most controversial team orders scandal in modern Formula 1. Rubens Barrichello dominated the entire weekend from pole position, leading all 71 laps. On the final lap, Ferrari team principal Jean Todt ordered Barrichello over the radio: 'Let Michael pass for the championship, please.' Exiting the final corner meters before the finish line, Barrichello lifted off the throttle, allowing Schumacher to sweep past to take the win. The podium ceremony descended into deafening boos and whistling; an embarrassed Schumacher forced Barrichello onto the top step and handed him the winner's trophy. The FIA subsequently fined Ferrari $1 million for podium protocol violations and outlawed team orders affecting race results.
