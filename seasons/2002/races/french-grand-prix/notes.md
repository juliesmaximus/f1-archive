## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The historic championship coronation. Kimi Räikkönen led and was on course for his maiden Grand Prix victory until lap 68 of 72, when he slid wide on oil dumped by Allan McNish's blown Toyota at the Adelaide hairpin. Michael Schumacher seized the opportunity, swept into the lead, and took the victory. With this win, Michael Schumacher mathematically clinched the 2002 Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship on July 21 with six rounds remaining—the earliest title clinch in modern Formula 1 history—equaling Juan Manuel Fangio's legendary record of 5 World Championships.
