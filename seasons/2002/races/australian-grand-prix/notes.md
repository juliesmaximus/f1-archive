## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Spectacular opening-lap carnage: polesitter Rubens Barrichello squeezed Ralf Schumacher approaching Turn 1; Ralf launched violently airborne over the rear of Barrichello's Ferrari, triggering an 8-car pileup that eliminated half the front-runners. Michael Schumacher drove to victory in the interim Ferrari F2001. Australian Mark Webber scored a heroic 5th place on his Formula 1 debut for perennial backmarkers Minardi, prompting Paul Stoddart to celebrate on the official podium.
