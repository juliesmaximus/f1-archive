## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher broke the all-time record for the most Grand Prix wins in a single season, taking his 10th victory of the year (surpassing his own and Nigel Mansell's previous record of 9 wins). Schumacher led Barrichello across the line for another dominant Ferrari 1-2, with Montoya 3rd.
