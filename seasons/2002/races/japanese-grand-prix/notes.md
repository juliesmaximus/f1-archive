## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The perfect culmination of an immortal season. Michael Schumacher dominated from pole position to claim his 11th victory of the year, leading Rubens Barrichello home for Ferrari's ninth 1-2 finish of the season. Kimi Räikkönen completed the podium in 3rd. Michael Schumacher completed an unprecedented feat: finishing on the podium in all 17 races of the 2002 season.
