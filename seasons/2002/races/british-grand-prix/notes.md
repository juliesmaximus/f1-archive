## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A wet-weather masterclass from Michael Schumacher and Ross Brawn. Montoya led from pole until heavy rain arrived on lap 13. While rivals pitted for intermediate tyres, Schumacher and Barrichello switched to full wet Bridgestones and lapped three seconds faster than the field. Schumacher won by 14 seconds over Barrichello (Ferrari 1-2), with Montoya 3rd.
