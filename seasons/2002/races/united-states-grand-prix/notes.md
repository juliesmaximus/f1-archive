## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Famous synchronized formation finish gone wrong. Schumacher led Barrichello comfortably throughout the race. On the final lap down the main straight, Schumacher slowed down deliberately to stage a simultaneous side-by-side photo-finish across the yard of bricks. Misjudging the distances slightly, Rubens Barrichello crossed the timing line just 0.011 seconds ahead to take the victory—the closest finish in US Grand Prix history. David Coulthard finished 3rd.
