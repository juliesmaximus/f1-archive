## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The racing debut of the legendary Ferrari F2002. Juan Pablo Montoya took pole, but Schumacher made an aggressive move into Turn 1 to lead. Montoya lost his front wing clipping Schumacher's rear wheel. Schumacher controlled the race to win by 1.5 seconds over brother Ralf Schumacher, with David Coulthard 3rd. Football legend Pelé was invited to wave the chequered flag but famously missed Schumacher crossing the line.
