## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jack Brabham (Brabham-Repco) — 1:48:31.300  
- **Pole Position:** Lorenzo Bandini  (2:07.800)
- **Fastest Lap:** Lorenzo Bandini (Ferrari) — 2:11.300 (Lap 30)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Scorching hot summer sun; 33°C ambient, tarmac melting at Thillois and Gueux  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Historic two-storey concrete grandstands along Route Nationale 31, Pavillon André Lambert, and Thillois hairpin bleachers  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Lorenzo Bandini took pole for Ferrari and led comfortably until his throttle cable snapped on lap 32. Jack Brabham took over the lead in his Brabham-Repco BT19 to win by 9.5s over Mike Parkes in the factory Ferrari 312. Denny Hulme completed the podium in 3rd. John Surtees debuted for Cooper-Maserati following his Ferrari walkout, retiring with fuel pump failure. Jim Clark withdrew after being struck in the eye by a bird during practice.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Monumental historical milestone: Jack Brabham became the first driver in Formula One history to win a World Championship Grand Prix driving a car bearing his own name. Final French Grand Prix held at the classic high-speed Reims-Gueux circuit.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
