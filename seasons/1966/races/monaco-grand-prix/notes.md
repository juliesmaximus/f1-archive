- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Quai Albert 1er grandstands, Casino terraces, and yachts in the harbour  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
A race of extreme attrition under the new 3.0L rules. Only 16 cars started, and only 4 cars were classified as official finishers! Jackie Stewart led the majority of the race to win for BRM in the 2.0L Tasman-engined BRM P261. Lorenzo Bandini finished 2nd for Ferrari. Graham Hill took 3rd, and Bob Bondurant 4th. Guy Ligier and Jo Bonnier were still running but had completed fewer than 90% of the winner's distance (85 laps required), so were not classified. Jim Clark took pole but retired with suspension failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Bruce McLaren gave the McLaren team its Formula One World Championship debut driving the McLaren M2B powered by a downsized Ford Indy V8, retiring after 9 laps with an oil leak.
- **Regulatory Framework:** First championship race under 3.0L naturally aspirated / 1.5L supercharged rules. Minimum weight: 500 kg. Scoring: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Best 5 results counted towards championships.
