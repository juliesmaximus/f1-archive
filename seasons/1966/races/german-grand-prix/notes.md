## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jack Brabham (Brabham-Repco) — 2:27  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Dense fog, torrential cold rain, standing water, and treacherous visibility across the 14-mile circuit  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 220,000 spectators enduring the relentless rain in the Eifel forests  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Start-und-Ziel grandstand and hillside camping terraces throughout Hatzenbach and Brünnchen  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jack Brabham turned up on the grid wearing a long false grey beard and walking with a cane, mocking British press critics who had called him 'too old' to win championships at age 40. Brabham then delivered an astonishing wet-weather masterclass, battling John Surtees' Cooper-Maserati in torrential rain to take his 4th consecutive Grand Prix victory by 44.4 seconds! Jochen Rindt took 3rd for Cooper. Tragic accident in practice: British driver John Taylor died from severe burns following a collision with Jacky Ickx's Matra F2 car.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Brabham's famous beard prank remains one of the most legendary psychological gestures in Grand Prix history. Jack Brabham firmly gripped the World Championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Formula Two (F2) cars were invited to bolster the grid, sharing the track but ineligible for World Championship points.
