## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Ludovico Scarfiotti (Ferrari) — 1:47:14.800  
- **Pole Position:** Mike Parkes  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry autumn conditions; 26°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 110,000 ecstatic tifosi packing the Autodromo  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Tribuna Centrale, Vedano, Ascari, and Curva Parabolica grandstands  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Ferrari introduced their new 36-valve 3.0L V12 engine and swept the front row. Ludovico Scarfiotti and teammate Mike Parkes finished 1-2 for Ferrari, with Scarfiotti taking his sole Formula One victory (he remains to this day the last Italian driver to win the Italian Grand Prix driving an Italian Ferrari). Denny Hulme finished 3rd. Jack Brabham retired on lap 7 with an oil leak, but his sole remaining championship challenger, John Surtees, retired with a broken fuel tank on lap 31. With Surtees out, Jack Brabham MATHEMATICALLY CLINCHED his 3rd World Drivers' Championship and Brabham's maiden Constructors' Championship!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jack Brabham made history as the FIRST AND ONLY driver in motorsport history to win the Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving a car bearing his own name (Brabham BT19 Repco). Brabham also won the Constructors' Championship.
