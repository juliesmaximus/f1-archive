### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
John Surtees took pole position in his Cooper T81-Maserati. In the race, Surtees dominated to win by 7.88s over World Champion Jack Brabham. Denny Hulme finished 3rd in the second Brabham. Surtees' victory secured 2nd place in the World Drivers' Championship and 2nd for Cooper-Maserati in the Constructors' Cup. Richie Ginther scored 4th in the Honda RA273 3.0L V12.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The final Grand Prix of the historic 1966 season. John Surtees' victory was the first win for a Maserati engine since Juan Manuel Fangio at the Nürburgring in 1957. Jack Brabham and Motor Racing Developments officially celebrated their twin World Championships.
