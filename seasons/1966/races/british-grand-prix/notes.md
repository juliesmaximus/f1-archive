## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jack Brabham (Brabham-Repco) — 2:13:13.400  
- **Pole Position:** Jack Brabham  (1:34.500)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jack Brabham (Brabham-Repco) — 1:37.000 (Lap 60)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Intermittent drizzle and greasy track conditions, cool 18°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main Brabham straight grandstand, Druids hairpin grandstands, and South Bank natural terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jack Brabham dominated from pole position, leading all 80 laps to score his second consecutive victory. Teammate Denny Hulme finished 2nd, delivering a dominant 1-2 sweep for Motor Racing Developments / Brabham. Graham Hill completed the podium in 3rd for BRM. Ferrari boycotted the race due to metalworkers' strikes in Italy.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Brabham took the lead of the World Drivers' Championship, proving the agility and bulletproof reliability of the simple SOHC Repco V8 over complex multi-cylinder rivals.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
