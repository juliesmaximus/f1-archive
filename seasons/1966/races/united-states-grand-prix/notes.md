## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-BRM) — 2:09:40.110  
- **Pole Position:** Jack Brabham  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, crisp, sunny autumn day; 14°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 75,000 spectators covering the Watkins Glen knolls  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Bog, and Fast Turn banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark pulled off an engineering miracle by winning in the Lotus 43 powered by the monstrous, complex, and notoriously unreliable 32-valve 16-cylinder BRM H16 engine (essentially two flat-8 engines sandwiched together). It was the ONLY victory for an H16 engine in Formula One World Championship history! Jochen Rindt finished 2nd for Cooper-Maserati, and John Surtees took 3rd. Newly crowned World Champion Jack Brabham retired with engine failure on lap 55.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Watkins Glen cemented its place as the richest and most popular overseas Grand Prix on the Formula One calendar.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Maximum prize fund in international motorsport ($102,000 total purse).
