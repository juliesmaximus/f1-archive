## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jack Brabham (Brabham-Repco) — 2:20:32.500  
- **Pole Position:** Jack Brabham  (1:28.100)
- **Fastest Lap:** Denny Hulme (Brabham-Repco) — 1:30.600 (Lap 2)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm and sunny with sea breezes; 23°C ambient, dry sandy track  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main pit straight grandstand and Hunserug dune spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jack Brabham took pole position. Jim Clark took the lead in the Lotus 33-Climax 2-litre and led for 50 laps before overheating forced a water stop. Jack Brabham swept past to win his third consecutive Grand Prix. Jochen Rindt took 2nd for Cooper-Maserati, with Graham Hill 3rd for BRM.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jack Brabham extended his championship lead to 32 points. Dan Gurney finished 5th, scoring the first championship points for the Anglo American Racers Eagle chassis (Eagle T1G Climax).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
