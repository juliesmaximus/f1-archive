# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1966 - Calendar

The 1966 Formula One season was the 17th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 9 championship rounds between May 22 and October 23, 1966.

The 1966 season marked the beginning of Formula One's dramatic "Return to Power" era, as maximum engine displacement was doubled from 1.5 litres unsupercharged to 3.0 litres (or 1.5 litres supercharged/turbocharged). While established teams struggled to adapt or find suitable engines, Australian Jack Brabham triumphed with his ingenious, lightweight Repco-powered Brabham BT19. Brabham won four consecutive Grands Prix and clinched his third World Drivers' Championship, becoming the first and only person in motorsport history to win the Formula One World Championship driving a car bearing his own name. Brabham also captured their maiden International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
