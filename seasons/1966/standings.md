# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1966 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the 9 championship rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Jack Brabham won his third World Drivers' Championship with 42 net points (45 gross, dropped 3 points from 4th place at Mexico), making motorsport history as the first and only person to win the Formula One World Championship driving a car bearing his own name.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results counted towards the championship.

Brabham-Repco won their maiden International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 42 net points (49 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Engine Regulations (The Return to Power):** Maximum engine displacement doubled from 1.5 litres to 3.0 litres naturally aspirated, or 1.5 litres supercharged/turbocharged. Minimum car weight increased to 500 kg.
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores:** Best 5 of 9 rounds counted towards both championships.
  - Jack Brabham scored 45 gross points, dropping 3 points (Mexico 4th) to finish with 42 net points.
  - Jochen Rindt scored 24 gross points, dropping 2 points (Great Britain 5th) to finish with 22 net points.
  - Brabham-Repco scored 49 gross points, dropping 7 points to finish with 42 net points.
  - Ferrari scored 32 gross points, dropping 1 point to finish with 31 net points.
  - Cooper-Maserati scored 35 gross points, dropping 5 points to finish with 30 net points.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jack Brabham became the first and only driver in Formula 1 history to win the World Championship driving a car of his own manufacture (Brabham BT19 Repco).
  - Brabham scored four consecutive victories in France, Great Britain, the Netherlands, and Germany.
  - Jim Clark and Lotus won the United States Grand Prix using the 32-valve 16-cylinder BRM H16 engine—the only win for an H16 engine in F1 history.
  - Ludovico Scarfiotti won the Italian Grand Prix for Ferrari, remaining the last Italian to win at Monza in a Ferrari.
  - Jackie Stewart's horrific crash at Spa sparked his historic campaign for circuit and medical safety in Formula One.
