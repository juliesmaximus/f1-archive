# Spanish Grand Prix - 2018 Round 5

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Gran Premio de Espana Emirates 2018
- **Circuit:** Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya (Barcelona, Spain)
- **Date:** 2018-05-13
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.655 km (2.892 mi), 66 laps, 307.104 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 120 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, overcast, ambient 19°C, track 35°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 90,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Lewis Hamilton takes commanding victory from pole. Massive lap 1 spin by Grosjean creates smoke screen and wipes out Gasly and Hulkenberg.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
