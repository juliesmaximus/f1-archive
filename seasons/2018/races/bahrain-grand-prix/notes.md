# Bahrain Grand Prix - 2018 Round 2

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 2018 Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Bahrain International Circuit (Sakhir, Bahrain)
- **Date:** 2018-04-08
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.412 km (3.363 mi), 57 laps, 308.238 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 7 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, clear night, ambient 28°C, track 31°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 33,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Sebastian Vettel holds off a charging Valtteri Bottas on worn soft tyres in a nail-biting finish by 0.699 seconds. Ferrari pit stop mechanic Francesco Cigarini suffers a broken leg when Kimi Raikkonen is given a green light before tyre change is complete.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
