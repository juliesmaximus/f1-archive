# Australian Grand Prix - 2018 Round 1

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** 2018 Formula 1 Rolex Australian Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Albert Park Grand Prix Circuit (Melbourne, Australia)
- **Date:** 2018-03-25
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.303 km (3.295 mi), 58 laps, 307.574 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 10 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 24°C, track 38°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 100,500 race day (295,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Sebastian Vettel takes victory for Ferrari via Virtual Safety Car timing after Haas suffer heartbreaking identical wheel-nut crossthread retirements for both Magnussen and Grosjean. Hamilton finishes second after leading from pole.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
