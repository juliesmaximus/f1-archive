# Italian Grand Prix - 2018 Round 14

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Gran Premio Heineken d'Italia 2018
- **Circuit:** Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (Monza, Italy)
- **Date:** 2018-09-02
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.793 km (3.600 mi), 53 laps, 306.720 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 183 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, overcast, ambient 22°C, track 30°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 95,000 race day (185,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Ferrari lockout front row, but Hamilton passes Vettel at the Roggia chicane on lap 1 (spinning Vettel) and later stalks and overtakes Raikkonen on heavily blistered tyres to break Italian hearts in Monza.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
