# Austrian Grand Prix - 2018 Round 9

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Eyetime Grosser Preis von Osterreich 2018
- **Circuit:** Red Bull Ring (Spielberg, Austria)
- **Date:** 2018-07-01
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.318 km (2.683 mi), 71 laps, 306.452 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 660 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 24°C, track 42°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 70,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Double retirement disaster for Mercedes (Bottas hydraulics, Hamilton fuel pressure). Max Verstappen wins Red Bull's home race ahead of Ferrari duo Raikkonen and Vettel.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
