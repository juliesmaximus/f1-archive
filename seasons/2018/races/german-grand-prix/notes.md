# German Grand Prix - 2018 Round 11

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Emirates Grosser Preis von Deutschland 2018
- **Circuit:** Hockenheimring (Hockenheim, Germany)
- **Date:** 2018-07-22
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.574 km (2.842 mi), 67 laps, 306.458 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 103 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Mixed: hot dry start, sudden rain shower on lap 44, ambient 27°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 71,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Turning point of the 2018 championship: Sebastian Vettel leads from pole until sliding into the Sachskurve gravel trap in sudden rain on lap 52. Lewis Hamilton charges from 14th on the grid to take an astonishing victory.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
