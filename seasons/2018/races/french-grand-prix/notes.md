# French Grand Prix - 2018 Round 8

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Pirelli Grand Prix de France 2018
- **Circuit:** Circuit Paul Ricard (Le Castellet, France)
- **Date:** 2018-06-24
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.842 km (3.630 mi), 53 laps, 309.690 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 410 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 24°C, track 42°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 65,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
French GP returns after 10-year hiatus at Circuit Paul Ricard. Lap 1 collision between Vettel and Bottas into Turn 1 earns Vettel a 5-second penalty as Hamilton cruises to victory from pole.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
