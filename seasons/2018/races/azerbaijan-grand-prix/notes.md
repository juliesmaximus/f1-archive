# Azerbaijan Grand Prix - 2018 Round 4

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 2018 Azerbaijan Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Baku City Circuit (Baku, Azerbaijan)
- **Date:** 2018-04-29
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 6.003 km (3.730 mi), 51 laps, 306.049 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** -20 m (below sea level)
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, gusty winds, ambient 17°C, track 24°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 32,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Total chaos in Baku: Red Bull teammates Ricciardo and Verstappen collide at 200 mph into Turn 1. Bottas leads towards victory until suffering a devastating tyre puncture from debris with 3 laps to go, handing victory to Hamilton.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
