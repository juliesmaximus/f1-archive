# Monaco Grand Prix - 2018 Round 6

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Grand Prix de Monaco 2018
- **Circuit:** Circuit de Monaco (Monte Carlo, Monaco)
- **Date:** 2018-05-27
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 3.337 km (2.074 mi), 78 laps, 260.286 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 15 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, warm, ambient 24°C, track 33°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 40,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Daniel Ricciardo redeems his 2016 heartbreak by winning despite losing MGU-K failure on lap 18 (down 25% horsepower, 6 of 8 gears), resisting Vettel's Ferrari for 60 laps around the tight streets.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
