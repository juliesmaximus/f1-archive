## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Gran Premio de Mexico 2018
- **Circuit:** Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez (Mexico City, Mexico)

- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.304 km (2.674 mi), 71 laps, 305.354 km

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing TAG Heuer)
- **Pole Position:** Daniel Ricciardo (Red Bull Racing TAG Heuer) - 1:14.759
- **Fastest Lap:** Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes) - 1:18.741 (lap 65)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Max Verstappen dominates to win at altitude. Lewis Hamilton finishes 4th, officially clinching his 5th FIA Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship, equalling Juan Manuel Fangio.

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
