# Canadian Grand Prix - 2018 Round 7

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Grand Prix Heineken du Canada 2018
- **Circuit:** Circuit Gilles Villeneuve (Montreal, Canada)
- **Date:** 2018-06-10
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.361 km (2.710 mi), 68 laps (officially shortened from 70 due to early chequered flag wave by model Winnie Harlow)
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 13 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 23°C, track 41°C

---

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Sebastian Vettel (Ferrari)
- **Pole Position:** Sebastian Vettel (Ferrari) - 1:10.764
- **Fastest Lap:** Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing TAG Heuer) - 1:13.864 (lap 65)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Sebastian Vettel dominates from pole to take Ferrari's first win in Montreal since Schumacher in 2004. Early chequered flag waved on lap 69 causes official results to be declared on lap 68 per regulations.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
