## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 2018 Rolex British Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Silverstone Circuit (Silverstone, UK)
- **Date:** 2018-07-08
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.891 km (3.660 mi), 52 laps, 306.198 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 153 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, blistering heatwave, ambient 30°C, track 52°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 140,500 race day (340,000 weekend)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Sebastian Vettel wins a classic Silverstone duel after Hamilton is spun at Turn 3 on lap 1 by Raikkonen and charges from last back to second.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
