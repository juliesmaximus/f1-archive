# Hungarian Grand Prix - 2018 Round 12

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Rolex Magyar Nagydij 2018
- **Circuit:** Hungaroring (Budapest, Hungary)
- **Date:** 2018-07-29
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.381 km (2.722 mi), 70 laps, 306.630 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 239 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sweltering, ambient 33°C, track 57°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 85,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Lewis Hamilton takes victory from pole with Bottas playing rear-guard before colliding with Vettel and Ricciardo in the closing laps.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
