# United States Grand Prix - 2018 Round 18

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Pirelli 2018 United States Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Circuit of the Americas (Austin, USA)
- **Date:** 2018-10-21
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.513 km (3.426 mi), 56 laps, 308.405 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 157 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 21°C, track 29°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 111,580 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Kimi Raikkonen takes an emotional, magnificent final Formula 1 career victory for Ferrari (his first win in 113 races since 2013 Australia), resisting Verstappen and Hamilton in a three-way finish.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
