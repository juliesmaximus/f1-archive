# Chinese Grand Prix - 2018 Round 3

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 2018 Heineken Chinese Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Shanghai International Circuit (Shanghai, China)
- **Date:** 2018-04-15
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.451 km (3.387 mi), 56 laps, 305.066 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 5 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, overcast, ambient 19°C, track 29°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 60,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Daniel Ricciardo charges from 6th to win with masterclass overtakes following a Safety Car deployed for Toro Rosso collision. Verstappen collides with Vettel at the hairpin, receiving a 10-second penalty.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
