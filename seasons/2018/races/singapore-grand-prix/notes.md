# Singapore Grand Prix - 2018 Round 15

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 2018 Singapore Airlines Singapore Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Marina Bay Street Circuit (Marina Bay, Singapore)
- **Date:** 2018-09-16
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.063 km (3.146 mi), 61 laps, 308.706 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 15 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, humid tropical night, ambient 30°C, track 34°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 85,000 race day (263,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Lewis Hamilton produces one of the greatest qualifying laps in Formula 1 history to claim pole by 0.3s, converting it to an unchallenged victory in Singapore.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Halo Cockpit Protection:** Mandatory titanium structure introduced in 2018 tested and verified under severe impact.
- **Engine Rules:** Stricter limit of 3 Internal Combustion Engines (ICE), MGU-H, and Turbochargers per driver per season.
- **Tyre Compounds:** Introduction of Pirelli Hypersoft and Superhard compounds across 7 dry tire specs.
