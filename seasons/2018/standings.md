## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton Five-Time World Champion:** Hamilton matched Juan Manuel Fangio's 5 World Titles, winning 11 races to clinch the championship at Mexico City.
- **Mandatory Halo Cockpit Protection:** Introduced amidst widespread debate, the titanium halo proved critically decisive at the Belgian GP, deflecting Fernando Alonso's flying McLaren over Charles Leclerc's Sauber.
- **Kimi Räikkönen Final Ferrari Victory:** Räikkönen scored his 21st career Grand Prix win at Austin (USA), ending a 113-race win drought since Australia 2013.
- **The Fight for Five:** Hamilton and Vettel entered the year tied on 4 titles each; Hamilton pulled clear during the second half of the season following Vettel's crash from the lead at Hockenheim.
