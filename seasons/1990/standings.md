## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The Suzuka Turn 1 Collision:** Ayrton Senna clinched his 2nd World Championship after colliding with Alain Prost at 160 mph into Turn 1 on lap 1 of the Japanese Grand Prix. Senna later admitted in 1991 that the collision was intentional in protest against FISA's refusal to move pole position to the clean side of the track.
- **Tyrrell 019 High-Nose Concept:** Designed by Harvey Postlethwaite and Jean-Claude Migeot, the Tyrrell 019 debuted at San Marino (Round 3) and introduced the revolutionary raised nose and anhedral front wing, channelling clean airflow under the monocoque into the underfloor diffuser. This aerodynamic concept revolutionized modern Formula 1 chassis design.
- **The 500th World Championship Grand Prix:** The season finale in Adelaide marked the 500th official World Championship Grand Prix since Silverstone 1950.
- **Final Year of Dropped Scores:** 1990 was the final season in Formula 1 history to employ the dropped scores ("best results") rule. From 1991 onwards, every race result counted toward both the Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
- **Tie-Breaker for 3rd in WDC:** Nelson Piquet and Gerhard Berger tied on 43 net points. Piquet was awarded 3rd place on countback by virtue of his 2 victories (Japan, Australia) compared to Berger's 0 victories.
