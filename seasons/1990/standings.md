## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The Suzuka Turn 1 Collision:** Ayrton Senna clinched his 2nd World Championship after colliding with Alain Prost at 160 mph into Turn 1 on lap 1 of the Japanese Grand Prix. Senna later admitted in 1991 that the move was intentional in response to FISA's refusal to move pole position to the clean side of the track.
- **Tyrrell 019 High-Nose Concept:** Designed by Harvey Postlethwaite and Jean-Claude Migeot, the Tyrrell 019 introduced the revolutionary raised nose and anhedral front wing, channelling clean airflow under the monocoque into the underfloor diffuser. This aerodynamic concept revolutionized Formula 1 chassis design for decades.
- **The 500th World Championship Grand Prix:** The season finale in Adelaide marked the 500th official World Championship race since Silverstone 1950.
- **Last Year of Best 11 Rule:** 1990 was the final season in Formula 1 history where dropped scores were used. From 1991 onwards, all race results counted towards both the Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
