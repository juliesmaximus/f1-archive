## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A damp track at the start challenged drivers with critical tyre crossover decisions. Gerhard Berger jumped the start from 2nd on the grid and was penalised 60 seconds by the race stewards. Knowing he had a 1-minute time penalty applied to his race time, Berger drove at qualifying pace for 70 laps, passing Senna on track and crossing the finish line 45 seconds ahead of his teammate. When the 60-second penalty was added to Berger's elapsed time, he was demoted from 1st to 4th place.

Ayrton Senna was classified as the race winner, with Nelson Piquet inherited 2nd place for Benetton and Nigel Mansell claiming 3rd for Ferrari. Alain Prost finished 5th, scoring 2 points which were later dropped as his lowest score under the best 11 results regulation.
