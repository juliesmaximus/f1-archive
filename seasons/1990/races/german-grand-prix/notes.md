## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna took pole position on the ultra-fast straights of Hockenheim. Benetton's Alessandro Nannini gambled on a non-stop strategy on Goodyear tyres, taking the lead on lap 34 when Senna made his scheduled tyre stop.

Senna emerged on fresh tyres and hunted Nannini down, closing a 4-second deficit in two laps. On lap 34, Senna drafted past Nannini into the Ostkurve chicane to regain the lead, holding off the Italian to secure his fourth win of the season and regain the lead of the World Championship. Nannini took a superb 2nd place, with Berger 3rd and Prost 4th.

## Disqualifications & Start Incidents
- **Philippe Alliot (Ligier-Ford):** Disqualified for receiving a push-start from marshals after taking evasive action during an opening-lap multi-car collision.
- **Stefano Modena (Brabham-Judd) & Emanuele Pirro (Dallara-Ford):** Retired on lap 1 following collisions at the start.
