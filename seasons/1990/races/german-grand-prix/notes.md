## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Senna stopped Prost's winning momentum. Senna took pole and fought off a relentless charge from Alessandro Nannini in the Benetton, who ran non-stop on tyres. Senna passed Nannini on fresh tyres on lap 34 to win by 6.5 seconds. Nannini finished 2nd and Gerhard Berger 3rd, while Prost finished 4th.
