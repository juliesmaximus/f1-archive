## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Shadowed by a horrific practice crash for Lotus driver Martin Donnelly, whose car disintegrated against the armco at 140 mph, throwing him onto the track with severe multi-trauma injuries. He survived thanks to Prof. Sid Watkins' emergency roadside tracheotomy. In the race, Prost took victory after Senna retired with a punctured radiator on lap 53. Mansell finished 2nd to seal a Ferrari 1-2, taking the title fight to Suzuka.
