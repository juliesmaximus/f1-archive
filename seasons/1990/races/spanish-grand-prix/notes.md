## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The weekend was overshadowed by a horrific qualifying accident on Friday for 26-year-old Lotus driver Martin Donnelly. Donnelly's Lotus 102 suffered front suspension failure entering the ultra-fast right-hander behind the pits at 140 mph, disintegrating on impact with the armco barrier. Donnelly was thrown onto the track, still strapped to his seat, suffering multiple severe fractures and internal trauma. His life was saved by trackside emergency intervention led by Professor Sid Watkins.

In the race, Ayrton Senna started from pole, but a punctured radiator on lap 53 forced his retirement. Alain Prost took full advantage to lead a Ferrari 1-2 with Nigel Mansell, cutting Senna's championship lead to 9 points and sending the title fight to a showdown at Suzuka. Alessandro Nannini finished 3rd in what would prove to be his final Formula 1 Grand Prix finish before a helicopter accident in Italy ended his F1 career.
