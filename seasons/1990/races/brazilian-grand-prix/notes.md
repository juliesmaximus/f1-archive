## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 returned to Interlagos on a newly shortened, modernized 4.3 km layout. Senna took pole and led comfortably until lap 41, when he collided with former teammate Satoru Nakajima while attempting to lap him, damaging his front wing. Alain Prost capitalized to score his first victory for Scuderia Ferrari, with Gerhard Berger 2nd and Senna recovering to 3rd.
