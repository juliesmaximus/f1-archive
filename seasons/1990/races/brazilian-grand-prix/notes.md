## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 returned to Interlagos for the first time since 1980 on a modernized, shortened 4.325 km layout featuring the newly created Senna 'S' complex connecting the pit straight to the Curva do Sol. Ayrton Senna claimed pole position and dominated the race, leading comfortably by over 10 seconds. On lap 41, while attempting to lap former Lotus teammate Satoru Nakajima's Tyrrell at the Junção corner, the two cars made contact, shearing off Senna's front wing.

Senna was forced to pit for a nose cone change, dropping behind Alain Prost and Gerhard Berger. Prost drove a measured tactical race without changing tyres to score his 40th career victory and his maiden win for Scuderia Ferrari in only his second start for the Maranello team. Senna recovered to finish 3rd on home soil.
