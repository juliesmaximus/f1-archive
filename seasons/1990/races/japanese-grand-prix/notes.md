## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The most controversial title decider in F1 history. Senna secured pole position but demanded that pole be moved to the clean left side of the track. When FISA president Jean-Marie Balestre and the stewards refused, Senna swore that if Prost led into Turn 1, he would not concede. Prost made a superior launch from the dirty side. Entering Turn 1 at 160 mph, Senna kept his foot flat on the throttle and rammed the rear-right of Prost's Ferrari, sending both cars skidding violently into the gravel trap. Both drivers were eliminated on the spot, clinching Senna's 2nd World Championship. Nelson Piquet went on to win for Benetton, leading Roberto Moreno in an emotional 1-2, with Aguri Suzuki taking 3rd to become the first Japanese driver on a Formula 1 podium.
