## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Stunning season opener featuring an iconic duel between Ayrton Senna and 21-year-old rookie Jean Alesi in the nimble Tyrrell-Ford 019 with revolutionary high-nose aerodynamics. Alesi led for 34 laps, repassing Senna immediately after Senna dived down the inside on lap 34. Senna made a second, decisive move on lap 35 to win, but praised Alesi's courage. Thierry Boutsen finished 3rd for Williams.
