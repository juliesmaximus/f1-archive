## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The 1990 season opener in downtown Phoenix featured an iconic duel between Ayrton Senna and Jean Alesi. Starting 4th on Pirelli street tyres in the agile Tyrrell 018 (prior to the debut of the high-nose 019 at Imola), Alesi made a sensational launch into Turn 1 to take the race lead. Alesi led for 34 laps, repassing Senna immediately on the brakes after Senna dived down the inside on lap 34. Senna made a second, decisive move into Turn 1 on lap 35 to seize the lead and claim victory, praising Alesi's courage and racecraft in post-race interviews.

Gerhard Berger qualified on pole on his McLaren debut but crashed into the tyre barrier on lap 9, later setting fastest lap on lap 34 before retiring with clutch failure. Nelson Piquet finished 4th for Benetton, with Stefano Modena 5th for Brabham and Satoru Nakajima 6th in the second Tyrrell.

## Disqualifications & Non-Starters
