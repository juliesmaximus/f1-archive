## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The San Marino Grand Prix witnessed the historic race debut of Harvey Postlethwaite and Jean-Claude Migeot's Tyrrell 019, introducing the revolutionary raised nose and anhedral front wing assembly that redefined Formula 1 aerodynamics.

Ayrton Senna claimed pole position and led until lap 4, when a broken rear wheel rim pitched his McLaren into the gravel at the Tosa hairpin. Williams teammate Thierry Boutsen took the lead until his Renault V10 suffered an engine failure on lap 18. Gerhard Berger assumed the lead, but Riccardo Patrese mounted a relentless charge in the second Williams FW13B, executing an audacious pass around the outside of Berger at the Tosa corner on lap 51 to claim his third career victory and his first since Kyalami 1983 (a gap of 6 years and 208 days).

## Incidents & Non-Classifications
- **Opening Lap Melee:** A dust cloud kicked up at Tamburello blinded midfield runners, causing Ivan Capelli (Leyton House) and Satoru Nakajima (Tyrrell) to collide, collecting Roberto Moreno (EuroBrun) and eliminating all three drivers on lap 1.
- **Pierluigi Martini (Minardi-Ford):** Sustained a cracked ankle in a heavy Friday qualifying crash at Acque Minerali when his throttle stuck, forcing his withdrawal from the weekend.
