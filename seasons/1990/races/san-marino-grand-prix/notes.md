## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Senna took pole and led until a wheel rim failure sent him into the gravel trap on lap 4. Thierry Boutsen led until his Renault V10 blew up on lap 18. Riccardo Patrese swept through to take victory for Williams-Renault, edging Gerhard Berger's McLaren by 5.1 seconds, with Alessandro Nannini 3rd for Benetton.
