## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A race etched in Formula 1 folklore. Just one race after failing to qualify in Mexico, Adrian Newey's Leyton House CG901 featured a redesigned floor and diffuser that generated enormous downforce on Paul Ricard's smooth asphalt. Team drivers Ivan Capelli and Maurício Gugelmin opted for a daring non-stop strategy on Goodyear tyres. When all frontrunners pitted for fresh rubber, Capelli inherited the lead with Gugelmin running 2nd.

Gugelmin held 2nd until his Judd engine expired on lap 58. Capelli heroically held off Alain Prost's Ferrari for 45 laps until an oil pressure drop slowed the turquoise car with just three laps remaining. Prost swept past into the Signes curve on lap 78 to claim Ferrari's 100th World Championship Grand Prix victory, with Capelli securing a remarkable 2nd place and Senna taking 3rd.

## Post-Race Disqualifications & Official Reconciliations
- **Andrea de Cesaris (Dallara-Ford):** Finished 15th on track (+2 laps) but was disqualified post-race when scrutineers found his Dallara F190 underweight. Jolpica records posText 'D', while Formula1.com preserves the provisional 15th on-track finish. Brabham, Nannini, Dalmas, and Mansell were promoted in official FIA classifications.
- **Thierry Boutsen (Williams-Renault):** Retired on lap 8 due to engine failure (corrected from 7 laps in local archive to match official F1.com classification).
