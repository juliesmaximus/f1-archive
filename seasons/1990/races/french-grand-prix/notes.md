## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nearly one of the greatest upsets in F1 history. The underdog Leyton House March team with Adrian Newey's aerodynamically exquisite CG901 ran non-stop on harder Goodyear tyres. Ivan Capelli led 45 laps and was on course for an improbable victory until Prost caught him with 3 laps left and forced an overtake into the Signes curve. Prost won Ferrari's 100th World Championship Grand Prix, with Capelli taking a heroic 2nd and Senna 3rd.
