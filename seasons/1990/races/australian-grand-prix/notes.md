## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The 500th World Championship Grand Prix. Senna led from pole until lap 61 when his gearbox failed and he crashed into the tyre barrier at the end of Dequetteville Terrace. Nelson Piquet held off a ferocious late charge from Nigel Mansell to win by 3.1 seconds, with Alain Prost 3rd. McLaren-Honda clinched the World Constructors' Championship.
