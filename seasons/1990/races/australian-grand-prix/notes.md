## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The 500th official World Championship Grand Prix in Formula 1 history. Ayrton Senna dominated from pole position, leading by 9 seconds until lap 61 when his gearbox failed, causing him to slide into the tyre barrier at the end of Dequetteville Terrace.

Nelson Piquet took the lead for Benetton, but Nigel Mansell launched a relentless late-race attack in his Ferrari, setting the fastest lap of the race on lap 75 (1:18.203) and making a daring lunge on Piquet at the hairpin on the final lap. Piquet held his nerve to win by 3.129 seconds, capturing his second consecutive Grand Prix victory and securing 3rd in the World Drivers' Championship on countback. McLaren-Honda clinched their sixth World Constructors' Championship.
