## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Thierry Boutsen led from pole position and produced an extraordinary defensive masterclass, holding off Ayrton Senna for all 77 laps. Senna mounted desperate challenges in the closing stages, nudging Boutsen's Williams at the chicane, but Boutsen held firm to win by 0.288 seconds. Nelson Piquet finished 3rd.
