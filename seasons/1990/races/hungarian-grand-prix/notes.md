## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Thierry Boutsen produced the defensive drive of his life around the tortuous Hungaroring. Williams locked out the front row in qualifying with Boutsen taking pole (1:17.919) ahead of teammate Riccardo Patrese. Boutsen led from start to finish, resisting relentless pressure from Alessandro Nannini and Ayrton Senna.

Senna attempted an aggressive dive down the inside of Nannini at the Chicane on lap 64, punting the Benetton out of the race. Senna then glued himself to Boutsen's rear wing for the final 13 laps. Boutsen placed his Williams FW13B perfectly in every braking zone, crossing the finish line just 0.288 seconds ahead of Senna to take his third and final Formula 1 Grand Prix victory.
