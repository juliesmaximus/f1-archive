## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna produced a peerless performance across the streets of the Principality, capturing pole position, fastest lap, and leading every single lap from start to finish to claim his 3rd Monaco Grand Prix victory.

The original start was red-flagged when Alain Prost and Gerhard Berger tangled at Mirabeau, completely blocking the track. At the restart, Senna controlled the pace ahead of Prost until the Ferrari driver retired on lap 30 with a battery failure. Jean Alesi, driving the high-nose Tyrrell 019, showcased breathtaking agility around the tight confines of Monaco, pressuring Senna throughout the closing stages to take 2nd place, just 1.087 seconds behind the McLaren, with Berger completing the podium in 3rd.

---

## Disqualifications & Penalties
- **Nelson Piquet (Benetton-Ford):** Spun and stalled at Loews Hairpin on lap 35. Marshals push-started his car to rejoin, violating sporting regulations against outside assistance. Piquet was officially disqualified with a black flag.
