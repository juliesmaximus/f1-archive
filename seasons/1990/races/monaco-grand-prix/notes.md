## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Senna secured pole position and dominated all 78 laps to claim his 3rd Monaco Grand Prix victory. Alain Prost retired with an electrical failure on lap 31. Jean Alesi drove another sensational race to finish 2nd in the Tyrrell-Ford, with Gerhard Berger completing the podium in 3rd.
