## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Alain Prost's greatest career victories. Qualifying only 13th due to focusing entirely on race setup in the bumpy conditions of Mexico City, Prost preserved his Goodyear tyres flawlessly during the race while rivals wore theirs out. Ayrton Senna led commanding until a slow puncture caused his right-rear tyre to shred on lap 63, forcing his retirement.

Prost scythed through the pack to take the lead on lap 61. Behind him, Nigel Mansell and Gerhard Berger engaged in a thrilling duel for 2nd place. On lap 68, Mansell executed one of the most daring overtakes in motorsport history, sweeping around the outside of Berger through the terrifying, 180-degree high-speed Peraltada curve to secure an astonishing Ferrari 1-2 finish.
