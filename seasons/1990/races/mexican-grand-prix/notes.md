## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Alain Prost's greatest tactical masterpieces. Qualifying down in 13th, Prost methodically preserved his Goodyear tyres on the abrasive, high-altitude track. Senna led until a slow puncture caused his right rear tyre to delaminate on lap 63. Prost took the lead to win, while teammate Nigel Mansell pulled off one of F1's most famous overtakes—passing Gerhard Berger around the outside of the terrifying 180 mph banked Peraltada corner with two laps remaining to secure a historic Ferrari 1-2.
