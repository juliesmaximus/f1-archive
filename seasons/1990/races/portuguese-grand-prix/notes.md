## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell claimed pole position (1:13.557) ahead of teammate Alain Prost for a Ferrari front-row lockout. At the start, Mansell bogged down with excessive wheelspin, sliding across the track towards Prost and crowding his teammate against the pit wall. The blunder allowed Senna and Berger to streak ahead into Turn 1, infuriating Prost and Ferrari team management.

Mansell spent the afternoon fighting back furiously. On lap 50, Mansell overtook Senna into Turn 1 to take the lead. On lap 61, a heavy collision between Aguri Suzuki and Alex Caffi left Caffi's Arrows trapped against the armco barrier, prompting a red flag that stopped the race. With more than 75% distance completed, full points were awarded, giving Mansell his only Grand Prix victory of 1990.
