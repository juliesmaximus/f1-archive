## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell took pole position but made a terrible start, spinning his rear wheels and swerving across the track, blocking teammate Prost and allowing Senna and Berger to slip through. Mansell recovered furiously, overtaking both McLarens to win his final race for Ferrari. Senna finished 2nd and Prost 3rd.
