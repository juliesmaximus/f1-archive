## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The 1990 Belgian Grand Prix was notorious for requiring three separate starts to get underway due to chaotic opening-lap accidents:
1. **Start 1:** Aguri Suzuki collided with Nelson Piquet at the start, pitching Nigel Mansell into the barriers and prompting an immediate red flag. Suzuki was eliminated.
2. **Start 2:** On the restart lap, Paolo Barilla lost control of his Minardi through Eau Rouge, crashing violently into the barriers and scattering debris across the track. A second red flag was deployed. Barilla was uninjured but could not restart.
3. **Start 3:** The third start finally proceeded cleanly. Ayrton Senna converted his pole position into the race lead and held off Alain Prost throughout 44 laps, taking his fourth consecutive Belgian Grand Prix victory (and fifth overall).
