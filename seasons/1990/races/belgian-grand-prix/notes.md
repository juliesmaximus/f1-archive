## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A chaotic start that required three attempts after high-speed pile-ups involving Aguri Suzuki and Paolo Barilla at Eau Rouge. Once under way, Senna controlled the race from pole, leading all 44 laps to win ahead of Alain Prost and Gerhard Berger.
