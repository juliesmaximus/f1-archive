## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell took pole position to the delight of the British crowd and led until his Ferrari gearbox failed on lap 55. A distraught Mansell threw his gloves to the crowd and announced his retirement from F1 (a decision later reversed). Prost drove a steady race to take his third consecutive victory, taking the lead of the World Drivers' Championship. Thierry Boutsen finished 2nd and Senna 3rd.
