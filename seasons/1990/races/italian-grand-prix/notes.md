## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The race was red-flagged on lap 1 when Derek Warwick suffered a horrifying 160 mph crash in the Lotus at the Parabolica, rolling multiple times and sliding down the track upside down. Warwick walked away unhurt and took the restart in the spare car. Senna dominated the restart to score a Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap) ahead of Prost and Berger.
