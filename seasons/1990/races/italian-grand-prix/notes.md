## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna claimed a majestic Grand Slam: taking pole position, setting fastest lap, winning the race, and leading all 53 laps at the Temple of Speed, in front of the passionately pro-Ferrari Tifosi.

On the opening lap of the original start, Derek Warwick suffered a terrifying 140 mph crash at the Parabolica in his Lotus-Lamborghini, striking the armco barrier, flipping upside down, and sliding along the asphalt. Amazingly, Warwick was unhurt, ran down pit lane, and took the restart in the spare car.

At the restart, Senna controlled the race from Prost's Ferrari. Riccardo Patrese finished 5th (+85.274s), completing all 53 laps (reconciling a discrepancy in Jolpica which had recorded 52 laps).
