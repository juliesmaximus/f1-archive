## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Longest Circuit in F1 History: Measuring 25.579 km (15.894 miles) through public roads, mountain villages (Spoltore, Cappelle sul Tavo), and the 6 km straightaway along Montesilvano beach to Pescara, it remains the LONGEST CIRCUIT IN FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP HISTORY.
- Ferrari Factory Boycott: Following the Mille Miglia disaster that killed Alfonso de Portago and nine spectators earlier in the year, and protesting government investigations, Enzo Ferrari boycotted the race, entering only a single private Ferrari for Luigi Musso.
- Moss Dominant Vanwall Win: Stirling Moss controlled the race from lap 10 after Musso's engine leaked oil, winning by over three minutes over newly crowned world champion Fangio.
- Moss Pit Stop for Drink: Moss held such a commanding lead that he stopped at the pits for a glass of water on the final lap before cruising to victory.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Longest track layout in Formula One World Championship history: 25.579 km (all-time record).
- Sole appearance of Pescara Circuit in the World Championship.
