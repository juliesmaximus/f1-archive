# 1957 Pescara Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXV Gran Premio di Pescara
- **Round**: 7 of 8 in the 1957 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 18 August 1957
- **Location**: Pescara Circuit, Pescara, Abruzzo, Italy
- **Circuit Length**: 25.579 km (15.894 miles)
- **Distance**: 18 laps (460.422 km / 286.093 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) - 9:44.600
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 9:44.600 (Lap 9) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 2:59:22.700 (153.978 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Stirling Moss (Vanwall) - 18 laps
  2. Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati) - 18 laps (+3:13.900)
  3. Harry Schell (Maserati) - 18 laps (+6:46.800)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Longest Circuit in Championship History**: At 25.579 km (15.894 miles), the public road triangular course winding through the Abruzzo villages and along the Adriatic coast remains the longest circuit ever used for an FIA Formula One World Championship Grand Prix.
- **Moss's Commanding Victory**: Stirling Moss drove a flawless race in the Vanwall VW5. After Luigi Musso retired with an oil leak on lap 10, Moss was unchallenged, winning by over 3 minutes from newly crowned World Champion Juan Manuel Fangio.
- **Ferrari Factory Boycott**: Enzo Ferrari boycotted the race to protest Italian government safety threats following the 1957 Mille Miglia disaster. Ferrari entered only a single privateer car for Luigi Musso.
- **Fastest Lap Tie**: Moss equalled Fangio's pole time to the tenth of a second on lap 9 (9:44.600) to collect the fastest lap bonus point.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Blisteringly hot August Italian summer sun; track surface melted in several braking zones.
