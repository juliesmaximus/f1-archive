## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The Greatest Drive in F1 History: Fangio started on half fuel tanks, intending to pit mid-race, while Ferrari planned to run non-stop with Peter Collins and Mike Hawthorn. By lap 12, Fangio built a 30-second lead and pitted. Disaster struck: the Maserati mechanics cross-threaded a rear wheel nut and dropped the wheel under the car. The pit stop took a agonizing 52 seconds. Fangio rejoined 48 seconds behind the Ferrari 1-2 with 10 laps remaining.
- Sublime Lap Record Assault: Driving at terrifying commitment, Fangio obliterated the lap record on almost every lap: 9:28.5... 9:25.3... 9:23.4... 9:17.4 (an astonishing 11 seconds faster than his own pole time!). He took corners in one gear higher than ever before and rode the ditch at Adenauer Forst.
- Penultimate Lap Overtake: On lap 21, Fangio caught Peter Collins at the North Curve, passing him on the inside while kicking up stones that shattered Collins' goggles. Two corners later, Fangio squeezed past Mike Hawthorn into the lead.
- Fangio 5th World Championship: Fangio won by 3.6 seconds, mathematically securing his FIFTH AND FINAL WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP. Fangio famously stated afterwards: 'I have never driven like that before in my life, and I know I will never be able to drive like that again.'
- F2 Class Inclusion: Porsche and Cooper entered 1.5L Formula Two cars running concurrently with the F1 field (Edgar Barth won the F2 class).

## Records Set or Equalled
- Juan Manuel Fangio clinched his 5th Formula One World Drivers' Championship (a record that stood for 46 years until Michael Schumacher in 2003).
- Fangio's 24th and final career World Championship Grand Prix victory (winning 46.15% of all races entered).
- Lap record shattered by 24.2 seconds compared to the 1956 race.
