## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Sam Hanks 'Laydown' Roadster Triumph: Sam Hanks won on his 13th attempt driving George Salih's revolutionary 'laydown' roadster (where the Offenhauser engine was tilted at 36 degrees from horizontal to drastically lower center of gravity).
- Emotional Retirement in Victory Lane: Immediately after taking the checkered flag and kissing the trophy queen, Hanks announced his immediate retirement from racing on live radio, fulfilling a promise to his wife Alice.
- Record Purse: Hanks collected over $103,000 from the record total purse of $300,000.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First Indianapolis 500 victory for a 'laydown' roadster design (George Salih / Sam Hanks).
- Fastest winning 500-mile race speed to date: 135.601 mph (218.229 km/h).
