# 1957 Indianapolis 500 - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XLI Indianapolis International Motor Sweepstakes
- **Round**: 3 of 8 in the 1957 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Thursday, 30 May 1957
- **Location**: Indianapolis Motor Speedway, Speedway, Indiana, USA
- **Circuit Length**: 4.023 km (2.500 miles)
- **Distance**: 200 laps (804.672 km / 500.000 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Pat O'Connor (Kurtis Kraft-Offenhauser) - 4:10.090 (4-lap average; 143.948 mph)
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Rathmann (Epperly-Offenhauser) - 1:02.750 (Lap 127; 143.426 mph) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Sam Hanks (Epperly-Offenhauser) - 3:41:14.250 (218.229 km/h / 135.601 mph)
- **Podium**:
  1. Sam Hanks (Epperly-Offenhauser) - 200 laps
  2. Jim Rathmann (Epperly-Offenhauser) - 200 laps (+21.460s)
  3. Jimmy Bryan (Kuzma-Offenhauser) - 200 laps (+2:13.970)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **The "Laydown" Offy Revolution**: Car builder George Salih designed the revolutionary Epperly roadster with the Offenhauser 4-cylinder engine tilted 36 degrees on its side ("laydown" engine), lowering the center of gravity and improving left-side weight distribution. The car dominated the race, taking a 1-2 finish with Sam Hanks and Jim Rathmann.
- **Hanks Retires in Victory Lane**: Veteran Sam Hanks won on his 13th attempt and broke into tears in Victory Lane, announcing his immediate retirement from racing as promised to his wife.
- **Fastest 500 to Date**: Sam Hanks shattered the race record by over 7 mph, averaging 135.601 mph for the full 500 miles.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Clear, warm Midwestern spring conditions; completely dry track.
