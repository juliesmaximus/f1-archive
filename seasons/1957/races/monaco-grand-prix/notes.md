## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Massive Chicane Pileup (Lap 4): Stirling Moss led from lap 1 in his Vanwall. On lap 4 approaching the harbour chicane, Moss crashed through the wooden barrier poles and debris. Chasing closely, Ferrari teammates Peter Collins and Mike Hawthorn collided with the barriers and Moss's wreckage, wiping out all three British stars in one instant.
- Fangio Dodges Destruction: Fangio in 2nd slipped through the narrow gap between wreckage and the harbour edge, taking the lead and commanding the race to win by 25.2 seconds over Tony Brooks in the Vanwall.
- Tony Brooks Vanwall Breakthrough: Tony Brooks scored Vanwall's first-ever World Championship podium finish (2nd place).

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship podium finish for Vanwall (Tony Brooks, 2nd).
- Maiden World Championship podium for Masten Gregory (3rd, Maserati 250F).
