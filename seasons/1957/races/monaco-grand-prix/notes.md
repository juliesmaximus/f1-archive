# 1957 Monaco Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XV Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco
- **Round**: 2 of 8 in the 1957 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 19 May 1957
- **Location**: Circuit de Monaco, Monte Carlo, Monaco
- **Circuit Length**: 3.145 km (1.954 miles)
- **Distance**: 105 laps (330.225 km / 205.192 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) - 1:42.700
- **Fastest Lap**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) - 1:45.600 [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) - 3:10:12.800 (104.165 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati) - 105 laps
  2. Tony Brooks (Vanwall) - 105 laps (+25.200s)
  3. Masten Gregory (Maserati) - 103 laps (+2 laps)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Lap 4 Chicane Carnage**: On lap 4, Stirling Moss (Vanwall) missed his braking point approaching the harbor chicane, crashing into the barrier and sending debris across the track. Running closely behind, Peter Collins (Ferrari) swerved to avoid Moss and hit the stone wall. Mike Hawthorn (Ferrari) collided with Collins' wrecked car. All three British drivers retired on the spot. Fangio, running behind them, braked hard and skillfully threaded through the wreckage undamaged.
- **Tony Brooks Vanwall Breakthrough**: Tony Brooks drove a measured race in the sole surviving Vanwall, finishing 2nd to score Vanwall's first World Championship podium finish.
- **Masten Gregory Maiden Podium**: American rookie Masten Gregory finished 3rd in a privateer Centro Sud Maserati, becoming the first American driver to score a podium in an overseas World Championship Grand Prix.
- **Fangio Maximum Points**: Fangio took pole, race victory, and fastest lap, taking a maximum 9 points.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, sunny Mediterranean spring weather; dry asphalt throughout.
