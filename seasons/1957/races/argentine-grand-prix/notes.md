## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Fangio Maserati Return: Returning to Maserati for 1957, Fangio dominated his home Grand Prix, winning by 18.3 seconds over teammate Jean Behra in a Maserati 1-2-3-4 sweep.
- Moss Throttle Heartbreak: Stirling Moss led from pole position before a broken throttle linkage dropped him out of contention; he shared car #18 with Cesare Perdisa to finish 8th.
- Castellotti Final Race: Eugenio Castellotti finished unclassified; tragically, he was killed two months later on 14 March 1957 during a private Ferrari test at Modena Autodrome aged 26.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Maserati achieved an unprecedented 1-2-3-4 finishing sweep (Fangio 1st, Behra 2nd, Menditeguy 3rd, Schell 4th).
- Fangio won his home Argentine Grand Prix for the 4th consecutive year.
