# 1957 Argentine Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: V Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Round**: 1 of 8 in the 1957 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 13 January 1957
- **Location**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre, Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Circuit Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 miles)
- **Distance**: 100 laps (391.200 km / 243.080 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Maserati 250F) - 1:42.600
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Maserati 250F) - 1:44.700 (Lap 75) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati 250F) - 3:00:55.900 (129.729 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Juan Manuel Fangio (Maserati) - 100 laps
  2. Jean Behra (Maserati) - 100 laps (+18.300s)
  3. Carlos Menditeguy (Maserati) - 99 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Maserati 1-2-3-4 Domination**: The factory Maserati 250F cars swept the top four finishing positions (Fangio 1st, Behra 2nd, Menditeguy 3rd, and Harry Schell 4th).
- **Moss's Throttle Linkage Trouble**: Stirling Moss led from pole but had his throttle linkage break on lap 10. He spent over 10 laps in the pits for repairs, rejoining to set the fastest lap of the race (1:44.700) and earn 1 championship point.
- **Fangio's Fourth Home Win**: Driving flawlessly before his home crowd in scorching Buenos Aires summer heat, Juan Manuel Fangio claimed his fourth consecutive Argentine Grand Prix victory.
- **Ferrari Shared Drive**: José Froilán González and Alfonso de Portago shared Ferrari #20 to finish 5th (+2 laps), earning 1 championship point each.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Extremely hot and humid summer weather with ambient temperatures exceeding 35°C; clear skies throughout.
