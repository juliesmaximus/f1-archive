## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Historic First British Car Victory: Tony Brooks and Stirling Moss shared car #20 to take victory for Vanwall, scoring the FIRST-EVER FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP VICTORY FOR A BRITISH CAR, breaking the decades-long continental monopoly.
- Shared Victory Relay: Stirling Moss took pole and led early in his Vanwall until an engine misfire struck on lap 22. Tony Brooks, still nursing injuries from a Le Mans crash, pitted on lap 26 and handed his Vanwall over to Moss. Moss unleashed an astonishing recovery drive, passing Jean Behra's Maserati when Behra's clutch disintegrated, and went on to win by 25.6 seconds over Luigi Musso's Ferrari. Moss and Brooks shared the 8 points (4 points each), with Moss taking 1 point for fastest lap.
- Fangio Transmission DNF: Juan Manuel Fangio's Maserati suffered clutch and engine failure on lap 49, marking his only retirement of the season.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship victory and pole position for a British car/constructor (Vanwall).
- First World Championship Grand Prix victory for Tony Brooks (shared).
- Final shared-car victory in Formula One World Championship history.
