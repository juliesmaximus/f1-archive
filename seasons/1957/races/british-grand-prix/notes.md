## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **First World Championship Win for a British Car**: A landmark date in Grand Prix history. Vanwall, created by industrialist Tony Vandervell, became the first British manufacturer to win a World Championship Grand Prix, ending years of Italian (Alfa Romeo, Ferrari, Maserati) and German (Mercedes-Benz) domination.
- **Historic Shared Victory**: Stirling Moss started on pole in car #18 and led before an engine misfire forced him into the pits on lap 22. Teammate Tony Brooks, still sore from injuries sustained at Le Mans, was called in on lap 26 in car #20. Moss took over Brooks' car in 9th position and mounted a ferocious recovery drive. Moss overtook Mike Hawthorn and Luigi Musso to win by 25.6 seconds.
- **Points Allocation**: Brooks and Moss split the 8 winning points (4 points each); Moss also took 1 point for the fastest lap (total 5 pts).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Overcast, cool British summer day with dry asphalt throughout.
