## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Inaugural Dutch Championship GP: Zandvoort hosted its first World Championship race, characterized by sand blown onto the tarmac from coastal dunes.
- Another Ascari Grand Slam: Ascari took pole, fastest lap, victory, and led all 90 laps without challenge.
- Ferrari 1-2-3: Ascari, Farina, and Luigi Villoresi delivered another complete Ferrari podium lockout, with Hawthorn 4th in the Cooper-Bristol.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Ascari scored his 7th career victory, breaking the all-time wins record.
- Ascari's 5th consecutive race win of the 1952 season.
