## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Youngest Winner Record**: At 22 years, 80 days old, Troy Ruttman became the youngest driver to win an official round of the Formula One World Championship — an all-time record that stood for 51 years until Fernando Alonso won the 2003 Hungarian GP at 22 years, 26 days.
- **First Turbocharged Diesel Pole**: Fred Agabashian claimed pole position in a Cummins Diesel turbocharged roadster (#28), marking the only pole position in World Championship history achieved by a diesel-powered car.
- **Bill Vukovich Heartbreak**: Bill Vukovich dominated the race, leading 150 laps and setting fastest lap (1:06.600), but crashed into the outer wall on lap 192 due to steering linkage failure with victory in sight.
- **Ascari Ferrari 375 Campaign**: Alberto Ascari was the only European Grand Prix regular in the field, driving a factory Ferrari 375 Special (#12). He qualified 19th and worked his way up to 8th before spinning out on lap 40 due to a collapsed wire wheel hub.
