## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari Sole European Entrant: Alberto Ascari entered a modified Ferrari 375 Special powered by a 4.5L V12. Qualifying 19th, he climbed to 8th before a Borrani wire-wheel hub collapsed on lap 40, sending him into a spin and retirement.
- Troy Ruttman Youngest Winner: Troy Ruttman (22 years, 80 days) won the race in the Kuzma-Offenhauser, becoming the youngest race winner in World Championship history — a record that stood for 51 years until Fernando Alonso in 2003.
- Vukovich Heartbreak: Bill Vukovich led 150 laps before a steering linkage failed with just 8 laps to go (lap 192), handing victory to Ruttman.
- First Pole for Diesel: Fred Agabashian scored pole position in the Cummins Diesel turbocharged entry, the only pole in F1 World Championship history for a diesel engine.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Youngest Grand Prix / World Championship winner: Troy Ruttman (22 years, 80 days) — stood until 2003.
- Only pole position by a diesel-powered car in World Championship history (Fred Agabashian).
