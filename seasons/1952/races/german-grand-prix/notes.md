## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari Clinches World Championship: With victory from pole, fastest lap, and leading all 18 laps, Alberto Ascari mathematically clinched the 1952 FIA World Drivers' Championship with two races to spare.
- Late Pit Stop Thriller: Ascari led comfortably when an oil leak forced a precautionary stop with two laps remaining. Farina swept past into the lead. Ascari rejoined, hunted down Farina on the 22.8 km lap, and overtook him at the Karussell to win by 14.1 seconds.
- Ferrari 1-2-3-4 Clean Sweep: Ferrari swept the top 4 positions: Ascari 1st, Farina 2nd, Fischer 3rd, Taruffi 4th.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Alberto Ascari officially crowned 1952 Formula One World Champion of Drivers.
- First constructor 1-2-3-4 lockout in World Championship history: Ferrari.
- Ascari tied Fangio's all-time record of 6 career Grand Prix victories.
