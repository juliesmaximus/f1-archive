## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Ascari Consecutive Wins Streak Begins**: Returning from Indianapolis, Alberto Ascari gave a masterclass in wet weather, qualifying on pole (4:37.000), setting fastest lap (4:55.000), and winning by nearly two minutes over teammate Nino Farina. This inaugurated Ascari's record-setting streak of 6 consecutive Grand Prix wins in 1952.
- **Jean Behra Lap 1 Lead**: Jean Behra briefly thrilled the crowds by leading lap 1 in his lightweight Gordini Type 16 before Ascari overtook him on lap 2.
- **Ferrari 1-2 Dominance**: The Aurelio Lampredi-designed 2.0-litre inline-4 Ferrari 500 proved unbeatable in the Formula Two regulations, claiming 1st and 2nd.
