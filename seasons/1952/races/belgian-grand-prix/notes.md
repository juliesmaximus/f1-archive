## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ascari Winning Streak Begins: Returning from Indianapolis, Alberto Ascari gave a masterclass in the rain, winning by nearly two minutes over Ferrari teammate Nino Farina. This began Ascari's historic streak of 6 consecutive Grand Prix victories.
- Gordini Early Threat: Local hero Jean Behra led lap 1 in his lightweight Gordini Type 16 before Ascari powered past on lap 2.
- Ferrari 1-2: Ferrari 500s finished 1st (Ascari) and 2nd (Farina), establishing the car's absolute supremacy in the Formula Two era.
- Fangio Absent Due to Injury: Juan Manuel Fangio broke his neck in a pre-season sports car crash at Monza on 8 June 1952, ruling him out for the entire 1952 championship.

## Records Set or Equalled
