- Ascari 6 Consecutive Wins Record: Ascari won his 6th consecutive race of the 1952 season, setting an all-time single-season consecutive wins record that stood until Sebastian Vettel won 9 in 2013.
- Maximum 36 Points: With his 6th win, Ascari achieved the maximum possible 36 points (four wins + 4 fastest lap points: 9+9+9+9=36; dropping 17.5 gross points!).
