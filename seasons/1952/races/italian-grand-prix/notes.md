# 1952 Round 8: XXIII Gran Premio d'Italia / Grand Prix d'Europe - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XXIII Gran Premio d'Italia / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Historic Name Notes**: 23rd running of the Italian Grand Prix; designated the honorary 'Grand Prix d'Europe'; season finale of the 1952 World Championship.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza
- **Location**: Monza, Lombardy, Italy
- **Host Country**: Italy
- **Track Length**: 6.300 km (3.915 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 162 m (531 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 80 laps / 504.000 km (313.171 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1952-09-07

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Warm, dry, and sunny late-summer conditions.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Ascari 6 Consecutive Wins Record**: Alberto Ascari won his 6th consecutive Grand Prix of the season, establishing an all-time record for consecutive Grand Prix victories in a single season that stood unbroken for 61 years until Sebastian Vettel won 9 consecutive races in 2013.
- **First Shared Fastest Lap Half-Points**: Alberto Ascari and José Froilán González set identical fastest race laps (2:06.100). Under official regulations, the 1 fastest lap bonus point was split equally, awarding 0.5 points to each driver.
- **Maserati A6GCM Competitiveness**: José Froilán González gave Maserati their first podium of the season with 2nd place, running close behind Ascari for much of the 80-lap distance.
- **Ascari Perfect 36 Net Points Score**: With 53.5 gross points scored across 1952, Ascari counted the theoretical maximum possible 36 net points (four wins with fastest lap: 9+9+9+9), dropping 17.5 gross points.
