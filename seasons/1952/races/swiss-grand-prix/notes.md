# 1952 Round 1: XII Großer Preis der Schweiz / XII Grand Prix de Suisse - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XII Großer Preis der Schweiz / XII Grand Prix de Suisse
- **Historic Name Notes**: 12th Swiss Grand Prix; first race run under 2.0-litre unblown Formula Two technical regulations for the World Drivers' Championship.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit Bremgarten
- **Location**: Bremgarten bei Bern, Canton of Bern, Switzerland
- **Host Country**: Switzerland
- **Track Length**: 7.280 km (4.524 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 550 m (1804 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 62 laps / 451.360 km (280.462 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1952-05-18

- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Main wooden grandstands along the Bremgarten start straight.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Formula Two Regulations Era: Following Alfa Romeo's withdrawal, the FIA switched the World Championship to 2.0-litre naturally aspirated (or 500cc supercharged) Formula Two rules to attract sufficient grid numbers.
- Ascari Absent for Indianapolis: Ferrari team leader Alberto Ascari skipped this race to prepare the Ferrari 375 for the Indianapolis 500.
- Piero Taruffi Maiden Victory: Piero Taruffi achieved his sole World Championship Grand Prix victory, leading home a Ferrari 1-2 with Rudi Fischer's private Ferrari 500.
- Farina Ignition Failure: Nino Farina led from pole until lap 16 when his magneto failed. He took over teammate André Simon's Ferrari 500, but that car also retired with magneto failure on lap 51.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Sole career Formula One World Championship victory for Piero Taruffi.
- First World Championship race run to 2.0-litre Formula Two engine specifications.
