# 1952 Round 1: XII Großer Preis der Schweiz / XII Grand Prix de Suisse - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XII Großer Preis der Schweiz / XII Grand Prix de Suisse
- **Historic Name Notes**: 12th Swiss Grand Prix; first championship race conducted under 2.0-litre unblown Formula Two technical regulations for the World Drivers' Championship.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit Bremgarten
- **Location**: Bremgarten bei Bern, Canton of Bern, Switzerland
- **Host Country**: Switzerland
- **Track Length**: 7.280 km (4.524 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 550 m (1804 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 62 laps / 451.360 km (280.462 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1952-05-18

## Weather, Environment & Track Conditions
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Cool, overcast, dry tarmac with tree-shaded sections through the Bremgarten forest.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Ferrari 500) – 2:47.500
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Piero Taruffi (Ferrari 500) – 2:49.100 (Lap 46) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Piero Taruffi (Ferrari 500) – 3:01:46.100 (9 pts: 8 win + 1 FL)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Piero Taruffi (Ferrari 500) – 3:01:46.100 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : Rudi Fischer (Ferrari 500) – +2:37.200 (+157.200s) [6 pts]
- **3rd** : Jean Behra (Gordini Type 16) – +1 lap [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- **Formula Two Era Commences**: With Alfa Romeo withdrawing from racing, the FIA moved the World Championship to 2.0-litre naturally aspirated (or 500cc supercharged) Formula Two rules to fill starting grids.
- **Ascari Absent for Indianapolis 500**: Ferrari team leader Alberto Ascari skipped this opening round to qualify the Ferrari 375 Special at Indianapolis.
- **Piero Taruffi Sole Championship Victory**: Piero Taruffi dominated the race in the factory Ferrari 500, winning his first and only World Championship Grand Prix victory by over 2.5 minutes.
- **Farina Magneto Retirements**: Nino Farina led early from pole position before retiring on lap 16 with magneto failure. He subsequently commandeered teammate André Simon's Ferrari 500 on lap 18, but that car also retired with magneto failure on lap 51.
