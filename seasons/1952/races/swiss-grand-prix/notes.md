# 1952 Round 1: Swiss Grand Prix Verified Race Record

- **Official Race Title**: XII Großer Preis der Schweiz
- **Circuit**: Circuit Bremgarten (Bern, Switzerland)
- **Track Length**: 7.280 km (4.524 mi) | **Race Distance**: 62 laps (451.360 km (280.462 mi))
- **Date**: 1952-05-18
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Sunny, warm, dry

## Verified Key Results

- **Pole Position**: Giuseppe 'Nino' Farina (Ferrari 500) - 2:47.5

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Introduction of Formula Two Regulations: Due to the withdrawal of Alfa Romeo and lack of competitive F1 entries, the 1952 championship was run entirely to Formula Two regulations (2.0L unblown).
- Ascari Missing for Indy 500: Lead Ferrari driver Alberto Ascari was absent in Switzerland, having travelled to the United States to practice and qualify for the Indianapolis 500.
- Farina Retirement: Nino Farina started on pole but suffered magneto failure on lap 16. He took over teammate André Simon's car, only for that car to also suffer magneto failure on lap 51.
- Piero Taruffi scored his first and only career World Championship Grand Prix victory.
