# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1979 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Jody Scheckter won the 1979 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Ferrari 312T4, scoring 51 net points (54 gross). Teammate Gilles Villeneuve finished as runner-up with 47 net points (53 gross), sealing a dominant 1-2 championship finish for Scuderia Ferrari.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Scuderia Ferrari captured their sixth World Constructors' Championship with 113 points, winning 6 Grands Prix with the Ferrari 312T4. Williams Grand Prix Engineering finished runner-up with 75 points and 5 victories.
