# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1979 - Calendar

The 1979 Formula One season was the 30th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 15 championship rounds between January 21 and October 7, 1979.

The 1979 season is revered as one of the golden years of Formula One:
- **Jody Scheckter & Ferrari's World Championship:** South African Jody Scheckter joined Scuderia Ferrari to pilot Mauro Forghieri's flat-12 ground-effect Ferrari 312T4. With clinical consistency and tactical maturity, Scheckter won 3 Grands Prix (Belgium, Monaco, Italy) to capture the **World Drivers' Championship**, clinching the title on Ferrari's home soil at Monza in a famous 1-2 finish ahead of loyal teammate Gilles Villeneuve. Scuderia Ferrari secured the **World Constructors' Championship** (their last Drivers' title for 21 years until Michael Schumacher in 2000).
- **The First Turbocharged Victory & The Battle of Dijon:** At the French Grand Prix at Dijon-Prenois on July 1, 1979, Jean-Pierre Jabouille took victory in the Renault RS10, scoring the **historic first Formula One victory for a turbocharged car**. The race was immortalized by the breathtaking final three laps, where Gilles Villeneuve (Ferrari) and René Arnoux (Renault) engaged in the greatest wheel-to-wheel, tire-banging duel in Grand Prix history.
- **The Rise of Williams Grand Prix Engineering:** Patrick Head's brilliant ground-effect Williams FW07 revolutionized the second half of the season. Veteran Clay Regazzoni scored **Williams's maiden Formula One victory** at Silverstone (British GP), followed by Alan Jones winning four of the final six races to establish Williams as the preeminent technological power of the incoming decade.
- **Ligier's Opening Blitz:** Guy Ligier's French team dominated the opening flyaway rounds with Jacques Laffite and Patrick Depailler in the Ligier JS11.
