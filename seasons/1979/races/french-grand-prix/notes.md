## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE GREATEST DUEL IN F1 HISTORY & FIRST TURBO VICTORY: Jean-Pierre Jabouille took pole in the twin-turbo Renault RS10, with teammate René Arnoux qualifying 2nd for an all-Renault front row. At the start, Gilles Villeneuve surged into the lead in his Ferrari 312T4 and led for 46 laps until tire degradation set in. Jabouille passed Villeneuve on lap 47 and pulled away to secure Renault's MAIDEN FORMULA ONE VICTORY—the **first World Championship Grand Prix victory for a turbocharged engine in Formula One history**, and an all-French triumph (French driver, French car, French engine, French tires [Michelin], French fuel [Elf]). Meanwhile, the battle for 2nd place became legendary: René Arnoux caught Villeneuve with three laps remaining. Over laps 78, 79, and 80, the two drivers engaged in an unbelievable, wheel-banging, sliding, tire-smoking duel, overtaking each other four times, touching wheels repeatedly through corners, kicking up dust and locking brakes at 160 mph! Villeneuve crossed the finish line 0.24 seconds ahead of Arnoux to take 2nd in motorsport's most iconic battle.
