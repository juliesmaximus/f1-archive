## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
VILLENEUVE'S WET-WEATHER MASTERCLASS: In Friday practice in torrential rain, Gilles Villeneuve produced one of the most astonishing performances in F1 history, lapping 9.6 seconds faster than second-placed Alan Jones! Alan Jones took pole in dry Saturday conditions. On race day, in cold rain, Villeneuve rocketed into the lead from 3rd on the grid. Jones chased Villeneuve until lap 36, when Jones pitted for dry tires; a loose rear wheel fell off exiting the pit lane. Villeneuve drove masterfully to take victory by 48.78 seconds over René Arnoux (Renault) and Didier Pironi (Tyrrell), securing runner-up in the World Championship.
