## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WILLIAMS 1-2 HOCKENHEIM CRUSH: Jean-Pierre Jabouille took pole in the turbo Renault RS10, but Alan Jones (Williams FW07) shot past into the lead at the start. Jabouille spun out on lap 8 attempting to catch Jones. Jones and Regazzoni utterly dominated the flat-out blast through the forest, securing Williams's first-ever 1-2 finish in Formula One history, Jones beating Regazzoni by 2.14 seconds with Jacques Laffite 3rd and championship leader Jody Scheckter 4th.
