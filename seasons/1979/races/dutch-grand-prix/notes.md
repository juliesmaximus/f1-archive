## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
VILLENEUVE'S THREE-WHEEL LEGEND & JONES WIN: René Arnoux took pole in the Renault. At the start, Alan Jones led, but on lap 11 Gilles Villeneuve pulled off an outrageous outside-pass at Tarzan Corner to take the lead. On lap 47, Villeneuve's left-rear tire delaminated entering Tarzan; he spun into the sand, recovered, and drove an entire 4.2 km lap on three wheels at full speed with the shredded tire flapping against the rear wing! Villeneuve refused to stop until reaching the pits, where terminal suspension damage finally forced his retirement. Alan Jones took victory—his third in a row—by 21.75 seconds over Jody Scheckter, who crucially claimed 6 points for 2nd.
