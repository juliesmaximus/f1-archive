## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SCHECKTER WORLD CHAMPION IN FERRARI 1-2 HOME TRIUMPH: Jean-Pierre Jabouille took pole in the turbo Renault RS10, but Jody Scheckter made a sensational start from 3rd to seize the lead. René Arnoux passed Scheckter on lap 2, but Arnoux's engine failed on lap 13, returning Scheckter to the lead with teammate Gilles Villeneuve slotting into 2nd. Under Ferrari team instructions, Villeneuve loyally acted as rearguard, shielding Scheckter from Clay Regazzoni. Scheckter crossed the finish line 0.46 seconds ahead of Villeneuve to win the Italian Grand Prix, mathematically clinching the **1979 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship** and sealing the **World Constructors' Championship** for Scuderia Ferrari! The Tifosi invaded the track in an overwhelming sea of red flags and flares.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Jody Scheckter became South Africa's only Formula One World Champion. This was Ferrari's final World Drivers' Championship for 21 years until Michael Schumacher in 2000.
