## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LIGIER GRAND SLAM & FIRST-LAP PILEUP: Jacques Laffite qualified on pole in the ground-effect Ligier JS11. On lap 1, John Watson and Jody Scheckter collided entering the fast chicane, triggering a massive eight-car pileup that brought out the RED FLAG. At the restart, Laffite took the lead and led all 53 laps to achieve a Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap), winning by 14.94 seconds over Carlos Reutemann (Lotus 79).
