## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
FERRARI 312T4 DEBUT & VILLENEUVE WIN: Scuderia Ferrari debuted their radical new ground-effect car, the 312T4, designed by Mauro Forghieri. Jean-Pierre Jabouille took pole in the turbocharged Renault RS01. On lap 2, a violent cloudburst struck, red-flagging the race. At the restart, Villeneuve gambled on wet tires while teammate Scheckter pitted early for slicks. When Villeneuve pitted for slicks on lap 15, Scheckter led, but severe front tire graining forced Scheckter to pit again on lap 52, handing victory to Gilles Villeneuve by 3.42 seconds over Scheckter for a sensational Ferrari 1-2 on debut!
