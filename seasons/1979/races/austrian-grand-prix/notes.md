## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALAN JONES BACK-TO-BACK: René Arnoux took pole position for Renault, but Gilles Villeneuve made a flying start from 5th to lead into the first corner. Alan Jones stalked Villeneuve in his Williams FW07 and swept past into the lead on lap 11. Jones controlled the high-speed sweepers to take his second consecutive victory, winning by 3.01 seconds over Gilles Villeneuve and Jacques Laffite. Scheckter finished 5th.
