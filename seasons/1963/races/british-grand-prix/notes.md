# 1963 British Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XVI RAC British Grand Prix
- **Round**: 5 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Saturday, 20 July 1963
- **Location**: Silverstone Circuit, Silverstone, Northamptonshire, United Kingdom
- **Circuit Length**: 4.711 km (2.927 miles)
- **Distance**: 82 laps (386.302 km / 240.037 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:34.400
- **Fastest Lap**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156/63) - 1:36.000 (Lap 3)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:14:09.600 (173.076 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 82 laps
  2. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 82 laps (+25.800s)
  3. Graham Hill (BRM) - 82 laps (+37.600s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Clark's Fourth Consecutive Win**: Before 110,000 partisan British spectators at Silverstone, Jim Clark drove a commanding lights-to-flag race, leading all 82 laps in his Lotus 25 to claim his fourth consecutive Grand Prix victory.
- **Surtees Pushes Ferrari to 2nd**: John Surtees hounded Clark in the opening laps, setting the fastest lap of the race (1:36.000) and finishing 2nd for Ferrari.
- **Hill's Fuel Starvation**: Graham Hill ran out of fuel on the final lap while in 2nd place, coasting across the line to be classified 3rd behind Surtees.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, dry British summer weather; clean asphalt throughout.
