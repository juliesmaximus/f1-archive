# 1963 Dutch Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XI Grote Prijs van Nederland
- **Round**: 3 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 23 June 1963
- **Location**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort, Netherlands
- **Circuit Length**: 4.193 km (2.605 miles)
- **Distance**: 80 laps (335.440 km / 208.433 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:31.600
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:33.700 (Lap 56)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:08:13.700 (156.908 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 80 laps
  2. Dan Gurney (Brabham-Climax) - 79 laps (+1 lap)
  3. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 79 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Clark Laps the Entire Field**: In another display of sheer dominance, Jim Clark qualified on pole, set the fastest lap, led every single lap, and crossed the line having lapped the entire field.
- **Second Grand Slam of the Season**: Clark took maximum points, extending his championship lead.
- **Dan Gurney's Second Podium**: Dan Gurney drove strongly in the Brabham BT7 to claim 2nd place, ahead of John Surtees' Ferrari.
- **BRM Engine Troubles**: Both factory BRM cars of Graham Hill and Richie Ginther suffered engine failures.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Sunny, warm North Sea seaside weather; dry sand-swept track.
