# 1963 Mexican Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: II Gran Premio de México
- **Round**: 9 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 27 October 1963
- **Location**: Magdalena Mixhuca, Mexico City, Mexico
- **Circuit Length**: 5.000 km (3.107 miles)
- **Distance**: 65 laps (325.000 km / 201.946 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:58.800
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:58.100 (Lap 52)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:09:52.100 (150.157 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 65 laps
  2. Jack Brabham (Brabham-Climax) - 65 laps (+1:41.100)
  3. Richie Ginther (BRM) - 65 laps (+1:54.700)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Inaugural Championship Mexican Grand Prix**: The first Mexican Grand Prix to be included in the official FIA Formula One World Championship, held at the newly constructed Magdalena Mixhuca parkland track at 2,285 meters (7,500 ft) elevation.
- **Clark Equals Ascari's Record with Grand Slam**: Jim Clark took pole, set fastest lap, and led every lap to take his 6th victory of the season, equalling Alberto Ascari's 1952 all-time record for most wins in a single World Championship season.
- **First Podium for Brabham Constructor**: Jack Brabham finished 2nd, marking the first-ever World Championship podium finish for a chassis bearing the Brabham name (Brabham BT7).
- **Altitude Challenges**: The thin air at Mexico City reduced naturally aspirated engine power by over 20%, forcing teams to re-jet carburetors and adjust fuel mixtures.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Clear, warm high-altitude autumn weather; completely dry asphalt throughout.
