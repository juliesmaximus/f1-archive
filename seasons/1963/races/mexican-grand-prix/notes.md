## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Inaugural Mexican Championship Grand Prix: Magdalena Mixhuca hosted its first official World Championship round, testing cars at extreme 2,240 m (7,349 ft) altitude where thin air reduced engine horsepower by nearly 25%.
- Clark Dominant Grand Slam: Jim Clark mastered the thin air, taking pole position (1:58.8), setting fastest lap (1:58.1), leading all 65 laps, and winning by 1 minute 41.1 seconds over Jack Brabham.
- Equal Record 6 Wins: With his 6th victory of the season, Clark equalled the all-time single-season wins record held by Alberto Ascari (1952) and Juan Manuel Fangio (1954).
- Local Hero Moisés Solana Debut: Mexican driver Moisés Solana made his World Championship debut in a customer BRM, qualifying 11th and finishing 11th.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First official World Championship Mexican Grand Prix.
- Highest altitude race in Formula One World Championship history: 2,240 m (7,349 ft).
- Jim Clark's 3rd Grand Slam of the 1963 season.
