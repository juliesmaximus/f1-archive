# 1963 Italian Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXXIV Gran Premio d'Italia
- **Round**: 7 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 8 September 1963
- **Location**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (5.750 km Road Course), Monza, Italy
- **Circuit Length**: 5.750 km (3.573 miles)
- **Distance**: 86 laps (494.500 km / 307.268 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156/63) - 1:37.300
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:38.900 (Lap 60)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:24:19.600 (205.575 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 86 laps
  2. Richie Ginther (BRM) - 86 laps (+1:35.000)
  3. Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax) - 85 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Jim Clark Clinches World Championship**: With his 5th victory of the season, 27-year-old Scottish farmer Jim Clark clinched his first Formula One World Drivers' Championship, and secured Team Lotus's first International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with three rounds still remaining.
- **Surtees' Engine Agony**: Home hero John Surtees started on pole in the Ferrari and engaged in a furious slipstreaming duel with Clark and Hill until his engine blew on lap 17.
- **Hill Clutch Failure**: Defending champion Graham Hill led before retiring with clutch failure on lap 50.
- **Safety Enhancements**: Monza organizers permanently restricted Formula One to the 5.750 km road course, removing the banked oval after drivers and constructors boycotted its rough surface.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, sunny Italian autumn day; fast and dry road circuit.
