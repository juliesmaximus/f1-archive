## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- John Surtees Maiden F1 Win: Motorcycle World Champion John Surtees achieved his FIRST CAREER FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX VICTORY, driving the redesigned Ferrari 156 Aero to win by 1 minute 17.5 seconds over Jim Clark.
- Clark Engine Misfire: Jim Clark took pole position with a record 8:45.8 and led early, but an engine cylinder misfire dropped him to 7 cylinders, forcing him to nurse his car home in 2nd place.
- Horrific Crash for Willy Mairesse: On lap 2 at Flugplatz, Ferrari driver Willy Mairesse crashed heavily, being thrown from his car and suffering severe internal and leg injuries that effectively ended his Formula One career.
- Ferrari's Sole 1963 Win: This was Scuderia Ferrari's only victory of the 1963 Formula One season.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship Grand Prix victory for John Surtees.
- Ferrari's first victory since the 1961 Italian Grand Prix.
