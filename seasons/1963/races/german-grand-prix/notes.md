# 1963 German Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXIV Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Round**: 6 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 4 August 1963
- **Location**: Nürburgring Nordschleife, Nürburg, Germany
- **Circuit Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 miles)
- **Distance**: 15 laps (342.150 km / 212.602 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 8:45.800
- **Fastest Lap**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156/63) - 8:47.000 (Lap 9)
- **Race Winner**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156/63) - 2:13:06.800 (154.218 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 15 laps
  2. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 15 laps (+1:17.500)
  3. Richie Ginther (BRM) - 15 laps (+2:44.900)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Surtees' Historic Maiden Grand Prix Victory**: 7-time motorcycle world champion John Surtees scored his first career Formula One World Championship Grand Prix win, delivering Ferrari's first victory of the 1963 season. Surtees conquered the grueling 22.8 km Nürburgring Nordschleife in commanding style, setting the fastest lap of 8:47.000.
- **Clark's Engine Misfire Heroics**: Jim Clark took pole but his Coventry Climax V8 began dropping cylinders on lap 2, running on 7 and eventually 6 cylinders. Clark nursed his ailing car across the remaining 13 laps, fending off Richie Ginther to preserve 2nd place and 6 valuable points.
- **Tragic Practice Accident**: German privateer driver Willy Heeks crashed in practice, and several drivers suffered severe mechanical failures on the rough Eifel crests.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Clear, warm summer weather; dry track throughout the 15 laps.
