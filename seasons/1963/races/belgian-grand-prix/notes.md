# 1963 Belgian Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXIII Grand Prix de Belgique
- **Round**: 2 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 9 June 1963
- **Location**: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps, Stavelot, Belgium
- **Circuit Length**: 14.120 km (8.774 miles)
- **Distance**: 32 laps (451.840 km / 280.760 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 3:54.100
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 3:58.100 (Lap 16)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:27:47.600 (183.334 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 32 laps
  2. Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax) - 32 laps (+4:54.000)
  3. Dan Gurney (Brabham-Climax) - 31 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **The Rain Miracle of Spa**: Widely regarded by motorsport historians as one of the greatest wet-weather driving masterclasses in Grand Prix history. Starting from 8th on the grid in blinding rain and fog that reduced visibility to near zero, Jim Clark launched into the lead before the end of the opening lap.
- **Clark Laps the Entire Field (Except 2nd)**: Driving with supernatural car control on the flooded 14.1 km circuit, Clark pulled away by up to 20 seconds per lap. By the finish, he had lapped every car in the race except Bruce McLaren, whom he defeated by an astronomical 4 minutes, 54 seconds!
- **Brabham Podium**: Dan Gurney brought his Brabham-Climax home in 3rd place, one lap down.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Torrential thunderstorm, standing water, and low cloud cover; treacherous wet conditions throughout.
