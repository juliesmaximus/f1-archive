# 1963 Round 2: XXIII Grote Prijs van België / XXIII Grand Prix de Belgique - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XXIII Grote Prijs van België / XXIII Grand Prix de Belgique
- **Historic Name Notes**: 23rd Belgian Grand Prix; regarded as one of the greatest wet-weather drives in motorsport history.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps
- **Location**: Francorchamps, Stavelot, Malmedy, Belgium
- **Host Country**: Belgium
- **Track Length**: 14.120 km (8.774 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 410 m (1345 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 32 laps / 451.840 km (280.760 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1963-06-09

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Torrential rain, thunderstorm downpours, thick mist and standing water across the Ardennes.
- **Spectator Attendance**: Over 60,000 spectators shivering in rain gear.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Eau Rouge wooden stands battered by driving rain.

## Verified Key Race Results
- **Pole Position**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 3:54.1
- **Fastest Race Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus 25-Climax) - 3:58.1 (Lap 16)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 2:27:47.600 (9 pts)

## Official Podium Finishers
- **1st** : Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) — 2:27:47.600 [9 pts]
- **2nd** : Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax) — +4:54.000 [6 pts]
- **3rd** : Dan Gurney (Brabham) — +1 Lap [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Jim Clark's Wet Weather Masterclass: Starting 8th on the grid, Clark shot into the lead by Eau Rouge on lap 1. Amidst blinding spray, aquaplaning conditions, and flooded roads, Clark drove with superhuman car control. He lapped the entire field except Bruce McLaren, winning by a staggering 4 MINUTES 54 SECONDS!
- Extreme Fog & Rain: Conditions became so atrocious in the second half of the race that Colin Chapman and BRM team manager Tony Rudd lobbied officials to red-flag the race; officials allowed it to run to completion.
- Willy Mairesse Crash: Local hero Willy Mairesse crashed in the spray, escaping uninjured.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Largest winning margin in modern Grand Prix history: 4 minutes 54.0 seconds (Jim Clark over Bruce McLaren).
- Clark won his 2nd consecutive Belgian Grand Prix.
