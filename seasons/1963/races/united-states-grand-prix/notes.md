# 1963 United States Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: VI United States Grand Prix
- **Round**: 8 of 10 in the 1963 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 6 October 1963
- **Location**: Watkins Glen International, Watkins Glen, New York, USA
- **Circuit Length**: 3.701 km (2.300 miles)
- **Distance**: 110 laps (407.164 km / 253.000 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 1:13.400
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:14.500 (Lap 50)
- **Race Winner**: Graham Hill (BRM P57) - 2:19:22.100 (176.708 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Graham Hill (BRM) - 110 laps
  2. Richie Ginther (BRM) - 110 laps (+34.300s)
  3. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 109 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **BRM 1-2 Sweep at Watkins Glen**: Graham Hill and Richie Ginther scored an emphatic 1-2 victory for British Racing Motors (BRM).
- **Clark's Dead Battery & Charge to 3rd**: Newly crowned champion Jim Clark suffered a flat battery on the starting grid. By the time mechanics pushed his car and installed a new battery, the field had completed a full lap. Clark rejoined in dead last and mounted a breathtaking recovery drive, setting the fastest lap of the race (1:14.500) and carving past the entire field to finish 3rd on the podium.
- **John Surtees Engine Failure**: Surtees took the lead from Hill on lap 7 and led until his engine expired on lap 82.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Crisp, bright autumn sunshine in upstate New York; dry and fast track.
