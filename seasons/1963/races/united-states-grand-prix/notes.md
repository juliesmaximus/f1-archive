## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Graham Hill Watkins Glen Triumph: Graham Hill drove a commanding race from pole position to win by 34.3 seconds over BRM teammate Richie Ginther in another BRM 1-2 sweep.
- Clark Battery Failure at Start: Newly crowned champion Jim Clark stalled on the grid due to a dead battery, was push-started after the field had completed a lap, rejoined in last place, set the fastest lap (1:14.5), and charged to finish 3rd on the podium.
- Surtees Engine Failure: John Surtees led from lap 7 to lap 82 before engine failure ended his race.
