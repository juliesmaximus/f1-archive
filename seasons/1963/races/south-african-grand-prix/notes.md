## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Clark's Record 7th Win & Maximum 54 Points**: Jim Clark led all 85 laps from pole position to take his 7th victory of the season, shattering Alberto Ascari's 11-year-old record for most wins in a single season. Clark finished the 1963 season with the maximum possible score of 54 points (under best-6 counting), having scored 73 gross points across the 10 races.
- **Gurney vs. Hill Duel**: Dan Gurney held off a charging Graham Hill by just 1 second to claim 2nd place in his Brabham BT7.
- **Team Lotus Perfect Season**: Lotus-Climax took their 7th win, finishing the Constructors' Cup with the maximum possible 54 points.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Very warm, breezy Indian Ocean coastal weather; completely dry track throughout.
