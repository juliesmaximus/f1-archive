## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Carlos Reutemann (Ferrari) — 1:45:07.720  
- **Pole Position:** James Hunt  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Hot, humid, dry; 30°C ambient, abrasive melting asphalt  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 110,000 Brazilian spectators  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Curva do Sol grandstands, Arquibancadas da Reta Oposta, and hillside slopes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
REUTEMANN WINS AT INTERLAGOS: James Hunt took pole in the McLaren M23. On a disintegrating track surface, Hunt led until tyre vibrations slowed him. Carlos Reutemann in the Ferrari 312T2 swept into the lead on lap 23 and pulled away to win by 10.71s over Hunt and Niki Lauda (Ferrari 1-3). Local heroes Carlos Pace and Emerson Fittipaldi crashed out on the crumbling surface.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Carlos Reutemann scored his first victory for Scuderia Ferrari.
