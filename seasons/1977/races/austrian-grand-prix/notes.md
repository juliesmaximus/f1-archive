## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Alan Jones (Shadow) — 1:37:16.490  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Damp start with intermittent showers, drying track; 19°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 90,000 spectators across the Styrian hillsides  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent start-finish grandstand and hillside banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
ALAN JONES & SHADOW SHOCK VICTORY: Niki Lauda took pole position in the Ferrari 312T2. In wet-to-drying conditions, Mario Andretti and James Hunt both crashed out with mechanical failures. Australian Alan Jones, driving for the grieving Shadow team (having replaced the late Tom Pryce), drove a magnificent race from 14th on the grid in the Shadow DN8 Ford Cosworth DFV, passing James Hunt and pulling away to score a sensational MAIDEN Grand Prix victory by 20.13 seconds ahead of Niki Lauda and Hans-Joachim Stuck! The victory was so unexpected that Austrian organizers had no recording of the Australian national anthem, playing 'Happy Birthday' on a brass trumpet instead!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Alan Jones became a Grand Prix winner, launching his career toward the 1980 World Championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** First and only Grand Prix victory for the Shadow team.
