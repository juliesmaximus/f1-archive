## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** James Hunt (McLaren) — 1:58:23.267  
- **Pole Position:** James Hunt  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, damp-to-drying track under autumn skies; 11°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 110,000 spectators packing the Watkins Glen hills  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Boot, and Seneca spectator knolls  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HUNT WINS & LAUDA WALKS OUT OF FERRARI: James Hunt took pole position in the McLaren M26. In damp conditions, Hunt drove a brilliant race, fending off Carlos Reutemann's Ferrari by 2.02 seconds to take victory! Jody Scheckter finished 3rd for Wolf. Niki Lauda finished 4th. Immediately following the race, having secured his championship bonus, Lauda shocked Enzo Ferrari and the motorsport world by ANNOUNCING HIS IMMEDIATE DEPARTURE FROM SCUDERIA FERRARI, refusing to drive the final two races in Canada and Japan!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the most acrimonious driver-team splits in Grand Prix history. James Hunt took his second consecutive Watkins Glen victory.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Ferrari signed young Canadian Gilles Villeneuve to replace Lauda for the final two races.
