## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Mario Andretti — 1:42:52.220

- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 26°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstands, Pegaso curve terraces, and Farina hillsides  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
ANDRETTI & LOTUS 78 TOTAL DOMINATION: Mario Andretti produced a dominant Grand Slam in the ground-effect Lotus 78 Ford Cosworth DFV: pole position, fastest lap, led all 84 laps, and won by 15.85 seconds over Carlos Reutemann (Ferrari) and Jody Scheckter (Wolf). Niki Lauda withdrew due to cracked ribs sustained in practice.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Mario Andretti took his second consecutive victory, moving into a three-way championship fight with Scheckter and Lauda.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 84 laps (285.936 km).
