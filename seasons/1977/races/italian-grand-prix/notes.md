### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA CLINCHES 2ND WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: James Hunt took pole in the McLaren M26. In the race, Mario Andretti took command in the ground-effect Lotus 78, pulling away to win by 16.96 seconds ahead of Niki Lauda (Ferrari) and Alan Jones (Shadow). By finishing 2nd, NIKI LAUDA MATHEMATICALLY CLINCHED HIS SECOND FORMULA ONE WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP, and Scuderia Ferrari clinched the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with three rounds remaining! However, behind the scenes, Lauda's relationship with Enzo Ferrari had fractured completely over the team's lack of support in 1976.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Niki Lauda became a double World Champion. Ferrari celebrated their third consecutive Constructors' Championship.
