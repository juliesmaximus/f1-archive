## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** James Hunt (McLaren) — 1:31:51.680  
- **Pole Position:** Mario Andretti  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, bright, clear autumn weather with Mount Fuji visible; 15°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 75,000 Japanese spectators  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Pit straight grandstands facing Mount Fuji and hairpin banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HUNT'S FINAL CAREER WIN & SPECTATOR TRAGEDY: Mario Andretti took pole for Lotus. James Hunt seized the lead at the start in the McLaren M26 and drove an untouchable race to take his 10th and FINAL career Grand Prix victory by 1m 02s over Carlos Reutemann (Ferrari) and Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell P34). Tragic accident on lap 6: Gilles Villeneuve (Ferrari) collided with Ronnie Peterson's Tyrrell at the end of the straight; Villeneuve's Ferrari catapulted through the air, landing in a restricted marshaling zone on the outer embankment. A track marshal and a Japanese photographer were killed, and ten spectators were injured.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
James Hunt's 10th and final career victory. The tragic crash resulted in Japan's 10-year absence from Formula One.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Formula One abandoned Fuji Speedway; the Japanese GP did not return to the calendar until 1987 at Suzuka.
