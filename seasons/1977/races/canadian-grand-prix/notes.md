## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jody Scheckter — 1:40:00.000  
- **Pole Position:** Mario Andretti — 1:11.385  
- **Fastest Lap:** Mario Andretti — 1:13.299  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cool, overcast, dry; 14°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Paddock grandstand, Corner 2 hillside banks, and Moss Corner infield  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
SCHECKTER WINS & VILLENEUVE FERRARI DEBUT: Mario Andretti took pole in the Lotus 78 and led for 60 laps until his engine blew. James Hunt took the lead before colliding with his own teammate Jochen Mass on lap 61! Jody Scheckter swept into the lead in the Wolf WR1 to take his 3rd victory of the season by 6.77s over Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell P34) and Jochen Mass. 27-year-old French-Canadian hero Gilles Villeneuve made his Scuderia Ferrari debut, qualifying 22nd and finishing 12th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Gilles Villeneuve's first race for Ferrari. Walter Wolf Racing secured 2nd place in the World Drivers' Championship with Scheckter.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Final Formula One Grand Prix held at Mosport Park on safety grounds.
