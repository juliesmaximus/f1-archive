## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 1:41:45.930  
- **Pole Position:** Mario Andretti  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Sunny, breezy, dry; 23°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 75,000 spectators across the sand dunes  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main pit straight grandstand and Tarzan hairpin dunes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA WINS AS HUNT & ANDRETTI TANGLE: Mario Andretti took pole for Lotus. On lap 6, Andretti and James Hunt collided at Tarzan corner while battling for the lead; Hunt retired on the spot and Andretti retired with engine failure on lap 14. Niki Lauda seized the lead in the Ferrari 312T2 and drove a masterclass to win by 1.81 seconds over Jacques Laffite (Ligier Matra) and Jody Scheckter (Wolf).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lauda's 3rd win of the season moved him to 63 points, holding a 21-point lead over Scheckter and needing only a podium at Monza to clinch his second World Championship.
