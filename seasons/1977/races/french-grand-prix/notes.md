### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
ANDRETTI PASSES WATSON ON FINAL LAP: The Dijon circuit was lengthened with the new 'Parabolique' loop. Mario Andretti took pole in the Lotus 78. John Watson in the Brabham-Alfa Romeo took the lead at the start and led for 79 of the 80 laps. On the very final lap entering the Courbe de Pouas, Watson's Alfa Romeo engine sputtered, starving for fuel! Mario Andretti swept past to take an astonishing last-lap victory by 1.55 seconds over Watson! James Hunt took 3rd for McLaren, and Niki Lauda was 5th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Mario Andretti took his 3rd win of the season, moving into 2nd in the championship, one point behind Niki Lauda (33 pts vs. 32 pts).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Circuit lengthened from 3.289 km to 3.800 km.
