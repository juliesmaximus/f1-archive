## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Gunnar Nilsson (Team Lotus) — 1:55:05.710  
- **Pole Position:** Mario Andretti  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Wet start, drying track under grey skies; 18°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 65,000 spectators across the Limburg pine woods  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered start-finish grandstand and grass mounds around Terlamen  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
GUNNAR NILSSON'S MAIDEN & SOLE VICTORY: Mario Andretti took pole for Lotus. On lap 1, Andretti collided with John Watson at the first corner, eliminating both! In damp-to-drying conditions, Niki Lauda led in the Ferrari. Swede Gunnar Nilsson in the second Lotus 78 carved through the field on Goodyear tyres, overtaking Lauda on lap 50 and pulling away to take his MAIDEN Formula One victory by 14.19 seconds ahead of Lauda and Ronnie Peterson (Tyrrell P34). Tragically, this would be Nilsson's only Grand Prix win; he passed away from cancer in October 1978.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Gunnar Nilsson became Sweden's second Grand Prix winner. Lauda moved to within one point of Scheckter in the title race.
