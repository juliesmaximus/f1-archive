### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LIGIER & MATRA V12 MAIDEN VICTORY: Mario Andretti took pole in the Lotus 78 and led for 69 laps until a fuel metering failure forced him to pit. French star Jacques Laffite in the stunning blue Ligier JS7, powered by the glorious screaming French Matra 3.0L V12 engine, seized the lead and pulled away to win by 8.44 seconds over Jochen Mass (McLaren) and Carlos Reutemann (Ferrari)! This marked the FIRST ALL-FRENCH VICTORY in Formula One World Championship history: a French chassis (Ligier), French engine (Matra), and French driver (Jacques Laffite)!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Monumental triumph for French motorsport: first victory for Guy Ligier's team and the Matra V12 engine.
