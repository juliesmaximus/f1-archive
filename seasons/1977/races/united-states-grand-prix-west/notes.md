### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MARIO ANDRETTI MAIDEN LONG BEACH WIN: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312T2. Jody Scheckter led from the green flag in the Wolf WR1. For 76 laps, Scheckter, Andretti (Lotus 78), and Lauda ran nose-to-tail in an electrifying three-car street battle. On lap 77, Scheckter suffered a slow front puncture; Mario Andretti dove down the inside into the Queen Mary hairpin to take the lead to the roar of American fans! Andretti won by 0.77s over Lauda, with Scheckter limping home 3rd. Andretti became the FIRST AMERICAN DRIVER TO WIN A FORMULA ONE RACE ON US SOIL!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Historic milestone: first American GP victory by an American in the United States, proving the revolutionary potential of Lotus's ground-effect sidepods.
