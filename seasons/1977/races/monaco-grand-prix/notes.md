### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
SCHECKTER & WOLF WIN MONACO: John Watson took pole for Brabham-Alfa Romeo, but Jody Scheckter launched his Wolf WR1 into the lead into Saint Dévote. Scheckter drove an immaculate race around the Principality, leading all 76 laps to win by 0.09s over a charging Niki Lauda (Ferrari) and Carlos Reutemann (Ferrari 3rd). Watson retired with gearbox failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jody Scheckter took his second win of the season for Walter Wolf Racing, regaining the lead of the World Drivers' Championship with 32 points.
