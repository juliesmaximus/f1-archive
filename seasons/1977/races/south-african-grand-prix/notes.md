### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HORRIFIC TRAGEDY OF TOM PRYCE & LAUDA'S RETURN TO WINNING: James Hunt took pole for McLaren. On lap 22, Renzo Zorzi pulled his Shadow off the main straight with a minor engine fire. Two young marshals crossed the 170 mph straight without authorization carrying heavy 40 lb fire extinguishers. 27-year-old Welsh driver Tom Pryce (Shadow), unsighted behind Hans-Joachim Stuck, struck 19-year-old marshal Jansen van Vuuren at full speed. Van Vuuren was killed instantly; the heavy fire extinguisher struck Pryce's helmet with fatal force, ripping it off and killing him instantly. Pryce's car continued down the straight and collided with Jacques Laffite at Crowthorne. Niki Lauda, unaware of the horror, dominated the race to take his FIRST VICTORY SINCE HIS 1976 CRASH by 5.2s ahead of Jody Scheckter (Wolf) and Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell P34).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the most horrific accidents in motorsport history. Niki Lauda's victory broke his post-accident win drought, taking the championship lead.
