### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE TURBO REVOLUTION BEGINS & HUNT WINS: James Hunt took pole position in the new McLaren M26. In practice, French constructor Renault made motorsport history by debuting the FIRST TURBOCHARGED CAR in Formula One history: the yellow-and-black **Renault RS01** powered by a 1.5-litre turbocharged V6 driven by Jean-Pierre Jabouille. Mocked by rivals as the 'Yellow Teapot' because it regularly retired in clouds of steam, the car initiated the turbo revolution that defined the 1980s. In the race, John Watson led for Brabham until fuel metering failure struck on lap 60. James Hunt swept past to win his home Grand Prix by 18.31s over Niki Lauda (Ferrari) and Gunnar Nilsson (Lotus)! Gilles Villeneuve made his sensational F1 debut for McLaren, qualifying 9th and finishing 11th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Watershed moment in F1 technology: the birth of the Turbo Era. James Hunt's final home Grand Prix victory.
- **Regulatory Framework:** 1.5-litre turbocharged / supercharged engines officially raced under equivalence formula.
