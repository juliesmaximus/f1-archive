- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent concrete stadium grandstands along Curva del Ciervo and pit straight  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
WALTER WOLF RACING WINS ON DEBUT: Reigning champion James Hunt took pole position in the McLaren M23 and led for 31 laps until suspension failure forced him out. In brutal heat that caused extreme driver exhaustion, South African Jody Scheckter in Canadian oil magnate Walter Wolf's brand-new Wolf WR1 Ford Cosworth DFV drove brilliantly to take an astonishing victory on the team's DEBUT race by 0.43 seconds ahead of Carlos Pace (Brabham) and Carlos Reutemann (Ferrari)! This marked only the third time in F1 history that a new constructor won on debut (after Alfa Romeo in 1950 and Mercedes in 1954).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Walter Wolf Racing achieved immediate legend status. Carlos Pace's final career podium before his tragic death in an air crash in March.
