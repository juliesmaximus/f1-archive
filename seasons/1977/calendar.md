# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1977 - Calendar

The 1977 Formula One season was the 28th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 17 championship rounds between January 9 and October 23, 1977—the longest season in Formula One history up to that point.

The 1977 season featured monumental regulatory, technical, and human storylines:
- **Niki Lauda's Second Championship & Ferrari Walkout:** Demonstrating extraordinary mental fortitude, Niki Lauda won three Grands Prix (South Africa, Germany, Netherlands) in the Ferrari 312T2 and clinched his **second World Drivers' Championship** at Monza. Having secured the title and weary of Enzo Ferrari's lack of support following his 1976 accident, Lauda walked out on Ferrari with two races remaining.
- **The Turbocharged Revolution:** At the British Grand Prix at Silverstone, Renault made motorsport history by debuting the **Renault RS01**, powered by a 1.5-litre turbocharged V6 engine driven by Jean-Pierre Jabouille. Dubbed the "Yellow Teapot", this was the **first turbocharged car in Formula One history**, initiating the engine era that would dominate the 1980s.
- **Walter Wolf Racing's Debut Win:** Walter Wolf Racing won on their World Championship debut in Argentina with Jody Scheckter in the Wolf WR1, finishing runner-up in the Drivers' Championship.
- **Ground Effect Emergence:** Colin Chapman's Lotus 78 introduced ground-effect sidepod aerodynamics, carrying Mario Andretti to four victories.
