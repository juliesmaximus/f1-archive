# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1977 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **8** results from the first 9 races and the best **7** results from the final 8 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Austrian Niki Lauda won his second World Drivers' Championship with 72 net points (72 gross), clinching the crown at Monza before leaving Scuderia Ferrari with two races remaining.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **8** results from the first 9 races and best **7** results from the final 8 races counted towards the championship.

Scuderia Ferrari won their fifth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 95 net points (97 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 8 results from rounds 1–9 and best 7 results from rounds 10–17 counted towards both championships.
- **Engine Evolution:** Renault debuted the revolutionary 1.5-litre turbocharged V6 (Renault RS01) at Silverstone, inaugurating Formula One's Turbo Era.
- **Ground Effect:** Colin Chapman and Lotus introduced sidepod ground-effect aerodynamics with the Lotus 78.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Niki Lauda won his second World Championship for Ferrari before walking out of the team with two races remaining.
  - Walter Wolf Racing won on their World Championship debut in Argentina with Jody Scheckter.
  - Mario Andretti became the first American to win a Grand Prix on US soil at Long Beach.
  - Alan Jones scored his maiden Grand Prix victory in Austria for Shadow.
  - Jacques Laffite and Ligier-Matra scored the first all-French Grand Prix victory in Sweden.
  - Tragic death of Tom Pryce at Kyalami.
  - Gilles Villeneuve made his Formula One debut for McLaren at Silverstone and Ferrari at Mosport.
