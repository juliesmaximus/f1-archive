## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **The Imola Safety Watershed:** The tragic deaths of Roland Ratzenberger and Ayrton Senna at the 1994 San Marino Grand Prix triggered the most comprehensive safety overhaul in motorsport history. Chicanes were added to high-speed circuits (Barcelona, Eau Rouge, Estoril, Montreal), front wing endplates raised, diffusers shortened, and wooden skid blocks (planks) mandated beneath car floors.
- **The 10 mm Wooden Plank Rule:** Introduced at Hockenheim, cars had to run a 10 mm beechwood skid block with a maximum 1 mm wear allowance to prevent teams from running dangerous ultra-low ride heights without active suspension.
- **Refuelling Returns:** In-race refuelling was reintroduced for the first time since 1983, creating complex pit stop strategies.
- **Michael Schumacher's 1st Title:** Michael Schumacher became Germany's first Formula 1 World Drivers' Champion, winning the championship by a single point (92 to 91) after a controversial collision with Damon Hill in Adelaide.
