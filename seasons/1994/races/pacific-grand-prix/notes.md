## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Inaugural Pacific Grand Prix at the tight TI Circuit Aida. Senna took his second pole position of the season but was spun around from behind at Turn 1 by Mika Häkkinen and subsequently struck by Nicola Larini's Ferrari, eliminating him immediately. Schumacher dominated to win by 1m15s over Gerhard Berger and Rubens Barrichello.
