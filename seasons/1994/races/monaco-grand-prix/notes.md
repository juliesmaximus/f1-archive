## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 gathered in somber reflection with the first two grid spots left vacant painted with the flags of Brazil and Austria. During Thursday practice, Sauber driver Karl Wendlinger crashed heavily at the Nouvelle Chicane, suffering critical head injuries. Schumacher took pole and led every lap to take his fourth consecutive win. Martin Brundle finished 2nd for McLaren-Peugeot and Gerhard Berger 3rd.
