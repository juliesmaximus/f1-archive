## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna made his Williams-Renault debut, claiming pole position by 0.328s. Michael Schumacher jumped into the lead during refuelling pit stops in the Benetton-Ford. Chasing Schumacher relentlessly, Senna spun out into the gravel at the Junção corner on lap 56. Schumacher won by a full lap over Damon Hill, with Jean Alesi finishing 3rd for Ferrari.
