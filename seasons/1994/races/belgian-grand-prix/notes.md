## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Schumacher took the chequered flag first ahead of Damon Hill. However, post-race scrutineering revealed that the newly introduced wooden skid block under Schumacher's Benetton was worn down to 8.4 mm (below the 9.0 mm minimum legal thickness). Benetton argued the wear was caused by a spin over the kerbs at Pouhon, but the FIA disqualified Schumacher, awarding victory to Damon Hill.
