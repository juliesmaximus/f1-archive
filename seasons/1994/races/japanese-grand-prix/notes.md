## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Damon Hill's greatest career drives. In torrential monsoon rain, Martin Brundle hydroplaned off and struck a track marshal, fracturing the marshal's leg. The race was red-flagged and restarted under aggregate timing. On worn wet tyres, Hill drove with unbelievable courage in blinding spray to beat Schumacher by 3.3 seconds on aggregate time. The victory took the championship to a winner-takes-all finale separated by a single point.
