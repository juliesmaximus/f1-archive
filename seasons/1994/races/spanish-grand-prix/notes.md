## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Damon Hill scored an emotional, cathartic victory for Williams-Renault—their first since Senna's death. Michael Schumacher pulled off one of the greatest technical drives in history: his Benetton gearbox jammed permanently in 5th gear on lap 24 while leading. Schumacher adapted his driving lines, carried extraordinary mid-corner speed, made two pit stops (feathering the clutch to pull away in 5th gear), and still finished 2nd. Mark Blundell finished 3rd for Tyrrell.
