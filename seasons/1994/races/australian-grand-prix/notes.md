## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The dramatic, controversial championship showdown. Nigel Mansell took pole position for Williams, but Schumacher and Hill swept into 1st and 2nd at the start. On lap 36, Schumacher led by a few tenths, ran wide at East Terrace, and clipped the wall. As Hill dived down the inside at the next corner, Schumacher turned in and their cars collided. Schumacher went airborne onto two wheels and into the tyre wall, out of the race. Hill limped to the pits with broken front suspension wishbones and was forced to retire. Michael Schumacher was crowned Germany's first Formula 1 World Drivers' Champion by one point (92 to 91). Nigel Mansell went on to win the race—the 31st and final Grand Prix victory of his career.
