## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Gerhard Berger gave Scuderia Ferrari their first victory in 59 races, breaking a four-year win drought dating back to Spain 1990. During Jos Verstappen's Benetton pit stop, a fuel spray ignited, engulfing the car and pit crew in a massive fireball; Verstappen and crew escaped with burns. Only eight cars finished a race of heavy attrition.
