## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The darkest weekend in modern Formula 1 history. On Friday, Rubens Barrichello suffered a violent airborne crash at the Variante Bassa, escaping with minor injuries. On Saturday qualifying, Austrian driver Roland Ratzenberger was killed instantly when his Simtek suffered front-wing failure at 195 mph at the Villeneuve curva. On Sunday, Ayrton Senna took his 65th and final pole position. On lap 7, following an early safety car restart, Senna's Williams FW16 suffered steering column / bottoming failure and left the track at 190 mph at the Tamburello corner, impacting the concrete retaining wall at 131 mph. Senna suffered fatal head trauma from a broken suspension upright. The tragedy led to a comprehensive overhaul of Formula 1 safety regulations under Prof. Sid Watkins and Max Mosley. Michael Schumacher won the restarted race ahead of Nicola Larini and Mika Häkkinen.
