## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Damon Hill won his home Grand Prix amidst immense regulatory controversy. On the formation lap, Schumacher repeatedly overtook polesitter Hill, violating sporting regulations. Schumacher was issued a 5-second stop-go penalty, but Benetton failed to call him in, resulting in a black flag. Schumacher eventually served a stop-go penalty and crossed the line 2nd, but was subsequently disqualified from the race and handed a two-race ban.
