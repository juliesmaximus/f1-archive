## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The first of Schumacher's two-race ban. Ferrari locked out the front row (Alesi pole, Berger 2nd). Alesi led until a gearbox failure during pit stops, leaving Hill to take victory ahead of Gerhard Berger and Mika Häkkinen, slashing Schumacher's championship lead to 11 points.
