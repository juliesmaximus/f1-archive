## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Sebastian Vettel Historic Fourth Consecutive Title:** Sebastian Vettel joined Juan Manuel Fangio, Alain Prost, and Michael Schumacher as only the fourth driver in Formula 1 history to win four World Drivers' Championships, clinching it at the Indian Grand Prix with three races to spare.
- **Record Nine Consecutive Victories & 13 Single-Season Wins:** Following the summer break, Vettel won nine consecutive Grands Prix (Belgium to Brazil), setting an all-time Formula 1 record for consecutive victories in a single season. His 13 wins across 2013 equalled Michael Schumacher's 2004 all-time record.
- **Red Bull Racing Fourth Consecutive Constructors' Championship:** Red Bull Racing secured their fourth straight Constructors' World Championship with 596 points and 13 victories.
- **End of the 2.4-Litre Naturally Aspirated V8 Era:** The 2013 Brazilian Grand Prix marked the final race for naturally aspirated V8 engines (2006–2013), concluding an iconic auditory chapter of the sport before the introduction of 1.6-litre V6 turbo-hybrid power units in 2014.
- **Mark Webber's Farewell:** Veteran Australian driver Mark Webber retired from Formula 1 after 215 starts, 9 victories, 42 podiums, and 13 pole positions, celebrating on the podium with teammate Vettel in Brazil without his helmet.
