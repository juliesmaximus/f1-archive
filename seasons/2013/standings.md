## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Sebastian Vettel's 9-Win Streak:** Won the final 9 consecutive races of the season (Belgium to Brazil), setting an all-time Formula 1 record.
- **Vettel's 4th Consecutive World Title:** Joined Fangio, Prost, and Schumacher as a 4-time champion, winning 13 races in 2013 (matching Schumacher's 2004 record).
- **The 'Multi 21' Scandal:** Vettel ignored team orders to pass Mark Webber in Malaysia.
- **Silverstone Tyre Crisis:** Four high-speed tyre failures led to mid-season tyre construction changes.
- **Farewell to Naturally Aspirated V8s:** The final season of the 2.4L V8 engine formula.
