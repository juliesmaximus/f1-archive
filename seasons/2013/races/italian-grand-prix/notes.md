## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel conquered Monza from pole position, leading Fernando Alonso by 5.4 seconds despite short-shifting to protect a gearbox issue in the closing laps. The Italian crowd booed Vettel vociferously on the podium, prompting podium interviewer John Surtees to praise Vettel's driving.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari confirmed Kimi Räikkönen would return to partner Alonso in 2014, replacing Felipe Massa.
