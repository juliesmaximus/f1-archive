## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The end of an era. Sebastian Vettel scored his 9th consecutive victory (an all-time unbroken Formula 1 record) and his 13th win of 2013 (matching Michael Schumacher's 2004 record). In his 215th and final Formula 1 Grand Prix, Mark Webber finished 2nd to complete a Red Bull 1-2. On the slow-down lap, Webber removed his helmet and balaclava to feel the wind and hear the roaring crowd in an iconic farewell. Fernando Alonso completed the podium in 3rd. This was the final race for naturally aspirated V8 engines before the turbo-hybrid era.
