## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Tactical masterclass by Kimi Räikkönen. While Red Bull and Ferrari were forced into aggressive three-stop tyre strategies due to severe Pirelli degradation, Räikkönen managed his tyres with sublime smoothness to execute a 2-stop strategy, winning by 12.4 seconds over Fernando Alonso and Sebastian Vettel.
