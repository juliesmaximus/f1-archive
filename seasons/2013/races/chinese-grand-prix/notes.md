## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso delivered an imperious performance, taking the lead on lap 5 and controlling the race on a clinical three-stop strategy to win by 10.1 seconds over Kimi Räikkönen (who finished 2nd despite heavy nose cone damage from a clash with Pérez). Hamilton held off a charging Vettel by 0.2 seconds at the line.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Mark Webber started from the pitlane, collided with Vergne, and lost his right-rear wheel on track.
