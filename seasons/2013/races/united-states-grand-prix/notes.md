## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
History was made in Austin! Sebastian Vettel broke Alberto Ascari and Michael Schumacher's record by scoring his 8th consecutive Grand Prix victory in a single season. Leading every lap from pole, Vettel beat Romain Grosjean's Lotus by 6.2 seconds. Mark Webber finished 3rd.
