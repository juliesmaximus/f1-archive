## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso's 32nd and final career Formula 1 victory! Starting 5th, Alonso executed an audacious pass around the outside of both Hamilton and Räikkönen through Turn 3 on lap 1. Ferrari pulled off an immaculate four-stop tyre strategy, Alonso winning by 9.3 seconds ahead of Kimi Räikkönen and teammate Felipe Massa before an ecstatic home crowd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Severe tyre degradation saw Mercedes tumble from a front-row lockout to 6th (Rosberg) and 12th (Hamilton).
