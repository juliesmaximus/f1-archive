## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The explosive 'Multi 21' scandal. Fernando Alonso crashed on lap 2 after damaging his front wing against Vettel. Red Bull controlled the race 1-2. After the final pitstops, team principal Christian Horner issued the strict radio instruction 'Multi 21' (Car 2 Webber ahead of Car 1 Vettel) to turn down engines and hold position. Vettel blatantly defied orders, attacking Webber furiously wheel-to-wheel through Turns 1 and 2, passing him on lap 46 to take victory. In the green room, an incandescent Webber confronted Vettel with the immortal words: 'Multi 21, Seb. Yeah, Multi 21.' Lewis Hamilton mistakenly pulled into the McLaren pit box instead of Mercedes before finishing 3rd.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
