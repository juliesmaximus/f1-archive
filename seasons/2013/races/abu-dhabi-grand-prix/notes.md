## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Vettel equaled Alberto Ascari's 1952/1953 record of 7 consecutive Grand Prix victories. Vettel blew past pole-sitter Webber at Turn 1 and checked out, winning by 30.8 seconds ahead of Webber and Rosberg. Kimi Räikkönen started from the back after a floor deflection penalty and retired on lap 1 after steering damage.
