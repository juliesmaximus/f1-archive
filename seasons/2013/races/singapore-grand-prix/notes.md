## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Arguably the most dominant performance of Sebastian Vettel's entire career. Scoring a supreme Grand Slam, Vettel pulled out a staggering 2-seconds-per-lap advantage after a Safety Car restart, winning by 32.6 seconds over Fernando Alonso. On the slow-down lap, Mark Webber hitched a ride on the sidepod of Alonso's Ferrari after his car caught fire; stewards handed Webber a 10-place grid penalty for walking onto an active track.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Webber's 'taxi ride' penalty caused widespread outrage among fans who felt the FIA was over-policing the sport.
