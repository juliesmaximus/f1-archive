## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel scored his maiden victory in Canada with an untouchable lights-to-flag drive, winning by 14.4 seconds over Fernando Alonso. Alonso produced a ferocious charge, passing Mark Webber and Lewis Hamilton in the closing laps to claim 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Tragic post-race accident: track marshal Mark Robinson was fatally run over by a recovery crane while retrieving Esteban Gutiérrez's Sauber.
