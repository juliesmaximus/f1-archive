## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg achieved an emotional victory on the streets where he grew up, exactly 30 years after his father Keke Rosberg won the Monaco Grand Prix in 1983. Rosberg led every single lap from pole position, surviving two safety cars and a red flag (after Pastor Maldonado's violent crash at Tabac).

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
News broke that Mercedes had conducted a secret, unauthorized 1,000 km tyre test with Pirelli at Barcelona following the Spanish GP; Ferrari and Red Bull launched a formal protest.
