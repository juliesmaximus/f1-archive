## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The Silverstone tyre explosion crisis. In a terrifying afternoon, four drivers suffered explosive left-rear tyre delaminations at 170+ mph: Lewis Hamilton (leading from pole), Felipe Massa, Jean-Éric Vergne, and Sergio Pérez. Sebastian Vettel looked set to win until his gearbox failed on lap 41. Nico Rosberg held off a ferocious charge from Mark Webber to take victory by 0.7 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The GPDA threatened a driver boycott unless Pirelli addressed the tyre safety crisis; Pirelli immediately replaced steel belts with Kevlar for future races.
