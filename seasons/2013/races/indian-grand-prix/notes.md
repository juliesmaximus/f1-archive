## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel became a four-time World Champion! Pitting from soft tyres on lap 2, Vettel carved through the field with clinical precision, regaining the lead on lap 29 to win by 29.8 seconds ahead of Nico Rosberg and Romain Grosjean. At 26 years, 116 days, Sebastian Vettel became the youngest four-time World Champion in Formula 1 history! After taking the chequered flag, Vettel brought his RB9 to the start-finish straight and spun smoke-filled celebratory donuts before kneeling down and bowing to his car in an immortal image. Red Bull Racing sealed their fourth consecutive Constructors' Championship.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Stewards reprimanded Vettel and fined Red Bull €25,000 for the donut celebration, sparking global criticism from fans who loved the display.
