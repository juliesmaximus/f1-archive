## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel produced a devastating display. Passing pole-sitter Nico Rosberg on lap 3, Vettel pulled away to win by 9.1 seconds. Lotus repeated their 2012 double-podium with Räikkönen 2nd and Grosjean 3rd. Fernando Alonso suffered a bizarre mechanical failure: his DRS flap stuck wide open; after pitting to force it shut, he opened it again, jamming it open a second time and finishing 8th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Paul di Resta led laps for Force India, finishing an impressive 4th.
