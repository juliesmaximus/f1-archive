## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel scored back-to-back Grand Slams (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap), surviving two Safety Car periods. Mark Webber was wiped out when Adrian Sutil spun into his Red Bull, setting the car ablaze. A track Jeep entered the circuit ahead of the Formula 1 cars before the Safety Car had deployed, creating a bizarre sight.
