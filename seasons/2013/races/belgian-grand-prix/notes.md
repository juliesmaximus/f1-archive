## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Beginning of the legendary 9-race winning streak! Sebastian Vettel slipstreamed pole-sitter Lewis Hamilton down the Kemmel Straight on lap 1 to take the lead. Vettel checked out, cruising to victory by 16.8 seconds ahead of Fernando Alonso. Kimi Räikkönen's record 27-race points-scoring streak ended due to a brake failure caused by a visor tear-off strip.
