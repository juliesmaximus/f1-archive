## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel finally won his home German Grand Prix for the first time in his career. Vettel withstood relentless, nerve-shredding pressure over the final 10 laps from Kimi Räikkönen's Lotus, crossing the line just 1.0 second ahead. A loose wheel from Mark Webber's car struck FOM cameraman Paul Allen in the pitlane, hospitalizing him.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
