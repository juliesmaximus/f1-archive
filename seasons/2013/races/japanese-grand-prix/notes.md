## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Romain Grosjean made an electrifying start from 4th to lead the race for Lotus. Red Bull split strategies: Webber ran a 3-stop while Vettel executed a 2-stop. Vettel passed Grosjean into Turn 1 on lap 41 to take his 5th consecutive win, leading a Red Bull 1-2 ahead of Webber.
