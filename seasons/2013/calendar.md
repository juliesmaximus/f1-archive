## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Vanity Panels:** Permitted non-structural laminates to smooth stepped noses.
- **Front Wing Rigidity:** Stricter deflection tests to eliminate flexi-wings.
- **Mid-Season Tyre Construction Change:** Pirelli switched from steel to Kevlar belt internal structures following Silverstone tyre delaminations.
- **Final V8 Year:** Concluding season of the 2.4L naturally aspirated V8 formula.
