## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Drama and heartbreak in the Principality. Jarno Trulli claimed a sensational maiden pole position and drove a masterclass to take his sole career Formula 1 victory, resisting intense pressure from Jenson Button by 0.4 seconds. Michael Schumacher's winning streak came to a shocking end inside the tunnel under Safety Car conditions on lap 46, when he braked hard to warm his brakes and was rear-ended by Juan Pablo Montoya, smashing the Ferrari's left-front suspension into the wall.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The tunnel clash sparked furious recriminations between Ferrari and Williams; stewards ruled no driver was solely to blame.
