## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A dramatic and terrifying race at the Brickyard. On lap 9, Ralf Schumacher suffered a catastrophic rear-tyre deflation at 190 mph entering the banked Turn 13, spinning backward into the concrete wall. Ralf suffered two fractured vertebrae and was sidelined for three months. Michael Schumacher overtook Barrichello on the restart to win. Zsolt Baumgartner scored Minardi's first World Championship point in two years in 8th place.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Montoya was black-flagged on lap 57 for abandoning his stalled car on the grid and jumping into the spare car after the 15-second signal.
