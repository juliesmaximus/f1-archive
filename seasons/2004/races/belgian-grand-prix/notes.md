## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The 700th Grand Prix in Scuderia Ferrari history and a legendary milestone. In a chaotic, incident-packed race featuring three safety car periods, Kimi Räikkönen produced a masterclass from 10th on the grid to score McLaren's only win of 2004. Michael Schumacher finished 2nd, which was enough to mathematically clinch his historic 7th World Drivers' Championship—at the circuit where he made his debut in 1991 and scored his first win in 1992.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Multi-car pileups on laps 1 and 30 eliminated Webber, Sato, Bruni, Pantano, and Button (after a massive 190 mph blowout on Kemmel Straight).
