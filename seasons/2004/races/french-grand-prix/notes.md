## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the greatest tactical masterstrokes in Formula 1 history. Fernando Alonso led from pole in his Renault. Recognizing they could not pass on track, Ross Brawn and Ferrari switched Michael Schumacher to an unprecedented four-stop strategy mid-race. Schumacher drove 70 laps of qualifying sprint laps, establishing a 22-second lead to pit a fourth time and rejoin ahead of Alonso to win by 8.3 seconds.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
