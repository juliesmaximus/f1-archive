## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Schumacher won his home Grand Prix for the third time. Kimi Räikkönen challenged relentlessly until his rear wing disintegrated at 200 mph into the Nordkurve, sending the McLaren violently into the tyre barriers. Jenson Button drove a heroic race with a loose helmet strap to finish 2nd from 13th on the grid.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
McLaren rear-wing structural failure prompted an immediate FIA safety inquiry.
