## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Schumacher fended off intense pressure from Juan Pablo Montoya, taking victory by 5.0 seconds. Jenson Button drove a stellar race to finish 3rd, scoring the maiden podium of his Formula 1 career for BAR-Honda.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Mark Webber stalled from 2nd on the grid in his Jaguar, triggering a drive-through penalty for jumping the restart.
