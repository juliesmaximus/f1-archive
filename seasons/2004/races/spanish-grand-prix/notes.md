## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Michael Schumacher matched Nigel Mansell's 1992 record of winning the opening five races of a season. Schumacher overcame a cracked exhaust header that was cooking the Ferrari's rear bodywork, nursing the car home 13 seconds ahead of Barrichello and Jarno Trulli.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
A track spectator (Jimmy Jump) ran across the main straight on the warm-up lap before being wrestled down by marshals.
