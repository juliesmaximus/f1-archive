## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Inaugural Chinese Grand Prix at the spectacular £150M Hermann Tilke facility shaped like the Chinese character 'shang' (上). Rubens Barrichello won from pole position, resisting Jenson Button and Kimi Räikkönen. Michael Schumacher endured a nightmare weekend: spun in qualifying, started from the pitlane, spun in the race, collided with Christian Klien, suffered a puncture, and finished 12th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Jacques Villeneuve made his F1 return replacing Jarno Trulli at Renault for the final three races, finishing 11th.
