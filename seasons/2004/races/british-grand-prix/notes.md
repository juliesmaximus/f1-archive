## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen took pole in the debut of McLaren's extensively redesigned MP4-19B chassis. Schumacher used a superior one-stop pit strategy to jump Räikkönen and secure victory by 2.1 seconds. Jarno Trulli suffered a massive crash at Bridge when his rear suspension failed.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Safety Car on lap 40 bunched the field after Trulli's high-speed barrel roll, setting up a frantic sprint to the flag.
