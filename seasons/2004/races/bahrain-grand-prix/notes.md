## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic inaugural Grand Prix in the Middle East. Ferrari dominated with a crushing 1-2 finish, Schumacher leading Barrichello by 1.3 seconds, with Jenson Button scoring back-to-back podiums in 3rd. Circuit was praised for modern paddock facilities and desert architecture.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Blowing sand created variable grip off the racing line, leading to several spins in practice.
