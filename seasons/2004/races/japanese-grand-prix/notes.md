## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Michael Schumacher (Ferrari)
- **Pole Position:** Michael Schumacher (Ferrari) - 1:33.542
- **Fastest Lap:** Rubens Barrichello (Ferrari) - 1:32.730 (Lap 30)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Typhoon Ma-on forced the complete cancellation of Saturday track activity, forcing qualifying to Sunday morning for the first time in F1 history. Michael Schumacher took pole Sunday morning and led a commanding Ferrari-Schumacher family 1-2 ahead of brother Ralf, scoring his record-setting 13th victory of 2004.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Historic first Sunday morning qualifying session due to the typhoon force winds on Saturday.
