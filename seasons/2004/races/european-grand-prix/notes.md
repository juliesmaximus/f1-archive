## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Schumacher rebounded with a devastating Grand Slam, leading from pole and winning by 17.9 seconds over Barrichello. Takuma Sato started on the front row and fought furiously for the podium, executing a bold lunge on Barrichello at Turn 1 that resulted in broken wing and eventual engine failure.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ralf Schumacher collided with teammate Juan Pablo Montoya and Cristiano da Matta at Turn 1 on the opening lap.
