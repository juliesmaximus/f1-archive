## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Jenson Button claimed his maiden career pole position with an astonishing lap for BAR-Honda, leading the first 8 laps. Schumacher unleashed devastating qualifying-style in-laps to leapfrog Button during the pit cycles, winning by 9.7 seconds to make it four wins from four races.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Montoya and Schumacher banged wheels at Tosa hairpin on lap 9; Montoya accused Schumacher of running him off the road, but stewards deemed it a racing incident.
