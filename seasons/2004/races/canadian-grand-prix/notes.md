## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Michael Schumacher (Ferrari)
- **Pole Position:** Ralf Schumacher (Williams-BMW) - 1:12.275
- **Fastest Lap:** Rubens Barrichello (Ferrari) - 1:13.622 (Lap 68)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Schumacher won from 6th on the grid via a two-stop strategy. Ralf Schumacher crossed the line 2nd ahead of Barrichello and Montoya. However, post-race scrutineering triggered an earthquake: both Williams-BMWs (Ralf and Montoya) and both Toyotas (Cristiano da Matta and Olivier Panis) were disqualified for illegal front brake cooling ducts that exceeded dimensional limits.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Williams and Toyota brake duct disqualification promoted Rubens Barrichello to 2nd and Jenson Button to 3rd.
