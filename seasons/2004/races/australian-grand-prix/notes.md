## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ominous display of absolute Ferrari supremacy. Michael Schumacher and Rubens Barrichello obliterated the opposition, crossing the finish line in a crushed formation 34 seconds clear of 3rd-placed Fernando Alonso. Schumacher set an all-time race lap record of 1:24.125.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
New single-engine per weekend rule came into force; several teams struggled with gearbox and hydraulic reliability.
