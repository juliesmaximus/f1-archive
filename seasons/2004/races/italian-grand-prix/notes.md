## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Miracle Ferrari 1-2 comeback on home soil. Barrichello started on wet tyres and fell to 9th when he pitted for slicks on lap 5. Michael Schumacher spun on the opening lap and dropped to 15th. In an astonishing display of relentless speed, both Ferraris carved through the entire field, Barrichello taking victory and Schumacher 2nd ahead of Jenson Button.
