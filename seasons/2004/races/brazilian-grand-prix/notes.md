## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Juan Pablo Montoya scored a farewell victory for Williams-BMW in his final race for the team before moving to McLaren. Montoya triumphed in a thrilling tactical duel against future teammate Kimi Räikkönen, crossing the line just 1.0 second ahead. Rubens Barrichello took pole but finished 3rd, ending his Interlagos curse with a podium finish.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Michael Schumacher started 18th after an engine change penalty and spun during the damp opening laps, fighting back to 7th.
