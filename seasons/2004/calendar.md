## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **One Engine Per Weekend:** Introduced to curb spiraling costs; an unscheduled engine change incurred a 10-place grid penalty.
- **Aerodynamic Changes:** Rear wing limited to two elements instead of three; engine cover fin dimensions increased.
- **Minimum Weight:** 605 kg (including driver and fluids).
- **Calendar Expansion:** 18 races, the longest season in F1 history to that date.
