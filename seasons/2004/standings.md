## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Michael Schumacher's 7th World Championship:** Schumacher clinched his 7th and final World Drivers' Championship, setting an all-time record of 13 victories in a single season (equaled by Sebastian Vettel in 2013 and later surpassed by Max Verstappen).
- **Ferrari F2004 Dominance:** Scuderia Ferrari secured their 6th consecutive Constructors' Championship with 262 points, winning 15 of 18 Grands Prix.
- **Inaugural Middle East & China Grands Prix:** Historic calendar expansion to the Bahrain International Circuit (Sakhir) and Shanghai International Circuit.
- **BAR-Honda Breakthrough:** BAR finished 2nd in the Constructors' Championship with Jenson Button scoring 10 podiums.
