- **Circuit Full Name**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (10 km Combined Banking)
- **Location**: Monza, Lombardy, Italy

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The British Constructors' Boycott: Monza organizers chose to run the high-speed 10.000 km combined layout, utilizing the brutal concrete banking (*sopraelevata*). The British teams (Cooper, Lotus, BRM) declared the track dangerously rough and structurally unsound for lightweight mid-engine cars and unanimously boycotted the Grand Prix.
- Phil Hill Wins for Ferrari: With the field reduced to factory Ferraris, privateers, and Formula Two cars, American Phil Hill dominated from pole, winning by over two minutes over Richie Ginther and Willy Mairesse in a Ferrari 1-2-3 sweep.
- The Last Win for a Front-Engine Car: This was the HISTORIC LAST-EVER FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX VICTORY FOR A FRONT-ENGINE CAR (Ferrari Dino 246), bringing the curtain down on the front-engine era in Formula One.
- First American-Born GP Winner: Phil Hill became the FIRST AMERICAN-BORN DRIVER TO WIN A FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX (outside the Indianapolis 500).

## Records Set or Equalled
- Last Formula One World Championship Grand Prix victory for a front-engine car (Ferrari Dino 246).
- First American-born driver to win a Formula One Grand Prix (Phil Hill).
- Maiden World Championship victory and pole position for future World Champion Phil Hill.
