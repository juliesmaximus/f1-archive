# 1960 Italian Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXXI Gran Premio d'Italia
- **Round**: 9 of 10 in the 1960 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 4 September 1960
- **Location**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (10 km Combined Road + Banked Oval Circuit), Monza, Italy
- **Circuit Length**: 10.000 km (6.214 miles)
- **Distance**: 50 laps (500.000 km / 310.686 miles)

- **Pole Position**: Phil Hill (Ferrari Dino 246) - 2:41.400
- **Fastest Lap**: Phil Hill (Ferrari Dino 246) - 2:43.600 [0 pts]
- **Race Winner**: Phil Hill (Ferrari Dino 246) - 2:21:09.200 (212.535 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 50 laps
  2. Richie Ginther (Ferrari) - 50 laps (+2:27.600)
  3. Willy Mairesse (Ferrari) - 49 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **British Teams Boycott**: When the Automobile Club of Milan decided to use the high-speed concrete banking for the combined 10 km track, the British Formula One Constructors Association (Cooper, Lotus, BRM) unanimously boycotted the event, declaring the rough banking lethally dangerous and fragile for lightweight mid-engine cars.
- **Historic First for an American Driver**: Phil Hill dominated the race from pole, becoming the first American driver to win a World Championship Grand Prix overseas.
- **Last Victory for a Front-Engine Car**: Phil Hill's win in the front-engine Ferrari Dino 246 marks the final World Championship Grand Prix victory for a front-engine car in Formula One history.
- **Ferrari 1-2-3 Sweep**: Ferrari swept the podium against privateer entries, with Richie Ginther 2nd and Willy Mairesse 3rd.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Very hot, sunny Italian early-autumn day; dry, violent concrete banking.
