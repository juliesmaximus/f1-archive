# 1960 Argentine Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: VII Gran Premio de la República Argentina
- **Round**: 1 of 10 in the 1960 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 7 February 1960
- **Location**: Autódromo 17 de Octubre, Buenos Aires, Argentina
- **Circuit Length**: 3.912 km (2.431 miles)
- **Distance**: 80 laps (312.960 km / 194.464 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Cooper-Climax T51) - 1:36.900
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Cooper-Climax T51) - 1:38.900 (Lap 37) [0 pts - FL point abolished in 1960]
- **Race Winner**: Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax T51) - 2:17:49.500 (136.248 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax) - 80 laps
  2. Cliff Allison (Ferrari) - 80 laps (+26.300s)
  3. Maurice Trintignant & Stirling Moss (Cooper-Climax) - 80 laps (+36.600s) [0 championship points awarded for shared drive]

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **McLaren's Second Consecutive Victory**: Bruce McLaren carried over his winning momentum from Sebring 1959, taking victory in the Buenos Aires heat to lead the World Championship.
- **Shared Drive Ineligibility**: Stirling Moss suffered a broken rear suspension on lap 41 while leading from pole. He took over teammate Maurice Trintignant's car #36 on lap 46, driving it across the finish line in 3rd place. However, because shared drive points were abolished, neither Moss nor Trintignant received points; 4th place finisher Carlos Menditeguy took the 3 points for 4th.
- **Scoring System Evolution**: 1960 marked the debut of the 8-6-4-3-2-1 points system (expanding scoring to 6th place) and the removal of the 1-point fastest lap bonus.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Oppressively hot, humid Buenos Aires summer conditions with temperatures above 35°C; completely dry track.
