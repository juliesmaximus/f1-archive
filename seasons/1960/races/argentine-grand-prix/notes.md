## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Scoring Regulation Change: Starting in 1960, the FIA abolished the 1 point awarded for fastest lap, introducing points for 6th place instead: 8-6-4-3-2-1 to the top 6 finishers.
- Bruce McLaren Wins Back-to-Back: Bruce McLaren took his second consecutive World Championship Grand Prix victory (following Sebring 1959), winning by 26.3 seconds over Cliff Allison's Ferrari.
- Moss Suspension Failure: Stirling Moss led from pole position before his rear suspension collapsed on lap 41. He took over Maurice Trintignant's Cooper, but shared car points were no longer awarded under new 1960 rules.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First race under the new 8-6-4-3-2-1 championship points system.
- Abolition of fastest lap bonus point and shared car championship points.
