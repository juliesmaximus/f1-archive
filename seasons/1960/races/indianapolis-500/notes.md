## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **The Greatest Duel in Indy 500 History**: Jim Rathmann and defending champion Rodger Ward staged what is universally considered the greatest two-car duel in the history of the Indianapolis 500. The two drivers traded the lead an extraordinary 14 times between laps 120 and 197, running inches apart at over 140 mph.
- **Tire Wear Decider**: With 3 laps remaining, Ward noticed the white fabric cords showing through his right-front tire and backed off to protect his safety, allowing Rathmann to pull away and take victory by 12.75 seconds.
- **Final World Championship Indy 500**: 1960 was the eleventh and final running of the Indianapolis 500 as an official round of the FIA Formula One World Championship (1950–1960).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Sunny, hot Midwestern day; dry track throughout the 500 miles.
