## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Epic 29-Lead-Change Duel: Jim Rathmann and Rodger Ward engaged in the most thrilling wheel-to-wheel duel in Indianapolis history, swapping the lead 29 times between them. Ward led until lap 196 when his right-front tyre wore down to the cord, forcing him to back off and allowing Rathmann to win by 12.75 seconds.
- Grandstand Collapse Tragedy: Before the start, a makeshift scaffolding grandstand in the infield collapsed, killing 2 spectators and injuring 82.
- End of the Indy-F1 World Championship Era: Following this event, the FIA removed the Indianapolis 500 from the World Championship calendar, ending the 11-year union (1950–1960).

## Records Set or Equalled
- Final Indianapolis 500 to count toward the FIA Formula One World Championship.
- All-time record 29 lead changes in an Indianapolis 500 (stood for over 50 years).
