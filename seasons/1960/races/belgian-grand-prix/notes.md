## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The Black Weekend of Spa: One of the deadliest weekends in Grand Prix history. During Friday practice, Stirling Moss suffered rear suspension failure at 140 mph at Burnenville, somersaulting into the trees and breaking both legs. Minutes later, British driver Mike Taylor suffered steering failure in his Lotus at Blanchimont, crashing into the forest and suffering career-ending spinal injuries.
- Tragic Fatalities of Bristow and Stacey: During the race on lap 19, 22-year-old British driver Chris Bristow lost control of his private Cooper at Burnenville, was thrown from his car into barbed wire, and was decapitated. Five laps later on lap 24, 26-year-old Lotus factory driver Alan Stacey was struck in the face by a bird at 140 mph on the Masta Kink, plunged through a hedge, and was killed in the flaming wreckage.
- Brabham Grand Slam in Mourning: Jack Brabham scored a Grand Slam (pole 3:50.0, fastest lap 3:51.9, victory, led all 36 laps), leading home a Cooper 1-2 with Bruce McLaren, completely unaware of the double fatality behind them.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First and only race in Formula One history where two drivers were killed in separate crashes during the same Grand Prix (Chris Bristow and Alan Stacey).
- Jack Brabham Grand Slam in the Cooper T53 'Lowline'.
