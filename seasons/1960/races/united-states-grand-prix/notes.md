# 1960 United States Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: III United States Grand Prix
- **Round**: 10 of 10 in the 1960 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 20 November 1960
- **Location**: Riverside International Raceway, Riverside, California, USA
- **Circuit Length**: 5.271 km (3.275 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (395.325 km / 245.625 miles)

  1. Stirling Moss (Lotus-Climax) - 75 laps

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **One-off Riverside Grand Prix**: The 1960 United States Grand Prix was held at the dusty Riverside International Raceway in Southern California, before moving permanently to Watkins Glen in 1961.
- **Moss Dominates from Pole**: Fully recovered from his severe Spa injuries, Stirling Moss dominated the race in the Rob Walker Lotus 18, leading all 75 laps to win ahead of works Lotus driver Innes Ireland.
- **Brabham Cockpit Fire Drama**: Newly crowned double champion Jack Brabham was chasing Moss when his Cooper suffered an overflowing fuel tank that ignited into a cockpit fire. Brabham pitted twice to extinguish the fire and wash away fuel, recovering to finish 4th.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, dry California desert sunshine; sandy track surface.
