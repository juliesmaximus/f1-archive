## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Stirling Moss Riverside Triumph: Stirling Moss made a triumphant return following his horrific Spa injuries, taking pole position and leading from start to finish in the Rob Walker Lotus 18-Climax to win by 38.0 seconds over Innes Ireland.
- Brabham Cockpit Fire: Newly crowned champion Jack Brabham suffered a fuel tank leak early on; raw fuel sprayed into the hot cockpit, twice igniting small fires that singed Brabham's overalls. Brabham pitted to extinguish the flames and finished 4th.
- End of the 2.5-Litre Engine Formula: This marked the final World Championship Grand Prix run to the 2.5-litre naturally aspirated engine regulations (1954–1960) before the controversial downscaling to 1.5-litre engines in 1961.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Sole appearance of Riverside International Raceway in the Formula One World Championship.
- Final Grand Prix of the 2.5-litre Formula One engine era (1954–1960).
