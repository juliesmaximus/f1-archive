# 1960 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1960 World Championship of Drivers and the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers. Only each driver's best 6 results out of the 10 rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship tally. Only each constructor's best 6 results out of the 9 eligible Grands Prix counted towards the Manufacturers' Cup (Indianapolis 500 did not count toward the Manufacturers' Cup).

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Points System**: Points were revised to 8-6-4-3-2-1 for the top 6 classified finishers (expanding points to 6th place).
- **Fastest Lap**: The 1 bonus point for fastest lap was eliminated starting in 1960.
- **Best 6 rule**: Only best 6 results counted for both Drivers and Constructors.
- **Shared drives**: Continued to be ineligible for championship points.
