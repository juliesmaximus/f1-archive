# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1975 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix (half-points awarded in Spain and Austria). Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Austrian Niki Lauda won his first World Drivers' Championship with 64.5 net points (64.5 gross), driving the transverse-gearbox Ferrari 312T.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix (half-points in Spain and Austria). Only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the championship.

Scuderia Ferrari won their third International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 72.5 net points (89.5 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers. Half-points awarded at Spanish GP (stopped on lap 29 following tragic Rolf Stommelen crash) and Austrian GP (stopped on lap 29 due to torrential rain).
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 7 results from rounds 1–8 and best 5 results from rounds 9–14 counted towards both championships.
  - Ferrari scored 89.5 gross points, dropping 17 points to finish with 72.5 net points.
  - Brabham-Ford scored 56 gross points, dropping 2 points to finish with 54 net points.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Niki Lauda won his maiden World Drivers' Championship, scoring 5 victories and taking 9 pole positions in the revolutionary Ferrari 312T.
  - Scuderia Ferrari claimed their 3rd International Cup for F1 Manufacturers, their first championship triumph in 11 years (since 1964).
  - Maiden Grand Prix victories for Carlos Pace (Brazil), Jochen Mass (Spain), James Hunt (Netherlands), and Vittorio Brambilla (Austria).
  - Lella Lombardi became the first and only woman in Formula One history to score World Championship points (6th place, 0.5 points in Spain).
  - Niki Lauda set the first sub-7-minute lap in Nürburgring Nordschleife history during German GP qualifying (6:58.600).
  - Tragic fatal accident of Mark Donohue following practice crash at Österreichring.
