## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren) — 1:22  
- **Pole Position:** Tom Pryce (1:19.360)
- **Fastest Lap:** Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari) — 1:20.900 (Lap 16)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm dry start, followed by an apocalyptic flash flood and torrential hailstorm on lap 53  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** BRDC grandstands at Woodcote, Copse, and Abbey; crowded runway banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE WOODCOTE HAILSTORM CHAOS: Tom Pryce took a sensational maiden pole position for Shadow. In the race, Carlos Pace led for Brabham. On lap 53, a sudden, freak torrential hailstorm and flash flood struck Woodcote corner. Within two laps, 14 of the remaining 19 cars aquaplaned off the flooded track into the catch fencing! The race was red-flagged on lap 56 and declared. Emerson Fittipaldi, who had pitted for wet tyres just prior to the deluge, was declared the winner ahead of Carlos Pace and Jody Scheckter. Only 5 cars were running when red-flagged. Full points awarded as over 75% completed.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi's 14th and final career Formula One World Championship victory.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Race red-flagged after 56 of 67 scheduled laps (263.816 km) due to torrential rain and track flooding.
