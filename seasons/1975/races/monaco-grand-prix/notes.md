## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 2:01:21.310  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Damp start with intermittent drizzle, drying track; 19°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 45,000 spectators lining harbour balconies and terraces  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Swimming Pool grandstands, Quai Albert 1er, and Casino square terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FERRARI ENDS 20-YEAR MONACO DROUGHT: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312T. In greasy conditions, Lauda dominated the early stages. As the track dried, Lauda timed his tyre stop perfectly to take victory by 2.78s over Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren) and Carlos Pace (Brabham), scoring Scuderia Ferrari's FIRST Monaco Grand Prix victory since Maurice Trintignant in 1955! Scheduled for 78 laps but stopped at 75 laps due to the 2-hour time limit.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Launched Niki Lauda's dominant surge toward the 1975 World Championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** First World Championship victory for the revolutionary transverse-gearbox Ferrari 312T.
