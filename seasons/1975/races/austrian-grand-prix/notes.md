## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Vittorio Brambilla (March) — 57:56.690  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Torrential rain, standing water, and thick fog; 14°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 90,000 soaked Austrian spectators  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent start-finish grandstand and hillside banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
BRAMBILLA CRASHES CROSSING FINISH LINE & DONOHUE TRAGEDY: The weekend was overshadowed by the fatal practice accident of American superstar Mark Donohue, whose March suffered a tyre blowout at Vöest-Hügel; Donohue died in hospital two days later. In torrential rain, Niki Lauda took pole but was passed by James Hunt and Italian Vittorio Brambilla ('The Monza Gorilla') in the orange March 751. Brambilla seized the lead on lap 19. On lap 29, the race was flagged off due to dangerous conditions. As Brambilla took the chequered flag to claim his SOLE career Grand Prix victory, he took both hands off the steering wheel to celebrate, spun on the streaming track, and smashed nose-first into the pit wall! Brambilla waved his crumpled steering wheel to the crowd. Half-points awarded.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Vittorio Brambilla scored his only Grand Prix victory and the final win for March Engineering. Tragically, American icon Mark Donohue passed away on August 19.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Race stopped after 29 of 54 scheduled laps (171.419 km) due to torrential rain. Half-points awarded.
