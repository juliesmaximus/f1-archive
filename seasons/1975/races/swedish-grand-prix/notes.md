### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA HAT-TRICK: Italian Vittorio Brambilla took an astonishing maiden pole position for March Engineering! In the race, Carlos Reutemann led in the Brabham BT44B until severe tyre blistering slowed him on lap 70. Niki Lauda swept past to take his third consecutive victory by 6.28s over Reutemann and Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari 3rd).
