## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jody Scheckter (Tyrrell) — 1:43:16.900  
- **Pole Position:** Carlos Pace  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 90,000 spectators across the Kyalami amphitheatre  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne spectator grass banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
SCHECKTER WINS AT HOME: Carlos Pace took pole for Brabham. Local hero Jody Scheckter in the Tyrrell 007 swept past Pace into the lead on lap 3 to the wild cheers of 90,000 South African fans! Scheckter drove an immaculate race to win by 3.74s over Carlos Reutemann (Brabham) and Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell). Emerson Fittipaldi took 4th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jody Scheckter became the first and only South African driver to win the South African Grand Prix at Kyalami.
