### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MONTJUÏC DISASTER: Drivers led by World Champion Emerson Fittipaldi boycotted Friday practice over loose, badly bolted Armco barriers; Fittipaldi withdrew in protest on Saturday. On lap 29, race leader Rolf Stommelen's Embassy Hill rear wing sheared off over a crest; the car somersaulted over the barriers into a spectator area, killing four people (two photographers, a fireman, and a spectator) and severely injuring Stommelen. Jochen Mass was leading in the McLaren when the race was stopped, scoring his MAIDEN Grand Prix victory ahead of Jacky Ickx and Carlos Reutemann. Half-points awarded (only 29 of 75 laps completed). Lella Lombardi finished 6th, becoming the FIRST AND ONLY WOMAN IN HISTORY TO SCORE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP POINTS (0.5 points)!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of Formula One's darkest weekends. The race marked the permanent end of Grand Prix racing at Montjuïc and Lella Lombardi's historic championship point.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Half-points awarded as under 60% distance completed. Formula One permanently abandoned Montjuïc.
