### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
CARLOS PACE'S HOMELAND TRIUMPH: Jean-Pierre Jarier took pole for Shadow and led comfortably until fuel metering failure stopped him on lap 32. São Paulo native Carlos Pace in the Gordon Murray-designed Brabham BT44B swept into the lead to the roar of 120,000 delirious fans, taking his FIRST and ONLY career Formula One victory by 5.64 seconds ahead of fellow countryman Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren 2nd)! Jochen Mass completed the podium in 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The legendary 1-2 sweep for Brazilian drivers Carlos Pace and Emerson Fittipaldi on home soil. Interlagos was later renamed the Autódromo José Carlos Pace in his honour following his tragic death in an air crash in 1977.
