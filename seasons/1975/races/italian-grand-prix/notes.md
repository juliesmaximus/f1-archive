- **Spectator Attendance:** Over 150,000 ecstatic tifosi packing the Autodromo  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Tribuna Centrale, Vedano, Ascari, and Parabolica spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FERRARI ENDS 11-YEAR TITLE DROUGHT: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312T. Teammate Clay Regazzoni seized the lead into the Curva Grande and drove a commanding race to take victory for Ferrari before 150,000 delirious Italian fans! Emerson Fittipaldi finished 2nd for McLaren. Niki Lauda drove a calm, calculated race to finish 3rd, MATHEMATICALLY CLINCHING HIS FIRST FORMULA ONE WORLD DRIVERS' CHAMPIONSHIP and delivering Scuderia Ferrari's first World Championship since 1964! Fans stormed the track in an unforgettable sea of red flags and flares.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Niki Lauda ended Ferrari's 11-year championship drought, crowned World Champion at age 26 on Ferrari's holy ground.
