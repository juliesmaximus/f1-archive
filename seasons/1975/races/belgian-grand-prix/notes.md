## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 1:43:53.980  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda (1:25.430)
- **Fastest Lap:** Clay Regazzoni (Ferrari) — 1:26.760 (Lap 11)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered start-finish grandstand and grass mounds around Terlamen  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA'S SECOND CONSECUTIVE WIN: Niki Lauda took pole position in the Ferrari 312T. Vittorio Brambilla led early in the March before tyre degradation dropped him back. Lauda swept into the lead on lap 6 and pulled away in supreme command, winning by 19.22 seconds over Jody Scheckter (Tyrrell) and Carlos Reutemann (Brabham). Emerson Fittipaldi retired with engine failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lauda took the lead of the World Drivers' Championship with 23 points.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 70 laps (295.4 km).
