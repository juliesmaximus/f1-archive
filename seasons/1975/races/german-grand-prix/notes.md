## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Carlos Reutemann (Brabham) — 1:41:14.100  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient, fast track conditions  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 250,000 spectators packing the Eifel mountains  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Start-und-Ziel tribüne, Karussell, and Brünnchen spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HISTORIC FIRST SUB-7 MINUTE LAP & REUTEMANN WINS: In practice, Niki Lauda made motorsport history by becoming the FIRST DRIVER EVER TO LAP THE NORDSCHLEIFE IN UNDER 7 MINUTES, setting pole position with an astonishing 6:58.6 (123.62 mph / 198.95 km/h)! In the race, Lauda led until a punctured front tyre on lap 9 dropped him to 3rd. Carlos Reutemann in the Gordon Murray Brabham BT44B took over to win by 1m 37s over Jacques Laffite (scoring Frank Williams' best result to date in 2nd). Clay Regazzoni suffered a tyre failure, and Hunt retired with wheel failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lauda's sub-7 minute lap cemented the Ferrari 312T's place in engineering legend. Carlos Reutemann took his 4th career Grand Prix win.
