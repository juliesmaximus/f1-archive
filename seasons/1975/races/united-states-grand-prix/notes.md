## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Niki Lauda (Ferrari) — 1:42:58.175  
- **Pole Position:** Niki Lauda  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, crisp, sunny autumn day; 13°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 115,000 spectators packing the Watkins Glen hills  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Boot, and Seneca spectator knolls  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
LAUDA'S CROWNING 5TH VICTORY: Newly crowned World Champion Niki Lauda took his 9th pole position of the season in the Ferrari 312T. Lauda dominated the race to take his 5th victory of the year by 4.94s over outgoing champion Emerson Fittipaldi (McLaren). Jochen Mass was 3rd. Clay Regazzoni was controversially black-flagged after ignoring blue flags while defending against Fittipaldi.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Niki Lauda concluded a transcendent season with 64.5 points, while Scuderia Ferrari secured the Constructors' Championship with 72.5 points.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Record $350,000 total purse ($50,000 to the winner).
