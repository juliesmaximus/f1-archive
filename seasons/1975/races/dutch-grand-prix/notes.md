- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main pit straight grandstand and Tarzan hairpin dunes  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
JAMES HUNT & HESKETH RACING GLORY: Niki Lauda took pole in the Ferrari 312T and led on wet tyres. On lap 7, charismatic 27-year-old British driver James Hunt made a brilliant gamble, pitting his private, unsponsored white Hesketh 308 for slick dry tyres. As others delayed, Hunt took the lead on lap 15. For the final 30 laps, Niki Lauda hounded Hunt relentlessly, his Flat-12 Ferrari filling Hunt's mirrors. Hunt delivered a masterclass in defensive car placement to WIN BY JUST 1.06 SECONDS, scoring his MAIDEN Formula One victory and the historic first win for Lord Alexander Hesketh's independent team! Clay Regazzoni took 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the most famous, romantic underdog victories in Grand Prix history. James Hunt proved his championship-winning credentials.
