# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1975 - Calendar

The 1975 Formula One season was the 26th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 14 championship rounds between January 12 and October 5, 1975.

The 1975 season belonged emphatically to **Niki Lauda** and **Scuderia Ferrari**. Driving Mauro Forghieri's masterpiece, the **Ferrari 312T** featuring a revolutionary transverse gearbox and 3.0-litre Flat-12 engine, Lauda took nine pole positions and five Grand Prix victories (Monaco, Belgium, Sweden, France, USA) to secure his maiden World Drivers' Championship with 64.5 points. Lauda clinched the championship at Monza, ending an 11-year drought for Ferrari and securing the **International Cup for F1 Manufacturers**.

The season was also highlighted by **James Hunt's romantic maiden victory** for privateer Hesketh Racing at Zandvoort, Carlos Pace's emotional home victory at Interlagos, and Niki Lauda becoming the first driver in history to lap the Nürburgring Nordschleife in under 7 minutes.
