# British Grand Prix - 2019 Round 10

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Rolex British Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Silverstone Circuit (Silverstone, UK)
- **Date:** 2019-07-14
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.891 km (3.660 mi), 52 laps, 306.198 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 153 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, breezy, ambient 20°C, track 28°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 141,000 race day (351,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Hamilton takes a record 6th British Grand Prix victory after capitalizing on Safety Car timing. Thrilling wheel-to-wheel duels between Leclerc and Verstappen; Vettel slams into the back of Verstappen at Club corner, earning a 10-second penalty.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
