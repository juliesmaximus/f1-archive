# Singapore Grand Prix - 2019 Round 15

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Singapore Airlines Singapore Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Marina Bay Street Circuit (Marina Bay, Singapore)
- **Date:** 2019-09-22
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.063 km (3.146 mi), 61 laps, 308.706 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 15 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, hazy tropical night, ambient 30°C, track 36°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 89,000 race day (268,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Sebastian Vettel takes his 53rd and final career Formula 1 victory, undercutting pole-sitter Leclerc on strategy to lead a Ferrari 1-2 under the Marina Bay floodlights.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
