# Mexican Grand Prix - 2019 Round 18

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Gran Premio de Mexico 2019
- **Circuit:** Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez (Mexico City, Mexico)
- **Date:** 2019-10-27
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.304 km (2.674 mi), 71 laps, 305.354 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 2,285 m (7,500 ft)
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 23°C, track 47°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 138,435 race day (345,694 weekend record)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Verstappen stripped of pole for yellow flag infringement in quali, tangles with Hamilton on lap 1. Hamilton executes an audacious 48-lap stint on hard tyres to defeat Ferrari's two-stop strategy and win in Mexico City.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
