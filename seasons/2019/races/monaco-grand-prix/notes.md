# Monaco Grand Prix - 2019 Round 6

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Grand Prix de Monaco 2019
- **Circuit:** Circuit de Monaco (Monte Carlo, Monaco)
- **Date:** 2019-05-26
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 3.337 km (2.074 mi), 78 laps, 260.286 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 15 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, overcast, ambient 22°C, track 34°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 40,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Emotional weekend following the passing of triple world champion Niki Lauda. Hamilton wears a tribute red helmet and survives 67 laps on graining medium tyres under relentless pressure and a late dive at the chicane from Max Verstappen (who carried a 5-second unsafe release penalty) to win in Lauda's memory.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
