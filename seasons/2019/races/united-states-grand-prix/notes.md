# United States Grand Prix - 2019 Round 19

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Emirates United States Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Circuit of the Americas (Austin, USA)
- **Date:** 2019-11-03
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.513 km (3.426 mi), 56 laps, 308.405 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 157 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, cool, sunny, ambient 19°C, track 28°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 115,000 race day (268,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Valtteri Bottas wins from pole, but Lewis Hamilton finishes 2nd to officially clinch his 6th FIA Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship, moving ahead of Juan Manuel Fangio into standalone second in all-time history behind Michael Schumacher.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
