# Bahrain Grand Prix - 2019 Round 2

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Bahrain International Circuit (Sakhir, Bahrain)
- **Date:** 2019-03-31
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.412 km (3.363 mi), 57 laps, 308.238 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 7 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, clear night, ambient 25°C, track 29°C

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) - 1:27.866
- **Fastest Lap:** Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) - 1:33.411 (lap 38)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Heartbreak for 21-year-old Charles Leclerc, who claims sensational maiden pole and leads comfortably until a short-circuit cylinder failure robs his Ferrari engine of power with 10 laps to go. Hamilton and Bottas pass him for a Mercedes 1-2, with a late Safety Car securing Leclerc's first career podium.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
