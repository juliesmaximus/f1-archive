# Russian Grand Prix - 2019 Round 16

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 VTB Russian Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Sochi Autodrom (Sochi, Russia)
- **Date:** 2019-09-29
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.848 km (3.634 mi), 53 laps, 309.745 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 5 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, mild, ambient 22°C, track 25°C

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) - 1:31.628
- **Fastest Lap:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) - 1:35.761 (lap 51)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Ferrari team strategy agreements unravel after Vettel passes Leclerc into Turn 1 and refuses to swap positions. Vettel's MGU-K failure on lap 28 triggers a Virtual Safety Car, gifting Hamilton a free pit stop to take victory.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
