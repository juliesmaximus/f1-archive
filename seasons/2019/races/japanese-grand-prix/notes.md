# Japanese Grand Prix - 2019 Round 17

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Japanese Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Suzuka Circuit (Suzuka, Japan)
- **Date:** 2019-10-13
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.807 km (3.608 mi), 52 laps (officially shortened from 53 due to system glitch waving chequered light 1 lap early)
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 45 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny after Typhoon Hagibis passed, ambient 22°C, track 30°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 89,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
All Saturday sessions cancelled due to Typhoon Hagibis; qualifying held Sunday morning with Ferrari locking out front row. Bottas jumps both Ferraris at the start to win, officially sealing Mercedes-AMG Petronas' record 6th consecutive Constructors' Championship.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
