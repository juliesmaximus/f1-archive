# Spanish Grand Prix - 2019 Round 5

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Emirates Gran Premio de Espana 2019
- **Circuit:** Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya (Barcelona, Spain)
- **Date:** 2019-05-12
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.655 km (2.892 mi), 66 laps, 307.104 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 120 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, warm, ambient 22°C, track 41°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 87,511 race day (160,428 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Hamilton leads from the start to take victory and fastest lap point ahead of Bottas, delivering an unprecedented fifth straight 1-2 finish for Mercedes.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
