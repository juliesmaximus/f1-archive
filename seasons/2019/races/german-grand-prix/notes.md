## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing Honda)
- **Pole Position:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) - 1:11.767
- **Fastest Lap:** Max Verstappen (Red Bull Racing Honda) - 1:16.645 (lap 61)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
An all-time chaotic classic in heavy rain at Hockenheim: Mercedes celebrate 125 years in motorsport wearing retro clothing, only to suffer a disastrous race (Bottas crashes out, Hamilton damages wing, gets penalty, spins, finishes 9th). Max Verstappen wins despite a 360-degree spin; Daniil Kvyat scores Toro Rosso's first podium in 11 years; Lance Stroll 4th for Racing Point.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
