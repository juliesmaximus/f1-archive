## Event Specifications

- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 6.003 km (3.730 mi), 51 laps, 306.049 km

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes) - 1:40.495
- **Fastest Lap:** Charles Leclerc (Ferrari) - 1:43.009 (lap 50)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Valtteri Bottas converts pole into victory, resisting intense pressure from Hamilton to secure Mercedes' record fourth consecutive 1-2 finish from the start of a season.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
