# Belgian Grand Prix - 2019 Round 13

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Johnnie Walker Belgian Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps (Spa, Belgium)
- **Date:** 2019-09-01
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 7.004 km (4.352 mi), 44 laps, 308.052 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 415 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, sunny, ambient 17°C, track 30°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 89,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Sombre weekend overshadowed by the tragic death of F2 driver Anthoine Hubert in Saturday's feature race. Charles Leclerc converts pole into his emotional maiden Formula 1 victory, dedicating the win to his childhood friend Hubert while resisting Hamilton by 0.9s.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
