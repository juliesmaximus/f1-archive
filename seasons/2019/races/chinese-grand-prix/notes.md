# Chinese Grand Prix - 2019 Round 3

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Heineken Chinese Grand Prix 2019 - 1,000th World Championship Grand Prix
- **Circuit:** Shanghai International Circuit (Shanghai, China)
- **Date:** 2019-04-14
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.451 km (3.387 mi), 56 laps, 305.066 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 5 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, mild, ambient 19°C, track 31°C

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes) - 1:31.547
- **Fastest Lap:** Pierre Gasly (Red Bull Racing Honda) - 1:34.742 (lap 55)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Milestone 1,000th World Championship race in F1 history (1950 Silverstone to 2019 Shanghai). Lewis Hamilton beats pole-sitter Bottas into Turn 1 to control the race and win ahead of Bottas and Vettel, sealing Mercedes' third straight 1-2.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
