## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Rolex Australian Grand Prix 2019
- **Circuit:** Albert Park Grand Prix Circuit (Melbourne, Australia)
- **Date:** 2019-03-17
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 5.303 km (3.295 mi), 58 laps, 307.574 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 10 m

## Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) - 1:20.486
- **Fastest Lap:** Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes) - 1:25.580 (lap 57)

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Valtteri Bottas jumps teammate Hamilton at the start and dominates to take victory by 20.8 seconds, also claiming the newly reintroduced bonus point for fastest lap ('To whom it may concern, fuck you'). Honda's first hybrid era podium with Verstappen finishing 3rd.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
