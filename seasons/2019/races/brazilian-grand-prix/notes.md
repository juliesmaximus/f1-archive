# Brazilian Grand Prix - 2019 Round 20

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Heineken Grande Premio do Brasil 2019
- **Circuit:** Autódromo José Carlos Pace (São Paulo, Brazil)
- **Date:** 2019-11-17
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.309 km (2.677 mi), 71 laps, 305.909 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 785 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, warm, ambient 22°C, track 48°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 78,000 race day

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Absolute madness at Interlagos: Ferrari teammates Vettel and Leclerc collide wheel-to-wheel down the back straight, destroying both cars; late safety car restart sees Hamilton clash with Albon; Pierre Gasly famously drag-races Hamilton to the line to take P2 for Toro Rosso; Carlos Sainz promoted from 20th to 3rd for McLaren's first podium since 2014; Verstappen wins.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
