# Hungarian Grand Prix - 2019 Round 12

## Event Specifications
- **Official Title:** Formula 1 Rolex Magyar Nagydij 2019
- **Circuit:** Hungaroring (Budapest, Hungary)
- **Date:** 2019-08-04
- **Circuit Length & Distance:** 4.381 km (2.722 mi), 70 laps, 306.630 km
- **Circuit Elevation / Altitude:** 239 m
- **Ambient & Track Weather:** Dry, ambient 25°C, track 46°C
- **Spectator Attendance:** 92,000 race day (230,000 weekend)

---

## Strategic Overview & Race Scenario
Masterclass strategy duel: Max Verstappen scores his maiden career pole position and leads the race until Mercedes execute a brilliant two-stop strategy on lap 48, allowing Hamilton on fresh mediums to hunt Verstappen down and pass him around the outside of Turn 1 with 3 laps to go.

---

## Technical & Regulatory Context
- **Fastest Lap Point:** 1 World Championship point awarded to the driver with the fastest lap of the race, provided they finish in the top 10.
- **Aerodynamic Rules:** Wider, simpler front wings and deeper rear wings to reduce turbulent 'dirty air' wake and aid overtaking.
- **Fuel Allowance:** Maximum race fuel capacity increased from 105 kg to 110 kg to encourage flat-out racing without extreme fuel-saving.
