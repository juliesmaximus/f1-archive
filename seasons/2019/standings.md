## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton Six-Time World Champion:** Hamilton moved to second on the all-time list behind Michael Schumacher, securing his 6th Drivers' Championship with 11 victories and 413 points.
- **Mercedes 6th Consecutive Championship Double:** Mercedes became the first constructor in Formula 1 history to win six consecutive World Drivers' and World Constructors' Championship doubles (2014–2019).
- **1,000th Formula 1 World Championship Race:** Celebrated at the Chinese Grand Prix in Shanghai on April 14, 2019, won by Lewis Hamilton.
- **Fastest Lap Bonus Point Reintroduced:** 1 World Championship point re-introduced for the driver setting the fastest lap of the Grand Prix, conditional on a top-10 finish.
- **Charles Leclerc Breakthrough:** Leclerc claimed 7 pole positions (the most of any driver in 2019) and took emotional maiden Grand Prix victories at Spa-Francorchamps and Monza.
- **Red Bull-Honda Partnership:** Red Bull began their engine partnership with Honda, scoring 3 victories with Max Verstappen (Austria, Germany, Brazil), delivering Honda's first F1 wins since 2006.
