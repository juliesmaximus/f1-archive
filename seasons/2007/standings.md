## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Kimi Räikkönen World Champion:** Overcame a 17-point deficit with two races to go to win the World Drivers' Championship by 1 point (110 to Hamilton's 109 and Alonso's 109).
- **Lewis Hamilton's Historic Rookie Season:** Finished 2nd in the World Championship with 4 wins, 6 poles, and 12 podiums, scoring podium finishes in his first 9 consecutive races.
- **The Spygate Scandal:** McLaren was disqualified from the Constructors' Championship and fined an unprecedented $100 million for possessing 780 pages of confidential Ferrari technical blueprints.
- **Robert Kubica's Miracle Survival:** Survived a violent 300 km/h crash at Montreal with minimal injuries.
- **Sebastian Vettel Debut:** Scored a point on debut at Indianapolis at 19 years, 349 days.
