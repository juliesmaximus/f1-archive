## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Kimi Räikkönen World Champion:** Overcame a 17-point deficit with two rounds remaining to capture the World Drivers' Championship by a single point (110 points to Hamilton's 109 and Alonso's 109) with victory in the season finale at Interlagos.
- **Lewis Hamilton's Historic Rookie Season:** Hamilton achieved the greatest rookie campaign in Formula 1 history, finishing 2nd in the World Championship on countback of 2nd places over teammate Alonso (5 to 4), taking 4 wins, 6 poles, and setting a record of 9 consecutive podiums from debut.
- **The Spygate Scandal:** In the biggest espionage controversy in motorsport history, McLaren was found guilty of possessing 780 pages of confidential Ferrari technical documentation, stripped of all 2007 Constructors' Championship points, and handed a record $100 million fine by the FIA World Motor Sport Council.
- **Robert Kubica's Miracle Survival:** Survived an unspeakable 300 km/h airborne crash into the concrete barriers during the Canadian Grand Prix, suffering only a sprained ankle and mild concussion.
- **Sebastian Vettel Debut:** Replaced the injured Kubica at Indianapolis, qualifying 7th and finishing 8th to become the youngest driver to score a World Championship point at 19 years, 349 days.
