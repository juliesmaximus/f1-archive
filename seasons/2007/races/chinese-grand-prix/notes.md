## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The turning point of the championship. Lewis Hamilton led from pole and looked poised to clinch the World Championship as a rookie. However, McLaren kept him out too long on worn intermediate tyres, wearing the right-rear tyre down to the white cord. When Hamilton finally pitted on lap 31, he slid off the pitlane entry gravel trap at walking speed and was beached, helpless to continue. Kimi Räikkönen won the race ahead of Alonso, blowing the championship wide open.
