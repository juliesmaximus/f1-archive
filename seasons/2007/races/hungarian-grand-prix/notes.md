## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Total civil war at McLaren. During Q3 pitstops, Fernando Alonso held stationary in the pit box for 10 extra seconds—blocking teammate Lewis Hamilton behind him so Hamilton could not complete his final flying lap! Alonso claimed pole, but the FIA stewards investigated the blatant blocking and handed Alonso a 5-place grid penalty, awarding pole to Hamilton. Hamilton controlled the race to win, resisting Kimi Räikkönen by 0.7 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The fallout from Hungary triggered the explosion of Spygate: Alonso threatened Ron Dennis with revealing incriminating emails between himself and Pedro de la Rosa, prompting Dennis to contact FIA President Max Mosley.
