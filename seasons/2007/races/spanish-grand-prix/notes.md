## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Felipe Massa survived wheel-to-wheel contact with Fernando Alonso at Turn 1 on the opening lap, forcing the Spaniard onto the gravel. Massa commanded the race to take back-to-back wins. Lewis Hamilton finished 2nd to take the outright lead of the World Championship in just his fourth race.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First race on the new chicane configuration before the final turn, introduced to slow cars entering the pit straight.
