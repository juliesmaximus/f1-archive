## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen cemented his status as the 'King of Spa', claiming his third consecutive victory at Spa-Francorchamps. Ferrari dominated with an imperious 1-2 finish ahead of Alonso and Hamilton. Just days earlier, the FIA World Motor Sport Council had expelled McLaren from the Constructors' Championship and fined them $100 million for Spygate, leaving Ferrari officially crowned 2007 World Constructors' Champions.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
McLaren accepted the $100M penalty and Constructors' exclusion after being found in unauthorized possession of 780 pages of Ferrari confidential technical data.
