## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ferrari asserted their authority with an emphatic 1-2 finish. Felipe Massa took pole and led early, but Kimi Räikkönen used devastating pace during the second pit stop window to emerge in the lead and secure his second victory of 2007. Lewis Hamilton finished 3rd to make it 8 podiums in 8 career races.
