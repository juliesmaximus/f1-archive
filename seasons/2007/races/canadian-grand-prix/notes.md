## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton scored his historic maiden Formula 1 victory from his first career pole position. The race was an insane thriller with four safety car periods. On lap 27, Robert Kubica suffered a horrific 300 km/h airborne crash approaching the hairpin, barrel-rolling along the concrete wall with the nose cone ripped off; miraculously, Kubica survived with only a sprained ankle and concussion. Massa and Fisichella were black-flagged for leaving the pitlane under red lights.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Alonso went off at Turn 1 four times and received a 10-second stop-go penalty for pitting when the pitlane was closed, finishing 7th.
