## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton thrilled British fans by taking pole position, but his light fuel load compromised his race strategy. Kimi Räikkönen delivered a clinical drive, running longer in the middle stint to leapfrog both Alonso and Hamilton and take a commanding victory by 2.4 seconds over Alonso.
