## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of Lewis Hamilton's greatest masterclasses. In appalling monsoon conditions that delayed the green flag behind the safety car for 19 laps, Hamilton drove with laser focus through zero-visibility spray to win. Fernando Alonso suffered a massive crash on lap 41, destroying his McLaren and handing Hamilton a 12-point championship lead with two races remaining.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari failed to start on extreme wet tyres due to missing an FIA email mandate, forcing both cars to pit immediately under the safety car.
