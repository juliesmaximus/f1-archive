## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Felipe Massa repeated his 2006 heroics by winning from pole position, leading a Ferrari 1-2 ahead of Kimi Räikkönen. Lewis Hamilton ran 3rd until his right-front tyre delaminated at 160 mph on lap 42, limping back to the pits and recovering to 5th.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Tyre blowout slashed Hamilton's championship lead to 5 points over Alonso.
