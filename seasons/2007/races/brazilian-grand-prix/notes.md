## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The Miracle of Interlagos. Entering the finale trailing Hamilton by 7 points and Alonso by 3 points, Kimi Räikkönen needed a miracle. At the start, Massa and Räikkönen jumped Hamilton. Hamilton attacked Alonso at Turn 4, ran wide, and dropped to 8th. On lap 8, Hamilton's McLaren suffered a sudden gearbox electrical glitch, slowing for 30 seconds before resetting, dropping him to 18th. Massa allowed Räikkönen past during the final pitstops to win. Hamilton recovered heroically to 7th, but it was not enough: Kimi Räikkönen clinched the World Drivers' Championship by 1 point (110 to Hamilton's 109 and Alonso's 109).

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
McLaren appealed the fuel temperature of BMW Sauber and Williams cars post-race; the FIA Court of Appeal dismissed the appeal, confirming Räikkönen as champion.
