## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Kimi Räikkönen scored a historic Grand Slam on his Scuderia Ferrari debut: pole, win, fastest lap, and led every lap except during pit stops. Rookie sensation Lewis Hamilton made an electrifying debut for McLaren, sweeping around the outside of teammate Fernando Alonso at Turn 1 to lead laps and finish 3rd on the podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton became the first driver to score a podium on his Formula 1 debut since Jacques Villeneuve in 1996.
