## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton made it back-to-back victories after a ferocious wheel-to-wheel duel with teammate Fernando Alonso. On lap 38, Alonso slipstreamed down the main straight and pulled alongside, but Hamilton defended the inside line brilliantly into Turn 1. 19-year-old Sebastian Vettel made his F1 debut for BMW Sauber, finishing 8th to become the youngest point-scorer in F1 history.
