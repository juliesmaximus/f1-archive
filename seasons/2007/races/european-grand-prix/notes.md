## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the most chaotic wet races in Formula 1 history. On lap 3, a torrential monsoon struck the circuit. Seven cars aquaplaned off Turn 1 into the gravel trap in identical fashion, including Lewis Hamilton, Jenson Button, and Nico Rosberg. Hamilton kept his engine running and was craned back onto the track by marshals. Rookie Markus Winkelhock led the race for Spyker after gambling on wet tyres before the start! Following a red flag, Alonso hunted down Massa in drying and wet conditions, banging wheels with the Brazilian at Turn 5 with 4 laps to go to take a dramatic victory.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Fiery altercation between Alonso and Massa in Italian before the podium ceremony, caught live on world television broadcast.
