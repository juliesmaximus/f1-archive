## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Felipe Massa dominated from pole position, holding off Lewis Hamilton by 2.3 seconds. Hamilton made history by becoming the first driver in the 58-year history of the World Championship to finish on the podium in each of his first three career Grands Prix.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Three-way tie in the World Drivers' Championship: Alonso, Räikkönen, and Hamilton all locked on 22 points.
