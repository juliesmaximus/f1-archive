## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
McLaren obliterated the field, lapping everyone except 3rd-placed Felipe Massa. Alonso won from pole, but Lewis Hamilton stalked him relentlessly, brushing the barriers multiple times. McLaren ordered both drivers to back off to protect a 1-2 finish, sparking post-race friction.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The FIA launched a formal investigation into McLaren for alleged team orders, ultimately finding no breach of sporting regulations.
