## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
McLaren humiliated Ferrari on home soil with a dominant 1-2 finish. Fernando Alonso led from pole, while Lewis Hamilton pulled off a sensational, audacious late-braking overtake on Kimi Räikkönen into the Variante del Rettifilo to take 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Held under the shadow of police raids on McLaren personnel as Italian magistrates served formal fraud notices in the Monza paddock regarding Spygate.
