## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
McLaren scored an emphatic 1-2 finish. Alonso and Hamilton stormed past pole-sitter Felipe Massa on the run down to Turn 1. Hamilton drove a breathtaking defensive masterclass, repeatedly frustrating Massa until the Brazilian ran wide into the gravel at Turn 4.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Alonso claimed his first victory for McLaren, ending Ferrari's dominant run.
