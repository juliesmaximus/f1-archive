## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Single Tyre Supplier:** Bridgestone became sole tyre supplier following Michelin's withdrawal.
- **Mandatory Tyre Compound Marking:** Softer 'Option' tyres marked with a white painted groove.
- **Engine Freeze:** Homologation freeze introduced on 2.4L V8 engines, limiting RPM to 19,000 rpm.
