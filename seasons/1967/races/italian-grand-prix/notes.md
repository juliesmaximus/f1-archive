### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
ONE OF THE GREATEST RACES IN MOTORSPORT HISTORY: Jim Clark took pole and led until lap 12 when a punctured tyre forced him to pit. Clark rejoined a full lap down in 16th place. Clark mounted an extraordinary, superhuman recovery drive, lapping up to 3 seconds faster than the leaders, unlapping himself on lap 32, slicing through the field, and incredibly RETAKING THE LEAD on lap 60! But on the final lap, Clark's fuel pump failed to scavenge the remaining gallons of fuel, causing the engine to sputter. Jack Brabham and John Surtees flew past on the main straight. Surtees in the brand new Honda RA300 ('Hondola') slipped past Brabham at the Parabolica to WIN BY JUST 0.2 SECONDS! Clark coasted across the line for 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Maiden race and maiden victory for the Lola-engineered Honda RA300. Clark's charge from a lap down to 1st remains widely cited by historians as one of the finest driving performances of all time.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
