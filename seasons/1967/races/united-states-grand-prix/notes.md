### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Graham Hill took pole position in the Lotus 49. In the race, Graham Hill and Jim Clark pulled away from the pack. Clark's rear suspension began to collapse on the final laps, with the right rear wheel displaying extreme positive camber. Clark delicately nursed the crippled car home to win by 6.3 seconds ahead of teammate Hill for a Lotus 1-2 finish! Denny Hulme finished 3rd in the Brabham-Repco, maintaining his championship lead over Jack Brabham, who finished 5th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
