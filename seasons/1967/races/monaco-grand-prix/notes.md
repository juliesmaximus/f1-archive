### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Tragic race overshadowed by the horrific accident of Ferrari team leader Lorenzo Bandini on lap 82. Exhausted from chasing leader Denny Hulme in punishing heat, Bandini clipped the chicane barrier; his Ferrari 312 overturned, ruptured its fuel tanks, and burst into an uncontrollable fireball against straw bales. Bandini suffered catastrophic third-degree burns over 70% of his body and succumbed three days later. The disaster led to the immediate worldwide banning of straw bales in Grand Prix racing and the introduction of mandatory fire-resistant Nomex overalls. Denny Hulme drove a flawless race to score his MAIDEN Grand Prix victory for Brabham-Repco, finishing a lap ahead of Graham Hill (Lotus-BRM).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme's first career victory launched his 1967 championship campaign. Bandini's death accelerated international safety reforms.
