## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Ford) — 1:59:25.600  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  (1:25.300)
- **Fastest Lap:** Denny Hulme (Brabham-Repco) — 1:27.000 (Lap 3)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** BRDC grandstands at Woodcote, Copse, and Abbey; crowded infield  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark took pole in the Lotus 49 Cosworth DFV and dominated the race, leading 51 of 80 laps to win his 5th British Grand Prix (and 3rd at Silverstone) by 12.8s over Denny Hulme's Brabham. Chris Amon took 3rd for Ferrari. Jack Brabham finished 4th. Graham Hill led early but retired with suspension and engine failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jim Clark became the first driver in history to win five British Grands Prix (1962, 1963, 1964, 1965, 1967).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Clark equalled Alain Prost's eventual British GP record.
