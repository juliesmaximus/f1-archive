## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  (1:47.560)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Ford) — 1:48.130 (Lap 52)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry, thin air; 23°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Colossal Estadio grandstands encircling Peraltada hairpin and pit straight terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark dominated qualifying with pole position by 0.76s. Clark led almost from start to finish to score his 4th victory of the season and 24th of his career, finishing 1m 25s ahead of Jack Brabham. Denny Hulme drove a calm, calculated race to finish 3rd. By finishing 3rd, Denny 'The Bear' Hulme CLINCHED his first and only World Drivers' Championship by 5 points over teammate and team owner Jack Brabham (51 net points to 46 net points). Brabham-Repco won their second consecutive Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Manufacturers).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme became the first New Zealander to win the Formula One World Drivers' Championship. Brabham celebrated back-to-back double World Championships.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 4 of last 5 races counted.
