## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Pole Position:** Graham Hill  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, overcast, dry; 24°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** Sparse crowd of only 20,000 spectators (disliked following the 24 Hours of Le Mans)  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Colossal 24 Hours of Le Mans permanent grandstands along the pit straight  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
The only World Championship Grand Prix ever held on the Bugatti layout inside the Circuit de la Sarthe. Graham Hill and Jim Clark qualified 1-2 for Lotus but both suffered suspension/differential failures on the twisty infield course. Jack Brabham and Denny Hulme took command to deliver a crushing 1-2 finish for Brabham-Repco, with Brabham winning by 49.5 seconds over Hulme. Jackie Stewart finished 3rd in the BRM.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The Bugatti Circuit was widely disliked by drivers and fans as dull and unworthy of the French Grand Prix; Formula One never returned. Brabham took the championship lead.
