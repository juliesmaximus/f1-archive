- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, wet, torrential rainstorms and thick spray throughout; 13°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent paddock grandstand, Corner 2 hillside banks, and Corner 5 bowl  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
The inaugural Canadian Grand Prix to count towards the Formula One World Championship. Run in treacherous torrential rain. Jim Clark took pole and led early in the Lotus 49 until electrical ignition issues from water soaking struck on lap 67. Jack Brabham and Denny Hulme demonstrated masterful wet-weather car control to score their third 1-2 sweep of the season, Brabham winning by over a minute. Dan Gurney finished 3rd in the Eagle-Weslake.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First Formula One World Championship round hosted in Canada. Jack Brabham closed the gap to Hulme in the title fight.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 90 laps (356.1 km).
