- **Fastest Lap:** Denny Hulme (Brabham-Repco) — 1:29.900 (Lap 3)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 26°C ambient, thin high-altitude air  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne spectator grass banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
First World Championship Grand Prix held at the newly constructed Kyalami circuit. Local Rhodesian privateer John Love led the race in his 2.7-litre Tasman Cooper-Climax, coming within 7 laps of a fairytale privateer victory before having to make an emergency pitstop for misfiring fuel injection. Mexican Pedro Rodríguez swept through to claim his maiden Formula One victory and the final Grand Prix win for the factory Cooper Car Company in the Cooper T81-Maserati.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Kyalami replaced East London as the permanent home of the South African Grand Prix. First F1 victory for Pedro Rodríguez and first win for Mexico.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of the first 6 races and best 4 of the last 5 races counted.
