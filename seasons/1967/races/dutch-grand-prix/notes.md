### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
ONE OF THE MOST FAMOUS RACES IN FORMULA ONE HISTORY: Colin Chapman unveiled the brand-new Lotus 49, featuring the revolutionary Ford Cosworth DFV (Double Four Valve) 3.0L V8, funded by Ford of Britain and engineered by Keith Duckworth and Mike Costin. The engine served as a fully stressed structural member of the chassis. Graham Hill took pole position by 0.5s on the car's debut. In the race, after Hill retired with timing gear failure, Jim Clark swept into the lead on lap 16 and won the race ON DEBUT by 23.6s over Jack Brabham! Denny Hulme finished 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The debut of the Ford Cosworth DFV engine altered motorsport history forever. The DFV engine family went on to win an unprecedented 155 World Championship Grands Prix, 12 Drivers' Championships, and 10 Constructors' titles between 1967 and 1983.
