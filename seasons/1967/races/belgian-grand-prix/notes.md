## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Dan Gurney (Eagle-Weslake) — 1:40:49.400  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient, fast track conditions  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 60,000 spectators across the 14-kilometre circuit  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main pit grandstand, Eau Rouge hillside banks, and Stavelot terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HISTORIC ALL-AMERICAN TRIUMPH: Jim Clark dominated qualifying with pole position at an average speed of 146.47 mph (235.72 km/h). In the race, Clark led comfortably until pitted with a spark plug issue. Dan Gurney in the exquisite titanium-and-magnesium Anglo American Racers Eagle T1G Weslake V12 charged into the lead, setting the fastest lap and winning by over a minute ahead of Jackie Stewart (BRM) and Chris Amon (Ferrari).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first and only Formula One World Championship victory for an American-built car driven by an American driver on European soil. Considered one of the most beautiful and iconic Grand Prix cars ever constructed.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
