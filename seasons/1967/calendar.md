# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1967 - Calendar

The 1967 Formula One season was the 18th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 11 championship rounds between January 2 and October 22, 1967.

The 1967 season was one of the most technologically consequential seasons in Grand Prix history. It witnessed the debut of the legendary **Lotus 49** and the revolutionary **Ford Cosworth DFV (Double Four Valve)** 3.0-litre V8 engine at Zandvoort, where Jim Clark took victory on debut. Despite Clark winning four races in the rapid Lotus 49, the championship was won by the relentless consistency and mechanical reliability of New Zealander **Denny Hulme** and teammate Jack Brabham driving the Brabham BT20/BT24 Repco. Hulme claimed his maiden World Drivers' Championship, and Brabham clinched their second consecutive International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
