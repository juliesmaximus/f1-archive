### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
One of the greatest slipstreaming battles in Grand Prix history. Over 76 laps, the lead changed hands officially 40 times between Jim Clark, Graham Hill, Jackie Stewart, and John Surtees. Clark retired on lap 63 with fuel pump failure. Surtees retired with clutch failure. On the penultimate lap, Graham Hill ran slightly wide at the Curva Parabolica, allowing rookie teammate Jackie Stewart to seize the lead and take his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory by 3.3 seconds over Hill. Dan Gurney finished 3rd for Brabham.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
First career Grand Prix victory for future triple World Champion Jackie Stewart in his debut season with BRM. BRM took a triumphant 1-2 finish on Ferrari's home soil.
