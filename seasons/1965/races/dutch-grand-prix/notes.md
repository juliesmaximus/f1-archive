## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) — 2:03:59.100  
- **Pole Position:** Graham Hill  (1:30.700)
- **Fastest Lap:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) — 1:30.600 (Lap 5)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Breezy and overcast with light sunny intervals, dry track; blown dune sand  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered main grandstand on pit straight, Tarzan hairpin viewing towers and dune ridges  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Graham Hill took pole position in the BRM P261, but Jim Clark charged past into the lead on lap 6. Clark steadily pulled away to claim his 5th consecutive GP victory of 1965, finishing 8.0 seconds ahead of Jackie Stewart, who passed Hill for 2nd place. Richie Ginther scored Honda's best result to date with 6th place (1 championship point).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Lotus and Clark put one hand firmly on both the Drivers' and Constructors' titles. Jackie Stewart's third runner-up finish of his rookie season.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
