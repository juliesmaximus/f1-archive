## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) — 2:23:34.800  
- **Pole Position:** Graham Hill  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Torrential rain, dense fog, standing water, and treacherous aquaplaning throughout  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 50,000 soaked spectators in Ardennes pine forests  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered grandstand opposite pits and natural earthen embankments along Eau Rouge and Blanchimont  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Torrential rain made conditions extraordinarily dangerous. Jim Clark took the lead from 2nd on the grid on lap 1 and dominated in the wet, winning by 44.8 seconds over rookie Jackie Stewart. Bruce McLaren finished 3rd in the Cooper. Clark recorded his fourth consecutive Belgian Grand Prix victory (1962, 1963, 1964, 1965).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Further solidified Jim Clark's unmatched mastery of the terrifying 14 km Spa circuit in wet conditions, despite Clark personally disliking the track.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
