## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) — 2:06  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, clear, and sunny; strong coastal wind blowing off the Indian Ocean  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 45,000 estimated spectators lining the sand dunes and grandstands  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Temporary wooden grandstands facing the pit straight and natural coastal dune spectator banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark produced a flawless Grand Slam (pole, fastest lap, led every lap, won). Mike Spence finished 4th for Lotus. Jo Bonnier and Jochen Rindt (Cooper debut) suffered mechanical retirements.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Held on New Year's Day (January 1), 1965, marking the earliest start to a Formula One World Championship season in history. Clark showcased the upgraded Lotus 33 Climax V8.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Best 6 results counted towards both Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
