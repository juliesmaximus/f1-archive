- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Overcast, cool breeze, dry track; 19°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** BRDC grandstands at Woodcote, Copse, and Abbey; crowded runway banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark took pole and pulled out an enormous 35-second lead. With 10 laps remaining, Clark's Coventry Climax V8 began losing oil pressure due to a failing scavenger pump. Clark masterfully killed the engine in high-speed sweeps (Stowe, Club) and coasted to preserve oil, nursing the dying car. Graham Hill hunted him down relentlessly, closing the gap to 3.2 seconds at the chequered flag, but Clark held on for his fourth consecutive British Grand Prix win.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the most tactical finishes in British Grand Prix history. Surtees finished 3rd for Ferrari. Jackie Stewart took 5th.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
