## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Richie Ginther (Honda) — 2:08:32.100  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, high-altitude sunshine; thin air, 23°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 80,000 cheering Mexican fans packing the stadium complex  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Colossal Estadio grandstands encircling Peraltada hairpin and pit straight balconies  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Richie Ginther took the lead from 3rd on the grid at the start in his transverse 1.5-litre V12 Honda RA272. Ginther drove an immaculate race, leading every single lap to score his MAIDEN Grand Prix victory, and the historic FIRST Formula One victory for HONDA and for GOODYEAR tyres! Dan Gurney finished 2nd for Brabham (just 2.89s behind), with Mike Spence 3rd for Lotus. Clark and Hill both retired with engine failures.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Monumental milestone: first Japanese constructor to win a Formula One World Championship Grand Prix. Final race of the 1.5L unsupercharged engine era before the 3.0L regulations took effect in 1966.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Final race of the 1.5-litre engine regulations.
