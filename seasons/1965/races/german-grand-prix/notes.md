### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark took pole position with a stunning 8:22.7 lap. In the race, Clark led every single lap to take victory by 15.9 seconds over BRM's Graham Hill. Dan Gurney finished 3rd in the Brabham. With this 6th consecutive victory, Jim Clark mathematically CLINCHED his second Formula One World Drivers' Championship and delivered the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers to Team Lotus with three rounds to spare, having scored the maximum possible 54 net points (6 wins x 9 points).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Clark became a two-time World Champion at the age of 29. Lotus celebrated their second Constructors' crown. Jochen Rindt scored 4th for Cooper.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Maximum possible score achieved.
