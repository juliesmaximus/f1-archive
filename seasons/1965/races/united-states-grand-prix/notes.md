### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Graham Hill took pole position and engaged in a fierce battle with Jim Clark and Dan Gurney. Clark retired on lap 11 with engine failure. Hill drove a commanding race to victory, fending off Dan Gurney's Brabham by 12.5 seconds. Jack Brabham finished 3rd in the sister car. Hill's victory secured 2nd place in the World Drivers' Championship.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Graham Hill's third consecutive victory at the United States Grand Prix at Watkins Glen (1963, 1964, 1965). Richest purse in Grand Prix racing.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
