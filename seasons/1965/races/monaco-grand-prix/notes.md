### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
Jim Clark skipped Monaco to race in and win the Indianapolis 500. On lap 25, leader Graham Hill was blocked by Bob Anderson's Brabham at the chicane, had to take the escape road, stall, and push his BRM back onto the track, dropping to 5th. Hill mounted a breathtaking charge to overtake Jackie Stewart, Lorenzo Bandini, and John Surtees to take an improbable victory (earning his reputation as 'Mr. Monaco'). Paul Hawkins plunged into Monaco harbour in his private Lotus 33 at the chicane on lap 79, escaping unhurt—echoing Alberto Ascari's 1955 splash.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Graham Hill's third consecutive Monaco Grand Prix victory. Debut Monaco podium for rookie Jackie Stewart in 3rd place.
