## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) — 2:41:38.400  
- **Pole Position:** Jim Clark  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm and sunny, dry track; loose volcanic volcanic gravel stones on track margins  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 65,000 spectators across the volcanic hillsides  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent pits grandstand and volcanic hillside natural terraces overlooking the mountain descent  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
First World Championship Grand Prix held at the spectacular Charade Circuit, dubbed the 'French Nürburgring' with 51 corners winding around an extinct volcano. Jim Clark delivered another dominant Grand Slam: pole position, fastest lap, led all 40 laps, and won by 26.3s over Jackie Stewart. John Surtees finished 3rd in the Ferrari 158.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme scored his first World Championship points, finishing 4th in the Brabham-Climax. Drivers universally praised Charade as one of the ultimate driver tests.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6.
