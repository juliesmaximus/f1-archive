# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1965 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Only the best **6** results from the 10 championship rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Jim Clark clinched his second World Drivers' Championship with the absolute maximum possible score of 54 points (6 victories x 9 points), dropping 0 points because his only other finishes were retirements or skipped rounds (Monaco).

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **6** results counted towards the championship.

Team Lotus won their second International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with the maximum possible score of 54 points.

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores:** Best 6 of 10 rounds counted towards both championships.
- **Engine Regulations:** Final year of the 1.5-litre unsupercharged engine formula (1961–1965).
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Jim Clark achieved the maximum possible 54 net championship points for the second time (previously 1963).
  - Clark became the only driver to win both the Formula One World Drivers' Championship and the Indianapolis 500 in the same calendar year (1965).
  - Jackie Stewart took his maiden Grand Prix victory at Monza in his rookie season.
  - Richie Ginther and Honda scored their maiden Grand Prix victory at Mexico City, representing the first F1 victory for a Japanese car and the first for Goodyear tyres.
