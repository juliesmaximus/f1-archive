# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1965 - Calendar

The 1965 Formula One season was the 16th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 10 championship rounds between January 1 and October 24, 1965.

This marked the fifth and final year of the 1.5-litre unsupercharged engine regulations (1961–1965). Jim Clark and Team Lotus dominated, winning six races to claim Clark's second World Drivers' Championship and Lotus's second International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with the maximum possible 54 net points. Clark also famously skipped the Monaco Grand Prix to win the 1965 Indianapolis 500 in the Lotus 38-Ford, becoming the first driver to conquer both in the same era.
