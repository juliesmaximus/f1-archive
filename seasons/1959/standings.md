# 1959 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1959 World Championship of Drivers and the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers. Only each driver's best 5 results out of the 9 rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship tally. Only each constructor's best 5 results out of the 8 eligible Grands Prix counted towards the Manufacturers' Cup (Indianapolis 500 did not count toward the Manufacturers' Cup).

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Drivers' Championship Points**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers. 1 bonus point awarded for fastest lap (split equally if shared).
- **Manufacturers' Cup Points**: 8-6-4-3-2 awarded to top 5 classified finishers for each make (only highest-placing car of each constructor scored). No bonus point for fastest lap.
- **Best 5 rule**: Only best 5 results counted for both Drivers and Constructors.
