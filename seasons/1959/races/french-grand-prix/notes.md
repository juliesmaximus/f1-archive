# 1959 French Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: 45e Grand Prix de l'ACF / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Round**: 4 of 9 in the 1959 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 5 July 1959
- **Location**: Reims-Gueux, Reims, France
- **Circuit Length**: 8.302 km (5.159 miles)
- **Distance**: 50 laps (415.100 km / 257.931 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Tony Brooks (Ferrari Dino 246) - 2:19.400
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Rob Walker Cooper-Climax) - 2:22.800 (Lap 40) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Tony Brooks (Ferrari Dino 246) - 2:01:26.500 (205.075 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Tony Brooks (Ferrari) - 50 laps
  2. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 50 laps (+27.500s)
  3. Jack Brabham (Cooper-Climax) - 50 laps (+1:37.700)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Scorching Reims Heat & Melting Asphalt**: The temperature soared above 40°C in the Champagne sun. The asphalt softened and melted, and passing cars hurled melting bitumen and stones at high velocity. Several drivers suffered cuts and eye injuries from flying asphalt chunks.
- **Tony Brooks Grand Slam Performance**: Tony Brooks started from pole, led from start to finish, and survived severe cockpit heat to win by 27.5 seconds, leading a Ferrari 1-2 finish with Phil Hill.
- **Behra-Ferrari Fallout**: Frustrated by engine failure while battling for 2nd, French driver Jean Behra confronted Ferrari team manager Romolo Tavoni in the restaurant after the race. The heated exchange turned physical, and Enzo Ferrari fired Behra immediately.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Oppressively hot summer heatwave; ambient temperature over 40°C; severe track deterioration.
