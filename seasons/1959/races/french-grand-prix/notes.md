## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Melting Tarmac Attrition: Intense sun melted the bitumen at Thillois hairpin and Gueux curve, gouging deep ruts and hurling gravel. Drivers suffered broken goggles and bruised faces. Masten Gregory collapsed from heat exhaustion after leading early.
- Tony Brooks Dominant Ferrari Win: Brooks led from pole position and dominated all 50 laps in his Ferrari Dino 246, winning by 27.5 seconds over teammate Phil Hill.
- Jack Brabham 3rd: Brabham stayed cool in his Cooper to take 3rd place, retaining his championship lead.
- Stirling Moss Black-Flagged: Moss spun at Thillois when his transmission seized, accepted a push-start from spectators to restart, and was disqualified by race officials.
