# 1959 Dutch Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: VIII Grote Prijs van Nederland
- **Round**: 3 of 9 in the 1959 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 31 May 1959
- **Location**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort, Netherlands
- **Circuit Length**: 4.193 km (2.605 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (314.475 km / 195.405 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jo Bonnier (BRM P25) - 1:36.000
- **Fastest Lap**: Stirling Moss (Rob Walker Cooper-Climax) - 1:36.700 [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Jo Bonnier (BRM P25) - 2:05:26.800 (150.397 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jo Bonnier (BRM) - 75 laps
  2. Jack Brabham (Cooper-Climax) - 75 laps (+14.200s)
  3. Masten Gregory (Cooper-Climax) - 75 laps (+1:23.000)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **First World Championship Victory for BRM**: After a decade of bitter mechanical failures and public ridicule, British Racing Motors (BRM) achieved its dream of winning a World Championship Grand Prix, as Jo Bonnier piloted the front-engine BRM P25 to victory.
- **Bonnier's Only Championship Win**: This was the first and only World Championship Grand Prix victory for Sweden's Jo Bonnier, who converted his pole position into an emotional victory.
- **Brabham Consolidates Lead**: By finishing 2nd behind Bonnier, Jack Brabham took a commanding early lead in the World Drivers' Championship.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Sunny, breezy seaside conditions; sand blown onto parts of the circuit.
