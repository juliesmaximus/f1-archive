## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- BRM Maiden Victory: Swedish driver Joakim 'Jo' Bonnier scored the FIRST-EVER WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX VICTORY FOR BRITISH RACING MOTORS (BRM) in the front-engine BRM P25, ending nearly a decade of heartbreak for the Bourne team.
- First Swedish GP Winner: Bonnier became the FIRST AND ONLY SWEDISH DRIVER TO WIN A FORMULA ONE GRAND PRIX until Ronnie Peterson in 1973.
- Jack Brabham 2nd: Brabham drove a steady race in his Cooper-Climax to finish 2nd (+14.2s), maintaining his championship lead.
- Moss Gearbox Heartbreak: Stirling Moss passed Bonnier for the lead on lap 60, only for his gearbox to fail on lap 63.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship victory and pole position for British Racing Motors (BRM).
- Maiden World Championship race victory and pole for Jo Bonnier.
- First Swedish driver to win a World Championship Grand Prix.
