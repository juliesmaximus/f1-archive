# 1959 Monaco Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XVII Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco
- **Round**: 1 of 9 in the 1959 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 10 May 1959
- **Location**: Circuit de Monaco, Monte Carlo, Monaco
- **Circuit Length**: 3.145 km (1.954 miles)
- **Distance**: 100 laps (314.500 km / 195.421 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Rob Walker Cooper-Climax T51) - 1:39.600
- **Fastest Lap**: Jack Brabham (Cooper-Climax T51) - 1:40.400 (Lap 83) [1 pt]
- **Race Winner**: Jack Brabham (Cooper-Climax T51) - 2:55:51.300 (107.304 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jack Brabham (Cooper-Climax) - 100 laps
  2. Tony Brooks (Ferrari) - 100 laps (+20.400s)
  3. Maurice Trintignant (Cooper-Climax) - 98 laps (+2 laps)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Brabham's Maiden Victory**: Australian Jack Brabham drove a flawless race in the factory mid-engine Cooper-Climax T51 to take his first career World Championship Grand Prix victory.
- **Moss's Transmission Heartbreak**: Stirling Moss started from pole and dominated the opening 80 laps before his transmission broke, handing the lead to Brabham.
- **Mid-Engine Breakthrough**: Cooper-Climax cars took 1st and 3rd on the podium, demonstrating the undeniable agility and balance of John Cooper's lightweight rear-engine design on the tight streets of Monaco.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, pleasant Mediterranean spring sunshine; completely dry asphalt.
