## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Jack Brabham Maiden Grand Prix Victory: Driving the factory Cooper T51-Climax, 33-year-old Australian Jack Brabham scored his FIRST career Formula One World Championship Grand Prix victory, winning by 20.4 seconds over Tony Brooks' Ferrari.
- Mid-Engine Domination: Cooper-Climax mid-engined cars proved uncatchable through the tight streets. Stirling Moss led from pole position for 81 laps until his transmission failed, handing the lead to Brabham.
- Brooks Heat Exhaustion: Tony Brooks battled severe cockpit heat in his front-engine Ferrari Dino 246, throwing up into his cockpit during the race but bravely holding onto 2nd place.
