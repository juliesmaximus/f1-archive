# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1972 - Calendar

The 1972 Formula One season was the 23rd season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 12 championship rounds between January 23 and October 8, 1972.

The 1972 season witnessed Brazilian sensation **Emerson Fittipaldi** capture his maiden World Drivers' Championship driving Colin Chapman's iconic black-and-gold **John Player Special Lotus 72D** powered by the Ford Cosworth DFV V8. Winning five Grands Prix (Spain, Belgium, Great Britain, Austria, Italy), Fittipaldi clinched the championship at Monza at the age of **25 years and 273 days**, becoming the **youngest World Drivers' Champion in Formula One history**—a record that stood for 33 years until Fernando Alonso won in 2005. Team Lotus claimed their fifth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers.
