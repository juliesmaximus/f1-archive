### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
JEAN-PIERRE BELTOISE'S WET MASTERPIECE: Run in apocalyptic torrential rain. Emerson Fittipaldi took pole position in the Lotus 72D, but French veteran Jean-Pierre Beltoise in the Marlborough BRM P160B launched from 4th on the grid into the lead into Saint Dévote. In a drive of breathtaking brilliance on Firestone rain tyres, Beltoise drove away from the entire world-class field, lapping everyone up to 3rd place and beating rain-master Jacky Ickx (Ferrari) by 38.2 seconds! Fittipaldi took 3rd, Stewart 4th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jean-Pierre Beltoise scored his FIRST and ONLY career Formula One World Championship victory, and the 17th and FINAL Grand Prix victory in the illustrious history of British Racing Motors (BRM).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scheduled for 80 laps (251.6 km). Paddock relocated to the harbour front for the first time.
