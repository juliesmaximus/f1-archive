## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Emerson Fittipaldi (Team Lotus) — 2:03:41.200  
- **Pole Position:** Jacky Ickx  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 26°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 55,000 spectators lining the Jarama bowl  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstand, Pegaso curve terraces, and Farina hillsides  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI'S FIRST WIN OF 1972: Jacky Ickx took pole for Ferrari. Jackie Stewart seized the lead in the Tyrrell 003 before making a rare driving error, spinning off into the catch fencing on lap 28. Emerson Fittipaldi in the black-and-gold Lotus 72D took over the lead, pulling away to win by a full lap over Jacky Ickx and Clay Regazzoni. Andrea de Adamich finished 4th in the Surtees.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi moved into a tie with Denny Hulme at the top of the World Drivers' Championship with 15 points.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 90 laps (306.36 km).
