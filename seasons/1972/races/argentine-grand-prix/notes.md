### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
REUTEMANN'S DEBUT POLE & STEWART'S WIN: Argentine rookie Carlos Reutemann caused an international sensation by taking pole position on his Formula One World Championship debut in the Brabham BT34! In the punishing heat, World Champion Jackie Stewart seized the lead in the Tyrrell 003 and dominated, winning by 25.96s over Denny Hulme (McLaren) and Jacky Ickx (Ferrari). Reutemann suffered severe tyre blistering, finishing 7th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The Argentine Grand Prix returned to the official championship calendar for the first time since 1960. Carlos Reutemann became only the fourth driver in history to score pole position on his Grand Prix debut.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 5 of first 6 races and best 5 of last 6 races counted.
