### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
STEWART REBOUNDS TO WIN: Peter Revson took pole in the McLaren M19A. In the race, newly crowned World Champion Emerson Fittipaldi led early before tyre degradation dropped him back. Jackie Stewart in the Tyrrell 005 Ford Cosworth DFV seized the lead on lap 49 and pulled away to win by 48.2 seconds over Peter Revson and Denny Hulme (McLaren 2-3). Carlos Reutemann finished 4th for Brabham.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart solidified 2nd place in the World Drivers' Championship behind Fittipaldi.
