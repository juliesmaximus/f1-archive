## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Fastest Lap:** Mike Hailwood (Surtees-Ford) — 1:18.900 (Lap 20)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit straight covered grandstands and Crowthorne spectator grass banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HULME WINS FOR McLAREN: Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 003 and led early until gearbox trouble forced him into retirement on lap 45. Denny Hulme in the McLaren M19A took command, fending off Emerson Fittipaldi's Lotus 72D by 14.1s to score McLaren's first Grand Prix win since 1969! American Peter Revson completed the podium in 3rd for McLaren.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Denny Hulme took the lead of the World Championship. Team Lotus unveiled the distinctive black-and-gold John Player Special livery.
