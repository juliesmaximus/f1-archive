### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI EDGES HULME: Emerson Fittipaldi qualified on pole in the Lotus 72D. In the race, Stewart had an uncompetitive outing with handling issues, finishing 7th outside the points. Fittipaldi drove a masterclass in tyre preservation to hold off Denny Hulme's McLaren by just 1.18 seconds! American Peter Revson took 3rd for McLaren, and Mike Hailwood finished 4th for Surtees.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
With 4 wins and 52 points, Emerson Fittipaldi entered Monza needing only to finish ahead of Stewart to clinch the World Championship.
