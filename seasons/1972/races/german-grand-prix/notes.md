### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
JACKY ICKX'S NORDSCHLEIFE MASTERPIECE: Belgian ace Jacky Ickx produced one of the most dominant performances in Nürburgring history in the Ferrari 312B2, taking pole position (7:07.0), setting the fastest lap, leading all 14 laps, and winning by 48.3 seconds over teammate Clay Regazzoni for a commanding Ferrari 1-2 sweep! Ronnie Peterson finished 3rd in the March. Jackie Stewart crashed on the final lap after colliding with Regazzoni. Emerson Fittipaldi retired on lap 10 with a fractured gearbox oil pipe.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jacky Ickx's 8th and final career Formula One victory. Reignited Ferrari's mathematical challenge in the championship.
