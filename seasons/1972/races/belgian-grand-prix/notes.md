### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL GP AT NIVELLES: The Belgian Grand Prix moved to the newly built Nivelles-Baulers circuit near Brussels following the abandonment of the dangerous 14km Spa track. Emerson Fittipaldi took pole in the Lotus 72D and drove an immaculate tactical race, holding off a fierce challenge from François Cevert's Tyrrell 002 to win by 2.66 seconds! Denny Hulme took 3rd for McLaren. Jackie Stewart missed the race due to a bleeding stomach ulcer.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi established a clear 9-point lead in the World Drivers' Championship over Denny Hulme.
