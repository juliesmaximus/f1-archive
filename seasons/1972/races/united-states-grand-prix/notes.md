## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell) — 1:41:45.354  
- **Pole Position:** Jackie Stewart  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Cold, crisp, sunny autumn day; 13°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 115,000 spectators packing the Watkins Glen hills  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Boot, and Seneca spectator knolls  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
CRUSHING TYRRELL 1-2 SWEEP: Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 005. Stewart and teammate François Cevert dominated from the green flag, pulling out an unassailable lead to score a dominant 1-2 finish for the Tyrrell Racing Organisation, Stewart winning by 32.2 seconds over Cevert! Denny Hulme finished 3rd for McLaren, and Ronnie Peterson was 4th. Jody Scheckter made an impressive Formula One debut in the third McLaren, finishing 9th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The final Grand Prix of the 1972 season. Jackie Stewart concluded the year with 4 wins, securing runner-up in the World Championship.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Record $275,000 total purse ($50,000 to the winner).
