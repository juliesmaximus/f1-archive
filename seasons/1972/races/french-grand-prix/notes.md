- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, bright, sunny; 27°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent pits grandstand and volcanic hillside natural terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FINAL GP AT CHARADE & HELMUT MARKO'S TRAGEDY: Chris Amon took pole for Matra and led until a puncture from the notorious volcanic stones forced a pitstop. On lap 9, Austrian driver Dr. Helmut Marko (BRM P160B) was following Emerson Fittipaldi's Lotus through a downhill bend when a sharp volcanic rock thrown up by Fittipaldi's rear tyre pierced Marko's visor, destroying his left eye and blinding him permanently, ending his driving career. Jackie Stewart took victory for Tyrrell by 27.7s ahead of Fittipaldi, with Amon charging back to 3rd.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The final Formula One Grand Prix held at the magnificent Charade Circuit. Helmut Marko later became Red Bull Racing's renowned motorsport advisor.
