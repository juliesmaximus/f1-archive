# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1972 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Brazilian Emerson Fittipaldi won his maiden World Drivers' Championship with 61 net points (61 gross), becoming the youngest World Champion in Formula One history (25 years, 273 days).

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the championship.

Team Lotus won the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 61 net points (61 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split into two mini-seasons: best 5 of first 6 rounds, best 5 of final 6 rounds.
  - McLaren-Ford scored 49 gross points, dropping 2 points to finish with 47 net points.
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.0-litre naturally aspirated or 1.5-litre supercharged/turbocharged. Minimum car weight 550 kg (raised by 20 kg).
- **Safety Regulations:** Mandatory safety foam in fuel tanks, crushable side structures, red rear safety light, and headrest dimensions introduced.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Emerson Fittipaldi won his maiden World Drivers' Championship at age 25y 273d, the youngest in Formula 1 history until 2005.
  - Carlos Reutemann scored pole position on his World Championship debut at the Argentine Grand Prix driving for Brabham.
  - Jean-Pierre Beltoise took his sole career victory at Monaco in torrential rain, giving BRM their 17th and final Grand Prix victory.
  - Bernie Ecclestone purchased the Brabham team from Ron Tauranac prior to the start of the season.
