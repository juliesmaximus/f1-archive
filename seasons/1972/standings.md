# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1972 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and the best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Brazilian Emerson Fittipaldi won his maiden World Drivers' Championship with 61 net points (61 gross), becoming the youngest World Champion in Formula One history (25 years, 273 days).

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **5** results from the first 6 races and best **5** results from the final 6 races counted towards the championship.

Team Lotus won their fifth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 61 net points (61 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 5 results from rounds 1–6 and best 5 results from rounds 7–12 counted towards both championships.
- **Safety Milestones:**
  - Monza introduced permanent chicanes at Curva Grande and Variante Ascari to reduce extreme slipstreaming speeds.
  - Tragic eye injury to Dr. Helmut Marko at Charade ended the circuit's use in Formula One.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Emerson Fittipaldi became the youngest World Drivers' Champion in history (25 years, 273 days), a record unbroken until 2005.
  - Team Lotus won their 5th Constructors' Championship in the iconic black-and-gold John Player Special livery.
  - Carlos Reutemann scored pole position on his Grand Prix debut at Buenos Aires.
  - Jean-Pierre Beltoise won Monaco in torrential rain, scoring the 17th and final Grand Prix victory for BRM.
