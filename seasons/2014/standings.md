## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Dawn of the Turbo-Hybrid Era:** 1.6L turbocharged V6 Power Units with MGU-K and MGU-H introduced.
- **Mercedes AMG Petronas Total Supremacy:** Won 16 of 19 races, scoring an all-time record 701 Constructors' points.
- **Lewis Hamilton Second World Championship:** Won 11 Grands Prix to clinch his second World Drivers' Championship.
- **Tragedy of Jules Bianchi:** The tragic accident at Suzuka in typhoon conditions that claimed the life of Jules Bianchi and revolutionized safety with the Virtual Safety Car and Halo.
- **The 'Abu Double' Finale:** One-off double points experiment awarded in Abu Dhabi.
