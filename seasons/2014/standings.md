## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Dawn of the Turbo-Hybrid Era:** 2014 introduced the revolutionary 1.6-litre turbocharged V6 power unit regulations featuring complex Energy Recovery Systems (MGU-K and MGU-H) and a strict 100 kg/h fuel flow limit.
- **Mercedes Hybrid Dominance & Silver War:** Mercedes AMG Petronas established absolute supremacy, scoring 701 points, 16 victories from 19 races, and an all-time record 11 1-2 finishes. Lewis Hamilton secured his second World Drivers' Championship with 11 victories, triumphing over teammate Nico Rosberg in a duel that concluded with a 50-point victory in the double-points Abu Dhabi finale.
- **Daniel Ricciardo Breakthrough:** In his first season with Red Bull Racing, Daniel Ricciardo was the sole non-Mercedes driver to win a race, taking three sensational victories (Canada, Hungary, Belgium) and outscoring four-time reigning World Champion Sebastian Vettel.
- **Williams Renaissance:** Swapping to Mercedes power units, Williams enjoyed their most competitive campaign in a decade, taking 3rd in the Constructors' Championship with 9 podiums and a 1-2 front-row lockout in Austria (Felipe Massa pole).
- **Tragedy at Suzuka:** During a torrential Japanese Grand Prix at Suzuka, Jules Bianchi suffered fatal head injuries after colliding with a recovery vehicle. Bianchi had earlier scored historic maiden points for Marussia with 9th place in Monaco.
