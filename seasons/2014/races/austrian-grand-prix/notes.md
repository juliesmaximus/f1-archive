## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Formula 1 returned to Austria after an 11-year absence. Williams stunned the paddock by locking out the front row in qualifying (Massa on pole, Bottas 2nd). In the race, Mercedes used superior pitstop execution to undercut both Williams, Rosberg leading Hamilton in a 1-2 finish. Valtteri Bottas scored his maiden Formula 1 podium in 3rd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Massa's pole was the only non-Mercedes pole position of the entire 2014 season.
