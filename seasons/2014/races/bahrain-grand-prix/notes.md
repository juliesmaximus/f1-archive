## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The 'Duel in the Desert'—an immortal masterpiece of pure racing. Hamilton jumped pole-sitter Rosberg at the start. After a Safety Car on lap 41 (following Esteban Gutiérrez's violent barrel roll after being punted by Maldonado), Rosberg attacked Hamilton on softer tyres. For 10 breathtaking laps, the two Mercedes duelled wheel-to-wheel, crossing over lines, locking brakes, and inches apart through every turn. Hamilton defended with supreme aggression to win by 1.0 second. Sergio Pérez scored Force India's first podium since 2009 in 3rd.
