## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg scored a commanding home victory from pole position. Lewis Hamilton suffered a brake failure in qualifying, crashing heavily in Q1, and took a 5-place gearbox penalty to start 20th. Hamilton unleashed a ferocious comeback drive, executing aggressive overtakes (damaging his front wing against Button) to finish 3rd on the podium behind Valtteri Bottas.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
