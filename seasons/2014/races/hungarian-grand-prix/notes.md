## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the greatest races of the hybrid era. Hamilton's car caught fire in qualifying, forcing a pitlane start. In treacherous mixed conditions, Safety Cars on laps 9 and 23 scrambled the order. Fernando Alonso led on 30-lap-old soft tyres in the closing laps. Behind him, Hamilton defended furiously against Rosberg, refusing Mercedes team orders to let Rosberg past! Meanwhile, Daniel Ricciardo on fresh tyres launched an electrifying charge: passing Hamilton around the outside of Turn 2 and sweeping past Alonso with 2 laps to go to score his second victory!
