## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Championship swing in the night. Nico Rosberg suffered a steering column wiring harness failure on the grid; unable to select gears, he started from the pitlane and retired on lap 13. Lewis Hamilton controlled the race, surviving a late Safety Car that erased his lead by pumping in qualifying-style laps to pit and overtake Sebastian Vettel to take victory, regaining the World Championship lead by 3 points.
