## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The race of the season and a maiden triumph for Daniel Ricciardo. On lap 37, both dominant Mercedes cars suffered simultaneous MGU-K failures, losing 160 bhp and overheating rear brakes. Hamilton retired with brake failure on lap 46. Rosberg soldiered on, leading a furious train of Pérez, Ricciardo, and Massa. With 3 laps remaining, Ricciardo made an audacious outside pass on Pérez into Turn 1, then hunted down Rosberg, sweeping past with DRS on the back straight to take an ecstatic maiden victory! On the final lap, Massa and Pérez collided violently at 190 mph into Turn 1, destroying both cars into the barriers.
