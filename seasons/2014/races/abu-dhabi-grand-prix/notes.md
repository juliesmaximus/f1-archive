## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The 'Abu Double' finale. Double points (50 for 1st) were awarded for the first and only time in F1 history. Hamilton made a rocket start from 2nd on the grid, blowing past pole-sitter Rosberg into Turn 1. On lap 25, Rosberg suffered a catastrophic ERS failure, losing hybrid power and tumbling down the order. Despite being advised to retire his crippled car with two laps to go, Rosberg bravely requested to stay out and finish the race, crossing the line 14th. Lewis Hamilton won the race to claim his second World Drivers' Championship (384 points to 317), celebrating with Prince Harry over team radio and holding the Union Jack flag on the podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The universally despised double-points gimmick was permanently scrapped immediately after the race.
