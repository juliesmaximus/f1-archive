## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The Mercedes collision at Les Combes. Hamilton took the lead at the start. On lap 2, Rosberg attacked down the Kemmel Straight. Into Les Combes, Rosberg clipped Hamilton's left-rear tyre with his front wing endplate, puncturing Hamilton's tyre and ending his race. In a hostile post-race team briefing, Toto Wolff slammed the incident as 'unacceptable', revealing Rosberg admitted he did not pull out to 'prove a point'. Amidst the Mercedes civil war, Daniel Ricciardo drove an immaculate race to score back-to-back victories for Red Bull ahead of Rosberg and Bottas.
