## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton suffered a terrible start from pole due to an electronic start-mode glitch, dropping to 4th. Hamilton unleashed blistering pace, carving past Magnussen and Massa into 2nd. Under relentless pressure from Hamilton, race leader Nico Rosberg locked his front brakes twice into the first chicane, taking the escape road on lap 29. Hamilton swept through into the lead to win, kicking off a five-race winning streak.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Rosberg's identical lock-ups into the Rettifilo escape road sparked conspiracy theories that he was ordered to yield.
