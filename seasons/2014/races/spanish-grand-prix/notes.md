## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton and Rosberg staged another thrilling duel. Leading from pole, Hamilton managed chronic oversteer in the closing laps as Rosberg bore down on fresher tyres. Rosberg attacked down the main straight on the final lap, but Hamilton held him off by just 0.6 seconds to take four consecutive wins and seize the World Championship lead.
