## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton achieved his third consecutive victory in commanding fashion, leading every lap from pole position. Nico Rosberg overcame a telemetry failure to recover from 4th to 2nd, securing another Mercedes 1-2 finish. Fernando Alonso took Ferrari's first podium of 2014 in 3rd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The chequered flag was mistakenly waved a lap early (on lap 54 instead of 56), meaning official results were backdated to lap 54 under sporting regulations.
