## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The dawn of the 1.6-litre V6 Turbo-Hybrid era. Pole-sitter Lewis Hamilton suffered an engine misfire (running on 5 cylinders) and retired on lap 2. Teammate Nico Rosberg dominated the race, winning by 26.7 seconds. Home hero Daniel Ricciardo crossed the line 2nd on his Red Bull debut but was disqualified hours later when FIA scrutineers discovered his car consistently exceeded the 100 kg/h fuel flow limit. Kevin Magnussen scored a podium on debut for McLaren in 2nd ahead of Jenson Button.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ricciardo's fuel-flow disqualification sparked fierce legal appeals from Red Bull, ultimately dismissed by the FIA International Court of Appeal.
