## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg ended Hamilton's five-race winning streak with an immaculate drive from pole position. Hamilton pushed hard to overcut Rosberg during the second pitstops, but spun into the Descida do Lago on lap 28, losing 7 seconds. Hamilton hunted Rosberg down in the final stint, crossing the line just 1.4 seconds behind, taking a 17-point lead to the Abu Dhabi finale.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Felipe Massa finished 3rd on home soil despite serving a 5-second penalty for speeding in the pitlane and accidentally pulling into the McLaren pit box.
