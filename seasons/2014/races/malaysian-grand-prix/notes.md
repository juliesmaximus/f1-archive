## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton produced a crushing Grand Slam: pole position, victory, fastest lap, and led every single lap. Hamilton beat teammate Nico Rosberg by 17.3 seconds to secure Mercedes' first 1-2 finish of the turbo-hybrid era. Sebastian Vettel completed the podium in 3rd.
