## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
On lap 1, Kimi Räikkönen suffered a massive 47G crash into the guardrail on the Wellington Straight, triggering a red flag for 1 hour to repair the barriers. Pole-sitter Nico Rosberg dominated until his gearbox failed on lap 29—his first retirement of 2014. Lewis Hamilton took over the lead, cruising to victory before his ecstatic home crowd and slashing Rosberg's championship lead to 4 points.
