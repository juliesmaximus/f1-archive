## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton scored his 10th victory of 2014. Pole-sitter Nico Rosberg led until lap 24, when Hamilton launched a brilliant, aggressive dive down the inside into Turn 12, forcing Rosberg wide and seizing the lead. Hamilton became the most successful British driver in F1 history with his 32nd career victory, surpassing Nigel Mansell.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Caterham and Marussia entered administration and missed the race, leaving an 18-car grid.
