## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Inaugural Russian Grand Prix around the 2014 Winter Olympic Park. At the start, Nico Rosberg locked up heavily attacking Hamilton into Turn 2, flat-spotting his tyres and pitting on lap 1. Rosberg drove 52 laps on a single set of medium tyres to finish 2nd, securing Mercedes AMG Petronas their historic first FIA Formula 1 World Constructors' Championship with three races to spare.
