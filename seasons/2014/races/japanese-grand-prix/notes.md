## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The darkest, most heartbreaking day in modern Formula 1. Typhoon Phanfone brought treacherous, deteriorating rain. Lewis Hamilton pulled off a breathtaking outside overtake on Nico Rosberg into Turn 1 on lap 29 to take the lead. On lap 42, Adrian Sutil aquaplaned off at Dunlop Curve (Turn 7). One lap later, Jules Bianchi aquaplaned at the same spot in worsening gloom and slid under a recovery tractor that was retrieving Sutil's car. The race was red-flagged on lap 46. Bianchi suffered severe diffuse axonal brain injuries and tragically passed away nine months later in July 2015. There were no podium celebrations.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Bianchi's tragic accident led to the permanent implementation of the Virtual Safety Car (VSC) and the development of the Halo cockpit protection system.
