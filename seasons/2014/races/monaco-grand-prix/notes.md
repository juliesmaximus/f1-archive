## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The Mirabeau controversy that ignited total war at Mercedes. On his final flying lap in Q3, Rosberg locked up and took to the escape road at Mirabeau, bringing out yellow flags and forcing teammate Hamilton to abort his lap, securing Rosberg pole position. In the race, Rosberg controlled the pace to win. Hamilton suffered from dirt in his left eye in the closing laps and defended 2nd from Ricciardo. In the post-race press conference, Hamilton refused to congratulate Rosberg, stating: 'We are not friends.'

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Jules Bianchi drove an astonishing race for Marussia, scoring the team's first-ever Formula 1 points in 9th place.
