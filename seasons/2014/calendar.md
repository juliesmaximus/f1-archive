## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **1.6L V6 Turbo-Hybrid Power Units:** Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) paired with MGU-K (120 kW) and MGU-H.
- **Fuel Flow Limit:** 100 kg/h maximum instantaneous flow rate; 100 kg fuel capacity.
- **Permanent Driver Numbers:** Drivers selected permanent career numbers (e.g., #44 Hamilton).
- **Double Points Finale:** 50 points awarded to winner of Abu Dhabi finale.
