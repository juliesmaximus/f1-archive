## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Abolition of Fastest Lap Point:** The bonus championship point for setting the fastest lap was abolished, reverting scoring strictly to standard race and sprint positions.
- **Weight Limit Revisions:** Driver minimum weight allowance raised from 80 kg to 82 kg, increasing minimum car mass without fuel from 798 kg to 800 kg.
- **Sprint Regulations:** Six sprint weekends (China, Miami, Spa, Austin, São Paulo, Qatar) continuing the decoupled Sprint Shootout format.
- **Historic Three-Way Title Decider:** The Drivers' World Championship culminated in a sensational three-way finale at Abu Dhabi between Lando Norris (423 pts), Max Verstappen (421 pts), and Oscar Piastri (410 pts), with Norris clinching his maiden World Championship by just 2 points.
- **McLaren Back-to-Back Constructors' Crowns:** McLaren secured their 10th World Constructors' Championship with 833 points and 14 Grand Prix victories (7 each for Norris and Piastri).
