## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lando Norris Maiden World Drivers' Championship:** In one of the closest and most dramatic championship battles in Formula 1 history, Lando Norris clinched his maiden World Championship with 423 points, edging four-time World Champion Max Verstappen (421 points) by just 2 points, with teammate Oscar Piastri finishing 3rd on 410 points. Norris became the 11th British driver to win the Formula 1 World Championship.
- **McLaren Consecutive Constructors' Titles:** McLaren dominated the Constructors' Championship with 833 points and 14 Grand Prix victories (7 each for Norris and Piastri), claiming their 10th World Constructors' Championship.
- **Lewis Hamilton Ferrari Debut:** After 12 seasons and 6 World Titles with Mercedes, Lewis Hamilton made his historic debut for Scuderia Ferrari alongside Charles Leclerc.
- **Rookie Sensation Andrea Kimi Antonelli:** 18-year-old Italian rookie Andrea Kimi Antonelli replaced Hamilton at Mercedes, scoring 3 podiums and 150 points to finish 7th in the World Championship.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Podium finishes for rookies Andrea Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) and Isack Hadjar (Racing Bulls).
  - Maiden podium for Nico Hülkenberg with Kick Sauber, ending his historic streak of starts without a podium.
  - Carlos Sainz scored 2 podiums in his debut season with Williams.
