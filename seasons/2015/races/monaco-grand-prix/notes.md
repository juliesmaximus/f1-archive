## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the most agonizing strategic blunders in Formula 1 history. Lewis Hamilton dominated completely from pole position, leading Nico Rosberg by 21 seconds. On lap 64, Max Verstappen crashed heavily into the Ste Devote barriers, triggering a Virtual Safety Car and full Safety Car. Miscalculating their pit stop window, Mercedes called Hamilton in for fresh super-soft tyres! Hamilton emerged in 3rd place behind Rosberg and Sebastian Vettel! On the restart with no overtaking possible around Monaco, Rosberg cruised to a shock victory (his third consecutive Monaco GP win), while a shell-shocked Hamilton stood disconsolate on the podium.
