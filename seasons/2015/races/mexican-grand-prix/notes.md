## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Historic return of the Mexican Grand Prix after a 23-year absence. Running at 2,240 metres altitude through the breathtaking Foro Sol baseball stadium packed with 40,000 cheering fans, Nico Rosberg produced a flawless drive from pole position to beat Lewis Hamilton by 1.9 seconds, kicking off a six-race winning streak. Both Ferraris retired (Vettel crashed, Räikkönen collided with Bottas).

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
