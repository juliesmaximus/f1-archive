## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton became a three-time World Champion! In a wild, chaotic wet-drying classic, Hamilton and Rosberg banged wheels into Turn 1 at the start. Rosberg recovered and led until lap 48, when he suffered wheelspin over a curb and slid wide, gifting Hamilton the lead! Hamilton took the victory to clinch the 2015 FIA Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship with three races to spare, matching his idol Ayrton Senna. In the cooldown room, a furious Rosberg hurled his 2nd-place Pirelli cap back across the room at Hamilton.
