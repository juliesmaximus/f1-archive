## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Held just days after the tragic funeral of Jules Bianchi, the entire grid gathered in a ring around Jules' helmet for an emotional minute of silence. At the start, Ferraris Vettel and Räikkönen stormed past the Mercedes into a shock 1-2. Hamilton ran wide at the chicane, collided with Ricciardo, received a drive-through penalty, and broke his front wing. In the closing laps, Ricciardo collided with Rosberg while battling for 2nd, puncturing Rosberg's tyre. Sebastian Vettel scored an emotional victory, saying over team radio: 'Merci Jules, cette victoire est pour toi. You will always be in our hearts.' Daniil Kvyat and Daniel Ricciardo completed a Red Bull 2-3.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Neither Mercedes finished on the podium for the first time since Brazil 2013.
