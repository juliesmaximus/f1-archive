## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton dominated from pole position to score his 39th career victory, leading Rosberg in another Mercedes 1-2. Sebastian Vettel attempted an ambitious one-stop strategy for Ferrari; on the penultimate lap as Vettel climbed Kemmel Straight in 3rd place, his right-rear tyre exploded at 190 mph! Romain Grosjean swept past to score an emotional final podium for Lotus.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
