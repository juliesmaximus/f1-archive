## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An all-time Silverstone thriller. At the start, Williams drivers Felipe Massa and Valtteri Bottas rocketed off the second row to lead a shock 1-2 ahead of Hamilton and Rosberg! Hamilton undercut Massa during the first pitstops to take the lead. On lap 43, rain began to fall. As Rosberg closed rapidly on slicks, Hamilton made a masterstroke decision to dive into the pits for intermediate tyres just as the heavens opened! Hamilton cruised to a joyous home victory by 10.9 seconds over Rosberg.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Williams' refusal to let Bottas pass Massa early on cost them a potential race victory.
