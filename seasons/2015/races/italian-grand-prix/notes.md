## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton produced a peerless Grand Slam: pole position, victory, fastest lap, and led every lap to win by 25 seconds ahead of Sebastian Vettel's Ferrari. Nico Rosberg's upgraded engine blew up with two laps remaining while chasing Vettel. Kimi Räikkönen stalled from 2nd on the grid, fighting back to 5th.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Post-race FIA investigation into Mercedes tyre pressures (found to be 0.3 psi below minimum at grid check) resulted in no penalty as pressures were compliant at mounting.
