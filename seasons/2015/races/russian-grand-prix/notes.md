## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Pole-sitter Nico Rosberg suffered a broken throttle pedal mechanism on lap 7, forcing his retirement. Lewis Hamilton cruised to victory by 5.9 seconds over Sebastian Vettel. On the final lap, Kimi Räikkönen collided with Valtteri Bottas into Turn 4 while battling for 3rd, putting Bottas in the wall and limping home 5th before receiving a 30-second penalty. The result officially secured Mercedes their second consecutive World Constructors' Championship.
