## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg hit back with a crushing Grand Slam: pole position, victory, and led almost every lap to win by 17.5 seconds over Lewis Hamilton. Hamilton recovered from a slow start and wheelspin on a three-stop strategy to jump Sebastian Vettel for 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
McLaren-Honda suffered brake failure on Fernando Alonso's car, nearly bowling over his front jack mechanic in the pitlane.
