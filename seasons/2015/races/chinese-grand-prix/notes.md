## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton scored a supreme Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap) to lead a Mercedes 1-2 ahead of Nico Rosberg, with Vettel 3rd. Tensions flared in the post-race press conference when Rosberg accused Hamilton of deliberately backing him into Vettel's Ferrari to compromise his race.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
