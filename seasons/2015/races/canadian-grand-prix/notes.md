## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton rebounded from Monaco heartbreak with a clinical lights-to-flag victory, resisting constant pressure from Nico Rosberg to win by 2.2 seconds. Valtteri Bottas capitalized on Kimi Räikkönen's spin at the hairpin to take Williams' first podium of 2015 in 3rd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Fuel and brake conservation became critical in the final 15 laps for both Mercedes drivers.
