## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Total Mercedes supremacy. Lewis Hamilton led a dominant 1-2 finish ahead of Nico Rosberg by 1.3 seconds, with Sebastian Vettel finishing 3rd on his Ferrari debut. Only 15 cars took the start due to financial and mechanical issues, and only 11 finished. 17-year-old Max Verstappen became the youngest driver in Formula 1 history at 17 years, 166 days.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Giedo van der Garde legal battle against Sauber over driver contract rights dominated the paddock in the build-up.
