## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg completed a hat-trick of victories to close out the season, winning from pole position by 8.2 seconds over Lewis Hamilton. Kimi Räikkönen completed the podium in 3rd. Mercedes finished the season with 16 wins, 18 pole positions, 12 one-two finishes, and 703 Constructors' Championship points.
