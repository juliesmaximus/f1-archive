## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg jumped pole-sitter Lewis Hamilton off the line with a superior start into Turn 1 and never looked back, winning by 8.8 seconds. Hamilton was handed a 5-second penalty for crossing the white pit exit line, cementing Rosberg's victory. On lap 1, Kimi Räikkönen lost rear traction and was overridden by Fernando Alonso's McLaren, the two cars coming to rest intertwined against the armco barriers.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Felipe Massa scored 3rd for Williams after Sebastian Vettel suffered a jammed right-rear wheel nut in the pits.
