## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton won from pole position, but the star of the night was Kimi Räikkönen. Running an alternative strategy on medium tyres in the middle stint and softs at the end, Räikkönen charged furiously, passing Nico Rosberg on the penultimate lap as Rosberg suffered brake-by-wire failure, finishing just 3.3 seconds behind Hamilton to score his first Ferrari podium since 2009.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton also suffered brake-by-wire failure on the final lap, limping across the line just in time.
