## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Miracle in Malaysia. In 56°C track temperatures, Ferrari's SF15-T demonstrated extraordinary tyre management on Pirelli medium tyres. While Mercedes pitted both cars under an early Safety Car, Vettel stayed out, executed a two-stop strategy, and defeated Hamilton by 8.5 seconds! This was Ferrari's first victory since Spain 2013, ending a 34-race drought. An ecstatic Vettel celebrated with emotional radio in Italian: 'Si ragazzi, mi senti? Grazie mille, forza Ferrari!' Max Verstappen finished 7th to become the youngest point-scorer in F1 history at 17 years, 180 days.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari's outright pace on merit stunned Mercedes and shattered expectations of another clean sweep.
