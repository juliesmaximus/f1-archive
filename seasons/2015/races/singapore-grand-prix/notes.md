## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Total disaster for Mercedes and supreme glory for Ferrari. Mercedes inexplicably lost their tyre temperature window, qualifying nearly 1.5 seconds off the pace! Sebastian Vettel scored a sublime Grand Slam from pole position, resisting Daniel Ricciardo for 61 laps to win his third race of 2015. On lap 37, a rogue spectator walked onto the active track near the Esplanade bridge, bringing out the Safety Car. Hamilton retired on lap 32 with a clamp failure on his turbocharger.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The track-invading spectator (Yogavitam Pravin Dhokia) was arrested and sentenced to six weeks in prison.
