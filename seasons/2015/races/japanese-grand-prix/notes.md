## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton asserted authority into Turn 1 and 2 at the start, going wheel-to-wheel with pole-sitter Rosberg and forcing him wide onto the curbs to take the lead. Hamilton dominated to match Ayrton Senna's career tally of 41 Grand Prix victories. On team radio, an exasperated Fernando Alonso yelled: 'GP2 engine! GP2! Aaargh!' in front of senior Honda executives.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
