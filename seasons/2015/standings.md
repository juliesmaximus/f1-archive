## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton Three-Time World Champion:** Hamilton matched Ayrton Senna's 3 World Titles, winning 10 races to clinch the crown at Austin.
- **Mercedes AMG Petronas Dominance:** 16 Wins, 18 Poles, 12 One-Twos, and 703 Constructors' Championship points.
- **Max Verstappen Debut:** At 17 years, 166 days, Verstappen became the youngest driver in F1 history.
- **Return of the Mexican Grand Prix:** Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez returned with the Foro Sol stadium complex.
- **Passing of Jules Bianchi:** F1 community united in grief following Bianchi's passing in July 2015.
