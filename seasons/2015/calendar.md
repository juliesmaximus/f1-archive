## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Virtual Safety Car (VSC):** Formally introduced following Jules Bianchi's 2014 crash to neutralize sectors safely without deploying the physical Safety Car.
- **Nosecone Safety Revisions:** Ugly 'anteater' noses outlawed in favor of lower, sleeker symmetrical aesthetic profiles.
- **Power Unit Limits:** Reduced to 4 Power Units per driver per season.
