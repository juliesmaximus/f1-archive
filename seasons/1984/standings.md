# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1984 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Niki Lauda won his third FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the McLaren MP4/2 TAG-Porsche, scoring 72 net points (5 victories, 9 podiums) to edge teammate Alain Prost (71.5 points, 7 victories, 9 podiums) by just **0.5 points**—the closest championship margin in Formula One history.

Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers in each Grand Prix. The best 11 results across the 16 rounds counted toward the Drivers' Championship. At the rain-shortened Monaco Grand Prix, half-points were awarded (4.5, 3, 2, 1.5, 1, 0.5) because the race was stopped on lap 31 before 75% distance. Team Tyrrell was disqualified from the entire championship, and all their results and points were expunged. Jo Gartner (5th in Italy for Osella) and Gerhard Berger (6th in Italy for ATS) were ineligible for championship points because both teams had entered only one car for the season; their points were not redistributed.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

McLaren International captured their second World Constructors' Championship in crushing fashion with 143.5 points, winning 12 of the 16 Grands Prix with Niki Lauda and Alain Prost in the TAG Porsche-powered McLaren MP4/2. Scuderia Ferrari finished distant runner-up with 57.5 points.

Under FIA regulations, chassis-engine combinations were scored as separate makes in the International Cup for F1 Constructors. All rounds and all finishes counted toward the Constructors' Championship.
