# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1984 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Niki Lauda won his third FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the McLaren MP4/2 TAG-Porsche, scoring 72 net points (5 victories) to edge teammate Alain Prost (71.5 points, 7 victories) by just **0.5 points**—the closest championship margin in Formula One history.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

McLaren International captured their second World Constructors' Championship in crushing fashion with 143.5 points, winning 12 of the 16 Grands Prix with Niki Lauda and Alain Prost in the TAG Porsche-powered McLaren MP4/2. Scuderia Ferrari finished distant runner-up with 57.5 points.
