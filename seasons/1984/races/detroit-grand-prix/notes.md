## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S BACK-TO-BACK & ATTRITION CARNAGE: Nelson Piquet claimed pole and survived two chaotic startline pileups. In extreme attrition that saw only six cars running at the checkered flag, Piquet led all 63 laps to win by 32.63 seconds over Martin Brundle (Tyrrell) and Elio de Angelis (Lotus). Following post-race scrutineering, Tyrrell was accused of adding lead shot to water tanks during pit stops to run underweight, leading to the disqualification of all Tyrrell results for the entire 1984 season.
