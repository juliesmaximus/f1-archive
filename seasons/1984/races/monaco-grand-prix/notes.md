## Technical & Sporting Regulations
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers (half-points awarded at Monaco due to early red flag).
- **Championship Count Rule:** Best 11 results counted toward the World Drivers' Championship; all results and finishes counted toward the World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors).
- **Specific Regulations:** Tyrrell Racing Organisation disqualified from all races of the 1984 championship. Second cars of Osella and ATS ineligible for championship points at Monza. Refuelling banned; 220-litre maximum fuel capacity.

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S RAIN LEGEND & ICKX'S RED FLAG CONTROVERSY: The most famous wet race in Formula One history. In blinding torrential rain on the streets of Monaco, Alain Prost led from pole. Nigel Mansell passed Prost on lap 11 to lead his first Grand Prix, but crashed on the white lines at Casino Square on lap 15. Meanwhile, 24-year-old rookie Ayrton Senna was performing miraculous feats in the underpowered Toleman TG184 Hart turbo, slicing past Lauda and Arnoux into 2nd place and taking 3 to 4 seconds per lap out of Prost's lead. Stefan Bellof was also charging furiously in the naturally aspirated Tyrrell. On lap 31, with Senna right on Prost's tail, clerk of the course Jacky Ickx red-flagged the race without consulting the stewards. Prost stopped on the grid; Senna waved in triumph thinking he had won, but under regulations the results were backdated to lap 31. Because the race ran less than 75% distance, half-points were awarded (Prost 4.5 pts, Senna 3 pts). In cruel historical irony, had Prost finished 2nd in a full race he would have scored 6 points and won the World Championship!
