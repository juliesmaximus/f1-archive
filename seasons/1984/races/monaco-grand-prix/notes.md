## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE DELUGE OF MONACO & SENNA'S LEGEND: Delayed by 45 minutes due to blinding rain. Alain Prost led from pole in the McLaren MP4/2. Nigel Mansell passed Prost for the lead on lap 11, only to spin out on the slippery white painted road markings up the hill to Casino Square on lap 16. Meanwhile, rookie Ayrton Senna in the Toleman TG184 Hart turbo produced an extraordinary wet-weather drive, passing Keke Rosberg, René Arnoux, and Niki Lauda (who spun out at Casino Square) to run 2nd! Lapping up to 3 seconds faster than Prost, Senna reduced the gap from 35 seconds to just 7 seconds by lap 31. Behind him, German rookie Stefan Bellof in the naturally aspirated Tyrrell 012 was lapping even faster in 3rd. Waving frantically at the marshals that conditions were undriveable, Prost slowed. Clerk of the Course Jacky Ickx abruptly displayed the RED FLAG and checkered flag on lap 31. Prost stopped his car just across the line; Senna swept past, raising his arm in victory, but under FIA rules the classification reverted to the previous completed lap (lap 31), granting victory to Prost. Crucially, because less than 75% of the distance was covered, **HALF-POINTS WERE AWARDED** (4.5 points to Prost). At season's end, this 4.5 points instead of 9 points cost Prost the World Championship by 0.5 points!
