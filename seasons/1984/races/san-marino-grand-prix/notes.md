## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST CRUSHES IMOLA & SENNA DNQ: Nelson Piquet claimed pole in the Brabham-BMW turbo. In the race, Alain Prost in the McLaren MP4/2 took the lead on lap 49 and controlled fuel consumption with surgical perfection to win by 13.41 seconds over René Arnoux (Ferrari) and Elio de Angelis (Lotus). Notably, rookie Ayrton Senna suffered tire compound disputes between Toleman and Pirelli, failing to set a lap time on Friday in the wet and suffering fuel pressure failure on Saturday—the **only time in Ayrton Senna's entire career that he failed to qualify for a Grand Prix**.
