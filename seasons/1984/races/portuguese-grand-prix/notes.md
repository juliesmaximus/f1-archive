## Technical & Sporting Regulations
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers (half-points awarded at Monaco due to early red flag).
- **Championship Count Rule:** Best 11 results counted toward the World Drivers' Championship; all results and finishes counted toward the World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors).
- **Specific Regulations:** Tyrrell Racing Organisation disqualified from all races of the 1984 championship. Second cars of Osella and ATS ineligible for championship points at Monza. Refuelling banned; 220-litre maximum fuel capacity.

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE 0.5-POINT TITLE DECIDER: The championship showdown at Autódromo do Estoril produced the closest championship climax in Formula One history. Alain Prost needed to win with Niki Lauda 3rd or lower to snatch the crown. Prost dominated the race from the front, taking his 7th victory of the season. Lauda started from 11th on the grid and methodically worked his way up through the field. On lap 52, Nigel Mansell's Lotus suffered brake failure and spun out of 2nd place, handing 2nd position to Lauda. Lauda cruised home behind Prost to claim the World Drivers' Championship by just 0.5 points (72 to 71.5), securing his third World Championship in legendary fashion.
