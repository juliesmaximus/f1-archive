## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE 0.5-POINT DECIDER (ESTORIL FINALE): Formula One returned to Portugal for the first time since 1960. Nelson Piquet claimed pole for Brabham. In the race, Alain Prost took the lead on lap 9 and drove flawlessly to win his 7th race of the season. To win the World Championship, Niki Lauda needed to finish 2nd. Starting from a lowly 11th on the grid, Lauda methodically picked off cars one by one. With 18 laps remaining, Nigel Mansell spun out of 2nd place in his Lotus due to brake failure. Lauda inherited 2nd place and brought his McLaren MP4/2 home 13.42 seconds behind Prost to clinch the **1984 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship by just 0.5 POINTS (72 to 71.5)**—the closest championship finish in Formula One history!
