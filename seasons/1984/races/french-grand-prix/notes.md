## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LAUDA DIJON MASTERCLASS & FAREWELL TO DIJON: Patrick Tambay put the Renault RE50 on pole. In the race, Niki Lauda in the McLaren MP4/2 started 9th but picked off rivals with breathtaking ease, overtaking Tambay for the lead on lap 41 to win by 7.12 seconds over Tambay and Nigel Mansell (Lotus). Alain Prost finished 7th with loose wheel lugs. This marked the **final French Grand Prix held at Dijon-Prenois**.
