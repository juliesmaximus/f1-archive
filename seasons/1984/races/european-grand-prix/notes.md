## Technical & Sporting Regulations
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers (half-points awarded at Monaco due to early red flag).
- **Championship Count Rule:** Best 11 results counted toward the World Drivers' Championship; all results and finishes counted toward the World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors).

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST KEEPS TITLE ALIVE AT NEW NÜRBURGRING: Formula One returned to the Nürburgring for the first time since Niki Lauda's 1976 inferno, competing on the newly constructed 4.542 km modern GP-Strecke. Nelson Piquet claimed pole for Brabham-BMW. Alain Prost took the lead at the start and controlled the race to take his sixth victory of the season, winning by 23.9 seconds over Michele Alboreto (Ferrari). Niki Lauda fought through from 15th on the grid to finish 4th, leaving Lauda leading the championship by 3.5 points heading into the Estoril finale.
