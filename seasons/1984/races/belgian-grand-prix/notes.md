## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALBORETO'S FERRARI MAIDEN WIN & ZOLDER FAREWELL: Michele Alboreto qualified on pole position in the Ferrari 126C4. Alboreto controlled the race with supreme authority, leading all 70 laps to claim his maiden victory for Scuderia Ferrari by 42.38 seconds over Derek Warwick (Renault) and René Arnoux (Ferrari). Both McLarens retired with electrical engine failures. This marked the **final Formula One Grand Prix held at Circuit Zolder**.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Michele Alboreto became the first Italian to win a Grand Prix for Ferrari since Ludovico Scarfiotti at Monza in 1966.
