## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET BURNING FEET VICTORY: Nelson Piquet claimed pole in the Brabham-BMW turbo. In the race, Piquet led all 70 laps unchallenged, but suffered severe burns to his right foot because Brabham had relocated an auxiliary oil radiator directly into the nosecone next to his pedals! Piquet hopped onto the podium barefoot in agony to celebrate victory by 2.61 seconds over teammate Alain Prost and Niki Lauda.
