## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LAUDA REIGNS AT BRANDS HATCH: Nelson Piquet claimed pole in the Brabham-BMW turbo. At the restart following a red flag for a multi-car crash at Bottom Bend, Alain Prost led until his gearbox failed on lap 37. Niki Lauda took over the lead in his McLaren MP4/2 and dominated to take victory by 42.12 seconds over Derek Warwick (Renault) and Ayrton Senna (scoring his second podium for Toleman!). With this win, Lauda closed to within 1.5 points of Prost in the championship.
