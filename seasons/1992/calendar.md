## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Engine Regulations:** 3.5-litre naturally aspirated engines (maximum 12 cylinders). Williams used Renault RS3C/RS4 3.5 V10; McLaren used Honda RA122E/B 3.5 V12; Benetton used Ford HBA7 3.5 V8; Ferrari used Tipo 038 3.5 V12.
- **Electronic Driver Aids Epoch:** 1992 marked the peak of electronic driver assistance systems, spearheaded by Williams' computer-controlled hydropneumatic active suspension, semi-automatic fly-by-wire gearbox, traction control, and launch control.
- **Historic Records Set:** Nigel Mansell set all-time Formula 1 single-season records with 9 race victories and 14 pole positions out of 16 rounds.
- **Earliest Modern Championship Clinch:** Mansell clinched the Drivers' Championship on August 16 at the Hungarian Grand Prix with five rounds remaining.
