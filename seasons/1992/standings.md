## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **Mansell's Historic Records:** Nigel Mansell set all-time Formula 1 single-season records with 9 victories and 14 pole positions out of 16 rounds. He won the championship with 108 points—nearly double his teammate Riccardo Patrese (56 points).
- **Earliest Modern Championship Clinch:** Mansell clinched the Drivers' Championship on August 16 at the Hungarian Grand Prix with five rounds still remaining.
- **The Williams FW14B Electronic Package:** Developed under Patrick Head, Adrian Newey, and Paddy Lowe, the FW14B is considered the most technologically sophisticated car of the electronic driver-aids era, boasting active ride-height hydraulic suspension, traction control, and blown diffusers.
- **Maiden Win for Michael Schumacher:** Schumacher scored his first career Grand Prix victory at Spa-Francorchamps for Benetton-Ford, finishing his first full season 3rd in the World Championship ahead of Ayrton Senna.
