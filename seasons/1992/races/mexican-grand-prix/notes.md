## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The active-suspension Williams FW14B proved completely unbeatable over the notorious bumps of the Hermanos Rodríguez circuit. Nigel Mansell captured pole position and led from start to finish, spearheading a second consecutive 1-2 finish for Williams-Renault ahead of Riccardo Patrese.

The race marked a historic milestone for 23-year-old German driver Michael Schumacher, who drove an exceptional race in the Benetton B191B-Ford to finish 3rd, scoring the very first podium finish of his illustrious Formula 1 career. Ayrton Senna suffered a heavy crash during practice in the Peraltada curve and retired from the race on lap 11 with transmission failure. This was the final Mexican Grand Prix held until the event returned in 2015.
