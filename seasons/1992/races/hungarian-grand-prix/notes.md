## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The championship coronation. Ayrton Senna won the race for McLaren, but Nigel Mansell drove a calculated recovery from an unscheduled tyre puncture to finish 2nd. With 92 points, Mansell mathematically clinched the 1992 Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship with five races still remaining—the earliest title clinch in modern F1 history.
