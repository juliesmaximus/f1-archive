## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most famous, thrilling finishes in Formula 1 history. Nigel Mansell looked certain to extend his winning streak to six, capturing pole position and pulling out a 30-second lead over Ayrton Senna. On lap 71, Mansell felt a loose wheel nut and pitted for fresh tyres.

Mansell rejoined 5.1 seconds behind Senna on fresh Goodyear rubber and immediately tore through the gap, setting a blistering fastest lap on lap 74 (1:21.598) that was nearly two seconds faster than Senna. Over the final three laps, Mansell filled Senna's mirrors, darting left and right at Loews, Mirabeau, the Chicane, and Rascasse. Senna placed his McLaren MP4/7A with precision, refusing to leave an inch of space.

Senna held on by 0.215 seconds to take an astonishing victory, ending Mansell's winning streak and equalling Graham Hill's record of 5 Monaco Grand Prix victories.
