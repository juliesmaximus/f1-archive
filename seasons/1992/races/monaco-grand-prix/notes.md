## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most thrilling finishes in Monaco history. Mansell took pole and led comfortably by 30 seconds until lap 71, when a loose wheel nut forced him into the pits for an unscheduled tyre stop. Senna swept into the lead. Mansell rejoined on fresh tyres and closed the 5-second gap in two laps, setting blistering lap records. Over the final 3 laps, Mansell was glued to Senna's rear wing, trying every possible move, but Senna positioned his McLaren with supreme precision to win by just 0.215 seconds.
