## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mansell shattered the all-time single-season win record, taking his 9th victory of the year. Riccardo Patrese suffered a terrifying high-speed airborne crash on the main straight at 170 mph after hitting the rear of Gerhard Berger's McLaren as Berger slowed to enter the pit lane. Patrese escaped unharmed. Gerhard Berger finished 2nd and Ayrton Senna 3rd.
