## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell claimed his 9th Grand Prix victory of the season, breaking Ayrton Senna's 1988 all-time record for the most race victories in a single championship year.

The Grand Prix was overshadowed by one of the most frightening accidents in modern F1 history. On lap 43, Gerhard Berger braked late to pit for fresh tyres. Following closely behind at 170 mph on the pit straight, Riccardo Patrese was unsighted and launched over the right rear tyre of Berger's McLaren.

Patrese's Williams was catapulted into the air, flying nose-up beneath the overhead pedestrian bridge before crashing back down on its undertray, spinning violently along the pit wall. Miraculously, Patrese emerged completely unscathed. Mansell dominated to win by 37 seconds, while Berger and Senna finished 2nd and 3rd for McLaren.
