## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Williams scored their 10th victory of the season as Riccardo Patrese claimed his sixth and final Formula 1 career Grand Prix victory.

Mansell qualified on pole position and led comfortably until lap 36, setting fastest lap (1:40.646) before his Renault V10 engine failed with a broken piston. Patrese assumed the lead and controlled the remaining laps to take victory, consolidating 2nd place in the World Drivers' Championship.

Ayrton Senna retired on lap 3 with engine failure in Honda's home race, but teammate Gerhard Berger salvaged 2nd place for McLaren. Martin Brundle finished 3rd for Benetton, scoring his fourth podium of the season.
