## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 returned to South Africa for the first time since 1985 following the dismantling of apartheid. Nigel Mansell and Williams debuted the technologically peerless FW14B featuring reactive active suspension. Mansell took pole by 0.74s, led every lap, and won by 24 seconds over teammate Riccardo Patrese, with Ayrton Senna a distant 3rd.
