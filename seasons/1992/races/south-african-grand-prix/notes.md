## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Formula 1 returned to South Africa for the first time since 1985 on a newly reconstructed, anti-clockwise 4.261 km Kyalami layout following the dismantling of apartheid.

The season opener witnessed the ominous, crushing debut of the Williams FW14B featuring Adrian Newey's aerodynamics, Patrick Head's engineering, and Paddy Lowe's active hydropneumatic suspension system. Nigel Mansell dominated throughout the weekend, securing pole position by 0.741 seconds over teammate Riccardo Patrese, setting the fastest lap on lap 70, and cruising to victory by 24 seconds to lead a dominant Williams-Renault 1-2. Reigning champion Ayrton Senna finished 3rd in an outclassed McLaren MP4/6B, admitting the active Williams was in another dimension.
