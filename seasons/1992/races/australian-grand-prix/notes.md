## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Season finale in Adelaide. Nigel Mansell took his 14th pole position of the season, establishing an all-time record for the most pole positions in a single season. In the race, Mansell led until lap 18, when Ayrton Senna attempted a desperate lunge down the inside at the final hairpin and rammed the rear of Mansell's Williams, eliminating both cars. Gerhard Berger went on to win ahead of Michael Schumacher and Martin Brundle.
