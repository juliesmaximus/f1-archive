## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell secured his 6th victory of the year after teammate Patrese yielded the lead to him following a red flag restart caused by torrential rain. Schumacher took out Senna on the opening lap at the Adelaide hairpin; Senna, dressed in street clothes, famously lectured Schumacher on the grid before the restart.
