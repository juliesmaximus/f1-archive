## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Heavy rain and opening lap chaos defined the French Grand Prix. On lap 1, Michael Schumacher attempted an aggressive pass on Ayrton Senna at the Adelaide hairpin, sliding into the McLaren and eliminating Senna on the spot.

Torrential rain worsened until race control red-flagged the event on lap 18. The race was restarted in a second part on aggregate timing. Nigel Mansell dominated the wet-dry second segment, claiming victory ahead of teammate Riccardo Patrese for a Williams 1-2. Martin Brundle scored his first podium of the season in 3rd for Benetton.

Following his opening lap retirement, Senna walked to the Benetton pit wall in plain clothes while Schumacher was preparing for the restart and delivered a stern, fatherly lecture on racecraft and patience to the young German.
