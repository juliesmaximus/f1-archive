## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A torrential downpour tested driver skill and electronic driver aids to the extreme. Nigel Mansell converted pole position into an immediate lead, setting the fastest lap of the race on lap 10 (1:42.503) in treacherous standing water.

Michael Schumacher confirmed his wet-weather mastery in the Benetton B192, scything past Jean Alesi and Ayrton Senna to claim 2nd place. Jean Alesi drove aggressively in the Ferrari F92A to take 3rd after colliding with Berger on lap 48. Senna spun out of 3rd place with two laps remaining while defending from Gerhard Berger, leaving Mansell to claim his 4th consecutive victory from the start of the season.
