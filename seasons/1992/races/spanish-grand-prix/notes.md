## Key Race Results & Milestones

---

## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mansell drove a masterly wet race to claim his fourth consecutive victory of the season, equaling Ayrton Senna's 1991 record. Michael Schumacher drove brilliantly in the rain to finish 2nd, with Jean Alesi taking 3rd for Ferrari after a late spin by Senna.
