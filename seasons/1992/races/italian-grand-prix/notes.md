## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Ayrton Senna claimed victory after both Williams FW14Bs suffered mechanical troubles (Patrese hydraulic failure while leading, Mansell gearbox failure). Martin Brundle finished 2nd for Benetton and Schumacher 3rd. Prior to the race, Nigel Mansell shocked the world by announcing he would leave Formula 1 at the end of the season to race in American IndyCar due to contract disputes with Frank Williams over Alain Prost's signing.
