## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The absolute zenith of "Mansell Mania." Nigel Mansell put on the most dominant single weekend performance in British Grand Prix history. In qualifying, Mansell recorded a lap of 1:18.965—an unbelievable 1.919 seconds faster than teammate Riccardo Patrese in 2nd and 2.7 seconds faster than Ayrton Senna in 3rd.

In the race, Mansell led every single lap, set fastest lap on lap 57, and claimed his 28th career victory by 39 seconds over Patrese, surpassing Sir Jackie Stewart's 19-year-old record of 27 wins to become the most successful British driver in Formula 1 history.

As Mansell completed his slowing-down lap, tens of thousands of ecstatic fans broke through security fences and engulfed his Williams on the track, creating one of the most iconic scenes in Silverstone lore.
