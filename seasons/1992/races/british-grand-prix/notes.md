## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
'Mansell Mania' reached fever pitch. Mansell qualified an astronomical 1.919 seconds ahead of Patrese and 2.741s ahead of Senna. Mansell dominated the race, winning by 39 seconds. Tens of thousands of British fans invaded the circuit on the slowing-down lap, swarming Mansell's car before he could reach the podium.
