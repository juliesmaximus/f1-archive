## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Exactly one year after his Formula 1 debut at the same venue, 23-year-old Michael Schumacher scored his maiden World Championship Grand Prix victory.

Intermittent rain created changing track conditions. Mansell and Patrese led early on wet tyres, with Schumacher running 3rd. On lap 30, Schumacher ran wide at the Stavelot curve onto the grass. As teammate Martin Brundle passed him, Schumacher noticed that Brundle's wet tyres were severely blistered and blistering on the drying asphalt.

Schumacher instantly concluded the track was ready for slick tyres and radioed the Benetton pits for an immediate stop. Mansell and Williams delayed their stop by three laps. By the time Mansell pitted, Schumacher's blistering out-laps on slicks had vaulted him into a 5-second lead. Mansell suffered cracked exhaust pipes and could not challenge, allowing Schumacher to claim the first of his record-shattering 91 career Grand Prix victories.
