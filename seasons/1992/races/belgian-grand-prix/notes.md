## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A historic milestone: Michael Schumacher scored his maiden Formula 1 victory for Benetton-Ford, exactly one year after his debut at Spa. When Schumacher ran wide at Stavelot on lap 30, he noticed teammate Brundle's tyres blistering on the drying track. Schumacher made the tactical masterstroke to pit immediately for slick tyres. As Mansell and Patrese delayed their stops, Schumacher built an unassailable lead, winning by 36 seconds.
