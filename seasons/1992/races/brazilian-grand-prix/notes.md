## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell secured his third straight victory of the season in crushing fashion. In qualifying, Mansell was an astonishing 2.199 seconds faster than Ayrton Senna's 3rd-placed McLaren MP4/7A.

In the race, Mansell and teammate Riccardo Patrese dominated completely, executing a Williams 1-2 finish and lapping the entire field up to 3rd-place Michael Schumacher. Ayrton Senna retired from his home Grand Prix on lap 17 with electrical failure, while McLaren teammate Gerhard Berger had already retired on lap 4 with electrical failure, highlighting McLaren-Honda's sudden technological deficit to the active Williams FW14B.
