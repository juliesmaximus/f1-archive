## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A rare race without a Williams victory. Senna took pole and led until his alternator failed on lap 37. Mansell spun out while attacking Senna into the final chicane on lap 15. Gerhard Berger drove a steady race to take victory for McLaren, ahead of Schumacher and Jean Alesi.
