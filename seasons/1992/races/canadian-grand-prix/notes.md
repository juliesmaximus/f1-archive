## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A dramatic race of attrition in Montreal. Ayrton Senna scored his first pole position of the season, edging Mansell by 0.097 seconds. In the race, Senna led Mansell until lap 15, when Mansell attempted an ambitious overtake into the final chicane. Mansell's Williams spun off over the kerbs into the gravel trap and stalled, eliminating him from the race.

Senna continued in the lead until lap 37, when electrical failure ended his race. Gerhard Berger inherited the lead and controlled the race to the finish, claiming victory for McLaren-Honda and setting fastest lap. Michael Schumacher finished 2nd for Benetton, with Jean Alesi scoring a welcome podium for Ferrari in 3rd.
