## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell took his 8th victory of the season, equalling Ayrton Senna's 1988 record for most wins in a single championship year. Mansell qualified on pole and made an early pit stop on lap 14 to change tyres, emerging in 3rd place behind Senna and Patrese.

Mansell carved through the field, passing teammate Patrese and then drafting past Senna into the Ostkurve chicane on lap 19 to reclaim the lead. Patrese set fastest lap on lap 36 and hunted down Senna for 2nd place. On the final lap into the Ostkurve, Patrese attempted an aggressive dive down the inside of Senna, spun off into the gravel trap, and dropped to 8th. Senna held on for 2nd, while Michael Schumacher delighted his home fans by finishing 3rd.
