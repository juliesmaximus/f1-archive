## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Mansell took his 8th victory of the season, equaling Ayrton Senna's 1988 record for the most wins in a single season. Riccardo Patrese attempted to pass Senna on the final lap for 2nd into the chicane, spun off into the gravel, leaving Senna 2nd and Schumacher 3rd.
