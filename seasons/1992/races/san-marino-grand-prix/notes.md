## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Nigel Mansell made Formula 1 history at Imola by winning his fifth consecutive Grand Prix from the start of a season, breaking Ayrton Senna's 1991 record of four consecutive opening wins.

Mansell claimed pole position by over a second, controlled the race from the start, and led another Williams-Renault 1-2 ahead of Riccardo Patrese, who set fastest lap on the final tour. Ayrton Senna secured 3rd for McLaren-Honda, nearly 49 seconds adrift, acknowledging that the Williams FW14B was out of reach under normal racing conditions.
