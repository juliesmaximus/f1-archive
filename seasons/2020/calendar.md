## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **COVID-19 Calendar Rescheduling:** Opening 10 races postponed or cancelled; season commenced behind closed doors with strict biosecure protocols and double-headers (Red Bull Ring, Silverstone, Sakhir).
- **Dual-Axis Steering (DAS):** Mercedes introduced revolutionary DAS allowing drivers to adjust front toe angle by pushing/pulling steering wheel (banned for 2021).
- **Emergency Circuits Return:** Portimão, Mugello, Nürburgring, Imola, and Istanbul Park added to calendar to secure a 17-race championship.
