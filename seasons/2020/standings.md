## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Lewis Hamilton Record 7th World Championship:** Hamilton equalled Michael Schumacher's record of 7 World Drivers' Championships with a commanding wet-weather victory at Istanbul Park, having earlier surpassed Schumacher's all-time win record (taking his 92nd career win at Portimão).
- **Mercedes 7th Consecutive Championship Double:** Mercedes broke Ferrari's historic record (2000–2004) by securing their 7th consecutive WDC and WCC double.
- **Pierre Gasly & AlphaTauri Maiden Triumph:** Pierre Gasly scored a sensational maiden victory at the Italian GP at Monza, becoming the first French race winner since Olivier Panis in 1996.
- **Sergio Pérez Breakthrough at Sakhir:** Pérez stormed from last on lap 1 to win the Sakhir GP for Racing Point, taking his maiden win in his 190th Grand Prix start.
- **Romain Grosjean Miracle Survival:** Grosjean miraculously survived a 67G crash piercing the metal barrier at the Bahrain GP, escaping the raging fireball with minor burns due to the titanium Halo.
