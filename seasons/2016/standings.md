## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Nico Rosberg World Champion:** Clinched his maiden World Championship by 5 points over Lewis Hamilton in Abu Dhabi, then retired 5 days later.
- **Mercedes AMG Petronas Record Dominance:** 19 Wins, 20 Pole Positions, 765 Constructors' Championship points.
- **Max Verstappen Youngest Winner in F1 History:** Won on Red Bull debut in Spain at 18 years, 228 days after Hamilton and Rosberg crashed on lap 1.
- **Inaugural Baku Grand Prix:** European Grand Prix at the spectacular Baku City Circuit.
