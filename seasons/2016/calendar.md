## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Record 21-Race Calendar:** The longest season in Formula 1 history to that date.
- **Tyre Choice Freedom:** Drivers allowed to choose three compounds per weekend from five available slicks (including new Ultra-Soft).
- **Radio Communication Ban:** Strictly restricted coaching assistance from pit wall to cockpit (repealed mid-season).
