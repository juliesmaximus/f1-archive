## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg scored a dominant Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap) to become only the fourth driver in F1 history to win the opening four races of a season (after Senna, Mansell, and Schumacher). On lap 1, Daniil Kvyat rear-ended Sebastian Vettel twice at Turns 2 and 3, sending Vettel into the wall and out of the race. The incident proved fatal for Kvyat's Red Bull career: days later, Red Bull demoted Kvyat to Toro Rosso and promoted Max Verstappen to the senior team.
