## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A legendary, earthquake day in Formula 1 history. On lap 1 into Turn 4, Lewis Hamilton attacked pole-sitter Nico Rosberg down the inside. Rosberg aggressively defended; Hamilton took to the grass, lost control, and slid broadside into Rosberg, wiping both Mercedes out of the race in the gravel! With Mercedes out, Red Bull and Ferrari battled for victory. 18-year-old Max Verstappen, in his first-ever race for Red Bull Racing, ran an immaculate two-stop strategy. For the final 30 laps, Verstappen resisted relentless pressure from 2007 champion Kimi Räikkönen, crossing the line to become the youngest Grand Prix winner in Formula 1 history at 18 years, 228 days!
