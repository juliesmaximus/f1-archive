## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Final-lap collision between the Mercedes teammates! Nico Rosberg led entering the final lap with failing brake-by-wire. Approaching Turn 2 (Remus), Hamilton pulled alongside on the outside. Rosberg held the inside line and refused to turn in, colliding with Hamilton's sidepod! Rosberg's front wing snapped off and lodged beneath his chassis, dragging sparks to the flag as Hamilton swept past to win. Max Verstappen finished 2nd, while Rosberg was demoted to 4th and handed a 10-second penalty and two penalty points.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Toto Wolff declared the collision 'brainless', warning that team orders would be imposed if collisions continued.
