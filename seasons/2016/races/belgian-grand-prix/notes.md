## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Chaos at La Source. Verstappen, Räikkönen, and Vettel collided three-wide into Turn 1. On lap 6, Kevin Magnussen suffered a violent 180 mph crash into the Raidillon tyre barriers, ripping the headrest off and bringing out a red flag. Nico Rosberg dominated from pole position to win. Lewis Hamilton started 21st after stockpiling 55 grid penalties for multiple Power Unit changes, mounting a sensational recovery drive to finish 3rd on the podium.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Verstappen's aggressive blocking on Räikkönen down Kemmel Straight drew intense criticism from Ferrari.
