## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
In his 200th Grand Prix start, Nico Rosberg drove arguably the finest race of his career. Taking pole position by 0.5 seconds, Rosberg controlled the race. In the closing laps, Daniel Ricciardo made an aggressive pitstop for fresh supersoft tyres, erasing a 25-second deficit at nearly 3 seconds per lap! Managing cooked brakes and heavy traffic, Rosberg held his nerve to cross the line just 0.488 seconds ahead of Ricciardo, retaking the World Championship lead.
