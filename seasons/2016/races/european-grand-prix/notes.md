## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Inaugural Grand Prix around the medieval walls and ultra-fast 2.2 km Caspian Sea straight of Baku City Circuit. Nico Rosberg achieved a flawless Grand Slam: pole, win, fastest lap, and led every lap to win by 16.6 seconds ahead of Sebastian Vettel. Lewis Hamilton endured an ERS engine mapping glitch and was unable to fix it due to strict FIA radio ban rules restricting team assistance.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
