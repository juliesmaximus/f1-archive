## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton bogged down off the line from pole position with excessive wheelspin, dropping to 6th by Turn 1. Nico Rosberg swept into the lead and cruised to a dominant victory by 15.0 seconds over Hamilton, reducing the championship gap to just 2 points. On the podium before a sea of tifosi, Rosberg led the crowd in singing the White Stripes' 'Seven Nation Army'.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
