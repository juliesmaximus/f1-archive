## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg won by 37.7 seconds from pole position to make it six consecutive wins dating back to 2015. At Turn 1, Sebastian Vettel collided with teammate Kimi Räikkönen while trying to avoid Daniil Kvyat's Red Bull. In the pre-podium green room, Vettel furiously confronted Kvyat, calling him a 'torpedo'. Hamilton started 22nd following an ERS failure, recovering to 7th.
