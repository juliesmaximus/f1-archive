## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton surged past pole-sitter Rosberg at the start to claim his fourth consecutive victory, extending his championship lead to 19 points entering the summer break. Rosberg was penalized 5 seconds for forcing Max Verstappen off the track at the hairpin, finishing 4th behind the Red Bull 2-3 of Ricciardo and Verstappen. Ricciardo famously drank champagne from his boot on the podium—popularizing the 'shoey'.
