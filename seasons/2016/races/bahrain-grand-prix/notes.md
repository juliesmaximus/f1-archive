## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Nico Rosberg (Mercedes)
- **Pole Position:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes) - 1:29.493
- **Fastest Lap:** Nico Rosberg (Mercedes) - 1:34.482 (lap 41)

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg took his second consecutive victory. Pole-sitter Lewis Hamilton was hit at Turn 1 by Valtteri Bottas, damaging his car and dropping to 9th before recovering to 3rd. Sebastian Vettel's Ferrari engine blew up on the formation lap. Stoffel Vandoorne made an impressive F1 debut deputizing for the injured Fernando Alonso, scoring a point in 10th for McLaren.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Bottas received a drive-through penalty for the first-corner collision with Hamilton.
