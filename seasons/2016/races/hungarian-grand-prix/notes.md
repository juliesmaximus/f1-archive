## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton overtook pole-sitter Nico Rosberg at Turn 1 on the opening lap and controlled the race to take victory by 1.9 seconds. Hamilton took the lead of the World Championship for the first time in 2016, becoming the most successful driver in Hungarian GP history with 5 wins.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
