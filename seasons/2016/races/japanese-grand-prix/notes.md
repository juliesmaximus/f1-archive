## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg took his 9th win of the season from pole position. Hamilton suffered a disastrous start on a damp patch on the grid, dropping to 8th before recovering to 3rd after attacking Verstappen in the final laps. The victory officially sealed Mercedes AMG Petronas their third consecutive World Constructors' Championship, and gave Rosberg a commanding 33-point lead with four races remaining.
