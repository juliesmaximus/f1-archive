## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An immortal wet-weather spectacle. In treacherous torrential rain halted by two red flags, Lewis Hamilton produced a flawless, serene masterclass to win from pole position. But the day belonged to 19-year-old Max Verstappen, who delivered arguably the greatest wet-weather drive of the 21st century. After making a brilliant save at 180 mph on the pit straight, Red Bull mistakenly pitted Verstappen for intermediate tyres. Falling to 16th with 16 laps remaining, Verstappen took impossible alternative lines right next to the barriers, passing car after car in blinding spray to charge onto the podium in 3rd! Rosberg finished 2nd, carrying a 12-point lead into the finale.
