## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Hamilton locked up and cut Turn 1 on the opening lap, maintaining the lead and cruising to victory ahead of Rosberg. The closing laps saw furious drama between Verstappen and Vettel: Verstappen ran wide cutting Turn 1 and refused to yield position to Vettel. On team radio, Vettel unleashed an unprecedented, profanity-laced tirade targeting race director Charlie Whiting: 'Here is a message for Charlie: F*** off! Honestly, f*** off!' Verstappen was handed a 5-second penalty in the cooldown room, promoting Vettel to the podium; hours later, Vettel was handed a 10-second penalty for moving under braking against Ricciardo, demoting Vettel to 5th and awarding 3rd to Ricciardo!

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Vettel's radio abuse of Charlie Whiting resulted in a formal FIA warning and public apology.
