## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The tactical showdown of the century. Rosberg needed 3rd to guarantee the title. Hamilton led from pole and deliberately drove at a crawl, backing Rosberg into the clutches of Sebastian Vettel and Max Verstappen. Mercedes technical chief Paddy Lowe intervened on team radio: 'Lewis, this is Paddy, we need you to pick up the pace to win this race.' Hamilton defiantly responded: 'Right now, I'm losing the world championship, so I'm not really bothered if I win or lose this race.' Under unbearable pressure, Rosberg withstood attacks from Vettel and Verstappen to cross the line in 2nd, clinching the 2016 FIA Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship by 5 points (385 to 380)! Five days later at the FIA Prize Giving in Vienna, Nico Rosberg stunned the world by announcing his immediate retirement from Formula 1.
