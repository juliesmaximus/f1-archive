## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Sebastian Vettel rocketed into the lead from 3rd on the grid with an astonishing start down the middle of both Mercedes into Turn 1. Hamilton and Rosberg made contact at Turn 1, forcing Rosberg wide onto the runoff. Ferrari switched Vettel to an aggressive two-stop strategy, but Hamilton made a one-stop strategy work to perfection, taking victory by 5.0 seconds over Vettel.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton dedicated his 45th career victory to boxing legend Muhammad Ali, who had passed away days earlier.
