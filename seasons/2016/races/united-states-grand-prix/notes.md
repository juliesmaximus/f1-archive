## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton scored his 50th career victory with a flawless lights-to-flag drive from pole position. Nico Rosberg finished 2nd to maintain his championship advantage. Daniel Ricciardo completed the podium in 3rd after teammate Max Verstappen suffered a gearbox failure.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Hamilton became only the third driver in Formula 1 history to achieve 50 Grand Prix wins (after Prost and Schumacher).
