## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Nico Rosberg won after surviving a red flag. On lap 17, Fernando Alonso suffered one of the most violent crashes in F1 history: clipping the rear of Esteban Gutiérrez's Haas at 190 mph, barreling into the concrete wall and rolling twice through the gravel, ending upside down. Alonso climbed out of the mangled wreckage on his own. Mercedes switched to medium tyres under the red flag, jumping Vettel's Ferrari to score a 1-2 finish. Debutant team Haas F1 scored points on debut with Romain Grosjean in 6th.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Disastrous 'musical chairs' elimination qualifying format was universally condemned and abandoned after just two races.
