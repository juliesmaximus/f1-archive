## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Heartbreak for Daniel Ricciardo and genius from Lewis Hamilton. Ricciardo dominated from pole in wet conditions. On lap 16, Nico Rosberg yielded position to Hamilton on team orders due to chronic brake temperature issues. Hamilton executed an ultra-long stint on extreme wets, bypassing intermediate tyres to switch directly to ultra-soft slicks. When Ricciardo pitted for slicks on lap 32, Red Bull did not have tyres ready, stranding Ricciardo for 13 agonizing seconds! Hamilton took the lead and defended masterfully to win by 7.2 seconds. Sergio Pérez finished 3rd for Force India.
