## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Lewis Hamilton scored a supreme home victory from pole position. Max Verstappen produced an astonishing wet-weather overtake around the outside of Nico Rosberg into the high-speed Becketts complex. In the closing laps, Rosberg suffered a gearbox failure; Mercedes instructed him over radio how to reset the sensor. Stewards handed Rosberg a 10-second post-race penalty for illegal radio assistance, demoting him to 3rd behind Verstappen.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Rosberg's penalty slashed his championship lead over Hamilton to just 1 point.
