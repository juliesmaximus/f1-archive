## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The championship-defining engine blowout. At Turn 1 on lap 1, Sebastian Vettel lunged down the inside of Verstappen, clipping Nico Rosberg and spinning the championship leader to the back of the grid. Lewis Hamilton dominated the race with a comfortable 22-second lead. Then, on lap 41 approaching Turn 1, Hamilton's brand-new Mercedes engine suffered a catastrophic blowout in flames! Hamilton cried out on radio: 'Oh no, no!' Red Bull scored a thrilling 1-2 finish, Daniel Ricciardo resisting a ferocious wheel-to-wheel duel with Max Verstappen through Turns 4, 5, and 6. Rosberg recovered brilliantly to 3rd, expanding his championship lead to 23 points.
