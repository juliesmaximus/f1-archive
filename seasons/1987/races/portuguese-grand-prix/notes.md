## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST ECLIPSES SIR JACKIE STEWART (WIN #28): Gerhard Berger claimed Ferrari's first pole in 2 years and led comfortably for 65 laps. Alain Prost pursued Berger relentlessly, applying intense pressure until Berger spun under braking at the hairpin on lap 68! Prost swept past to claim his 28th career Grand Prix victory, surpassing Sir Jackie Stewart's 14-year-old all-time Formula One win record (27 wins). Nelson Piquet finished 3rd to extend his title lead over Mansell, who retired with engine failure.
