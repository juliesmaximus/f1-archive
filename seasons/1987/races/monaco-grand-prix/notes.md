## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S FIRST MONACO WIN & ACTIVE SUSPENSION: Nigel Mansell led comfortably from pole until a turbo failure halted his Williams on lap 29. Ayrton Senna took the lead in the computer-controlled active-suspension Camel Lotus 99T Honda, flawlessly managing tire degradation over the bumpy street course to take his historic first Monaco Grand Prix victory by 33.2 seconds over Nelson Piquet and Alain Prost.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Ayrton Senna's maiden Monaco Grand Prix victory and the first World Championship victory for an active-suspension car in F1 history.
