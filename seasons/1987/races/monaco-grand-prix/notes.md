## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA'S FIRST OF SIX MONACO WINS & ACTIVE SUSPENSION: Nigel Mansell took pole in the Williams-Honda and led comfortably for 29 laps until an exhaust manifold crack destroyed his turbo. Ayrton Senna in the Camel Lotus 99T Honda, utilizing Peter Wright's computerized **active suspension system**, smoothed out Monaco's harsh street bumps and preserved his tires. Senna took the lead and cruised to victory by 33.21 seconds over Nelson Piquet and Michele Alboreto! This was the **first of Ayrton Senna's record six Monaco Grand Prix victories**, and the **first Formula One victory for an active-suspension car**.
