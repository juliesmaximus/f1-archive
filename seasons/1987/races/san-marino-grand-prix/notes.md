## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S TAMBURELLO SURVIVAL & MANSELL WIN: On Friday, Nelson Piquet suffered a high-speed tire failure at Tamburello at 180 mph, smashing violently into the concrete wall. FISA medical delegate Professor Sid Watkins banned Piquet from racing due to severe concussion. Ayrton Senna took pole in the active-suspension Lotus, but Nigel Mansell controlled the race to take victory by 27.6 seconds over Senna, with Ferrari's Michele Alboreto taking 3rd to delight the Italian crowd.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mansell claimed his first victory of the year, while Piquet later revealed the crash left him with permanent depth-perception and insomnia issues.
