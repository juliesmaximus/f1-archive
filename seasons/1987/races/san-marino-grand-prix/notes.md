## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S 180 MPH TAMBURELLO CRASH & MANSELL WIN: During Friday practice, Nelson Piquet suffered a high-speed rear tire failure entering the flat-out Tamburello curve at 180 mph. The Williams FW11B slammed backwards into the concrete retaining wall; Piquet suffered severe concussion and bruising, and was barred from racing by Professor Sid Watkins (an injury that permanently damaged Piquet's depth perception). In Sunday's race, Nigel Mansell drove a commanding race from pole position to win by 27.54 seconds over Ayrton Senna (Lotus-Honda) and Michele Alboreto (Ferrari).
