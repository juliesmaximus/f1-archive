## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL'S CRASH & PIQUET WDC CLINCH; FERRARI SUZUKA TRIUMPH: During Friday qualifying at Suzuka's S-Curves, Nigel Mansell suffered a violent crash into the tire barriers, suffering severe spinal concussion. Hospitalized in Nagoya, Mansell was ruled out of the weekend and the season finale, mathematically handing the **1987 World Drivers' Championship to Nelson Piquet**! In the race, Gerhard Berger scored Ferrari's first Grand Prix victory since Germany 1985 (breaking a 37-race drought) with a commanding lights-to-flag drive ahead of Ayrton Senna and Stefan Johansson.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Nelson Piquet clinched his 3rd World Championship, and Ferrari ended a devastating two-year win drought at the inaugural Suzuka Grand Prix.
