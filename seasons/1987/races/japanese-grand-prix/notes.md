## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL SPINAL CRASH & PIQUET WDC CORONATION: Inaugural Grand Prix at Suzuka. During Friday qualifying, title contender Nigel Mansell suffered a violent crash at the uphill S-Curves at 140 mph; the car spun backwards into the barriers, lifting into the air and slamming down hard, fracturing Mansell's spinal vertebrae. Sidelined for the remainder of the season, Mansell's withdrawal mathematically crowned **Nelson Piquet the 1987 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Champion**! In Sunday's race, Gerhard Berger dominated from pole position in the Ferrari F1/87 to claim Ferrari's first victory in over two years, winning by 17.38 seconds over Ayrton Senna and Stefan Johansson.
