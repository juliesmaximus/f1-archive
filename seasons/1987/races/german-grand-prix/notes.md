## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
TURBO ATTRITION CARNAGE & PIQUET WINS: Nigel Mansell led from pole until his Honda turbo blew on lap 25. Alain Prost led until his McLaren suffered alternator failure on lap 39. Nelson Piquet preserved his machinery to take a comfortable victory ahead of Stefan Johansson (who nursed his McLaren across the line on three wheels after a tire exploded on the final lap) and Ayrton Senna. Alain Prost completed 39 laps (< 90% distance threshold) and was classified NC by Formula1.com.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Nelson Piquet seized the World Championship lead with his first win of the season as Mansell's reliability faltered.
