## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S HOCKENHEIM TRIUMPH & ATTRITION: Nigel Mansell qualified on pole in the Williams-Honda and led until an engine cylinder head cracked on lap 25. Alain Prost inherited the lead in his McLaren until an alternator belt snapped with 5 laps to go. Nelson Piquet in the Williams FW11B Honda turbo swept into the lead, claiming his first victory of the season by a crushing 1 minute 39.59 seconds over Stefan Johansson (who drove the final lap on three wheels after a tire puncture!). Piquet took the outright lead of the World Championship.
