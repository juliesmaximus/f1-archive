## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
BACK-TO-BACK STREET WINS FOR ACTIVE SUSPENSION: Nigel Mansell led from pole until suffering severe cramping in his right leg and handling issues. Ayrton Senna drove a breathtaking non-stop race in the active-suspension Lotus 99T, preserving his tires completely without pitting. Senna romped home 33.8 seconds ahead of Alain Prost and Gerhard Berger to take back-to-back street victories and seize the lead of the World Championship.
