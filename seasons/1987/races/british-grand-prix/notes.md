## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL'S LEGENDARY SILVERSTONE CHARGE: Nelson Piquet decided to run non-stop. Nigel Mansell developed a severe tire vibration and pitted on lap 35 for fresh rubber, emerging 29 seconds behind Piquet with only 30 laps remaining! Mansell drove the race of his life, breaking the Silverstone lap record 11 times. With 2 laps to go, Mansell caught Piquet down Hangar Straight, sold him a breathtaking dummy dummying to the outside, then dived inside into Stowe corner to take the lead to the roar of 100,000 fans! Mansell ran out of fuel on the slowing-down lap and was swallowed by ecstatic British fans.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mansell's 29-second comeback and Stowe overtake is revered as one of the greatest charges in Grand Prix history.
