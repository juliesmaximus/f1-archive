## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL'S LEGENDARY STOWE DUMMY PASS: Nelson Piquet qualified on pole in the Williams-Honda. Piquet chose not to stop for tires, while Mansell suffered severe wheel vibration and pitted on lap 35, rejoining **28.4 seconds behind Piquet with 30 laps remaining**. In the most sensational pursuit in Silverstone history, Mansell smashed the lap record 11 times, carving nearly a second per lap out of Piquet's lead. On lap 63, down Hangar Straight at 170 mph, Mansell feigned to the outside and dived inside Piquet into Stowe Corner to take the lead! Mansell crossed the line to win by 1.918 seconds before running out of fuel on the slowing-down lap as tens of thousands of ecstatic fans invaded the track! Mansell kissed the spot on the track where he had passed Piquet.
