## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
MANSELL WHEEL NUT DISASTER: Nigel Mansell qualified on pole and dominated the race, building a 15-second lead over teammate Nelson Piquet. With just 6 laps to go, a loose wheel nut flew off Mansell's right-rear wheel coming out of Turn 4, sending his car into the runoff and heartbreaking retirement! Piquet inherited the lead and took victory by 37.7 seconds over Ayrton Senna and Alain Prost.
