## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
SENNA DISQUALIFIED & FERRARI 1-2: Gerhard Berger claimed pole and dominated the race. Ayrton Senna chased Berger across the line in 2nd place, but was disqualified during post-race scrutineering when his Lotus 99T was discovered with oversized brake ducts! Michele Alboreto was elevated to 2nd to seal Ferrari's first 1-2 finish since 1985. Thierry Boutsen took 3rd for Benetton. Yannick Dalmas crossed the line 5th for Larrousse Lola, but was ineligible for championship points as a second-car entry; Roberto Moreno was promoted to 6th to score AGS's historic first-ever Formula One point.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Berger secured back-to-back victories to close 1987, signaling Ferrari's full resurgence as the turbo era entered its final year.
