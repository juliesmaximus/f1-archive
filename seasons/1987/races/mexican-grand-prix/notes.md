## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios

On lap 30, Derek Warwick had a massive suspension crash at the Peraltada curve, bringing out the RED FLAG. At the restart, Nelson Piquet led on the road, but Nigel Mansell had built a 7-second buffer in the first heat. Mansell won on aggregate timing by 26.16 seconds over Piquet and Riccardo Patrese (Brabham). Heading into Japan, Piquet held a 12-point championship lead (73 to 61).
