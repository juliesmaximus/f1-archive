## Technical & Sporting Regulations
- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 classified finishers.
- **Championship Count Rule:** Best 11 results counted towards the Drivers' Championship; all results counted for the Constructors' Championship.
- **Specific Regulations:** Race stopped on lap 30 due to Derek Warwick's heavy crash into the tire wall at the Peraltada; aggregate timing applied to decide final results.

---

## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
AGGREGATE WIN FOR MANSELL: Nigel Mansell took pole and led, but Nelson Piquet led the restart after Derek Warwick's frightening 150 mph crash destroyed the Peraltada barriers. Because results were determined on aggregate time, Mansell stayed right behind Piquet on track, knowing his first-heat margin was more than enough. Mansell was declared the winner by 26.2 seconds on aggregate over Piquet, with Riccardo Patrese taking 3rd for Brabham-BMW.

---

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mansell took his 6th victory of the season, setting up a decisive showdown at Suzuka with a 12-point deficit.
