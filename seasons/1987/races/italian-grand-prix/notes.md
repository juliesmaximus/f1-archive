## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S ACTIVE SUSPENSION MONZA MASTERCLASS: Nelson Piquet took pole position in the Williams FW11B fitted with Williams's newly introduced reactive **active suspension**. In the race, Ayrton Senna in the active Lotus-Honda gambled on completing the race without stopping for tires and led Piquet. On lap 43, Senna was held up by backmarker Piercarlo Ghinzani entering the Parabolica; Senna slid wide into the gravel trap, allowing Piquet to sweep past into the lead! Piquet won by 1.80 seconds over Senna and Nigel Mansell.
