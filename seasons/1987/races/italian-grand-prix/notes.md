## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET DEBUTS WILLIAMS ACTIVE SUSPENSION & SENNA GRAVEL SLIP: Nelson Piquet convinced Frank Williams and Patrick Head to fit active suspension to his FW11B, securing pole position. In the race, Ayrton Senna ran non-stop in his active Lotus and led Piquet. On lap 43, Senna was held up by Piercarlo Ghinzani's Ligier into the Parabolica, ran wide onto the gravel, and had to catch a violent slide! Piquet blasted past into the lead and held off a charging Senna to win by 1.8 seconds, with Nigel Mansell finishing 3rd in the conventional passive Williams.
