# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1987 - Calendar

The 1987 Formula One season was the 38th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 championship rounds between April 12 and November 15, 1987.

The 1987 season was defined by technological innovation, bitter intra-team rivalry, and the coronation of a triple world champion:
- **Nelson Piquet's Third World Championship:** Brazilian master Nelson Piquet won his **third FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship** in the Williams FW11B Honda Turbo, scoring 73 net points (76 gross). Demonstrating superhuman resilience after surviving a horrific 180 mph crash during Friday practice at Imola's Tamburello curve, Piquet utilized superior tactical intelligence and early active suspension to defeat teammate Nigel Mansell.
- **Williams-Honda FW11B Supremacy:** Williams Grand Prix Engineering dominated the season, winning 9 Grands Prix to claim their **fourth World Constructors' Championship** with 137 points.
- **The Pop-Off Valve & The Jim Clark / Colin Chapman Trophies:** As a transition toward the 1989 ban on turbos, FISA introduced a mandatory 4.0-bar pop-off valve on turbo engines and established dedicated sub-championships for naturally aspirated 3.5-litre cars: Jonathan Palmer (Tyrrell-Ford) won the Jim Clark Trophy for drivers, and Tyrrell won the Colin Chapman Trophy for constructors.
- **Prost Surpasses Sir Jackie Stewart:** At the Portuguese Grand Prix at Estoril, Alain Prost scored his **28th career Grand Prix victory**, breaking Sir Jackie Stewart's 14-year-old all-time Formula One win record.
- **Ayrton Senna & Active Suspension:** In the bright yellow Camel Lotus 99T Honda, Ayrton Senna scored back-to-back victories at Monaco and Detroit utilizing computer-controlled active suspension.
- **Inaugural Japanese Grand Prix at Suzuka:** Formula One made its historic debut at Suzuka International Racing Course, won by Gerhard Berger for Ferrari.
