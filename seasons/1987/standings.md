# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1987 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Nelson Piquet won his third FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Williams FW11B Honda Turbo, scoring 73 net points (76 gross) with 3 victories and 11 podiums. Nigel Mansell (Williams-Honda) finished runner-up with 61 net points (6 victories).

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Williams Grand Prix Engineering captured their fourth World Constructors' Championship with a dominant 137 points, winning 9 Grands Prix with Nigel Mansell and Nelson Piquet in the Williams FW11B Honda Turbo. McLaren International finished runner-up with 76 points.
