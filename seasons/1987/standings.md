# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1987 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

The 1987 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship was decided by the best 11 results out of 16 rounds. Points were awarded to the top six finishers on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis.

Nelson Piquet won his third Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Williams FW11B Honda Turbo, scoring 73 net points (76 gross) with 3 victories and 11 podium finishes. Piquet showcased tactical supremacy and supreme consistency to defeat teammate Nigel Mansell (61 net / 61 gross points, 6 victories), whose title challenge ended after a heavy practice crash at Suzuka. Ayrton Senna finished 3rd with 57 points (2 victories in the Lotus 99T Honda with active suspension), while Alain Prost finished 4th with 46 points (3 victories), surpassing Sir Jackie Stewart's all-time win record with his 28th career triumph at Estoril.

## Naturally Aspirated Sub-Championships (1987)

To encourage the return of 3.5-litre naturally aspirated engines ahead of the 1989 turbo ban, the FIA created two dedicated trophies for naturally aspirated cars:

### Jim Clark Trophy (Drivers)
1. Jonathan Palmer (Tyrrell-Ford) - **95 points**
2. Philippe Streiff (Tyrrell-Ford) - **74 points**
3. Philippe Alliot (Larrousse Lola-Ford) - **43 points**
4. Ivan Capelli (March-Ford) - **38 points**
5. Pascal Fabre (AGS-Ford) - **35 points**
6. Roberto Moreno (AGS-Ford) - **4 points**
7. Yannick Dalmas (Larrousse Lola-Ford) - **0 points** (ineligible)

### Colin Chapman Trophy (Constructors)
1. Tyrrell-Ford - **169 points**
2. Larrousse Lola-Ford - **43 points**
3. AGS-Ford - **39 points**
4. March-Ford - **38 points**
