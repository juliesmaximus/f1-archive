## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **3.0-Litre Engine Formula:** Engine displacement was reduced from 3.5 litres to 3.0 litres to limit escalating speeds and horsepower following the 1994 tragedies.
- **Minimum Weight with Driver:** For the first time, minimum car weight (595 kg) included the driver to avoid disadvantaging taller drivers.
- **Schumacher's Second Title & Benetton's Constructors' Crown:** Michael Schumacher clinched his second consecutive Drivers' Championship with 9 wins, matching Nigel Mansell's 1992 record. Benetton won their first and only World Constructors' Championship.
- **Jean Alesi's Emotional Win:** Jean Alesi scored his sole career Grand Prix victory in Montreal on his 31st birthday driving the legendary #27 Ferrari V12.
- **Häkkinen Adelaide Crash:** Mika Häkkinen survived a life-threatening qualifying accident thanks to an emergency roadside tracheotomy by Prof. Sid Watkins.
