*Note on Constructors' points & tiebreakers:*
- *Brazilian GP Fuel Penalty:* Benetton and Williams initially finished 1st (Schumacher) and 2nd (Coulthard) in Brazil, but were disqualified when Elf fuel samples failed chemical fingerprint matching. On appeal to the FIA International Court of Appeal on 13 April 1995, the drivers' positions and points were reinstated, but both constructors were stripped of their Constructors' points for the race (Benetton lost 10 points; Williams lost 6 points).
- *Footwork vs Tyrrell:* Footwork and Tyrrell tied on 5 points; Footwork was awarded 8th place on countback by virtue of Gianni Morbidelli's 3rd-place podium at the Australian Grand Prix, compared to Tyrrell's best finish of 5th place (Mika Salo at Italy and Australia).

## Championship Nuances & Regulatory Milestones
- **3.0-Litre Engine Formula:** Engine displacement was reduced from 3.5 litres to 3.0 litres naturally aspirated, reigning in excessive speeds following the 1994 Imola fatalities.
- **Driver-Inclusive Minimum Weight:** For the first time, minimum car weight (595 kg) included the driver and racing gear, eliminating unfair advantages for lighter drivers.
- **Schumacher's 2nd Title & Benetton's WCC Crown:** Michael Schumacher won 9 races (matching Nigel Mansell's 1992 single-season record) to become a double World Champion at age 26, clinching the title at the Pacific Grand Prix at Aida with two rounds to spare. Benetton captured their first and only World Constructors' Championship.
- **Jean Alesi's Emotional Montreal Victory:** Jean Alesi claimed his solitary Formula 1 victory at the Canadian Grand Prix on his 31st birthday driving the #27 Ferrari 412 T2 V12. After running out of fuel on the slowing-down lap amid track-invading fans, he hitched a famous ride back to the pits on Michael Schumacher's Benetton.
- **Häkkinen Adelaide Recovery:** Mika Häkkinen survived a life-threatening high-speed puncture and crash into the tyre barriers during Friday qualifying in Adelaide, saved by an emergency roadside tracheotomy performed by FIA medical delegate Prof. Sid Watkins.
