## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of the most popular victories in Formula 1 history. Michael Schumacher dominated from pole until lap 57, when an electrical/gearbox glitch trapped his Benetton in 3rd gear. Jean Alesi in the V12 Ferrari 412 T2 swept past to take his first and only career Grand Prix victory on his 31st birthday. The crowd invaded the circuit on the cooldown lap, and Jean Alesi ran out of fuel, hitching a famous ride back to the pits on the sidepod of Michael Schumacher's Benetton.
