## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Another explosive Hill-Schumacher collision. While lapping Taki Inoue's Footwork approaching the Roggia chicane on lap 24, Hill misjudged his braking and slammed into the back of Schumacher's Benetton, eliminating both. Both Ferrari drivers (Alesi and Berger) led until rear-wheel / suspension failures took them out. Johnny Herbert took his second career victory for Benetton ahead of Heinz-Harald Frentzen (maiden podium for Sauber-Ford) and Mika Salo.
