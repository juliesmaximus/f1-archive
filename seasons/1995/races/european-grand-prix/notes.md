## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Schumacher's defining career drives. Jean Alesi drove a heroic race on slick tyres on a drying track and led by 30 seconds. Schumacher pitted for fresh slick tyres with 20 laps remaining and unleashed an astonishing sprint, slashing Alesi's lead by two seconds per lap. On lap 65 with two laps to go, Schumacher made a breathtaking pass around the outside of Alesi into the Veedol chicane to snatch a sensational victory.
