## Key Race Results & Milestones

- **Race Winner:** Damon Hill (Williams-Renault)

---

## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Return of the Argentine Grand Prix for the first time since 1981. Damon Hill took victory ahead of Jean Alesi (Ferrari) and Michael Schumacher after a multi-car collision on lap 1 necessitated a red flag restart.
