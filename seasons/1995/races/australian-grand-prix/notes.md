## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The final Adelaide Grand Prix before the Australian GP moved to Melbourne. During Friday practice, Mika Häkkinen suffered a horrific 120 mph crash when a tyre punctured at Brewery Bend; he was saved by an emergency tracheotomy performed at trackside by Prof. Sid Watkins and intensive care doctors. In the race, Coulthard crashed in the pit entry road while leading, and Schumacher collided with Alesi. Damon Hill took victory by two full laps over Olivier Panis (Ligier) and Gianni Morbidelli (Footwork) in an extraordinary race of attrition.
