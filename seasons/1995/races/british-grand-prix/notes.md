## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Dramatic collision between championship protagonists. Damon Hill chased Schumacher closely and made a desperate dive down the inside into Priory on lap 46; the cars collided and both were eliminated in the gravel trap. Johnny Herbert inherited the lead to score a fairy-tale maiden Grand Prix victory for Benetton on home soil, ahead of Jean Alesi and David Coulthard.
