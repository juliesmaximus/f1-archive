## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
The championship decider. Coulthard took pole and led early, but Schumacher executed a brilliant three-stop strategy to leapfrog both Williams cars. Schumacher won by 15 seconds over Coulthard and Hill, officially clinching his 2nd World Drivers' Championship at age 26, becoming the youngest double World Champion in Formula 1 history.
