## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
First race of the new 3.0-litre engine regulations. Schumacher won for Benetton-Renault ahead of Coulthard and Berger. Post-race fuel sample testing initially disqualified Schumacher and Coulthard for fuel irregularities (Elf fuel did not match registered sample). Upon appeal, the drivers were reinstated with their points, but Benetton and Williams were stripped of their constructors' points.
