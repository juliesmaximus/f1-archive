## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
Michael Schumacher became the first German driver in history to win the German Grand Prix in a German-engined car (Renault). Damon Hill started from pole but crashed out on lap 2 at the Ostkurve chicane due to driveshaft failure. Schumacher cruised to victory ahead of Coulthard and Berger.
