## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
A redesigned, slowed Imola circuit featuring chicanes at Tamburello and Villeneuve. Schumacher took pole but crashed out on lap 10 on slick tyres on a damp track. Damon Hill took victory ahead of the Ferraris of Jean Alesi and Gerhard Berger.
