## Historical Context & Narrative Summary
One of Michael Schumacher's greatest drives. Starting 16th on the grid after wet qualifying, Schumacher carved through the field. When rain fell mid-race, Hill pitted for wet tyres while Schumacher stayed out on slick tyres. Schumacher produced an astonishing defensive masterclass on a soaking track, holding off Hill's wet-shod Williams for several laps. Schumacher went on to win from 16th on the grid, with Hill 2nd and Martin Brundle 3rd for Ligier.
