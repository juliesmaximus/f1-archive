### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
THE WOODCOTE MULTI-CAR DISASTER & REVSON'S MAIDEN WIN: Ronnie Peterson took pole for Lotus. On the opening lap, McLaren rookie Jody Scheckter lost control at Woodcote corner while running 4th, spinning across the track and rebounding into the pit wall. A massive chain reaction pileup ensued behind him, involving nine cars (Scheckter, Hailwood, Beltoise, Oliver, Depailler, Adamich, Follmer, Beuttler, and Williamson). Italian Andrea de Adamich suffered severe compound leg fractures, taking marshals 50 minutes to cut him free. The race was red-flagged and restarted over 67 laps. American Peter Revson drove a brilliant tactical race in the McLaren M23 to score his MAIDEN Grand Prix victory by 2.8s over Ronnie Peterson and Denny Hulme! James Hunt scored 4th for Hesketh.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Peter Revson became only the second American driver (after Dan Gurney) to win a World Championship Grand Prix for a non-Ferrari team.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Race stopped on lap 2 and restarted; original start declared void.
