### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP BRAZILIAN GP: Formula One held its first official World Championship round in Brazil on the colossal 7.96 km Interlagos circuit. Ronnie Peterson took pole for Lotus. In the race, national hero Emerson Fittipaldi swept into the lead on lap 1 to the ecstatic roar of 100,000 fans. Fittipaldi drove a flawless race to win by 13.5s over Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell) and Denny Hulme (McLaren), sending the nation into carnival celebration!

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi became an immortal sporting icon in Brazil, scoring back-to-back wins to start 1973.
- **Regulatory Framework:** First official World Championship Brazilian Grand Prix.
