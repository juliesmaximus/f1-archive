## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell) — 1:57:44.300  
- **Pole Position:** Jackie Stewart  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 45,000 spectators lining the harbour terraces and balconies  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** New Swimming Pool grandstands, Quai Albert 1er, and Casino square terraces  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
STEWART EQUALS GRAHAM HILL RECORD: The Monaco circuit debuted its iconic new layout, extending around the new Rainier III Nautical Stadium (Swimming Pool complex) and creating La Rascasse and Anthony Noghès corners. Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 006 and drove a flawless race to win by 1.3s over teammate François Cevert, sealing another Tyrrell 1-2 finish! Emerson Fittipaldi was 3rd. Stewart equalled Graham Hill's record of 25 Grand Prix wins.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart's 3rd Monaco GP victory and 25th career win, moving within four points of Fittipaldi in the title fight.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Circuit lengthened from 3.145 km to 3.278 km with new Swimming Pool and Rascasse sections.
