### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
TRAGEDY OF ROGER WILLIAMSON: On lap 8, British driver Roger Williamson's March suffered a suspected tyre failure at the high-speed Tunnel Oost curve, crashed into the barriers, flipped upside down, and caught fire with Williamson trapped inside. Close friend David Purley immediately stopped his March, abandoned his race, and courageously ran across the track to desperately attempt to flip the burning car right-side-up and fight the flames with a small handheld extinguisher. Track marshals lacked fireproof suits and failed to assist. Williamson tragically died of asphyxiation before a fire truck arrived. The race was controversially NOT stopped. Jackie Stewart and François Cevert dominated to score a sombre 1-2 finish for Tyrrell, Stewart winning by 15.8 seconds over Cevert! James Hunt scored his maiden podium in 3rd for Hesketh Racing.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart's 26th career victory broke Jim Clark's all-time Formula One win record (25 wins). Williamson's preventable death led to a massive overhaul of marshal safety equipment and mandatory fire tender deployments.
- **Regulatory Framework:** David Purley was awarded the George Medal for his extraordinary heroism.
