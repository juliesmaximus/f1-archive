### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FIRST USE OF SAFETY CAR IN FORMULA ONE HISTORY: Run in pouring rain and chaos. Following a violent collision between François Cevert and Jody Scheckter on lap 32, the FIA deployed a Safety Car for the FIRST TIME in World Championship history—a yellow Porsche 914 driven by Canadian driver Eppie Wietzes. However, Wietzes mistakenly pulled in front of Howden Ganley (Iso-Marlboro) instead of the actual leader, allowing several drivers to gain an unearned lap. Massive scoring confusion ensued for hours after the race. Peter Revson in the McLaren M23 was officially declared the winner by 32.7 seconds ahead of Emerson Fittipaldi and Jackie Oliver! Stewart finished 5th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Peter Revson's second career Grand Prix victory. Timing chaos highlighted the need for standardized safety car protocols.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Historic first deployment of a Safety Car in Formula One history.
