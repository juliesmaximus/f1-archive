## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Emerson Fittipaldi (Team Lotus) — 1:56:18.220  
- **Pole Position:** Clay Regazzoni (1:10.540)
- **Fastest Lap:** Emerson Fittipaldi (Lotus-Ford) — 1:11.220 (Lap 79)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Hot, humid summer sun; 30°C ambient, track temperature 46°C  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent concrete stadium grandstands along the Curva del Ciervo and pit straight  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI WINS OPENER: Clay Regazzoni took pole on his BRM debut. In the race, François Cevert led in the Tyrrell 006 until reigning World Champion Emerson Fittipaldi in the Lotus 72D mounted a fierce attack, passing Cevert with 10 laps remaining to win by 4.69 seconds. Jackie Stewart finished 3rd in the second Tyrrell. Jacky Ickx was 4th for Ferrari.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Emerson Fittipaldi opened his title defense with victory.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Split scoring system: best 7 of first 8 races and best 6 of last 7 races counted.
