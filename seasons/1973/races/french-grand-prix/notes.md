## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Ronnie Peterson (Team Lotus) — 1:41:36.520

- **Fastest Lap:** Denny Hulme (McLaren-Ford) — 1:50.990 (Lap 52)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 28°C ambient, brisk Mistral wind  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Main pit grandstands, Virage du Pont, and wide paved runoffs  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
MAIDEN WIN FOR RONNIE PETERSON: Jackie Stewart took pole in the Tyrrell 006. In the race, McLaren rookie Jody Scheckter seized the lead at the start. On lap 42, Emerson Fittipaldi attempted an aggressive overtake at the final corner; the two cars collided heavily, taking Fittipaldi out. Ronnie Peterson swept into the lead in the Lotus 72E and held off François Cevert by 4.09 seconds to score his MAIDEN Formula One World Championship victory! Carlos Reutemann finished 3rd for Brabham. Stewart finished 4th.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Ronnie Peterson scored the first of his 10 career Grand Prix wins. Jackie Stewart took the lead of the World Championship (42 pts vs. Fittipaldi's 41 pts).
