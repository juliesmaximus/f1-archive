### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL SWEDISH GP & LATE HEARTBREAK: Formula One's first World Championship race in Scandinavia. Local hero Ronnie Peterson took pole position and led 80 of the 82 laps in the Lotus 72E, looking destined for a fairytale maiden victory. Cruelly, with just two laps remaining, Peterson's rear tyre began deflating from a slow puncture. Denny Hulme in the McLaren M23 charged past on the penultimate lap to snatch victory by 4.0 seconds! Peterson nursed the crippled Lotus to 2nd place to the roars of the crowd. François Cevert took 3rd for Tyrrell.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first World Championship Grand Prix held in Scandinavia. Denny Hulme claimed his 7th career Grand Prix victory.
