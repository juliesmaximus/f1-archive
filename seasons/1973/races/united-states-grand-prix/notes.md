## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Crisp, sunny, cool autumn day; 14°C ambient  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 115,000 spectators packing the Watkins Glen hills  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Kendall main grandstand, The Boot, and Seneca spectator knolls  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
TRAGEDY OF FRANÇOIS CEVERT & STEWART RETIRES: In Saturday morning qualifying, 29-year-old French star François Cevert was killed instantly when his Tyrrell 006 violently crashed into the uphill Esses barriers. Devastated, Ken Tyrrell withdrew the entire team immediately. Jackie Stewart, who had kept secret his decision to retire after this 100th race, decided never to race again, concluding his career on 99 starts. In the race, Ronnie Peterson took pole and led from start to finish in the Lotus 72E, winning by 0.68s over James Hunt (scoring 2nd in the private Hesketh March), with Carlos Reutemann 3rd for Brabham. Team Lotus won the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers by 3 points over Tyrrell (92 pts vs. 89 gross / 82 net).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
One of the saddest weekends in Grand Prix history. Jackie Stewart retired as a triple World Champion with 27 wins, while Formula One mourned François Cevert.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Team Lotus won the Constructors' Championship with 92 gross / 92 net points.
