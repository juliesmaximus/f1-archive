### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
STEWART'S 27TH AND FINAL VICTORY: Jackie Stewart took pole position in the Tyrrell 006 with a lap of 7:07.8. Stewart and teammate François Cevert delivered one of the most dominant performances in Grand Prix history, leading from the green flag in a formation 1-2 sweep over the entire 14-mile Nordschleife! Stewart crossed the line to win by 4.0 seconds ahead of Cevert, with Jacky Ickx 3rd for McLaren. It was Jackie Stewart's 27th career Grand Prix victory—an ALL-TIME RECORD that stood for 14 years until Alain Prost surpassed it in 1987. Stewart moved to 60 points, holding a 15-point lead over Cevert and 18 over Fittipaldi.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Jackie Stewart's final career Grand Prix victory, widely considered his absolute masterclass at the Nürburgring Nordschleife.
