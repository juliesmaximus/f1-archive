### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
HAILWOOD'S HEROISM & STEWART'S WIN: Denny Hulme took pole for McLaren. On lap 3, Dave Charlton spun into Mike Hailwood (Surtees) and Clay Regazzoni (BRM). Regazzoni's fuel tanks ignited into a blazing inferno with the driver unconscious inside. Hailwood heroically abandoned his car, plunged into the flames, ignored agonizing burns to unbuckle Regazzoni, and dragged him to safety—saving Regazzoni's life and later receiving the George Medal for bravery. Jackie Stewart drove a masterclass from 16th on the grid (after a practice crash) to take victory by 24.5s ahead of Peter Revson (McLaren) and Emerson Fittipaldi.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Mike Hailwood's selfless heroism remains one of the most revered acts in motorsport history. Stewart revived his championship assault.
