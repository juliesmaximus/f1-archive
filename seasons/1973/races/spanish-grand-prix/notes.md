## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Emerson Fittipaldi (Team Lotus) — 1:48:18.700  
- **Pole Position:** Ronnie Peterson (1:21.800)
- **Fastest Lap:** Ronnie Peterson (Lotus-Ford) — 1:23.800 (Lap 13)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Estadi Olímpic terraces, Miramar grandstands, and Teatre Grec banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
FITTIPALDI CAPITALIZES ON RETIREMENTS: Ronnie Peterson took pole in the Lotus 72E and led for 56 laps until his gearbox failed. Jackie Stewart took the lead before his brake seals failed on lap 47. Emerson Fittipaldi inherited the lead to win by 42.7s over François Cevert (Tyrrell) and George Follmer (scoring a podium in the new Shadow DN1).

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Fittipaldi expanded his championship lead to 12 points over Stewart (31 pts vs. 19 pts).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6. Scheduled for 75 laps (284.325 km).
