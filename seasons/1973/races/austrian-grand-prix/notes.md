## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Ronnie Peterson (Team Lotus) — 1:28:48.780  
- **Pole Position:** Emerson Fittipaldi (1:34.980)
- **Fastest Lap:** Carlos Pace (Surtees-Ford) — 1:37.290 (Lap 46)
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 25°C ambient  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Permanent start-finish grandstand and hillside banks  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
PETERSON DOMINATES: Emerson Fittipaldi took pole in the Lotus 72E and led until an electrical failure on lap 48 forced him out. Teammate Ronnie Peterson seized the lead to win by 9.01s over World Championship leader Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell 006) and Carlos Pace (Surtees). Cevert retired with suspension failure.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
Stewart extended his championship lead to 66 points, needing only a 4th-place finish at Monza to clinch his third World Championship.
