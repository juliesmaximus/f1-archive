## Race Summary & Key Scenarios

- **Winner:** Jackie Stewart (Tyrrell) — 1:42:13.430  
- **Pole Position:** Ronnie Peterson  
- **Weather & Track Conditions:** Warm, sunny, dry; 24°C ambient, freshly laid track surface disintegrating into loose gravel  
- **Spectator Attendance:** 50,000 spectators at the newly resurfaced circuit  
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure:** Covered start-finish grandstand and grass mounds around the Terlamen curve  

### Race Anomalies, Incidents & Key Events
INAUGURAL GP AT ZOLDER & TYRRELL 1-2: The Belgian GP debuted at Circuit Zolder. The brand-new asphalt surface broke up severely during practice and the race, creating treacherous loose gravel. Ronnie Peterson took pole for Lotus. Jackie Stewart and François Cevert demonstrated supreme car control in their Tyrrell 006s to score a dominant 1-2 finish, Stewart winning by 31.8 seconds over Cevert! Emerson Fittipaldi finished 3rd in the Lotus.

### Historical & Regulatory Context
The first World Championship Grand Prix at Circuit Zolder, which became the Belgian GP's primary venue throughout the 1970s and early 1980s.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Drivers threatened to boycott over crumbling track surface before emergency asphalt repairs were completed on Saturday night.
