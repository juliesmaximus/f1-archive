# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1973 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis to the top six finishers at each Grand Prix. Under the split-season dropped-score system, only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and the best **6** results from the final 7 races counted towards the final Drivers' Championship classification.

Sir Jackie Stewart won his third World Drivers' Championship with 71 net points (71 gross), retiring immediately following the death of teammate François Cevert.

## International Cup for F1 Manufacturers (Constructors' Championship)

Points were awarded on a 9-6-4-3-2-1 basis for the highest-finishing car of each manufacturer at each Grand Prix. Only the best **7** results from the first 8 races and best **6** results from the final 7 races counted towards the championship.

Team Lotus won their sixth International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 92 net points (96 gross).

## Regulatory Notes & Scoring System

- **Scoring System:** 9-6-4-3-2-1 for top 6 finishers.
- **Dropped Scores Rule:** Split championship scoring system: best 7 results from rounds 1–8 and best 6 results from rounds 9–15 counted towards both championships.
- **Safety Milestones:**
  - First use of the Safety Car in Formula One history at the Canadian Grand Prix (Mosport Park).
  - Tragic death of Roger Williamson at Zandvoort led to massive overhauls in fire safety and trackside rescue protocols.
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - Sir Jackie Stewart won his 3rd World Drivers' Championship, setting an all-time record of 27 Grand Prix victories before retiring.
  - Team Lotus won their 6th Constructors' Championship.
  - Maiden Grand Prix victories for Ronnie Peterson and Peter Revson.
  - Mike Hailwood awarded the George Medal for rescuing Clay Regazzoni from a burning car at Kyalami.
  - Tragic death of François Cevert at Watkins Glen.
