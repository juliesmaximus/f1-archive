# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1973 - Calendar

The 1973 Formula One season was the 24th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 15 championship rounds between January 28 and October 7, 1973.

The 1973 season was a dramatic, historic year that marked the crowning glory and emotional conclusion of **Sir Jackie Stewart's** legendary career. Driving the Derek Gardner-designed **Tyrrell 006** Ford Cosworth DFV, Stewart took five victories (South Africa, Belgium, Monaco, Netherlands, Germany) to claim his third and final World Drivers' Championship, setting an all-time victory record of 27 Grand Prix wins that stood unbroken for 14 years.

However, the season ended in profound tragedy at Watkins Glen when Stewart's beloved teammate and protégé **François Cevert** was killed during Saturday qualifying. Ken Tyrrell withdrew the team, and Stewart retired from Formula One on the eve of his 100th Grand Prix start. Team Lotus, led by Emerson Fittipaldi and Ronnie Peterson, won the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers with 92 points (10 points ahead of Tyrrell).

The season also witnessed the **first use of the Safety Car in Formula One history** at the Canadian Grand Prix at Mosport Park.
