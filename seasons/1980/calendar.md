# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1980 - Calendar

The 1980 Formula One season was the 31st season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 14 official championship rounds between January 13 and October 5, 1980.

The 1980 season was defined by intense political warfare, technological transitions, and the coronation of a new world champion:
- **Alan Jones & Williams Grand Prix Engineering Dynasty:** Australian Alan Jones won 5 Grands Prix in the Patrick Head-designed Williams FW07B to claim his **maiden World Drivers' Championship**, while Williams Grand Prix Engineering captured their **historic first World Constructors' Championship** with 120 points.
- **The FISA-FOCA War:** Political conflict between Jean-Marie Balestre's FISA (governing body) and Bernie Ecclestone's FOCA (constructors' association) reached boiling point over ground-effect sliding skirts. The Spanish Grand Prix at Jarama was declared non-championship when FISA withdrew sanctioning after works teams (Ferrari, Renault, Alfa Romeo) boycotted.
- **Nelson Piquet's Rise:** Brazilian young gun Nelson Piquet scored his maiden Grand Prix victory at Long Beach in the Brabham BT49, taking the title fight against Jones down to the penultimate race in Montreal.
- **The Italian GP at Imola:** Due to major renovations at Monza, the Italian Grand Prix was staged at the Autodromo Dino Ferrari in Imola for the only time in World Championship history, won by Nelson Piquet.
- **Farewell to Watkins Glen:** Watkins Glen hosted its 20th and final Formula One Grand Prix, concluding two decades as America's home of Grand Prix racing.
