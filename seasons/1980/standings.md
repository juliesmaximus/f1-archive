# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1980 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Alan Jones won the 1980 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship driving the Williams FW07B Ford Cosworth DFV, scoring 67 net points (71 gross) with 5 victories. Nelson Piquet (Brabham) finished as runner-up with 54 net points.
