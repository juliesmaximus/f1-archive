## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
RENAULT TURBO HIGH-ALTITUDE 1-2: At Kyalami's 1,525-meter elevation, the turbocharged Renault V6 suffered virtually no power loss from thin air compared to the 20% loss suffered by naturally aspirated Ford Cosworth engines. Renault locked out the front row (Jabouille pole, Arnoux 2nd). Jabouille retired with a puncture, allowing René Arnoux to claim his second consecutive victory, finishing 34.07 seconds ahead of Jacques Laffite (Ligier) and Didier Pironi (Ligier).
