## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S MAIDEN WIN & REGAZZONI TRAGEDY: Nelson Piquet claimed pole position in the Brabham BT49. In the race, Piquet drove flawlessly from start to finish, leading all 80 laps to score his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory** and achieve a Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led every lap), winning by 49.212 seconds over Riccardo Patrese. The race was overshadowed on lap 51 when Clay Regazzoni suffered a sudden brake pedal failure in his Ensign at 170 mph at the end of the long straight, crashing into Ricardo Zunino's stationary Brabham and the concrete barriers; Regazzoni suffered severe spinal injuries that left him paralyzed.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Maiden Grand Prix victory for future triple World Champion Nelson Piquet, and the career-ending accident of beloved veteran Clay Regazzoni.
