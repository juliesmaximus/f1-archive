## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LIGIER WHEEL RIM COLLAPSE & JONES VICTORY: Ligier dominated qualifying (Didier Pironi pole, Jacques Laffite 2nd). In the race, Pironi built an enormous lead until lap 18, when his cracked front-left wheel rim deflated his tyre. Laffite inherited the lead until lap 44, when his rear-left wheel rim cracked and exploded at Hawthorn bend, pitching him off into the catch fencing! Alan Jones seized the lead in his Williams FW07B to win before Williams's home crowd by 11.0 seconds over Nelson Piquet (Brabham).
