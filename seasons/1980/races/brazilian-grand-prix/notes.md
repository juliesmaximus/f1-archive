## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ARNOUX & RENAULT TURBO POWER: Jean-Pierre Jabouille took pole in the Renault RE20 turbo, but Gilles Villeneuve (Ferrari 312T5) made a stunning start to lead early until his engine expired. René Arnoux took over in the other Renault turbo, outpacing the naturally aspirated Ford DFV runners along the long uphill climb of Arquibancadas to claim his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory** by 21.86 seconds over Elio de Angelis (Lotus) and Alan Jones (Williams).
