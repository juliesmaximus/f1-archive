## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE IMOLA ITALIAN GP & PIQUET TAKES CHAMPIONSHIP LEAD: With Monza under extensive pit renovations, the Italian Grand Prix was held at Imola for the only time in World Championship history. René Arnoux took pole in the Renault. Nelson Piquet started 5th in the Brabham BT49 but scythed through the field, passing Arnoux on lap 4 to take the lead. Piquet led the remaining 57 laps to take his second consecutive victory by 28.93 seconds over Alan Jones! With this victory, Piquet surged into the lead of the World Championship with 54 points to Jones's 53.
