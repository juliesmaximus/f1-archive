## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
DEPAILLER TRIBUTE & LAFFITE REDEMPTION: The Grand Prix took place in profound sorrow following the tragic death of Patrick Depailler in testing at Hockenheim nine days prior. Alan Jones qualified on pole for Williams. In the race, Jones led until lap 40 when a deflating tire forced him into the pits. Jacques Laffite in the Ligier JS11/15 swept into the lead, claiming an emotional victory dedicated to his late friend and former teammate Depailler, winning by 3.19 seconds over Carlos Reutemann.
