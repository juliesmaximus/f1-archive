## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
BROKEN ASPHALT & JONES OFF-TRACK CHARGE: Newly resurfaced asphalt disintegrated under extreme suction from ground-effect sliding skirts. Alan Jones took pole in the Williams FW07B. On lap 18, Jones spun off into the grass avoiding a patch of loose aggregate and dropped to 4th. He pitted to clear grass from his radiators, then unleashed an astonishing recovery charge, passing Nelson Piquet, Jacques Laffite, and Keke Rosberg to win by 24.59 seconds over Nelson Piquet (Brabham) and Keke Rosberg (Fittipaldi Automotive scoring their maiden podium!).
