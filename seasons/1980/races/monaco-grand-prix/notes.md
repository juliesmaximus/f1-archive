## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
STE DÉVOTE CARNAGE & REUTEMANN WIN: Didier Pironi took pole for Ligier. At the start, Derek Daly in the Tyrrell 009 flew over the rear wheels of Bruno Giacomelli's Alfa Romeo into Sainte Dévote, somersaulting over Alain Prost and Jean-Pierre Jarier in a sensational start crash! Pironi led for 54 laps in tricky damp-drying conditions until his gearbox failed, crashing into the barrier at Casino Square. Carlos Reutemann took the lead in his Williams FW07B to win by 1 minute 13.6 seconds over Jacques Laffite and Nelson Piquet.
