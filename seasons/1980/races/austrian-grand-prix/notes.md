## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
JABOUILLE'S SECOND CAREER WIN: René Arnoux took pole position in the Renault RE20 turbo. In the race, Alan Jones made an exceptional start to lead for Renault and Williams. René Arnoux suffered severe tire blistering and pitted, leaving teammate Jean-Pierre Jabouille to mount a charge. Jabouille took the lead on lap 21 and nurse his overheating turbo engine to cross the line just 0.82 seconds ahead of Alan Jones! This was Jabouille's second and final career Formula One victory.
