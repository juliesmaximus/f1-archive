## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ALAN JONES WINS WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP: Nelson Piquet qualified on pole in the Brabham BT49 with Jones 2nd. At the start, Jones squeezed Piquet into the concrete wall on the narrow approach to the first chicane, triggering an eight-car chain-reaction crash that RED-FLAGGED the race! Piquet had to take the restart in the spare car fitted with a fragile qualifying engine. Piquet led Jones until lap 24, when Piquet's engine blew. Alan Jones swept by and controlled the race to win by 15.54 seconds over Carlos Reutemann, mathematically clinching the **1980 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship** and securing Williams's **first World Constructors' Championship**!

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Alan Jones became the second Australian World Champion in F1 history (after Sir Jack Brabham), and Williams secured their first World Championship double.
