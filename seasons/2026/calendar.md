## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications (2026 Regulations)
- **Next-Generation Hybrid Power Units:** 50/50 power distribution between internal combustion engine (approx. 400 kW / 535 hp) and electrical energy recovery system (350 kW / 470 hp MGU-K). Abolition of the complex MGU-H.
- **100% Advanced Sustainable Drop-in Fuel:** Mandate for completely fossil-free, 100% sustainable fuels compatible with existing combustion engines.
- **Active Aerodynamics ("Z-Mode" & "X-Mode"):** Introduction of movable front and rear wings operated by driver controls:
  - *Z-Mode:* High-downforce configuration deployed automatically for corners.
  - *X-Mode:* Low-drag, streamlined configuration activated on straights to reduce drag and increase top speed.
- **Manual Override Mode (MOM):** Replaces conventional DRS. Trailing cars within 1 second receive an on-demand electrical boost deploying additional battery energy up to 337 km/h to facilitate overtaking.
- **Nimble Chassis Architecture:** Narrower track (1,900 mm, -100 mm reduction), shorter wheelbase (maximum 3,400 mm, -200 mm reduction), and minimum car mass lowered by 30 kg to 768 kg.
- **New Manufacturer & Constructor Entrants:**
  - *Audi F1 Team:* Full German works entry following the total acquisition of Sauber, competing with a proprietary in-house power unit developed at Neuburg an der Donau.
  - *Cadillac F1 Team:* American works entry backed by General Motors / TWG Global, joining the grid as the 11th constructor with Ferrari customer power units ahead of proprietary GM power unit development.
