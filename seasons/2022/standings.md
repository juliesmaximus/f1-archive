## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Max Verstappen Record 15 Grand Prix Wins:** Max Verstappen broke the all-time record for most wins in a single Formula 1 season (15 wins), surpassing Michael Schumacher (13 in 2004) and Sebastian Vettel (13 in 2013). Verstappen also set the record for most championship points scored in a season (454).
- **Red Bull Racing Double Championship:** Red Bull secured their fifth Constructors' Championship (first since 2013) with 759 points and 17 victories, snapping Mercedes' record eight-year consecutive Constructors' Championship streak (2014–2021).
- **Historic Milestones:**
  - **Carlos Sainz Maiden Win & Pole:** Sainz scored his maiden career pole and victory at the British Grand Prix (Silverstone) in his 150th Grand Prix start.
  - **George Russell Maiden Win:** Russell claimed his maiden Grand Prix victory at São Paulo (Interlagos), leading a Mercedes 1-2.
  - **Kevin Magnussen Shock Maiden Pole:** In changing wet conditions, Kevin Magnussen scored Haas F1 Team's and his own first career pole position at São Paulo.
  - **Sebastian Vettel Retirement:** Four-time World Champion Sebastian Vettel concluded his illustrious F1 career at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix after 299 starts, 53 wins, 57 poles, and 4 World Championships.
