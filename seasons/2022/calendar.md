## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **Ground Effect Aerodynamic Revolution:** Major overhaul replacing over-body aerodynamic reliance with underfloor Venturi tunnels, generating ground-effect downforce to dramatically reduce dirty air and enable closer racing.
- **Wheel & Tyre Evolution:** Transition from 13-inch wheels to 18-inch low-profile Pirelli tyres equipped with aerodynamic wheel covers and front tyre deflectors.
- **Sustainable Fuel Mandate:** Introduction of E10 fuel containing 10% second-generation sustainable ethanol.
- **Financial Regulations:** Operational cost cap reduced to $140 million base (indexed with inflation allowance).
- **Sprint Format Revisions:** Sprint points expanded to the top 8 finishers (8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1). Under sporting regulations, the official "Pole Position" statistic was restored to the driver setting fastest time in Friday Q3 qualifying.
