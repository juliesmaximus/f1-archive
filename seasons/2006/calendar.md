## Key Regulatory Features & Technical Specifications
- **2.4-Litre V8 Formula:** 90° V8 naturally aspirated engines mandatory.
- **Tyre Changes Return:** In-race tyre pitstops re-legalized after the 2005 ban.
- **Knockout Qualifying:** Three-stage elimination format (Q1, Q2, Q3) introduced.
- **Mass Damper Ban:** Tuned mass dampers outlawed mid-season under moveable aerodynamic device rulings.
