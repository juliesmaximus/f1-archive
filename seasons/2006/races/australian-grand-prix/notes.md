## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Carnage in Albert Park with four safety car periods. Fernando Alonso overtook Jenson Button on the first restart and drove away to win. Michael Schumacher suffered a heavy 150 mph crash into the pit straight wall on lap 33. On the final lap, Jenson Button's Honda engine blew in a ball of flame 50 metres before the finish line; Button stopped short intentionally to avoid a 10-place penalty for the next race.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Christian Klien and Felipe Massa crashed at Turn 3 on lap 1; Montoya spun on the warm-up lap and later retired with engine shutdown.
