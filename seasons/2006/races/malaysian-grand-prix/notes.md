## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Renault recorded a crushing 1-2 finish. Giancarlo Fisichella dominated from pole position, while Fernando Alonso drove an astonishing recovery race from 7th on the grid (heavily fuelled after a fuel rig error in qualifying) to finish 2nd ahead of Jenson Button's Honda.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Both Ferraris took 10-place grid penalties for precautionary engine changes.
