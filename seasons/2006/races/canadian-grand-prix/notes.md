## 3. Official Race Classification Summary
- **Winner:** Fernando Alonso (Renault)
- **Pole Position:** Fernando Alonso (Renault) - 1:14.942
- **Fastest Lap:** Kimi Räikkönen (McLaren-Mercedes) - 1:15.841 (lap 22)

---

## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Alonso drove a commanding race from pole position to win for the fourth time in a row. Kimi Räikkönen challenged early but lost time with a botched pitstop (stalled engine) and spun when running wide over marbles. On the penultimate lap, Schumacher overtook Räikkönen to take 2nd.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Final Formula 1 race for Juan Pablo Montoya; after colliding with teammate Räikkönen on lap 2 and retiring, Montoya left McLaren to race in NASCAR.
