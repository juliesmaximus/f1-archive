## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
The championship swung decisively. Ferrari locked out the front row and Michael Schumacher led comfortably until lap 37, when a catastrophic engine failure—his first in six years—erupted into smoke on the Degner curve! Fernando Alonso swept through to take victory, establishing a virtually unassailable 10-point lead heading to the finale. In the paddock, Schumacher visited and hugged every single Ferrari mechanic to thank them.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Schumacher's gracious handling of his engine failure was widely hailed as a legendary display of sportsmanship.
