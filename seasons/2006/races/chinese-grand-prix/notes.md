## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Michael Schumacher's 91st and final Grand Prix victory. In changing wet-drying conditions, Alonso led comfortably on Michelin wet tyres until Renault changed only his front tyres at the first stop, causing severe understeer. Schumacher carved through from 6th on the grid, overtaking Fisichella around the outside into Turn 1 to win. Schumacher and Alonso were now tied on 116 points with two races remaining!

---
