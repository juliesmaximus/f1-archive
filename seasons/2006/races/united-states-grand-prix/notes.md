## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Ferrari dominance on Bridgestone tyres restored order at Indianapolis. Michael Schumacher and Felipe Massa locked out the front row and scored an untouchable 1-2 finish. A massive 7-car pileup triggered by Juan Pablo Montoya at Turn 2 on the opening lap eliminated Montoya, Räikkönen, Heidfeld, Button, Webber, Speed, and Montagny.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Turn 2 start pileup was the catalyst for Montoya's immediate departure from McLaren and Formula 1.
