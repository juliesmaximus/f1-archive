## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An immortal season finale. Felipe Massa became the first Brazilian to win at Interlagos since Ayrton Senna in 1993, driving to a joyous victory. Fernando Alonso finished 2nd to officially clinch his second consecutive World Drivers' Championship. But the day belonged to Michael Schumacher in his farewell race: a fuel pressure problem left him 10th on the grid, and a puncture from Giancarlo Fisichella dropped him to 19th, nearly a full lap behind. Schumacher unleashed the drive of a titan, lapping over 1.5 seconds faster than the leaders, executing breathtaking overtakes (including an audacious dive on Kimi Räikkönen into Senna S) to finish 4th.
