## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Total Ferrari supremacy in Schumacher's backyard. Schumacher and Massa cruised to an effortless 1-2 finish, while Renault suffered from the FIA's sudden ban on their tuned mass damper system, Alonso finishing a distant 5th and seeing his championship lead slashed to 11 points.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The FIA's technical directive banning Renault's tuned mass damper as a 'moveable aerodynamic device' sparked outrage from Flavio Briatore.
