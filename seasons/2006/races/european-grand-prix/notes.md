## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Tactical masterclass by Ferrari. Fernando Alonso led from pole, but Schumacher unleashed blistering in-laps during the second pit stop cycle to leapfrog Alonso and take an emphatic home victory by 3.7 seconds. Felipe Massa finished 3rd, scoring the first podium of his Formula 1 career.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Ferrari's 248 F1 showed overwhelming race pace advantage on Bridgestone rubber.
