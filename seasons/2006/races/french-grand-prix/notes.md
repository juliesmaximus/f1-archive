## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Michael Schumacher made history by becoming the first driver to win the same Grand Prix eight times (1994, 1995, 1997, 1998, 2001, 2002, 2004, 2006). Schumacher controlled the race from pole on a three-stop strategy, beating Fernando Alonso by 10.1 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Pedro de la Rosa took over Juan Pablo Montoya's seat at McLaren for the remainder of the season, finishing 7th.
