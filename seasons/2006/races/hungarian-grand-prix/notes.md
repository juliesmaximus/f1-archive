## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
An immortal, spellbinding wet classic. Both Schumacher and Alonso received 2-second qualifying penalties for practice infractions, starting 11th and 15th. In the driving rain, Alonso produced one of the greatest wet drives in history, slicing from 15th to the lead within 18 laps. However, on lap 51, a loose wheel nut following a pitstop sent Alonso spinning into the barriers out of the race. Schumacher stayed out on worn intermediate tyres on a dry track and was overtaken by Button, De la Rosa, and Heidfeld before retiring with suspension damage. Jenson Button drove an impeccably judged race from 14th on the grid to score his long-awaited maiden Formula 1 victory in his 113th start.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Pedro de la Rosa scored his sole career F1 podium in 2nd for McLaren, and Robert Kubica finished 7th on debut for BMW Sauber before being disqualified for an underweight car.
