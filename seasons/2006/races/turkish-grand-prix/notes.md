## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Felipe Massa scored his maiden career pole position and maiden Grand Prix victory with a flawless lights-to-flag drive. Fernando Alonso beat Michael Schumacher to 2nd by 0.081 seconds in a breathtaking drag race to the finish line after Schumacher was stacked behind Massa in the pits during a safety car period.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
The Turkish Grand Prix organizers were fined $5 million by the FIA after Turkish Cypriot leader Mehmet Ali Talat presented the winner's trophy, breaching the FIA's political neutrality statutes.
