## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Fernando Alonso became the first Spanish driver in history to win the Spanish Grand Prix. In front of an electrified sell-out crowd of over 130,000 fans clad in Asturian blue, Alonso controlled the race from pole position to beat Michael Schumacher by 18.5 seconds.
