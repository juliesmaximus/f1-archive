## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Debut of the new 2.4-litre V8 engine era and knockout qualifying. Michael Schumacher matched Ayrton Senna's all-time record of 65 pole positions on Saturday. In the race, Fernando Alonso executed a thrilling pitstop duel, exiting the pits side-by-side with Schumacher into Turn 1 and defending the inside line to win by just 1.2 seconds. Rookie Nico Rosberg set fastest lap on his F1 debut.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
First race under three-stage knockout qualifying (Q1/Q2/Q3) format.
