## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
One of the most infamous qualifying scandals in F1 history. In the dying seconds of Q3, Michael Schumacher set provisional pole, then locked his inside front tyre at the Rascasse corner and stopped his car against the barrier, preventing Alonso and Webber from beating his time. Following an 8-hour stewards inquiry, the FIA stripped Schumacher of all qualifying times for deliberate obstruction and sent him to the back of the grid. Alonso won from pole, while Schumacher drove a ferocious recovery race to 5th.

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Schumacher's 'Rascasse parking' provoked international fury, with rival drivers calling it deliberate cheating.
