## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
Michael Schumacher broke Ayrton Senna's pole position record with his 66th career pole. In the race, Schumacher and Alonso staged a thrilling role-reversal of their 2005 Imola epic: Alonso chased Schumacher furiously for the final 20 laps, but Schumacher placed his Ferrari 248 F1 immaculately to win by 2.0 seconds.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Christijan Albers survived a horrific high-speed barrel roll into the gravel on lap 1 after being punted by Yuji Ide's Super Aguri. The FIA subsequently revoked Ide's Super Licence.
