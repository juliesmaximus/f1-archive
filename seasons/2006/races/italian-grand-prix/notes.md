## 4. Notable Scenarios & Race Dynamics
A seismic watershed in Formula 1 history. Michael Schumacher won the Italian Grand Prix before a sea of delirious tifosi. In the post-race press conference, Schumacher officially announced his retirement from Formula 1 at the end of the season. Fernando Alonso's Renault engine blew in a plume of smoke on lap 43, reducing his championship lead over Schumacher to just 2 points.

---

## 5. Controversies, Penalties & Stewards Decisions
Alonso was hit with a highly controversial 5-place grid penalty for allegedly impeding Felipe Massa in qualifying, prompting Alonso to state: 'I no longer consider Formula 1 a sport.'
