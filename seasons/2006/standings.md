## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Fernando Alonso Double World Champion:** Alonso became the youngest back-to-back World Drivers' Champion in history (25 years, 85 days).
- **Michael Schumacher's First Retirement:** 7-time World Champion Michael Schumacher announced his retirement at Monza, finishing with 91 career victories.
- **Introduction of the 2.4L V8 Engine Formula:** Replaced the legendary 3.0L V10s, revving to over 20,000 rpm.
- **Debut of Knockout Qualifying (Q1/Q2/Q3):** The revolutionary three-part qualifying format was introduced, proving immensely popular and enduring into the modern era.
