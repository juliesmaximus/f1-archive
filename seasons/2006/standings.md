## 3. Season Highlights & Historical Significance
- **Fernando Alonso Double World Champion:** Alonso defended his crown to become the youngest back-to-back double World Drivers' Champion in Formula 1 history at the time (25 years, 85 days), winning 7 Grands Prix and scoring 14 podiums.
- **Michael Schumacher's First Retirement:** Seven-time World Champion Michael Schumacher announced his retirement from Formula 1 at Monza following his 90th Grand Prix victory, concluding his Ferrari career in Brazil with 91 career wins.
- **Introduction of the 2.4L V8 Engine Formula:** High-revving 90° V8 engines became mandatory across the grid (except for Scuderia Toro Rosso, which ran a rev-limited 3.0L V10), ending the iconic 3.0L V10 era.
- **Debut of Knockout Qualifying (Q1/Q2/Q3):** The revolutionary three-part knockout qualifying elimination format made its championship debut at the Bahrain Grand Prix.
- **Jenson Button's Maiden Victory:** In torrential and changing conditions at the Hungarian Grand Prix, Jenson Button drove from 14th on the grid to score his maiden Formula 1 victory for Honda.
