## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
REUTEMANN & MICHELIN MAIDEN RADIAL WIN: The Brazilian GP relocated from São Paulo's bumpy Interlagos to Rio's flat coastal Jacarepaguá circuit. Ronnie Peterson took pole in the Lotus 78, but Carlos Reutemann in the Ferrari 312T3 swept into the lead at the start. Utilizing Michelin's breakthrough radial ply tires, Reutemann dominated in the extreme heat, winning by 49.13 seconds over local idol Emerson Fittipaldi, who scored an emotional 2nd place for his family's Copersucar-Fittipaldi team! This marked the very first Grand Prix victory for Michelin radial tyres in Formula One history.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
First Formula One World Championship victory for Michelin radial tire technology, sparking the tire war against Goodyear.
