## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
DEPAILLER'S MAIDEN WIN: Carlos Reutemann took pole for Ferrari, but John Watson (Brabham) and Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell 008) passed him into Sainte Dévote. Watson led until locking his brakes at the chicane on lap 38. Depailler took the lead and drove flawlessly through the twisting harbor streets to claim his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory** after 69 starts and 15 career podiums, winning by 22.45 seconds over Niki Lauda.
