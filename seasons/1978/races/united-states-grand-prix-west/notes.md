## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
VILLENEUVE CRASH & REUTEMANN VICTORY: Carlos Reutemann took pole in the Ferrari 312T3, but teammate Gilles Villeneuve shot into the lead from 2nd on the grid. Villeneuve led majestically for 38 laps until coming to lap Clay Regazzoni's Shadow. Villeneuve mistimed the overtake and launched over Regazzoni's rear wheel into the barriers. Carlos Reutemann inherited the lead and took a commanding victory over Mario Andretti by 11.061 seconds.
