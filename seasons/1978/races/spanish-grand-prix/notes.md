## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
LOTUS 1-2 CRUSHING DISPLAY: Both Team Lotus drivers had the Lotus 79 at their disposal. Mario Andretti took pole and Ronnie Peterson qualified 2nd. James Hunt took the lead at the start in his McLaren M26, but on lap 6 Andretti breezed past with vastly superior cornering grip generated by ground effect. Andretti and Peterson delivered a crushing 1-2 finish for Lotus, Peterson crossing 19.56 seconds behind Andretti.
