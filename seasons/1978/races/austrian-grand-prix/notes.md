## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PETERSON'S WET-WEATHER BRILLIANCE: Ronnie Peterson qualified on pole. On lap 7, torrential rain caused chaotic aquaplaning; Mario Andretti spun off and crashed into Carlos Reutemann, bringing out the RED FLAG. At the restart on a sodden track, Peterson displayed his legendary car control, mastering treacherous conditions to win by 47.44 seconds over Patrick Depailler (Tyrrell) and Gilles Villeneuve (Ferrari, scoring his maiden podium!).
