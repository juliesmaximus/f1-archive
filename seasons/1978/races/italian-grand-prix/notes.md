## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PETERSON TRAGEDY & ANDRETTI CHAMPIONSHIP: Mario Andretti qualified on pole. The start procedure was badly mishandled: the green light was illuminated before the back markers had stopped, compressing the pack into the narrow approach to the Rettifilo chicane. James Hunt touched wheels with Ronnie Peterson's older Lotus 78 (Peterson's 79 had broken in morning warmup), pitching Peterson hard into the Armco barrier; the fuel tank ruptured and ignited. Vittorio Brambilla's Surtees was struck on the helmet by a flying wheel. Hunt, Regazzoni, and Depailler pulled Peterson from the burning wreckage; Peterson suffered severe multiple leg fractures. At the late restart, Andretti and Gilles Villeneuve jumped the start and crossed the line 1st and 2nd, but both were penalized one minute, handing victory to Niki Lauda in the Brabham BT46. Tragically, overnight at Niguarda Hospital in Milan, Peterson suffered bone marrow embolism into his bloodstream and died on Monday morning, September 11. Mario Andretti clinched the **World Drivers' Championship**, but celebrations were muted in deep sorrow.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
Mario Andretti became the second American World Champion (after Phil Hill in 1961), overshadowed by the tragic loss of teammate Ronnie Peterson.
