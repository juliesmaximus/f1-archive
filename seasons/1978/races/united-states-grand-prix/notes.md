## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
REUTEMANN WINS AT THE GLEN: Newly crowned World Champion Mario Andretti claimed pole position for Lotus before his home crowd. Reutemann in the Ferrari 312T3 made a superb start to seize 2nd behind Andretti, then took the lead when Andretti's engine began misfiring. Reutemann pulled away to take his 4th victory of the season by 19.73 seconds over Alan Jones, who scored Williams Grand Prix Engineering's very first podium in Formula One history in the Williams FW06!
