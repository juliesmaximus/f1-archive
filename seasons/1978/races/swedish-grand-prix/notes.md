## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE BRABHAM BT46B 'FAN CAR' LEGEND: In response to Lotus's ground effect, Brabham chief designer Gordon Murray fitted a giant extraction fan to the rear of the Brabham BT46B, claiming it was strictly to cool the Alfa Romeo flat-12 engine. In reality, the fan sucked air from underneath the sealed chassis floor, creating astronomical levels of aerodynamic downforce even at slow speeds. Niki Lauda qualified 3rd and followed Mario Andretti until Andretti's engine blew on lap 47. Lauda then sped away to win by an astonishing 34.6 seconds over Riccardo Patrese! Competitors protested furiously, citing safety concerns over thrown gravel and moving aerodynamic devices. The CSI upheld the win as legal under existing rules, but Brabham owner Bernie Ecclestone voluntarily withdrew the car to avoid political war, making this the FAN CAR'S ONLY RACE AND ONLY WIN.
