## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
GILLES VILLENEUVE MAIDEN HOME VICTORY: The Canadian GP moved to a new purpose-built track on Montreal's man-made Île Notre-Dame island. Reigning world champion Mario Andretti and pole-sitter Jean-Pierre Jarier (Lotus) battled early. Jarier dominated the first 49 laps until an oil leak halted his Lotus. French-Canadian hero Gilles Villeneuve in the Ferrari 312T3 assumed the lead amidst deafening cheers. Villeneuve controlled the final 21 laps in freezing cold to claim his **maiden Formula One victory** on home soil, hoisted onto the podium with Canadian Prime Minister Pierre Trudeau presenting the trophy!

## Historical Significance & Legacy
One of the most emotional moments in Canadian sports history; the circuit was later renamed Circuit Gilles Villeneuve in his honor following his death in 1982.
