# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1978 - Calendar

The 1978 Formula One season was the 29th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 16 championship rounds between January 15 and October 8, 1978.

The 1978 season stands as one of the most technologically watershed and emotionally dramatic years in Grand Prix history:
- **The Lotus 79 "Black Beauty" & Ground Effect Dominance:** Colin Chapman, Peter Wright, and Martin Ogilvie perfected the underbody venturi tunnel ground-effect concept with sliding side skirts in the Lotus 79. American Mario Andretti won 6 Grands Prix to capture the **World Drivers' Championship**, while Team Lotus captured the **World Constructors' Championship**.
- **Ronnie Peterson's Tragic Triumph:** Swedish maestro Ronnie Peterson accepted number-two status behind Andretti, winning 2 races and securing the runner-up championship spot posthumously after sustaining fatal complications following a massive multiple-car start collision at the Italian Grand Prix at Monza.
- **The Brabham BT46B "Fan Car" Controversy:** Gordon Murray engineered a massive rear extraction fan powered by the Alfa Romeo flat-12 engine, officially claiming it was for cooling. At Anderstorp (Swedish GP), Niki Lauda demolished the field to win. Following fierce protests from rival constructors, Bernie Ecclestone voluntarily withdrew the car, and the CSI subsequently banned vacuum-fan extraction devices.
- **Gilles Villeneuve's Fairy-Tale Maiden Win:** At the inaugural Canadian Grand Prix on the Île Notre-Dame circuit in Montreal, French-Canadian hero Gilles Villeneuve scored his maiden Formula One victory in front of an ecstatic home crowd in the Ferrari 312T3.
- **Patrick Depailler's Maiden Monaco Win:** Tyrrell stalwart Patrick Depailler achieved his long-sought maiden career victory around the streets of Monaco.
