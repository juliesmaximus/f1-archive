# 1964 Monaco Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXII Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco
- **Round**: 1 of 10 in the 1964 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 10 May 1964
- **Location**: Circuit de Monaco, Monte Carlo, Monaco
- **Circuit Length**: 3.145 km (1.954 miles)
- **Distance**: 100 laps (314.500 km / 195.421 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:34.000
- **Fastest Lap**: Graham Hill (BRM P261) - 1:33.900 (Lap 53)
- **Race Winner**: Graham Hill (BRM P261) - 2:41:19.500 (117.000 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Graham Hill (BRM) - 100 laps
  2. Richie Ginther (BRM) - 100 laps (+2.400s)
  3. Peter Arundell (Lotus-Climax) - 97 laps (+3 laps)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **BRM 1-2 Sweep**: Graham Hill won his second consecutive Monaco Grand Prix, leading teammate Richie Ginther in an emphatic 1-2 finish for British Racing Motors (BRM).
- **Clark & Gurney Retirements**: Jim Clark led from pole until an anti-roll bar collapse on lap 37 forced him into retirement. Dan Gurney then took the lead in his Brabham until a broken gearbox halted his car on lap 62, promoting Hill to the lead.
- **Peter Arundell Maiden Podium**: In his official World Championship debut for Team Lotus, British driver Peter Arundell drove cleanly to finish 3rd on the podium.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Sunny, pleasant Mediterranean spring afternoon; clean, dry track throughout.
