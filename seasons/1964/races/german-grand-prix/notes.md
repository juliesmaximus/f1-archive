# 1964 German Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXV Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Round**: 6 of 10 in the 1964 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 2 August 1964
- **Location**: Nürburgring Nordschleife, Nürburg, Germany
- **Circuit Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 miles)
- **Distance**: 15 laps (342.150 km / 212.602 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156 Aero) - 8:38.400
- **Fastest Lap**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156 Aero) - 8:39.000 (Lap 11)
- **Race Winner**: John Surtees (Ferrari 156 Aero) - 2:12:04.800 (155.426 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 15 laps
  2. Graham Hill (BRM) - 15 laps (+1:15.600)
  3. Lorenzo Bandini (Ferrari) - 15 laps (+4:52.800)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Surtees' Masterclass & Championship Revival**: John Surtees delivered an imperial performance in the Ferrari 156 Aero, qualifying on pole, setting fastest lap, and leading 14 of 15 laps to defeat Graham Hill by 1 minute, 15 seconds. The win ignited Surtees' championship campaign.
- **Tragic Death of Carel Godin de Beaufort**: During practice, popular Dutch gentleman driver Count Carel Godin de Beaufort lost control of his bright orange Porsche 718 at the treacherous Bergwerk corner. He was thrown from the cockpit and suffered fatal head and chest injuries, dying in a Cologne hospital the following evening.
- **Debut of Honda in Formula One**: Honda made its historic Formula One World Championship debut with the RA271, featuring a transverse 1.5-litre V12 engine revving to 12,000 rpm, driven by American Ronnie Bucknum (retired on lap 12 after steering failure).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Cool, overcast Eifel morning; dry track during the Grand Prix.
