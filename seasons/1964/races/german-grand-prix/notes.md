# 1964 Round 6: XXV Großer Preis von Deutschland - Verified Race Record

## Circuit & Geographic Metadata
- **Official Race Title**: XXV Großer Preis von Deutschland
- **Historic Name Notes**: 25th German Grand Prix at the Nürburgring Nordschleife; debut of Honda in Formula One.
- **Circuit Full Name**: Nürburgring Nordschleife
- **Location**: Nürburg, Eifel Mountains, Rhineland-Palatinate, West Germany
- **Host Country**: West Germany
- **Track Length**: 22.810 km (14.173 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 620 m (2034 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 15 laps / 342.150 km (212.602 mi)
- **Official Race Date**: 1964-08-02

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Warm, dry, sunny mountain weather.
- **Spectator Attendance**: Over 350,000 spectators.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Start-und-Ziel grandstand and Karussell embankments packed.

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- John Surtees Nordschleife Masterclass: Driving the new V8-powered Ferrari 158, John Surtees took pole position and dominated the race, winning by 1 minute 15.6 seconds over Graham Hill's BRM.
- Honda Formula One World Championship Debut: Japanese manufacturer Honda made their official World Championship debut with the RA271, powered by a transverse 1.5L V12 producing 220 bhp at 12,000 RPM, driven by American Ronnie Bucknum (retired on lap 12 after steering failure).
- Carel Godin de Beaufort Fatality: During practice on Saturday 1 August, Dutch aristocrat Carel Godin de Beaufort suffered a fatal crash at Bergwerk in his orange Porsche 718, succumbing to his injuries the following day aged 30.
- Clark Engine DNF: Clark retired on lap 1 with transmission and engine failure.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship Grand Prix for Honda as a constructor and engine manufacturer.
- John Surtees' 2nd career Formula One victory (both at the Nürburgring Nordschleife).
- Tragic death of Dutch racing pioneer Carel Godin de Beaufort.
