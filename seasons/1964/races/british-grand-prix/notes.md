- Jim Clark's Brands Hatch Grand Slam: Jim Clark mastered the undulating Kent circuit, taking pole position (1:38.1), fastest lap (1:38.8), victory, and leading all 80 laps to score his 4th career Grand Slam, holding off Graham Hill by 2.8 seconds.
- Inaugural Brands Hatch Championship GP: The famous circuit hosted its first World Championship race, marking the start of alternating British GPs with Silverstone.
