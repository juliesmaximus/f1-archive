# 1964 British Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XVII RAC British Grand Prix / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Round**: 5 of 10 in the 1964 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Saturday, 11 July 1964
- **Location**: Brands Hatch Circuit, West Kingsdown, Kent, United Kingdom
- **Circuit Length**: 4.265 km (2.650 miles)
- **Distance**: 80 laps (341.196 km / 212.009 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:38.100
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:38.800 (Lap 73)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:15:07.000 (151.785 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 80 laps
  2. Graham Hill (BRM) - 80 laps (+2.800s)
  3. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 80 laps (+1:20.600)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Inaugural Grand Prix at Brands Hatch**: The RAC British Grand Prix made its World Championship debut on the natural amphitheater curves of Brands Hatch in Kent, designated the honorary 'Grand Prix d'Europe' for 1964.
- **Clark's Third Consecutive British GP Win**: Jim Clark took pole, set fastest lap, and led all 80 laps to take his third consecutive British Grand Prix victory, resisting relentless pressure from Graham Hill to win by 2.8 seconds.
- **Clark Extends Title Lead**: Clark's third win of the year extended his championship lead to 4 points over Graham Hill.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Warm, dry British summer day; clean track throughout.
