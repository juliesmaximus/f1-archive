## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Jim Clark Zandvoort Mastery: Jim Clark took the lead from 2nd on the grid on lap 1 and dominated all 80 laps to win by 53.6 seconds over John Surtees' Ferrari.
- Dan Gurney Maiden Brabham Pole: Dan Gurney scored the FIRST-EVER POLE POSITION FOR THE BRABHAM CHASSIS (1:31.2) before steering failure forced retirement on lap 23.
- Peter Arundell Back-to-Back Podiums: Arundell finished 3rd again in the second works Lotus.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First World Championship pole position for a Brabham chassis (Dan Gurney).
- Jim Clark's 2nd consecutive Dutch Grand Prix victory.
