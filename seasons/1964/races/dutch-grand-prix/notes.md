# 1964 Dutch Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XII Grote Prijs van Nederland
- **Round**: 2 of 10 in the 1964 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 24 May 1964
- **Location**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort, Netherlands
- **Circuit Length**: 4.193 km (2.605 miles)
- **Distance**: 80 laps (335.440 km / 208.433 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Dan Gurney (Brabham-Climax BT7) - 1:31.200
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 1:32.800 (Lap 6)
- **Race Winner**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 25) - 2:07:35.400 (157.694 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 80 laps
  2. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 80 laps (+53.600s)
  3. Peter Arundell (Lotus-Climax) - 79 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Clark's Dominant Zandvoort Mastery**: Jim Clark shot past pole-sitter Dan Gurney into the Tarzan corner at the start, leading every single lap to take his first victory of the 1964 season by 53.6 seconds over John Surtees.
- **Dan Gurney's Broken Steering**: Pole-sitter Dan Gurney ran 2nd until broken steering forced his retirement on lap 23.
- **Arundell's Second Consecutive Podium**: Peter Arundell backed up his Monaco performance with another steady drive to finish 3rd for Team Lotus.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Sunny, windy North Sea weather; dry track with blowing sand in corners.
