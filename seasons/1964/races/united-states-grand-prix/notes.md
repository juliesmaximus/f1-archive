# 1964 United States Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: VII United States Grand Prix
- **Round**: 9 of 10 in the 1964 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 4 October 1964
- **Location**: Watkins Glen International, Watkins Glen, New York, USA
- **Circuit Length**: 3.701 km (2.300 miles)
- **Distance**: 110 laps (407.164 km / 253.000 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 33) - 1:12.650
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 33) - 1:12.700 (Lap 63)
- **Race Winner**: Graham Hill (BRM P261) - 2:16:38.000 (178.691 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Graham Hill (BRM) - 110 laps
  2. John Surtees (Ferrari) - 110 laps (+30.500s)
  3. Jo Siffert (Brabham-BRM) - 109 laps (+1 lap)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Hill Wins to Seize Championship Lead**: Graham Hill drove an imperious race in his BRM P261 to take victory by 30.5 seconds over John Surtees. The win gave Hill a 5-point lead over Surtees and 9 points over Clark heading into the final round in Mexico.
- **Blue-and-White Ferrari Livery**: Following Enzo Ferrari's dispute with the Italian motorsport federation (ACI) over the homologation of the 250 LM sports car, Ferrari surrendered his Italian racing license. The team entered the US Grand Prix under the North American Racing Team (NART) banner, painting the cars in American racing colors of blue and white.
- **Jo Siffert Maiden Podium**: Swiss driver Jo Siffert drove a magnificent race in his privately entered Brabham-BRM to finish 3rd, scoring his maiden World Championship podium.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Crisp, sunny autumn conditions in upstate New York; clean, dry track throughout.
