## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ferrari Races in Blue and White: Furious at the Italian Automobile Club (ACI) for failing to support the homologation of the mid-engine Ferrari 250 LM sports car, Enzo Ferrari surrendered his Italian racing competitor's license in protest. Ferrari entered his cars under Luigi Chinetti's North American Racing Team (NART), painting the factory Ferraris in American racing colors: BLUE AND WHITE instead of traditional Rosso Corsa!
- Graham Hill Watkins Glen Triumph: Graham Hill won by 30.5 seconds over John Surtees in the blue-and-white Ferrari.
- Clark Lotus Swap: Jim Clark led until engine injection trouble struck on lap 40; he took over teammate Mike Spence's Lotus 33, but retired with fuel pump failure.

## Records Set or Equalled
- Sole season in history where factory Ferrari Formula One cars raced in blue-and-white livery (NART).
- Graham Hill took his 2nd consecutive United States Grand Prix win.
