# 1964 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1964 World Championship of Drivers and the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers. Only each driver's best 6 results out of the 10 rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship tally. Only each constructor's best 6 results out of the 10 rounds counted towards the Manufacturers' Cup.

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Points System**: 9-6-4-3-2-1 awarded to the top 6 classified finishers in both the Drivers' Championship and Manufacturers' Cup.
- **Best 6 rule**: Only best 6 results counted for both Drivers and Constructors.
- **Constructors' Eligibility**: Only the highest-placing car of each make scored points.
