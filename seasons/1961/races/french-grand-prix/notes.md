# 1961 French Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: 47e Grand Prix de l'ACF / Grand Prix d'Europe
- **Round**: 4 of 8 in the 1961 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 2 July 1961
- **Location**: Reims-Gueux, Reims, France
- **Circuit Length**: 8.302 km (5.159 miles)
- **Distance**: 52 laps (431.704 km / 268.248 miles)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Baghetti's Miracle Debut Victory**: In one of the greatest fairy tales in Formula One history, 26-year-old Italian rookie Giancarlo Baghetti won the Grand Prix on his World Championship debut driving a Ferrari 156 entered by the Italian racing federation (FISA).
- **Photo-Finish Slipstreaming Climax (0.1s Margin)**: The works Ferraris of Hill, von Trips, and Ginther all suffered mechanical retirements or spins. Baghetti found himself in a fierce three-car slipstreaming war against Porsche drivers Dan Gurney and Jo Bonnier. On the final sprint out of Thillois hairpin, Baghetti slingshotted past Gurney's Porsche 718 to take victory by just 0.1 seconds (a single car length).
- **All-Time Milestone**: Baghetti became the only driver in modern Formula One history to win a World Championship Grand Prix on his debut (excluding the very first race in 1950 won by Farina).
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Oppressively hot midsummer Champagne weather; ambient temperature exceeding 38°C; melting track surface.
