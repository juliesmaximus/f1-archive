## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Giancarlo Baghetti's Miracle Debut Win: Italian rookie Giancarlo Baghetti entered his VERY FIRST FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX driving a customer Ferrari 156 for the FISA federation. When the factory Ferraris of Hill, von Trips, and Ginther all dropped out with engine and spin issues, Baghetti found himself in a heart-stopping slipstreaming battle against Dan Gurney's Porsche 718.
- Final Corner Slipstream Decider: On the 52nd and final lap, Gurney led out of Thillois hairpin. Baghetti tucked into Gurney's slipstream and slingshotted alongside at 160 mph, winning by ONE-TENTH OF A SECOND (0.1s) across the finish line!
- Unbroken Historical Record: Giancarlo Baghetti remains the ONLY DRIVER IN FORMULA ONE HISTORY TO WIN HIS MAIDEN WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX (excluding Nino Farina who won the very first race in 1950).

## Records Set or Equalled

- Closest finish at Reims: 0.1 seconds (Baghetti over Gurney).
