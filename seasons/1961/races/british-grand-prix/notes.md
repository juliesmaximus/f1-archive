# 1961 British Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XIV RAC British Grand Prix
- **Round**: 5 of 8 in the 1961 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Saturday, 15 July 1961
- **Location**: Aintree Motor Racing Circuit, Liverpool, United Kingdom
- **Circuit Length**: 4.828 km (3.000 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (362.100 km / 225.000 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Phil Hill (Ferrari 156) - 1:58.800
- **Fastest Lap**: Tony Brooks (BRM-Climax) - 1:57.800 (Lap 72)
- **Race Winner**: Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari 156) - 2:40:53.600 (135.265 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari) - 75 laps
  2. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 75 laps (+46.000s)
  3. Richie Ginther (Ferrari) - 75 laps (+46.800s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Ferrari 1-2-3 Wet Sweep**: Heavy rain fell on race day, creating treacherous standing water around the Aintree horse-racing circuit. Wolfgang von Trips took the lead on lap 7 and mastered the spray to win comfortably, leading a dominant Ferrari 1-2-3 sweep with Phil Hill and Richie Ginther.
- **Historical 4WD Debut**: The revolutionary 4-wheel-drive Ferguson P99 made its World Championship debut driven by Jack Fairman. Stirling Moss took over the car after retiring his own Lotus, setting competitive lap times before the car was black-flagged and disqualified for receiving an illegal push-start in the pit lane.
- **Tony Brooks Fastest Lap**: Tony Brooks set the fastest lap of the race (1:57.800) in the closing stages in his BRM-Climax.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Heavy torrential rain at the start, easing to persistent drizzle and drying track; very wet asphalt.
