- **Historic Name Notes**: 14th running of the RAC British Grand Prix at Aintree.

- **Location**: Aintree, Merseyside, England, United Kingdom
- **Host Country**: United Kingdom
- **Track Length**: 4.828 km (3.000 mi)
- **Track Altitude**: 20 m (66 ft) above sea level
- **Race Distance**: 75 laps / 362.100 km (224.999 mi)

## Weather, Environment & Spectators
- **Weather & Track Conditions**: Heavy rain, soaked track with standing water and mist across Merseyside.
- **Spectator Attendance**: Over 80,000 hardy British spectators.
- **Grandstand & Infrastructure Notes**: Grand National horse-racing grandstands sheltered fans from driving rain.

- **1st** : Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari) — 2:40:53.600 [9 pts]

- **3rd** : Richie Ginther (Ferrari) — +46.800 [4 pts]

## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- Ferrari 1-2-3 Wet Sweep: Despite treacherous wet conditions, the Sharknose Ferraris completely dominated, taking a 1-2-3 sweep led by Wolfgang von Trips, with Phil Hill 2nd and Richie Ginther 3rd.
- First 4WD and ABS F1 Car: The Ferguson P99, entered by Rob Walker and driven by Jack Fairman and Stirling Moss, became the FIRST FOUR-WHEEL-DRIVE CAR AND FIRST CAR WITH ANTI-LOCK BRAKES (Dunlop Maxaret) to compete in a World Championship Grand Prix (disqualified for a push-start).
- Championship Fight Narrows: Wolfgang von Trips took the championship lead with 27 points to Phil Hill's 25 points.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First 4-wheel-drive (4WD) and anti-lock brake (ABS) car in World Championship history: Ferguson P99.
- Wolfgang von Trips took the lead of the World Drivers' Championship.
