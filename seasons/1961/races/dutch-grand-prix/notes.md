# 1961 Dutch Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: IX Grote Prijs van Nederland
- **Round**: 2 of 8 in the 1961 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Monday, 22 May 1961
- **Location**: Circuit Zandvoort, Zandvoort, Netherlands
- **Circuit Length**: 4.193 km (2.605 miles)
- **Distance**: 75 laps (314.475 km / 195.405 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Phil Hill (Ferrari 156) - 1:35.700
- **Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax 21) - 1:35.500 (Lap 7)
- **Race Winner**: Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari 156) - 2:01:52.100 (154.821 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari) - 75 laps
  2. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 75 laps (+0.900s)
  3. Jim Clark (Lotus-Climax) - 75 laps (+13.100s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Historic Zero-Retirement, Zero-Pit-Stop Grand Prix**: A unique, historic event in Formula One World Championship history: all 15 cars that started the race finished the race, and not a single car made a pit stop during the entire 75 laps.
- **First German World Championship Victory**: Count Wolfgang von Trips took the lead into the Tarzan corner on lap 1 and held off teammate Phil Hill to become the first German driver to win a World Championship Grand Prix.
- **Jim Clark's First World Championship Fastest Lap**: Jim Clark set the fastest lap of the race (1:35.500) and finished 3rd, taking his second career podium for Team Lotus.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Mild, breezy seaside conditions; completely dry asphalt throughout.
