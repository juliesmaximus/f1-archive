## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- The Zero-Retirement, Zero-Pit-Stop Miracle: In an extraordinary display of mechanical reliability, EVERY SINGLE CAR THAT STARTED THE RACE FINISHED THE RACE (15 starters, 15 classified finishers). Not a single car made a pit stop during the entire 75-lap Grand Prix — a feat that was never repeated until the 2005 United States GP (6 cars) and 2005 Italian GP (20 cars).
- Wolfgang von Trips Maiden Win: German Count Wolfgang 'Taffy' von Trips won by 0.9 seconds over Ferrari teammate Phil Hill, becoming the FIRST GERMAN DRIVER TO WIN A FORMULA ONE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP GRAND PRIX.
- Jim Clark Maiden Podium Battle: 25-year-old Jim Clark starred in the new works Lotus 21, battling wheel-to-wheel with Phil Hill to finish 3rd.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First Formula One World Championship race with ZERO retirements and ZERO pit stops (15 starters, 15 finishers).
- First German driver to win a World Championship Grand Prix: Wolfgang von Trips.
