# 1961 Italian Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XXXII Gran Premio d'Italia
- **Round**: 7 of 8 in the 1961 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 10 September 1961
- **Location**: Autodromo Nazionale di Monza (10 km Combined Road + Banked Oval Circuit), Monza, Italy
- **Circuit Length**: 10.000 km (6.214 miles)
- **Distance**: 43 laps (430.000 km / 267.189 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Wolfgang von Trips (Ferrari 156) - 2:46.300
- **Fastest Lap**: Giancarlo Baghetti (Ferrari 156) - 2:48.400 (Lap 2)
- **Race Winner**: Phil Hill (Ferrari 156) - 2:03:13.000 (209.387 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 43 laps
  2. Dan Gurney (Porsche) - 43 laps (+31.200s)
  3. Bruce McLaren (Cooper-Climax) - 43 laps (+2:28.400)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **The Catastrophic Monza Disaster**: Entering the Vedano braking zone before the Parabolica curve on lap 2, championship leader Wolfgang von Trips moved over and touched wheels with Jim Clark's Lotus. Von Trips' Ferrari went airborne, struck the wire spectator fence, vaulted up the dirt embankment, and rolled violently back onto the track. Wolfgang von Trips and 14 spectators were killed, and dozens more seriously injured. Jim Clark walked away unhurt.
- **Race Allowed to Continue**: Organizers allowed the race to proceed to prevent panic and facilitate ambulance access on the perimeter roads.
- **Phil Hill Becomes First American World Champion**: Phil Hill, driving without knowledge of his teammate's death, took the lead and drove to victory. With von Trips fatally injured, Phil Hill clinched the 1961 World Drivers' Championship, becoming the first American driver to win the Formula One World Championship.
- **Ferrari Clinches Constructors' Cup**: Ferrari secured their first International Cup for F1 Manufacturers on home soil.
- **End of the Monza Banking**: This race marked the permanent abandonment of the high-speed concrete banking in Formula One World Championship history.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Very warm, clear late-summer afternoon; dry track.
