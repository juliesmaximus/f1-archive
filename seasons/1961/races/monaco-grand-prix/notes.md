# 1961 Monaco Grand Prix - Race Notes

## Official Race Identification
- **Official Name**: XIX Grand Prix Automobile de Monaco
- **Round**: 1 of 8 in the 1961 FIA Formula One World Championship
- **Date**: Sunday, 14 May 1961
- **Location**: Circuit de Monaco, Monte Carlo, Monaco
- **Circuit Length**: 3.145 km (1.954 miles)
- **Distance**: 100 laps (314.500 km / 195.421 miles)

## Official Results & Key Metrics
- **Pole Position**: Stirling Moss (Rob Walker Lotus-Climax 18) - 1:39.100
- **Fastest Lap**: Richie Ginther (Ferrari 156) & Stirling Moss (Lotus-Climax 18) - 1:36.300
- **Race Winner**: Stirling Moss (Rob Walker Lotus-Climax 18) - 2:45:50.100 (113.788 km/h)
- **Podium**:
  1. Stirling Moss (Lotus-Climax) - 100 laps
  2. Richie Ginther (Ferrari) - 100 laps (+3.600s)
  3. Phil Hill (Ferrari) - 100 laps (+41.300s)

## Verified Race Scenarios & Primary-Source Facts
- **Moss's Tactical Masterpiece**: In blistering heat, Stirling Moss removed the side body panels of his dark blue Rob Walker Lotus 18 to allow cool air to flow over his legs and cockpit. Despite giving away over 30 horsepower to the factory Ferrari 156 "Sharknose" cars of Richie Ginther, Phil Hill, and Wolfgang von Trips, Moss drove 100 flat-out laps with miraculous precision to win by 3.6 seconds.
- **Debut of 1.5-Litre Engine Formula**: The opening round for the controversial new 1.5-litre engine regulations. Ferrari arrived with their dominant rear-engine 120-degree V6 Dino 156.
- **Ginther's Relentless Pursuit**: Richie Ginther drove the race of his life, repeatedly attacking Moss and setting the co-fastest lap (1:36.300) before taking 2nd.
- **Track & Weather Conditions**: Extremely hot, sunny Mediterranean afternoon; completely dry track.
