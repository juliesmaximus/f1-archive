## Verified Race Scenarios, Anomalies & Controversies
- 1.5-Litre Formula Regulations Debut: The FIA introduced 1,500cc maximum engine capacity to reduce speeds. Scoring updated to award 9 points for 1st place (9-6-4-3-2-1).
- Moss's Tactical Masterpiece: In his underpowered 4-cylinder Lotus 18, Stirling Moss removed the side cockpit panels for cooling and drove on the ragged edge for 100 laps to resist the relentless three-pronged assault of the factory 'Sharknose' Ferrari 156 V6s (Richie Ginther, Phil Hill, Wolfgang von Trips), winning by just 3.6 seconds.
- Ginther's Sensational Chase: Richie Ginther set the fastest lap (1:36.3) in the 120-degree V6 Ferrari, finishing 2nd.

## Records Set or Equalled
- First race under the 9-6-4-3-2-1 World Championship points scoring system.
- Stirling Moss won his 3rd career Monaco Grand Prix.
