# 1961 FIA Formula One World Championship Standings

Final official standings for the 1961 World Championship of Drivers and the International Cup for F1 Manufacturers. Only each driver's best 5 results out of the 8 rounds counted towards the final Drivers' Championship tally. Only each constructor's best 5 results out of the 8 rounds counted towards the Manufacturers' Cup.

### Championship Regulations & Scoring System:
- **Engine Regulations**: Introduction of the new 1.5-litre unsupercharged engine formula (reduced from 2.5 litres).
- **Drivers' Championship Points**: 9-6-4-3-2-1 for the top 6 classified finishers (win points increased to 9).
- **Manufacturers' Cup Points**: 8-6-4-3-2-1 for the top 6 classified finishers for each make (only highest-placing car of each constructor scored; win remained 8 points for Constructors).
- **Best 5 rule**: Only best 5 results counted for both Drivers and Constructors.
