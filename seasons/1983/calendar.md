# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1983 - Calendar

The 1983 Formula One season was the 34th season of FIA Formula One motor racing. It consisted of 15 championship rounds between March 13 and October 15, 1983.

The 1983 season was a historic turning point marked by major technical revolutions, tactical breakthroughs, and the triumph of turbocharged power:
- **First Turbocharged World Drivers' Champion:** Brazilian Nelson Piquet drove Gordon Murray's dart-shaped Brabham BT52 powered by Paul Rosche's 850 bhp BMW M12/13 turbocharged 4-cylinder engine to capture his **second World Drivers' Championship** (59 points), becoming the **first driver in Formula One history to win the World Championship in a turbocharged car**.
- **The Flat-Bottom Regulatory Mandate:** In the interest of safety following the horrific accidents of 1982, FISA abruptly banned ground-effect venturi tunnels and sliding side skirts, mandating completely flat stepped floors between the wheels.
- **The Pit Stop Revolution:** Gordon Murray pioneered planned mid-race refuelling and tire changes, running half fuel loads to maximize qualifying pace and high-grip soft compound tires, transforming Grand Prix tactics forever.
- **John Watson's Historic Miracle from 22nd:** At Long Beach, McLaren's John Watson started 22nd on the grid and drove through the field to win, setting the **all-time Formula One record for the lowest grid position to win a Grand Prix** (with teammate Niki Lauda finishing 2nd from 23rd!).
- **Final Victory for the Ford Cosworth DFV:** At the Detroit Grand Prix, Michele Alboreto scored Tyrrell's victory—marking the **155th and final World Championship victory for the legendary Ford Cosworth DFV engine**, ending an era of naturally aspirated dominance spanning 17 seasons.
- **Return to Modern Spa-Francorchamps:** Formula One returned to a modernized, shortened 7.0 km Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps for the first time since 1970.
