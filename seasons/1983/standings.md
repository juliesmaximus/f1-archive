# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1983 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Nelson Piquet became the first driver in Formula One history to win the FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship in a turbocharged car, scoring 59 net points in Gordon Murray's Brabham BT52 powered by the BMW M12/13 turbocharged 4-cylinder engine. Alain Prost finished as runner-up with 57 net points for Renault.

Scoring system: 9-6-4-3-2-1 points awarded to the top 6 classified finishers in each Grand Prix. The best 11 results across the 15 rounds counted toward the Drivers' Championship. In the season opener in Brazil, Keke Rosberg finished 2nd but was disqualified for an illegal push-start in the pit lane; the FIA elected not to advance the other finishers, leaving 2nd place vacant and the 6 second-place points unawarded.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Scuderia Ferrari captured their eighth World Constructors' Championship with 89 points and 4 victories with the Ferrari 126C2B and 126C3 driven by René Arnoux and Patrick Tambay. Renault finished runner-up with 79 points.

Under FIA regulations, chassis-engine combinations were scored as separate makes in the International Cup for F1 Constructors. All rounds and all finishes counted toward the Constructors' Championship.
