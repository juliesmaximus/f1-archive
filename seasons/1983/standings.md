# Formula 1 World Championship Season 1983 - Final Standings

## World Drivers' Championship

Nelson Piquet became the first driver in Formula One history to win the FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship with a turbocharged engine, scoring 59 net points in the Brabham BT52 BMW Turbo. Alain Prost finished as runner-up with 57 net points.

## World Constructors' Championship (International Cup for F1 Constructors)

Scuderia Ferrari captured their eighth World Constructors' Championship with 89 points and 4 victories with the Ferrari 126C2B and 126C3 driven by René Arnoux and Patrick Tambay. Renault finished runner-up with 79 points.
