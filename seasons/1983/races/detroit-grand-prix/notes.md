## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
HISTORIC FINAL WIN FOR FORD COSWORTH DFV & TYRRELL: On the concrete-lined Detroit street track, naturally aspirated cars challenged the turbo giants. René Arnoux led until his electrical system failed on lap 31. Nelson Piquet's Brabham led until suffering a slow puncture on lap 51, forcing an emergency pit stop. Michele Alboreto in Ken Tyrrell's nimble Tyrrell 011 swept into the lead, crossing the line to take his second career Grand Prix victory. This was the 155th and final Formula One World Championship victory for the legendary Ford Cosworth DFV engine (spanning 1967–1983) and the final Grand Prix victory in the history of Team Tyrrell.
