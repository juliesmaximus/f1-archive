## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE FORD COSWORTH DFV'S 155TH & FINAL VICTORY: René Arnoux took pole in the Ferrari turbo. On the abrasive, bumpy city streets, turbo engines overheated and suffered violent lag. Nelson Piquet led in the Brabham-BMW until a flat tire on lap 51 forced a pit stop. Michele Alboreto in the nimble, naturally aspirated Tyrrell 011 Ford Cosworth DFV seized the lead. Alboreto drove with clinical precision to take victory by 7.70 seconds over Keke Rosberg (Williams) and John Watson (McLaren). This marked the **155th and final Formula One victory for the legendary Ford Cosworth DFV engine**, concluding the most successful engine chapter in Grand Prix history.
