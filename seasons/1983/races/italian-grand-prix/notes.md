## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S MONZA RESURGENCE: Brabham introduced new lightweight rear wings and upgraded BMW M12/13 turbo mapping and fuel blending. Riccardo Patrese took pole, but Nelson Piquet took command of the race on lap 3 after Patrese's engine failed. Piquet dominated with blistering speed, winning by 10.2 seconds over René Arnoux (Ferrari). Prost retired on lap 26 with a blown turbo, slashing Prost's championship lead to just 5 points over Arnoux and Piquet.
