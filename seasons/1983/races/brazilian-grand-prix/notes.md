## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S BT52 DEBUT CRUSH & ROSBERG DISQUALIFICATION: In the first race under the new flat-bottom regulations, reigning World Champion Keke Rosberg took pole in the Williams FW08C. Nelson Piquet in Gordon Murray's brand-new dart-shaped Brabham BT52 BMW Turbo dominated the race, executing a scheduled pit stop and pulling away to win by 20.47 seconds. Rosberg crossed the line 2nd but was disqualified after receiving an illegal push-start in the pit lane following a fuel flash-fire during his pit stop. The 2nd-place points were left unallocated.
