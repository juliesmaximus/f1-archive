## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PIQUET'S DOMINANT OPENER & ROSBERG PUSH-START DISQUALIFICATION: In the season opener under new flat-bottom rules, Nelson Piquet took a dominant home victory in the Gordon Murray-designed Brabham BT52 BMW Turbo, executing a perfectly planned mid-race pit stop for fuel and tires. Keke Rosberg qualified on pole in the Williams FW08C Ford. During his pit stop on lap 28, fuel spilled onto Rosberg's hot exhaust, sparking a sudden cockpit fire. Rosberg leaped out, extinguished the flames, jumped back in, and was push-started by Williams mechanics down the pit lane. He drove an extraordinary recovery back to 2nd at the chequered flag, but was subsequently disqualified by the stewards for the push-start. The FIA chose not to advance the trailing drivers; 2nd place was left vacant and the 6 second-place points were left unawarded. Niki Lauda was classified 3rd (4 pts), Jacques Laffite 4th (3 pts), Patrick Tambay 5th (2 pts), and Marc Surer 6th (1 pt). Elio de Angelis was also disqualified for illegally switching to the spare car with a Ford engine after his Renault turbo failed on the parade lap.
