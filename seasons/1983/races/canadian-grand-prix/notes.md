## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ARNOUX'S MAIDEN FERRARI WIN: René Arnoux claimed his first victory for Scuderia Ferrari driving the 126C2B, leading 67 of the 70 laps from pole position. Eddie Cheever drove a stellar race for Renault to finish 2nd, with Patrick Tambay completing the podium in 3rd for Ferrari. Danny Sullivan was disqualified from 10th place because his Tyrrell 011 was found to be 4 kg underweight at the post-race weigh-in.
