## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
KEKE ROSBERG'S SLICK TIRE GAMBLE: Alain Prost took pole in the turbo Renault. On a damp, greasy track, every major team fitted wet weather tires except Williams: Keke Rosberg and Jacques Laffite gambled on slick Goodyear tires. Rosberg made a lightning launch, rocketing from 5th to lead into Sainte Dévote. Sliding the car on the edge of adhesion, Rosberg was up to 5 seconds per lap faster than the wet-shod field, building an astronomical lead of over 30 seconds within 10 laps! Rosberg dominated to win by 18.475 seconds over Nelson Piquet.
