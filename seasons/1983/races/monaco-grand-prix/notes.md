## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
ROSBERG'S SLICK-TIRE MASTERCLASS IN MONACO: Rain fell before the start, leaving the Monte Carlo streets damp and greasy. While all the turbo leaders (Prost, Arnoux, Cheever, Tambay) opted for wet Goodyear and Michelin tires, reigning World Champion Keke Rosberg made the daring tactical gamble to start on dry slick Goodyear tires in his naturally aspirated Williams FW08C Ford. Rosberg's gamble paid off instantly: he rocketed from 5th to 1st before Sainte Dévote, pulling out a staggering 38-second lead over the gingerly tip-toeing turbo cars within the first 10 laps as the track rapidly dried, cruising to a commanding 18.4-second victory over Nelson Piquet.
