## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
INバUGURAL STANDALONE EUROPEAN GP & PIQUET WINS: Brands Hatch hosted the first standalone 'European Grand Prix'. Elio de Angelis took pole in the Lotus-Renault turbo. Riccardo Patrese led early until colliding with de Angelis. Nelson Piquet in the Brabham BT52 assumed the lead, drove with clinical precision, and won by 6.57 seconds over Alain Prost (Renault) and Nigel Mansell (Lotus). With back-to-back victories, Piquet slashed Prost's championship lead to just two points (51 to 49) heading into the finale!
