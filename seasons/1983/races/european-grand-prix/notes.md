## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
INAUGURAL EUROPEAN GP & PIQUET'S MASTERCLASS: Organized at Brands Hatch to replace the cancelled New York GP, this was the inaugural standalone European Grand Prix in World Championship history. Elio de Angelis scored Lotus-Renault's first turbo pole position. In the race, Nelson Piquet delivered a tactical masterclass in the Brabham BT52B, managing tire wear and fuel stops to win by 6.5 seconds over Alain Prost, with Nigel Mansell 3rd for Lotus. Arnoux finished 9th with tire troubles. The victory moved Piquet within two points of Prost (51 to 49) heading into the Kyalami finale.
