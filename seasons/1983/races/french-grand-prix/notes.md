## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
PROST'S HOME DOMINATION & RE40 MAIDEN WIN: Alain Prost and the Renault factory team introduced the brand-new carbon-fibre Renault RE40 chassis on home soil at Paul Ricard. Prost delivered an untouchable grand slam: capturing pole position, setting fastest lap, and leading 51 of the 54 laps to win by 29.7 seconds over Nelson Piquet (Brabham-BMW) and Eddie Cheever (Renault), igniting Prost's championship campaign.
