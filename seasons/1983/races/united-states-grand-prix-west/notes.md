## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
WATSON'S RECORD MIRACLE WIN FROM 22ND ON THE GRID: The final Formula One Grand Prix at Long Beach produced one of the most astonishing races in motorsport history. The McLaren MP4/1C cars of John Watson and Niki Lauda suffered severe tire warming problems in qualifying, lining up in an abysmal 22nd and 23rd on the grid. On race day, running harder Michelin compounds, both McLarens demonstrated unbelievable race pace, scything through the field in tandem. Up front, Patrick Tambay's Ferrari was taken out in a collision with Keke Rosberg. Watson and Lauda surged through to 1st and 2nd by lap 45, cruising home to take a sensational McLaren 1-2. John Watson's victory from 22nd on the grid set the all-time Formula One record for the lowest starting position to ever win a World Championship Grand Prix.
