## Key Race Incidents & Tactical Scenarios
THE GREATEST COMEBACK IN F1 HISTORY (WATSON FROM 22ND): Ferrari locked out the front row (Tambay pole, Arnoux 2nd). The McLarens struggled severely on hard qualifying tires, leaving John Watson 22nd and Niki Lauda 23rd on the grid! In the race, on softer Michelin race compounds, Watson and Lauda launched the most astonishing charge in Grand Prix history. Watson carved through the pack at over a second per lap, passing Jacques Laffite for the lead on lap 45 to take victory by 27.99 seconds over teammate Niki Lauda! Watson's victory from **22nd on the grid stands as the all-time Formula One record for the lowest starting grid position to win a Grand Prix**.

## Historical Significance & Legacy
John Watson established the unbreakable all-time Formula One record of winning from 22nd on the grid.
