# Circuit de Monaco — History & Statistics

The **Circuit de Monaco** is the legendary street circuit winding through the harbor and streets of the Principality of Monaco (Monte Carlo and La Condamine). First held in 1929 and forming part of the inaugural 1950 World Championship calendar, Monaco is universally recognized as the most glamorous, demanding, and prestigious race in motorsport, forming the "Triple Crown of Motorsport" alongside the 24 Hours of Le Mans and the Indianapolis 500.

---

## Circuit Specifications

- **Location**: Monte Carlo & La Condamine, Monaco (43°44′05″N, 07°25′14″E)
- **Current Track Length**: 3.337 km (2.074 mi) — The shortest circuit on the Formula One calendar.
- **Turns**: 19 (Sainte Dévote, Beau Rivage, Massenet, Casino Square, Mirabeau Haute, The Fairmont Hairpin — slowest corner in F1 at ~48 km/h, Mirabeau Bas, Portier, The Tunnel, Nouvelle Chicane, Tabac, Louis Chiron, Swimming Pool Section, La Rascasse, Anthony Noghès).
- **Race Distance**: 78 laps (260.286 km / 161.734 mi) — The only Grand Prix that does not adhere to the FIA mandated minimum 305 km race distance.
- **Official Race Lap Record**: 
  - **1:12.909** — Lewis Hamilton, Mercedes F1 W12 EQ Performance (2021 Monaco Grand Prix, Lap 69).
  - *Source*: FIA Official Classification [2021 Monaco GP](https://www.formula1.com/en/results.html/2021/races/1067/monaco/fastest-laps.html)
- **All-Time Outright Qualifying Record**: 
  - **1:10.166** — Lewis Hamilton, Mercedes F1 W10 EQ Power+ (2019 Monaco Grand Prix, Q3 Pole Lap).
  - *Source*: FIA Official Qualifying Classification [2019 Monaco GP](https://www.formula1.com/en/results.html/2019/races/1004/monaco/qualifying.html)

## Most Successful Drivers at Monaco
1. **Ayrton Senna** ("The Master of Monaco") — **6 wins** (1987, 1989, 1990, 1991, 1992, 1993; including 5 consecutive wins).
2. **Graham Hill** ("Mr. Monaco") — **5 wins** (1963, 1964, 1965, 1968, 1969).
3. **Michael Schumacher** — **5 wins** (1994, 1995, 1997, 1999, 2001).
4. Alain Prost — 4 wins (1984, 1985, 1986, 1988).
5. Lewis Hamilton — 3 wins (2008, 2016, 2019).
6. Stirling Moss — 3 wins (1956, 1960, 1961).
7. Jackie Stewart — 3 wins (1966, 1971, 1973).
8. Nico Rosberg — 3 wins (2013, 2014, 2015).
