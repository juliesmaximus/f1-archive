# Circuit Paul Ricard - Historic Circuit Dossier

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Le Castellet, Var, Provence-Alpes-Cote d'Azur, France
- **Elevation:** 410 m (1,345 ft)
- **First World Championship Grand Prix:** 1971 (Won by Jackie Stewart, Tyrrell)
- **2018 Return Grand Prix:** 2018 (Won by Lewis Hamilton, Mercedes)
- **Layout Length (2018 Grand Prix):** 5.842 km (3.630 mi) with North Chicane dividing Mistral Straight
- **Turns:** 15

---

## Distinctive Characteristics
- **Mistral Straight:** Historic 1.8 km high-speed straight, bisected by chicane for modern safety regulations.
- **Run-off Areas:** Renowned patented Blue and Red Line abrasive high-friction asphalt runoff systems designed to slow cars without gravel traps.
- **Signes Curve:** Flat-out sweeping right-hander taken at over 320 km/h.
