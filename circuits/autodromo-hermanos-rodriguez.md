# Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez — Mexico City Grand Prix

## Specifications
- Length: 4.304 km
- Turns: 17
- Race distance: 71 laps (305.354 km)
- Altitude: 2,285 m (7,497 ft) — highest circuit in World Championship history
- Air density: ~20-25% lower than sea level

## Official Lap Records
- Race lap record: 1:17.774 — Valtteri Bottas (Mercedes W12, Lap 66, 2021-11-07)
  Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/results.html/2021/races/1104/mexico/fastest-laps.html
- Qualifying lap record: 1:14.759 — Daniel Ricciardo (Red Bull RB14, Q3, 2018-10-27)
  Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/results.html/2018/races/997/mexico/qualifying.html

## Modern Era Notes (2015–present)
- Redesigned by Hermann Tilke. Track runs through Foro Sol stadium.
- 2017 & 2018: Lewis Hamilton clinched 4th and 5th titles here.
- 2021: Max Verstappen won from 3rd on the grid.
- 2023: Verstappen 5th Mexico win.
- 2024: Carlos Sainz pole and win for Ferrari.
