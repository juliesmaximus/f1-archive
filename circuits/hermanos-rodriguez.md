# Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Magdalena Mixhuca Sports City, Mexico City, Mexico
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke (redevelopment)
- **Altitude:** 2,240 m (7,350 ft) above sea level (highest in F1)
- **Inaugural Modern Grand Prix:** November 1, 2015
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 71 laps / 305.354 km
- **Layout Length:** 4.304 km (2.674 miles)
- **Corners:** 17 (10 right, 7 left)

---

## The High-Altitude Colosseum
- **Thin Air Dynamics:** At 2,240m altitude, air density is 25% lower than at sea level. Cars run maximum Monaco downforce wings but achieve Monza top speeds (360+ km/h) due to reduced aerodynamic drag.
- **Foro Sol Baseball Stadium:** The track bisects a former baseball stadium packed with 40,000 screaming fans, creating the most electric podium stadium atmosphere in motorsport.
