# Sochi Autodrom - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Sochi, Krasnodar Krai, Russian Federation
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** October 12, 2014
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 53 laps / 309.745 km
- **Layout Length:** 5.848 km (3.634 miles)
- **Corners:** 18 (12 right, 6 left)

---

## The Olympic Park Circuit
- **Turn 3 Parabolika:** A 650-metre constant-radius left-hander sweeping around the Medals Plaza, subjecting tyres to continuous lateral loading for 8 seconds.
- **Smooth Asphalt:** Known for extremely low surface abrasive degradation, frequently allowing one-stop or zero-degradation strategies.
