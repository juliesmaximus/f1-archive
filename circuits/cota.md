# Circuit of the Americas (COTA) - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Austin, Texas, USA
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke / Tavo Hellmund
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** November 18, 2012
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 56 laps / 308.405 km
- **Layout Length:** 5.513 km (3.426 miles)
- **Corners:** 20 (11 left, 9 right) - Anti-clockwise direction
- **Elevation Change:** 41 m

---

## America's Purpose-Built F1 Cathedral
- **Turn 1 Blind Crest:** A dramatic 41-metre climb into an expansive, wide-entry hairpin inspiring audacious overtaking maneuvers.
- **Iconic Homages:** High-speed esses (Turns 3–6) inspired by Silverstone's Maggotts/Becketts; stadium section (Turns 12–15) inspired by Hockenheim; long multi-apex right-hander (Turns 16–18) echoing Istanbul Park's Turn 8.
