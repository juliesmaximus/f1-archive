# Circuit Profile: Caesars Palace Grand Prix Circuit (Las Vegas)

## Overview & Track History
The **Caesars Palace Grand Prix Circuit** was a temporary, flat street circuit constructed in the sprawling asphalt parking lot and adjacent open grounds of the iconic Caesars Palace hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip in Nevada, United States.

Conceived by Formula One commercial impresario Bernie Ecclestone and Las Vegas promoters to replace Watkins Glen as America's season-ending showcase, the circuit hosted the season finale of the FIA Formula One World Championship in **1981** and **1982**. In both years, the race decided the World Drivers' Championship in scorching, physically brutal conditions.

---

## Circuit Specifications
- **Location:** Las Vegas Strip, Paradise/Las Vegas, Nevada, United States
- **Coordinates:** 36°07'01\"N, 115°10'30\"W
- **Circuit Type:** Temporary parking lot circuit / barrier course
- **Length:** 3.650 km (2.268 miles)
- **Turns:** 14 (primarily repetitive, slow-speed left-right switchbacks)
- **Direction:** Counter-clockwise
- **Elevation Change:** 0 meters (completely flat asphalt)
- **Surface:** Abrasive car park asphalt prone to breaking up under heat and ground-effect load

---

## Physical Challenges & Driver Ordeal
Because the circuit was run counter-clockwise—unusual for the era—and featured a continuous series of high-load left-hand curves with no banking or long resting straights, drivers experienced immense lateral G-forces directly acting upon their neck muscles.

Compounded by brutal desert ambient temperatures exceeding 37°C (99°F) and stifling radiant asphalt heat over 50°C, the track caused unprecedented physical exhaustion:
- **1981 Championship Finale:** Nelson Piquet suffered violent vomiting in his cockpit from heat exhaustion and lost neck muscle control over the final 20 laps, having to lean his helmet against the cockpit coaming to finish 5th and clinch his maiden World Championship.
- **1982 Championship Finale:** Michele Alboreto scored his maiden Grand Prix victory for Tyrrell, while Keke Rosberg finished 5th to clinch the 1982 World Championship.

## Formula One Grands Prix Hosted
1. **1981 Caesars Palace Grand Prix (October 17, 1981):**
   - Winner: Alan Jones (Williams FW07C Ford)
   - Pole Position: Carlos Reutemann (Williams FW07C) - 1:17.821
   - Fastest Lap: Didier Pironi (Ferrari 126CK) - 1:20.156
   - World Champion Decided: Nelson Piquet (Brabham)
2. **1982 Caesars Palace Grand Prix (September 25, 1982):**
   - Winner: Michele Alboreto (Tyrrell 011 Ford)
   - Pole Position: Alain Prost (Renault RE30B) - 1:16.356
   - Fastest Lap: Michele Alboreto (Tyrrell 011) - 1:19.639
   - World Champion Decided: Keke Rosberg (Williams)

---

## Demise & Legacy
Following poor attendance, complaints from casino management that the race disrupted high-roller gambling operations, and intense driver criticism regarding the uninspired car park layout, Formula One abandoned the venue after 1982. The track was converted into a modified modified oval for CART IndyCar in 1983 and 1984 before the land was permanently redeveloped for the Forum Shops and hotel expansions.

Formula One did not return to Las Vegas until the 2023 Las Vegas Grand Prix, which utilized a full-scale street circuit encompassing the Las Vegas Strip.
