# Bahrain International Circuit (Sakhir) - Circuit History

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Sakhir, Kingdom of Bahrain
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** April 4, 2004
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 57 laps / 308.238 km
- **Grand Prix Layout Length:** 5.412 km (3.363 miles)
- **Corners:** 15 (9 right, 6 left)
- **Altitude:** 16 m above sea level

---

## Historical Significance
- **First F1 Race in the Middle East:** Marked Formula 1's historic expansion into the Gulf region.
- **Desert Challenge:** Built in the heart of the desert, track surfaces were treated with adhesive spray to prevent sand blowing across the asphalt.
- **Iconic Features:** Oasis complex, 8-storey Sakhir VIP Tower, Turn 1 braking zone (Michael Schumacher curve), and the high-speed Turn 9/10 downhill off-camber complex.
