# Buddh International Circuit - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** October 30, 2011
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 60 laps / 307.249 km
- **Layout Length:** 5.125 km (3.185 miles)
- **Corners:** 16 (9 right, 7 left)
- **Elevation Change:** 14 m

---

## India's Grand Prix Temple
- **Turn 3 Parabolic Elevation:** Dramatic uphill climb out of Turn 1 followed by an off-camber crest leading to the 1.06 km straight.
- **Turn 10/11 Banking:** Inspired by Istanbul Park's Turn 8, a fast multi-apex double-right sweeper with banking.
- **Unbroken Vettel Record:** Sebastian Vettel won all three Indian Grands Prix held (2011, 2012, 2013), clinching his 4th World Title there in 2013.
