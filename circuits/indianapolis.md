# Indianapolis Motor Speedway (Road Course) - Circuit Dossier

## Overview
- **Location:** Speedway, Indiana, United States
- **Length:** 4.192 km (2.605 miles)
- **Turns:** 13
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** 2000 (September 24, 2000)
- **Elevation:** 220 m above sea level
- **Characteristics:** Combines an infield technical twisty road section with the famous banked Turn 1 oval corner (taken in reverse as Turn 13) and the 1-kilometre full-throttle oval main straight.

---

## Formula 1 History at Indianapolis
In 2000, Indianapolis Motor Speedway owner Tony George partnered with Bernie Ecclestone to construct a bespoke infield road course inside the legendary 'Brickyard' oval:
- **Attendance Record:** The inaugural 2000 race drew an estimated 220,000 spectators, establishing an all-time single-day attendance record for modern Formula 1.
- **High-Speed Banking:** Cornering banked Turn 13 at over 200 mph placed unprecedented lateral and vertical tyre loads on cars, famously precipitating the 2005 Michelin tyre crisis.
