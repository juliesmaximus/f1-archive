# Istanbul Park - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Tuzla, Istanbul, Turkey
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** August 21, 2005
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 58 laps / 309.396 km
- **Grand Prix Layout Length:** 5.338 km (3.317 miles)
- **Corners:** 14 (8 left, 6 right) - Anti-clockwise direction
- **Elevation Change:** 45.9 m

---

## The Masterpiece of Modern Circuit Design
Istanbul Park is widely acclaimed as Hermann Tilke's greatest creation, featuring extreme elevation shifts and high-speed directional changes:
- **Turn 8 (The Quadruple Apex):** A fearsome, 640-metre left-hand sweeping corner taken at 260 km/h (160 mph), subjecting drivers to 5.0 lateral Gs for nearly 8 continuous seconds.
- **Anti-Clockwise Orientation:** Demanded extreme neck endurance from drivers unaccustomed to reverse centrifugal loads.
