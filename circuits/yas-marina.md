# Yas Marina Circuit - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Yas Island, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** November 1, 2009
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 55 laps / 305.355 km
- **Grand Prix Layout Length:** 5.554 km (inaugural layout)
- **Corners:** 21 (original layout) - Anti-clockwise direction

---

## The Twilight Spectacle
- **Day-into-Night:** Race starts under natural setting sun and finishes under thousands of high-intensity floodlights.
- **Subterranean Pit Exit:** Unique pitlane exit passing through a tunnel beneath Turn 1.
- **Yas Viceroy Hotel:** Runs directly through the five-star hotel illuminated by an animated LED canopy.
