# Circuit Profile: Autódromo do Estoril

## Overview & Track History
The **Autódromo do Estoril** (officially *Autódromo Fernanda Pires da Silva*), located near the resort town of Cascais in the Lisbon District of Portugal, was a premier European venue on the FIA Formula One World Championship calendar for 13 consecutive seasons from **1984 to 1996**.

Set against the rolling hills of the Estoril coast, the circuit featured a challenging combination of a long 985-metre straight exposed to fierce Atlantic crosswinds, technical medium-speed complexes, and the daunting high-speed Curva Parabólica that demanded exceptional chassis balance and commitment.

---

## Circuit Specifications
- **Location:** Alcabideche, Cascais, Portugal
- **Coordinates:** 38°45'03\"N, 9°23'39\"W
- **Circuit Length:** 4.350 km (1984–1993) / 4.360 km (with chicane, 1994–1996)
- **Turns:** 13 (original layout) / 14 (with chicane)
- **Direction:** Clockwise
- **Key Turns:**
  - *Curva 1 & 2:* Fast right-hand sweepers off the start/finish straight.
  - *Curva do Tanque:* Tricky uphill off-camber hairpin.
  - *Parabólica Ayrton Senna:* Huge 180-degree high-G carousel onto the 1 km pit straight.

## Monumental Formula One Moments
1. **1984 (The 0.5-Point Title Decider):** Niki Lauda charged from 11th on the grid to finish 2nd behind Alain Prost, securing his **third World Drivers' Championship by 0.5 points** in the closest championship outcome in Grand Prix history.
2. **1985 (Ayrton Senna's Maiden Win):** In biblical, torrential rain, 25-year-old Ayrton Senna produced a performance of pure genius in the Lotus 97T Renault. Senna took his **maiden Formula One victory**, lapping the entire field up to second place and finishing 1 minute 2.97 seconds ahead of Michele Alboreto.
3. **1987 (Prost Breaks Stewart's Record):** Alain Prost took victory in the McLaren MP4/3, claiming his **28th career Grand Prix victory** to surpass Sir Jackie Stewart's 14-year-old all-time Formula One win record.
4. **1989 (Mansell Black-Flag Collision):** Nigel Mansell reversed in the pit lane in his Ferrari, was black-flagged, and three laps later collided with title-contender Ayrton Senna at Curva 1, putting Senna out of the race.
5. **1996 (Villeneuve's Outside Pass):** Rookie Jacques Villeneuve pulled off an iconic pass around the outside of reigning champion Michael Schumacher at the flat-out Parabolica curve on lap 16 to win for Williams.

## Grand Prix Statistics & Records
- **Most Wins (Driver):** Alain Prost (3 victories: 1984, 1987, 1988); Nigel Mansell (3 victories: 1986, 1990, 1992).
- **Most Wins (Constructor):** Williams (6 victories: 1986, 1991, 1992, 1994, 1995, 1996), McLaren (3 victories).
- **Official F1 Race Lap Record:** 1:13.041 (Damon Hill, Williams FW15C, 1993).
