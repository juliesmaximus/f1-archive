# Marina Bay Street Circuit - Circuit History & Technical Guide

## Circuit Overview
- **Location:** Marina Bay, Singapore
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke / KBR
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** September 28, 2008 (First F1 Night Race)
- **Grand Prix Distance:** 61 laps / 308.828 km
- **Layout Length:** 5.067 km (3.148 miles)
- **Corners:** 23 (inaugural layout) - Anti-clockwise direction

---

## The Jewel of the Night
- **Night Lighting System:** Illuminated by over 1,600 bespoke projectors emitting 3,000 lux (four times brighter than a football stadium).
- **Physical Demands:** Singapore immediately became the most physically grueling race on the calendar due to 80% humidity, 30°C night heat, bumpy street surfaces, and race durations near the 2-hour limit.
