# Sepang International Circuit - Circuit Dossier

## Overview
- **Location:** Sepang, Selangor, Malaysia
- **Architect:** Hermann Tilke
- **Inaugural Grand Prix:** 1999 (October 17, 1999)
- **Length:** 5.543 km (3.444 miles)
- **Turns:** 15 (10 right, 5 left)
- **Elevation:** 18 m above sea level
- **Climate / Environment:** Extreme tropical heat (ambient 35°C, track 50°C) and oppressive humidity with frequent afternoon monsoons.

---

## Architectural Revolution
Sepang was the first modern Formula 1 circuit designed by architect Hermann Tilke. It redefined Grand Prix venue architecture with:
1. **Sweeping High-Speed Flow:** Two massive 1-kilometre straights linked by the iconic slow hairpin (Turn 15) and Turn 1 complex.
2. **Expansive Run-Off Areas:** Wide paved asphalt and gravel run-off areas that set the global FIA standard for modern safety.
3. **Double-Sided Grandstand Architecture:** Hibiscus flower-inspired grandstand roofs offering panoramic visibility across the entire circuit.
