## 5. Streak & Single-Season Records

- **Most Consecutive Grand Prix Wins**:
  - **10 consecutive wins** — Max Verstappen (2023 Miami Grand Prix to 2023 Italian Grand Prix).
  - *Previous record*: Sebastian Vettel (9 consecutive wins, 2013 Belgian GP to 2013 Brazilian GP).
  - *Source*: FIA Official 2023 Classifications, [Formula1.com Record Article](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.verstappen-charges-to-record-breaking-10th-consecutive-f1-win.html)
- **Most Wins in a Single Season**:
  - **19 wins** — Max Verstappen (2023 season, 22 races total, 86.36% win percentage).
  - *Previous record*: Max Verstappen (15 wins in 2022); Michael Schumacher (13 in 2004); Sebastian Vettel (13 in 2013).
  - *Source*: FIA 2023 Championship Classification.
- **Highest Win Percentage in a Single Season**:
  - **86.36%** — Max Verstappen (19 wins from 22 races in 2023).
  - *Previous record*: Alberto Ascari (75.00%, 6 wins from 8 races in 1952).
- **Most Consecutive Podiums**:
  - **19 consecutive podiums** — Michael Schumacher (2001 United States GP to 2002 Japanese GP; 100% podium rate in 2002 season).
- **Most Pole Positions in a Single Season**:
  - **15 poles** — Sebastian Vettel (2011 season, 19 races total, Red Bull RB7).
- **Youngest Driver to Start a Grand Prix**:
  - **Max Verstappen** — 17 years, 166 days (2015 Australian GP, Scuderia Toro Rosso STR10).
- **Youngest Grand Prix Winner**:
  - **Max Verstappen** — 18 years, 228 days (2016 Spanish GP, Red Bull Racing RB12).
- **Oldest Grand Prix Winner**:
  - **Luigi Fagioli** — 53 years, 22 days (1951 French GP, Alfa Romeo 159).
- **Oldest World Drivers' Champion**:
  - **Juan Manuel Fangio** — 46 years, 41 days (1957 season).
- **Most Grand Prix Starts**:
  - **Fernando Alonso** — **405+ starts** (2001 Australian GP to 2026 season).
