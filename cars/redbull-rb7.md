# Red Bull RB7 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Red Bull Racing
- **Chief Technical Officer:** Adrian Newey
- **Chief Designer:** Rob Marshall
- **Engine:** Renault RS27-2011, 2,400 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 18,000 rpm + 80 bhp KERS
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Red Bull 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 640 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero

---

## Absolute Dominance: 18 Poles from 19 Races
The RB7 stands as one of the most statistically dominant racing machines in Grand Prix history:
- **18 Pole Positions:** 15 by Sebastian Vettel, 3 by Mark Webber (missing pole only at Korea).
- **Cold-Blowing & Hot-Blowing Diffuser:** Maximized exhaust gas energization through the floor even when the throttle was closed.
- **Championship Record:** 12 Wins, 27 Podiums, 650 Constructors' Championship points.
