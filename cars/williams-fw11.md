# Historic Car Profile: Williams FW11 & FW11B (1986–1987)

## Overview & Turbo Era Supremacy
The **Williams FW11** and its evolved successor, the **FW11B**, designed by **Patrick Head** and aerodynamicist **Frank Dernie**, were the definitive turbocharged powerhouses of Formula One's mid-1980s golden age. Powered by Honda's peerless 1.5-litre twin-turbocharged V6 engine, the FW11 series delivered **back-to-back World Constructors' Championships** for Williams Grand Prix Engineering in **1986** and **1987**, as well as the **1987 World Drivers' Championship** for Nelson Piquet.

Across two seasons and 32 starts, the FW11 and FW11B captured **18 Grand Prix victories, 19 pole positions, and 33 podiums**, dominating Grand Prix racing through raw horsepower, exceptional chassis rigidity, and cutting-edge active suspension development.

---

## Technical Innovation & Design Architecture

### 1. The Honda RA166E & RA167E Turbo V6
- **Configuration:** 1,494 cc (1.5 L), 80-degree V6, twin-turbocharged, 24 valves, dual overhead camshafts.
- **Power Output (1986):** Over 1,000 bhp @ 12,000 RPM in race trim; ~1,200–1,300 bhp in qualifying trim running up to 4.5 bar boost.
- **Power Output (1987):** When FISA introduced a mandatory 4.0-bar pop-off valve to restrict power, Honda engineered the RA167E with variable boost mapping and specialized combustion chambers, maintaining over 1,000 bhp and unmatched fuel efficiency under the 195-litre fuel capacity limit.

### 2. Reactive Active Suspension (1987)
- While Team Lotus introduced a fully computer-controlled active suspension system, Frank Dernie and Patrick Head developed a simpler, lighter, and highly effective **hydropneumatic reactive suspension** for the FW11B.
- Nelson Piquet adopted the system midway through 1987, debuting it to a dominant victory at the Italian Grand Prix at Monza. The system kept the car at an aerodynamically optimal ride height under all speeds and braking loads, giving Piquet a decisive handling advantage over traditional passively suspended cars.

## Technical Specifications (1987 FW11B)
- **Chassis:** Moulded carbon-fibre composite monocoque with Kevlar reinforcement
- **Engine:** Honda RA167E, 1.5 L, 80° V6 twin-turbocharged
- **Turbochargers:** Twin IHI turbochargers with twin air-to-air intercoolers
- **Transmission:** Williams/Hewland 6-speed longitudinal manual
- **Brakes:** SEP Carbone Industrie carbon-on-carbon discs and pads
- **Suspension:** Double wishbones with pushrod-operated hydropneumatic active actuators (Piquet) / conventional coil springs (Mansell)
- **Weight:** 540 kg (minimum weight limit)
- **Tires:** Goodyear radial tires

## Championship Summary
- **1986 (FW11):** 9 wins in 16 races (Mansell 5, Piquet 4). World Constructors' Champions (141 points). Piquet and Mansell pushed each other to the brink, taking the Drivers' title fight to Adelaide where Alain Prost capitalized on Mansell's tire blowout.
- **1987 (FW11B):** 9 wins in 16 races (Mansell 6, Piquet 3). World Constructors' Champions (137 points). World Drivers' Champion: Nelson Piquet (73 net points).
