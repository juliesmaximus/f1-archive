# Ferrari F399 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Rory Byrne (Chief Designer), Nikolas Tombazis (Aerodynamics)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 048, 2,997 cc (3.0 L) 80° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~790 bhp @ 16,300 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone

---

## Historical Significance: Ending the Constructors' Drought
The Ferrari F399 holds an immortal place in Scuderia Ferrari history:
- **Ending 16 Years of Hurt:** Won the 1999 FIA Formula 1 World Constructors' Championship with 128 points, Ferrari's first constructors' title since 1983.
- **Resilience Under Adversity:** After team leader Michael Schumacher broke his leg at Silverstone, Eddie Irvine stepped up to score 4 victories and take the Drivers' Championship battle to the final lap at Suzuka.
- **Sepang 1-2 Masterclass:** Dominated the inaugural Malaysian Grand Prix upon Michael Schumacher's return.
