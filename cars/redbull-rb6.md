# Red Bull RB6 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Red Bull Racing
- **Chief Technical Officer:** Adrian Newey
- **Chief Designer:** Rob Marshall
- **Head of Aerodynamics:** Peter Prodromou
- **Engine:** Renault RS27-2010, 2,400 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 18,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Red Bull 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 620 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone Potenza Slicks

---

## Aerodynamic Pinnacle: The Blown Diffuser
Widely considered Adrian Newey's aerodynamic masterpiece of the late V8 era:
- **Exhaust-Blown Diffuser:** Exhaust outlets routed directly across the floor into the side channels of the diffuser, energizing rear downforce even during off-throttle cornering.
- **Pull-Rod Rear Suspension:** Extremely compact packaging allowing maximum airflow over the diffuser.
- **Championship Record:** 9 Wins (5 by Vettel, 4 by Webber), 15 Pole Positions (from 19 races), delivering Red Bull their historic maiden World Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
