# Renault R26 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mild Seven Renault F1 Team
- **Designers:** Bob Bell (Technical Director), Dino Toso (Chief Aerodynamicist)
- **Engine:** Renault RS26, 2,398 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~775 bhp @ 20,500 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Renault 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 605 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Michelin

---

## Technical Innovation & World Championship Triumph
The definitive machine of the inaugural 2.4L V8 era:
- **Flawless Mechanical Reliability:** Alonso finished in the top two in 14 of the 18 races.
- **Aerodynamic Efficiency:** Advanced rear diffuser and sculpted engine cover optimized flow to the rear wing.
- **Double-Double Glory:** Delivered Fernando Alonso his second consecutive World Drivers' Championship and Renault their second consecutive World Constructors' Championship.
