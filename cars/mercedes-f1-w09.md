# Mercedes-AMG F1 W09 EQ Power+ - Technical Dossier

## Technical Specifications
- **Category:** Formula 1 (2018 FIA Regulations)
- **Engine / Power Unit:** Mercedes-AMG M09 EQ Power+ (1.6-litre, 90° V6 turbocharged hybrid)
- **Energy Recovery System (ERS):** Integrated MGU-K and MGU-H capable of 120 kW (161 bhp) deployment
- **Transmission:** Mercedes 8-speed forward + 1 reverse sequential semi-automatic
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Safety:** First car engineered around the mandatory Grade 5 Titanium Halo cockpit protection system
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero slicks & Cinturato rain tyres

---

## Competition Record (2018 Season)
- **Drivers:** Lewis Hamilton (No. 44), Valtteri Bottas (No. 77)
- **Races:** 21
- **Wins:** 11 (Hamilton 11)
- **Pole Positions:** 13 (Hamilton 11, Bottas 2)
- **Fastest Laps:** 10 (Bottas 7, Hamilton 3)
- **World Constructors' Championship:** 1st Place (655 points) - 5th consecutive title
- **World Drivers' Championship:** Lewis Hamilton 1st Place (408 points) - 5th World Drivers' Championship
