# Williams FW19 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Rothmans Williams Renault
- **Designers:** Adrian Newey (Chief Designer), Patrick Head (Technical Director)
- **Engine:** Renault RS9 / RS9B, 2,998 cc (3.0 L) 71° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~760 bhp @ 15,200 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and aramid composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Williams 6-speed transverse semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Historical Significance & Championship Record
The Williams FW19 was the final Adrian Newey-designed Williams car and the last Williams chassis to win the Formula 1 World Drivers' and Constructors' Championships:
- **Renault RS9 V10 Masterpiece:** With a wider 71° vee angle, the RS9 lowered the car's centre of gravity and produced exceptional top-end power.
- **1997 Record:** 8 Wins from 17 Grands Prix (Villeneuve 7, Frentzen 1), 11 Pole Positions (Villeneuve 10, Frentzen 1), 123 Constructors' Championship Points.
- **Historic Title:** Crowned Jacques Villeneuve the 1997 Formula 1 World Drivers' Champion, exactly 20 years after his father Gilles Villeneuve debuted in Formula 1.
