# Mercedes F1 W06 Hybrid - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team
- **Technical Director:** Paddy Lowe
- **Head of Power Unit Development:** Andy Cowell
- **Engine:** Mercedes-Benz PU106B Hybrid, 1,600 cc (1.6 L) 90° V6 Single Turbocharged
- **Power Output:** ~870 bhp (710 bhp ICE + 160 bhp MGU-K) @ 15,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Moulded carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Mercedes 8-speed forward sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 702 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero

---

## Statistical Hegemony
The F1 W06 Hybrid is one of the most statistically dominant cars in Grand Prix history:
- **16 Wins from 19 Races:** 10 by Lewis Hamilton, 6 by Nico Rosberg.
- **18 Pole Positions & 12 One-Two Finishes.**
- **World Championship Double:** Clinched both World Titles with 703 Constructors' points.
