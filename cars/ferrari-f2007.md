# Ferrari F2007 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Aldo Costa (Technical Director), Nikolas Tombazis (Chief Designer)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 056, 2,398 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750–770 bhp @ 19,000 rpm (rev-limited)
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal quick-shift sequential
- **Weight:** 605 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone Potenza

---

## Championship Record & Technical Mastery
- **Title Triumph:** Carried Kimi Räikkönen to the 2007 World Drivers' Championship and won the World Constructors' Championship.
- **Quick-Shift Gearbox:** Featured Ferrari's innovative seamless-shift gearbox eliminating torque interrupt between gear shifts.
- **Racing Record:** 9 Wins (6 by Räikkönen, 3 by Massa), 9 Pole Positions, and 12 Fastest Laps from 17 races.
