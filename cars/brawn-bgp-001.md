# Brawn BGP 001 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Brawn GP Formula One Team
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Team Principal), Jörg Zander (Deputy Technical Director), Loïc Bigois (Head of Aerodynamics)
- **Engine:** Mercedes-Benz FO 108W, 2,398 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 18,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 605 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone Potenza Slicks

---

## The Masterpiece of Brackley & Double Diffuser
Born from £100M+ Honda R&D before Honda's withdrawal, the BGP 001:
- **Double Diffuser:** Channeled airflow through an opening in the stepped floor into a second, upper diffuser deck, creating colossal rear downforce without excess drag.
- **Perfect Balance:** Despite rushing a late engine installation of the Mercedes V8 into a chassis designed for Honda, the car exhibited extraordinary low-speed mechanical grip and tyre preservation.
- **100% Championship Record:** The only chassis in Formula 1 history to win both the World Drivers' and Constructors' Championships in the team's only season.
