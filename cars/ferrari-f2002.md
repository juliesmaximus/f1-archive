# Ferrari F2002 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Rory Byrne (Chief Designer), Paolo Martinelli (Engine Director)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 051/B, 2,997 cc (3.0 L) 90° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~865 bhp @ 18,300 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic with titanium casing
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone

---

## Technical Superiority & Perfection
The Ferrari F2002 is widely regarded as one of the most dominant racing machines in motorsport history:
- **Ultra-Compact Transmission:** Rory Byrne and Ross Brawn designed a revolutionary ultra-light, cast-titanium gearbox casing that enabled extreme undercut sidepods, maximizing airflow to the rear diffuser.
- **Tipo 051 V10 Engine:** revving to over 18,000 rpm, it produced benchmark power, throttle response, and unmatched reliability.
- **Immortal Racing Record:**
  - Won 14 of the 15 races in which it competed in 2002 (93.3% win rate).
  - Delivered Michael Schumacher's 5th World Championship and Ferrari's 4th consecutive Constructors' title.
  - Achieved a 100% podium finish rate throughout its competitive lifespan.
