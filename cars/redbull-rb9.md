# Red Bull RB9 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Infiniti Red Bull Racing
- **Chief Technical Officer:** Adrian Newey
- **Chief Designer:** Rob Marshall
- **Head of Aerodynamics:** Peter Prodromou
- **Engine:** Renault RS27-2013, 2,400 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 18,000 rpm + 80 bhp KERS
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Red Bull 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 642 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero

---

## The Undefeated Machine of the Late V8 Era
The RB9 became one of the most dominant racing machines in motorsport history:
- **13 Wins from 19 Races:** All 13 by Sebastian Vettel (matching Michael Schumacher's 2004 single-season record).
- **9 Consecutive Wins:** Won every race from Belgium to Brazil.
- **Coandă Diffuser Perfection:** Mastered exhaust-gas aerodynamic sealing around the rear tyres.
- **Fourth Consecutive World Championship Double:** Red Bull Racing (596 pts) and Sebastian Vettel (397 pts).
