# Renault R25 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mild Seven Renault F1 Team
- **Designers:** Pat Symonds (Executive Director of Engineering), Bob Bell (Technical Director), Dino Toso (Chief Aerodynamicist)
- **Engine:** Renault RS25, 2,998 cc (3.0 L) 72° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~900 bhp @ 19,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Renault 6-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 605 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Michelin

---

## Technical Mastery: The Tuned Mass Damper
The Renault R25 introduced one of the most ingenious mechanical innovations in Formula 1 history:
- **Tuned Mass Damper:** A 9 kg suspended mass located inside the nosecone that vibrated in counter-phase to chassis pitch and heave, keeping the front wing ride height rock-steady over curbs and uneven surfaces.
- **Championship Triumph:** 8 Wins (7 by Fernando Alonso, 1 by Giancarlo Fisichella), 7 Pole Positions, and 191 Constructors' Championship Points, delivering Renault both World Titles.
- **Final V10 World Champion:** The R25 was the last car to win the Formula 1 World Championship powered by a naturally aspirated V10 engine before the 2.4L V8 formula arrived in 2006.
