# Ferrari F1-2000 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Rory Byrne (Chief Designer), Nikolas Tombazis (Aerodynamics)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 049, 2,997 cc (3.0 L) 90° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~805 bhp @ 17,300 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone

---

## Historical Significance: Ending 21 Years of Drought
The Ferrari F1-2000 is one of the most revered cars in Ferrari history:
- **Title Breakthrough:** Delivered Scuderia Ferrari's first World Drivers' Championship in 21 years (since Jody Scheckter in 1979) and their second consecutive Constructors' title.
- **Tipo 049 V10 Engine:** A newly designed 90° vee angle (wider than the 80° Tipo 048) lowered the car's center of gravity and permitted an aerodynamically cleaner, lower rear engine cover.
- **2000 Racing Record:** 10 Wins from 17 Grands Prix (Schumacher 9, Barrichello 1), 10 Pole Positions, 170 Constructors' Championship Points.
