# Benetton B194 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mild Seven Benetton Ford
- **Designers:** Rory Byrne (Chief Designer), Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Willem Toet (Aerodynamics)
- **Engine:** Ford Zetec-R, 3,498 cc (3.5 L) 75° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~740 bhp @ 14,500 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Benetton 6-speed longitudinal transverse semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 515 kg
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Design Philosophy & Championship Record
The Benetton B194 was the car that propelled Michael Schumacher to his first Formula 1 World Championship:
- **Aerodynamic Agility:** Designed by Rory Byrne with a distinctive high-nose cone and sculpted sidepods, the B194 possessed sharp turn-in response ideally suited to Michael Schumacher's aggressive driving style.
- **Ford Zetec-R V8 Engine:** Although giving away horsepower to Renault's V10 and Ferrari's V12, the Ford V8 was lightweight and fuel-efficient, enabling Ross Brawn to execute flexible, aggressive refuelling pit strategies.
- **1994 Championship Record:** 8 Wins (all by Schumacher), 6 Pole Positions, 92 Drivers' Championship Points.
