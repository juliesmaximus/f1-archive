# Mercedes F1 W07 Hybrid - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team
- **Technical Director:** Paddy Lowe
- **Head of Power Unit Development:** Andy Cowell
- **Engine:** Mercedes-Benz PU106C Hybrid, 1,600 cc (1.6 L) 90° V6 Single Turbocharged
- **Power Output:** ~900–950 bhp @ 15,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Moulded carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Mercedes 8-speed forward sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 702 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero

## Statistical Peak of the Hybrid Era
Statistically one of the greatest Formula 1 racing cars of all time:
- **19 Wins from 21 Races:** 10 by Lewis Hamilton, 9 by Nico Rosberg (90.48% win rate).
- **20 Pole Positions from 21 Races:** (95.24% pole rate).
- **765 Constructors' Championship Points:** Delivering Mercedes their third consecutive World Championship double.
