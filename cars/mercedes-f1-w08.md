# Mercedes F1 W08 EQ Power+ - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mercedes AMG Petronas Motorsport
- **Technical Director:** James Allison
- **Head of Power Unit Development:** Andy Cowell
- **Engine:** Mercedes-AMG M08 EQ Power+, 1,600 cc (1.6 L) 90° V6 Turbocharged Hybrid
- **Power Output:** ~950–980 bhp @ 15,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Moulded carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Mercedes 8-speed forward sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 728 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero (wider 2017 specification)

---

## The 'Diva' That Won Both World Titles
Dubbed a 'diva' by Toto Wolff due to its narrow operating temperature window:
- **Longest Wheelbase on the Grid:** 3,760 mm wheelbase optimized aerodynamic downwash efficiency.
- **Title Triumph:** 12 Wins (9 by Hamilton, 3 by Bottas), 15 Pole Positions, and 668 Constructors' points.
