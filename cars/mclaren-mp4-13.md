# McLaren MP4/13 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** West McLaren Mercedes
- **Designers:** Adrian Newey (Technical Director), Neil Oatley (Chief Designer), Henri Durand (Aerodynamics)
- **Engine:** Mercedes-Benz / Ilmor FO110G, 2,998 cc (3.0 L) 72° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~780 bhp @ 17,200 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** McLaren 6-speed longitudinal semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone

---

## Adrian Newey's Masterpiece for the Narrow-Track Era
The McLaren MP4/13 was Adrian Newey's first design for McLaren and the definitive car of Formula 1's 1998 technical revolution:
- **Torsional Stiffness & Low C.o.G.:** Newey lowered the nosecone and packaged the ultra-lightweight Ilmor-Mercedes V10 low in the chassis to optimize weight distribution under the new 1.8-metre narrow track rules.
- **Bridgestone Tyre Integration:** McLaren made the crucial pre-season switch from Goodyear to Bridgestone tyres, unlocking superior sidewall stiffness and durability under the new grooved tyre formula.
- **1998 Championship Record:** 9 Wins from 16 Grands Prix (Häkkinen 8, Coulthard 1), 12 Pole Positions, 156 Constructors' Championship Points.
- **Double World Championship:** Crowned Mika Häkkinen World Drivers' Champion and gave McLaren their first Constructors' title since 1991.
