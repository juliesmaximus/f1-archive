# Ferrari F2003-GA - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Rory Byrne (Chief Designer)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 052, 2,997 cc (3.0 L) 90° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~845–910 bhp @ 18,300–19,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone
- **Name Dedication:** 'GA' dedicated to Gianni Agnelli, head of FIAT, who passed away in January 2003.

---

## Technical Innovations & Championship Triumph
Introduced at Round 5 in Spain, the F2003-GA featured:
- **Longer Wheelbase:** Extended wheelbase to improve high-speed stability and aerodynamic balance.
- **Periscopic Sidepod Chimneys:** Enhanced cooling airflow around the rear wing and diffuser.
- **Historic Milestone:** Carried Michael Schumacher to his record-breaking 6th World Drivers' Championship and Ferrari to their 5th consecutive World Constructors' Championship.
