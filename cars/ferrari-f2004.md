# Ferrari F2004 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Rory Byrne (Chief Designer), Aldo Costa (Head of Design)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 053, 2,997 cc (3.0 L) 90° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~920–940 bhp @ 19,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 605 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone

---

## Technical Mastery & Absolute Dominance
Widely considered one of the fastest and most aerodynamically complete racing cars in motorsport history:
- **15 Wins from 18 Races:** 13 by Michael Schumacher, 2 by Rubens Barrichello.
- **12 Pole Positions & 14 Fastest Laps.**
- **Unbroken Track Records:** The F2004 held outright lap records at Melbourne, Montreal, Monza, and Shanghai for over a decade.
- **Engine Longevity:** Built for the new one-engine-per-weekend rule, the Tipo 053 achieved flawless reliability while producing over 920 horsepower at 19,000 rpm.
