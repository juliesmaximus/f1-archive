# Historic Car Profile: Lotus 97T (1985)

## Overview & The Senna Breakthrough
The **Lotus 97T** is an iconic racing car in Grand Prix history, forever remembered as the machine in which **Ayrton Senna** scored his breathtaking maiden Formula One victory and established his legendary status as the master of qualifying speed and wet-weather control.

Designed by French technical director **Gérard Ducarouge** for the 1985 FIA Formula One World Championship, the 97T was powered by Renault's potent 1.5-litre turbocharged V6 engine and adorned in the classic black-and-gold John Player Special livery. In the hands of Ayrton Senna and Elio de Angelis, the 97T claimed **3 Grand Prix victories and 8 pole positions** (7 by Senna), taking Team Lotus to 3rd place in the World Constructors' Championship.

---

## Technical Innovation & Design Architecture

### 1. Advanced Aerodynamics & Winglets
- Gérard Ducarouge introduced sculpted carbon-composite sidepods with prominent aerodynamic bargeboards behind the front wheels to condition airflow around the cockpit and into the sidepod radiators.
- The 97T was among the first Grand Prix cars to feature auxiliary rear aerodynamic "winglets" mounted ahead of the rear wheels on the sidepod tops, generating localized rear downforce without inducing heavy induced drag on long straights.

### 2. The Renault Gordini EF15 Turbo V6
- **Configuration:** 1,492 cc (1.5 L), 90-degree V6, twin-turbocharged.
- **Turbochargers:** Twin Garrett AiResearch turbochargers with twin air-to-air intercoolers.
- **Power Output:** ~800–850 bhp @ 11,500 RPM (Race trim); upwards of **1,000+ bhp** in unrestricted qualifying trim running boost pressures above 4.0 bar.
- Coupled to a Lotus-Hewland 5-speed manual gearbox and riding on Goodyear radial tires.

## Historic Racing Milestones

### 1. The Estoril Masterpiece (April 21, 1985)
- In torrential, biblical rain at the Portuguese Grand Prix at Estoril, 25-year-old Ayrton Senna took his first career pole position.
- In race conditions described as virtually undriveable, Senna put on a display of supernatural car control, sliding the 97T through standing water at full throttle to claim his **maiden Formula One World Championship victory**.
- Senna won by **1 minute 2.97 seconds**, lapping every single car in the field up to second place (Michele Alboreto) and achieving a Grand Slam (pole, win, fastest lap, led all 67 laps).

### 2. Imola Victory for De Angelis (May 5, 1985)
- Teammate Elio de Angelis finished 2nd on the road at the San Marino Grand Prix at Imola, and was awarded victory after Alain Prost's McLaren was found to be 2 kg underweight in post-race scrutineering.

### 3. Senna's Spa Triumph (September 15, 1985)
- At the rescheduled Belgian Grand Prix at Spa-Francorchamps, Senna started 2nd in damp conditions, passed Alain Prost into the first corner, and drove with surgical precision to take his second victory of the season by 28.42 seconds over Nigel Mansell.

## Statistical Summary
- **Races Competed:** 16 Grands Prix in 1985
- **Wins:** 3 (Senna: Portugal, Belgium; De Angelis: San Marino)
- **Podiums:** 9
- **Pole Positions:** 8 (Senna 7, De Angelis 1)
- **Fastest Laps:** 3 (all Ayrton Senna)
- **World Constructors' Championship:** 3rd (71 points)
