# Williams FW15C - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Canon Williams Renault
- **Designers:** Adrian Newey (Chief Designer), Patrick Head (Technical Director), Paddy Lowe (Control Systems)
- **Engine:** Renault RS5, 3,493 cc (3.5 L) 67° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~780 bhp @ 14,500 rpm
- **Transmission:** Williams 6-speed transverse semi-automatic with full automatic mode
- **Suspension:** Hydraulic reactive active suspension system
- **Electronic Systems:** Traction control, Anti-Lock Braking (ABS), fly-by-wire throttle, push-to-pass active ride height adjustment
- **Weight:** 505 kg
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Technical Pinnacle of Formula 1 Electronics
The Williams FW15C represents the undisputed zenith of electronic technology in Formula 1 history. Adrian Newey and Patrick Head integrated every conceivable electronic driver aid into an aerodynamically refined package:
1. **Active Hydraulic Suspension:** Actuators micro-adjusted ride heights thousands of times per lap, guaranteeing maximum aerodynamic efficiency of the front wing, venturi tunnels, and rear diffuser under all conditions.
2. **ABS & Power Brakes:** Anti-lock braking allowed drivers to brake at the absolute theoretical limit into tight corners without locking tyres.
3. **Automatic Gearshifting:** The gearbox could shift through all gears automatically using wheel-speed and engine-RPM algorithms.
4. **Push-to-Pass Telemetry:** By altering the active suspension rake angle on long straights, drag was reduced by raising the rear ride height, functioning as an early precursor to DRS.

The FW15C was so dominant that the FIA banned all active electronic driving aids at the end of the 1993 season, ensuring the FW15C remains the most technologically complex Grand Prix car ever raced.

## 1993 Racing Record
- **10 Wins from 16 Grands Prix** (Prost 7, Hill 3).
- **15 Pole Positions from 16 Grands Prix** (Prost 13, Hill 2).
- **168 Constructors' Championship Points** (McLaren 2nd with 84 points).
- **World Drivers' & World Constructors' Champions**.
