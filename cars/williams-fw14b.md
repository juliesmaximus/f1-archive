# Williams FW14B - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Canon Williams Team
- **Designers:** Adrian Newey (Chief Aerodynamicist), Patrick Head (Technical Director), Paddy Lowe (Control Systems)
- **Engine:** Renault RS3C / RS4, 3,493 cc (3.5 L) 67° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 14,200 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and aramid composite monocoque
- **Suspension:** Hydraulic reactive active suspension system (independent electronic actuators on all four wheels)
- **Transmission:** Williams 6-speed transverse semi-automatic sequential gearbox
- **Electronics:** Traction control, automatic upshifting, active ride height control
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Technical Superiority & Active Suspension
The Williams FW14B is widely acclaimed as the technologically most sophisticated car in Formula 1 history. While other teams had experimented with active suspension, Williams and Paddy Lowe perfected the electronic-hydraulic control algorithms:
1. **Constant Aerodynamic Platform:** The system adjusted hydraulic actuators at each wheel instantaneously, holding the chassis completely flat under hard braking, cornering, and acceleration.
2. **Optimum Ground Clearance:** By maintaining a constant 20 mm underbody clearance, Adrian Newey's diffuser and front wing operated at maximum theoretical downforce at all times.
3. **Cornering Speeds:** The car generated unprecedented g-forces through fast sweepers, allowing Nigel Mansell to qualify up to 2 seconds faster than the entire field.

## 1992 Racing Record
- **10 Wins from 16 Grands Prix** (Mansell 9, Patrese 1).
- **15 Pole Positions from 16 Grands Prix** (Mansell 14, Patrese 1).
- **6 One-Two Finishes**.
- **164 Constructors' Championship Points** (Williams scored more points than McLaren and Benetton combined).
