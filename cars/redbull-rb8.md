# Red Bull RB8 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Red Bull Racing
- **Chief Technical Officer:** Adrian Newey
- **Chief Designer:** Rob Marshall
- **Head of Aerodynamics:** Peter Prodromou
- **Engine:** Renault RS27-2012, 2,400 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 18,000 rpm + 80 bhp KERS
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Red Bull 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 640 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero

---

## Technical Adaptation: The Coandă Effect Exhausts
Following the ban on blown diffusers, Adrian Newey pioneered:
- **Coandă Downwash Ramp:** Channeled high-velocity exhaust gases down sculpted sidepod ramps directly into the sealing gap between tyre squish and diffuser sidewalls.
- **Stepped Nose Slot:** Incorporated a cooling air duct into the mandatory stepped nose transition.
- **Triple-Double Glory:** Clinched Red Bull Racing's third consecutive World Drivers' and Constructors' Championships.
