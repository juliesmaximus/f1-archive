# Benetton B195 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mild Seven Benetton Renault
- **Designers:** Rory Byrne (Chief Designer), Ross Brawn (Technical Director)
- **Engine:** Renault RS7, 2,998 cc (3.0 L) 67° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 14,300 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Benetton 6-speed longitudinal semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 595 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Championship Record & Technical Evolution
The Benetton B195 was the machine that delivered Benetton Formula's sole World Constructors' Championship:
- **Renault V10 Power:** Benetton secured equal engine parity with Williams by signing a customer deal with Renault for the RS7 V10, eliminating their engine deficit.
- **Championship Record:** 11 Wins from 17 races (Schumacher 9, Herbert 2), 4 Pole Positions, 137 Constructors' Championship Points.
- **Twin Championship Triumph:** Secured both the World Drivers' and World Constructors' Championships before Michael Schumacher departed for Scuderia Ferrari in 1996.
