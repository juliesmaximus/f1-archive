# Mercedes F1 W05 Hybrid - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Mercedes AMG Petronas F1 Team
- **Technical Director:** Paddy Lowe
- **Head of Power Unit Development:** Andy Cowell (Mercedes-AMG High Performance Powertrains)
- **Engine:** Mercedes-Benz PU106A Hybrid, 1,600 cc (1.6 L) 90° V6 Single Turbocharged
- **Power Output:** ~840 bhp (680 bhp ICE + 160 bhp MGU-K) @ 15,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Moulded carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Mercedes 8-speed forward sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 691 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero

---

## The Split-Turbo Masterpiece
The Mercedes F1 W05 Hybrid introduced the most revolutionary engine design innovation of the modern era:
- **Split-Turbo Architecture:** The compressor was located at the front of the engine and the turbine at the rear, connected by a shaft running through the V of the engine with the MGU-H nestled in between. This dramatically lowered intake air temperatures, reduced turbo lag to zero, and packaged the engine with unprecedented aerodynamic tightness.
- **Historic Dominance:** 16 Wins from 19 Races, 18 Pole Positions, 11 One-Two Finishes, and 701 Constructors' Championship Points (all-time record at that date).
