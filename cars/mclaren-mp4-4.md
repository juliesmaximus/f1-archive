# McLaren MP4/4 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Honda Marlboro McLaren
- **Designer:** Gordon Murray (Technical Director), Steve Nichols (Chief Designer)
- **Engine:** Honda RA168E, 1,494 cc (1.5 L) 80° V6 twin-turbocharged (mid-mounted)
- **Boost Pressure:** 2.5 bar (mandatory FIA pop-off valve regulation)
- **Power Output:** ~650 bhp @ 12,500 rpm (race trim), ~700 bhp (qualifying trim)
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and Kevlar honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** McLaren / Weismann 6-speed manual longitudinal gearbox
- **Weight:** 540 kg (minimum regulated weight)
- **Fuel Capacity:** 150 litres (Shell fuels)
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Dominance & Historical Significance
The McLaren MP4/4 is widely regarded as one of the greatest racing cars in motorsport history. In the final season of Formula 1's first turbo era (1988), the MP4/4 achieved:
- **15 Wins out of 16 Grands Prix** (93.75% win rate, an all-time record unbroken until 2023).
- **15 Pole Positions out of 16 rounds** (Senna 13, Prost 2).
- **10 One-Two Finishes**.
- **199 Constructors' Championship Points** (McLaren scored nearly triple the points of runner-up Ferrari).
- **1,003 out of 1,031 Laps Led** during the entire 1988 season (97.3% of all laps raced).

## Design Philosophy: Ultra-Lowline Concept
Gordon Murray brought the extreme lowline packaging concept pioneered on the Brabham BT55 to McLaren. By designing a bespoke, low-profile dry-sump 80° V6 engine with Honda, Murray and Steve Nichols were able to drop the car's overall frontal area and centre of gravity dramatically. Driver seating was placed in an unprecedented reclined position (30° tilt), allowing unobstructed airflow across the rear wing and producing immense downforce with minimal drag.
