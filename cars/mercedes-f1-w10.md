# Mercedes-AMG F1 W10 EQ Power+ - Technical Dossier

## Technical Specifications
- **Category:** Formula 1 (2019 FIA Regulations)
- **Engine / Power Unit:** Mercedes-AMG M10 EQ Power+ (1.6-litre, 90° V6 turbocharged hybrid)
- **Energy Recovery System (ERS):** Integrated MGU-K and MGU-H capable of 120 kW (161 bhp) deployment
- **Transmission:** Mercedes 8-speed forward + 1 reverse sequential semi-automatic
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Aero Concept:** B-spec front wing and sidepod aerodynamic package introduced at Barcelona test 2
- **Tyres:** Pirelli P Zero slicks (thinner tread gauge) & Cinturato rain tyres

---

## Competition Record (2019 Season)
- **Drivers:** Lewis Hamilton (No. 44), Valtteri Bottas (No. 77)
- **Races:** 21
- **Wins:** 15 (Hamilton 11, Bottas 4)
- **Pole Positions:** 10 (Bottas 5, Hamilton 5)
- **Fastest Laps:** 9 (Hamilton 6, Bottas 3)
- **World Constructors' Championship:** 1st Place (739 points) - Record 6th consecutive championship double
- **World Drivers' Championship:** Lewis Hamilton 1st Place (413 points) - 6th World Drivers' Championship
