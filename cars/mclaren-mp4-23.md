# McLaren MP4-23 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Vodafone McLaren Mercedes
- **Designers:** Paddy Lowe (Engineering Director), Neil Oatley (Design Director), Pat Fry (Chief Engineer)
- **Engine:** Mercedes-Benz FO 108V, 2,398 cc (2.4 L) 90° V8 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~760 bhp @ 19,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** McLaren 7-speed longitudinal quick-shift sequential
- **Weight:** 605 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone Potenza

---

## Complex Aerodynamics & World Championship Triumph
The pinnacle of the unrestricted aerodynamic winglet era:
- **Intricate Aero Devices:** Featured multi-tier front-wing bridges, 'viking horns' on the airbox, nose cone shark fins, and sculpted sidepod bargeboards.
- **World Drivers' Championship:** Carried Lewis Hamilton to his maiden World Drivers' Championship with 5 wins, 7 poles, and 10 podium finishes.
