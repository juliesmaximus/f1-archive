# Ferrari 641 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari SpA
- **Designers:** John Barnard (Concept), Enrique Scalabroni, Steve Nichols
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 036, 3,498 cc (3.5 L) 65° V12 naturally aspirated (mid-mounted)
- **Power Output:** ~680 bhp @ 12,750 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and Kevlar honeycomb monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal semi-automatic sequential with steering wheel paddle shifters
- **Weight:** 503 kg
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Design Evolution & Legacy
The Ferrari 641 was a refined evolution of John Barnard's revolutionary 640. Developed by Steve Nichols and Enrique Scalabroni, it featured a longer wheelbase, revised cooling ducts, and improved hydraulic reliability for the 7-speed paddle-shift gearbox.
- **Aesthetic Perfection:** Renowned as one of the most beautiful Formula 1 cars ever created, a Ferrari 641 is permanently exhibited in the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA) in New York City.
- **Racing Record:** Won 6 Grands Prix in 1990 (Prost 5, Mansell 1), including Ferrari's historic 100th Grand Prix victory at Le Castellet and the famous 1-2 finish in Mexico.
