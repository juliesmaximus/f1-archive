# Williams FW18 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Rothmans Williams Renault
- **Designers:** Adrian Newey (Chief Designer), Patrick Head (Technical Director)
- **Engine:** Renault RS8 / RS8B, 2,998 cc (3.0 L) 67° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~750 bhp @ 15,000 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and aramid composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Williams 6-speed transverse semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 595 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Goodyear

---

## Dominance & Engineering Excellence
The Williams FW18 is widely regarded as Adrian Newey's most reliable and perfectly balanced Grand Prix design:
- **Aerodynamic Packaging:** Conceived to accommodate the newly mandated high cockpit safety sides without compromising clean airflow to the rear wing and engine airbox.
- **Dominant Record:** 12 Wins from 16 Grands Prix (Hill 8, Villeneuve 4), 12 Pole Positions, 175 Constructors' Championship Points (more than double runner-up Ferrari's 70 points).
- **Historic Double:** Secured both the World Drivers' Championship for Damon Hill and the Constructors' Championship for Williams-Renault.
