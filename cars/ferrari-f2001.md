# Ferrari F2001 - Technical Dossier & Racing Record

## Overview
- **Team:** Scuderia Ferrari Marlboro
- **Designers:** Ross Brawn (Technical Director), Rory Byrne (Chief Designer)
- **Engine:** Ferrari Tipo 050, 2,997 cc (3.0 L) 90° V10 naturally aspirated
- **Power Output:** ~825 bhp @ 17,300 rpm
- **Chassis:** Carbon-fibre and honeycomb composite monocoque
- **Transmission:** Ferrari 7-speed longitudinal sequential semi-automatic
- **Weight:** 600 kg (with driver)
- **Tyres:** Bridgestone

---

## Aerodynamic Innovation: The Dropped-Nose Concept
Designed by Rory Byrne for the new front-wing height regulations (raising the wing 50 mm above ground level to cut downforce), the F2001 introduced:
- **Drooping Anteater Nose:** The nose dipped sharply towards the front wing to channel air directly beneath the chassis and into the underfloor diffuser.
- **Sculpted Shark-Gill Sidepods:** Perforated heat-extraction chimneys and gills on the sidepod tops optimized cooling without aerodynamic drag penalties.
- **Championship Record:** 9 Wins from 17 Grands Prix, 10 Pole Positions, 179 Constructors' Championship Points (more than double runner-up McLaren's 102 points).
