# McLaren Formula 1 Team — History & Statistics

Founded in 1963 by New Zealander Bruce McLaren, McLaren is the second-oldest active team in Formula One after Scuderia Ferrari and the second most successful constructor in Grand Prix history. Based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, Surrey, United Kingdom, the team won the **2024 and 2025 World Constructors' Championships** and the **2025 World Drivers' Championship** with Lando Norris.

---

## Team Overview & Record (as of October 2026)

- **Official Full Name**: McLaren Formula 1 Team
- **Headquarters**: McLaren Technology Centre, Woking, Surrey, United Kingdom
- **License Nationality**: British
- **CEO**: Zak Brown
- **Team Principal**: Andrea Stella
- **World Constructors' Championships**: **10** (1974, 1984, 1985, 1988, 1989, 1990, 1991, 1998, 2024, 2025)
- **World Drivers' Championships**: **13** (Fittipaldi 1974; Hunt 1976; Lauda 1984; Prost 1985, 1986, 1989; Senna 1988, 1990, 1991; Häkkinen 1998, 1999; Hamilton 2008; Norris 2025)
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **198**
- **Total Pole Positions**: **168**
- **Total Podiums**: **532**
- **First Race**: 1966 Monaco Grand Prix (Monaco, Bruce McLaren in McLaren M2B)
- **First Win**: 1968 Belgian Grand Prix (Spa-Francorchamps, Bruce McLaren)
