# Oracle Red Bull Racing — History & Statistics

Founded in 2005 following Red Bull GmbH's purchase of Jaguar Racing, Red Bull Racing is based in Milton Keynes, Buckinghamshire, United Kingdom. Competing under an Austrian racing license, Red Bull has established itself as one of the most dominant forces in modern Formula One history, capturing 6 World Constructors' Championships (2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2022, 2023) and 8 World Drivers' Championships (Sebastian Vettel 2010–2013; Max Verstappen 2021–2024).
