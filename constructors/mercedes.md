# Mercedes-AMG Formula One Team — History & Statistics

Mercedes-Benz has competed in Formula One across two dominant eras: first in the 1950s (1954–1955) with Juan Manuel Fangio and Stirling Moss driving the legendary W196, and in the modern hybrid era (2010–present) based in Brackley and Brixworth, United Kingdom. Mercedes holds the all-time record for the most consecutive World Constructors' Championships (8 consecutive titles from 2014 to 2021). As of Round 18 of the 2026 season, Mercedes leads the World Constructors' Championship with 607 points and 12 victories.

---

## Team Overview & Record (as of October 2026)

- **Official Full Name**: Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS Formula One Team
- **Headquarters**: Brackley (Chassis) & Brixworth (High Performance Powertrains), Northamptonshire, UK
- **License Nationality**: German
- **Team Principal / CEO**: Toto Wolff
- **World Constructors' Championships**: **8** (2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021)
- **World Drivers' Championships**: **9** (Fangio 1954, 1955; Hamilton 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020; Rosberg 2016)
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **137** (9 in 1954–1955, 116 in 2014–2021, 1 in 2022, 3 in 2024, 2 in 2025, 12 in 2026 through R18)
- **Total Pole Positions**: **145**
- **Total Podiums**: **306**
- **First Race**: 1954 French Grand Prix (Reims-Gueux, 1954-07-04, 1-2 finish with Fangio and Kling)
