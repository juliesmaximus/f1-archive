# Audi Revolut F1 Team — History & Statistics

Audi Sport made its official factory entrance into the FIA Formula One World Championship for the **2026 season**, coinciding with the introduction of the new 50/50 electrical/internal combustion power unit and sustainable fuel regulations. The team is based between Neuburg an der Donau, Germany (Audi Formula Racing GmbH - Power Unit) and Hinwil, Switzerland (Sauber Motorsport AG chassis operations, acquired 100% by AUDI AG).

---

## Team Overview & Record (as of October 2026)

- **Official Full Name**: Audi Revolut F1 Team
- **Headquarters**: Hinwil, Zurich, Switzerland (Chassis) & Neuburg an der Donau, Bavaria, Germany (Audi Power Unit)
- **License Nationality**: German
- **Chief Technical & Operating Officer**: Mattia Binotto
- **Team Principal**: Jonathan Wheatley
- **World Constructors' Championships**: 0
- **First Grand Prix**: 2026 Australian Grand Prix (Albert Park, Melbourne, 2026-03-08)
- **First Points Scored**: 2026 Australian Grand Prix (Gabriel Bortoleto finished P9, scoring 2 points)
- **Total Grand Prix Starts**: 18
- **2026 Constructors' Points**: 23 points (8th in WCC through Round 18)
