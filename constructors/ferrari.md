# Scuderia Ferrari — History & Statistics

Scuderia Ferrari is the oldest, most successful, and only constructor to have competed in every single Formula One World Championship season since the inaugural 1950 championship. Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1929, the team is based in Maranello, Italy. Ferrari holds the all-time records for most World Constructors' Championships (16), most World Drivers' Championships (15), most Grand Prix victories (246), and most podium finishes (824).

- **Total Grand Prix Starts**: 1,114
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **246**
- **Total Pole Positions**: **253**
- **Total Podiums**: **824**
- **First Race**: 1950 Monaco Grand Prix (Monaco, 1950-05-21, Alberto Ascari finished 2nd)
- **First Win**: 1951 British Grand Prix (Silverstone, 1951-07-14, José Froilán González)
