## Historical Significance

Founded in 1960 by double World Champion driver Jack Brabham and brilliant Australian engineer Ron Tauranac, Motor Racing Developments (MRD) became one of the greatest and most innovative constructors in motor racing history.

- **The Driver-Constructor Feat (1966):** Jack Brabham made motorsport history by winning the 1966 World Drivers' Championship driving the Repco-powered Brabham BT19. He remains the only driver in Formula One history to win the World Championship driving a car bearing his own name.
- **Customer Car Giant:** Under Tauranac's engineering leadership, Brabham was the largest manufacturer of customer single-seater racing cars in the world during the 1960s, constructing more than 500 customer chassis for Formula One, Formula Two, Formula Three, and Indianapolis.
- **The Ecclestone & Gordon Murray Era (1972–1988):** Bought by Bernie Ecclestone in 1972 with South African designer Gordon Murray as technical chief, Brabham became the sport's technical vanguard:
  - Pioneered carbon-carbon brakes (1976).
  - Designed the revolutionary Brabham BT46B "Fan Car", which won its only race at Anderstorp in 1978 with Niki Lauda before being voluntarily withdrawn.
  - Re-introduced tactical pit stops and refuelling into modern Formula One in 1982.
  - Won the **first turbo-powered Drivers' Championship** in Formula One history in 1983 with Nelson Piquet driving the BMW turbo-powered Brabham BT52.
  - Created the radical ultra-lowline Brabham BT55 "skateboard car" in 1986.
