# Fernando Alonso - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Fernando Alonso Díaz
- **Born:** July 29, 1981 (Oviedo, Asturias, Spain)
- **World Drivers' Championships:** 2 (2005, 2006)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 370+ (active)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 32
- **Pole Positions:** 22
- **Podiums:** 106
- **Fastest Laps:** 26
- **F1 Teams:** Minardi (2001), Renault (2003–2006, 2008–2009), McLaren (2007, 2015–2018), Ferrari (2010–2014), Alpine (2021–2022), Aston Martin (2023–present)

---

## The Matador of Oviedo
Fernando Alonso emerged in 2003 under Flavio Briatore's Renault team as one of the most complete and formidable racing drivers in history:
- **Maiden Pole Position:** Malaysian GP 2003 (youngest pole-sitter at that time).
- **Maiden Grand Prix Victory:** Hungarian GP 2003 (youngest GP winner at 22 years, 26 days).
- **Aggressive Driving Style:** Famous for violently aggressive turn-in inducing controlled understeer on Michelin tyres, extracting maximum velocity.
