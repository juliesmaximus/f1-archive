# Fernando Alonso — Career Statistics & Profile

Fernando Alonso Díaz (born 29 July 1981 in Oviedo, Asturias, Spain) is a Spanish racing driver competing for Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team. A two-time World Drivers' Champion (2005, 2006 with Renault), Alonso is celebrated as one of the fiercest, most adaptable, and durable competitors in motorsport history, holding the all-time record for the most Formula One Grand Prix starts (405+).
