# Kimi Räikkönen - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Kimi-Matias Räikkönen ('The Iceman')
- **Born:** October 17, 1979 (Espoo, Finland)
- **World Drivers' Championships:** 1 (2007 with Scuderia Ferrari)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 349 (2001–2021)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 21
- **Pole Positions:** 18
- **Podiums:** 103
- **Fastest Laps:** 46 (3rd all-time in F1 history)
- **F1 Teams:** Sauber (2001), McLaren (2002–2006), Ferrari (2007–2009, 2014–2018), Lotus (2012–2013), Alfa Romeo (2019–2021)

---

## The Iceman
Famous for unshakeable composure, blistering raw speed, and laconic radio communications:
- **Fastest F1 Lap in History (2018):** Monza pole lap in the Ferrari SF71H at 263.587 km/h (163.785 mph), the fastest lap in Formula 1 history.
- **Ferrari's Last Champion:** As of 2026, Kimi Räikkönen remains Scuderia Ferrari's most recent World Drivers' Champion (2007).
