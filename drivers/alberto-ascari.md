# Alberto Ascari — Career Statistics & Profile

Alberto Ascari (13 July 1918 – 26 May 1955) was an Italian racing driver who became Formula One's first back-to-back World Drivers' Champion (1952, 1953) driving for Scuderia Ferrari. The son of legendary pre-war racer Antonio Ascari, Alberto was renowned for his precision, devastating qualifying speed from the front of the grid, and unflappable consistency. He holds the record for the highest percentage of wins in a single season (75.0% in 1952) and the joint-record for consecutive Grand Prix victories (7 consecutive wins in 1952–1953, or 9 if non-participated Indianapolis 500 races are excluded).

---

## Career Summary

- **World Drivers' Championships**: **2** (1952, 1953 — Ferrari's first World Champion)
- **First Grand Prix**: 1950 Monaco Grand Prix (Circuit de Monaco, Ferrari 125, Finished 2nd)
- **First Grand Prix Win**: 1951 German Grand Prix (Nürburgring Nordschleife, Ferrari 375)
- **Final Grand Prix Win**: 1953 Swiss Grand Prix (Circuit Bremgarten, Ferrari 500)
- **Final Grand Prix**: 1955 Monaco Grand Prix (Crashed into Monaco harbour, survived with broken nose; died 4 days later testing a Ferrari sports car at Monza)
- **Total Grand Prix Starts**: 32 (33 entries)
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **13** (40.63% win percentage)
- **Total Pole Positions**: **14** (43.75% pole percentage)
- **Total Podiums**: **17**
- **Total Career Points**: 107.64 (gross) / 140.14 (including dropped scores)

## Benchmark Historical Records
1. **Highest Win Percentage in a Single Season**: 75.0% (6 wins out of 8 championship rounds in 1952).
2. **Most Consecutive Grand Prix Wins in a Season**: 6 consecutive wins in 1952 (7 consecutive including 1953 Argentine GP, 9 excluding Indianapolis 500).
3. **Most Fastest Laps in a Single Season**: 6 fastest laps from 8 races in 1952 (tied with Kimi Räikkönen in 2005/2008 and Michael Schumacher in 2004).
4. **First Back-to-Back World Champion**: 1952 and 1953 titles for Scuderia Ferrari.
