# Maurice Trintignant — Career Statistics & Profile

Maurice Bienvenu Jean Paul Trintignant (30 October 1917 – 13 February 2005), nicknamed *Le Pétoulet* ("The Rat Dropping" — a humorous nickname earned after rat droppings caused his Bugatti's fuel system to fail after WWII), was a French racing driver who won two Monaco Grands Prix (1955, 1958) and the 1954 24 Hours of Le Mans for Ferrari. He was the first French driver to win a Formula One World Championship Grand Prix.
