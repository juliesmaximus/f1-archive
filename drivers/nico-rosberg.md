# Nico Rosberg - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Nico Erik Rosberg
- **Born:** June 27, 1985 (Wiesbaden, West Germany)
- **World Drivers' Championships:** 1 (2016 with Mercedes)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 206 (2006–2016)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 23
- **Pole Positions:** 30
- **Podiums:** 57
- **Fastest Laps:** 20
- **F1 Teams:** Williams (2006–2009), Mercedes (2010–2016)

---

## The Champion Who Walked Away at the Summit
- **Father-Son World Champions:** Joined Damon Hill as the only father-son champions in F1 history (Keke Rosberg in 1982, Nico in 2016).
- **Relentless Psychological & Physical Dedication:** Stripped 1 kg of leg muscle to save 0.03 seconds per lap in qualifying, hired a mental coach, and eliminated all distractions to defeat Lewis Hamilton in 2016.
- **Shock Retirement:** Announced his immediate retirement five days after winning the World Championship at the FIA Gala in Vienna, bowing out at the absolute pinnacle of motorsport.
