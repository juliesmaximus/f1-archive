# Driver Profile: Keke Rosberg

## Overview & Historic Legacy
Keijo Erik "Keke" Rosberg (born December 6, 1948) is a Finnish racing legend who captured the **1982 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship**, becoming the first Nordic driver to win the world title. Renowned for his flamboyant, sideways car control, blistering one-lap qualifying pace, and chain-smoking swagger, Rosberg became an icon of the 1980s turbo and ground-effect eras.

Rosberg won the 1982 World Championship with Frank Williams's team despite winning only a single Grand Prix that season, relying on raw speed, fearless racecraft, and relentless consistency in the naturally aspirated Williams FW08 against the far more powerful but fragile turbo machines of Ferrari and Renault. He is the father of 2016 Formula One World Champion Nico Rosberg, making them one of only two father-son pairs to both win the World Drivers' Championship (alongside Graham and Damon Hill).

## Formula One Career

### Struggles & The Theodore Miracle (1978–1981)
- Scored an astonishing upset victory in only his second Formula One appearance at the non-championship 1978 BRDC International Trophy at Silverstone in torrential rain, driving the unheralded Theodore TR1.
- Campaigned for ATS, Theodore, and the struggling Fittipaldi Automotive (1980–1981), scoring a podium (3rd) in Argentina in 1980 alongside Emerson Fittipaldi.

### The Williams World Championship (1982–1985)
- Signed by Frank Williams for 1982 as a late replacement following Alan Jones's retirement.
- **1982 World Championship Season:** In a chaotic, tragic, and fiercely contested 16-race season featuring 11 different winners, Rosberg took his **maiden Formula One victory** at the Swiss Grand Prix at Dijon. Across the season, he scored five additional podiums, clinching the **World Drivers' Championship** at the Caesars Palace finale in Las Vegas with 44 points.
- **The Turbo Transition & Monaco Magic (1983):** In the naturally aspirated FW08C against the turbocharged horde, Rosberg produced a wet-weather tactical masterpiece at the 1982 Monaco Grand Prix, starting on slick tires on a damp track to lap the entire field up to second place and take a sensational victory.
- **The 160 MPH Lap at Silverstone (1985):** At the 1985 British Grand Prix at Silverstone, Rosberg drove Patrick Head's 1,100 bhp Williams-Honda FW10 to set the fastest qualifying lap in Formula One history at **160.9 mph (258.9 km/h)**—with a slowly deflating right-rear tire—a record that stood for 16 years. Won the 1985 Dallas, Detroit, and Adelaide GPs.

### McLaren Finale (1986)
- Partnered Alain Prost at McLaren in 1986 in the MP4/2C before retiring from Formula One at the end of the year. Went on to win the 1991 World Sportscar Championship with Peugeot and manage Finnish champions JJ Lehto and Mika Häkkinen.
