## Historical Significance

John Surtees remains the only competitor in motorsport history to win World Championships on both two wheels and four wheels:
- **Motorcycle Grand Prix World Championships (7):** 500cc titles in 1956, 1958, 1959, and 1960; 350cc titles in 1958, 1959, and 1960, all aboard factory MV Agustas.
- **Formula 1 World Drivers' Championship (1):** 1964 with Scuderia Ferrari (driving the Ferrari 158).

Known as *"Il Grande John"* by Italian tifosi, Surtees combined technical brilliance, relentless physical stamina, and profound mechanical empathy.

## Formula 1 Career Overview

### Early Years (1960–1962)
- Made his F1 debut in 1960 for Colin Chapman's Team Lotus at Monaco, scoring a podium (2nd) in only his second race at Silverstone and pole position in his third at Porto.
- Competed for Yeoman Credit Cooper in 1961 and Bowmaker-Yeoman Lola in 1962, taking poles at Zandvoort and second places at Silverstone and the Nürburgring.

### Ferrari Era & The 1964 World Championship (1963–1966)
- Joined Scuderia Ferrari in 1963, taking his maiden Grand Prix victory at the ferocious Nürburgring Nordschleife.
- In 1964, behind the wheel of Mauro Forghieri's V8-powered Ferrari 158, Surtees conquered the German Grand Prix and Italian Grand Prix at Monza.
- In the dramatic Mexico City season finale, racing in the blue-and-white colors of the North American Racing Team (NART), Surtees finished second to capture the Drivers' Championship by a single point over Graham Hill (40 to 39 net points).
- Surtees survived a near-fatal 150 mph sports car crash at Mosport Park in September 1965, astonishing doctors by returning to win the 1966 season-opener at Spa-Francorchamps in torrential rain.
- Acrimonious fallout with Ferrari team manager Eugenio Dragoni during the 1966 24 Hours of Le Mans led Surtees to walk out of Ferrari mid-season while leading the championship.

### Honda, BRM, and Team Surtees (1967–1972)
- Joined Honda for 1967, developing the Lola-based Honda RA300 ("Hondola") and winning on its debut at Monza in one of the closest finishes in history (0.2s ahead of Jack Brabham).
- After an unsuccessful year with BRM in 1969, founded his own constructor, **Team Surtees**, competing as driver-owner until retiring from driving at the end of 1972.
- Continued managing Team Surtees in Formula 1, Formula 2, and Formula 5000 until 1978.
