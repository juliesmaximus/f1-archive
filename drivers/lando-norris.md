# Lando Norris — Career Statistics & Profile

Lando Norris (born 13 November 1999 in Bristol, England) is a British racing driver competing for the McLaren Formula 1 Team. Norris captured the **2025 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship**, ending Max Verstappen's four-year streak and securing McLaren's first Drivers' title since Lewis Hamilton in 2008.

## 2025 World Championship Campaign

Norris secured the 2025 title in a thrilling season-long duel with Max Verstappen and teammate Oscar Piastri. Winning 7 Grands Prix, Norris amassed 423 points to defeat Verstappen by 2 points (421) in the finale at Abu Dhabi.
