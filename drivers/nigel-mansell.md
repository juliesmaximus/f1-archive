# Driver Profile: Nigel Mansell

## Overview & The Lionhearted Champion
Nigel Ernest James Mansell CBE (born August 8, 1953) is a British racing icon and the **1992 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Champion**. Revered by British fans as "Red Five" and christened *"Il Leone"* (The Lion) by the passionate Italian Tifosi for his ferocious, wheel-banging bravery and never-say-die fighting spirit, Mansell is one of the most beloved and dramatic figures in motorsport history.

Mansell claimed 31 Grand Prix victories, held the all-time British Formula One win record for over two decades, and in 1993 became the only driver in motorsport history to hold both the **Formula One World Championship** and the **CART IndyCar World Series Championship** simultaneously.

## Formula One Career

### Colin Chapman's Lotus Protégé (1980–1984)
- Debuted with Team Lotus in 1980 under the personal mentorship of Colin Chapman, who saw raw genius beneath Mansell's rough exterior.
- Overcame horrific burns in his debut at the Austrian GP (fuel leak in the cockpit) and scored his first podium (3rd) at Zolder in 1981.
- In 1984, Mansell scored his maiden pole position at the Dallas Grand Prix, famously attempting to push his stricken Lotus across the finish line in 100°F heat before collapsing unconscious from heat exhaustion.

### The Williams-Honda Rivalries & Agonies (1985–1988)
- Joined Frank Williams and Patrick Head in 1985 alongside Keke Rosberg. Scored his **maiden Grand Prix victory** at the 1985 European Grand Prix at Brands Hatch, followed immediately by victory in South Africa.
- **1986 Championship Heartbreak:** Won 5 races and entered the Adelaide season finale needing only 3rd place to clinch the title. On lap 64 at 180 mph down Brabham Straight, Mansell's left-rear tire exploded in a violent shower of sparks, robbing him of the championship in one of motorsport's most tragic moments.
- **1987 Silverstone Miracle:** Mansell hunted down teammate Nelson Piquet, selling him a dummy at 170 mph into Stowe Corner with two laps remaining to take an iconic home victory before ecstatic British fans who stormed the track. A 180 mph crash during qualifying at Suzuka injured his spine, handing the title to Piquet.

### Ferrari & The Birth of "Il Leone" (1989–1990)
- The last driver personally signed by Enzo Ferrari before his death.
- Won on his Ferrari debut at the 1989 Brazilian Grand Prix in the revolutionary semi-automatic gearbox Ferrari 640.
- At the 1989 Hungarian Grand Prix, Mansell produced one of F1's greatest drives, charging from **12th on the grid** to pass Ayrton Senna around a backmarker into Turn 3 to win!

### The Williams-Renault FW14B Masterpiece (1991–1992)
- Reunited with Williams in 1991, winning 5 races and engaging in the legendary wheel-to-wheel sparking duel with Ayrton Senna down the Barcelona straight.
- **1992 World Championship Dominance:** Driving Adrian Newey and Patrick Head's active-suspension masterpiece, the Williams-Renault FW14B, Mansell steamrolled the competition:
  - Won the first 5 races of the season (a new all-time record).
  - Won 9 races in a single season (record that stood for 10 years).
  - Claimed 14 pole positions out of 16 races (record that stood for 19 years).
  - Clinched the **World Drivers' Championship** in August at the Hungarian Grand Prix with five races to spare.

### IndyCar Triumph & F1 Farewell (1993–1995)
- Moved to America in 1993, winning the **CART PPG Indy Car World Series** as a rookie with Newman/Haas Racing.
- Returned to Williams in late 1994 following the death of Ayrton Senna, scoring his **31st and final Formula One victory** at the 1994 Australian Grand Prix in Adelaide from pole position.
