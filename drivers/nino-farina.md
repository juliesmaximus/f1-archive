# Giuseppe "Nino" Farina — Career Statistics & Profile

Emilio Giuseppe "Nino" Farina (30 October 1906 – 30 June 1966) was an Italian racing driver who became the **inaugural FIA Formula One World Drivers' Champion in 1950**. Renowned for his aggressive, straight-arm driving posture and fearless composure, Farina won the first-ever World Championship Grand Prix (Silverstone 1950) driving the supercharged Alfa Romeo 158 "Alfetta".

---

## Career Summary

- **World Drivers' Championships**: **1** (1950 — Inaugural Champion)
- **First Grand Prix**: 1950 British Grand Prix (Silverstone, 1950-05-13, Started P1, Won race)
- **First Grand Prix Win**: 1950 British Grand Prix (Silverstone, 1950-05-13)
- **Final Grand Prix Win**: 1953 German Grand Prix (Nürburgring Nordschleife, Ferrari 500)
- **Final Grand Prix**: 1955 Italian Grand Prix (Monza, Ferrari 555)
- **Total Grand Prix Starts**: 33
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **5** (Silverstone 1950, Bremgarten 1950, Monza 1950, Spa 1951, Nürburgring 1953)
- **Total Pole Positions**: **5**
- **Total Podiums**: **20**
- **Total Career Points**: 115.33 (gross) / 127.33 (including dropped scores)

## Benchmark Historical Records
1. **First Formula One World Champion**: Clinched title at Monza on 3 September 1950.
2. **First Grand Slam in F1 History**: Silverstone 1950 (Pole position, victory, fastest lap, led every lap).
3. **Oldest Debut Race Winner**: Won his debut World Championship Grand Prix aged 43 years, 195 days.
