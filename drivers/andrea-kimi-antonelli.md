# Andrea Kimi Antonelli — Career Statistics & Profile

Andrea Kimi Antonelli (born 25 August 2006 in Bologna, Italy) is an Italian racing driver competing for the Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS Formula One Team. After winning back-to-back junior titles in Italian F4, ADAC F4, and Formula Regional European Championship, he skipped FIA Formula 3 to enter FIA Formula 2 in 2024 with Prema Racing. Signed by Mercedes as successor to Lewis Hamilton alongside George Russell, Antonelli leads the 2026 FIA Formula One World Drivers' Championship as of Round 18.
