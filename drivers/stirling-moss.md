# Sir Stirling Moss — Career Statistics & Profile

Sir Stirling Craufurd Moss OBE (17 September 1929 – 12 April 2020) was an English racing driver widely acknowledged as "the greatest driver never to win the World Championship". An icon of British motorsport and the epitome of sportsmanship, Moss won 16 Formula One World Championship Grands Prix and finished as championship runner-up four consecutive times (1955, 1956, 1957, 1958) and third three times (1959, 1960, 1961).

---

## Career Summary

- **World Drivers' Championships**: 0 (Championship runner-up: 1955, 1956, 1957, 1958)
- **First Grand Prix**: 1951 Swiss Grand Prix (Circuit Bremgarten, HWM-Alta)
- **First Grand Prix Podium**: 1954 Belgian Grand Prix (Spa-Francorchamps, Maserati 250F, finished 3rd)
- **First Grand Prix Win**: 1955 British Grand Prix (Aintree, Mercedes-Benz W196 — first home British winner)
- **Final Grand Prix Win**: 1961 German Grand Prix (Nürburgring Nordschleife, Lotus 18-Climax — tactical wet/dry genius defeating superior Ferraris)
- **Final Grand Prix**: 1962 Glover Trophy at Goodwood (survived catastrophic crash that ended his frontline professional career)

## Benchmark Historical Milestones
1. **First British Winner of the British Grand Prix**: Aintree 1955 driving the Mercedes-Benz W196 ahead of teammate Juan Manuel Fangio.
2. **First World Championship Win for a Mid-Engine Car**: 1958 Argentine Grand Prix driving Rob Walker's tiny 1.9-litre rear-mid-engine Cooper T43-Climax without making a tyre stop, revolutionizing Formula One design forever.
3. **Sportsmanship Legend**: At the 1958 Portuguese Grand Prix, Moss successfully intervened with stewards to prevent title rival Mike Hawthorn from being disqualified after Hawthorn reversed on track; the saved points enabled Hawthorn to beat Moss to the 1958 World Championship by a single point.
4. **1955 Mille Miglia Record**: Set the all-time course record in the Mercedes-Benz 300 SLR with Denis Jenkinson: 1,000 miles in 10 hours, 7 minutes, 48 seconds (average speed 99.28 mph / 159.65 km/h).
