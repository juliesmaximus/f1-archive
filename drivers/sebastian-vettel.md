# Sebastian Vettel - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Sebastian Vettel
- **Born:** July 3, 1987 (Heppenheim, West Germany)
- **World Drivers' Championships:** 4 (2010, 2011, 2012, 2013 with Red Bull Racing)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 299 (2007–2022)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 53 (4th all-time)
- **Pole Positions:** 57 (4th all-time)
- **Podiums:** 122
- **Fastest Laps:** 38
- **F1 Teams:** BMW Sauber (2007), Toro Rosso (2007–2008), Red Bull Racing (2009–2014), Ferrari (2015–2020), Aston Martin (2021–2022)

---

## The Boy Wonder of Heppenheim
- **Youngest World Champion in F1 History:** 23 years, 134 days (Abu Dhabi 2010).
- **Most Consecutive Wins in F1 History:** 9 consecutive victories in 2013 (Belgium to Brazil).
- **Most Wins in a Single Season (shared):** 13 wins in 2013.
