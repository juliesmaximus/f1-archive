## Historical Significance

Jody David Scheckter is the **first and only driver from the continent of Africa to win the Formula One World Drivers' Championship** (1979). Emerging as a wild, breathtakingly aggressive rookie who triggered the 1973 Woodcote pileup, Scheckter matured into one of the most intelligent, calculating, and complete Grand Prix drivers of his era.

---

## Formula 1 Career Overview

### Tyrrell & The Six-Wheeled P34 (1974–1976)
- Signed by Ken Tyrrell for 1974 following Jackie Stewart's retirement and François Cevert's death.
- Scored his maiden victory in Sweden and won the British GP at Brands Hatch, fighting for the World Championship until the finale and finishing 3rd overall.
- Won his home race, the 1975 South African Grand Prix at Kyalami.
- In 1976, made history by winning the Swedish Grand Prix at Anderstorp in the radical **six-wheeled Tyrrell P34**, which remains the **only six-wheeled car to win a Formula One Grand Prix**. Finished 3rd in the championship.

### Walter Wolf Racing (1977–1978)
- Joined Canadian oil magnate Walter Wolf's brand-new team, winning sensationally on the team's debut at the 1977 Argentine Grand Prix in the Wolf WR1.
- Won Monaco and Canada, finishing runner-up in the 1977 World Championship behind Niki Lauda.

### Scuderia Ferrari & World Championship (1979–1980)
- Partnered the mercurial Gilles Villeneuve at Scuderia Ferrari in 1979, driving the Flat-12 ground-effect **Ferrari 312T4**.
- Combining clinical consistency with race-winning pace, Scheckter won in Belgium, Monaco, and clinched the **1979 World Drivers' Championship** on home soil at Monza with a Ferrari 1-2 finish ahead of Villeneuve.
- Scheckter was Ferrari's last World Drivers' Champion for 21 years until Michael Schumacher won the title in 2000.
- Retired from racing at the end of 1980 at age 30, subsequently founding firearms simulator company FATS Inc. and the award-winning biodynamic Laverstoke Park Farm in England.
