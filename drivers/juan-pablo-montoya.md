# Juan Pablo Montoya - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Juan Pablo Montoya Roldán
- **Born:** September 20, 1975 (Bogotá, Colombia)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 94 (2001–2006)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 7
- **Pole Positions:** 13
- **Podiums:** 30
- **Fastest Laps:** 12
- **F1 Teams:** Williams (2001–2004), McLaren (2005–2006)

---

## The Fearless Colombian
Juan Pablo Montoya arrived in Formula 1 in 2001 after winning the 1999 CART Championship and the 2000 Indianapolis 500 on debut. Renowned for his uncompromising wheel-to-wheel combat:
- **Interlagos Turn 1 (2001):** Squeezed past Michael Schumacher on the restart in his 3rd career Grand Prix.
- **Monza Maiden Win (2001):** Scored his first victory at the Italian Grand Prix for Williams-BMW.
- **All-Time Speed Lap Record (Monza 2004):** Set the fastest lap in Formula 1 history at 262.242 km/h (162.950 mph) in the Williams FW26—a record that stood for 14 years.
