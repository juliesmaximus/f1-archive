# Michael Schumacher — Career Statistics & Profile

Michael Schumacher (born 3 January 1969 in Hürth, North Rhine-Westphalia, West Germany) is a German racing driver who redefined professional Formula One fitness, technical leadership, and dominance. Driving for Jordan, Benetton, Scuderia Ferrari, and Mercedes, Schumacher won seven Formula One World Drivers' Championships (1994, 1995, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004), a joint record shared with Lewis Hamilton. He led Ferrari to six consecutive Constructors' titles (1999–2004).

---

## Career Summary

- **World Drivers' Championships**: **7** (1994, 1995, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004)
- **First Grand Prix**: 1991 Belgian Grand Prix (Spa-Francorchamps, Jordan-Ford 191, Qualified P7)
- **First Grand Prix Win**: 1992 Belgian Grand Prix (Spa-Francorchamps, Benetton-Ford B192)
- **Final Grand Prix Win**: 2006 Chinese Grand Prix (Shanghai International Circuit, Ferrari 248 F1)
- **Final Grand Prix**: 2012 Brazilian Grand Prix (Interlagos, Mercedes F1 W03, Finished 7th)
- **Total Grand Prix Starts**: 306
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **91** (72 for Ferrari, 19 for Benetton)
- **Total Pole Positions**: **68**
- **Total Podiums**: **155**
- **Total Career Points**: 1,566 (Under contemporary scoring systems)
