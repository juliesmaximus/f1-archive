# Michael Schumacher - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Michael Schumacher
- **Born:** January 3, 1969 (Hürth-Hermülheim, West Germany)
- **World Championships:** 7 (1994, 1995, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 306 (1991–2006, 2010–2012)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 91
- **Pole Positions:** 68
- **Podiums:** 155
- **Fastest Laps:** 77
- **F1 Teams:** Jordan (1991), Benetton (1991–1995), Ferrari (1996–2006), Mercedes (2010–2012)

## 1991 Debut: The Spa Breakthrough
In August 1991, regular Jordan Grand Prix driver Bertrand Gachot was imprisoned in London following an altercation with a taxi driver. Jordan team owner Eddie Jordan urgently needed a replacement for the Belgian Grand Prix at Spa-Francorchamps. Managed by Willi Weber and supported by Mercedes-Benz (for whom Schumacher drove in the World Sportscar Championship), Schumacher was granted a test drive at Silverstone.

Despite never having driven a Formula 1 car around Spa, Schumacher stunned the paddock by qualifying the Jordan 191 in 7th position on the grid, matching the team's best qualifying of the season. Although his clutch failed on the opening lap of the race, his shocking speed initiated a high-stakes contract tug-of-war. Flavio Briatore moved swiftly to sign Schumacher for Benetton, ousting Roberto Moreno. Schumacher made his Benetton debut at Monza two weeks later, scoring his first championship points with a 5th-place finish.
