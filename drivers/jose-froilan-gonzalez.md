# José Froilán González — Career Statistics & Profile

José Froilán González (5 October 1922 – 15 June 2013), nicknamed *El Cabezón* ("Fat Head") or "The Pampas Bull", was an Argentine racing driver forever immortalized in motorsport history for scoring **Scuderia Ferrari's first-ever Formula One World Championship victory** at the 1951 British Grand Prix at Silverstone. A burly, physical, and exceptionally courageous driver, González finished as runner-up in the 1954 World Championship and also won the 1954 24 Hours of Le Mans for Ferrari.

- **Final Grand Prix**: 1960 Argentine Grand Prix (Buenos Aires, Ferrari Dino 246)

## The Historic Silverstone 1951 Triumph

On 14 July 1951 at Silverstone, González produced one of the most consequential drives in Formula 1 history:
1. Took Ferrari's maiden pole position with a lap of 1:43.4.
2. Led 60 of the 90 laps in the 4.5L naturally aspirated Ferrari 375.
3. Defeated his mentor and compatriot Juan Manuel Fangio's supercharged Alfa Romeo 159 by 51 seconds.
4. Ended Alfa Romeo's unbroken winning streak in European Formula One championship races.
