# Juan Manuel Fangio — Career Statistics & Profile

Juan Manuel Fangio (24 June 1911 – 17 July 1995), nicknamed *El Maestro* ("The Master"), was an Argentine racing driver who dominated the first decade of Formula One, winning five World Drivers' Championships (1951, 1954, 1955, 1956, 1957) with four different constructors (Alfa Romeo, Maserati, Mercedes-Benz, Ferrari)—a record that remains unbroken. Statistically, Fangio holds the highest win percentage in Formula One history at **46.15%** (24 wins from 52 starts).

---

## Career Summary

- **World Drivers' Championships**: **5** (1951, 1954, 1955, 1956, 1957)
- **First Grand Prix**: 1950 British Grand Prix (Silverstone, Alfa Romeo 158)
- **First Grand Prix Win**: 1950 Monaco Grand Prix (Circuit de Monaco, 1950-05-21)
- **Final Grand Prix Win**: 1957 German Grand Prix (Nürburgring Nordschleife, Maserati 250F — widely considered the greatest drive in F1 history)
- **Final Grand Prix**: 1958 French Grand Prix (Reims-Gueux, Maserati 250F, finished 4th)
- **Total Grand Prix Starts**: 51 (52 entries)
- **Total Grand Prix Wins**: **24** (46.15% win percentage — all-time record)
- **Total Pole Positions**: **29** (55.77% pole percentage — all-time record)
- **Total Podiums**: **35** (67.31% podium percentage)
- **Total Career Points**: 245 (gross) / 277.64 (net counted points)
