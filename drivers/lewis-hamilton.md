# Sir Lewis Hamilton — Career Statistics & Profile

Sir Lewis Carl Davidson Hamilton MBE HonFREng (born 7 January 1985 in Stevenage, Hertfordshire, England) is a British racing driver competing for Scuderia Ferrari HP. Statistically the most successful driver in Formula One history, Hamilton holds the outright records for most World Drivers' Championships (7, tied with Michael Schumacher), most race victories (106), most pole positions (104), and most podium finishes (203).
