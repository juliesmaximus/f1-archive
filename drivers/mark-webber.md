# Mark Webber - Historical Biography & Career Record

## Career Overview
- **Full Name:** Mark Alan Webber
- **Born:** August 27, 1976 (Queanbeyan, New South Wales, Australia)
- **Grand Prix Starts:** 215 (2002–2013)
- **Grand Prix Wins:** 9
- **Pole Positions:** 13
- **Podiums:** 42
- **Fastest Laps:** 19
- **F1 Teams:** Minardi (2002), Jaguar (2003–2004), Williams (2005–2006), Red Bull Racing (2007–2013)

---

## The Gritty Aussie Warrior
Renowned for raw qualifying speed, uncompromising racecraft, and ferocious bravery:
- **Two-Time Monaco GP Winner:** Won the prestigious Monaco Grand Prix in 2010 and 2012.
- **Iconic Overtakes:** Legendary pass on Fernando Alonso into the compression of Eau Rouge at Spa in 2011.
- **Uncompromising Integrity:** Famous for speaking his mind on team radio ('Not bad for a number two driver' and 'Multi 21, Seb').
